# 10 OVERDOSAGE

Overdosage with ANECTINE may result in neuromuscular block beyond the time needed for surgery and anesthesia.
This may be manifested by skeletal muscle weakness, decreased respiratory reserve, low tidal volume, or apnea.
The primary treatment is maintenance of a patent airway and respiratory support until recovery of normal respiration is assured.
Depending on the dose and duration of ANECTINE administration, the characteristic depolarizing neuromuscular block (Phase I) may change to a block with characteristics superficially resembling a non-depolarizing block (Phase II) [see Warnings and Precautions (5.8)].
