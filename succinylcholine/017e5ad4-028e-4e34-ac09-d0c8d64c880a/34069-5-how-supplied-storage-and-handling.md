# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Succinylcholine chloride injection, USP is supplied as a clear, colorless solution in the following concentrations and packages:

| Unit of Sale | Concentration |
| --- | --- |
| NDC 70710-1377-2 A box containing 25 Multiple-dose Fliptop Vials | 200 mg/10 mL (20 mg/mL) |

Refrigeration of undiluted succinylcholine chloride injection will assure full potency until expiration date.

Store in refrigerator 2 °C to 8 °C (36 °F to 46 °F).
The multiple-dose vials are stable for up to 14 days at room temperature without significant loss of potency.

Call your doctor for medical advice about side effects.
You may report side effects to

FDA at 1-800-FDA-1088.
