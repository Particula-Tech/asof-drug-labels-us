# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

QUELICIN™ (Succinylcholine Chloride Injection, USP) is supplied as a clear, colorless solution in the following concentration and package:

| Unit of Sale | Concentration |
| --- | --- |
| NDC 0409-6629-02 Tray containing 25 Multiple-dose Fliptop Vials | 200 mg/10 mL (20 mg/mL) |

Refrigeration of undiluted QUELICIN will assure full potency until expiration date.

Store in refrigerator 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F).
The multiple-dose vials are stable for up to 14 days at room temperature without significant loss of potency.
