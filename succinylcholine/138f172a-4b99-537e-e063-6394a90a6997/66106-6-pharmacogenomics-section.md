# Pharmacogenomics

RYR1 and CACNA1S are polymorphic genes, and multiple pathogenic variants have been associated with malignant hyperthermia susceptibility (MHS) in patients receiving succinylcholine, including Anectine.
Case reports as well as ex-vivo studies have identified multiple variants in RYR1 and CACNA1S associated with MHS.
Variant pathogenicity should be assessed based on prior clinical experience, functional studies, prevalence information, or other evidence (see CONTRAINDICATIONS, WARNINGS).
