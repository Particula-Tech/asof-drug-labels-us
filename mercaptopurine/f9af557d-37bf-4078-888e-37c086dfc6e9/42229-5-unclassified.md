# CAUTION

PURINETHOL (mercaptopurine) is a potent drug.
It should not be used unless a diagnosis of acute lymphatic leukemia has been adequately established and the responsible physician is experienced with the risks of PURINETHOL and knowledgeable in assessing response to chemotherapy.
