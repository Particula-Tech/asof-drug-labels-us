# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

MERCAPTOPURINE oral suspension is supplied as 2,000 mg/100 mL (20 mg/mL) pink to brown viscous liquid in amber glass multiple-dose bottles.
In addition, a press-in bottle adapter and two oral dispensing syringes (one 1 mL and one 5 mL) are provided.
Each carton NDC 62484-1020-4 contains 1 bottle of MERCAPTOPURINE NDC 62484-1020-3.

- Store MERCAPTOPURINE between 59ºF to 77ºF (15ºC to 25ºC).
  Store in a dry place.

MERCAPTOPURINE is a hazardous drug.
Follow special handling and disposal procedures.1
