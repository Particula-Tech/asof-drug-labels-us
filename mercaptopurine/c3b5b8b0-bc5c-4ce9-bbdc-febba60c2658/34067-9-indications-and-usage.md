# INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Mercaptopurine is indicated for maintenance therapy of acute lymphatic (lymphocytic, lymophoblastic) leukemia as part of a combination regimen.
The response to this agent depends upon the particular subclassification of acute lymphatic leukemia and the age of the patient (pediatric or adult).

Mercaptopurine is not effective prophylaxis or treatment of central nervous system leukemia.

Mercaptopurine is not effective in acute myelogenous leukemia, chronic lymphatic leukemia, the lymphomas (including Hodgkin’s Disease), or solid tumors.
