# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

PURIXAN (mercaptopurine) oral suspension is supply as 2000 mg/100 mL (20 mg/mL) is a pink to brown viscous liquid in amber glass multiple-dose bottles.
In addition, a press-in bottle adapter and two oral dispensing syringes (one 1 mL and one 5 mL) are provided.
Each carton NDC 62484-0020-2 contains 1 bottle of PURIXAN NDC 62484-0020-1.

- Store PURIXAN between 59ºF to 77ºF (15ºC to 25ºC).
  Store in a dry place.

PURIXAN is a cytotoxic drug.
Follow special handling and disposal procedures.1
