# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Oxaliplatin injection, USP is supplied in clear, glass, single-dose vials with gray elastomeric stoppers and aluminum flip-off seals containing 50 mg or 100 mg as a clear, colorless, sterile, preservative-free, aqueous solution at a concentration of 5 mg/mL.

NDC 67457-469-10: 50 mg/10 mL single-dose vial with grey flip-off seal individually packaged in a carton.

NDC 67457-442-20: 100 mg/20 mL single-dose vial with red flip-off seal individually packaged in a carton.

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]
Do not freeze and protect from light (keep in original outer carton).
Discard unused portion.

Oxaliplatin injection is a cytotoxic drug.
Follow applicable special handling and disposal procedures.1 The use of gloves is recommended.
If a solution of oxaliplatin injection contacts the skin, wash the skin immediately and thoroughly with soap and water.
If oxaliplatin injection contacts the mucous membranes, flush thoroughly with water.
