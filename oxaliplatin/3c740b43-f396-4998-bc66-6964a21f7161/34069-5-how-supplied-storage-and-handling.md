# HOW SUPPLIED

ELOXATIN is supplied in clear, glass, single-use vials with gray elastomeric stoppers and aluminum flip-off seals containing 50 mg, 100 mg or 200 mg of oxaliplatin as a sterile, preservative-free, aqueous solution at a concentration of 5 mg/ml.
Water for Injection, USP is present as an inactive ingredient.

NDC 0024-0590-10: 50 mg single-use vial with green flip-off seal individually packaged in a carton.

NDC 0024-0591-20: 100 mg single-use vial with dark blue flip-off seal individually packaged in a carton.

NDC 0024-0592-40: 200 mg single-use vial with orange flip-off seal individually packaged in a carton.
