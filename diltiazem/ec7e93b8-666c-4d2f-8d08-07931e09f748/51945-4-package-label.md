PRINCIPAL DISPLAY PANEL - 60 mg

NDC 0378-6060-01

Twice-a-Day Dosage

DILTIAZEM HYDROCHLORIDE EXTENDED-RELEASE CAPSULES, USP 60 mg*

100 CAPSULES (Rx only)

*Each extended-release capsule contains diltiazem hydrochloride, USP 60 mg (equivalent to 55.1 mg as diltiazem).

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP using a child-resistant closure.

Keep container tightly closed.

Keep this and all medication out of the reach of children.

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[See USP for Controlled Room Temperature.]

Usual Dosage: See accompanying prescribing information.
