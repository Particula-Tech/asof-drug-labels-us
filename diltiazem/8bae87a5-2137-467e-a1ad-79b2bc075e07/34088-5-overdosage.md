# 10 OVERDOSAGE

Overdosage experience is limited.
In the event of overdosage or an exaggerated response, appropriate supportive measures should be employed.

Diltiazem does not appear to be removed by peritoneal or hemodialysis.
Limited data suggest that plasmapheresis may hasten diltiazem elimination following overdose.

The intravenous LD50's in mice and rats were 60 and 38 mg/kg, respectively.
The toxic dose in humans is not known.
