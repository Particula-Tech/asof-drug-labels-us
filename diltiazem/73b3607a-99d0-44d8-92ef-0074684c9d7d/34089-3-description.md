# 11 DESCRIPTION

Diltiazem hydrochloride is a nondihydropyridine calcium channel blocker (slow channel blocker or calcium antagonist).
Chemically, diltiazem hydrochloride is 1,5-benzothiazepin-4(5H)-one, 3-(acetyloxy)-5-[2-(dimethylamino)ethyl]-2, 3-dihydro-2-(4-methoxyphenyl)-, monohydrochloride, (+)-cis-.

Diltiazem hydrochloride is a white to off-white crystalline powder with a bitter taste.
It is soluble in water, methanol and chloroform.
It has a molecular weight of 450.99.
Diltiazem Hydrochloride Extended-Release Tablets are formulated as a once-a-day extended-release tablet for oral administration containing 120 mg, 180 mg, 240 mg, 300 mg, 360 mg or 420 mg of diltiazem hydrochloride.

Tablets also contain: carnauba wax, colloidal silicon dioxide, croscarmellose sodium, ethyl acrylate and methyl methacrylate copolymer dispersion, hydrogenated vegetable oil, hypromellose, magnesium stearate, microcrystalline cellulose, microcrystalline wax, polydextrose, polyethylene glycol, polysorbate, povidone, pregelatinized starch, simethicone, sodium starch glycolate, sucrose stearate, talc, and titanium dioxide.
