# HOW SUPPLIED

Diltiazem Hydrochloride Tablets USP are available as:

30 mg tablets; film-coated, round, biconvex, faint orange, debossed "93"-"318", packaged in bottles of 100 and 500.

60 mg tablets; film-coated, round, biconvex, orange, scored, debossed "93"-"319", packaged in bottles of 100 and 500.

90 mg tablets; film-coated, oblong, faint orange, scored, debossed "93"-"320", packaged in bottles of 100.

120 mg tablets; film-coated, oblong, orange, scored, debossed "93"-"321", packaged in bottles of 100.

Store at controlled room temperature, between 20o and 25oC (68o and 77oF) (see USP).

Dispense contents in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP, with a child-resistant closure (as required).
