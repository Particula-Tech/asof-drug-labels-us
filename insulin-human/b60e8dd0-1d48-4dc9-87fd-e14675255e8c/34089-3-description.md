# DESCRIPTION

Humulin is synthesized in a special non–disease–producing laboratory strain of Escherichia coli bacteria that has been genetically altered by the addition of the gene for human insulin production.
Humulin R (U–500) consists of zinc–insulin crystals dissolved in a clear fluid.
Humulin R (U–500) is a sterile solution and is for subcutaneous injection.
It should not be used intravenously or intramuscularly.
The concentration of Humulin R (U–500) is 500 units/mL.

Each milliliter contains 500 units of biosynthetic human insulin, 16 mg glycerin, 2.5 mg Metacresol as a preservative, and zinc–oxide calculated to supplement endogenous zinc to obtain a total zinc content of 0.017 mg/100 units.
Sodium hydroxide and/or hydrochloric acid may be added during manufacture to adjust the pH.
