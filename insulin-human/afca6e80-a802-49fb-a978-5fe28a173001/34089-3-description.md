# 11 DESCRIPTION

Insulin human is a short-acting human insulin.
It is a polypeptide hormone and is produced by recombinant DNA technology, utilizing Pichia pastoris (a yeast) as the production organism.
Insulin human is regular human insulin and has the empirical formula C257H383N65O77S6 and a molecular weight of 5808.

Figure 1: Structural formula of Insulin Human

MYXREDLIN (insulin human) in 0.9% sodium chloride injection for intravenous use is a sterile, preservative-free, nonpyrogenic, clear, aqueous, and colorless solution supplied in a 100 mL GALAXY single-dose container.
MYXREDLIN contains 100 units of insulin human in 100 milliliters of 0.9% sodium chloride injection.
Each milliliter of solution contains 1 unit Insulin Human, USP; 0.412 mg Dibasic Sodium Phosphate Anhydrous, USP; 0.29 mg Monobasic Sodium Phosphate Monohydrate, USP; 9 mg Sodium Chloride, USP; and Water for Injection, USP.
The pH range is 6.5-7.2.
