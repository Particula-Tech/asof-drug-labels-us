# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

AFREZZA is contraindicated:

- During episodes of hypoglycemia [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3)].
- In patients with chronic lung disease, such as asthma or COPD, because of the risk of acute bronchospasm [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
- In patients with a previous severe hypersensitivity reaction to any regular human insulin product or any of the inactive ingredients in AFREZZA.
  Severe, life-threatening, generalized allergy, including anaphylaxis, can occur with AFREZZA [see Warnings and Precautions (5.7)].
