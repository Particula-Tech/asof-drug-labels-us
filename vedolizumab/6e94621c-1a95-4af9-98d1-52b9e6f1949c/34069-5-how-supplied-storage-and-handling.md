# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

ENTYVIO (vedolizumab) is supplied in sterile 20 mL single-use glass vials, containing 300 mg of vedolizumab as a white to off-white cake.

| NDC 64764-300-20 | 300 mg single-dose vial in individual carton |
| --- | --- |

Refrigerate unopened vials at 2° to 8°C (36º to 46ºF).
Retain in original package to protect from light.
