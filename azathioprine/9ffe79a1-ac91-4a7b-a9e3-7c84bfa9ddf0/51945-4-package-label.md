# VIAL LABEL

NDC 0143-9566-01 Rx only Azathioprine Sodium for Injection, USP

Equivalent to 100 mg/vial FOR INTRAVENOUS USE ONLY

STERILE LYOPHILIZED MATERIAL

Usual Dosage: See package insert.

Each vial contains azathioprine sodium, equivalent to 100 mg

azathioprine, sodium hydroxide and, if necessary, hydrochloric

acid to adjust pH.

Preparation of solution: Inject into the vial 10 mL Sterile Water

for Injection.

Swirl the vial until a clear solution results.
Use within 24 hours.

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room

Temperature].

Protect from light.
Retain in carton until time of use
