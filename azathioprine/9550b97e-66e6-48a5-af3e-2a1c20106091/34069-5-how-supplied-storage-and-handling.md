# HOW SUPPLIED:

20-mL vial, each containing the equivalent of 100 mg azathioprine (as the sodium salt) (NDC 65483-551-01).

Store at 20-25° C (USP controlled Room Temperature) (68° to 77°F) and protect from light.

The sterile, lyophilized sodium salt is yellow, and should be dissolved in Sterile Water for Injection (see DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION: Parenteral Administration).
