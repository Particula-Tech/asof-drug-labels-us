# HOW SUPPLIED

Nifedipine Capsules, USP are available as white opaque, oblong soft gelatin capsules containing 10 mg of nifedipine.
Each capsule is imprinted with " HP 194" in black ink.
They are supplied as follows:

Bottles of 90: 10 mg (NDC 62135-739-90)

Nifedipine Capsules, USP are available as peach opaque, oblong soft gelatin capsules containing 20 mg of nifedipine.
Each capsule is imprinted with " HP 195" in black ink.
They are supplied as follows:

Bottles of 90: 20 mg (NDC 62135-740-90)

The capsules should be protected from light and moisture and stored at Controlled Room Temperature 68° to 77°F (20° to 25°C); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F), in the manufacturer's original container.
