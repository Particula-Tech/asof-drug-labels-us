# CONTRAINDICATIONS

Concomitant administration with strong P450 inducers, such as rifampin, are contraindicated since the efficacy of nifedipine tablets could be significantly reduced.
(See PRECAUTIONS, Drug Interactions.)

Nifedipine must not be used in cases of cardiogenic shock.

Adalat is contraindicated in patients with a known hypersensitivity to any component of the tablet.
