# HOW SUPPLIED

Nifedipine Extended-Release Tablets, USP are supplied as 60 mg unscored, round film-coated tablets as follows:

| Strength | Color | Markings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 60 mg | Mustard yellow | 60 mg unscored, round, film-coated tablets, engraved with "B" on one side and "60" on the other side. |

Nifedipine Extended-Release Tablets, USP are supplied in:

| Strength | Quantity | NDC Number |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 60 mg | Bottles of 100 | 68682-106-10 |
| 60 mg | Bottles of 300 | 68682-106-30 |

The tablets should be protected from light and moisture and stored at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Dispense in tight, light-resistant containers.
