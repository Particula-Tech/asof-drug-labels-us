# HOW SUPPLIED

Nifedipine extended-release tablets, USP, are supplied as 30 mg and 60 mg reddish brown, unscored, film-coated, round tablets, debossed with “B” on one side and “30” or “60” on the other.

Nifedipine extended-release tablets are supplied in:

| Bottles of 100 | 30 mg | NDC 68682-108-10 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bottles of 300 | 30 mg | NDC 68682-108-30 |
| Bottles of 100 | 60 mg | NDC 68682-109-10 |
| Bottles of 300 | 60 mg | NDC 68682-109-30 |

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Protect from moisture and humidity.

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container.

Packed with desiccant.
