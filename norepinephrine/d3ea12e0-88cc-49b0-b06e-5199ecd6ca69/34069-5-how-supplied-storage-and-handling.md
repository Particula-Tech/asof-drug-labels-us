# HOW SUPPLIED

Supplied as:

4 mg/4 mL Single-dose Vials in boxes of 10 (NDC No. 67457-852-04).

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15º to 30ºC (59º to 86ºF).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]
Protect from light.
