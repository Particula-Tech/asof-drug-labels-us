# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Norepinephrine Bitartrate Injection, USP, is a sterile, colorless solution for injection intended for intravenous use.
It contains the equivalent of 1 mg of norepinephrine base per 1 mL (4 mg/4 mL).
It is available as 4 mg/4 mL in single-dose amber glass vials.
Supplied as:

| NDC | Strength | How supplied |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 51991-983-99 - Vial 51991-983-17 - Carton of 10 | 1 mg/mL | 4 mg/4 mL in 5 mL glass vial; Carton of 10 × 4 mL Single-dose Vials |

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

Store in original carton until time of administration to protect from light.
Discard unused portion.
