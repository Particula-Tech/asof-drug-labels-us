# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Norepinephrine Bitartrate Injection, USP, is a sterile, colorless to pale yellow solution for injection intended for intravenous use.
It contains the equivalent of 1 mg of norepinephrine base per 1 mL (4 mg/4 mL).
It is available as 4 mg/4 mL in single-dose amber glass vials.

Supplied as:

| Unit of Sale | Concentration |
| --- | --- |
| NDC 43066-997-10Single-dose vials in boxes of 10 | 4 mg/4 mL(1 mg/mL) |

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

Store in original carton until time of administration to protect from light.
Discard unused portion.
