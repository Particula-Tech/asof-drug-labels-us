HYCAMTIN®

(topotecan) Capsules

0.25 mg

Rx only

HYCAMTIN is cytotoxic.

HANDLING: HYCAMTIN capsules should not be opened or crushed.
Direct contact of the capsule content with skin or mucous membranes should be avoided.
If such contacts occur, wash thoroughly with soap and water.

Store refrigerated 2o to 8oC (36o to 46oF).

Store bottle protected from light in the original outer carton.

Dosage: See accompanying prescribing information.
