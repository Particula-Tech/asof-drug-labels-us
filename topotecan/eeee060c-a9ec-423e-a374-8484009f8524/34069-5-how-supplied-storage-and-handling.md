# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

HYCAMTIN for injection is supplied as a sterile, lyophilized, buffered, light yellow to greenish powder for reconstitution in 4-mg (free base) single-dose vials.

NDC 0078-0674-61 (package of 1)

Store between 20°C and 25°C (68°F and 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature] in original carton.
Protect from light.

HYCAMTIN for injection is a cytotoxic drug.
Follow applicable handling and disposal procedures.1
