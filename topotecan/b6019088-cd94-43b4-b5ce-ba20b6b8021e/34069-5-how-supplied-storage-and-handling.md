# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Topotecan hydrochloride for injection, USP is supplied as a sterile, lyophilized, buffered, light yellow to greenish powder.

Topotecan hydrochloride for injection, USP is supplied as follows:

| Product No. | Unit of Sale | Strength | Each |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 760210 | NDC 63323-762-10 Individually packaged | 4 mg (base) per vial | NDC 63323-762-10 Single Dose Vial |

The container closure is not made with natural rubber latex

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light; product is light-sensitive.
Retain in carton.
Handle and dispose of topotecan hydrochloride for injection, USP consistent with recommendations for the handling and disposal of hazardous drugs1.
