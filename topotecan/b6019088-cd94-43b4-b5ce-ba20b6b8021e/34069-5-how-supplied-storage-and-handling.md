# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Topotecan hydrochloride for injection is supplied as a sterile, lyophilized, buffered, light yellow to greenish powder.
Topotecan hydrochloride for injection is supplied as follows:

| Product No. | NDC No. | Strength |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 760210 | 63323-762-10 | 4 mg (free base) per vial | Single dose vial, packaged individually. |
| 760217 | 63323-762-17 | 4 mg (free base) per vial | Single dose vial, in packages of 5. |

The container closure is not made with natural rubber latex.

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light; product is light-sensitive.
Retain in carton.
Handle and dispose of topotecan hydrochloride for injection consistent with recommendations for the handling and disposal of hazardous drugs1.
