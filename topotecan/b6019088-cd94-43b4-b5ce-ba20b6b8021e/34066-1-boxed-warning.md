# WARNING: BONE MARROW SUPPRESSION

Topotecan hydrochloride can cause severe myelosuppression.
Administer only to patients with baseline neutrophil counts of greater than or equal to 1,500 cells/mm 3 and platelet counts greater than or equal to 100,000 cells/mm 3.
Monitor blood cell counts [see Warnings and Precautions(5.1)].
