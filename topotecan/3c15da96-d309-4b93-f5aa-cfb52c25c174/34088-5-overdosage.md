# OVERDOSAGE

There is no known antidote for overdosage with HYCAMTIN.
The primary anticipated complication of overdosage would consist of bone marrow suppression.

One patient on a single-dose regimen of 17.5 mg/m2 given on day 1 of a 21-day cycle had received a single dose of 35 mg/m2.
This patient experienced severe neutropenia (nadir of 320/mm3) 14 days later but recovered without incident.

The LD10 in mice receiving single intravenous infusions of HYCAMTIN was 75 mg/m2 (CI 95%: 47 to 97).
