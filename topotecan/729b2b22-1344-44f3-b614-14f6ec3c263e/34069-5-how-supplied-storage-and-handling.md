# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Topotecan Hydrochloride for Injection is supplied as a sterile, lyophilized, yellow powder.

Topotecan Hydrochloride for Injection is supplied in 4-mg (free base) single‑dose vials.

NDC 16729-151-31 (package of 1)

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at controlled room temperature between 20°C and 25°C (68°F and 77°F) [see USP].
Protect from light in original carton.
Handle and dispose of topotecan hydrochloride for injection consistent with recommendations for the handling and disposal of hazardous drugs 1.
