# 11 DESCRIPTION

Topotecan is a semi-synthetic derivative of camptothecin and is an anti-tumor drug with topoisomerase I-inhibitory activity.

The chemical name for topotecan hydrochloride is (S)-10-[(dimethylamino)methyl]-4-ethyl-4,9-dihydroxy-1H-pyrano[3',4':6,7]indolizino[1,2-b]quinoline-3,14-(4H,12H)-dione 1.25 hydrochloride.
It has the molecular formula C23H23N3O5 nHCl (n=1.25) and a molecular weight of 467.02.
Topotecan hydrochloride is soluble in water and melts with decomposition at 213°C to 218°C.

Topotecan injection is supplied as a sterile, non-pyrogenic, clear, yellow to yellow-green solution at a topotecan free base concentration of 4 mg/4 mL (1 mg/mL) available in single use vials.
Each mL of Topotecan injection contains topotecan hydrochloride equivalent to 1 mg of topotecan as free base, 5 mg tartaric acid, NF and water for injection, USP.
Hydrochloric acid and/or sodium hydroxide may be used for pH adjustment to pH 2.6 – 3.2.

The solution must be diluted before administration by intravenous infusion.
