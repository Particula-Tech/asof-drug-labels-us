# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

How Supplied

Topotecan Injection is supplied in 4 mg/4 mL (1 mg/mL) single use vials, in packages of 1 vial per carton.

NDC 0409-0302-01

(package of 1)

Storage

Unopened vials of Topotecan Injection are stable until the date indicated on the package when stored at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) and protected from light in the original package.

Topotecan Injection diluted for infusion is stable between 20°C and 25°C (68°F and 77°F) in ambient lighting conditions for 24 hours.
