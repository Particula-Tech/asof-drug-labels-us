# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Topotecan Injection is supplied in 4 mg/4 mL (1 mg/mL topotecan free base) single-dose vials.
Each vial contains 4 mL of the sterile, clear, yellow to yellow-green solution.

NDC 0409-0302-01 (Package of 1 single-dose vial)

Store refrigerated between 2°C and 8°C (36°F and 46°F) in the original carton to protect from light.
Discard unused portion.

Topotecan Injection is a cytotoxic drug.
Follow applicable handling and disposal procedures.1
