# WARNING: BONE MARROW SUPPRESSION

Do not give Topotecan Injection to patients with baseline neutrophil counts less than 1,500 cells/mm 3.
In order to monitor the occurrence of bone marrow suppression, primarily neutropenia, which may be severe and result in infection and death, monitor peripheral blood counts frequently on all patients receiving Topotecan Injection.
