# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Topotecan Injection is supplied in the following:

1 mg/mL Multiple Dose Vial

NDC 16729-243-30 (package of 1)

4 mg/4 mL (1 mg/mL) Multiple Dose Vial

NDC 16729-243-31 (package of 1)

Unopened vials of Topotecan Injection are stable until the date indicated on the package when stored at controlled room temperature between 20ºC and 25°C (68°F and 77°F) with excursions allowed from 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F).
Retain in carton to protect from light.

Topotecan Injection is supplied as a multiple dose vial.
Studies have shown the product is stable for 28 days after initial puncture when stored at room temperature.

Topotecan Injection diluted for infusion is stable for 4 hours at room temperature or 24 hours at refrigerated temperature in ambient lighting conditions.
