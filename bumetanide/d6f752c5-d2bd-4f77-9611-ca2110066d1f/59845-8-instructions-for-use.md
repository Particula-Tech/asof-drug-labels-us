Enbymyst™ (bumetanide nasal spray) 0.5 mg per device

This Instructions for Use contains Information on how to use ENBUMYST

- Talk to your healthcare provider on how to use ENBUMYST.
- Read this Instructions for Use before using ENBUMYST.

Storing ENBUMYST

- Store in carton at room temperature of 59°F to 77°F (15°C to 25°C).
- Do not freeze.
- Keep ENBUMYST and all medicines out of the reach of children.

Important Information You Need to Know Before Using ENBUMYST

- For use in the nose only.
- Each blister contains 1 device.
  Do not remove from blister until ready to use.
- Check the expiration date.
  Do not use past the expiration date on the blister or if the device appears damaged.
- Do not test, prime (pre-spray), or reuse the device.
  Each device can only be sprayed 1 time.
- Refer to the table and only remove the number of devices needed for your prescribed dose (up to 4).

| Dose | Number of Nasal Spray Devices |
| --- | --- |
| 0.5 mg | 1 device |
| 1 mg | 2 devices |
| 1.5 mg | 3 devices |
| 2 mg | 4 devices |

INSTRUCTIONS FOR USE

| Step 1 | Remove Device from Blister |
| --- | --- |
|  | Before first device use only, blow your nose gently to clear both nostrils. Remove the number of blisters needed for your prescribed dose from the carton. Peel back the tab with the triangle to open the blister and remove the device. |
| Step 2 | Properly Hold Device |
|  | Hold ENBUMYST with your thumb on the bottom of the plunger and your index and middle fingers on either side of the nozzle. Do not press the plunger yet. |
| Step 3 | Insert Device into Nose |
|  | Tilt your head back slightly. Gently insert the tip of the nozzle into one nostril until your fingers on either side of the nozzle are against the bottom of your nose. |
| Step 4 | Press Plunger Firmly |
|  | Gently press the other nostril with your finger to keep it closed. Make sure that the tip of the device is pointed straight into the nose and not against the side (wall) of the nose. Slowly breath through the open nostril. Press the plunger firmly to give the dose of ENBUMYST. |
| Step 5 | Remove and Dispose of Device |
|  | Remove ENBUMYST nasal spray from the nostril after giving the dose. Dispose of (throw away) the used device and blister in the household trash. |
| Dose Completion | Alternate Nostrils |
|  | If your prescribed dose requires more than 1 ENBUMYST device, repeat Steps 1 to 5 to complete your dose. Alternate each time between your right and left nostrils. Note: Do not blow your nose. Keep your head tilted back for approximately 1 minute before using another device. This will allow the medicine to go into your nasal passage without dripping out. |
