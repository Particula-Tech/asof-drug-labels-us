# HOW SUPPLIED

Bumetanide Tablets, USP are supplied in bottles of 100 and 500 as:

0.5 mg tablets, green-round, bisected; imprinted E 128

1 mg tablets, yellow-round, bisected; imprinted E 129

2 mg tablets, beige to light brown-round, bisected; imprinted E 130

## Storage

Store at 20°-25°C (68°-77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Dispense contents in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP with a child-resistant closure, as required.
