# HOW SUPPLIED

Bumetanide Tablets USP, for oral administration, are available as:

0.5 mg: Light green colored, elliptical, flat-face, beveled edged tablets, engraved "BUMEX 0.5" on one side, and supplied as:

NDC 42799-119-01 bottles of 100

1 mg: Yellow colored, elliptical, flat-face, beveled edged tablets, engraved "BUMEX 1" on one side, and supplied as:

NDC 42799-120-01 bottles of 100

NDC 42799-120-02 bottles of 500

2 mg: Peach colored, elliptical, flat-face, beveled edged tablets, engraved "BUMEX 2" on one side, and supplied as:

NDC 42799-121-01 bottles of 100

NDC 42799-121-02 bottles of 500

Store tablets at 59° to 86°F (15° to 30°C).

Dispense contents in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP with a child-resistant closure, as required.

To report SUSPECTED ADVERSE REACTIONS, call 1-866-982-5438 or FDA at 1-800-FDA-1088 or www.fda.gov/medwatch.
