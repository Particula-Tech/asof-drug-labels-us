Recommended Storage Conditions for OZEMPIC

Single-patient-use pens (with multiple weekly doses):

Prior to first use, the OZEMPIC pen should be stored in a refrigerator between 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F).
Do not store in the freezer or directly adjacent to the refrigerator cooling element.
Do not freeze OZEMPIC and do not use OZEMPIC if it has been frozen.
OZEMPIC must be kept in the original carton until time of administration.

After first use of the OZEMPIC pen, the pen can be stored for 56 days at controlled room temperature 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) or in a refrigerator 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F).
Do not freeze.
Keep the pen cap on when not in use.
OZEMPIC should be protected from excessive heat and sunlight.

Always remove and safely discard the needle after each injection and store the OZEMPIC pen without an injection needle attached.
Always use a new needle for each injection.

Single-dose prefilled syringes: Store the OZEMPIC prefilled syringe refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F).
If needed, the syringe can be kept from 8°C to 30°C (46°F to 86°F) up to 28 days.
Do not freeze OZEMPIC and do not use OZEMPIC if it has been frozen.
Protect OZEMPIC from light.
OZEMPIC must be kept in the original carton until time of administration.
Discard the OZEMPIC syringe after use.
