# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

OZEMPIC is contraindicated in patients with:

- •A personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC) or in patients with Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia syndrome type 2 (MEN 2) [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
- •A prior serious hypersensitivity reaction to semaglutide or to any of the excipients in OZEMPIC.
  Serious hypersensitivity reactions including anaphylaxis and angioedema have been reported with OZEMPIC [see Warnings and Precautions (5.7)].
