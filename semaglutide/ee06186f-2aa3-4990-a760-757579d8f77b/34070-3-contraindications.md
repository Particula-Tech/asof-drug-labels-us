# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

WEGOVY is contraindicated in the following conditions:

- •A personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC) or in patients with Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia syndrome type 2 (MEN 2) [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
- •A prior serious hypersensitivity reaction to semaglutide or to any of the excipients in WEGOVY.
  Serious hypersensitivity reactions, including anaphylaxis and angioedema, have been reported with semaglutide [see Warnings and Precautions (5.6)].
