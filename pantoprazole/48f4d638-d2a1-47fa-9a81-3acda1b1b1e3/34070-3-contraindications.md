# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

PROTONIX is contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity reactions including anaphylaxis to the formulation [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)] or any substituted benzimidazole.
Hypersensitivity reactions may include anaphylaxis, anaphylactic shock, angioedema, bronchospasm, acute interstitial nephritis, and urticaria [see Adverse Reactions (6)].
