# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

How Supplied

Pantoprazole sodium delayed-release tablets USP 20 mg are yellow, round, biconvex coated tablets printed with “R332” on one side with black ink and plain on the other side.
They are supplied in bottles of 30’s, 90’s, 100’s, 500’s, 1000’s, unit dose package of 30 (3 x 10) and unit dosage package of 100 (10 x 10).

Storage

Store pantoprazole sodium delayed-release tablets at 20°–25°C (68°–77°F); excursions permitted to 15°-30°C (59°-86°F).
