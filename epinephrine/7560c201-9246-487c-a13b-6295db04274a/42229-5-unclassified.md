EPIPEN® 0.3 mg EPINEPHRINE AUTO-INJECTOR Auto-Injector for Intramuscular Injection of Epinephrine For the Emergency Treatment of Allergic Reactions (Anaphylaxis) Delivers a single 0.3 mg intramuscular dose of epinephrine from epinephrine injection, USP, 1:1000 (0.3 mL).

EPIPEN® JR 0.15 mg EPINEPHRINE AUTO-INJECTOR Auto-Injector for Intramuscular Injection of Epinephrine For the Emergency Treatment of Allergic Reactions (Anaphylaxis)

Delivers a single 0.15 mg intramuscular dose of epinephrine from epinephrine injection, USP, 1:2000 (0.3 mL).

IMPORTANT INFORMATION

- DO NOT REMOVE GRAY SAFETY RELEASE UNTIL READY FOR USE.
- A SINGLE DOSE OF 0.3 ML OF SOLUTION IS DISPENSED.
  THE MAJORITY OF THE DRUG PRODUCT, 1.7 ML, REMAINS IN THE AUTO-INJECTOR AFTER ACTIVATION AND CANNOT BE USED.
