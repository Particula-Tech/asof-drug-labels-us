## 16.2 STORAGE AND HANDLING

Epinephrine is light sensitive and should be stored in the outer case provided to protect it from light.
Store at 20°to 25°C (68°to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°to 30°C (59°to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Do not refrigerate.
Before using, check to make sure the solution in the auto-injector is clear and colorless.
Replace the auto-injector if the solution is discolored, cloudy, or contains particles.
