# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Epinephrine Injection USP, 1 mg/10 mL (0.1 mg/mL) is a clear colorless solution available in a single-dose Luer-Jet™ Luer-Lock prefilled syringe packaged in a carton.
It is supplied in the following presentation.

| Unit of Sale | Concentration |
| --- | --- |
| NDC 76329-3318-1 (Stock No. 3318) Shrink wrapped bundle of 10 individual cartons (10 mL each) | 1 mg/10 mL (0.1 mg/mL) |

Epinephrine is light sensitive.
Protect from light until ready to use.
Do not refrigerate.
Protect from freezing.
Store at room temperature, between 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
(See USP Controlled Room Temperature.)
Protect from alkalis and oxidizing agents.
