# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Epinephrine Injection is supplied as a clear, colorless solution as follows:

Each carton contains 1 multiple dose vial containing 30 mg/30 mL (1 mg/mL) epinephrine injection, USP in an amber glass vial

NDC 72572-238-01 30 mL Multiple Dose Vial

Epinephrine is light sensitive.
Protect from light until ready to use.

Do not refrigerate.
Protect from freezing.

Vial and contents must be discarded 30 days after initial use.

Store at room temperature, between 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
(See USP Controlled Room Temperature.)
Protect from alkalis and oxidizing agents.

The container closure is not made with natural rubber latex.
