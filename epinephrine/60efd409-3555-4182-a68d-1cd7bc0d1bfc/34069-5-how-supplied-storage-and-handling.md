# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Epinephrine in 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection is a clear and colorless solution filled in a single-dose 250 mL VIAFLO container.

Epinephrine in 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection are supplied as below:

Carton of 20 bags in a 4 mg/250 mL (16 mcg/mL) strength, NDC 0338-0006-20.

Carton of 20 bags in a 16 mg/250 mL (64 mcg/mL) strength, NDC 0338-0024-20.

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Epinephrine is light sensitive.
Protect from light until ready to use.
Store in overpouch until time of use.

Protect from alkalis and oxidizing agents.

VIAFLO container is not made with natural rubber latex, DEHP, or PVC.
