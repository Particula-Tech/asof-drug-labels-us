# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Epinephrine Injection USP, 1 mg/mL is a sterile solution containing 1 mg/1 mL epinephrine in a 2 mL clear glass ampule.
Supplied in a box of 10 single-use ampules (NDC 54288-103-10).

Epinephrine is light sensitive.
Protect from light until ready to use.

Do not refrigerate.
Protect from freezing.

Store at room temperature, between 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
(See USP Controlled Room Temperature.)
Protect from alkalis and oxidizing agents.

Inspect visually for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration.
Do not use the solution if it is colored or cloudy, or if it contains particulate matter.
