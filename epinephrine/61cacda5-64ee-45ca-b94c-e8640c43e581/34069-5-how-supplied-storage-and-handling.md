# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Each carton contains 1 multiple-dose vial containing 30 mg/30 mL (1 mg/mL) epinephrine injection, USP as a clear, colorless, sterile solution in a 30 mL amber glass vial.

NDC 0517-3030-01 30 mL Multiple-Dose Vial

Vial and contents must be discarded 30 days after initial use.

Store between 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Epinephrine is light sensitive.
Protect from light and freezing.

Inspect visually for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration.
Do not use the solution if it is colored or cloudy, or if it contains particulate matter.
