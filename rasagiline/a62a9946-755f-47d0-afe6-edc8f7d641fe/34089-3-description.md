# 11 DESCRIPTION

Rasagiline tablets contain rasagiline (as the mesylate), a propargylamine-based drug indicated for the treatment of idiopathic Parkinson’s disease.
Rasagiline mesylate is designated chemically as: 1H-Inden-1-amine,2,3-dihydro-N-2-propynyl-,(1R)-, methanesulfonate.
The molecular formula of rasagiline mesylate is C12H13N•CH4SO3 and its molecular weight is 267.34.

Rasagiline mesylate is a white to off-white powder, freely soluble in water or ethanol and sparingly soluble in isopropanol.
Each rasagiline tablet for oral administration contains 0.5 mg or 1 mg of rasagiline (equivalent to 0.78 mg or 1.56 mg of rasagiline mesylate).

Each rasagiline tablet also contains the following inactive ingredients: lactose monohydrate, pregelatinized starch, corn starch, talc, stearic acid, and citric acid monohydrate.
