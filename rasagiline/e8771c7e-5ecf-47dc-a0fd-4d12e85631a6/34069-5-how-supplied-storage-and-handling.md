# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Rasagiline is supplied as round, flat, beveled tablets, available in bottles and unit dose blister packages as follows:

Each 0.5 mg tablet is white to off-white, round, flat, beveled tablets, debossed with "C12" on one side and plain on the other side and is available as follows:

Bottle of 30 tablets NDC 33342-308-07

Each 1 mg tablet is white to off-white, round, flat, beveled tablets, debossed with "C13" on one side and plain on the other side and is available as follows:

Bottle of 30 tablets NDC 33342-309-07

Store at 20º to 25ºC (68º to 77ºF); excursions permitted between 15º to 30ºC (59° to 86ºF) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
