# 11 DESCRIPTION

Rasagiline tablets contain rasagiline (as the mesylate), a propargylamine-based drug indicated for the treatment of idiopathic Parkinson’s disease.
Rasagiline mesylate is designated chemically as: 1H-Inden-1-amine, 2, 3-dihydro-N-2-propynyl-, (1R)-, methanesulfonate.
The molecular formula of rasagiline mesylate is C 12H 13N•CH 4SO 3and its molecular weight is 267.34.

Rasagiline mesylate is a white to off-white powder, freely soluble in water or ethanol and sparingly soluble in isopropanol.
Each rasagiline tablet for oral administration contains rasagiline mesylate equivalent to 0.5 mg or 1 mg of rasagiline base.

Each rasagiline tablet also contains the following inactive ingredients: corn starch, colloidal silicon dioxide, mannitol, pregelatinized starch, stearic acid and talc.
