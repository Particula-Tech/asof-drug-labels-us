# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Rasagiline tablets are available as follows:

0.5 mg tablets are white to off-white, round tablets, debossed with “CE”on one side and “159”on the other side.

NDC 62135-676-30 in bottles of 30 tablets

1 mg tablets are white to off-white, round tablets, debossed with “CE”on one side & “160”on the other side.

NDC 62135-677-30 in bottles of 30 tablets

Storage

Store at 20º to 25ºC (68º to 77ºF) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from moisture.
