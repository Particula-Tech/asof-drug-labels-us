# 3. DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Rasagiline 0.5 mg Tablets: White to off-white, circular, flat faced beveled edge, uncoated tablets, debossed with “0.5” on one face and plain on the other side containing, as the active ingredient, rasagiline mesylate equivalent to 0.5 mg of rasagiline base.

Rasagiline 1 mg Tablets: White to off-white, circular, flat faced beveled edge, uncoated tablets, debossed with “1” on one face and plain on the other side containing, as the active ingredient, rasagiline mesylate equivalent to 1 mg of rasagiline base.
