# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Cinacalcet is available as film-coated tablets.

- 30 mg tablets are light-green, film-coated, oval-shaped tablets, debossed with “379” on one side and plain on other side.
- 60 mg tablets are light-green, film-coated, oval-shaped tablets, debossed with “380” on one side and plain on other side.
- 90 mg tablets are light-green, film-coated, oval-shaped tablets, debossed with “600” on one side and plain on other side.
