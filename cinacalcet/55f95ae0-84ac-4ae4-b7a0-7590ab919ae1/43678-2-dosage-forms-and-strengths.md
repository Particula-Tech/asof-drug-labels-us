# 3 DOSAGE FORMS & STRENGTHS

Cinacalcet tablets is available as film-coated tablets.

Cinacalcet tablets are formulated as light green colored, oval shaped, film coated tablets debossed with “A33”, “A34”, “A35” on one side and “30” or “60” or “90” on the other side of the 30 mg, 60 mg, or 90 mg strengths, respectively.
