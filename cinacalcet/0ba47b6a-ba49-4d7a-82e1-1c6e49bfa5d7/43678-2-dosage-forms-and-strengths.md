# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Cinacalcet tablets are available as film-coated tablets.

Cinacalcet tablets are formulated as light-green, film coated, oval-shaped tablets marked with "SCT" on one side and "30" or "60" or "90" on the opposite side of the 30 mg, 60 mg, or 90 mg strengths, respectively.
