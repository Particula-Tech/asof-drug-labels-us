# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Cinacalcet 30 mg tablets are formulated as light-green, film-coated, oval-shaped tablets marked with "SCT" on one side and "30" on the opposite side, packaged in bottles of 30 tablets.
(NDC 64380-883-04)

Cinacalcet 60 mg tablets are formulated as light-green, film-coated, oval-shaped tablets marked with "SCT" on one side and "60" on the opposite side, packaged in bottles of 30 tablets.
(NDC 64380-884-04)

Cinacalcet 90 mg tablets are formulated as light-green, film-coated, oval-shaped tablets marked with "SCT" on one side and "90" on the opposite side, packaged in bottles of 30 tablets.
(NDC 64380-885-04)

Storage

Store at 25oC (77oF); excursions permitted between 15°C to 30oC (59°F to 86oF).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
