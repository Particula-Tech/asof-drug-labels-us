# HOW SUPPLIED

## TENORMIN I.V. Injection

TENORMIN I.V.
Injection, NDC 0310-0108, is supplied as 5 mg atenolol in 10 mL ampules of isotonic citrate-buffered aqueous solution.

## Storage

Protect from light.
Keep ampules in outer packaging until time of use.
Store at controlled room temperature, 20-25°C (68-77°F) [see USP].
