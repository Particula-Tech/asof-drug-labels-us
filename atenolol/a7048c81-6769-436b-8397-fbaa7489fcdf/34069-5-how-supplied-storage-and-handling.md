# HOW SUPPLIED

Atenolol Tablets, USP are available containing 25 mg, 50 mg or 100 mg of atenolol.

The 25 mg tablets are white, round, unscored tablets debossed with M on one side and A2 on the other side.
They are available as follows:

NDC 0378-0218-01 bottles of 100 tablets

NDC 0378-0218-10 bottles of 1000 tablets

The 50 mg tablets are white, round, scored tablets debossed with M on one side and 231 above the score on the other side.
They are available as follows:

NDC 0378-0231-01 bottles of 100 tablets

NDC 0378-0231-10 bottles of 1000 tablets

The 100 mg tablets are white, round, unscored tablets debossed with M on one side and 757 on the other side.
They are available as follows:

NDC 0378-0757-01 bottles of 100 tablets

NDC 0378-0757-10 bottles of 1000 tablets

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[See USP for Controlled Room Temperature.]

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP using a child-resistant closure.
