# HOW SUPPLIED

Atenolol tablets USP, 25 mg (white to off-white marble finish, round, flat beveled edges tablets, debossed with "93" on one side and "787" on the other side) are supplied in bottles of 100 and 1000.

Atenolol tablets USP, 50 mg (round, flat, white tablets identified with score on one side, debossed with the numbers "93" above and "752" below the score) are supplied in bottles of 100 and 1000.

Atenolol tablets USP, 100 mg (round, flat, white tablets, unscored, debossed with the numbers "93" and "753" on one side) are supplied in bottles of 100 and 500.

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP, with a child-resistant closure (as required).
