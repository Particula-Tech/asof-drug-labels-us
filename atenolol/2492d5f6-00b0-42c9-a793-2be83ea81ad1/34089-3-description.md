# DESCRIPTION

Atenolol, a synthetic, beta1-selective (cardioselective) adrenoreceptor-blocking agent, may be chemically described as benzeneacetamide, 4 -[2'-hydroxy-3'-[(1- methylethyl) amino] propoxy]-.
The molecular and structural formulas are:

Atenolol, USP has a molecular weight of 266.
It is a white or almost white powder, sparingly soluble in water; soluble in absolute alcohol and practically insoluble in ether.

Atenolol tablets, for oral administration, are available as 25 mg, 50 mg and 100 mg tablets.

Inactive Ingredients: Citric acid (anhydrous), colloidal silicon dioxide, croscarmellose sodium, magnesium stearate, microcrystalline cellulose, povidone.
