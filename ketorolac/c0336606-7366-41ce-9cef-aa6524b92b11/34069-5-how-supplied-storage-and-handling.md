# HOW SUPPLIED

TORADOLORAL 10 mg tablets are round, white, film-coated, red printed tablets.
There is a large T printed on both sides of the tablet, with TORADOL on one side, and ROCHE on the other, available in bottles of 100 tablets (NDC 0004-0273-01).

## Storage

Store bottles at 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F).
