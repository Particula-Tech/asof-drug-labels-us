# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Preservative-free SPRIX Nasal Spray is supplied in boxes containing 5 single-day nasal spray bottles (NDC 0517-8880-05) or 1 single-day nasal spray bottle (NDC 0517-8880-01).
Each single-day nasal spray bottle contains a sufficient quantity of solution to deliver 8 sprays for a total of 126 mg of ketorolac tromethamine.
Each spray delivers 15.75 mg of ketorolac tromethamine.
The delivery system is designed to administer precisely metered doses of 100 μL per spray.

## 16.2 Storage

Protect from light and freezing.
Store unopened SPRIX between 36°F and 46°F (2°C and 8°C).
During use, keep containers of SPRIX Nasal Spray at controlled room temperature, between 59°F and 86°F (15°C and 30°C), out of direct sunlight.
Bottles of SPRIX should be discarded within 24 hours of priming.
