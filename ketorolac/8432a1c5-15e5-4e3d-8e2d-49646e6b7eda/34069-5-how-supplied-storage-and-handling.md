# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Ketorolac tromethamine ophthalmic solution 0.4% is supplied sterile, in white opaque plastic LDPE bottles with white droppers, with a gray high impact polystyrene (HIPS) caps as follows:

5 mL in 10 mL bottle NDC 60758-773-05

Storage: Store at 15oC to 25oC (59oF to 77oF).
Protect from light.
After opening, ketorolac tromethamine ophthalmic solution can be used until the expiration date on the bottle.
