# 8USE IN SPECIFIC POPULATIONS

## 8.1Pregnancy

Risk Summary

There are no adequate or well-controlled studies with ketorolac tromethamine ophthalmic solution in pregnant women.
No evidence of teratogenicity has been observed in rats or rabbits with ketorolac tromethamine ophthalmic solution at clinically relevant doses.

## 8.2Lactation

Risk Summary

It is not known whether ketorolac when given topically is present in human milk.
Because many drugs are excreted in human milk, caution should be exercised when ketorolac tromethamine ophthalmic solution is administered to a nursing woman.

## 8.4 Pediatric Use

Safety and effectiveness of ketorolac tromethamine in pediatric patients below the age of 3 have not been established.

## 8.5 Geriatric Use

No overall differences in safety or effectiveness have been observed between elderly and other adult patients.
