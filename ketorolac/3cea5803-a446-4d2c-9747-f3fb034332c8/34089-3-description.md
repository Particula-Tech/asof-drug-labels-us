# 11 DESCRIPTION

ACUVAIL® (ketorolac tromethamine ophthalmic solution) 0.45% is a member of the pyrrolo-pyrrole group of nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) for ophthalmic use.
Its chemical name is (±)-5-Benzoyl-2,3-dihydro-1H-pyrrolizine-1-carboxylic acid, compound with 2-amino-2-(hydroxymethyl)-1,3-propanediol (1:1), and its molecular weight is 376.40.
Its molecular formula is C19H24N2O6.
Its chemical structure is:

ACUVAIL® solution is supplied as a sterile isotonic aqueous 0.45% preservative-free solution, with a pH of approximately 6.8.
ACUVAIL® solution is a racemic mixture of R-(+) and S-(-)- ketorolac tromethamine.
Ketorolac tromethamine may exist in three crystal forms.
All forms are equally soluble in water.
The pKa of ketorolac is 3.5.
This white to off-white crystalline substance discolors on prolonged exposure to light.
The osmolality of ACUVAIL® solution is approximately 285 mOsml/kg.

Contains: Active: ketorolac tromethamine 0.45%.
Inactives: Carboxymethylcellulose; sodium chloride; sodium citrate dihydrate; and purified water with sodium hydroxide and/or hydrochloric acid to adjust pH.
