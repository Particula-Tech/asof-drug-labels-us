# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

PREZISTA (darunavir) 75 mg tablets are supplied as white, caplet-shaped, film-coated tablets containing darunavir ethanolate equivalent to 75 mg of darunavir per tablet.
Each tablet is debossed with "75" on one side and "TMC" on the other side.

PREZISTA (darunavir) 150 mg tablets are supplied as white, oval-shaped, film-coated tablets containing darunavir ethanolate equivalent to 150 mg of darunavir per tablet.
Each tablet is debossed with "150" on one side and "TMC" on the other side.

PREZISTA (darunavir) 300 mg tablets are supplied as orange, oval-shaped, film-coated tablets containing darunavir ethanolate equivalent to 300 mg of darunavir per tablet.
Each tablet is debossed with "300" on one side and "TMC114" on the other side.

PREZISTA (darunavir) 400 mg tablets are supplied as light orange, oval-shaped, film-coated tablets containing darunavir ethanolate equivalent to 400 mg of darunavir per tablet.
Each tablet is debossed with "400" on one side and "TMC" on the other side.

PREZISTA (darunavir) 600 mg tablets are supplied as orange, oval-shaped, film-coated tablets containing darunavir ethanolate equivalent to 600 mg of darunavir per tablet.
Each tablet is debossed with "600" on one side and "TMC" on the other side.

PREZISTA tablets are packaged in bottles in the following configuration: 75 mg tablets—bottles of 480 (NDC 59676-563-01) 150 mg tablets—bottles of 240 (NDC 59676-564-01) 300 mg tablets—bottles of 120 (NDC 59676-560-01) 400 mg tablets—bottles of 60 (NDC 59676-561-01) 600 mg tablets—bottles of 60 (NDC 59676-562-01)

Storage:

Store PREZISTA tablets at 25°C (77°F); with excursions permitted to 15°-30°C (59°-86°F).
