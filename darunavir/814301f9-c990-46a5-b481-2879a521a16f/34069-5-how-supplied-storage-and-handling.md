# HOW SUPPLIED

PREZISTA (darunavir) tablets are supplied as orange, oval-shaped, film-coated tablets containing darunavir ethanolate equivalent to 300 mg of darunavir per tablet.
Each tablet is debossed with "300" on one side and "TMC114" on the other side.
PREZISTA tablets are packaged in bottles in the following configuration:

300 mg tablets—bottles of 120 (NDC 59676-560-01)

## Storage

Store PREZISTA tablets at 25°C (77°F); with excursions permitted to 15°-30°C (59°-86°F).
