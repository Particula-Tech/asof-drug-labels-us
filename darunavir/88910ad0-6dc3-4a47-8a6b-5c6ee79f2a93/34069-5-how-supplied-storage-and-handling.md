# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Darunavir Tablets, 600 mg are orange colored, oval shaped, biconvex, film-coated tablets debossed with ‘D’ on one side and ‘600’ on another side.
Bottles of 60 NDC 59651-085-60 Darunavir Tablets, 800 mg are dark red colored, oval shaped, biconvex, film-coated tablets debossed with ‘D’ on one side and ‘800’ on another side.
Bottles of 30 NDC 59651-086-30 Storage: Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Keep darunavir tablets out of reach of children.
