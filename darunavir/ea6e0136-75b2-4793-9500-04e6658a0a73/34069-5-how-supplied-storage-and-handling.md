# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Darunavir 600 mg tablets are supplied as Beige colored, oval-shaped, film-coated tablets debossed with “M” on one side and “600” on the other side.
Darunavir 800 mg tablets are supplied as brown colored, oval-shaped, film-coated tablets debossed with “M” on one side and “800” on the other side.
Darunavir tablets are packaged in bottles in the following configuration:

- 600 mg tablets — bottles of 60 (NDC 72205-184-60)
- 800 mg tablets — bottles of 30 (NDC 72205-185-30)

Darunavir Tablets

- Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Keep darunavir tablets out of reach of children.
