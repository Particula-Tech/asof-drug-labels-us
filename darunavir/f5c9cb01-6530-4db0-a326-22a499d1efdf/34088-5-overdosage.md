# 10 OVERDOSAGE

Human experience of acute overdose with darunavir/ritonavir is limited.
No specific antidote is available for overdose with darunavir.
Treatment of overdose with darunavir consists of general supportive measures including monitoring of vital signs and observation of the clinical status of the patient.
Since darunavir is highly protein bound, dialysis is unlikely to be beneficial in significant removal of the active substance.
