# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Darunavir 600 mg tablets are supplied as white colored, oval shaped, film-coated tablets containing 600 mg of darunavir per tablet.
Each tablet is debossed with ‘DA600’ on one side and plain on the other side.

Bottle of 60 tablets NDC 43598-704-60

Bottle of 500 tablets NDC 43598-704-05

Darunavir 800 mg tablets are supplied as blue colored, oval shaped, film-coated tablets containing 800 mg of darunavir per tablet.
Each tablet is debossed with ‘DA800’ on one side and plain on the other side.

Bottle of 30 tablets NDC 43598-705-30

Bottle of 500 tablets NDC 43598-705-05

Storage

• Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Keep darunavir tablets out of reach of children.
