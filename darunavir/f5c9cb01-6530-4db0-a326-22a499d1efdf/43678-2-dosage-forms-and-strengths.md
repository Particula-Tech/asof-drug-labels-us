# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

• 600 mg: White colored, oval shaped, film-coated tablets containing 600 mg of darunavir.
Each tablet is debossed with ‘DA600’ on one side and plain on the other side.

• 800 mg: Blue colored, oval shaped, film-coated tablets containing 800 mg of darunavir.
Each tablet is debossed with ‘DA800’ on one side and plain on the other side.
