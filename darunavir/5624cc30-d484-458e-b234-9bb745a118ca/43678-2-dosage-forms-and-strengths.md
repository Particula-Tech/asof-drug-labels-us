# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Darunavir Tablets

- 600 mg: beige, capsule-shaped, biconvex, film coated tablets containing darunavir hydrate equivalent to 600 mg of darunavir.
  Each tablet is debossed with "C248" on one side and "plain" on the other side.
- 800 mg: white to off-white, capsule-shaped, biconvex, film coated tablets containing darunavir hydrate equivalent to 800 mg of darunavir.
  Each tablet is debossed with "C244" on one side and "plain" on the other side.
