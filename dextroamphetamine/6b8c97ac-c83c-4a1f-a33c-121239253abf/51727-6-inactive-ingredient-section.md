# INACTIVE INGREDIENTS

Each tablet, for oral administration, contains dextroamphetamine sulfate, USP in either 5 mg, 10 mg, 15 mg, 20 mg or 30 mg.
Each tablet also contains the following inactive ingredients: colloidal silicon dioxide, crospovidone, microcrystalline cellulose and stearic acid.

The 5 mg tablets also contain D&C Red #27 and FD&C Yellow #6.
The 10 mg tablets also contain FD&C Red #40, FD&C Yellow #6 and FD&C Blue #2.
The 15 mg tablets also contain FD&C Blue #1, FD&C Blue #2, and FD&C Red #40.
The 20 mg tablets also contain FD&C Blue #1 and D&C Red #27.
The 30 mg tablets also contain D&C Yellow #10.
