# DESCRIPTION

Dextroamphetamine sulfate is the dextro isomer of the compound d,l-amphetamine sulfate, a sympathomimetic amine of the amphetamine group.
Chemically, dextroamphetamine is d-alpha-methylphenethylamine, and is present in all forms of Dextroamphetamine Sulfate Tablets, USP as the neutral sulfate.
It has the chemical formula of (C9H13N)2 • H2SO4 and a molecular weight of 368.49.

Structural Formula:

Each tablet, for oral administration, contains dextroamphetamine sulfate USP, 5 mg or 10 mg.
Each tablet also contains the following inactive ingredients: Microcrystalline Cellulose, Povidone, Silicon Dioxide, and Stearic Acid.
