# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

VEMLIDY tablets containing 25 mg of tenofovir alafenamide are yellow, round, film-coated, debossed with "GSI" on one side and "25" on the other side.
Each bottle contains 30 tablets (NDC 61958-2301-1), a silica gel desiccant, polyester coil, and is closed with a child-resistant closure.

Store below 30 °C (86 °F).

- Keep container tightly closed.
- Dispense only in original container.
