# HOW SUPPLIED

Milrinone Lactate Injection is supplied as 10 mL, 20 mL and 50 mL single-dose vials containing a sterile, clear, colorless to pale yellow solution.
Each mL contains milrinone lactate equivalent to 1 mg milrinone.

Discard unused portion after initial use.
Store between 20 to 25°C (68 to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Avoid freezing.

Exposure of pharmaceutical products to heat should be minimized.
Avoid excessive heat.
