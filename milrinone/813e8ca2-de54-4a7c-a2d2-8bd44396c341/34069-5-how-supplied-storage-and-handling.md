# HOW SUPPLIED

Milrinone Lactate Injection is supplied as 10 mL, 20 mL and 50 mL single-dose vials containing a sterile, clear, colorless to pale yellow solution.
Each mL contains milrinone lactate equivalent to 1 mg milrinone.

| Unit of Sale | Concentration |
| --- | --- |
| NDC 0409-0212-01 10 single-dose vials in a carton | 10 mg/10 mL (1 mg/mL) |
| NDC 0409-0212-02 10 single-dose vials in a carton | 20 mg/20 mL (1 mg/mL) |
| NDC 0409-0212-03 1 single-dose vial in a carton | 50 mg/50 mL (1 mg/mL) |

Discard unused portion after initial use.
Store between 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Avoid freezing.

Exposure of pharmaceutical products to heat should be minimized.
Avoid excessive heat.
Protect from freezing.
