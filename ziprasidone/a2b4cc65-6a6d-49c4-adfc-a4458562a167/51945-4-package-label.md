# PRINCIPAL DISPLAY PANEL – 20 mg/mL

NDC 58151-137-81 Contains 10 of NDC 58151-137-32

10 Single Dose Vials

GEODON® for Injection (ziprasidone mesylate)

equivalent to 20 mg/mL* of ziprasidone

PROTECT FROM LIGHT.

Sterile

For Intramuscular Use Only

Rx only

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature] in dry form.

PROTECT FROM LIGHT.

DOSAGE AND USE See accompanying prescribing information.
Reconstitute to 20 mg/mL with 1.2 mL of Sterile Water for Injection (only).
Shake vigorously until all the drug is dissolved to afford a colorless to pale pink solution.

*Each mL (when reconstituted) contains 20 mg of ziprasidone and 4.7 mg of methanesulfonic acid solubilized by 294 mg of sulfobutylether β-cyclodextrin sodium (SBECD).

Following reconstitution, GEODON for Injection can be stored, when protected from light, for up to 24 hours at 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) or up to 7 days refrigerated, 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F).

For Intramuscular Use Only
