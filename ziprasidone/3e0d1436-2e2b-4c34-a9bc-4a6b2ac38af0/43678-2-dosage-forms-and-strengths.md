# 3 DOSAGE FORMS & STRENGTHS

Ziprasidone hydrochloride capsules are differentiated by capsule color/size and are imprinted in black ink with strength on the body & a unique number on the cap.
Ziprasidone hydrochloride capsules are supplied for oral administration in 20 mg (blue/white), 40 mg (blue/blue), 60 mg (white/white), and 80 mg (blue/white) capsules.
They are supplied in the following strengths and package configurations:
