# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Ziprasidone capsules, USP 20 mg are size #4 hard gelatin capsules, white opaque cap with white opaque body, imprinted with " CE" on the cap and " 91" on the body with black ink, filled with white to pink granular powder.

Ziprasidone capsules, USP 40 mg are size #3 hard gelatin capsules, blue opaque cap with white opaque body, imprinted with " CE" on the cap and " 92" on the body with black ink, filled with white to pink granular powder.

Ziprasidone capsules, USP 60 mg are size #2 hard gelatin capsules, pink opaque cap with white opaque body, imprinted with " CE" on the cap and " 93" on the body with black ink, filled with white to pink granular powder.

Ziprasidone capsules, USP 80 mg are size #1 hard gelatin capsules, blue opaque cap with blue opaque body, imprinted with " CE" on the cap and " 94" on the body with black ink, filled with white to pink granular powder.
