# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

LIMITED POPULATION: DEFENCATH® is indicated to reduce the incidence of catheter-related bloodstream infections (CRBSI) in adult patients with kidney failure receiving chronic hemodialysis (HD) through a central venous catheter (CVC).
This drug is indicated for use in a limited and specific population of patients [see Clinical Studies (14)].

Limitations of Use

The safety and effectiveness of DEFENCATH have not been established for use in populations other than adult patients with kidney failure receiving chronic HD through a CVC.
