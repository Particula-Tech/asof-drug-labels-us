# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Heparin sodium injection, USP (porcine), contains parabens and is available as follows:

| Strength | NDC | Fill Volume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 USP Heparin units/mL | 87188-101-90 | 1 mL fill in a 2 mL vial, Pack of 25 vials |
| 10,000 USP Heparin units/10 mL (1,000 USP Heparin units/mL) | 87188-101-80 | 10 mL fill in a 10 mL vial, Pack of 25 vials |
| 30,000 USP Heparin units/30 mL (1,000 USP Heparin units/mL) | 87188-101-70 | 30 mL fill in a 30 mL vial Pack of 25 vials |
| 5,000 USP Heparin units/mL | 87188-102-90 | 1 mL fill in a 2 mL vial, Pack of 25 vials |
| 10,000 USP Heparin units/mL | 87188-103-90 | 1 mL fill in a 2 mL vial, Pack of 25 vials |

The above products are available in multiple dose, flip-top vials.

Use only if solution is clear and seal intact.
Do not use if solution is discolored or contains a precipitate.

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Avoid excessive heat.
Do not freeze.
