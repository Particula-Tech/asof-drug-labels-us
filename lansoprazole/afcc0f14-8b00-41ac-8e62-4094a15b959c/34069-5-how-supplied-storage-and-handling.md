# 16. HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Lansoprazole Delayed-release Capsules, USP, 15 mg, are hard gelatin capsule with "" imprinted in grey ink on green colored cap and "806" imprinted in black ink on opaque white colored body.
The 30 mg capsules are hard gelatin capsule with "" imprinted in white ink on black colored cap and "807" imprinted in black ink on opaque white colored body.

They are available as follows:

NDC 70756-806- 30 Bottles of 30: 15-mg capsules

NDC 70756-807- 30 Bottles of 30: 30-mg capsules

NDC 70756-807- 90 Bottles of 90: 30-mg capsules

NDC 70756-807- 51 Bottles of 500: 30-mg capsules

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15-30°C (59-86°F).
