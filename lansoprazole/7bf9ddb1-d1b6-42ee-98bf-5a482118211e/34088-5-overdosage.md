# OVERDOSAGE

Single intravenous doses of PREVACID at 218 mg/kg in mice (approximately 30 times the recommended human dose based on BSA) and 167 mg/kg in rats (approximately 46 times the recommended human dose based on BSA) were lethal.
The symptoms of acute toxicity were decreased locomotor response, ataxia, ptosis and tonic convulsions.

PREVACID is not removed from the circulation by hemodialysis.
