# OVERDOSAGE

Single intravenous doses of lansoprazole at 218 mg/kg in mice (approximately 30 times the recommended human dose based on body surface area) and 167 mg/kg in rats (approximately 46 times the recommended human dose based on body surface area) were lethal.
The symptoms of acute toxicity were decreased locomotor response, ataxia, ptosis and tonic convulsions.

Lansoprazole is not removed from the circulation by hemodialysis.
