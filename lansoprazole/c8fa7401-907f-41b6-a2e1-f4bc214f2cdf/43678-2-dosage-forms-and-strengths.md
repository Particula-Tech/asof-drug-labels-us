# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

- 15 mg capsules have a green opaque cap, green opaque body, hard-shell gelatin capsule filled with white to off-white pellets.
  The capsule is axially printed with MYLAN over 8015 in black ink on both the cap and body.
- 30 mg capsules have a pink opaque cap, pink opaque body, hard-shell gelatin capsule filled with white to off-white pellets.
  The capsule is axially printed with MYLAN over 8030 in black ink on both the cap and the body.
