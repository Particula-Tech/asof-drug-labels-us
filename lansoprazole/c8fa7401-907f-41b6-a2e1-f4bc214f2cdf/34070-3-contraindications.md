# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Lansoprazole delayed-release capsules are contraindicated in patients with known severe hypersensitivity to any component of the formulation.
Hypersensitivity reactions may include anaphylaxis, anaphylactic shock, angioedema, bronchospasm, acute interstitial nephritis, and urticaria [see Adverse Reactions (6)].

For information about contraindications of antibacterial agents (clarithromycin and amoxicillin) indicated in combination with lansoprazole delayed-release capsules, refer to the CONTRAINDICATIONS section of their prescribing information.
