# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Lansoprazole Delayed-Release Capsules, USP are available containing 15 mg or 30 mg of lansoprazole, USP.

The 15 mg capsules are hard gelatin capsules with a green opaque cap and green opaque body filled with white to off-white pellets.
The capsules are axially printed with MYLAN over 8015 in black ink on both the cap and the body.
They are available as follows:

NDC 0378-8015-93 bottles of 30 capsules

The 30 mg capsules are hard gelatin capsules with a pink opaque cap and pink opaque body filled with white to off-white pellets.
The capsules are axially printed with MYLAN over 8030 in black ink on both the cap and the body.
They are available as follows:

NDC 0378-8030-93 bottles of 30 capsules

NDC 0378-8030-77 bottles of 90 capsules

NDC 0378-8030-05 bottles of 500 capsules

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP using a child-resistant closure.

PHARMACIST: Dispense a Medication Guide with each prescription.
