# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Lansoprazole Delayed-Release Capsules, 15 mg, are blue/pink hard gelatin capsules of size ‘3’ with ‘LPZ 15’ imprint on body and ‘ap’ logo imprint on cap with black color ink and containing reddish brown color, nearly spherical granules.
The 30 mg capsules are green/pink hard gelatin capsule of size ‘1’ with ‘LPZ 30’ imprint on body and ‘ap’ logo imprint on cap with black color ink and containing reddish brown color, nearly spherical granules.
They are available as follows:

NDC 27241-019-03 Bottles of 30: 15 mg capsules

NDC 27241-019-10 Bottles of 100: 15 mg capsules

NDC 27241-020-03 Bottles of 30: 30 mg capsules

NDC 27241-020-09 Bottles of 90: 30 mg capsules

NDC 27241-020-10 Bottles of 100: 30 mg capsules

NDC 27241-020-50 Bottles of 500: 30 mg capsules

NDC 27241-020-90 Bottles of 1000: 30 mg capsules

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F).
