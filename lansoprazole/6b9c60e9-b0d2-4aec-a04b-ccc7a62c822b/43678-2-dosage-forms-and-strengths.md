# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Lansoprazole delayed-release capsules, USP:

- 15 mg capsules are blue/pink hard gelatin capsules of size ‘3’ with ‘LPZ 15’ imprint on body and ‘ap’ logo imprint on cap with black color ink and containing reddish brown color, nearly spherical granules.
- 30 mg capsules are green/pink hard gelatin capsule of size ‘1’ with ‘LPZ 30’ imprint on body and ‘ap’ logo imprint on cap with black color ink and containing reddish brown color, nearly spherical granules.
