# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

- 15 mg tablets are white to off-white, flat, beveled round tablet with off-white to grayish speckles, debossed with "15" on one side of the tablet and plain on the other side.
- 30 mg tablets are white to off-white, flat, beveled round tablet with off-white to grayish speckles, debossed with "30" on one side of the tablet and plain on the other side.
