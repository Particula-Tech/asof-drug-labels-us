# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

HYLENEX recombinant is contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity to hyaluronidase or any of the excipients in HYLENEX recombinant.
A preliminary skin test for hypersensitivity to HYLENEX recombinant can be performed.
The skin test is made by an intradermal injection of approximately 0.02 mL (3 Units) of a 150 Unit/mL solution.
A positive reaction consists of a wheal with pseudopods appearing within 5 minutes and persisting for 20 to 30 minutes and accompanied by localized itching.
Transient vasodilation at the site of the test, i.e., erythema, is not a positive reaction.
Discontinue HYLENEX recombinant if sensitization occurs.
