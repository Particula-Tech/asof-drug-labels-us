# 13 Nonclinical Toxicology

## 13.1 Carcinogenesis, Mutagenesis, Impairment of Fertility

Long-term animal studies have not been performed to assess the carcinogenic or mutagenic potential of hyaluronidase.
When hyaluronidase was subcutaneously administered to cynomolgus monkeys in a 39-week general toxicity study, no toxicity to the male or female reproductive systems was reported for measured parameters (i.e., semen analyses, hormone levels, menstrual cycles, gross pathology, histopathology, organ weight data), at dose levels up to 2 mg/kg (approximately 220,000 USP units/kg).
