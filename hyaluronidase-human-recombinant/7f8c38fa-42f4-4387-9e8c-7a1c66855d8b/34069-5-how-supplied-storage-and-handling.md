# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

OPDIVO QVANTIG™ (nivolumab and hyaluronidase-nvhy) injection is a sterile, preservative-free, clear to opalescent and colorless to yellow solution for subcutaneous use.
It is supplied as an individually packaged single-dose vial as follows:

| Carton Contents | NDC |
| --- | --- |
| 300 mg nivolumab and 5,000 units hyaluronidase per 2.5 mL (120 mg/2,000 units per mL) single-dose vial | 0003-3120-01 |
| 600 mg nivolumab and 10,000 units hyaluronidase per 5 mL (120 mg/2,000 units per mL) single-dose vial | 0003-6120-01 |

Store OPDIVO QVANTIG vials in a refrigerator at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) in the original carton to protect from light.

Do not freeze or shake.
