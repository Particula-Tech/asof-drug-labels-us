# 10 OVERDOSAGE

Overdosage of pegfilgrastim products may result in leukocytosis and bone pain.
Events of edema, dyspnea, and pleural effusion have been reported in a single patient who administered pegfilgrastim on 8 consecutive days in error.
In the event of overdose, the patient should be monitored for adverse reactions [see Adverse Reactions (6)].
