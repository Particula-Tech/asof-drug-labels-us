# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Neulasta is supplied in a prefilled single use syringe containing 6 mg pegfilgrastim, supplied with a 27-gauge, 1/2-inch needle with an UltraSafe® Needle Guard.

The needle cover of the prefilled syringe contains dry natural rubber (a derivative of latex).

Neulasta is provided in a dispensing pack containing one syringe (NDC 55513-190-01).

Store refrigerated between 2° to 8°C (36° to 46°F) in the carton to protect from light.
Do not shake.
Discard syringes stored at room temperature for more than 48 hours.
Avoid freezing; if frozen, thaw in the refrigerator before administration.
Discard syringe if frozen more than once.
