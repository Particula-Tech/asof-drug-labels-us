# HOW SUPPLIED

Misoprostol Tablets 100-mcg tablets are round, white flat-faced beveled edge tablets, debossed “160” on one side and “n” on other side; supplied as:

Bottles of 60: 43386-160-06

Bottles of 120: 43386-160-12

Misoprostol Tablets 200-mcg tablets are round, white flat-faced beveled edge bisected tablets, debossed “161” above the bisect and “n” below the bisect and plain on the other side; supplied as:

Bottles of 60: 43386-161-06

Bottles of 100: 43386-161-01

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Store in a dry area.
