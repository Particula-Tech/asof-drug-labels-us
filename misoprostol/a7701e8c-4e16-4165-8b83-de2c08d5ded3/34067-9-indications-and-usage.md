# INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Carefully consider the potential benefits and risks of diclofenac sodium/misoprostol tablets and other treatment options before deciding to use diclofenac sodium/misoprostol tablets.
Use the lowest effective dose for the shortest duration consistent with individual patient treatment goals (see WARNINGS).

Diclofenac sodium/misoprostol tablets are indicated for treatment of the signs and symptoms of osteoarthritis or rheumatoid arthritis in patients at high risk of developing NSAID-induced gastric and duodenal ulcers and their complications.
See WARNINGS, Gastrointestinal Effects - Risk of Ulceration, Bleeding, and Perforation for a list of factors that may increase the risk of NSAID-induced gastric and duodenal ulcers and their complications.
