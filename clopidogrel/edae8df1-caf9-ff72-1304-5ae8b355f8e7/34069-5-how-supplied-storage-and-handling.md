# HOW SUPPLIED

Clopidogrel Tablets USP, 75 mg are reddish-brown, round, unscored, film coated tablets, imprinted "APO" on one side and "CL" over "75" on the other side.
They are supplied as follows:

Bottles of 30 NDC 60505-0253-1

Bottles of 90 NDC 60505-0253-2

Bottles of 1000 NDC 60505-0253-3

100 Unit Dose NDC 60505-0253-4

## Storage

Store at 25° C (77° F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30° C (59° to 86° F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
