# DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

## Recent MI, Recent Stroke or Established Peripheral Arterial Disease

The recommended daily dose of clopidogrel tablets is 75 mg once daily.

## Acute Coronary Syndrome

For patients with acute coronary syndrome (unstable angina/non-Q-wave MI), clopidogrel bisulfate should be initiated with a single 300 mg loading dose and then continued at 75 mg once daily.
Aspirin (75 mg-325 mg once daily) should be initiated and continued in combination with clopidogrel bisulfate.
In CURE, most patients with Acute Coronary Syndrome also received heparin acutely (see CLINICAL STUDIES).

Clopidogrel tablets can be administered with or without food.

No dosage adjustment is necessary for elderly patients or patients with renal disease (See Clinical Pharmacology: Special Populations.)
