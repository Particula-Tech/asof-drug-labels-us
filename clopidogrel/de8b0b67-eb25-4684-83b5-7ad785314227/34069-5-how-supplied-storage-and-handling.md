# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Plavix (clopidogrel tablets) 75 mg are available as pink, round, biconvex, film-coated tablets debossed with "75" on one side and "1171" on the other.
Tablets are provided as follows:

| NDC 0024-1171-90 | Bottles of 90 |
| --- | --- |

Plavix (clopidogrel tablets) 300 mg are available as pink, oblong, film-coated tablets debossed with "300" on one side and "1332" on the other.
Tablets are provided as follows:

| NDC 0024-1332-30 | Unit-dose packages of 30 |
| --- | --- |

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C–30°C (59°F–86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
