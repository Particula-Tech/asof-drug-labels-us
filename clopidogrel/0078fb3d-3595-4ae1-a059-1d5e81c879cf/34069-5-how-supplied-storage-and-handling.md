# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Clopidogrel Tablets USP, 75 mg are pink colored, round, biconvex, beveled edge, film-coated tablets debossed with ‘E’ on one side and ‘34’ on the other side.
Bottles of 30 NDC 65862-357-30 Bottles of 90 NDC 65862-357-90 Bottles of 100 NDC 65862-357-01 Bottles of 500 NDC 65862-357-05 Bottles of 1000 NDC 65862-357-99 10 x 10 Unit-dose Tablets NDC 65862-357-10 Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from moisture.
Preserve in well-closed containers.
