# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Plavix (clopidogrel bisulfate) 75 mg tablets are available as pink, round, biconvex, film-coated tablets debossed with "75" on one side and "1171" on the other.
Tablets are provided as follows:

- NDC 63653-1171-6 Bottles of 30
- NDC 63653-1171-1 Bottles of 90
- NDC 63653-1171-5 Bottles of 500
- NDC 63653-1171-3 Blisters of 100

Plavix (clopidogrel bisulfate) 300 mg tablets are available as pink, oblong, film-coated tablets debossed with "300" on one side and "1332" on the other.
Tablets are provided as follows:

- NDC 63653-1332-2 Unit-dose packages of 30
- NDC 63653-1332-3 Unit-dose packages of 100

Store at 25° C (77° F); excursions permitted to 15°–30° C (59°–86° F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
