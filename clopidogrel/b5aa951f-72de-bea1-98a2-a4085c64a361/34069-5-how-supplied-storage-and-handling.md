# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Clopidogrel tablets USP, 75 mg are white, film coated, round, biconvex tablets debossed with “R” on one side and “196” on other side and are supplied in bottles of 30, 90, 100 and 500.

Bottles of 30 NDC 55111-196-30

Bottles of 90 NDC 55111-196-90

Bottles of 100 NDC 55111-196-01

Bottles of 500 NDC 55111-196-05

Preserve in well-closed containers.
Store at 20°-25°C (68°-77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature]
