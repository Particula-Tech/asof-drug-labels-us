# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Exjade is contraindicated in patients with:

- Serum creatinine greater than 2 times the age-appropriate ULN or CLCr less than 40 mL/min [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)];
- Poor performance status;
- High-risk myelodysplastic syndromes;
- Advanced malignancies;
- Platelet counts less than 50 x 109/L;
- Known hypersensitivity to deferasirox or any component of Exjade [see Warnings and Precautions (5.6), Adverse Reactions (6.2)].
