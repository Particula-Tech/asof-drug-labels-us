# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

JADENU is contraindicated in patients with:

- Serum creatinine greater than 2 times the age-appropriate ULN or ClCr less than 40 mL/min [see Warning and Precautions (5.1)];
- Poor performance status;
- High-risk myelodysplastic syndromes;
- Advanced malignancies;
- Platelet counts <50 x 109/L;
- Known hypersensitivity to deferasirox or any component of JADENU [see Warnings and Precautions (5.6), Adverse Reactions (6.2)].
