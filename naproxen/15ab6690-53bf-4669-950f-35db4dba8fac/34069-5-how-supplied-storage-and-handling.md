# HOW SUPPLIED

NAPRELAN® (naproxen sodium) Controlled-Release Tablets are available as follows:

NAPRELAN® 375: white, capsule-shaped tablet with “N” on one side and “375” on the reverse; in bottles of 100; NDC 59630-375-10.
Each tablet contains 412.5 mg naproxen sodium equivalent to 375 mg naproxen.

NAPRELAN® 500: white, capsule-shaped tablet with “N” on one side and “500” on the reverse; in bottles of 75; NDC 59630-850-75.
Each tablet contains 550 mg naproxen sodium equivalent to 500 mg naproxen.

NAPRELAN® 750: white-capsule-shaped tablet with “N” on one side and “750” on the reverse; in bottles of 30; 59630-777-03.
Each tablet contains 825 mg naproxen sodium equivalent to 750 mg naproxen.

Rx only US Patent 5,637,320

Store at controlled room temperature, 20° to 25° C (68° to 77° F).

PHARMACIST: Dispense in a well-closed container.
