# CARCINOGENESIS, MUTAGENESIS, IMPAIRMENT OF FERTILITY

A two year study was performed in rats to evaluate the carcinogenic potential of naproxen at doses of 8 mg/kg/day, 16 mg/kg/day, and 24 mg/kg/day (50 mg/m2, 100 mg/m2, and 150 mg/m2).
The maximum dose used was 0.28 times the systemic exposure to humans at the recommended dose.
No evidence of tumorigenicity was found.
