# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Naproxen sodium 375 mg, 500 mg, and 750 mg are controlled-release tablets supplied as:

375 mg: white, capsule-shaped tablet with “N” on one side and “375” on the reverse; in bottles of 100; NDC 47781-153-01.
Each tablet contains 412.5 mg naproxen sodium equivalent to 375 mg naproxen.

500 mg: white, capsule-shaped tablet with “N” on one side and “500” on the reverse; in bottles of 75; NDC 47781-154-75.
Each tablet contains 550 mg naproxen sodium equivalent to 500 mg naproxen.

750 mg: white, capsule-shaped tablet with “N” on one side and “750” on the reverse; in bottles of 30; NDC 47781-155-30.
Each tablet contains 825 mg naproxen sodium equivalent to 750 mg naproxen.

Storage Store at room temperature, 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F), excursions permitted 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
PHARMACIST: Dispense in a well-closed container.
