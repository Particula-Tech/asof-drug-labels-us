# 1 INDICATIONS & USAGE

NAPROXEN Tablets, Naproxen Delayed-Release Tablets, and NAPROXEN SODIUM are indicated for: the relief of the signs and symptoms of: • rheumatoid arthritis • osteoarthritis • ankylosing spondylitis • Polyarticular Juvenile Idiopathic Arthritis NAPROXEN Tablets and NAPROXEN SODIUM are also indicated for: the relief of signs and symptoms of: • tendonitis • bursitis • acute gout the management of: • pain • primary dysmenorrhea
