# 11 DESCRIPTION

Naproxen Tablets, USP is nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug productavailable as follows: White tablets containing 250 mg, 375 mg and 500 mg of naproxen for oral administration.
Naproxen, USP is a propionic acid derivative related to the arylacetic acid group of nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs.
The chemical name for naproxen, USP is (S)-6-methoxy-α-methyl-2-naphthaleneacetic acid.
Naproxen has a molecular weight of 230.26 and a molecular formula of C14H14O3.
Naproxen, USP has the following structural formula:

Molecular Formula: C14H14O3

MW = 230.26

Naproxen, USP is an odorless, white to off-white crystalline substance.
It is lipid-soluble, practically insoluble in water at low pH and freely soluble in water at high pH.
The octanol/water partition coefficient of naproxen at pH 7.4 is 1.6 to 1.8.
The inactive ingredients in naproxen tablets include: croscarmellose sodium, povidone and magnesium stearate.
