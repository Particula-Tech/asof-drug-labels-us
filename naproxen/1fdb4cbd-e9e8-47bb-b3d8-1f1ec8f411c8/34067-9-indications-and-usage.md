# 1 INDICATIONS & USAGE

Naproxen Tablets, USP are indicated for:

the relief of the signs and symptoms of:

- rheumatoid arthritis
- osteoarthritis
- ankylosing spondylitis
- Polyarticular Juvenile Idiopathic Arthritis
- tendonitis
- bursitis
- acute gout

the management of

- pain
- primary dysmenorrhea
