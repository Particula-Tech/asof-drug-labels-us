# HOW SUPPLIED

## NAPROSYN Suspension

125 mg/5 mL (contains 39 mg sodium per 5 mL): Available as light orange colored, pineapple-orange flavored suspension in 1 pint (473 mL) light-resistant bottles (NDC 70868-700-16).

Store at 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F); avoid excessive heat, above 40°C (104°F).

Dispense in light-resistant containers.

Keep out of reach of children.

Shake gently before use.
