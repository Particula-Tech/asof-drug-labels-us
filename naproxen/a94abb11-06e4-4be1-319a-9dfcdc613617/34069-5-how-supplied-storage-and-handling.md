# HOW SUPPLIED

TREXIMET contains 119 mg of sumatriptan succinate equivalent to 85 mg of sumatriptan and 500 mg of naproxen sodium and is supplied as blue film-coated tablets debossed on one side with TREXIMET in compact containers of 9 tablets with a specially formulated, non-removable desiccant (NDC 0173-0750-00).

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15°-30°C (59°-86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Do not repackage; dispense and store in original container.
