# INDICATIONS AND USAGE

TREXIMET is indicated for the acute treatment of migraine attacks with or without aura in adults.
Carefully consider the potential benefits and risks of TREXIMET and other treatment options when deciding to use TREXIMET.

TREXIMET is not intended for the prophylactic therapy of migraine or for use in the management of hemiplegic or basilar migraine (see CONTRAINDICATIONS).
Safety and effectiveness of TREXIMET have not been established for cluster headache.
