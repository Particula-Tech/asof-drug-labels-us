# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

LANOXIN Tablets have "LANOXIN" on one side and are supplied as follows:

| Mcg | Scored | Color | Imprint | NDC 59212-xxxx-xx |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bottle/100 | Unit dose/100 |  |  |  |  |
| 62.5 | No | Peach | U3A | 240-55 | Not applicable |
| 125 | Yes | Yellow | Y3B | 242-55 | 242-56 |
| 250 | Yes | White | X3A | 249-55 | 249-56 |

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15 to 30°C (59 to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature] in a dry place and protect from light.
Keep out of reach of children.
Dispense in tight, light-resistant container.
