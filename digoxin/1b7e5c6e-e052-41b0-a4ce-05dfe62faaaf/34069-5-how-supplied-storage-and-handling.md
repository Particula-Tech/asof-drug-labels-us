# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Digoxin Oral Solution, USP 0.05 mg (50 mcg) per 1 mL is a clear, colorless solution available in one strength as follows:

0.05 mg per 1 mL Oral Solution

NDC 0054-0057-46: Bottle of 60 mL with a calibrated dropper

Storage

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature] and protect from light.
