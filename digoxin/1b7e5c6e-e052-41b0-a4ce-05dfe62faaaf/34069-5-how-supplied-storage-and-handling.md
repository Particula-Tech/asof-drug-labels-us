# HOW SUPPLIED

Digoxin Oral Solution, USP [50 mcg (0.05 mg) per mL] is available in two forms of packaging: as unit-dose Patient Cups™ and as bottles with child-resistant closures.
Once open, the bottles are used with graduated droppers provided in the carton.
Starting at 0.2 mL, this 1 mL dropper is marked in divisions of 0.1 mL, corresponding to 5 mcg or 0.005 mg of digoxin.

Each bottle contains 60 mL of solution (3 mg of digoxin), and Patient Cups are each filled to deliver 2.5 mL of solution (0.125 mg of digoxin) or 5 mL of solution (0.25 mg of digoxin).
There are 10 Patient Cups in each shelf pack, and 4 shelf packs in each shipping package.
The National Drug Code designations of the various packages are

NDC 0054-0057-16 2.5 mL Patient Cups

NDC 0054-0057-46 60 mL bottle

## Storage

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature] and protect from light.
