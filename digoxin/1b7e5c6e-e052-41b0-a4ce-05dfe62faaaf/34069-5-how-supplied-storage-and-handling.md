# HOW SUPPLIED

Digoxin Oral Solution, USP [50 mcg (0.05 mg) per mL] is available in 60 mL (3 mg of digoxin) bottles with child-resistant closures.
Once open, the bottles are used with graduated droppers provided in the carton.
Starting at 0.2 mL, this 1 mL dropper is marked in divisions of 0.1 mL, corresponding to 5 mcg or 0.005 mg of digoxin.

The National Drug Code designations of the package is

NDC 0054-0057-46 60 mL bottle

## Storage

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature] and protect from light.
