# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Each 1 mL of clear, colorless Digoxin Oral Solution USP contains 0.05 mg (50 mcg).

The Digoxin Oral Solution USP bottles are to be used with the graduated droppers provided in the carton.
Starting at 0.2 mL, this 1 mL dropper is marked in divisions of 0.1 mL, corresponding to 5 mcg or 0.005 mg of digoxin.

NOTE: The calibrated dropper supplied with the 60 mL bottle of Digoxin Oral Solution USP is not appropriate to measure doses below 0.2 mL.
Doses less than 0.2 mL require appropriate methods or measuring devices designed to administer an accurate amount to the patient, such as a graduated syringe.
