# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Digoxin injection is contraindicated in patients with:

- •Ventricular fibrillation [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)]
- •Known hypersensitivity to digoxin (reactions seen include unexplained rash, swelling of the mouth, lips or throat or a difficulty in breathing).
  A hypersensitivity reaction to other digitalis preparations usually constitutes a contraindication to digoxin.
