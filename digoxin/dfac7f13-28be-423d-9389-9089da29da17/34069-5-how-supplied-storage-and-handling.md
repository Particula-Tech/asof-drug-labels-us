# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Digoxin tablets USP, 125 mcg are light yellow, round, flat-faced beveled edge tablets debossed with “981” on top of bisect on one side and plain on the other side.
Bottles of 100: NDC 0115-9811-01 Bottles of 500: NDC 0115-9811-02 Bottles of 1000: NDC 0115-9811-03 Digoxin tablets USP, 250 mcg are off-white to light tan, round, convex tablets debossed with “982” on top of bisect on one side and plain on the other side.
Bottles of 100: NDC 0115-9822-01 Bottles of 500: NDC 0115-9822-02 Bottles of 1000: NDC 0115-9822-03 Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Keep out of reach of children.
Dispense in tightly-closed, light-resistant container as defined in the USP, with child-resistant closure, as required.
