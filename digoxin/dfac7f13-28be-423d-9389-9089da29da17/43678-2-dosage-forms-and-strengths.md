# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Digoxin tablets USP, 125 mcg are light yellow, round, flat-faced beveled edge tablets debossed with “981” on top of bisect on one side and plain on the other side.

Digoxin tablets USP, 250 mcg are off-white to light tan, round, convex tablets debossed with “982” on top of bisect on one side and plain on the other side.
