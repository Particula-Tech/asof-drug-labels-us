# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

125 mcg (0.125 mg) tablets are light yellow, round, flat-faced beveled edge tablets debossed with "981" on top of bisect on one side and plain on the other side.

250 mcg (0.25 mg) tablets are off-white to light tan, round, convex tablets debossed with "982" on top of bisect on one side and plain on the other side.
