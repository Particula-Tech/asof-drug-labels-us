# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Digoxin Tablets USP, 125 mcg are Yellow, Round, Scored Tablets, Debossed "W 40" on Scored Side

NDC 0143-1240-01: Bottles of 100 tablets.
NDC 0143-1240-10: Bottles of 1000 tablets.
NDC 0143-1240-51: Bottles of 5000 tablets.

Digoxin Tablets USP, 250 mg are White, Round, Scored Tablets, Debossed "WW 41" on Scored Side

NDC 0143-1241-01: Bottles of 100 tablets.
NDC 0143-1241-10: Bottles of 1000 tablets.
NDC 0143-1241-51: Bottles of 5000 tablets.

Store in a dry place at 20º to 25ºC (68º to 77ºF) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature] and protect from light.
Keep out of reach of children.

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP using a child-resistant closure.
