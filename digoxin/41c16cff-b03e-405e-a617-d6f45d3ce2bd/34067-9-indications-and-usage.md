# INDICATIONS AND USAGE:

## Heart Failure

Digoxin Tablets are indicated for the treatment of mild to moderate heart failure.
Digoxin Tablets increase left ventricular ejection fraction and improve heart failure symptoms as evidenced by exercise capacity and heart failure-related hospitalizations and emergency care, while having no effect on mortality.
Where possible, Digoxin Tablets should be used with a diuretic and an angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitor, but an optimal order for starting these three drugs cannot be specified.

## Atrial Fibrillation

Digoxin Tablets are indicated for the control of ventricular response rate in patients with chronic atrial fibrillation.
