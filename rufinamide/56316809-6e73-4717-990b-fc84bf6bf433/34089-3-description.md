# 11 DESCRIPTION

Rufinamide is a triazole derivative structurally unrelated to currently marketed antiepileptic drugs (AEDs).
Rufinamide has the chemical name 1-[(2,6-difluorophenyl)methyl]-1H-1,2,3-triazole-4 carboxamide.
It has an empirical formula of C10H8F2N4O and a molecular weight of 238.20.
The drug substance is a white crystalline powder.
Rufinamide is practically insoluble in water, slightly soluble in tetrahydrofuran and in methanol, and very slightly soluble in ethanol and in acetonitrile.

- 

Rufinamide Tablets, USP are available for oral administration containing either 200 mg or 400 mg of rufinamide, USP.
Each film-coated tablet contains the following inactive ingredients: croscarmellose sodium, hypromellose, lactose monohydrate, magnesium stearate, microcrystalline cellulose, Opadry Pink, pregelatinized corn starch and sodium lauryl sulfate.
Opadry Pink contains: hypromellose, iron oxide red, propylene glycol, talc and titanium dioxide.
