# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Rufinamide Tablets, USP

200 mg tablets are supplied as an oval, light pink to pink biconvex tablet, scored on both sides, and debossed with product identification “54 961” on one side.

NDC 0054-0425-13: Bottle of 30 Tablets NDC 0054-0425-23: Bottle of 120 Tablets

400 mg tablets are supplied as an oval, light pink to pink biconvex tablet, scored on both sides, and debossed with product identification “54 457” on one side.

NDC 0054-0426-13: Bottle of 30 Tablets NDC 0054-0426-23: Bottle of 120 Tablets

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]
Protect from moisture.
Replace cap securely after opening.
