# 1INDICATIONS AND USAGE

## 1.1 Premenstrual Dysphoric Disorder (PMDD)

SARAFEM is indicated for the treatment of premenstrual dysphoric disorder (PMDD) [see Clinical Studies (14.1)].

The effectiveness of SARAFEM in long-term use (that is, for more than 6 months) has not been systematically evaluated in placebo-controlled trials.
The use of SARAFEM for extended periods should be periodically re-evaluated for the individual patient [see Dosage and Administration (2.1)].
