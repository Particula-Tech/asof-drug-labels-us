# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Fluoxetine Capsules USP, 10 mg contain fluoxetine hydrochloride, USP equivalent to 10 mg fluoxetine, and are available as white, opaque capsules printed with PLIVA 647 in green band on cap and body.

Fluoxetine Capsules USP, 20 mg contain fluoxetine hydrochloride, USP equivalent to 20 mg fluoxetine, and are available as white, opaque capsules printed with PLIVA 648 in green band on cap only.
