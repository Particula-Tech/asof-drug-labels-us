# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Fluoxetine capsules USP, 40 mg, contain fluoxetine hydrochloride, equivalent to 40 mg fluoxetine, and are available as hard gelatin capsules with a blue cap and orange body.
Both the body and cap of the #2 capsules are imprinted “TEVA” and “7198.”
They are available in bottles of 30, 100, and 500 and in unit-dose boxes of 100 (10 x 10).

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Protect from light.

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP, with a child-resistant closure (as required).
