# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Fluoxetine capsules USP, 10 mg, contain fluoxetine hydrochloride, equivalent to 10 mg fluoxetine, and are available as hard gelatin capsules with a powder blue cap and body.
Both the body and cap of the #3 capsule are imprinted “93” and “42.”

Fluoxetine capsules USP, 40 mg, contain fluoxetine hydrochloride, equivalent to 40 mg fluoxetine, and are available as hard gelatin capsules with a blue cap and orange body.
Both the body and cap of the #2 capsule are imprinted “93” and “7198.”
