# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

## 1.1 Major Depressive Disorder

Fluoxetine oral solution, USP is indicated for the treatment of Major Depressive Disorder [see Clinical Studies (14.1)].
The efficacy of fluoxetine oral solution, USP in MDD was established in one 5 week trial, three 6 week trials, and one maintenance study in adults.
The efficacy of fluoxetine oral solution, USP was also established in two 8 to 9 week trials in pediatric patients aged 8 to 18 years; doses greater than 20 mg/day have not been studied in pediatric patients with MDD.
The usefulness of the drug in adult and pediatric patients receiving fluoxetine oral solution, USP for extended periods, should periodically be re-evaluated [see Dosage and Administration (2.1)] .

## 1.2 Obsessive Compulsive Disorder

Fluoxetine oral solution, USP is indicated for the treatment of Obsessive Compulsive Disorder (OCD) [see Clinical Studies (14.2)] .
The efficacy of fluoxetine oral solution, USP in OCD was demonstrated in two 13 week trials in adults and one 13 week trial in pediatric patients aged 7 to 17 years.
The effectiveness of fluoxetine oral solution, USP in long-term use, i.e., for more than 13 weeks, has not been systematically evaluated in placebo-controlled trials.
Therefore, the physician who elects to use fluoxetine oral solution, USP for extended periods, should periodically re-evaluate the long-term usefulness of the drug for the individual patient [see Dosage and Administration (2.2)] .

## 1.3 Bulimia Nervosa

Fluoxetine oral solution, USP is indicated for the treatment of moderate to severe Bulimia Nervosa [see Clinical Studies (14.3)].
The efficacy of fluoxetine oral solution, USP in Bulimia was demonstrated in two 8 week trials and one 16 week trial in adults.
The physician who elects to use fluoxetine oral solution, USP for extended periods should periodically re-evaluate the long-term usefulness of the drug for the individual patient [see Dosage and Administration (2.3)] .

## 1.4 Panic Disorder

Fluoxetine oral solution, USP is indicated for the treatment of Panic Disorder, with or without agoraphobia [see Clinical Studies (14.4) ] .
The efficacy of fluoxetine oral solution, USP in Panic Disorder was demonstrated in two 12-week trials in adults.
The effectiveness of fluoxetine oral solution, USP in long-term use, i.e., for more than 12 weeks, has not been established in placebo-controlled trials.
Therefore, the physician who elects to use fluoxetine oral solution, USP for extended periods, should periodically re-evaluate the long-term usefulness of the drug for the individual patient [see Dosage and Administration (2.4)] .
