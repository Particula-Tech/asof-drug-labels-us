# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Fluoxetine Oral Solution, USP is a mint flavored, colorless to straw colored liquid available in:

20 mg per 5 mL Bottles of 120 mL NDC 62135-730-41

Dispense in a tight, light resistant container as defined in USP.
Protect from light.

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
