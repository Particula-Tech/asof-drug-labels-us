# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Carbidopa, levodopa and entacapone tablets are contraindicated in patients:

- •Taking nonselective monoamine oxidase (MAO) inhibitors (e.g., phenelzine and tranylcypromine).
  These nonselective MAO inhibitors must be discontinued at least two weeks prior to initiating therapy with carbidopa, levodopa and entacapone tablets.
- •With narrow-angle glaucoma.
