# PRINCIPAL DISPLAY PANEL - 10 mg/100 mg Tablet Bottle Label - 3915-68

NDC 0006-3915-68

Sinemet® (carbidopa-levodopa) Tablets

10 mg/100 mg

Each tablet contains 10 mg carbidopa (anhydrous equivalent) and 100 mg levodopa.

Store at 25°C (77°F), excursions permitted to 15-30°C (59-86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Store in a tightly closed container, protected from light and moisture.

Rx only

100 Tablets
