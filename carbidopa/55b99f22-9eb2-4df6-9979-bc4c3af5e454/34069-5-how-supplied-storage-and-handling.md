# HOW SUPPLIED

Carbidopa Tablets, 25 mg, are light pink, round, standard convex shaped tablets with '123' embossed over the functional scoring on one side and plain on the other side.

They are supplied as follows:

- NDC 42799-123-01 bottles of 100 tablets
- NDC 42799-123-02 bottles of 1000 tablets

## Storage

Store at 25°C (77°F), excursions permitted to 15 to 30°C (59 to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
