When LODOSYN (carbidopa) is to be given to carbidopa-naive patients who are being treated with levodopa, the two drugs should be given at the same time, starting with no more than 20 to 25% of the previous daily dosage of levodopa when given without LODOSYN (carbidopa).
At least twelve hours should elapse between the last dose of levodopa and initiation of therapy with LODOSYN(carbidopa)and levodopa.See theWARNINGS and DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATIONsections beforeinitiating therapy.
