# HOW SUPPLIED

LODOSYN Tablets, 25 mg, are round, orange-colored compressed tablets that are on one side flat beveled edge, bisect, embossed “711” and embossed “LODOSYN” on the other side.

1. They are supplied as follows: NDC 25010-711-15 bottles of 100.

## Storage

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
