# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

DHIVY is contraindicated in patients

- Currently taking a nonselective monoamine oxidase (MAO) inhibitor (e.g., phenelzine, linezolid, and tranylcypromine) or have recently (within 2 weeks) taken a nonselective MAO inhibitor.
  Hypertension can occur if these drugs are used concurrently [see Drug Interactions (7.1)].
- With known hypersensitivity to any component of DHIVY [see Adverse Reactions (6)].
