# 11 DESCRIPTION

Tranexamic acid is trans-4-(aminomethyl)cyclohexanecarboxylic acid, an antifibrinolytic agent.
Tranexamic acid is a white crystalline powder.
The structural formula is

1. Empirical Formula: C8H15NO2 Molecular Weight: 157.2

Tranexamic Acid in Sodium Chloride Injection is a clear to colorless sterile, nonpyrogenic injectable solution for intravenous administration.
Each IV bag contains 1000 mg tranexamic acid, USP, 700 mg of sodium chloride, USP and Water for Injection, USP.
The aqueous solution has a pH of 6.5 to 8.0.
