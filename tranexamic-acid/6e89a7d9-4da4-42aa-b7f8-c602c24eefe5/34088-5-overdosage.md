# OVERDOSAGE

Cases of overdosage of CYKLOKAPRON have been reported.
Based on these reports, symptoms of overdosage may be gastrointestinal, e.g., nausea, vomiting, diarrhea; hypotensive, e.g., orthostatic symptoms; thromboembolic, e.g., arterial, venous, embolic; visual impairment; mental status changes; myoclonus, and rash.
