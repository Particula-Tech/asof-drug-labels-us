# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

LYSTEDA (tranexamic acid) tablets are provided as white oval-shaped tablets.
Each tablet is debossed with the marking “XP650”and are supplied as:

| Quantity | Package Type | NDC Number |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 30 tablets | HDPE bottle | 66479-650-30 |
| 30 tablets | Carton containing 5 blister cards with 6 tablets per card | 66479-650-31 |
| 100 tablets | HDPE bottle | 66479-650-01 |
| 500 tablets | HDPE bottle | 66479-650-50 |

Storage

Store at room temperature 25° C (77° F); excursions permitted to 15-30° C (59-86° F). [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
