# CONTRAINDICATIONS

Tranexamic acid injection is contraindicated:

1. 1.In patients with acquired defective color vision, since this prohibits measuring one endpoint that should be followed as a measure of toxicity (see WARNINGS).
2. 2.In patients with subarachnoid hemorrhage.
   Anecdotal experience indicates that cerebral edema and cerebral infarction may be caused by tranexamic acid injection in such patients.
3. 3.In patients with active intravascular clotting.
4. 4.In patients with hypersensitivity to tranexamic acid or any of the ingredients.
