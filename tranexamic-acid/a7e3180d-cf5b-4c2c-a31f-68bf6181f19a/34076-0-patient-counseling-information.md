# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

Advise the patient to read the FDA-approved patient labeling (Patient Information).

Thromboembolic Risk Inform patients that LYSTEDA may increase the risk of venous and arterial thrombosis or thromboembolism and to contact their healthcare provider for any signs or symptoms suggestive of thromboembolism [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].

Advise patients to discontinue use of LYSTEDA and promptly report visual and ocular symptoms to their health care provider as retinal venous and arterial occlusion have been reported in patients using LYSTEDA [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].

Severe Allergic Reactions Inform patients that they should stop LYSTEDA and seek immediate medical attention if they notice symptoms of a severe allergic reaction (e.g., shortness of breath or throat tightening) [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)].

Administration Instructions Instruct patients to take LYSTEDA only during menstruation and for a maximum of 5 days each month [see Recommended Dosage (2.1)].
