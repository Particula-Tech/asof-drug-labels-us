# DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

For most patients, the recommended dose of Cyclobenzaprine HCl tablets is 5 mg three times a day.
Based on individual patient response, the dose may be increased to 10 mg three times a day.
Use of Cyclobenzaprine HCl tablets for periods longer than two or three weeks is not recommended.
(See INDICATIONS AND USAGE).

Less frequent dosing should be considered for hepatically impaired or elderly patients (see PRECAUTIONS, Impaired Hepatic Function, and Use in the Elderly).
