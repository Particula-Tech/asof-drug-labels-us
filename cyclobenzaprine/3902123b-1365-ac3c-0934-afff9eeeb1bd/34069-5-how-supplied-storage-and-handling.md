# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

AMRIX extended-release capsules are available in 15 and 30 mg strengths, packaged in bottles of 60 capsules.
AMRIX 15 mg capsules (NDC 63459-700-60) are orange/orange and are embossed in blue ink with “15 mg” on the body, and Cephalon “C” logo, “Cephalon”, and a dashed band on the cap.
AMRIX 30 mg capsules (NDC 63459-701-60) are blue/orange and are embossed in white ink with “30 mg” on the body, and Cephalon “C” logo, “Cephalon”, and a dashed band on the cap.

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP/NF.

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15 - 30°C (59 - 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
