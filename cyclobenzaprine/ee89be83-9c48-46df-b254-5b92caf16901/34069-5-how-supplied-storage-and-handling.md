# HOW SUPPLIED

FLEXERIL tablets are available in 5 mg and 10 mg dosage strengths.
The 5 mg tablets are yellow-orange, 5-sided D-shaped, film coated tablets, coded FLEX over 5 on one side and without coding on the other.
The 10 mg tablets are butterscotch yellow, 5-sided D-shaped, film coated tablets, coded FLEXERIL on one side and without coding on the other.
The two dosage strengths are supplied as follows:

| 5 mg 100 count bottle | NDC 50580-280-10 |
| --- | --- |
| 10 mg 100 count bottle | NDC 50580-874-11 |
