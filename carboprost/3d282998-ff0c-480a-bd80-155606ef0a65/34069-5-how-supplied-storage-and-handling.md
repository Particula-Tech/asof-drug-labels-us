# HOW SUPPLIED

HEMABATE Sterile Solution is available in the following packages: 1 mL ampoules NDC 0009-0856-05 10 × 1 mL ampoules NDC 0009-0856-08 Each mL of HEMABATE contains carboprost tromethamine equivalent to 250 mcg of carboprost.

HEMABATE must be refrigerated at 2° to 8° C (36° to 46° F).
