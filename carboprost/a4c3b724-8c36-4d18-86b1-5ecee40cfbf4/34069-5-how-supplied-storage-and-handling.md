# HOW SUPPLIED

Carboprost tromethamine injection, USP is a sterile, clear colorless solution and is available in the following package:

250 mcg per mL 1 mL Single-Dose Vials NDC 55150-459-01 Packaged in a Carton of 10 NDC 55150-459-10

Each mL of carboprost tromethamine injection, USP contains carboprost tromethamine, USP equivalent to 250 mcg of carboprost.

Carboprost tromethamine injection, USP must be refrigerated at 2° to 8°C (36° to 46°F).

Discard unused portion.

The vial stopper is not made with natural rubber latex.
