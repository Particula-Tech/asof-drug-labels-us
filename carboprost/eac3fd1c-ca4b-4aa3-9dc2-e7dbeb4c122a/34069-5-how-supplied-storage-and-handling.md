# HOW SUPPLIED

Carboprost Tromethamine Injection USP, 250 mcg/mL is supplied as a clear, colorless solution and is available in the following packages:

250 mcg/mL (1 mL):

1 mL Single-dose Vial: NDC 70121-1680-1

10 Vials in 1 Carton: NDC 70121-1680-7

Each mL of carboprost tromethamine injection, USP contains carboprost tromethamine, USP equivalent to 250 mcg of carboprost.

Carboprost tromethamine injection, USP must be refrigerated at 2° to 8°C (36° to 46°F).
Discard unused portion.
