# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Ticagrelor tablets 90 mg are supplied as white or off-white round film-coated tablets, debossed “L60” on one side and blank on the other side.

Bottles of 60 – NDC 72516-018-06

Storage and Handling

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP controlled room temperature].
