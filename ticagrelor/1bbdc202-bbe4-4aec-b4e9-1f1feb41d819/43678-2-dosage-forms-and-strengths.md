# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Ticagrelor tablets 60 mg is supplied as a light yellow to yellow, oval shaped, film-coated tablet debossed with “137” on one side and “A” on the other side.

Ticagrelor tablets 90 mg is supplied as a light yellow to yellow, round shaped, film-coated tablet debossed with “A91” on one side and plain on the other side.
