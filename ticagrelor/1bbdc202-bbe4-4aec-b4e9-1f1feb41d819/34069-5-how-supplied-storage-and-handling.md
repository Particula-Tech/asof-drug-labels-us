# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Ticagrelor tablets are available as follows:

60 mg - Light yellow to yellow, oval shaped, film-coated tablet debossed with “137” on one side and “A” on the other side.
Tablets are supplied in bottles of 60 (NDC 0480-2688-06).

90 mg - Light yellow to yellow, round shaped, film-coated tablet debossed with “A91” on one side and plain on the other side.
Tablets are supplied in bottles of 60 (NDC 0480-2695-06).

Storage and Handling

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP controlled room temperature].

Dispense in a tight container as defined in the USP.
