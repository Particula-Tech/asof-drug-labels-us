# WARNING:RISK OF EMBRYO-FETAL TOXICITY DUE TO EXCIPIENTS

This formulation of Vancomycin Injection, USP is not recommended for use during pregnancy because it contains the excipients polyethylene glycol (PEG) 400 and N-acetyl-D-alanine (NADA), which caused fetal malformations in animal reproduction studies.
If use of vancomycin is needed during pregnancy, use other available formulations of vancomycin [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1) and Use in Specific Populations (8.1)].
