# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Vancomycin Injection, USP is a ready to use clear, colorless to light brown solution in single-dose flexible bags containing 500 mg vancomycin in 100 mL, 750 mg vancomycin in 150 mL, 1 g vancomycin in 200 mL, 1.25 g vancomycin in 250 mL, 1.5 g vancomycin in 300 mL, 1.75 g vancomycin in 350 mL and 2 g vancomycin in 400 mL of liquid [see Description (11)].
The flexible bags are supplied in sealed aluminum overpouches.
