# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Injection: TYZAVAN (Vancomycin Injection, USP) is a ready to use clear, colorless to light brown sterile solution in single-dose flexible bags in the following strengths: 500 mg vancomycin in 100 mL (5 mg/mL), 750 mg vancomycin in 150 mL (5 mg/mL), 1 g vancomycin in 200 mL (5 mg/mL), 1.25 g vancomycin in 250 mL (5 mg/mL), 1.5 g vancomycin in 300 mL (5 mg/mL), 1.75 g vancomycin in 350 mL (5 mg/mL) and 2 g vancomycin in 400 mL (5mg/mL) of Water for Injection [see Description (11)].
