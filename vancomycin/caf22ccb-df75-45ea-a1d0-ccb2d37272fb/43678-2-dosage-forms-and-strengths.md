# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Vancomycin Hydrochloride for Injection, USP is a sterile lyophilized powder for injection supplied as an off-white to light tan colored powder or plug in single-dose vials containing vancomycin hydrochloride, USP equivalent to 250 mg, 750 mg, 1.25 g, or 1.5 g of vancomycin base.
