# HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Vancomycin Hydrochloride for Injection, USP is supplied as a sterile powder in single-dose fliptop vials that contain the vancomycin equivalent of either 500 mg, 750 mg, 1 g or 1.5 g.

| Unit of Sale | Fill |
| --- | --- |
| NDC 0409-4332-01 Tray of 10 Single-dose Fliptop Vials | 500 mg/vial |
| NDC 0409-6531-02 Tray of 10 Single-dose Fliptop Vials | 750 mg/vial |
| NDC 0409-6533-01 Tray of 10 Single-dose Fliptop Vials | 1 g/vial |
| NDC 0409-6533-21 Carton of 10 Single-dose Fliptop Vials | 1 g/vial |
| NDC 0409-3515-01 Tray of 10 Single-dose Fliptop Vials | 1.5 g/vial |

Prior to reconstitution, store at 20 to 25°C (68 to 77°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]
