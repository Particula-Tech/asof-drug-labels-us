# DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

Adults — VANCOCIN HCl Capsules are used in treating antibiotic-associated pseudomembranous colitis caused by C. difficile and staphylococcal enterocolitis.
VANCOCIN HCl Capsules are not effective for other types of infections.
The usual adult total daily dosage is 500 mg to 2 g administered orally in 3 or 4 divided doses for 7 to 10 days.

Pediatric Patients — The usual daily dosage is 40 mg/kg in 3 or 4 divided doses for 7 to 10 days.
The total daily dosage should not exceed 2 g.
