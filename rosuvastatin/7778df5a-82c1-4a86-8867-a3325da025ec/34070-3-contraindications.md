# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Rosuvastatin is contraindicated in the following conditions: • Acute liver failure or decompensated cirrhosis [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3)]. • Hypersensitivity to rosuvastatin or any excipients in rosuvastatin.
Hypersensitivity reactions including rash, pruritus, urticaria, and angioedema have been reported with rosuvastatin [see Adverse Reactions (6.1)].
