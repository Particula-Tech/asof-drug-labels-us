Storage

Store EZALLOR SPRINKLE (rosuvastatin) capsules at 20º to 25ºC (68º to 77ºF); excursions permitted between 15° and 30°C (59° and 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Protect from moisture.
