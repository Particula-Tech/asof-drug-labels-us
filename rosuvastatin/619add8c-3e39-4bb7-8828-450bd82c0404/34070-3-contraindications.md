# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

EZALLOR SPRINKLE is contraindicated in patients with:

- Acute liver failure or decompensated cirrhosis [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3)].
- Hypersensitivity to rosuvastatin or any excipients in EZALLOR SPRINKLE.
  Hypersensitivity reactions, including rash, pruritus, urticaria, and angioedema, have been reported with EZALLOR SPRINKLE [see Adverse Reactions (6.1)].
