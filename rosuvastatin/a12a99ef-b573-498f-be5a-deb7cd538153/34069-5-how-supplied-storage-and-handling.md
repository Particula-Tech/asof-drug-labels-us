# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Rosuvastatin Tablets, USP are supplied as:

5 mg: White to off-white, round tablets, debossed with “CE”on one side and “161”on the other side.

NDC 62135-690-90 Bottles of 90

10 mg: White to off-white, round tablets, debossed with “CE”on one side and “162”on the other side.

NDC 62135-691-90 Bottles of 90

20 mg: White to off-white, round tablets, debossed with “CE”on one side and “163”on the other side.

NDC 62135-692-90 Bottles of 90

40 mg: White to off-white, oval shaped tablets, debossed with “CE”on one side and “164”on the other side.

NDC 62135-693-30 Bottles of 30

Storage

Store at controlled room temperature, 20ºC to 25ºC (68ºF to 77ºF); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F and 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from moisture.
