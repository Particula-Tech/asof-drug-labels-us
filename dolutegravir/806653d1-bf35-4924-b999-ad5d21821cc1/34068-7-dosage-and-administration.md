# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

## 2.1 Recommended Dosage

The recommended dosage of JULUCA is one tablet taken orally once daily with a meal [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].
One tablet of JULUCA contains 50 mg of dolutegravir and 25 mg of rilpivirine.

## 2.2 Recommended Dosage with Rifabutin Coadministration

If JULUCA is coadministered with rifabutin, take an additional 25-mg tablet of rilpivirine with JULUCA once daily with a meal for the duration of the rifabutin coadministration [see Drug Interactions (7.4)].
