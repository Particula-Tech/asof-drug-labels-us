# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Each DOVATO tablet contains 50 mg of dolutegravir as dolutegravir sodium and 300 mg lamivudine and is an oval, biconvex, white, film-coated tablet, debossed with “SV 137” on one face.

Bottle of 30 tablets with child-resistant closure NDC 49702-246-13.

Blister pack of 30 tablets with child-resistant closure NDC 49702-246-33.

Store below 30°C (86°F).
