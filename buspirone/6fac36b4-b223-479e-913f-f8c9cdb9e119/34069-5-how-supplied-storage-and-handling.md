# HOW SUPPLIED

BusPIRone hydrochloride tablets, USP are available as:

5 mg - white to off-white, round, scored on one side of the tablet, and debossed “93” and “53” on the other side.
Tablets are packaged in bottles of 100 and 500.

10 mg - white to off-white, round, scored on one side of the tablet, and debossed “93” and “54” on the other side.
Tablets are packaged in bottles of 100 and 500.

15 mg - white to off-white, rectangular tablets that can either be bisected or trisected.
They are debossed “93” and “1003” on bisect segments, and are debossed “5” on each trisect segment.
Tablets are packaged in bottles of 100 and 500.

30 mg - white to off-white, rectangular tablets that can either be bisected or trisected.
They are debossed “93” and “5200” on bisect segments, and are debossed “10” on each trisect segment.
Tablets are packaged in bottles of 60 and 500.

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP, with a child-resistant closure (as required).

KEEP THIS AND ALL MEDICATIONS OUT OF THE REACH OF CHILDREN.
