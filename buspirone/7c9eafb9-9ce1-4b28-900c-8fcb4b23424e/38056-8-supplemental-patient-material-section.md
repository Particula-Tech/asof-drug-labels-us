# BUSPIRONE HYDROCHLORIDE TABLETS Patient Instruction Sheet

HOW TO USE:

BUSPIRONE HYDROCHLORIDE TABLETS, USP 15 mg and 30 mg

In convenient multi-scored tablet form

Response to buspirone varies among individuals.
Your physician may find it necessary to adjust your dosage to obtain the proper response.

This multi-scored tablet design makes dosage adjustments easy.
Each tablet is scored and can be broken accurately to provide any of the following dosages.

If your doctor prescribed the 15 mg tablet:

15 mg (the entire tablet)

10 mg (two-thirds of a tablet)

5 mg (one-third of a tablet)

7.5 mg (one-half of a tablet)

If your doctor prescribed the 30 mg tablet:

30 mg (the entire tablet)

20 mg (two-thirds of a tablet)

10 mg (one-third of a tablet)

15 mg (one-half of a tablet)

To break a multi-scored tablet accurately and easily, hold the tablet between your thumbs and index fingers close to the appropriate tablet score (groove) as shown in the picture.
Then, with the tablet score facing you, apply pressure and snap the tablet segments apart (segments breaking incorrectly should not be used).
