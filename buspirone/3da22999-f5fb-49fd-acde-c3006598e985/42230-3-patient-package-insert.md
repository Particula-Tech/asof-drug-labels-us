# PATIENT INFORMATION BUSPIRONE HYDROCHLORIDE TABLETS (byoo-SPY-rone-HIGH-droe-KLOR-ide)

HOW TO USE:

BusPIRone Hydrochloride Tablets 15 mg and 30 mg

Response to buspirone varies among individuals.
Your physician may find it necessary to adjust your dosage to obtain the proper response.

This tablet design makes dosage adjustments easy.
Each tablet is scored and can be broken accurately to provide any of the following dosages:

To break a tablet accurately and easily, hold the tablet between your thumbs and index fingers close to the appropriate tablet score (groove) as shown below.
Then, with the tablet score facing you, apply pressure and snap the tablet segments apart (segments breaking incorrectly should not be used).
