# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

Advise the patient and/or their caregivers to read the FDA-approved patient labeling (Medication Guide).

Abuse, Misuse, and Addiction

Educate patients and their families about the risks of abuse, misuse, and addiction of Methylphenidate HCl Extended-Release Capsules CD, which can lead to overdose and death, and proper disposal of any unused drug [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)], Drug Abuse and Dependence (9.2), Overdosage (10)].
Advise them to store Methylphenidate HCl Extended-Release Capsules CD in a safe place, preferably locked, and instruct them to not give Methylphenidate HCl Extended-Release Capsules CD to anyone else.

Risks to Patients with Serious Cardiac Disease

Advise patients and/or their caregivers that there are potential risks to patients with serious cardiac disease, including sudden death, with Methylphenidate HCl Extended-Release Capsules CD use.
Instruct them to contact a healthcare provider immediately if the patient develops symptoms such as exertional chest pain, unexplained syncope, or other symptoms suggestive of cardiac disease [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)].

Increased Blood Pressure and Heart Rate

Instruct patients and their caregivers that Methylphenidate HCl Extended-Release Capsules CD can cause elevations of their blood pressure and heart rate [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3)].

Psychiatric Adverse Reactions

Advise patients and their caregivers that Methylphenidate HCl Extended-Release Capsules CD, at the recommended dosage, can cause psychotic or manic symptoms, even in patients without a prior history of psychotic symptoms or mania [see Warnings and Precautions (5.4)].

Priapism

Advise male patients and/or their caregivers of the possibility of priapism.
Instruct them to seek immediate medical attention in the event of priapism [see Warnings and Precautions (5.5)].

Peripheral Vasculopathy, Including Raynaud’s Phenomenon

Instruct patients and/or their caregivers about the risk of peripheral vasculopathy, including Raynaud’s phenomenon, and associated signs and symptoms; to report to their healthcare provider immediately with any signs of unexplained wounds appearing on fingers or toes while taking Methylphenidate HCl Extended-Release Capsules CD [see Warnings and Precautions (5.6)].

Long-Term Suppression of Growth in Pediatric Patients

Advise patients and/or their caregivers that Methylphenidate HCl Extended-Release Capsules CD may cause slowing of growth and weight loss in pediatric patients [see Warnings and Precautions (5.7)].

Increased Intraocular Pressure and Glaucoma

Advise patients and/or their caregivers what increased intraocular pressure and glaucoma may occur during Methylphenidate HCl Extended-Release Capsules CD treatment [see Warnings and Precautions (5.9)].

Motor and Verbal Tics, and Worsening of Tourette Syndrome

Advise patients and/or their caregivers that motor and verbal tics and worsening of Tourette syndrome may occur during Methylphenidate HCl Extended-Release Capsules CD treatment.
Instruct patients to notify their healthcare provider if emergence of new tics or worsening of tics or Tourette syndrome occurs [see Warnings and Precautions (5.10)].

Alcohol Use

Advise patients to avoid alcohol while taking Methylphenidate HCl Extended-Release Capsules CD.
Consumption of alcohol while taking Methylphenidate HCl Extended-Release Capsules CD may result in a more rapid release of the dosage of methylphenidate [see Drug Interactions (7)].

Pregnancy Registry

Advise patients and/or their caregivers that there is a pregnancy exposure registry that monitors pregnancy outcomes in women exposed to Methylphenidate HCl Extended-Release Capsules CD during pregnancy [see Use in Specific Populations (8.1)].

Lactation

Advise Methylphenidate HCl Extended-Release Capsules CD-treated breastfeeding women to monitor their infants for agitation, poor sleeping patterns, changes in feeding, and reduced weight gain [see Use in Specific Populations (8.2)].

Administration Instructions

Instruct patients and their caregivers that the Methylphenidate HCl Extended-Release Capsules CD extended-release capsules and the contents of the extended-release capsules must not be crushed or chewed.
Instruct patients and their caregivers to either (1) swallow whole the extended-release capsules, or (2) alternatively, open the extended-release capsules and sprinkle the contents onto a small amount (tablespoon) of applesauce and take immediately and not to store for future use [see Dosage and Administration (2.3)].
