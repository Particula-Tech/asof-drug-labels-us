# 11 DESCRIPTION

Chemically, methylphenidate hydrochloride is d,l (racemic)-threo-methyl α-phenyl-2-piperidineacetate hydrochloride.
Its empirical formula is C14H19NO2•HCl.
Methylphenidate hydrochloride, USP is a white, odorless, crystalline powder.
Its solutions are acid to litmus.
It is freely soluble in water and in methanol, soluble in alcohol, and slightly soluble in chloroform and in acetone.

Methylphenidate HCl Extended-Release Capsules CD (methylphenidate hydrochloride) extended-release capsules is a CNS stimulant.
The extended-release capsules for oral administration comprise both immediate-release (IR) and extended-release (ER) beads such that 30% of the dosage is provided by the IR component and 70% of the dosage is provided by the ER component.
Methylphenidate HCl Extended-Release Capsules CD is available in six extended-release capsule strengths containing:

- 10 mg of methylphenidate hydrochloride (3 mg IR; 7 mg ER) (equivalent to 10 mg of methylphenidate),
- 20 mg of methylphenidate hydrochloride (6 mg IR; 14 mg ER) (equivalent to 20 mg of methylphenidate),
- 30 mg of methylphenidate hydrochloride (9 mg IR; 21 mg ER) (equivalent to 30 mg of methylphenidate),
- 40 mg of methylphenidate hydrochloride (12 mg IR; 28 mg ER) (equivalent to 40 mg of methylphenidate),
- 50 mg of methylphenidate hydrochloride (15 mg IR; 35 mg ER) (equivalent to 50 mg of methylphenidate)
- 60 mg of methylphenidate hydrochloride (18 mg IR; 42 mg ER) (equivalent to 60 mg of methylphenidate).

Methylphenidate HCl Extended-Release Capsules CD contains the following inactive ingredients: Dibutyl sebacate, ethylcellulose aqueous dispersion, gelatin, hydroxypropyl methylcellulose, polyethylene glycol, povidone, sugar spheres, and titanium dioxide.

The extended-release capsules contain the following color agents:

- 10 mg: FD&C Blue No. 2, FDA/E172 Yellow Iron Oxide
