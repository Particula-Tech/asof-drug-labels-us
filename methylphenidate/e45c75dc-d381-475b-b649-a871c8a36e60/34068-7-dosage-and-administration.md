# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

## 2.1 Pretreatment Screening

Prior to treating patients with Methylphenidate HCl Extended-Release Capsules CD, assess:

- The risk for abuse, misuse, and addiction [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
- For the presence of cardiac disease (e.g., perform a careful history, family history of sudden death or ventricular arrhythmia, and physical exam) [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)].
- The risk for developing a manic episode (e.g., history of depressive symptoms or a family history of suicide, bipolar disorder, or depression) [see Warnings and Precautions (5.4)].
- For a family history for tics or Tourette syndrome and clinically evaluate patients for motor or verbal tics or Tourette syndrome [see Warnings and Precautions (5.10)].

## 2.2 Recommended Dosage

The recommended starting dosage of Methylphenidate HCl Extended-Release Capsules CD in pediatric patients 6 years of age and older is 20 mg once daily.
Dosage may be adjusted weekly in 10 mg to 20 mg increments to the maximum recommended dosage of 60 mg per day.

## 2.3 Administration Instructions

Take Methylphenidate HCl Extended-Release Capsules CD orally once daily in the morning, before breakfast [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].

Swallow the extended-release capsules whole with the aid of liquids.
Alternatively, open the extended-release capsules and sprinkle the contents onto a small amount (tablespoon) of applesauce and administer immediately and then drink fluids.
Do not store for future use.
Do not crush or chew the extended-release capsules.

## 2.4 Dosage Reduction and Discontinuation

If paradoxical aggravation of ADAD symptoms or Methylphenidate HCl Extended-Release Capsules CD-associated adverse reactions occur, reduce dosage or, if necessary, discontinue Methylphenidate HCl Extended-Release Capsules CD.
If ADHD improvement is not observed after appropriate dosage adjustment over a one-month period, discontinue Methylphenidate HCl Extended-Release Capsules CD.
