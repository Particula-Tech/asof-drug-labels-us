# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

ADHANSIA XR is contraindicated in patients:

- With a known hypersensitivity to methylphenidate or other components of ADHANSIA XR.
  Hypersensitivity reactions such as angioedema and anaphylactic reactions have been reported in patients treated with other methylphenidate products [see Adverse Reactions (6.2)].
- Receiving concomitant treatment with monoamine oxidase inhibitors (MAOIs), and also within 14 days following discontinuation of treatment with a MAOI, because of the risk of hypertensive crisis [see Drug Interactions (7.1)].
