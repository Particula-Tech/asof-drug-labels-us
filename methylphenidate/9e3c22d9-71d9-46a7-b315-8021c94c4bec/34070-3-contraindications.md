# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Methylin is contraindicated in patients:

- with known hypersensitivity to methylphenidate or other components of Methylin.
  Hypersensitivity reactions such as angioedema and anaphylactic reactions have been reported in patients treated with methylphenidate [see Adverse Reactions (6)].
- receiving concomitant treatment with monoamine oxidase inhibitors (MAOIs), or within 14 days following discontinuation of treatment with an MAOI, because of the risk of hypertensive crises [see Drug Interactions (7)].
