# 7 DRUG INTERACTIONS

## 7.1 MAO Inhibitors

Do not administer JORNAY PM concomitantly with MAOIs or within 14 days after discontinuing MAOI treatment.
Concomitant use of MAO inhibitors and CNS stimulants can cause hypertensive crisis.
Potential outcomes include death, stroke, myocardial infarction, aortic dissection, ophthalmological complications, eclampsia, pulmonary edema, and renal failure [see Contraindications (4)].
