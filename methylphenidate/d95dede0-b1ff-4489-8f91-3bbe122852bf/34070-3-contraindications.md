# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

JORNAY PM is contraindicated in patients:

- With a history of hypersensitivity to methylphenidate or other components of JORNAY PM.
  Hypersensitivity reactions such as angioedema and anaphylactic reactions have been reported in patients treated with methylphenidate products [see Adverse Reactions (6)].
- Receiving concomitant treatment with monoamine oxidase (MAO) inhibitors, or within 14 days following discontinuation of a monoamine oxidase inhibitor, because of the risk of hypertensive crisis [see Drug Interactions (7.1)].
