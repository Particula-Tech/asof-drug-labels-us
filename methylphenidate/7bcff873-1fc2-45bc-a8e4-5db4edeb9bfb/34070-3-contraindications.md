# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

## 4.1 Hypersensitivity to Methylphenidate

Methylphenidate Transdermal System is contraindicated in patients known to be hypersensitive to methylphenidate or other components of the product (polyester/ethylene vinyl acetate laminate film backing, acrylic adhesive, silicone adhesive, and fluoropolymer-coated polyester) [see Description (11)].

## 4.2 Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors

Methylphenidate Transdermal System is contraindicated during treatment with monoamine oxidase inhibitors, and within a minimum of 14 days following discontinuation of treatment with a monoamine oxidase inhibitor (hypertensive crises may result).
