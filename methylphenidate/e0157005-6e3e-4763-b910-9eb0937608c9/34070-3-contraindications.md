# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

## 4.1 Hypersensitivity to Methylphenidate or other Components of QUILLIVANT XR

QUILLIVANT XR is contraindicated in patients known to be hypersensitive to methylphenidate, or other components of QUILLIVANT XR.
Hypersensitivity reactions such as angioedema and anaphylactic reactions have been reported in patients treated with other methylphenidate products [see Adverse Reactions (6.2)].

## 4.2 Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors

QUILLIVANT XR is contraindicated during treatment with monoamine oxidase inhibitors, and also within 14 days following discontinuation of treatment with a monoamine oxidase inhibitor (MAOI), because of the risk of hypertensive crisis [see Drug Interactions (7.1)].
