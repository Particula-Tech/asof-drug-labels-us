# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

QUILLIVANT XR is indicated for the treatment of Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD).

The efficacy of QUILLIVANT XR was established in a 2-week, placebo-controlled, laboratory classroom, crossover study in children aged 6-12 years with a diagnosis of ADHD.
Patients in the trial met DSM-IV-TR® criteria for ADHD.
Accumulated efficacy data from other methylphenidate products were also considered [see Clinical Studies (14)].
