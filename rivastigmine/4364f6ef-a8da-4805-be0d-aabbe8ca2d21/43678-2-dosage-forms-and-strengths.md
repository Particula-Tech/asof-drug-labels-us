# 3 DOSAGE FORMS & STRENGTHS

## 3.1 Rivastigmine tartrate Capsules

Capsules, containing rivastigmine tartrate equivalent to 1.5 mg, 3 mg, 4.5 mg, or 6 mg of rivastigmine base, are available as follows: 1.5 mg capsule – Yellow/yellow, "CL7" on cap and "1.5 mg" on body imprinted with red ink.
3 mg capsule – Orange/orange, "CL8" on cap and "3 mg" on body imprinted with red ink.
4.5 mg capsule – Red/red, "CL9" on cap and "4.5 mg" on body imprinted with white ink.
6 mg capsule – Red/orange, "CL10" on cap and "6 mg" on body imprinted with white ink.
