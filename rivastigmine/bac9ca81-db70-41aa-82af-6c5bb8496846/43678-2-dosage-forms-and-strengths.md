# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

## 3.1 Rivastigmine Tartrate Capsules

Capsules, containing rivastigmine tartrate equivalent to 1.5 mg, 3 mg, 4.5 mg, or 6 mg of rivastigmine base, are available as follows:

1.5 mg Capsule – yellow, “145” is printed in black on the body and cap of the capsule.

3 mg Capsule – orange, “146” is printed in black on the body and cap of the capsule.

4.5 mg Capsule – orange, “147” is printed in black on the body and cap of the capsule.

6 mg Capsule – orange, “148” is printed in black on the body and cap of the capsule.
