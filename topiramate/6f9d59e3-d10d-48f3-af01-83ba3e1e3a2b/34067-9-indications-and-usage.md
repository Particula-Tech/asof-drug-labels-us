# INDICATIONS AND USAGE

## Monotherapy Epilepsy:

Topiramate capsules (sprinkle) are indicated as initial monotherapy in patients 10 years of age and older with partial onset or primary generalized tonic-clonic seizures.

Effectiveness was demonstrated in a controlled trial in patients with epilepsy who had no more than 2 seizures in the 3 months prior to enrollment.
Safety and effectiveness in patients who were converted to monotherapy from a previous regimen of other anticonvulsant drugs have not been established in controlled trials.

## Adjunctive Therapy Epilepsy:

Topiramate capsules (sprinkle) are indicated as adjunctive therapy for adults and pediatric patients ages 2 to16 years with partial onset seizures, or primary generalized tonic-clonic seizures, and in patients 2 years of age and older with seizures associated with Lennox-Gastaut syndrome.
