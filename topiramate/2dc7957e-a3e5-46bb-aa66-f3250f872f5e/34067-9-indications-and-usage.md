# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

## 1.1 Monotherapy Epilepsy

TROKENDI XR ®is indicated as initial monotherapy for the treatment of partial-onset or primary generalized tonic-clonic seizures in patients 6 years of age and older [see Clinical Studies (14.2)].

## 1.2 Adjunctive Therapy Epilepsy

TROKENDI XR ®is indicated as adjunctive therapy for the treatment of partial-onset seizures, primary generalized tonic-clonic seizures, and seizures associated with Lennox-Gastaut syndrome in patients 6 years of age and older [see Clinical Studies (14.3)].

## 1.3 Migraine

TROKENDI XR ®is indicated for the preventive treatment of migraine in patients 12 years of age and older [see Clinical Studies (14.4)].
