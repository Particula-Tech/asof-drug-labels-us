# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

TROKENDI XR is contraindicated in patients with:

- recent alcohol use (i.e., within 6 hours prior to and 6 hours after TROKENDI XR use) [see Warnings and Precautions (5.5)].
- a history of hypersensitivity reaction to topiramate, TROKENDI XR, or any of the inactive ingredients of TROKENDI XR.
  Anaphylaxis and angioedema have occurred [see Warnings and Precautions (5.14)].
