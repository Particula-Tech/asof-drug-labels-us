# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

TROKENDI XR ® is contraindicated in patients:

- With recent alcohol use (i.e., within 6 hours prior to and 6 hours after TROKENDI XR ® use) [see Warnings and Precautions (5.5)]
- With metabolic acidosis who are taking concomitant metformin [see Warnings and Precautions (5.4) and Drug Interactions (7.6)]
