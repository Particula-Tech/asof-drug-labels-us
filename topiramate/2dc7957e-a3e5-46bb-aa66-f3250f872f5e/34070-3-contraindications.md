# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Trokendi XR™ is contraindicated in patients:

- With recent alcohol use (i.e., within 6 hours prior to and 6 hours after Trokendi XR™ use) [see Warnings and Precautions (5.
  4)]
- With metabolic acidosis who are taking concomitant metformin [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3) and Drug Interactions (7.6)]
