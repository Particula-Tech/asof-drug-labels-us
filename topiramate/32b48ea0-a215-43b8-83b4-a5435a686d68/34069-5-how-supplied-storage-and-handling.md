# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Topiramate Tablets Topiramate Tablets, 25 mg are white, circular, biconvex, film-coated tablets debossed with ‘E’ on one side and ‘22’ on the other side.
Bottles of 60 NDC 65862-171-60 Topiramate Tablets, 50 mg are light yellow colored, circular, biconvex, film-coated tablets debossed with ‘E’ on one side and ‘33’ on the other side.
Bottles of 60 NDC 65862-172-60 Topiramate Tablets, 100 mg are dark yellow colored, circular, biconvex, beveled edge, film-coated tablets debossed with ‘E’ on one side and ‘23’ on the other side.
Bottles of 60 NDC 65862-173-60 Topiramate Tablets, 200 mg are pink colored, circular, biconvex, beveled edge, film-coated tablets debossed with ‘E’ on one side and ‘24’ on the other side.
Bottles of 60 NDC 65862-174-60 Storage and Handling Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from moisture.
