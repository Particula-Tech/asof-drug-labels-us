# 11 DESCRIPTION

Topiramate is a sulfamate-substituted monosaccharide.
Topiramate Tablets USP are available as 25 mg, 50 mg, 100 mg, and 200 mg round tablets for oral administration.

Topiramate USP is a white crystalline powder with a taste Topiramate USP is most soluble in alkaline solutions containing sodium hydroxide or sodium phosphate and having a pH of 9 to 10.
It is freely soluble in acetone, chloroform, dimethylsulfoxide, and ethanol.
The solubility in water is 9.8 mg/mL.
Its saturated solution has a pH of 6.3.
Topiramate USP has the molecular formula C12H21NO8S and a molecular weight of 339.37.
