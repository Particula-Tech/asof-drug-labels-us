# 9 DRUG ABUSE AND DEPENDENCE

## 9.1 Controlled Substance

Topiramate Extended-Release Capsules are not a controlled substance.

## 9.2 Abuse

The abuse and dependence potential of Topiramate Extended-Release Capsules have not been evaluated in human studies.

## 9.3 Dependence

Topiramate Extended-Release Capsules have not been systematically studied in animals or humans for its potential for tolerance or physical dependence.
