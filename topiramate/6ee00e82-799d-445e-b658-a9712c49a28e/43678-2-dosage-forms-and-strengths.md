# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Topiramate tablets, USP are available as round, biconvex, film-coated tablets in the following strengths and colors:

25 mg: Light yellow with 'U' debossed on one side and '115' debossed on other side.

50 mg: White to off-white, bevel edged with 'U' debossed on one side and '116' debossed on other side.

100 mg: Salmon, bevel edged with 'U' debossed on one side and '117' debossed on other side.

200 mg: Yellow, bevel edged with 'U' debossed on one side and "118' debossed on other side.
