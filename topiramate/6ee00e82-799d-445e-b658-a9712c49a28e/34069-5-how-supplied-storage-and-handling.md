# 16. HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Topiramate Tablets USP are available as:

25 mg Tablet:

Light yellow, round, biconvex, film-coated tablets with 'U' debossed on one side and '115' debossed on other side.

Bottles of 60: NDC 29300-115-16

Bottles of 100: NDC 29300-115-01

Bottles of 1000: NDC 29300-115-10

50 mg Tablet:

White to off-white, round, bevel edged, biconvex, film-coated tablets with 'U' debossed on one side and '116' debossed on other side.

Bottles of 60: NDC 29300-116-16

Bottles of 100: NDC 29300-116-01

Bottles of 1000: NDC 29300-116-10

100 mg Tablet:

Salmon, round, bevel edged, biconvex, film-coated tablets with 'U' debossed on one side and '117' debossed on other side.

Bottles of 60: NDC 29300-117-16

Bottles of 100: NDC 29300-117-01

Bottles of 1000: NDC 29300-117-10

200 mg Tablet:

Yellow, round, bevel edged, biconvex, film-coated tablets with 'U' debossed on one side and '118' debossed on other side.

Bottles of 60: NDC 29300-118-16

Bottles of 100: NDC 29300-118-01

Bottles of 1000: NDC 29300-118-10

Storage and Handling

Store at 200-250C (680-770F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from moisture.

Dispense in a tight, light–resistant container.
