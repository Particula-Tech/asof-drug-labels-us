# 11 DESCRIPTION

EPRONTIA (topiramate) oral solution is available as a 25 mg/mL solution for oral administration.

Topiramate has the molecular formula C 12H 21NO 8S and a molecular weight of 339.36.

Topiramate is a white crystalline powder with a bitter taste.
Topiramate is a sulfamate-substituted monosaccharide.
Topiramate is most soluble in alkaline solutions containing sodium hydroxide or sodium phosphate and having a pH of 9 to 10.
It is freely soluble in acetone, chloroform, dimethylsulfoxide, and ethanol.
The solubility in water is 9.8 mg/mL.
Its saturated solution has a pH of 6.3.

EPRONTIA oral solution is colorless to slightly yellow colored clear viscous liquid.
EPRONTIA contains the following inactive ingredients: glycerin, methylparaben, mixed berry flavor, polyethylene glycol, propylparaben, and sucralose.
