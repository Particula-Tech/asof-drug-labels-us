# DESCRIPTION

Twenty-eight (28) white tablets each containing 90 mcg of levonorgestrel, USP (17α)-(–)13-ethyl-17-hydroxy-18, 19-dinorpregn-4-en-20-yn-3-one, a totally synthetic progestogen, and 20 mcg of ethinyl estradiol, USP (17α)-19-norpregna-1,3,5(10)-trien-20-yne-3,17-diol.
The inactive ingredients present are croscarmellose sodium, lactose monohydrate, magnesium stearate, microcrystalline cellulose, and povidone.
