# DESCRIPTION

Twenty-eight (28) green tablets each containing 90 mcg of levonorgestrel (17α)-(–)13-ethyl-17-hydroxy-18, 19-dinorpregn-4-en-20-yn-3-one, a totally synthetic progestogen, and 20 mcg of ethinyl estradiol, (17α)-19-norpregna-1,3,5(10)-trien-20-yne-3,17-diol.
The inactive ingredients present are titanium dioxide, macrogol/PEG 3000 NF, talc, polyvinyl alcohol, lecithin (soya), FD&C Blue #2 Aluminum Lake, FD&C Yellow #5 Aluminum Lake, FD&C Red #40 Aluminum Lake, lactose monohydrate, magnesium stearate and pregelatinized starch.
