# HOW SUPPLIED

Levonorgestrel and ethinyl estradiol tablets USP (0.09 mg levonorgestrel USP and 0.02 mg ethinyl estradiol USP) are brownish peach to light brown, round, biconvex, film-coated tablets debossed with ‘E8’ on one side and are available as follows:

1. NDC 68462-637-29 1 carton containing 3 individual aluminum pouches.
   Inside each aluminum pouch is 1 blister card containing 28 active tablets.

Store at 20o to 25oC (68° to 77oF); excursions permitted to 15º to 30oC (59º to 86oF) [See USP controlled room temperature].
