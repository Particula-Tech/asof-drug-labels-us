# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

STELARA™ does not contain preservatives.
STELARA™ is available in single-use vials containing 45 mg or 90 mg of ustekinumab.

The NDC number for the 45 mg vial is 57894-060-02.

The NDC number for the 90 mg vial is 57894-061-02.

Storage and Stability

Store STELARA™ upright and refrigerated at 2ºC to 8ºC (36ºF to 46ºF).
Keep the product in the original carton to protect from light until the time of use.
Do not freeze.
Do not shake.
STELARA™ does not contain a preservative; discard any unused portion.
