# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

STELARA™ solution is colorless to slightly yellow in appearance and contains 90 mg ustekinumab per mL.

- 45 mg/0.5 mL in a single-use prefilled syringe
- 90 mg/1 mL in a single-use prefilled syringe
- 45 mg/0.5 mL in a single-use vial
- 90 mg/1 mL in a single-use vial
