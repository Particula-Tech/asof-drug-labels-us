# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Tablets:

- sitagliptin 50 mg and metformin HCl 500 mg tablets are white to off-white, oval shaped, biconvex, film coated tablets debossed with "1786" on one side and plain on the other side.
- sitagliptin 50 mg and metformin HCl 1,000 mg tablets are reddish brown, oval shaped, biconvex, film coated tablets debossed with "1787" on one side and plain on the other side.
