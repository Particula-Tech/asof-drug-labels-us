# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

- 25 mg tablets are light pink, round, film-coated tablets debossed with “TV” on one side and “2Y” on the other side
- 50 mg tablets are light brown, round, film-coated tablets debossed with “TV” on one side and “3Y” on the other side
- 100 mg tablets are light brown, round, film-coated tablets debossed with “TV” on one side and “7Y” on the other side.
