# 11 DESCRIPTION

Sitagliptin tablets, USP contain sitagliptin phosphate, USP an orally-active inhibitor of the dipeptidyl peptidase- 4 (DPP-4) enzyme.

Sitagliptin phosphate monohydrate is described chemically as 7-[(3R)-3-amino-1-oxo-4-(2,4,5- trifluorophenyl)butyl]-5,6,7,8-tetrahydro-3-(trifluoromethyl)-1,2,4-triazolo[4,3-a]pyrazine phosphate (1:1) monohydrate.

The molecular formula is C16H15F6N5O•H3PO4•H2O and the molecular weight is 523.32.

Sitagliptin phosphate monohydrate is a white to off-white, crystalline, non-hygroscopic powder.
It is soluble in water and N,N-dimethyl formamide; slightly soluble in methanol; very slightly soluble in ethanol, acetone, and acetonitrile; and insoluble in isopropanol and isopropyl acetate.

Each film-coated tablet of sitagliptin contains 32.13 mg, 64.25 mg, or 128.5 mg of sitagliptin phosphate monohydrate, which is equivalent to 25 mg, 50 mg, or 100 mg, respectively, of free base and the following inactive ingredients: anhydrous dibasic calcium phosphate, croscarmellose sodium, magnesium stearate, microcrystalline cellulose, and sodium stearyl fumarate.
In addition, the film coating contains the following inactive ingredients: polyethylene glycol 3350, polyvinyl alcohol-part hydrolyzed, red iron oxide, talc, titanium dioxide, and yellow iron oxide.
