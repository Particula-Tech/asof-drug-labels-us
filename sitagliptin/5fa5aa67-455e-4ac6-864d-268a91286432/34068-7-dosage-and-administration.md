# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

## 2.1 Recommended Dosing

The recommended dose of sitagliptin tablets is 100 mg once daily.
Sitagliptin tablets can be taken with or without food.

## 2.2 Recommendations for Use in Renal Impairment

Assess renal function prior to initiation of sitagliptin tablets and periodically thereafter.

For patients with an estimated glomerular filtration rate [eGFR] greater than or equal to 45 mL/min/1.73 m2 to less than 90 mL/min/1.73 m2, no dosage adjustment for sitagliptin tablets is required.

For patients with moderate renal impairment (eGFR greater than or equal to 30 mL/min/1.73 m2 to less than 45 mL/min/1.73 m2), the dose of sitagliptin tablets is 50 mg once daily.

For patients with severe renal impairment (eGFR less than 30 mL/min/1.73 m2) or with end-stage renal disease (ESRD) requiring hemodialysis or peritoneal dialysis, the dose of sitagliptin tablets is 25 mg once daily.
Sitagliptin may be administered without regard to the timing of dialysis.
