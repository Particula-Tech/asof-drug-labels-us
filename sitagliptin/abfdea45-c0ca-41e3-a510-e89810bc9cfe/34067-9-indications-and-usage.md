# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

ZITUVIO® is indicated as an adjunct to diet and exercise to improve glycemic control in adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus.

Limitations of Use

ZITUVIO is not recommended in patients with type 1 diabetes mellitus.

ZITUVIO has not been studied in patients with a history of pancreatitis.
It is unknown whether patients with a history of pancreatitis are at increased risk for the development of pancreatitis while using ZITUVIO.
[see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
