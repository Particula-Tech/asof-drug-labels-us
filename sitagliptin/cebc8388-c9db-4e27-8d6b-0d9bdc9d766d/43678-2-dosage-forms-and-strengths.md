# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

1. •100 mg tablets are brown, round, biconvex, film-coated tablets debossed with “SN” on one side and “100” on the other side.
2. •50 mg tablets are pink, round, biconvex, film-coated tablets debossed with “SN” on one side and “50” on the other side.
3. •25 mg tablets are dark pink, round, biconvex, film-coated tablets debossed with “SN” on one side and “25” on the other side.
