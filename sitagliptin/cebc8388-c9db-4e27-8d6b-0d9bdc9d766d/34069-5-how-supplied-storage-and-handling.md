# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Sitagliptin tablets, USP are available as follows:

25 mg tablets are dark pink, round, biconvex, film-coated tablets debossed with “SN” on one side and “25” on the other side.

NDC 0781-5766-31, 30 tablets in one bottle

NDC 0781-5766-92, 90 tablets in one bottle

50 mg tablets are pink, round, biconvex, film-coated tablets debossed with “SN” on one side and “50” on the other side.

NDC 0781-5768-31, 30 tablets in one bottle

NDC 0781-5768-92, 90 tablets in one bottle

100 mg tablets are brown, round, biconvex, film-coated tablets debossed with “SN” on one side and “100” on the other side.

NDC 0781-5770-31, 30 tablets in one bottle

NDC 0781-5770-92, 90 tablets in one bottle

NDC 0781-5770-10, 1000 tablets in one bottle

Storage

Store at 20ºC to 25ºC (68ºF to 77ºF); excursions permitted to 15ºC to 30ºC (69ºF to 86ºF) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container.

KEEP OUT OF REACH OF CHILDREN.
