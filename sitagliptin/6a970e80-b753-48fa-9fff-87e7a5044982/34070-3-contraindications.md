# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Sitagliptin Tablets is contraindicated in patients with a history of a serious hypersensitivity reaction to sitagliptin or any of the excipients in Sitagliptin Tablets.
Serious hypersensitivity reactions, including anaphylaxis and angioedema have been reported with sitagliptin.
[see Warnings and Precautions (5.5); Adverse Reactions (6.2)].
