# 11 DESCRIPTION

OPIPZA contains aripiprazole, an atypical antipsychotic drug.
Aripiprazole is 7-[4-[4-(2,3-dichlorophenyl)-1-piperazinyl]butoxy]-3,4-dihydrocarbostyril.
Aripiprazole has a pKa of 6.2.
It is freely soluble in dichloromethane, sparingly soluble in toluene, and insoluble in methanol and water.
The empirical formula is C23H27Cl2N3O2 with a molecular weight of 448.38.

OPIPZA is for oral administration and is available in 2 mg, 5 mg, and 10 mg strengths.
