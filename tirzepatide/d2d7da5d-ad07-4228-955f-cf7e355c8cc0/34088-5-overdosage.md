# 10 OVERDOSAGE

In the event of an overdosage, consider contacting the Poison Help line (1-800-222-1222) or a medical toxicologist for additional overdosage management recommendations.
Initiate appropriate supportive treatment according to the patient's clinical signs and symptoms.
A period of observation and treatment for these symptoms may be necessary, taking into account the half-life of tirzepatide of approximately 5 days.
