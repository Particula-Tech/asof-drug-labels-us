# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Sevelamer carbonate 800 mg tablets are supplied as off-white, film coated, modified capsule shaped, biconvex tablets de-bossed with “I” on one side and “8” on the other side of the tablet.

Bottle of 270 (NDC 69097-967-93)

Storage: Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[See USP controlled room temperature].

Protect from moisture.

Dispense in tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP.
