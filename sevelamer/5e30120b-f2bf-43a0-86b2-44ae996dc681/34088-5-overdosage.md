# 10 OVERDOSAGE

Renagel has been given to normal healthy volunteers in doses of up to 14 grams per day for eight days with no adverse effects.
Renagel has been given in average doses up to 13 grams per day to hemodialysis patients.
There are no reports of overdosage with Renagel in patients.
Since Renagel is not absorbed, the risk of systemic toxicity is low.
