# ADVERSE REACTIONS

Side effects reported with the administration of hydroxyzine pamoate are usually mild and transitory in nature.

## Anticholinergic:

Dry mouth.

## Central Nervous System:

Drowsiness is usually transitory and may disappear in a few days of continued therapy or upon reduction of the dose.
Involuntary motor activity, including rare instances of tremor and convulsions, has been reported, usually with doses considerably higher than those recommended.
Clinically significant respiratory depression has not been reported at recommended doses.

In post-marketing experience, the following additional undesirable effects have been reported:

## Body as a Whole:

allergic reaction.

## Nervous System:

headache.

## Psychiatric:

hallucination.

## Skin and Appendages:

pruritus, rash, urticaria.
