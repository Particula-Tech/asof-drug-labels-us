Hydroxyzine hydrochloride is designated chemically as (±)2-[2-[4-(p-Chloro-α-phenylbenzyl)-1-piperazinyl]ethoxy]ethanol dihydrochloride.

Inactive Ingredients for Hydroxyzine Hydrochloride Oral Solution, USP (Syrup) are: alcohol 0.5% (v/v), citric acid anhydrous, purified water, sodium benzoate, sodium citrate, sucrose.
It may also contain sodium hydroxide for pH adjustment.
