# HOW SUPPLIED

Hydroxyzine Pamoate Capsules USP 25 mg (equivalent to 25 mg hydroxyzine hydrochloride) are light green/dark green capsules imprinted “E 613” available in bottles of 100, 250, 500 and 1000.

Hydroxyzine Pamoate Capsules USP 50 mg (equivalent to 50 mg hydroxyzine hydrochloride) are dark green/white capsules imprinted “E 615” available in bottles of 100, 500 and 1000.

## Storage

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from moisture.
Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP, with a child-resistant closure (as required).
