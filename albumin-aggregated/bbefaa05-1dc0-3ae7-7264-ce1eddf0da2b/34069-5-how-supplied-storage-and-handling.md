# HOW SUPPLIED

DRAXIMAGE® MAA (NDC 65174.270.30) comprises:

- 30 multi-dose glass vials.
  Each vial contains a non-radioactive sterile, non-pyrogenic lyophilized mixture of: 2.5 mg of albumin aggregated, 5 mg of human serum albumin, 0.06 mg (minimum) of stannous chloride (maximum stannous and stannic chloride 0.11 mg), and 1.2 mg of sodium chloride;
- 30 Radiation Labels;
- 1 Package Insert.

HCl and/or NaOH has been used for pH adjustment.
The vials are sealed under an atmosphere of nitrogen.
