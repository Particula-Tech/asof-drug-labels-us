# 15REFERENCES

1. Method of calculation: “S” Absorbed Dose per Unit Cumulated Activity for Selected Radionuclides and Organs, MIRD Pamphlet No. 11 (1975).
2. Used biologic data from Kaul et al., Berlin, 1973.
3. For the newborn, 1-year old, and 5-year old, the “S” values calculated form the preliminary phantoms of ORNL were used.
   The 10-year old, 15-year old and adult “S” values are from Henrichs, et al., Berlin, 1980.
