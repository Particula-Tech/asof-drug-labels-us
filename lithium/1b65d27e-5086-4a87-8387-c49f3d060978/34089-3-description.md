# DESCRIPTION

Lithium carbonate extended-release tablets USP contain lithium carbonate, a white odorless alkaline powder with molecular formula Li2CO3 and molecular weight 73.89.
Lithium is an element of the alkali metal group with atomic number 3, atomic weight 6.94 and an emission line at 671 nm on the flame photometer.
Each light pink to pink colored circular, biconvex, film-coated, extended-release tablet contains 300 mg of lithium carbonate.
This slowly dissolving film-coated tablet is designed to give lower serum lithium peak concentrations than obtained with conventional oral lithium dosage forms.
Inactive ingredients consist of sodium chloride, polyvinyl pyrrolidone, sorbitol, sodium starch glycolate, sodium lauryl sulfate, calcium stearate VG, opadry pink 03B54202, isopropyl alcohol.Opadry pink 03B54202 consists of the following components: HPMC 2910, titanium dioxide, polyethylene glycol 400 and iron oxide red.

Product meets USP Dissolution Test 1.
