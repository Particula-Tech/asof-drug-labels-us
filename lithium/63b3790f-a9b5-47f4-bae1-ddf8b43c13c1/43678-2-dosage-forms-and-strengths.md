# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Each 150 mg capsule for oral administration contains: lithium carbonate, USP 150 mg and is two piece light gray hard gelatin capsules (size ‘4’) imprinted with ‘150’ on body & ‘G220’ on cap.

Each 300 mg capsule for oral administration contains: lithium carbonate, USP 300 mg and is a two piece pink hard gelatin capsules (size ‘2’) imprinted with ‘300’ on body and ‘G221’ on cap.

Each 600 mg capsule for oral administration contains: lithium carbonate, USP 600 mg and is two piece hard gelatin capsules (size ‘0’ elongated) pink colored cap and light gray colored body imprinted with ‘600’ on body and ‘G222’ on cap.
