# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Each 150 mg capsule for oral administration contains: lithium carbonate USP 150 mg and is a two piece light gray hard gelatin capsules (size ‘4’) imprinted with ‘150’ on the body & ‘G220’ on the cap.

Each 300 mg capsule for oral administration contains: lithium carbonate USP 300 mg and is a two piece pink hard gelatin capsules (size ‘2’) imprinted with ‘300’ on the body and ‘G221’ on the cap.

Each 600 mg capsule for oral administration contains: lithium carbonate USP 600 mg and is a two piece hard gelatin capsules (size ‘0’ elongated) pink colored cap and light gray colored body imprinted with ‘600’ on the body and ‘G222’ on the cap.
