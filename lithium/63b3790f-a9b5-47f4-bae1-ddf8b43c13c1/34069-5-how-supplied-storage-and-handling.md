# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Lithium Carbonate Capsules, USP

Each 150 mg capsule for oral administration contains: lithium carbonate, USP, 150 mg and is two piece light gray hard gelatin capsules (size ‘4’) imprinted with ‘150’ on body & ‘G220’ on cap.

NDC 68462-220-11: Unit-dose box of 100 (10 blister cards each containing 10 capsules).

NDC 68462-220-01: Bottles of 100 capsules.

Each 300 mg capsule for oral administration contains: lithium carbonate, USP, 300 mg and is a two piece pink hard gelatin capsules (size ‘2’) imprinted with ‘300’ on body and ‘G221’ on cap.

NDC 68462-221-11: Unit-dose box of 100 (10 blister cards each containing 10 capsules).

NDC 68462-221-01: Bottles of 100 capsules.

NDC 68462-221-10: Bottles of 1000 capsules.

Each 600 mg capsule for oral administration contains: lithium carbonate, USP, 600 mg and is two piece hard gelatin capsules (size ‘0’ elongated) pink colored cap and light gray colored body imprinted with ‘600’ on body and ‘G222’ on cap.

NDC 68462-222-11: Unit-dose box of 100 (10 blister cards each containing 10 capsules).

NDC 68462-222-01: Bottles of 100 capsules

Storage

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]
Protect from moisture.
Dispense in a tight, child-resistant container as defined in the USP/NF.
