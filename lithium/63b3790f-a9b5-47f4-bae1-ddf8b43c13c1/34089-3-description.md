# 11 DESCRIPTION

Lithium is an element of the alkali-metal group with atomic weight of 6.94.

Lithium Carbonate, USP is a white, light, alkaline powder with molecular formula Li2CO3 and molecular weight 73.89.

Each capsule contains 150 mg, 300 mg, or 600 mg lithium carbonate, USP and the following inactive ingredients: colloidal silicon dioxide, gelatin, sodium lauryl sulfate, talc, titanium dioxide, FD&C Blue No. 1, FD&C Red No. 40, D&C Yellow No. 10 and the imprinting ink contains shellac, propylene glycol, potassium hydroxide, and black iron oxide.
