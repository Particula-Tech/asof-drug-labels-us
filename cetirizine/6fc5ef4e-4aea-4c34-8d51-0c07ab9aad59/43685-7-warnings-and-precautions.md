# 5 WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS

## 5.1 Contamination of Tip and Solution

As with any eye drop, care should be taken not to touch the eyelids or surrounding areas with the tip of the single-use container in order to avoid injury to the eye and to prevent contaminating the tip and solution.
Discard the single-use container after using in each eye.

## 5.2 Contact Lens Wear

Patients should be advised not to wear a contact lens if their eye is red.

ZERVIATE ® should not be instilled while wearing contact lenses.
Remove contact lenses prior to instillation of ZERVIATE ®.
The preservative in ZERVIATE ®, benzalkonium chloride, may be absorbed by soft contact lenses.
Lenses may be reinserted 10 minutes following administration of ZERVIATE ®.
