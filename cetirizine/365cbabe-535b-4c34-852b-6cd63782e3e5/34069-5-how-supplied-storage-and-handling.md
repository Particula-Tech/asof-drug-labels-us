# HOW SUPPLIED

Cetirizine hydrochloride oral solution, USP is a clear, colorless to pale yellow solution with a grape flavor.
Each teaspoonful (5 mL) contains 5 mg cetirizine hydrochloride, USP.

Cetirizine hydrochloride oral solution, USP is available as:

4 fl. oz (118 mL): NDC 65162-684-86

16 fl. oz (473 mL): NDC 65162-684-90

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F); [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Dispense in tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP.

KEEP THIS AND ALL DRUGS OUT OF THE REACH OF CHILDREN.
