# DESCRIPTION

Cetirizine hydrochloride, USP is an orally active and selective H 1-receptor antagonist.
The chemical name is (±) - [2- [4- [(4-chlorophenyl)phenylmethyl] -1- piperazinyl] ethoxy]acetic acid, dihydrochloride.
Cetirizine hydrochloride is a racemic compound with a molecular formula of C 21H 25ClN 2O 3•2HCl.

Cetirizine hydrochloride, USP is a white, crystalline powder and is water soluble.
Cetirizine hydrochloride oral solution, USP is a colorless to slightly yellow oral solution containing cetirizine hydrochloride at a concentration of 1 mg/mL (5 mg/5 mL) for oral administration.
The pH is between 4 and 5.
The inactive ingredients of the oral solution are: glacial acetic acid, glycerin, grape flavor, methylparaben, propylene glycol, propylparaben, purified water, sodium acetate, and sucrose.
