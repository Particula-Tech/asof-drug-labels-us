# DESCRIPTION

Cetirizine hydrochloride is an orally active and selective H1-receptor antagonist.
The chemical name is (±) - [2- [4- [(4-chlorophenyl)phenylmethyl] -1- piperazinyl] ethoxy]acetic acid, dihydrochloride.
Cetirizine hydrochloride is a racemic compound with an empirical formula of C21H25ClN2O3•2HCl.

Cetirizine hydrochloride is a white, crystalline powder and is water soluble.
Cetirizine hydrochloride oral solution, USP is a clear, colorless to slightly yellow solution containing cetirizine hydrochloride at a concentration of 1 mg/mL (5 mg/5 mL) for oral administration.
The pH is between 4 and 5.
The inactive ingredients of the oral solution are: glacial acetic acid; glycerin; methylparaben; propylene glycol; propylparaben; sodium acetate; sucrose; and water.
