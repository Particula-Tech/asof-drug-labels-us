# HOW SUPPLIED

Cetirizine Hydrochloride Oral Solution, USP is colorless to slightly yellow and available in fruity or grape flavor.
Each teaspoon (5 mL) contains 5 mg cetirizine hydrochloride.
Cetirizine Hydrochloride Oral Solution, USP is supplied as follows:

NDC 62135-058-41 (Fruity Flavor) 120 mL bottles

NDC 62135-058-47 (Fruity Flavor) 473 mL bottles

NDC 62135-057-41 (Grape Flavor) 120 mL bottles

NDC 62135-057-47 (Grape Flavor) 473 mL bottles

STORAGE: Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
