# 5 WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS

## 5.1 Somnolence/Sedation

In clinical trials with QUZYTTIR and cetirizine hydrochloride tablets, the occurrence of somnolence/sedation has been reported in some patients.
Exercise due caution when driving a car or operating potentially dangerous machinery.
Avoid concurrent use of QUZYTTIR with alcohol or other CNS depressants because additional reduction in alertness and additional impairment of CNS performance may occur.
