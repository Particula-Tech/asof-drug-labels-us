| Read all of this Patient Information and Instructions for Use (see the other side of this Patient Information leaflet) before using MUSE as it contains important information. These materials provide visual instructions and more detailed information on how to use MUSE. |
| What is MUSE?MUSE is a prescription medicine used to treat erectile dysfunction, commonly called impotence. Erectile dysfunction is the inability to attain or maintain an erection enough for sexual intercourse. |
| Do not use MUSE if you: •are allergic to alprostadil (the active ingredient in MUSE).•have an abnormally formed penis, including narrowing of the urethra (urethral stricture), infection or swelling of the glans of the penis (balanitis), the opening of the urethra is not located at the tip of the penis (severe hypospadias), severe curvature of the penis, or short-term (acute) or long-lasting (chronic) swelling of the urethra.•have conditions that might result in long-lasting erections (priapism), such as sickle cell anemia or trait, the bone marrow makes too many platelets (thrombocythemia), the bone marrow makes too many red blood cells (polycythemia), or tumor of the bone marrow (multiple myeloma).•have been advised not to undertake sexual activity.•have sexual intercourse with a pregnant woman unless the couple uses a condom barrier. |
| Before you use MUSE, tell your healthcare provider about all of your medical conditions, including if: •you take anticoagulant medicines (medicines that prevent blood clots) or have a bleeding disorder.•you take blood pressure medicines.•you have a history of fainting.•your female partner is of childbearing potential. It is recommended that couples use adequate contraception while using MUSE. MUSE should not be used if the female partner is pregnant unless the couple uses a condom barrier. MUSE does not protect you or your partner from any sexually transmitted diseases (STDs). Latex condoms can protect against STDs. Tell your healthcare provider about all the medicines you take, including prescription and over-the-counter medicines, vitamins, and herbal supplements. |
| How should I use MUSE? •Use MUSE exactly as your healthcare provider tells you to use it.•You and your healthcare provider will decide the dose of MUSE that works best for you. Your healthcare provider may change your dose if needed to achieve the response that works best for you.•The MUSE applicator contained in each foil pouch is for a single dose (one-time use).•Do not use MUSE more than 2 times in a 24-hour period.•See the Instructions for Use that comes with MUSE for detailed instructions on the right way to use MUSE.•Be sure to straighten your penis to its full length when inserting the MUSE applicator.•After you use MUSE, the erection process will begin within 5 to 10 minutes and may last 30 to 60 minutes. However, the length of time that the erection lasts will differ from person to person. oIf your erection is rigid for 4 or more hours, call your healthcare provider right away. |
| What should I avoid while using MUSE? •Fainting can happen within 1 hour after using MUSE. Avoid activities such as driving or hazardous tasks where injury could result if dizziness or fainting were to occur after using MUSE. |
| What are the possible side effects of MUSE?The most serious side effects of MUSE include: •Prolonged erection. If your erection is rigid for 4 or more hours, call your healthcare provider right away.•Fainting The most common side effects of MUSE include: •Pain in the penis, urethra, or testicles.•Warmth or burning sensation in the urethra.•Redness of the penis. •Minor urethral bleeding or spotting.•Light-headedness or dizziness. If you do experience dizziness or feel faint, lie down right away and raise your legs. If symptoms continue, call your healthcare provider right away.•Flu-like symptoms.•Headache.•Back pain or pelvic pain.•Stuffy or runny nose (rhinitis).•Respiratory infection.•Body pain.•Swelling of leg veins.•Rapid pulse.•Pain in the legs or perineum (area between the penis and rectum). Tell your healthcare provider if you have any side effect that bothers you or that does not go away.These are not all the possible side effects of MUSE. For more information, ask your healthcare provider or pharmacist. Call your doctor for medical advice about side effects. You may report side effects to FDA at 1-800-FDA-1088. You may also report side effects to Meda Pharmaceuticals Inc. at 1-877-446-3679 (1-877-4-INFO-RX). |
| How should I store MUSE? •Store unopened MUSE foil pouches in a refrigerator between 36°F to 46°F (2°C to 8°C).•You may store MUSE at room temperature between 68°F to 77°F (20°C to 25°C), for up to 14 days before use. •Do not expose MUSE to temperatures above 86°F (30°C). Keep MUSE and all medicines out of the reach of children. |
| General information about the safe and effective use of MUSE.Medicines are sometimes prescribed for purposes other than those listed in a Patient Information leaflet. Do not use MUSE for a condition for which it was not prescribed. Do not give MUSE to other people, even if they have the same symptoms that you have. It may harm them. You can ask your pharmacist or healthcare provider for information about MUSE that is written for health professionals. |
