# STORAGE AND HANDLING

Store unopened foil pouches in a refrigerator at 2°– 8°C (36°– 46°F).
Do not expose MUSE to temperatures above 30°C (86°F).
MUSE may be kept by the patient at room temperature (below 30°C or 86°F) for up to 14 days prior to use.
