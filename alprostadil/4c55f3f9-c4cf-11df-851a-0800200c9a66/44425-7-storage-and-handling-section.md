# STORAGE AND HANDLING

Store unopened foil pouches in a refrigerator at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F).
Do not expose MUSE to temperatures above 30°C (86°F).
MUSE may be kept by the patient at room temperature, 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F), for up to 14 days before use.
