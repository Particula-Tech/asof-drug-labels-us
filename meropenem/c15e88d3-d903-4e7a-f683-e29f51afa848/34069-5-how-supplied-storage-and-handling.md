# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

MERREM I.V. is supplied in 20 mL and 30 mL injection vials containing sufficient meropenem to deliver 500 mg or 1 g for intravenous administration, respectively.
The dry powder should be stored at controlled room temperature 20-25ºC (68-77ºF) [see USP].

500 mg Injection Vial (NDC 0310-0325-20)

1 g Injection Vial (NDC 0310-0321-30)
