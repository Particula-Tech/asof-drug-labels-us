# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

MERREM IV is supplied in 20 mL and 30 mL injection vials containing sufficient meropenem to deliver 500 mg or 1 gram for intravenous administration, respectively.
The dry powder should be stored at controlled room temperature 20°–25°C (68°–77°F) [see USP].

- 500 mg Injection Vial (NDC 0310-0325-20)
- 1 gram Injection Vial (NDC 0310-0321-30)
