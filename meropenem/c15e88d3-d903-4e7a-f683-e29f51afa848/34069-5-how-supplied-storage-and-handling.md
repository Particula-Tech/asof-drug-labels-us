# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

MERREM IV is supplied in 20 mL and 30 mL injection vials containing sufficient meropenem to deliver 500 mg or 1 gram for intravenous administration, respectively.
The dry powder should be stored at controlled room temperature 20º-25ºC (68º-77ºF) [see USP].

1. 500 mg Injection Vial (NDC 0310-0325-20)
2. 1 gram Injection Vial (NDC 0310-0321-30)
