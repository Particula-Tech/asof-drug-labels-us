# 10 OVERDOSAGE

In the event of overdose, discontinue VABOMERE and institute general supportive treatment.

Meropenem and vaborbactam can be removed by hemodialysis.
In subjects with end-stage renal disease (ESRD) administered meropenem 1 gram and vaborbactam 1 gram, the mean total recovery in dialysate following a hemodialysis session was 38% and 53% of the administered dose of meropenem and vaborbactam, respectively.

No clinical information is available on the use of hemodialysis to treat VABOMERE overdosage.
