# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Meropenem for Injection is supplied in 20 mL and 30 mL injection vials containing sufficient meropenem to deliver 500 mg or 1 gram for intravenous administration, respectively.
The dry powder should be stored at controlled room temperature 20º - 25º C (68º -77º F) [see USP].

- 500 mg Injection Vial (NDC 44647-032-10) 1 gram Injection Vial (NDC 44647-033-10)
