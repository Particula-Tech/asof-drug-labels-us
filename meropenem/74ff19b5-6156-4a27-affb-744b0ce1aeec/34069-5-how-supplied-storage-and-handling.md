# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Meropenem for Injection, USP is supplied in 20 mL and 30 mL injection vials containing sufficient meropenem to deliver 500 mg or 1 gram for intravenous administration, respectively.
The dry powder should be stored at controlled room temperature 20º to 25ºC (68º to 77ºF) [see USP].
