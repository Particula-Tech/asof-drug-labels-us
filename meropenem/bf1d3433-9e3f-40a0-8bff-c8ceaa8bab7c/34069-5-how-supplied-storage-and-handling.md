# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Meropenem for Injection, USP is a sterile, pyrogen-free, white to pale yellow crystalline powder and is supplied in vials containing sufficient meropenem to deliver 500 mg or 1 g for intravenous administration, respectively.

Meropenem for Injection, USP is available as follows: 500 mg/vial: 10 Vials in a carton NDC 55150-207-20 1 g/vial: 10 Vials in a carton NDC 55150-208-30

The dry powder should be stored at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
The vial stoppers are not made with natural rubber latex.
