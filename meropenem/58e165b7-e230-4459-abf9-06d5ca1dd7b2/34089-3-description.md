# 11 DESCRIPTION

Meropenem for Injection, USP is a sterile, pyrogen-free, synthetic, carbapenem antibacterial for intravenous administration.
It is (4R,5S,6S)-3- [[(3S,5S)-5-(Dimethylcarbamoyl)-3-pyrrolidinyl]thio]-6- [(1R)-1-hydroxyethyl]-4-methyl-7-oxo-1-azabicyclo[3.2.0]hept-2-ene-2-carboxylic acid trihydrate.
Its empirical formula is C 17H 25N 3O 5S•3H 2O with a molecular weight of 437.52.

Meropenem for injection, USP is a white to slight yellow crystalline powder.
The solution varies from colorless to yellow depending on the concentration.
The pH of freshly constituted solutions is between 7.3 and 8.3.
Meropenem is freely soluble in N,N-dimethylformamide; soluble in 5% dibasic potassium phosphate solution; sparingly soluble in water and 5% monobasic potassium phosphate solution; practically insoluble in dichloromethane, 96% ethanol, acetone, anhydrous ethanol and anhydrous ether.

When re-constituted as instructed, each 1 gram Meropenem for Injection, USP vial will deliver 1 gram of meropenem and 90.2 mg of sodium as sodium carbonate (3.92 mEq).
Each 500 mg Meropenem for Injection, USP vial will deliver 500 mg meropenem and 45.1 mg of sodium as sodium carbonate (1.96 mEq) [see Dosage and Administration (2.4)].
