# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Meropenem for injection, USP is supplied in 20 mL single-dose vials containing sufficient meropenem to deliver 500 mg or 1 gram for intravenous administration, respectively.
The dry powder should be stored at controlled room temperature 20º-25ºC (68º-77ºF) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
