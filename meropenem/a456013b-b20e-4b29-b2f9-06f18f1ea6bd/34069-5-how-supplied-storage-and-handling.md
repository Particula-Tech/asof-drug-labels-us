# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Meropenem for injection, USP (I.V.) is supplied in 20 mL and 30 mL injection vials containing sufficient meropenem to deliver 500 mg or 1 gram for intravenous administration, respectively.
The dry powder should be stored at controlled room temperature 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
