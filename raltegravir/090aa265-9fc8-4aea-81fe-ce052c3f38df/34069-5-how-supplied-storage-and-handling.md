# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Raltegravir tablets USP, 400 mg are white to off-white oval shaped film coated tablets debossed with “H I” on one side and “4” on other side of the tablet.
They are supplied as follows:

Bottles of 60 tablets (NDC 31722-584-60)

Storage and Handling

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) with excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
