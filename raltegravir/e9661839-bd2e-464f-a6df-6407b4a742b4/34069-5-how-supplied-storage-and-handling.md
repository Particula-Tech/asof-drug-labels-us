# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Raltegravir tablets USP, 600 mg are Yellow, modified capsule shaped, biconvex, film-coated tablets debossed with "LU" on one side and "J04" on the other side.

They are supplied as follows:

NDC 70748-327-07 unit-of-use bottles of 60.

Storage and Handling

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F and 86°F). see USP Controlled Room Temperature.
Store in the original package with the bottle tightly closed.
