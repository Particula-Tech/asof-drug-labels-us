# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

NASCOBAL is a nasal spray available in a dosage strength of 500 mcg cyanocobalamin/0.1 mL (per actuation).
It is supplied in boxes of 4 single-use nasal spray devices and a package insert (NDC 49884-270-82).

Protect from light.
Keep covered in carton until ready to use.
Store upright at controlled room temperature 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F).
Protect from freezing.
