# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

CaloMist (cyanocobalamin, USP) Nasal Spray is a solution of cyanocobalamin, USP, for administration as a metered spray to the nasal mucosa.
Each bottle of CaloMist contains 10.7 mL of a 25 mcg/0.1 mL solution of cyanocobalamin.
The spray solution has a pH between 6.5 and 7.5.
After initial priming, each spray delivers 25 mcg of cyanocobalamin.
Each bottle will deliver 60 sprays for a total of thirty 50 mcg doses of CaloMist.
