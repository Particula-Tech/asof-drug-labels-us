# DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

Rubramin PC (Cyanocobalamin Injection) should be visually inspected for particulate matter and color prior to administration; the solution is clear red.

Do not administer intravenously.

## Schilling Test*

The flushing dose is 1000 mcg injected intramuscularly.
For procedure see the insert packaged with Rubratope®-57 (Cyanocobalamin Co 57) capsules or diagnostic kit (see the DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION section).
