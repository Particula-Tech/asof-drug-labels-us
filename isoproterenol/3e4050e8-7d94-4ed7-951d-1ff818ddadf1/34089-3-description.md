# 11 DESCRIPTION

Isoproterenol hydrochloride injection, USP is 3,4-Dihydroxy-α-[(isopropylamino)methyl] benzyl alcohol hydrochloride, a synthetic sympathomimetic amine that is structurally related to epinephrine but acts almost exclusively on beta receptors.
Isoproterenol hydrochloride is white to practically white, crystalline powder and the molecular formula is C11H17NO3 ·HCl.

| Sodium Chloride | 7 mg |
| Water for Injection | qs 1 mL |

The pH is adjusted between 2.5 and 4.5 with hydrochloric acid and/or sodium hydroxide.

The sterile solution is nonpyrogenic clear, colorless or practically colorless liquid free from visible particles and can be administered by the intravenous route.
