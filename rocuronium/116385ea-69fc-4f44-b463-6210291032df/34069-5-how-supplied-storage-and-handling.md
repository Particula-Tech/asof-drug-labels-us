# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Rocuronium bromide injection is a sterile, nonpyrogenic, isotonic, clear colorless to yellow or orange solution, free from visible particles and is supplied as follows: 50 mg per 5 mL (10 mg / mL) 5 mL Multiple Dose Vials in a Carton of 10 NDC 55150-225-05

100 mg per 10 mL (10 mg / mL)

10 mL Multiple Dose Vials in a Carton of 10 NDC 55150-226-10 Rocuronium bromide injection should be stored in a refrigerator, 2° to 8°C (36° to 46°F).
DO NOT FREEZE.
Upon removal from refrigeration to room temperature storage conditions (25°C/77°F), use rocuronium bromide injection within 60 days.
Use opened vials of rocuronium bromide injection within 30 days.
The vial stoppers are not made with natural rubber latex.
Safety and Handling: There is no specific work exposure limit for rocuronium bromide injection.
In case of eye contact, flush with water for at least 10 minutes.
