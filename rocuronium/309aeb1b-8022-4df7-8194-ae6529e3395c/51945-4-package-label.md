Storage: Rocuronium Bromide Injection should be stored under refrigeration, 2 to 8°C (36 to 46°F).
DO NOT FREEZE.
Upon removal from refrigeration to room temperature storage conditions (25°C/77°F), use Rocuronium Bromide Injection within 60 days.
Use opened vials of Rocuronium Bromide Injection within 30 days.

Sterile, nonpyrogenic.
Each mL contains: 10 mg rocuronium bromide, 2 mg sodium acetate.
Adjusted to isotonicity with 3.1 mg/mL NaCI and to a pH of 4 with acetic acid and/or NaOH.
