# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Rocuronium Bromide Injection is a clear, colorless to yellow/orange solution, free from visible particulate matter and available in single-dose vials and single-dose prefilled syringes in the following packaging configurations:

| RF762250 | NDC 76045-221-50 Unit of 10 | 50 mg per 5 mL (10 mg per mL) | NDC 76045-221-05 Single-Dose Prefilled Syringe This product is RFID-enabled. |
| 762250 | NDC 76045-220-50 Unit of 10 | 50 mg per 5 mL (10 mg per mL) | NDC 76045-220-05 Single-Dose Prefilled Syringe |

The container closure is not made with natural rubber latex.

Rocuronium Bromide Injection should be stored at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); Excursion permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F and 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
DO NOT FREEZE.

Safety and Handling

There is no specific work exposure limit for Rocuronium Bromide Injection.
In case of eye contact, flush with water for at least 10 minutes.

Single-Dose Vial and Single-Dose Prefilled Syringe.
Discard Unused Portion.
