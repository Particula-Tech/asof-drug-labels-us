INSTRUCTIONS FOR USE

NOTES:

- Do not introduce any other fluid into the syringe at any time.
- Do not dilute for intravenous push.
- Do not re-sterilize the syringe.
- Do not use this product on a sterile field.
- This product is for single dose only.

1. Visually inspect the syringe.
   Parenteral drug products should be inspected visually for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration, whenever solution and container permit.
   Do not use if the prefilled syringe has been damaged.
2. Twist the tamper evident (TE) cap to remove.
   The syringe tip cap should remain attached.
   (Figure 2)
3. Push plunger rod slightly to break the stopper loose while syringe tip cap is still on.
4. Remove and discard the syringe tip cap (Figure 3).
5. Expel air bubble(s).
   Adjust the dose (if applicable).
6. Administer the dose.
   Discard the used syringe into an appropriate receptacle.
