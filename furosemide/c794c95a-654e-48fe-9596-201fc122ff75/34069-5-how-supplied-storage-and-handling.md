# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Store at room temperature 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F and 86°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]
Protect from light.
