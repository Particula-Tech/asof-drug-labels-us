# HOW SUPPLIED

Furosemide Tablets, USP 20 mg are available as white, round, flat face, beveled edge, tablets debossed “ CE” on one side and “ 174” on the other side, containing 20 mg of Furosemide, USP and supplied as follows:

NDC 62135-753-90 in bottles of 90 tablets

Furosemide Tablets, USP 40 mg are available as white, round, flat face, beveled edge bisected debossed with “ CE” on one side of the break line and " 175" on the other side containing 40 mg of Furosemide, USP and supplied as follows:

NDC 62135-754-90 in bottles of 90 tablets

Dispense in a well-closed, light-resistant container as defined in the USP, with a child-resistant closure (as required).
Exposure to light may cause slight discoloration.
Discolored tablets should not be dispensed.

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

KEEP THIS AND ALL MEDICATIONS OUT OF THE REACH OF CHILDREN.
