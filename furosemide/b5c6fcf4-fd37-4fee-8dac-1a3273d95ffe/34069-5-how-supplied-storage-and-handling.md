# HOW SUPPLIED

FUROSEMIDE TABLET 20 mg are supplied as white, round tablets in Bottles of 100 (NDC 69315-116-01), and 1000 (NDC 69315-116-10).
The 20 mg tablets are imprinted with "EP 116" on one side and plain on the other.

FUROSEMIDE TABLETS 40 mg are supplied as white, round, scored tablets in Bottles of 100 (NDC 69315-117-01) and 1000 (NDC 69315-117-10).
The 40 mg tablets are imprinted with "EP 117" on one side and "40" on the other.

FUROSEMIDE TABLETS 80 mg are supplied as white, round, scored tablets in Bottles of 100 (NDC 69315-118-01) and 500 (NDC 69315-118-05).The 80 mg tablets are imprinted with "EP 118" on one side and "80" on the other.

Note: Dispense in well-closed, light-resistant containers.
Exposure to light might cause a slight discoloration.
Discolored tablets should not be dispensed.

Meets USP Dissolution Test 2

Store at 25° C (77° F); excursions permitted to 15 to 30° C (59 to 86° F). [See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]
