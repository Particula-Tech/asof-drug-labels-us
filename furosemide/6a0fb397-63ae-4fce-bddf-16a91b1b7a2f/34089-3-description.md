# DESCRIPTION

Furosemide is a diuretic which is an anthranilic acid derivative.
Furosemide Tablets, USP for oral administration contain furosemide, USP as the active ingredient and the following inactive ingredients: corn starch, lactose monohydrate, magnesium stearate, pregelatinized starch, and talc.
Chemically, it is 4-chloro-N-furfuryl-5-sulfamoylanthranilic acid.
Furosemide is available as white-off white tablets for oral administration in dosage strengths of 20 mg, 40 mg and 80 mg.
Furosemide is a white to off-white odorless crystalline powder.
It is practically insoluble in water, sparingly soluble in alcohol, freely soluble in dilute alkali solutions and insoluble in dilute acids.
