# HOW SUPPLIED

Furosemide Tablets, USP

20 mg: White-off white, oval, debossed "3169" on one side and debossed "V" on the reverse side, available as follows:

- •Bottles of 100: NDC 43547-401-10
- •Bottles of 1000: NDC 43547-401-11
- •Bottles of 5000: NDC 43547-401-51

40 mg: White-off white, round, scored, debossed "3170" over "V" on one side and plain on the reverse side, available as follows:

- •Bottles of 100: NDC 43547-402-10
- •Bottles of 1000: NDC 43547-402-11
- •Bottles of 5000: NDC 43547-402-51

80 mg: White-off white, round, scored, debossed "3171" over "V" on one side and plain on the reverse side, available as follows:

- •Bottles of 100: NDC 43547-403-10
- •Bottles of 500: NDC 43547-403-50
- •Bottles of 1000: NDC 43547-403-11

Note: Dispense in well-closed, light-resistant containers.
Exposure to light might cause a slight discoloration.
Discolored tablets should not be dispensed.

Store at 68° to 77°F (20° to 25°C); excursions permitted to 59° to 86°F (15° to 30°C) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
