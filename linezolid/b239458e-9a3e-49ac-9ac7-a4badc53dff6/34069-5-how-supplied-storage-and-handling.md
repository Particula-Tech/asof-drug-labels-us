# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.2 Tablets

Linezolid Tablets, 600 mg are white to off white, oval shaped, bevel edged, biconvex film coated tablets, debossed with 'I' on one side and '22' on other side.
20 tablets in HDPE bottle NDC 31722-749-20 30 tablets in HDPE bottle NDC 31722-749-30 100 tablets in HDPE bottle NDC 31722-749-01 Unit-dose package of 150 (15x10) tablets NDC 31722-749-31

## 16.4 Storage

Store at 20º to 25ºC (68º to 77ºF) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light.
Keep bottles tightly closed to protect from moisture.
