# 7 DRUG INTERACTIONS

## 7.1 Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors

Linezolid is a reversible, nonselective inhibitor of monoamine oxidase.
[seeContraindications (4.2) and Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].

## 7.2 Adrenergic and Serotonergic Agents

Linezolid has the potential for interaction with adrenergic and serotonergic agents.
[seeWarnings and Precautions (5.3, 5.6) and Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].
