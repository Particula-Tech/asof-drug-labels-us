# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Linezolid for oral suspension is available as a dry, white to off-white, orange-flavored granule/powder.
When constituted as directed, each bottle will contain 150 mL of a suspension providing the equivalent of 100 mg of linezolid per each 5 mL.
Linezolid for oral suspension is supplied as follows:

| 100 mg/5 mL in 240-mL glass bottles | NDC 59762-1308-1 |
| --- | --- |

Store at 25ºC (77ºF).
Protect from light.
Keep bottles tightly closed to protect from moisture.
