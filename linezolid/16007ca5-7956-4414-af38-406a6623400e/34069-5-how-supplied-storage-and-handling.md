# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.3 Oral Suspension

Linezolid for Oral Suspension is available as a dry, white to tan, orange-flavored granule/powder.
When constituted as directed, each bottle will contain 150 mL of a suspension providing the equivalent of 100 mg of linezolid per each 5 mL.
Linezolid for Oral Suspension is supplied as follows:

0054-0319-50 100 mg/5 mL, 150 mL per bottle

## 16.4 Storage

Store at 20º to 25ºC (68º to 77ºF).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light.
Keep bottles tightly closed to protect from moisture.
