# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Linezolid injection is available in a single-use, ready-to-use flexible plastic container (VisIV™ Container) in a foil laminate overwrap.
The container is available in the following package size:

| 600 mg linezolid in a 300 mL bag (2 mg/mL) | NDC 0409-4883-01 |
| --- | --- |

Store at 20 to 25°C (68 to 77°F), excursions permitted to 15 to 30°C (59 to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light.
It is recommended that the containers be kept in the overwrap until ready to use.
Protect containers from freezing.
