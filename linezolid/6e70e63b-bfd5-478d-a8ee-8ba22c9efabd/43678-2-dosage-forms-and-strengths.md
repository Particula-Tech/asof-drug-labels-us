# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

ZYVOX I.V.
Injection: 100-mL (200 mg linezolid), 200-mL (400 mg linezolid) and 300-mL (600 mg linezolid) single-use, ready-to-use flexible plastic infusion bags in a foil laminate overwrap.
The infusion bags and ports are latex-free.

ZYVOX 600 mg Tablet: white, capsule-shaped, film-coated tablet printed with "ZYVOX 600 mg"

ZYVOX for Oral Suspension: dry, white to off-white, orange-flavored granule/powder.
When constituted as directed, each bottle will contain 150 mL of a suspension providing the equivalent of 100 mg of linezolid per each 5 mL.
