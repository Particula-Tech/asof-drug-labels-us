# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.2 Tablets

Linezolid tablets 600 mg are available as “White to off white, oval shaped, film-coated tablets debossed with “L340” on one side and plain on the other side.

20 tablets in HDPE bottle NDC 46708-183-20

30 tablets in HDPE bottle NDC 46708-183-30

100 tablets in HDPE bottle NDC 46708-183-31

1000 tablets in HDPE bottle NDC 46708-183-91

## 16.4 Storage and Handling

Store at 25ºC (77ºF); excursions permitted to 15 to 30°C (59 to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light.
Keep bottles tightly closed to protect from moisture.
