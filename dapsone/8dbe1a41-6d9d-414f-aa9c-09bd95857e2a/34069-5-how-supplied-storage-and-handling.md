# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Dapsone gel, 5%, is supplied in the following size tubes:

NDC 60758-670-30 30 gram laminate tube

NDC 60758-670-60 60 gram laminate tube

NDC 60758-670-90 90 gram laminate tube

Store dapsone gel at controlled room temperature, 20°-25°C (68°-77°F), excursions permitted to 15°-30ºC (59°-86ºF).
Protect from freezing.
