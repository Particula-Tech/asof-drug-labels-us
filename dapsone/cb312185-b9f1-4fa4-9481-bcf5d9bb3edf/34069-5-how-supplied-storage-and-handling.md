# HOW SUPPLIED

Dapsone Tablets USP, 25 mg are available as white, round tablet.
One side scored and engraved with "DAP" above and "25" below the score and the other side is plain in a Unit of Use carton of 30 tablets (2 × 15).
The blisters are light and child-resistant.
NDC 51672-4197-2.
