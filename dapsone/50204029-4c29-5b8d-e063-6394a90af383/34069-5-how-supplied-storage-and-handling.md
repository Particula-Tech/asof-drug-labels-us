# HOW SUPPLIED

Dapsone Tablets USP, 25 mg are available as white to off white, oval shaped tablets debossed with “SL2” on one side and scored on other side.

They are available as follows: Bottle of 30 tablets: NDC 85185-104-01 Bottle of 100 tablets: NDC 85185-104-02

Dapsone Tablets USP, 100 mg are available as white to off white, oval shaped tablets debossed with “SL1” on one side and scored on other side.

They are available as follows: Bottle of 30 tablets: NDC 85185-105-01 Bottle of 100 tablets: NDC 85185-105-02

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light.
As with all medications, keep out of reach of children.
