# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Boncresa is contraindicated in:

- Patients with hypocalcemia: Pre-existing hypocalcemia must be corrected prior to initiating therapy with Boncresa [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
- Pregnant women: Denosumab products may cause fetal harm when administered to a pregnant woman.
  In women of reproductive potential, pregnancy testing should be performed prior to initiating treatment with Boncresa [see Use in Specific Populations (8.1)].
- Patients with hypersensitivity to denosumab products: Boncresa is contraindicated in patients with a history of systemic hypersensitivity to any component of the product.
  Reactions have included anaphylaxis, facial swelling, and urticaria [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3) and Adverse Reactions (6.2)].
