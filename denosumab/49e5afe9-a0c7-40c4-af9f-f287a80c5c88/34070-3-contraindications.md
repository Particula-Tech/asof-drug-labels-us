# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Prolia is contraindicated in:

- Hypocalcemia: Pre-existing hypocalcemia must be corrected prior to initiating therapy with Prolia [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
- Pregnancy: Prolia may cause fetal harm when administered to a pregnant woman.
  In women of reproductive potential, pregnancy testing should be performed prior to initiating treatment with Prolia [see Use in Specific Populations (8.1)].
- Hypersensitivity: Prolia is contraindicated in patients with a history of systemic hypersensitivity to any component of the product.
  Reactions have included anaphylaxis, facial swelling, and urticaria [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3 Adverse Reactions (6.2)].
