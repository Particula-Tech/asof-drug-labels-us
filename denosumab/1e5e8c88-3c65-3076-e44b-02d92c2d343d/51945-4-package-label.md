Each single-dose prefilled syringe contains:

1 mL solution of 60 mg denosumab-kyqq, glacial acetic acid (0.234 mg), polysorbate 20 (0.1 mg), sodium acetate (1.075 mg), sorbitol (47 mg), Water for Injection, USP.

Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) in the original carton to protect from light.
Do not freeze.
Avoid excessive shaking.
Prior to administration, Bosaya may be allowed to reach room temperature up to 25°C (77°F) in the original container.

Once removed from the refrigerator, Bosaya must not be exposed to temperatures above 25°C (77°F) and must be used within 30 days.
Discard Bosaya if not used within the 30 days.
Do not use Bosaya after the expiry date printed on the label.

Recommended Dosage: See Prescribing Information.

Bosaya is administered once every 6 months.
