# 11 DESCRIPTION

Denosumab-kyqq is a human IgG2 monoclonal antibody with affinity and specificity for human RANKL (receptor activator of nuclear factor kappa-B ligand).
Denosumab-kyqq has an approximate molecular weight of 147 kDa and is produced in genetically engineered mammalian (Chinese hamster ovary) cells.

Bosaya (denosumab-kyqq) injection is a sterile, preservative-free, clear to slightly opalescent, colorless to pale yellow solution for subcutaneous use.

Each 1 mL single-dose prefilled syringe of Bosaya contains 60 mg denosumab-kyqq, glacial acetic acid (0.234 mg),polysorbate 20 (0.1 mg), sodium acetate (1.075 mg), sorbitol (47 mg), Water for Injection (USP), and may contain sodium hydroxide/acetic acid to adjust pH of 5.2.
