# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

Derma-Smoothe/FS® is not for oral, ophthalmic, or intravaginal use.

The dosing of Derma-Smoothe/FS® is different for adult and pediatric patients.

## 2.1 Adult Patients with Atopic Dermatitis

Apply Derma-Smoothe/FS® as a thin film to the affected areas three times daily.

## 2.2 Pediatric Patients with Atopic Dermatitis

Moisten skin and apply Derma-Smoothe/FS® as a thin film to the affected areas twice daily for up to four weeks.
