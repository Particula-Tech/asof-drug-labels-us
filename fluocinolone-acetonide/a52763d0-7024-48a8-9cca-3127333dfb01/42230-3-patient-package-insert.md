# PATIENT INFORMATION

CAPEX® SHAMPOO (FLUOCINOLONE ACETONIDE) TOPICAL SHAMPOO, 0.01%

Important: Capex Shampoo is for external use only.
Avoid contact with eyes.
In case of eye contact, wash eyes liberally with water.

What is Capex Shampoo?

Capex Shampoo is a topical corticosteroid treatment in a shampoo-delivery system that is indicated for scalps that are itchy, flaky (dandruff) and red.

What should I tell my doctor before using Capex Shampoo?

Before you use Capex Shampoo, tell your doctor if you:

- Have a history of allergic reaction to any of the components of Capex
- Have other medical conditions
- Are pregnant or planning to become pregnant.
  It is not known if Capex shampoo can harm your unborn baby.
  Talk to your doctor if you are pregnant or plan to become pregnant.
- Are breastfeeding or plan to breastfeed.
  It is not known if Capex Shampoo passes into your breast milk and if it can harm your baby.
  Talk to your doctor about the best way to feed your baby if you use Capex Shampoo.

Tell your doctor about all the medicines you take, including prescription and nonprescription medicines, vitamins and herbal supplements.
Using Capex Shampoo with certain other medicines may affect each other and can cause serious side effects.
Know the medicines you take.
Keep a list of them to show your doctor and pharmacist when you get a new medicine.

How should I use Capex Shampoo?

1. Shake Capex Shampoo well.
2. Wet the hair and scalp thoroughly, apply a sufficient amount (approximately one ounce) of Capex Shampoo and massage it into the scalp.
3. Allow Capex Shampoo to remain on the scalp for approximately 5 minutes.
   (Note: This is important.)
4. Rinse thoroughly with water.
   Conditioner will not be necessary.
5. Do not bandage or otherwise cover or wrap your scalp unless directed by the physician.
6. Discard the content of the bottle after 2 months.
7. Use Capex Shampoo exactly as your doctor tells you.
   Do not use more Capex Topical Shampoo than prescribed.
   Call your doctor if you are not sure how to use this product.

What are the possible side effects of Capex Shampoo?

The following are some local reactions reported with topical steroids: dryness, burning, itching and irritation.

How should I store Capex Shampoo?

Store at room temperature, tightly closed.

What are the ingredients in Capex Shampoo?

Each fluocinolone capsule contains 12 mg of fluocinolone acetonide, 548 mg of dibasic calcium phosphate dihydrate USP, and 240 mg of talc USP.
The shampoo base contains aluminum acetate dibasic, benzalkonium chloride solution, boric acid, citric acid anhydrous, cocamido-ether-sulfate complex, cocoamine oxide, lauramide DEA, magnesium aluminum silicate, methylparaben, oat flour, propylene glycol, propylparaben, purified water, and fragrances, with D&C Yellow #10 and FD&C Blue #1 as coloring.
