# 13 NONCLINICAL TOXICOLOGY

## 13.1 Carcinogenesis, mutagenesis, impairment of fertility

No carcinogenicity, genotoxicity, or fertility studies were conducted with DERMA-SMOOTHE/FS Scalp Oil.
However, some corticosteroids are genotoxic in various genotoxicity tests (i.e., the in vitrohuman peripheral blood lymphocyte chromosome aberration assay with metabolic activation, the in vivomouse bone marrow micronucleus assay, the Chinese hamster micronucleus test, and the in vitromouse lymphoma gene mutation assay).
