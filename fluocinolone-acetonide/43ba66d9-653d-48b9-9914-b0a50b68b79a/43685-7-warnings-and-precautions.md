# 5. WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS

## 5.1. Intravitreal Injection-related Effects

Intravitreal injections, including those with YUTIQ, have been associated with endophthalmitis, eye inflammation, increased or decreased intraocular pressure, and choroidal or retinal detachments.
Hypotony has been observed within 24 hours of injection and has resolved within 2 weeks.
Patients should be monitored following the intravitreal injection [see Patient Counseling Information (17)].

## 5.2. Steroid-related Effects

Use of corticosteroids including YUTIQ may produce posterior subcapsular cataracts, increased intraocular pressure and glaucoma.
Use of corticosteroids may enhance the establishment of secondary ocular infections due to bacteria, fungi, or viruses.

Corticosteroids are not recommended to be used in patients with a history of ocular herpes simplex because of the potential for reactivation of the viral infection.

## 5.3. Risk of Implant Migration

Patients in whom the posterior capsule of the lens is absent or has a tear are at risk of implant migration into the anterior chamber.
