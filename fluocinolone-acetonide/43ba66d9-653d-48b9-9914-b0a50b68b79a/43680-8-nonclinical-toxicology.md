# 13. NONCLINICAL TOXICOLOGY

## 13.1 Carcinogenesis, Mutagenesis, Impairment of Fertility

Long-term animal studies have not been conducted to determine the carcinogenic potential or the effect on fertility of YUTIQ.

Fluocinolone acetonide was not genotoxic in vitroin the Ames test (S. typhimurium and E. coli) and the mouse lymphoma TK assay, or in vivoin the mouse bone marrow micronucleus assay.
