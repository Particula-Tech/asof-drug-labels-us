# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Vivelle-Dot is contraindicated in women with any of the following conditions:

1. 1.Undiagnosed abnormal genital bleeding [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)].
2. 2.Breast cancer or a history of breast cancer [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)].
3. 3.Estrogen-dependent neoplasia [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)].
4. 4.Active DVT, PE, or a history of these conditions [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
5. 5.Active arterial thromboembolic disease (for example, stroke and MI), or a history of these conditions [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
6. 6.Known anaphylactic reaction, or angioedema, or hypersensitivity to Vivelle-Dot
7. 7.Hepatic impairment or disease
8. 8.Protein C, protein S, or antithrombin deficiency, or other known thrombophilic disorders
