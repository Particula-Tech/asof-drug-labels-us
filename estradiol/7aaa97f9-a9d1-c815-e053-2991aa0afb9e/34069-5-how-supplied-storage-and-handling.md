# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Each Femring (estradiol acetate vaginal ring) is individually packaged in a pouch consisting of one side medical grade paper and the other side polyester/polyethylene laminate.

N 72495-201-05 Femring 0.05 mg/day (estradiol acetate vaginal ring) is available in single units.
N 72495-202-10 Femring 0.10 mg/day (estradiol acetate vaginal ring) is available in single units.

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at 20º C to 25º C (68º F to77º F); excursions permitted to -20º C to 40º C (-4º F to 104º F).

Do not store unpouched.
Insert immediately upon removal from the protective pouch.
