# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

ESTRADIOL TRANSDERMAL SYSTEM is indicated for:

## 1.1 Treatment of Moderate to Severe Vasomotor Symptoms Due to Menopause

## 1.2 Prevention of Postmenopausal Osteoporosis

Limitation of Use When prescribing solely for the prevention of postmenopausal osteoporosis, first consider the use of non-estrogen medications.
Consider estrogen therapy only for women at significant risk of osteoporosis.
