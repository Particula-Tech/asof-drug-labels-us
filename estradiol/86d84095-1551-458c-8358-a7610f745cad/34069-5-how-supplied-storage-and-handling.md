# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Evamist (NDC 64011-215-41) is supplied as a homogeneous solution of estradiol USP, octisalate USP and alcohol USP.
The liquid formulation of Evamist is packaged in a glass vial fitted with a metered-dose pump.
The unit is encased in a plastic housing with a conical bell opening that controls the distance, angle, and area of application of the metered-dose spray.
Each metered-dose pump contains 8.1 mL and is designed to deliver 56 sprays of 90 mcL after priming.
One spray contains 1.53 mg estradiol.

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Keep out of reach of children.

Alcohol and alcohol-based liquids are flammable.
Avoid fire, flame or smoking until the spray has dried.

Store at room temperature 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursion permitted between 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F).
Do not freeze.
