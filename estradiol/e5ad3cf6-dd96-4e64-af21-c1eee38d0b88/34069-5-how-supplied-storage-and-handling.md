# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Each Vagifem (estradiol vaginal tablets), 10 mcg and 25 mcg, is contained in a disposable, single-use applicator, packaged in a blister pack.
Cartons contain 8 or 18 applicators with inset tablets.

Vagifem 25 mcg

8 applicators: NDC 0169-5173-03

18 applicators: NDC 0169-5173-04

Vagifem 10 mcg

8 applicators: NDC 0169-5176-03

18 applicators: NDC 0169-5176-04

Keep out of reach of children

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at 25ºC (77ºF), excursions permitted to 15ºC-30ºC (59ºF-86ºF).
Do not refrigerate.
