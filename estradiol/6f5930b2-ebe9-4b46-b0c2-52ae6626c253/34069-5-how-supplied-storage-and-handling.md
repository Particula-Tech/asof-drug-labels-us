# HOW SUPPLIED

Estrasorb (estradiol topical emulsion), nominal 0.05 mg/day:

Estrasorb is packaged in foil-laminated pouches.
A daily dose of Estrasorb is two foil-laminated pouches.
Each pouch contains 1.74-grams.
Each 1.74-gram, foil-laminated pouch contains 4.35 mg of estradiol hemihydrate USP, EP.
Each box of Estrasorb contains fourteen 1.74-gram, foil-laminated pouches, packaged in a:

1-month supply carton of 56 pouches, NDC 99207-190-56

Store at 20–25°C (68–77°F); excursions permitted to 15–40°C (59–104°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
