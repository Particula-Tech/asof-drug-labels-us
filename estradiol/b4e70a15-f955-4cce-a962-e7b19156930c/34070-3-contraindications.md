# CONTRAINDICATIONS

Estraderm therapy should not be used in women with any of the following conditions:

1. Undiagnosed abnormal genital bleeding.
2. Known, suspected or history of breast cancer.
3. Known or suspected estrogen-dependent neoplasia.
4. Active DVT, PE, or a history of these conditions.
5. Active arterial thromboembolic disease (for example, stroke and myocardial infarction) or a history of these conditions.
6. Known anaphylactic reaction or angioedema or hypersensitivity to Estraderm.
7. Known liver impairment or disease.
8. Known protein C, protein S, or antithrombin deficiency, or other known thrombophilic disorders.
9. Known or suspected pregnancy.
