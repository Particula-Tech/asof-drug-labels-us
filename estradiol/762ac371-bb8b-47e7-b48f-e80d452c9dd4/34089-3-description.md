# DESCRIPTION

EstroGel® (estradiol gel) contains 0.06% estradiol in an absorptive hydroalcoholic gel base formulated to provide a controlled release of the active ingredient.
It is a clear, colorless gel, which is odorless when dry.
The gel is applied over a large area (750 cm2) of the skin in a thin layer.
The recommended area of application is the arm, from wrist to shoulder.
An EstroGel unit dose of 1.25 g contains 0.75 mg of estradiol.

Estradiol is a white crystalline powder, chemically described as estra-1,3,5(10)-triene-3,17β-diol.
It has an empirical formula of C18H24O2 and molecular weight of 272.39.
