# CONTRAINDICATIONS

EstroGel should not be used in women with any of the following conditions:

1. Undiagnosed abnormal genital bleeding
2. Known, suspected, or history of breast cancer
3. Known or suspected estrogen-dependent neoplasia
4. Active deep vein thrombosis, pulmonary embolism, or history of these conditions
5. Active or recent (within the past year) arterial thromboembolic disease (for example, stroke and myocardial infarction)
6. Known liver dysfunction or disease
7. Known hypersensitivity to any of the ingredients in EstroGel
8. Known or suspected pregnancy.
