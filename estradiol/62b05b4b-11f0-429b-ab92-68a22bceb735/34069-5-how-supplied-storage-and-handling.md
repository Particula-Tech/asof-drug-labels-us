# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

ELESTRIN is estradiol 0.06 percent in a colorless, non-staining hydroalcoholic gel supplied in a non-aerosol, metered-dose pump container constructed of polypropylene.
The drug product is contained within a collapsible inner liner/bag consisting of an inner and outer layer of low density polyethylene with a resealable polypropylene cap.
Each 144 g individually packaged pump container is capable of delivering 87 g of gel in 100 metered doses.
Each metered dose contains 0.87 g of gel.

NDC 18860-490-02

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F).
