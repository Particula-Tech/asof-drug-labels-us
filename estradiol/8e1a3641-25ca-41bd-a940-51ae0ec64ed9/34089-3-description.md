# DESCRIPTION

Elestrin® (estradiol gel) contains 0.06% estradiol in a hydroalcoholic gel base.
The gel is applied onto the skin in a thin layer.
The recommended area of application is the upper arm to shoulder (approximately 320 cm2).
One pump actuation delivers Elestrin® in a unit dose of 0.87 g, which contains 0.52 mg of estradiol.
The 0.87 g dose provides systemic delivery of 0.0125 mg of estradiol daily.
The 1.7 g dose, two pump actuations, provides systemic delivery of 0.0375 mg daily.

Estradiol is a white crystalline powder, chemically described as estra-1,3,5(10)-triene-3,17-diol, (17β)-.
It has a molecular formula of C18H24O2•½H2O and molecular weight of 281.4.
