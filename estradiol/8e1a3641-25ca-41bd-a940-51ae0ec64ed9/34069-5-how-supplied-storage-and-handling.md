# HOW SUPPLIED

Elestrin is estradiol 0.06% in a colorless, non-staining hydroalcoholic gel supplied in a non-aerosol, metered-dose pump container constructed of polypropylene.
The drug product is contained within a collapsible inner liner/bag consisting of an inner and outer layer of low density polyethylene with a resealable polypropylene cap.
Each individually packaged pump contains 144 g of gel and is capable of delivering 100 metered doses of approximately 0.87 g of drug product per pump actuation.

NDC 18860-490-02

Keep out of reach of children.

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F).
