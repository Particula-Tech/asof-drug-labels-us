# HOW SUPPLIED

SOLODYN® (MINOCYCLINE HCl, USP) Extended Release Tablets are supplied as aqueous film coated tablets containing minocycline hydrochloride equivalent to 45 mg, 65 mg, 90 mg, 115 mg, or 135 mg minocycline.
