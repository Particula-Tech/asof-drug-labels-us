# 11 DESCRIPTION

SUBVENITE (lamotrigine) oral suspension, an AED of the phenyltriazine class, is chemically unrelated to existing AEDs.
Lamotrigine’s chemical name is 3,5-diamino-6-(2,3-dichlorophenyl)-as-triazine, its molecular formula is C9H7N5Cl2, and its molecular weight is 256.09.
Lamotrigine, USP is a white to pale cream-colored powder and has a pKa of 5.7.
Lamotrigine, USP is very slightly soluble in water (0.17 mg/mL at 25°C) and slightly soluble in 0.1 M HCl (4.1 mg/mL at 25°C).
