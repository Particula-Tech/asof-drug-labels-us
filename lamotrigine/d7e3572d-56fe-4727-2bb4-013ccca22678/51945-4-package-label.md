Tips to Help You Remember

to Take Your Medicine

Taking your medicine as directed by your

doctor is one of the most important things

you can do.
Here are some tips that can help

you remember to take your medicine.

• Take your medicine with another daily

habit, like when you brush your teeth or

when you go to bed.

• Set an alarm to help you remember to

take your medicine.

• Leave yourself a reminder note on the

bathroom mirror.

• Keep your medicine in a place where you

will see it daily.

It is also important to refill your medicine on

time, so that you don't miss doses.
Here are

some tips to help you remember to refill your

medicine.

• Mark your calendar with the date you

need to plan to refill your medicine.

• Make sure you have enough refills to last

you until your next doctor visit.

• If you run out of refills unexpectedly, call

your doctor or pharmacist right away.
