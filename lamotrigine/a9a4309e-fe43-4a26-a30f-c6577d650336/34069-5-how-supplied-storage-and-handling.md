# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Lamotrigine Tablets 25 mg are white to off-white colored, shield shaped uncoated tablets debossed with “D” and “93” on one side and scoreline on other side.
Bottles of 60 NDC 65862-227-60 Bottles of 100 NDC 65862-227-01 Bottles of 1000 NDC 65862-227-99 10 x 10 Unit-dose Tablets NDC 65862-227-10 Lamotrigine Tablets 100 mg are peach colored, mottled, shield shaped uncoated tablets debossed with “D” and “94” on one side and scoreline on other side.
Bottles of 60 NDC 65862-228-60 Bottles of 100 NDC 65862-228-01 Bottles of 1000 NDC 65862-228-99 10 x 10 Unit-dose Tablets NDC 65862-228-10 Lamotrigine Tablets 150 mg are cream colored, mottled, shield shaped uncoated tablets debossed with “D” and “95” on one side and scoreline on other side.
Bottles of 60 NDC 65862-229-60 Bottles of 100 NDC 65862-229-01 Bottles of 1000 NDC 65862-229-99 10 x 10 Unit-dose Tablets NDC 65862-229-10 Lamotrigine Tablets 200 mg are blue colored, mottled, shield shaped uncoated tablets debossed with “D” and “96” on one side and scoreline on other side.
Bottles of 60 NDC 65862-230-60 Bottles of 100 NDC 65862-230-01 Bottles of 1000 NDC 65862-230-99 10 x 10 Unit-dose Tablets NDC 65862-230-10 Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Keep in a dry place and protect from light.
Blister Packs: If the product is dispensed in a blister pack, the patient should be advised to examine the blister pack before use and not use if blisters are torn, broken, or missing.
