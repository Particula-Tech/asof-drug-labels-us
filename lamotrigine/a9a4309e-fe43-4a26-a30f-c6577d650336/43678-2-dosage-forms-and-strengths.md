# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

## 3.1 Tablets

25 mg, white to off-white colored, shield shaped uncoated tablets debossed with “D” and “93” on one side and scoreline on other side.
100 mg, peach colored, mottled, shield shaped uncoated tablets debossed with “D” and “94” on one side and scoreline on other side.
150 mg, cream colored, mottled, shield shaped uncoated tablets debossed with “D” and “95” on one side and scoreline on other side.
200 mg, blue colored, mottled, shield shaped uncoated tablets debossed with “D” and “96” on one side and scoreline on other side.

## 3.4 Potential Medication Errors

Patients should be strongly advised to visually inspect their tablets to verify that they are receiving lamotrigine tablets as well as the correct formulation of lamotrigine tablets each time they fill their prescription.
Depictions of the lamotrigine tablets can be found in the Medication Guide that accompanies the product.
