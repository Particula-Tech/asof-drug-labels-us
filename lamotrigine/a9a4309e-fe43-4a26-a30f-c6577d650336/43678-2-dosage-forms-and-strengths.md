# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

## 3.1 Tablets

Lamotrigine Tablets USP, 25 mg

White to off-white colored, shield shaped uncoated tablets debossed with “D” and “93” on one side and scoreline on other side.

Lamotrigine Tablets USP, 100 mg

Peach colored, mottled, shield shaped uncoated tablets debossed with “D” and “94” on one side and scoreline on other side.

Lamotrigine Tablets USP, 150 mg

Cream colored, mottled, shield shaped uncoated tablets debossed with “D” and “95” on one side and scoreline on other side.

Lamotrigine Tablets USP, 200 mg

Blue colored, mottled, shield shaped uncoated tablets debossed with “D” and “96” on one side and scoreline on other side.
