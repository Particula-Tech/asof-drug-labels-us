# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

## 3.1 Tablets

25 mg – white to off-white, round-shaped tablets, debossed with “J” and “245” on one side and scoreline on the the other side of the tablet.

100 mg – white to off-white, round-shaped tablets, debossed with “J” and "246" on one side and scoreline on the other side of the tablet.

150 mg – white to off-white, round-shaped tablets, debossed with “J” and "247" on one side and scoreline on the other side of the tablet..

200 mg – white to off-white, round-shaped tablets, debossed with “J” and "248" on one side and scoreline on the other side of the tablet.

## 3.4 Potential Medication Errors

Patients should be strongly advised to visually inspect their tablets to verify that they are receiving lamotrigine as well as the correct formulation of lamotrigine each time they fill their prescription.
Descriptions of the lamotrigine tablets can be found in the Medication Guide that accompanies the product.
