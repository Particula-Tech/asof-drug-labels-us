# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

## 3.1 Lamotrigine Tablets USP

Lamotrigine tablets USP, 25 mg are light yellow, round, flat, bevel edged, uncoated tablets, debossed “RDY” on one side and “220” on other side with bisect line

Lamotrigine tablets USP, 100 mg are light yellow, round, flat, bevel edged, uncoated tablets, debossed “RDY” on one side and “221” on other side with bisect line.

Lamotrigine tablets USP, 150 mg are light yellow, round, flat, bevel edged, uncoated tablets, debossed “RDY” on one side and “222” on other side with bisect line.

Lamotrigine tablets USP, 200 mg are light yellow, round, flat, bevel edged, uncoated tablets, debossed “RDY” on one side and “223” on other side with bisect line.
