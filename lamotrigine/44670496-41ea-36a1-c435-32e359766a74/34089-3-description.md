# 11 DESCRIPTION

Lamotrigine, an AED of the phenyltriazine class, is chemically unrelated to existing AEDs. lamotrigine's chemical name is 3,5-diamino-6-(2,3-dichlorophenyl)-as-triazine, its molecular formula is C9H7N5Cl2, and its molecular weight is 256.09.
Lamotrigine USP is a white to pale cream-colored powder and has a pKa of 5.56.
Lamotrigine USP is slightly soluble in methanol.
