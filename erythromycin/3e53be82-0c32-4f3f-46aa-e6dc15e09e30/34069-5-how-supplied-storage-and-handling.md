# HOW SUPPLIED

E.E.S.
200 LIQUID (erythromycin ethylsuccinate oral suspension, USP) is supplied in 1 pint bottles (NDC 0074-6306-16) and in 100-mL bottles (NDC 0074-6306-13).

E.E.S.
400® LIQUID (erythromycin ethylsuccinate oral suspension, USP) is supplied in 1 pint bottles (NDC 0074-6373-16) and in 100-mL bottles (NDC 0074-6373-13).

Both liquid products require refrigeration to preserve taste until dispensed.
Refrigeration by patient is not required if used within 14 days.

E.E.S.
GRANULES (erythromycin ethylsuccinate for oral suspension, USP) is supplied in 100-mL (NDC 0074-6369-02) and 200-mL (NDC 0074-6369-10) size bottles.

E.E.S.
400 Filmtab tablets (erythromycin ethylsuccinate tablets, USP) 400 mg, are supplied as pink tablets imprinted with the Abbott “A” logo, and two letter Abbo-Code designation, EE, in bottles of 100 (NDC 0074-5729-13), 500 (NDC 0074-5729-53) and 1000 (NDC 0074-5729-19) and in ABBO-PAC unit dose strip packages of 100 (NDC 0074-5729-11).

## Recommended storage

Store tablets below 86°F (30°C).

Store granules, prior to mixing, below 86°F (30°C).
After mixing, refrigerate and use within 10 days.
