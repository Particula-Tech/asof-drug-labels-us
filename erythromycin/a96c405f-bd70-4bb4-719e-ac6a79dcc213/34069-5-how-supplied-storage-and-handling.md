# HOW SUPPLIED

Erythrocin Lactobionate-I.V.
(Sterile Erythromycin Lactobionate, USP) is supplied as a sterile, lyophilized powder in packages of ten vials (NDC 0409-6482-01), each vial containing the equivalent of 500 mg of erythromycin.

Store at 20 to 25°C (68 to 77°F).
