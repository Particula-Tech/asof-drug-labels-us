# ADVERSE REACTIONS

In controlled clinical trials, the total incidence of adverse reactions associated with the use of Staticin was approximately 13%.
These were dryness (7%), oiliness (2%), pruritus (2%), tenderness (2%), desquamation (2%), erythema (1%) and irritation (1%).

The following additional adverse reactions have been reported occasionally: peeling, generalized urticarial reactions and itching.
