# HOW SUPPLIED

ERYGEL® (Erythromycin Topical Gel USP) 2% is supplied in plastic tubes in the following sizes: 30g - NDC 0259-4312-30 and 60 g - NDC 0259-4312-60.

Note: FLAMMABLE.
Keep away from heat and flame.
Store and dispense in original container.
Keep tube tightly closed.
Store between 15° and 25°C (59° and 77°F).
