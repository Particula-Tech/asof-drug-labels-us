# HOW SUPPLIED

E.E.S.
GRANULES (erythromycin ethylsuccinate for oral suspension, USP) is supplied in 100-mL (NDC 24338-134-02) and 200-mL (NDC 24338-136-10) size bottles.

E.E.S.
400 film-coated tablets (erythromycin ethylsuccinate tablets, USP) 400 mg, are supplied as pink tablets imprinted with the two letter designation, EE, in bottles of 100 (NDC 24338-100-13).

## Recommended storage

Store tablets below 86°F (30°C).

Store granules, prior to mixing, below 86°F (30°C).
After mixing, refrigerate and use within 10 days.
