# HOW SUPPLIED

Each Erythromycin Delayed-Release Capsule, USP, 250 mg has an orange transparent body and orange opaque cap.
The cap and body are imprinted with "250 mg" in black ink.
Each capsule contains 250 mg erythromycin as enteric-coated pellets.
The pellets are colored white.

The capsules are available as follows:

NDC 75907-076-01 Bottle of 100 capsules

## STORAGE CONDITIONS

Store at controlled room temperature 15° C to 30° C (59° F to 86° F).
