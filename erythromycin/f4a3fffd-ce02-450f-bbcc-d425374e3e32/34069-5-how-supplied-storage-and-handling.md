# HOW SUPPLIED

Erythromycin Ethylsuccinate Granules 200 mg per 5 mL (erythromycin ethylsuccinate for oral suspension, USP) are pink granules with a cherry aroma and are supplied in 100-mL (NDC 52536-134-13) and 200-mL (NDC 52536-136-13) size bottles.
Following reconstitution Erythromycin Ethylsuccinate Granules become a pink opaque suspension with a cherry aroma.

## Recommended storage

Store granules, prior to mixing, at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
After mixing, refrigerate and use within 10 days.
