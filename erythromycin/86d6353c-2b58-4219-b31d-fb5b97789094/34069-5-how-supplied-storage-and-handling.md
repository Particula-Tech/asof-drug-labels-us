# HOW SUPPLIED

Erythromycin Ethylsuccinate 200 (erythromycin ethylsuccinate for oral suspension, USP) is supplied in bottles of 100 mL (NDC 52536-132-13).

Erythromycin Ethylsuccinate 400 (erythromycin ethylsuccinate for oral suspension, USP) is supplied in bottles of 100 mL (NDC 52536-130-13).

## Recommended Storage

Store Erythromycin Ethylsuccinate 200 and Erythromycin Ethylsuccinate 400, prior to mixing, below 86°F (30°C).
After reconstitution, Erythromycin Ethylsuccinate 200 and Erythromycin Ethylsuccinate 400 must be stored at or below 77°F (25°C) and used within 35 days; refrigeration is not required.
