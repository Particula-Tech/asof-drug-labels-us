# 11 DESCRIPTION

Icosapent ethyl, a lipid-regulating agent, is supplied as either a 500 milligram or a 1 gram, transparent, liquid-filled soft gelatin capsule for oral use.

Each icosapent ethyl capsule contains either 500 milligrams of icosapent ethyl (in a 500 milligram capsule) or 1 gram of icosapent ethyl (in a 1 gram capsule).
Icosapent ethyl is an ethyl ester of the omega-3 fatty acid eicosapentaenoic acid (EPA).
The molecular formula of icosapent ethyl is C22H34O2 and the molecular weight is 330.5.
