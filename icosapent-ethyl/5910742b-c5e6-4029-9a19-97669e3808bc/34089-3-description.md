# 11 DESCRIPTION

Icosapent ethyl, a lipid-regulating agent, is supplied as either a 0.5 gram or a 1 gram liquid-filled soft gelatin capsule for oral use.

Icosapent ethyl is an ethyl ester of the omega-3 fatty acid eicosapentaenoic acid (EPA).
The molecular formula of icosapent ethyl is C22H34O2 and the molecular weight is 330.50.

The capsule is imprinted with white pharmaceutical ink and contains following inactive ingredients: shellac and titanium dioxide.
