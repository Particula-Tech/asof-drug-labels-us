# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

Assess lipid levels before initiating therapy.
Identify other causes (e.g., diabetes mellitus, hypothyroidism, or medications) of high triglyceride levels and manage as appropriate.
[see Indications and Usage (1)].

Patients should engage in appropriate nutritional intake and physical activity before receiving VASCEPA, which should continue during treatment with VASCEPA.

The daily dose of VASCEPA is 4 grams per day taken as 2 capsules twice daily with food.

Patients should be advised to swallow VASCEPA capsules whole.
Do not break open, crush, dissolve, or chew VASCEPA.
