# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Regadenoson injection is supplied as a sterile, preservative-free solution containing 0.08 mg/mL regadenoson in the following package:

| Product Name | NDC No. | Pack configuration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regadenoson Injection 0.4 mg /5mL (0.08 mg/mL) | 68083-175-10 | Ten single-dose 5 mL vials in one carton |

Store at controlled room temperature, 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]
