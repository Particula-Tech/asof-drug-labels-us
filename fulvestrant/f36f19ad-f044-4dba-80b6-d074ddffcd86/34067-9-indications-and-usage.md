# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Monotherapy

Fulvestrant injection is indicated for the treatment of:

- HR-positive advanced breast cancer in postmenopausal women with disease progression following endocrine therapy.

Combination Therapy

Fulvestrant injection is indicated for the treatment of HR-positive, HER2-negative advanced or metastatic breast cancer in combination with palbociclib or abemaciclib in women with disease progression after endocrine therapy.
