# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

Advise the patient to read the FDA-approved patient labeling (Patient Information).

1. Risk of Bleeding:
2. Because FASLODEX is administered intramuscularly, it should be used with caution in patients with bleeding disorders, decreased platelet count, or in patients receiving anticoagulants (for example, warfarin) [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
3. Embryo-Fetal Toxicity:
4. •Advise females of reproductive potential of the potential risk to a fetus and to use effective contraception during treatment with FASLODEX and for one year after the last dose.
   Advise females to inform their healthcare provider of a known or suspected pregnancy [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3) and Use in Specific Populations (8.1),(8.3)].
5. Lactation:
6. •Advise women not to breast-feed during treatment with FASLODEX and for one year after the last dose [see Use in Specific Populations (8.2)].
