# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Venlafaxine Extended Release Tablets are available as: - 37.5 mg tablets (round, biconvex, white coated tablets with OS301 printed on one side) - 75 mg tablets (round, biconvex, white coated tablets with OS302 printed on one side) - 150 mg tablets (round, biconvex, white coated tablets with OS303 printed on one side) - 225 mg tablets (round, biconvex, white coated tablets with OS304 printed on one side)
