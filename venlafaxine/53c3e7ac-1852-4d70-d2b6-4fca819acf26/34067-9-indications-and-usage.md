# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

## 1.1 Major Depressive Disorder

Effexor XR (venlafaxine hydrochloride) extended-release capsules are indicated for the treatment of major depressive disorder (MDD).
Efficacy was established in three short-term (4, 8, and 12 weeks) and two long-term, maintenance trials.

## 1.2 Generalized Anxiety Disorder

Effexor XR is indicated for the treatment of Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD).
Efficacy was established in two 8-week and two 26-week placebo-controlled trials.

## 1.3 Social Anxiety Disorder

Effexor XR is indicated for the treatment of Social Anxiety Disorder (SAD), also known as social phobia.
Efficacy was established in four 12-week and one 26-week, placebo-controlled trials.

## 1.4 Panic Disorder

Effexor XR is indicated for the treatment of Panic Disorder (PD), with or without agoraphobia.
Efficacy was established in two 12-week placebo-controlled trials.
