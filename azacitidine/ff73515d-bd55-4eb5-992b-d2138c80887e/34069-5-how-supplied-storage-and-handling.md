# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

How Supplied

Azacitidine for Injection is supplied as a lyophilized powder in 100 mg single-dose vials packaged in cartons of 1 vial (NDC 0143-9606-01).

Storage

Store unreconstituted vials at 20º to 25ºC (68º to 77ºF), excursions permitted to 15º to 30ºC (59º to 86ºF) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Handling and Disposal

Azacitidine for Injection is a cytotoxic drug.
Follow applicable special handling and disposal procedures.1
