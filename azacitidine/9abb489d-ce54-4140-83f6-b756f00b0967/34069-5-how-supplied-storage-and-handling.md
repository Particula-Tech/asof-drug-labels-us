# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

How Supplied

Azacitidine for injection is supplied as a lyophilized powder in 100 mg single-dose vials packaged in cartons of 1 vial (NDC 51991-797-98).

Storage

Store unreconstituted vials at 25° C (77° F); excursions permitted to 15°-30° C (59°-86° F) (See USP Controlled Room Temperature).

Handling and Disposal

Azacitidine for injection is a cytotoxic drug.
Follow applicable special handling and disposal procedures.1
