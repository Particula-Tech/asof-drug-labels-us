# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

How Supplied

VIDAZA (azacitidine for injection) is supplied as a lyophilized powder in 100 mg single-dose vials packaged in cartons of 1 vial (NDC 59572-102-01).

Storage

Store unreconstituted vials at 25º C (77º F); excursions permitted to 15º-30º C (59º-86º F) (See USP Controlled Room Temperature).

Handling and Disposal

VIDAZA is a hazardous drug.
Follow applicable special handling and disposal procedures.
