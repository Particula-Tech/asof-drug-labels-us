# 11 DESCRIPTION

Azacitidine is a nucleoside metabolic inhibitor with a molecular formula of C8H12N4O5 and a molecular weight of 244 g/mol.
The chemical name is: 4-amino-1-β-D-ribofuranosyl-s-triazin-2(1H)-one and the chemical structural is:

Azacitidine is a white to off-white solid.
Azacitidine was found to be soluble in aqueous media across a pH range from 1.0 to 7.0.

ONUREG (azacitidine) is supplied as film-coated tablets containing 200 mg or 300 mg of azacitidine for oral use.
Each core tablet contains the following inactive ingredients: croscarmellose sodium, magnesium stearate, mannitol, and silicified microcrystalline cellulose.
The 200 and 300 mg tablet coating contains hypromellose, lactose monohydrate, polyethylene glycol, titanium dioxide, and triacetin.
In addition, the 200 mg tablet coating contains iron oxide red and the 300 mg tablet coating contains black iron oxide, iron oxide red, and iron oxide yellow.
