# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Arsenic trioxide injection is supplied as a sterile, clear, colorless solution in 10 mL glass, single-dose vials.

| NDC | Arsenic trioxide injection, 1 mg/mL | Packaging |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 49315-005-10 | 10 mg per 10 mL single-dose vial | 10 vials per carton |

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F).
Do not freeze.

Arsenic trioxide is a cytotoxic drug.
Follow applicable special handling and disposal procedures.1
