# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Arsenic trioxide injection is supplied as a sterile, clear, colorless solution in 10 mL glass, single-dose vial.

NDC 50742-438-10 10 mg/10 mL (1 mg/mL) single dose, flip-off vial in package of 1.

This container closure is not made with natural rubber latex.

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15 - 30°C (59 - 86°F).
Do not freeze.

Arsenic trioxide injection is a cytotoxic drug.
Follow applicable special handling and disposal procedures.1
