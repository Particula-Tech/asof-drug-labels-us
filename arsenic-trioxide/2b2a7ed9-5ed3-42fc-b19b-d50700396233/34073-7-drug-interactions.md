# 7 DRUG INTERACTIONS

Drugs That Can Prolong the QT/QTc Interval

Concomitant use of these drugs and Arsenic Trioxide Injection may increase the risk of serious QT/QTc interval prolongation.
Discontinue or replace with an alternative drug that does not prolong the QT/QTc interval while patient is using Arsenic Trioxide Injection.
Monitor ECGs more frequently in patients when it is not feasible to avoid concomitant use.

Drugs That Can Lead to Electrolyte Abnormalities

Electrolyte abnormalities increase the risk of serious QT/QTc interval prolongation.
Avoid concomitant administration of drugs that can lead to electrolyte abnormalities.
Monitor electrolytes more frequently in patients who must receive concomitant use of these drugs and Arsenic Trioxide Injection.

Drugs That Can Lead to Hepatotoxicity

Concomitant use of these drugs and Arsenic Trioxide Injection, particularly when given in combination with tretinoin, may increase the risk of serious hepatotoxicity.
Discontinue or replace with an alternative drug that does not cause hepatotoxicity while the patient is using Arsenic Trioxide Injection.
Monitor liver function tests more frequently in patients when it is not feasible to avoid concomitant use.
