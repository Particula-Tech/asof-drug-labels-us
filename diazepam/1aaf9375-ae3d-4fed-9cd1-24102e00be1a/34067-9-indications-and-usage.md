# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Diazepam is indicated for the:

- •Treatment of anxiety disorders
- •Short-term treatment of anxiety symptoms
- •Treatment of tremor, acute agitation, hallucinosis, and impending delirium tremens or delirium tremens in acute alcohol withdrawal
- •Adjunctive treatment of skeletal muscle spasm due to:
  - oreflex spasm to local pathology
  - ospasticity caused by upper motor neuron disorders
  - oathetosis
  - ostiff-person syndrome
- •Adjunctive treatment of seizure disorders

Limitations of Use

- •The effectiveness of diazepam for long-term use (greater than 4 months) has not been evaluated.
- •Diazepam is not recommended as maintenance monotherapy for the treatment of seizure disorders.
