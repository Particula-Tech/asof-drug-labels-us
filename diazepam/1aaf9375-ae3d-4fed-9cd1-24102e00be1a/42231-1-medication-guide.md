| What is the most important information I should know about Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution?Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution may cause serious side effects, including: •Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution is a benzodiazepine medicine. Taking benzodiazepines with opioid medicines, alcohol, or other central nervous system (CNS) depressants (including street drugs) can cause severe drowsiness, breathing problems (respiratory depression), coma and death. Get emergency help right away if any of the following happens: oshallow or slowed breathing,obreathing stops (which may lead to the heart stopping),oexcessive sleepiness (sedation). •Risk of abuse, misuse, and addiction. There is a risk of abuse, misuse, and addiction with benzodiazepines, including Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution, which can lead to overdose and serious side effects including coma and death. oSerious side effects including coma and death have happened in people who have abused or misused benzodiazepines, including Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution. These serious side effects may also include delirium, paranoia, suicidal thoughts or actions, seizures, and difficulty breathing. Call your healthcare provider or go to the nearest hospital emergency room right away if you get any of these serious side effects.oYou can develop an addiction even if you take Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution exactly as prescribed by your healthcare provider.oTake Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution exactly as your healthcare provider prescribed.oDo not share your Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution with other people.oKeep Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution in a safe place and away from children. Properly dispose of any unused medicine. •Physical dependence and withdrawal reactions. Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution can cause physical dependence and withdrawal reactions. oDo not suddenly stop taking Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution. Stopping Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution suddenly can cause serious and life-threatening side effects, including, unusual movements, responses, or expressions, seizures, sudden and severe mental or nervous system changes, depression, seeing or hearing things that others do not see or hear, an extreme increase in activity or talking, losing touch with reality, and suicidal thoughts or actions. Call your healthcare provider or go to the nearest hospital emergency room right away if you get any of these symptoms.oSome people who suddenly stop benzodiazepines have symptoms that can last for several weeks to more than 12 months, including, anxiety, trouble remembering, learning, or concentrating, depression, problems sleeping, feeling like insects are crawling under your skin, weakness, shaking, muscle twitching, burning or prickling feeling in your hands, arms, legs or feet, and ringing in your ears.oPhysical dependence is not the same as drug addiction. Your healthcare provider can tell you more about the differences between physical dependence and drug addiction.oDo not take more Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution than prescribed or take Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution for longer than prescribed. •Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution can make you sleepy or dizzy and can slow your thinking and motor skills. oDo not drive, operate heavy machinery, or do other dangerous activities during treatment with Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution.oDo not drink alcohol or take other drugs that may make you sleepy or dizzy during treatment with Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution without first talking to your healthcare provider. When taken with alcohol or drugs that cause sleepiness or dizziness, Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution may make your sleepiness or dizziness much worse. •Suicide actions and thoughts. Like other antiepileptic drugs, Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution may cause suicidal thoughts or actions in a very small number of people, about 1 in 500, including when it is used for other indications besides seizures. Tell your healthcare provider right away if you get any of these symptoms, especially if they are new, worse, or worry you: othoughts about suicide or dyingonew or worse depressionofeeling agitated or restlessotrouble sleeping (insomnia)oacting aggressive, being angry or violentoother unusual changes in behavior or moodosuicide attemptsonew or worse anxiety or irritabilityoan extreme increase in activity and talking (mania)onew or worse panic attacksoacting on dangerous impulses How can I watch for early symptoms of suicidal thoughts and actions? oPay attention to any changes, especially sudden changes, in mood, behaviors, thoughts, or feelings.oKeep all follow-up visits with your healthcare provider as scheduled. Tell your healthcare provider between visits as needed, especially if you are worried about symptoms. Suicidal thoughts or actions can be caused by things other than medicines. If you have suicidal thoughts or actions, your healthcare provider may check for other causes. See “What are the possible side effects of Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution?” for more information about side effects. |
| What is Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution?Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution is a prescription medicine used: •to treat anxiety disorders•short-term to treat anxiety symptoms•To treat symptoms of suddenly stopping drinking alcohol (acute alcohol withdrawal) which can include shaking (tremor), agitation, seeing or hearing things that others do not see or hear (hallucinations), and sudden and severe mental or nervous system changes (delirium tremens)•along with other medicines to treat muscle spasms from: oinjuries or strainsocertain neurological conditionsoa movement disorder called athetosisostiff-person syndrome •along with other medicines to treat seizure disorders Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution is a federal controlled substance (C-IV) because it contains diazepam that can be abused or lead to dependence. Keep Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution in a safe place to prevent misuse and abuse. Selling or giving away Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution may harm others and is against the law. Tell your healthcare provider if you have abused or been dependent on alcohol, prescription medicines or street drugs.It is not known if Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution is effective for use for longer than 4 months.Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution is not recommended alone as maintenance therapy for the treatment of seizure disorders.Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution is not recommended for use in children less than 6 months of age. |
| Who should not take Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution?Do not take Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution if you: •are allergic to diazepam or any of the ingredients in Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution. See the end of this Medication Guide for a complete list of ingredients in Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution.•have an eye problem called acute narrow-angle glaucoma |
| Before taking Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution, tell your healthcare provider about all of your medical conditions, including if you: •have a history of drug or alcohol abuse•have or had depression, mood problems, or suicidal thoughts or behavior•have lung or breathing problems•have liver or kidney problems•are pregnant or plan to become pregnant. oTaking Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution late in pregnancy can cause your baby to have symptoms of sedation (breathing problems, sluggishness, low muscle tone) and/or withdrawal symptoms (jitteriness, irritability, restlessness, shaking, excessive crying, feeding problems).oTell your healthcare provider right away if you become pregnant or think you are pregnant during treatment with Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution.oThere are pregnancy registries for women who take Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution during pregnancy. The purpose of these registries is to collect information about the health of you and your baby. If you become pregnant during treatment with Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution, talk to your healthcare provider about registering with the appropriate registry: ▪the National Pregnancy Registry for Psychiatric Medications by calling 1-866-961-2388 or visiting https://womensmentalhealth.org/researchpregnancyregistry/.▪the North American Antiepileptic Drug (NAAED) Pregnancy Registry by calling 1-888-233-2334 or visiting http://www.aedpregnancyregistry.org/. •are breastfeeding or plan to breastfeed. Diazepam passes into your breast milk. oTalk to your healthcare provider about the best way to feed your baby during treatment with Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution.oIf you breastfeed during treatment with Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution, monitor your baby for excessive sleepiness, poor feeding, and poor weight gain. Get medical help right away if your baby gets any of these symptoms. Tell your healthcare provider about all the medicines you take, including prescription and over-the-counter medicines, vitamins, and herbal supplements. Taking Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution with certain other medicines can cause side effects or affect how well Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution or the other medicines work. Do not start or stop other medicines without talking to your healthcare provider. |

| What are the possible side effects of Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution?Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution may cause serious side effects, including: •See “What is the most important information I should know about Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution?”•Worsening of preexisting depression. Tell your healthcare provider if you get symptoms of worsening depression.•Abnormal thoughts and behavior (paradoxical reactions). Symptoms include aggressive behavior, agitation, anger, irritability, confusion, beliefs that are not true, hallucinations, anxiety, nightmares, and increased muscle stiffness or tightness. These reactions are more likely to happen in children and elderly people. Tell your healthcare provider if you get any of these symptoms.•Slowed and stopped breathing that may lead to death when benzodiazepines, including Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution, are taken alone or with certain other medicines. Slowed and stopped breathing has happened in people who have breathing problems and take benzodiazepines like Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution. Tell your healthcare provider right away if you get any breathing problems.•Increase in eye pressure in people with open-angle glaucoma. See “Who should not take Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution?” The most common side effects of Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution include: •drowsiness•tiredness•muscle weakness•loss of control of body movements (ataxia) These are not all the possible side effects of Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution.Call your doctor for medical advice about side effects. You may report side effects to FDA at 1-800-FDA-1088.You may also report side effects to Hikma Pharmaceuticals USA Inc. at 1-800-962-8364. |

Keep Diazepam Intensol™ Oral Solution and all medicines out of the reach of children. |
