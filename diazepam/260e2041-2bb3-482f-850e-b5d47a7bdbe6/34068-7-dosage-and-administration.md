# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

## 2.1 Recommended Dosage in Adults Less Than 65 Years of Age

Table 1 provides the recommended oral dosage of VALIUM by indication for adults less than 65 years of age.

Table 1: Recommended Dosage in Adults Less than 65 Years of Age

| Indication | Recommended Oral Dosage |
| --- | --- |
| Short-term treatment of anxiety symptoms | 2 mg to 10 mg, two to four times daily |
| Treatment of acute alcohol withdrawal[see Indications and Usage (1) for the complete indication] | 10 mg, three to four times during the first 24 hours; after 24 hours reduce dosage to 5 mg, three to four times daily, as needed. The recommended dosage is intended for most patients. However, based on severity of symptoms, some patients may require higher dosages. In such cases, increase the dosage gradually, as needed and tolerated, to reduce the risk of adverse reactions. |
| Adjunctive treatment of skeletal muscle spasm | 2 mg to 10 mg, three to four times daily |
| Adjunctive treatment of seizure disorders | 2 mg to 10 mg, two to four times daily |

## 2.2 Recommended Dosage in Specific Populations

Pediatric Patients 6 Months of Age and Older

The recommended starting dosage in pediatric patients 6 months of age and older is 1 mg to 2.5 mg orally, three or four times daily.
Increase the dosage gradually as needed and tolerated [see Use in Specific Populations (8.4)].

Geriatric or Debilitated Patients

The recommended starting dosage in geriatric patients 65 years of age and older or debilitated patients is 2 mg to 2.5 mg orally, once or twice daily.
Increase the dosage gradually as needed and tolerated [see Use in Specific Populations (8.5, 8.8)].

Patients with Hepatic Impairment

In patients with mild or moderate hepatic impairment, reduce the recommended dosage by half compared to those with normal hepatic function [see Dosage and Administration (2.1)].
VALIUM is not recommended in patients with severe hepatic impairment [see Use in Specific Populations (8.6)].

## 2.3 Discontinuation or Dosage Reduction of VALIUM

To reduce the risk of withdrawal reactions, use a gradual taper to discontinue VALIUM or reduce the dosage.
If a patient develops withdrawal reactions, consider pausing the taper or increasing the dosage to the previous tapered dosage level.
Subsequently decrease the dosage more slowly [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3), Drug Abuse and Dependence (9.3)].
