# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

VALIUM tablets are round, flat-faced and scored with a V-shaped perforation and beveled edges:

- 2 mg: white, engraved with 2 and VALIUM® on the outside edge and vertically scored on reverse side
- 5 mg: yellow, engraved with 5 and VALIUM® on the outside edge and vertically scored on reverse side
- 10 mg: blue, engraved with 10 and VALIUM® on the outside edge and vertically scored on reverse side
