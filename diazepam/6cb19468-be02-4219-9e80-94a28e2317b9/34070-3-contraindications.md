# CONTRAINDICATIONS

Diazepam is contraindicated in patients with a known hypersensitivity to diazepam and, because of lack of sufficient clinical experience, in pediatric patients under 6 months of age.
Diazepam is also contraindicated in patients with myasthenia gravis, severe respiratory insufficiency, severe hepatic insufficiency, and sleep apnea syndrome.
It may be used in patients with open- angle glaucoma who are receiving appropriate therapy, but is contraindicated in acute narrow-angle glaucoma.
