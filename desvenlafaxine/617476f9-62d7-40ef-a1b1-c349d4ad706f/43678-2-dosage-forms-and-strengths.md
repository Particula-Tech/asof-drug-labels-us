# ​3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Desvenlafaxine extended-release tablets are available as 25mg, 50 mg and 100 mg tablets.

25 mg, light pink, square pyramid tablet imprinted with “A112” in black ink on the flat side

50 mg, light pink, square pyramid tablet imprinted with “WPI” over “3659” on the flat side

100 mg, reddish-orange, square pyramid tablet imprinted with “WPI” over “3660” on the flat side
