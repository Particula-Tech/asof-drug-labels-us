# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Desvenlafaxine extended-release tablet, a serotonin and norepinephrine reuptake inhibitor (SNRI), is indicated for the treatment of major depressive disorder (MDD) [see CLINICAL STUDIES (14) and DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION (2.1)].
The efficacy of desvenlafaxine has been established in four short-term (8-week, placebo-controlled studies) and two maintenance studies in adult outpatients who met DSM-IV criteria for major depressive disorder.
