# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

## 2.1 Recommended Dosing

The recommended dose of TRADJENTA is 5 mg once daily.

TRADJENTA tablets can be taken with or without food.

## 2.2 Concomitant Use with a Sulfonylurea

When TRADJENTA is used in combination with an insulin secretagogue (e.g., sulfonylurea), a lower dose of the insulin secretagogue may be required to reduce the risk of hypoglycemia [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
