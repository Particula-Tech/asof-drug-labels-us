# 7 DRUG INTERACTIONS

## 7.1 Inducers of P-glycoprotein or CYP3A4 Enzymes

Rifampin decreased linagliptin exposure suggesting that the efficacy of TRADJENTA may be reduced when administered in combination with a strong P-gp or CYP 3A4 inducer.
Therefore, use of alternative treatments is strongly recommended when linagliptin is to be administered with P-gp or CYP 3A4 inducer [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].
