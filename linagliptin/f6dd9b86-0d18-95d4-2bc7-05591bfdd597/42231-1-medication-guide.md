| What are JENTADUETO and JENTADUETO XR? JENTADUETO and JENTADUETO XR are prescription medicines that contain 2 diabetes medicines, linagliptin and immediate- or extended-release metformin. JENTADUETO or JENTADUETO XR can be used along with diet and exercise to lower blood sugar in adults with type 2 diabetes when treatment with both linagliptin and metformin is appropriate.JENTADUETO and JENTADUETO XR are not for people with type 1 diabetes.JENTADUETO and JENTADUETO XR are not for people with diabetic ketoacidosis (increased ketones in the blood or urine).If you have had pancreatitis in the past, it is not known if you have a higher chance of getting pancreatitis while you take JENTADUETO or JENTADUETO XR.It is not known if JENTADUETO or JENTADUETO XR is safe and effective in children under 18 years of age. |  |  |  |  |

See the end of this Medication Guide for a complete list of ingredients in JENTADUETO or JENTADUETO XR. | How should I take JENTADUETO or JENTADUETO XR? Take JENTADUETO or JENTADUETO XR exactly as your doctor tells you to take it.Take JENTADUETO or JENTADUETO XR each day with meals. Taking JENTADUETO or JENTADUETO XR with meals may lower your chance of having an upset stomach.Take JENTADUETO 2 times each day.Take JENTADUETO XR 1 time each day.Take JENTADUETO XR tablets whole. Do not break, cut, crush, dissolve, or chew JENTADUETO XR tablets before swallowing. If you cannot swallow JENTADUETO XR tablets whole, tell your doctor.You may see something that looks like the JENTADUETO XR tablet in your stool (bowel movement). If you see tablets in your stool, talk to your doctor. Do not stop taking JENTADUETO XR without talking to your doctor.If you miss a dose, take it with food as soon as you remember. If you do not remember until it is time for your next dose, skip the missed dose and go back to your regular schedule. Do not take 2 doses of JENTADUETO or JENTADUETO XR at the same time.If you take too much JENTADUETO or JENTADUETO XR, call your doctor or Poison Control Center at 1-800-222-1222 or go to the nearest hospital emergency room right away.Your doctor may tell you to take JENTADUETO or JENTADUETO XR along with other diabetes medicines. Low blood sugar can happen more often when JENTADUETO or JENTADUETO XR is taken with certain other diabetes medicines. | What is type 2 diabetes?Type 2 diabetes is a condition in which your body does not make enough insulin, and/or the insulin that your body produces does not work as well as it should. Your body can also make too much sugar. When this happens, sugar (glucose) builds up in the blood. This can lead to serious medical problems. The main goal of treating diabetes is to lower your blood sugar to a normal level. High blood sugar can be lowered by diet and exercise, and by certain medicines when necessary.Talk to your doctor about how to prevent, recognize, and take care of low blood sugar (hypoglycemia), high blood sugar (hyperglycemia), and other problems you have because of your diabetes. |  |  |  |  |
