# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

TORPENZtablets® for oral use are available containing 2.5 mg, 5 mg, 7.5 mg, or 10 mg of everolimus.

The 2.5 mg tablets are white to off white, capsule shaped, flat faced, beveled edged tablets debossed with "B 2.5" on one side and plain on the other side.
They are supplied as follows:

Bottles of 30 with a child-resistant closure, NDC 0245-0822-30

Unit-Dose cartons of 28, NDC 0245-0822-04

(4 blister cards with embedded desiccants containing 7 tablets each)
