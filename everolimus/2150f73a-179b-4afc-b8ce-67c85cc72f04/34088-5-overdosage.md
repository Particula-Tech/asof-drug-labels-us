# 10 OVERDOSAGE

In animal studies, everolimus showed a low acute toxic potential.
No lethality or severe toxicity were observed in either mice or rats given single oral doses of 2000 mg/kg (limit test).

Reported experience with overdose in humans is very limited.
Single doses of up to 70 mg have been administered.
The acute toxicity profile observed with the 70 mg dose was consistent with that for the 10 mg dose.
