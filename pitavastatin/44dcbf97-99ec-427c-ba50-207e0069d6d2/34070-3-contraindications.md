# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

LIVALO is contraindicated in the following conditions:

- Concomitant use of cyclosporine [see Drug Interactions (7)].
- Acute liver failure or decompensated cirrhosis [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3)].
- Hypersensitivity to pitavastatin or any excipents in LIVALO.
  Hypersensitivity reactions including angioedema, rash, pruritus, and urticaria have been reported with LIVALO [see Adverse Reactions (6)].
