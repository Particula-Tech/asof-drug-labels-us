# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Pitavastatin tablets are contraindicated in the following conditions: • Concomitant use of cyclosporine [see Drug Interactions (7)]. • Acute liver failure or decompensated cirrhosis [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3)]. • Hypersensitivity to pitavastatin or any excipents in pitavastatin.
Hypersensitivity reactions including angioedema, rash, pruritus, and urticaria have been reported with pitavastatin [see Adverse Reactions (6)].
