# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Pitavastatin tablets 1 mg are white to off-white, round, bevel edged biconvex film coated tablets debossed with ‘H1’ on one side and ‘P’ on other side.
They are supplied as follows: Bottles of 90 tablets NDC 31722-875-90 Pitavastatin tablets 2 mg are white to off-white, round, bevel edged biconvex film coated tablets debossed with ‘H2’ on one side and ‘P’ on other side.
They are supplied as follows: Bottles of 90 tablets NDC 31722-876-90 Pitavastatin tablets 4 mg are white to off-white, round, biconvex film coated tablets debossed with ‘H4’ on one side and ‘P’ on other side.
They are supplied as follows: Bottles of 90 tablets NDC 31722-877-90 Storage Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light.
