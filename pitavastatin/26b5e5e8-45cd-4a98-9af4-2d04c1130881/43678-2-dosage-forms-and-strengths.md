# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Pitavastatin Tablets are available containing 1.045 mg, 2.090 mg or 4.180 mg of pitavastatin calcium equivalent to 1 mg, 2 mg or 4 mg of pitavastatin, respectively.

- •The 1 mg tablets are white to off-white, film-coated, round, unscored tablets debossed with M on one side of the tablet and PU1 on the other side.
- •The 2 mg tablets are white to off-white, film-coated, round, unscored tablets debossed with M on one side of the tablet and PU2 on the other side.
- •The 4 mg tablets are white to off-white, film-coated, round, unscored tablets debossed with M on one side of the tablet and PU4 on the other side.
