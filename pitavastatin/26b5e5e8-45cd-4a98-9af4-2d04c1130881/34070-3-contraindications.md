# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Pitavastatin tablets are contraindicated in the following conditions:

- •Concomitant use of cyclosporine [see Drug Interactions (7)].
- •Acute liver failure or decompensated cirrhosis [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3)].
- •Hypersensitivity to pitavastatin or any excipients in pitavastatin tablets.
  Hypersensitivity reactions including angioedema, rash, pruritus, and urticaria have been reported with pitavastatin tablets [see Adverse Reactions (6)].
