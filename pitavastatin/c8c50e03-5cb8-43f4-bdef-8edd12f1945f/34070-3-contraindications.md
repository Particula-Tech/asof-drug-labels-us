# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

ZYPITAMAG is contraindicated in the following conditions:

- Known hypersensitivity to pitavastatin or any inactive ingredient in ZYPITAMAG.
  Hypersensitivity reactions including angioedema, rash, pruritus, and urticaria have been reported with pitavastatin [see Adverse Reactions (6.1)].
- Concomitant use of cyclosporine [see Drug Interactions (7)].
- Active liver disease including unexplained persistent elevations of hepatic transaminase levels [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3)].
- Pregnancy [see Use in Specific Populations (8.1, 8.3)].
- Lactation.
  It is not known if pitavastatin is present in human milk; however, another drug in this class passes into breast milk.
  Since HMG-CoA reductase inhibitors have the potential for serious adverse reactions in breastfed infants, females who require pitavastatin treatment should not breastfeed their infants [see Use in Specific Populations (8.2)].
