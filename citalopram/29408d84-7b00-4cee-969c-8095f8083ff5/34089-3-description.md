# DESCRIPTION

Citalopram hydrobromide is an orally administered selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor (SSRI) with a chemical structure unrelated to that of other SSRI's or of tricyclic, tetracyclic, or other available antidepressant agents.
Citalopram hydrobromide is a racemic bicyclic phthalane derivative designated (±)-1-(3-dimethylaminopropyl)-1-(4-fluorophenyl)-1,3-dihydroisobenzofuran-5-carbonitrile, HBr with the following structural formula:

The molecular formula is C20H22BrFN2O and its molecular weight is 405.35.

Citalopram hydrobromide occurs as a fine white to off-white powder.
Citalopram hydrobromide is sparingly soluble in water and soluble in ethanol.

Citalopram hydrobromide is available as an oral solution.

Citalopram Hydrobromide Oral Solution contains citalopram hydrobromide equivalent to 2 mg/mL citalopram base.
It also contains the following inactive ingredients: methylparaben, peppermint stick, propylene glycol, propylparaben, purified water, and sorbitol solution.
