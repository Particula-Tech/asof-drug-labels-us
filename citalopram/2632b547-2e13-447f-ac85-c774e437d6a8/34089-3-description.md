# DESCRIPTION

Citalopram hydrobromide, an orally administered selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor (SSRI) with a chemical structure unrelated to that of other SSRIs or of tricyclic, tetracyclic, or other available antidepressant agents.
Citalopram hydrobromide is a racemic bicyclic phthalane derivative designated (±)-1-(3-dimethylaminopropyl)-1-(4-fluorophenyl)-1,3-dihydroisobenzofuran-5-carbonitrile, hydrobromide with the following structural formula:

The molecular formula is C20H22BrFN2O and its molecular weight is 405.35.
Citalopram hydrobromide USP occurs as a fine, white to off-white powder.
Citalopram hydrobromide is sparingly soluble in water and soluble in ethanol.
Citalopram is available as film-coated round and capsule shaped tablets.
The 20 mg and 40 mg tablets are scored in tablet dosage form.
Their strengths reflect their citalopram base equivalent content.
The 10 mg, 20 mg and 40 mg strength tablets contain 12.49 mg, 24.98 mg and 49.96 mg of citalopram hydrobromide, respectively.
The tablets also contain the following inactive ingredients: copovidone, corn starch, croscarmellose sodium, lactose monohydrate, magnesium stearate, hypromellose, microcrystalline cellulose, polyethylene glycol, and titanium dioxide.
Iron oxides are used as coloring agents in the peach (10 mg) and light pink (20 mg) tablets.
