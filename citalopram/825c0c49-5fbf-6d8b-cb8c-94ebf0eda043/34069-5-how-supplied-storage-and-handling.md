# HOW SUPPLIED

Citalopram Tablets, USP are available containing 10 mg, 20 mg or 40 mg of citalopram base.

The 10 mg tablets are film-coated beige, round, biconvex unscored tablets debossed with MX31 on one side of the tablet and blank on the other side.
They are available as follows:

NDC 0378-6231-01 bottles of 100 tablets

NDC 0378-6231-05 bottles of 500 tablets

The 20 mg tablets are film-coated pink, round, biconvex scored tablets debossed with MX32 on one side of the tablet and blank on the other side.
They are available as follows:

NDC 0378-6232-01 bottles of 100 tablets

NDC 0378-6232-05 bottles of 500 tablets

The 40 mg tablets are film-coated white, round, biconvex scored tablets debossed with MX33 on one side of the tablet and blank on the other side.
They are available as follows:

NDC 0378-6233-01 bottles of 100 tablets

NDC 0378-6233-05 bottles of 500 tablets

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[See USP for Controlled Room Temperature.]

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP using a child-resistant closure.

PHARMACIST: Dispense a Medication Guide with each prescription.
