# CONTRAINDICATIONS

Concomitant use in patients taking monoamine oxidase inhibitors (MAOIs) is contraindicated (see WARNINGS).

Citalopram is contraindicated in patients with congenital long QT syndrome (see WARNINGS, PRECAUTIONS, and Drug Interactions).

Concomitant use in patients taking pimozide is contraindicated (see PRECAUTIONS).

Citalopram HBr is contraindicated in patients with a hypersensitivity to citalopram or any of the inactive ingredients in citalopram HBr tablets.
