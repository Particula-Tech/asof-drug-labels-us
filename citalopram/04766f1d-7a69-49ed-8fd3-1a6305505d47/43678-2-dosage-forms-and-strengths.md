# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Citalopram tablets, USP are available as: • 10 mg: Tan coloured, round shaped, biconvex film coated tablets with ‘10’ debossed on one side and plain on the other side. • 20 mg: Tan coloured, oval shaped, biconvex film coated tablets with ‘2│0’ debossed (‘2’ on left side and ‘0’ on right side of the break line) on one side and ‘1010’ on the other side. • 40 mg: Tan coloured, oval shaped, biconvex film coated tablets with ‘4│0’ debossed (‘4’ on left side and ‘0’ on right side of the break line) on one side and ‘1011’ on the other side
