JalynTM

(dutasteride and tamsulosin HCl)

Capsules

0.5 mg/ 0.4 mg

Rx only

30 Capsules

WARNING: JALYN should not be used by women or children.
Women who are or may potentially be pregnant should not use or handle JALYN Capsules (see prescribing information).
If contact is made with leaking capsule, wash immediately with soap and water.

Capsules should be swallowed whole and not chewed or opened.

Each capsule contains 0.5 mg dutasteride and 0.4 mg tamsulosin hydrochloride.

Usual Dosage: 1 capsule once daily.
See prescribing information for dosage information.

Store at 25oC (77oF); excursions permitted 15o to 30oC (59o to 86oF) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Dispense in a well-closed container as defined in the USP.

Do not use if printed safety seal under cap is broken or missing.
