# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Tamsulosin hydrochloride capsules USP, 0.4 mg are supplied in high density polyethylene bottles as follows: Hard gelatin capsules, size ‘2’ Olive Green colored cap and Light Yellow colored body with “160” imprinted in black ink on cap and body, containing white to off white pellets.
Bottles of 30’s with Child Resistant Cap……………NDC 62756-160-83 Bottles of 100’s with Child Resistant Cap…………..NDC 62756-160-88 Bottles of 100’s with Non Child Resistant Cap……..NDC 62756-160-08 Bottles of 500’s with Non Child Resistant Cap……..NDC 62756-160-13 Bottles of 1000’s with Non Child Resistant Cap…....NDC 62756-160-18 Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15° and 30°C (59° and 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Keep tamsulosin hydrochloride capsules and all medicines out of reach of children.
