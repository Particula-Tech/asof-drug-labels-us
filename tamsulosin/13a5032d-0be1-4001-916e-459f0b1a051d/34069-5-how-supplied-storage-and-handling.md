# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Tamsulosin hydrochloride capsules USP 0.4 mg are supplied in high density polyethylene bottles containing 30, 100 or 500 hard gelatin capsules with an olive green opaque cap and an orange opaque body.
The cap is imprinted with the code "TSL 0.4" in black ink.

Tamsulosin hydrochloride capsules USP, 30 capsules (NDC 63672-0021-1).

Tamsulosin hydrochloride capsules USP, 100 capsules (NDC 63672-0021-6).

Tamsulosin hydrochloride capsules USP, 500 capsules (NDC 63672-0021-7).

Store at 25°C (77°F),. excursions permitted to 15°– 30°C (59°– 86°F)[see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Keep tamsulosin hydrochloride capsules USP and all medicines out of reach of children.
