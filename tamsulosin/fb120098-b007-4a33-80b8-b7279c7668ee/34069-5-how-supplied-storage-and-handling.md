# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Tamsulosin hydrochloride capsules USP, 0.4 mg are supplied in high density polyethylene bottles containing hard gelatin capsules with light green cap and yellow body imprinted with “TML 0.4” on cap and body.

NDC 0781-2076-01 in bottles of 100 capsules

NDC 0781-2076-10 in bottles of 1000 capsules

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15°-30°C (59°-86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Keep tamsulsoin hydrochloride capsules, USP and all medicines out of reach of children.
