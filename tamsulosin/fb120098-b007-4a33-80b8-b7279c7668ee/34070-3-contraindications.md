# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Tamsulosin hydrochloride capsules, USP are contraindicated in patients known to be hypersensitive to tamsulosin hydrochloride or any component of tamsulosin hydrochloride capsules, USP.
Reactions have included skin rash, urticaria, pruritus, angioedema, and respiratory symptoms [see ADVERSE REACTIONS (6.2)].
