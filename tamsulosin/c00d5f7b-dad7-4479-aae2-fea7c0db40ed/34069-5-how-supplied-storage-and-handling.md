# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

FLOMAX capsules 0.4 mg are supplied in high density polyethylene bottles containing 100 hard gelatin capsules with olive green opaque cap and orange opaque body.
The capsules are imprinted on one side with Flomax 0.4 mg and on the other side with BI 58.

FLOMAX capsules 0.4 mg, 100 capsules (NDC 0597-0058-01)

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C–30°C (59°F–86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Keep FLOMAX capsules and all medicines out of reach of children.
