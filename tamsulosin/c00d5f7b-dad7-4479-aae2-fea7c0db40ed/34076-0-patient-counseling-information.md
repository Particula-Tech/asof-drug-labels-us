# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

See FDA-Approved Patient Labeling (17.6).

## 17.1 Hypotension

Patients should be told about the possible occurrence of symptoms related to postural hypotension, such as dizziness, when taking FLOMAX capsules, and they should be cautioned about driving, operating machinery, or performing hazardous tasks [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].

## 17.2 Priapism

Patients should be advised about the possibility of priapism as a result of treatment with FLOMAX capsules and other similar medications.
Patients should be informed that this reaction is extremely rare, but if not brought to immediate medical attention, can lead to permanent erectile dysfunction (impotence) [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3)].

## 17.3 Screening for Prostate Cancer

Prostate cancer and BPH frequently co-exist; therefore, patients should be screened for the presence of prostate cancer prior to treatment with FLOMAX capsules and at regular intervals afterwards [see Warnings and Precautions (5.4)].

## 17.4 Intraoperative Floppy Iris Syndrome

Patients considering cataract surgery should be advised to tell their ophthalmologist that they have taken FLOMAX capsules [see Warnings and Precautions (5.5)].

## 17.5 Administration

Patients should be advised not to crush or chew the FLOMAX capsules.

Patient labeling is provided as a tear-off leaflet at the end of this prescribing information.
