# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Bimatoprost ophthalmic solution, 0.03% is supplied sterile in opaque white low density polyethylene bottle which is closed with white low density polyethylene nozzle and then with turquoise colored high density polyethylene cap in the following sizes: 2.5 mL fill in a 5 mL container - NDC 68083-295-01 5 mL fill in a 10 mL container - NDC 68083-296-01 7.5 mL fill in a 10 mL container - NDC 68083-439-01 Storage: Store at 2° to 25°C (36° to 77°F).
After opening, bimatoprost ophthalmic solution, 0.03% can be used until the expiration date stamped on the bottle.
