Bimatoprost ophthalmic solution, 0.03% is supplied sterile in opaque white low density polyethylene bottle which is closed with white low density polyethylene nozzle and then with turquoise colored high density polyethylene cap in the following sizes: 2.5 mL fill in a 5 mL container - NDC 62332-507-25 5 mL fill in a 10 mL container - NDC 62332-507-05 Storage: Store at 2 to 25°C (36 to 77°F).
