# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

LUMIGAN® (bimatoprost ophthalmic solution) 0.01% is supplied sterile in opaque white low density

polyethylene ophthalmic dispenser bottles and tips with turquoise polystyrene caps in the following sizes:

2.5 mL fill in a 5 mL container - NDC 0023-3205-03 5 mL fill in a 10 mL container - NDC 0023-3205-05 7.5 mL fill in a 10 mL container - NDC 0023-3205-08

Storage: Store at 2°C to 25°C (36°F to 77°F).

After opening, LUMIGAN® 0.01% can be used until the expiration date on the bottle.
