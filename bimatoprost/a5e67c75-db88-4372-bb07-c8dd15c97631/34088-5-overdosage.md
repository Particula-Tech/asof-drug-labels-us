# 10 OVERDOSAGE

No information is available on overdosage in humans.
If overdose with LUMIGAN® 0.01% and 0.03% (bimatoprost ophthalmic solution) occurs, treatment should be symptomatic.

In oral (by gavage) mouse and rat studies, doses up to 100 mg/kg/day did not produce any toxicity.
This dose expressed as mg/m2 is at least 70 times higher than the accidental dose of one bottle of LUMIGAN® 0.03% for a 10 kg child.
