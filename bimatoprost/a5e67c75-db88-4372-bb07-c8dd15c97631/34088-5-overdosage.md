# 10 OVERDOSAGE

No information is available on overdosage in humans.
If overdose with LUMIGAN® (bimatoprost ophthalmic solution) 0.01% occurs, treatment should be symptomatic.

In oral (by gavage) mouse and rat studies, doses up to 100 mg/kg/day did not produce any toxicity.
This

dose expressed as mg/m2 is at least 210 times higher than the accidental dose of one bottle of LUMIGAN® 0.01% for a 10 kg child.
