# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Bimatoprost ophthalmic solution, 0.03% is supplied sterile in white opaque polypropylene bottles with translucent polypropylene droppers and turquoise opaque polypropylene caps in the following sizes:

2.5 mL fill in 5 mL container - NDC 60505-0583-4 5 mL fill in 5 mL container - NDC 60505-0583-1 7.5 mL fill in 10 mL container - NDC 60505-0583-5

Storage: Store at 2°C to 25°C (36°F to 77°F).
After opening, bimatoprost ophthalmic solution, 0.03% can be used until the expiration date stamped on the bottle.
