## 16.1 How Supplied

The 10 mg capsules are hard-shell gelatin capsules with a purple opaque cap and purple opaque body filled with white to off-white powder.
The capsules are axially printed with MYLAN over LE 10 in black ink on cap and body.

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

Protect from light.

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP using a child-resistant closure.

PHARMACIST: Dispense a Medication Guide with each prescription.
