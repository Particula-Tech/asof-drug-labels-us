## 7.2 Interference with Laboratory Test

Allow for an adequate washout period between administration of lisdexamfetamine dimesylate and radioactive diagnostic agents used for dopamine transporter (DAT) visualization.
Lisdexamfetamine dimesylate can interfere with the test results of a radioactive diagnostic agent (ioflupane I-123) that is used for DAT visualization by binding and internalization of the DAT, which may result in lower DAT in the striatum.
This may lead to false-positive diagnostic results.
