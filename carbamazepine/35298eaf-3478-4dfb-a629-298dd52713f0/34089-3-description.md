# DESCRIPTION

Carbamazepine USP, is an anticonvulsant and specific analgesic for trigeminal neuralgia, available for oral administration as a suspension of 100 mg/5 mL (teaspoon).
Its chemical name is 5 H-dibenz[b,f]azepine-5-carboxamide, and its structural formula is:

Carbamazepine, USP is a white to off-white powder, practically insoluble in water and soluble in alcohol and in acetone.
Its molecular weight is 236.27.

Inactive Ingredients: citric acid anhydrous, FD&C Yellow No. 6, hypromellose, potassium sorbate, propylene glycol, purified water, sorbitol solution, sucrose, vanillin, natural lime flavor, natural orange flavor and xanthan gum.
It may contain 10% citric acid solution or 10% sodium citrate solution to adjust pH between 3.0 and 5.0.
