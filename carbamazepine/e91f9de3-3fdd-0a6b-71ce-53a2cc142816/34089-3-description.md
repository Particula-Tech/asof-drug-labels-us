# DESCRIPTION

Carbamazepine extended-release capsules are an anticonvulsant and specific analgesic for trigeminal neuralgia, available for oral administration as 100 mg, 200 mg and 300 mg extended-release capsules of Carbamazepine, USP.
Carbamazepine is a white to off-white powder, practically insoluble in water and soluble in alcohol and in acetone.
Its molecular weight is 236.27.

Carbamazepine extended-release capsules are a multi-component capsule formulation consisting of extended release granules.

Inactive ingredients: ethylcellulose, colloidal silicon dioxide, and stearic acid.

The 100 mg, 200 mg and 300 mg capsule shells contain gelatin-NF, FD&C Blue #2, yellow iron oxide, titanium dioxide and are imprinted with black ink.
