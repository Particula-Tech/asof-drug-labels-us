# 11 DESCRIPTION

CARNEXIV (carbamazepine) injection is available as a clear, colorless, sterile solution for intravenous infusion.

The chemical name of carbamazepine, an anticonvulsant, is 5H-dibenz[b,f]azepine-5-carboxamide, and its molecular weight is 236.27.

Carbamazepine is a white to off-white powder, practically insoluble in water and soluble in alcohol and in acetone.

CARNEXIV injection is supplied in single-dose 20 mL vials containing 200 mg carbamazepine.
Each mL contains 10 mg of carbamazepine, 250 mg of betadex sulfobutyl ether sodium, and 0.78 mg of sodium phosphate monobasic dihydrate in water for injection.
The product may contain sodium hydroxide and/or hydrochloric acid for pH adjustment to pH 6.2.
