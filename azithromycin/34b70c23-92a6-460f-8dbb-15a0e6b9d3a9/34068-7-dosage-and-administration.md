# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

## 2.1 Important Administration Instructions

Administer azithromycin tablets with or without food.
However, increased tolerability has been observed when tablets are taken with food.

## 2.2 Recommended Dosage for Sexually Transmitted Diseases

The recommended dose of azithromycin for the treatment of non-gonococcal urethritis and cervicitis due to C.trachomatis is a single 1 g (1000 mg) dose of azithromycin tablets.

## 2.3 Recommended Dosage for Mycobacterial Infections

Prevention of Disseminated MAC Infections

The recommended dose of azithromycin for the prevention of disseminated Mycobacterium avium complex (MAC) disease is: 1,200 mg taken once weekly.
This dose of azithromycin may be combined with the approved dosage regimen of rifabutin.

Treatment of Disseminated MAC Infections

Azithromycin should be taken at a daily dose of 600 mg, in combination with ethambutol at the recommended daily dose of 15 mg/kg.
Other antimycobacterial drugs that have shown in vitro activity against MAC may be added to the regimen of azithromycin plus ethambutol at the discretion of the physician or health care provider.
