# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

ZITHROMAX Capsules

ZITHROMAX capsules (azithromycin capsules, USP); imprinted with “Pfizer 305” are supplied in red opaque hard gelatin capsules containing azithromycin dihydrate equivalent to 250 mg of azithromycin.
These are packaged in bottles and blister cards of 6 capsules (Z PAKS) as follows; however, ZITHROMAX capsules (azithromycin capsules, USP) are not currently being marketed:

Bottles of 50 NDC 0069-3050-50

Boxes of 3 (Z PAKS of 6) NDC 0069-3050-34

Unit Dose package of 50 NDC 0069-3050-86

Store capsules below 30°C (86°F).

ZITHROMAX Tablets

ZITHROMAX 600 mg tablets (azithromycin tablets, USP); engraved on front with "PFIZER" and on back with "308" are supplied as white, modified oval shaped, film coated tablets containing azithromycin dihydrate equivalent to 600 mg azithromycin.
These are packaged in bottles of 30 tablets.
ZITHROMAX tablets are supplied as follows; however, ZITHROMAX tablets (azithromycin tablets, USP) are not currently being marketed:

Bottles of 30 NDC 0069-3080-30

Store tablets at or below 30°C (86°F).

ZITHROMAX for Oral Suspension

ZITHROMAX for oral suspension (a white to off-white powder with a cherry-banana odor) is supplied in single-dose packets containing azithromycin dihydrate equivalent to 1 g of azithromycin as follows:

Boxes of 3 single-dose packets (1 g) NDC 0069-3051-75

Store single-dose packets below 30°C (86°F).
