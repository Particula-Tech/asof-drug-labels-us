# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

ZITHROMAX for injection is supplied in lyophilized form under a vacuum in a 10 mL vial equivalent to 500 mg of azithromycin for intravenous administration.
Each vial also contains sodium hydroxide and 413.6 mg citric acid.

These are packaged as follows:

|  | 10 vials of 500 mg | NDC 0069-3150-83 |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  | 10 vials of 500 mg with 1 Vial-Mate® Adaptor each | NDC 0069-3150-14 |

Before reconstitution, store vials at or below 30°C (86°F) [see Dosage and Administration (2.3)].
