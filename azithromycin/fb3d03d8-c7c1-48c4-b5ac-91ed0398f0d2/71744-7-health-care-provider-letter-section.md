| Subject: | Zithromax® (azithromycin) 250 mg Tablets to Assist with U.S. Drug Shortage; Product Package and Label Contain Differences from the U.S. Approved Package and Label |

April 2020

Dear Health Care Provider,

Due to the current critical shortage of Azithromycin tablets in the United States (U.S.) market, Pfizer Inc. (Pfizer) is coordinating with the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) to increase the availability of the drug.

In support of the public health and medical response to the COVID-19 pandemic, Pfizer initiated a one-time distribution of Zithromax® (azithromycin) 250 mg Tablets to the U.S. market.
This product was originally intended for use outside of the United States, so its packaging states, "DONATION FOR TREATMENT OF TRACHOMA ONLY" and its labeling does not comply with the U.S. approved labeling for azithromycin.

At this time, no other entity except Pfizer and Direct Relief are authorized by the FDA to distribute this Zithromax® (azithromycin) 250 mg tablets labeled f or Trachoma donation in the United States.

This product is manufactured in Pfizer's Puerto Rico facility, an FDA-registered facility, and is the same manufacturing site that manufactures Zithromax® (azithromycin) 250 mg tablets for the U.S market.

Effective immediately, and during this temporary period as part of this distribution, Pfizer is mak in g the following presentation of Zithromax® (azithromycin) 250 mg tablets available:

Azithromycin is an antibiotic indicated to treat patients with certain bacterial infections caused by susceptible organisms.

T h e Zithromax® (azithromycin) 250 mg tablets labeled "DONATION FOR TREATMENT OF TRACHOMA ONLY" are manufactured using the same process and specifications as the U.S. approved 250 mg tablets.
The key substantive differences are in the format and content of the labeling between U.S approved Zithromax® (azithromycin) 250 mg tablets and the donated Trachoma labeled product.

The U.S. prescribing information for azithromycin, including the FDA approved indications for azithromycin, is contained in the U.S. approved package insert at the end of this letter and is also available on line at https://www.pfizer.com/products/product-detail/zithromax.

The health care provider should refer to the U.S. prescribing information when prescribing the Zithromax® (azithromycin) 250 mg tablets.

Azithromycin is not approved for the treatment of COVID—19 or other viral infections.
The safety of azithromycin treatment, either alone or in combination with other therapies, has not been evaluated in patients with COVID-19.
