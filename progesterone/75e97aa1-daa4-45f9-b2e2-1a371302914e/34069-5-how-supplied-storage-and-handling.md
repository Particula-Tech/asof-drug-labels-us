# HOW SUPPLIED

Crinone is available in the following strengths:

4% gel (45 mg) in a single use, disposable, white polypropylene vaginal applicator with a teal twist-off cap.
Each applicator contains 1.3 g of gel and delivers 1.125 g of gel.

NDC 0023-6150-04: 6 Single-use prefilled applicators.

8% gel (90 mg) in a single use, disposable, white polypropylene vaginal applicator with a teal twist-off cap.
Each applicator contains 1.3 g of gel and delivers 1.125 g of gel.

NDC 0023-6151-08: 15 Single-use prefilled applicators.
Each applicator is wrapped and sealed in a foil overwrap.
Store at 20-25ºC (68-77ºF).
