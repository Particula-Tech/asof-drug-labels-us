# CONTRAINDICATIONS

PROMETRIUM is contraindicated in women with any of the following conditions:

1. 1.Hypersensitivity to its ingredients.
   PROMETRIUM Capsules contain peanut oil and is contraindicated in patients allergic to peanuts.
2. 2.Abnormal genital bleeding of unknown etiology.
3. 3.Known, suspected, or history of breast cancer.
4. 4.Active deep vein thrombosis, pulmonary embolism or history of these conditions.
5. 5.Active arterial thromboembolic disease (for example, stroke and myocardial infarction), or a history of these conditions.
6. 6.Known liver dysfunction or disease.
