# HOW SUPPLIED

Progesterone capsules, 100 mg, are supplied as round, light orange soft gelatin capsules, printed with “A87”.
They are available as follows:

Bottles of 100: NDC 65162-807-10 Bottles of 500: NDC 65162-807-50

Progesterone capsules, 200 mg, are supplied as oval, light orange soft gelatin capsules, printed with “A80”.
They are available as follows:

Bottles of 100: NDC 65162-808-10 Bottles of 500: NDC 65162-808-50

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Protect from excessive moisture.

Dispense in tight, light-resistant container as defined in USP/NF, accompanied by a Patient Insert.

Keep out of reach of children.
