# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

## 2.1 General Dosing Information

The dose of Endometrin is 100 mg administered vaginally two or three times daily starting at oocyte retrieval and continuing for up to 10 weeks total duration.
Efficacy in women 35 years of age and older has not been clearly established.
The appropriate dose of Endometrin in this age group has not been determined.
