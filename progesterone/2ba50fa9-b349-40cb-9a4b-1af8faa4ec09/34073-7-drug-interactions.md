# 7 DRUG INTERACTIONS

Drugs known to induce the hepatic cytochrome-P450-3A4 system may increase the elimination of progesterone, lower progesterone exposure in ENDOMETRIN-treated women, and reduce the efficacy of ENDOMETRIN.

The effect of concomitant vaginal products on the exposure of progesterone from ENDOMETRIN has not been assessed.
ENDOMETRIN is not recommended for use with other vaginal products (such as antifungal products) as this may alter progesterone release and absorption from the ENDOMETRIN vaginal insert.
