# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Roflumilast tablets are indicated as a treatment to reduce the risk of COPD exacerbations in patients with severe COPD associated with chronic bronchitis and a history of exacerbations.

Limitations of Use

Roflumilast is not a bronchodilator and is not indicated for the relief of acute bronchospasm.

Roflumilast tablet 250 mcg is a starting dose, for the first 4 weeks of treatment only and is not the effective (therapeutic) dose.
