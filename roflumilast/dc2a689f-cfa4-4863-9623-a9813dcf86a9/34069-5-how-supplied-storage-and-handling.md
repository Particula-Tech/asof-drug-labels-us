# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Roflumilast tablets 500 mcg are supplied as white to off white colored, circular shaped, flat face, beveled edge, uncoated tablets, debossed with ‘0.5’ on one face and plain on other face.
Each tablet contains 500 mcg of roflumilast.

Roflumilast tablets are available:

Bottles of 30 NDC 42571-259-30

Bottles of 90 NDC 42571-259-90

Bottles of 500 NDC 42571-259-05

Carton of 11x10 Unit-dose Tablets NDC 42571-259-17

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store roflumilast tablets at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
