# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

- Roflumilast 250 mcg tablets are white to off-white, round tablets, flat faced beveled edge, debossed with “T” and “250” on one side and plain on the other side.
- Roflumilast 500 mcg tablets are white to off-white, round tablets, flat faced beveled edge, debossed with “T” and “500” on one side and plain on the other side.
