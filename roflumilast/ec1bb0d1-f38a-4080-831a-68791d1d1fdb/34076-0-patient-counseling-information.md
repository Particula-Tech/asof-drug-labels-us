# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

Advise the patient or caregiver to read the FDA-approved patient labeling (Patient Information).

Lactation

Advise patients to use ZORYVE cream on the smallest area of skin and for the shortest duration possible while breastfeeding.
Instruct patients who are breastfeeding not to apply ZORYVE cream directly to the nipple and areola to avoid direct infant exposure.
Instruct patients to avoid inadvertent contact of treated areas with infant skin [see Use in Specific Populations (8.2)].
