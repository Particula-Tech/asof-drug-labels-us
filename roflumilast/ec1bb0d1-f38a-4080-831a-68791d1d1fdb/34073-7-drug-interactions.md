# 7 DRUG INTERACTIONS

## 7.1 Drugs that Inhibit Cytochrome P450 (CYP) Enzymes

The co-administration of roflumilast with systemic CYP3A4 inhibitors or dual inhibitors that inhibit both CYP3A4 and CYP1A2 simultaneously (e.g., erythromycin, ketoconazole, fluvoxamine, enoxacin, cimetidine) may increase roflumilast systemic exposure and may result in increased adverse reactions.
The risk of such concurrent use should be weighed carefully against benefit [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].

## 7.2 Oral Contraceptives Containing Gestodene and Ethinyl Estradiol

The co-administration of roflumilast with oral contraceptives containing gestodene and ethinyl estradiol may increase roflumilast systemic exposure and may result in increased side effects.
The risk of such concurrent use should be weighed carefully against benefit [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].
