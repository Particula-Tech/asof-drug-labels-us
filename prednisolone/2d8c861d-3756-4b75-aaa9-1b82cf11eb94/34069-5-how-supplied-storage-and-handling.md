# HOW SUPPLIED

Prednisolone sodium phosphate oral solution is a clear, colorless to light yellow-colored, raspberry flavored solution containing 6.7 mg prednisolone sodium phosphate (5 mg prednisolone base) per 5 mL (teaspoonful).

NDC 62135-330-41: 120 mL bottle

Storage and Handling

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
May be refrigerated.
Keep tightly closed and out of the reach of children.
