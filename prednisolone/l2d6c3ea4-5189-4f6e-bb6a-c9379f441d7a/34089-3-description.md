# DESCRIPTION

ECONOPRED® and ECONOPRED® Plus (Prednisolone Acetate) are adrenocortical steroid products prepared as sterile ophthalmic suspensions.
The active ingredient is represented by the chemical structure:

Each mL contains: Active: Prednisolone Acetate 1.0% or 0.125%.
Preservative: Benzalkonium Chloride 0.01%.
Vehicle: Hydroxypropyl Methylcellulose.
Inactive: Dibasic Sodium Phosphate, Polysorbate 80, Edetate Disodium, Glycerin, Citric Acid and/or Sodium Hydroxide (to adjust pH), Purified Water.
DM-02, DM-03
