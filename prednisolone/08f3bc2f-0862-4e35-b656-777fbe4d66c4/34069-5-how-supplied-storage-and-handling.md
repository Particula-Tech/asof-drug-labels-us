# HOW SUPPLIED

PEDIAPRED (prednisolone sodium phosphate, USP) Oral Solution is a colorless to light straw colored solution containing 6.7 mg prednisolone sodium phosphate (5 mg prednisolone base) per 5 mL (teaspoonful).

| NDC 53014-250-01 | 120 mL bottle |
| --- | --- |

Store at 4°-25°C (39°-77°F).
May be refrigerated.
Keep tightly closed and out of the reach of children.
