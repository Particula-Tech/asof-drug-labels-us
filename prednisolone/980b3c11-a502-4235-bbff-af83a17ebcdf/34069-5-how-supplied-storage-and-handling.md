# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Flo-Pred (prednisolone acetate oral suspension) is a cherry flavored milky white viscous liquid containing 5.6 mg or 16.7 mg of prednisolone acetate (equivalent to 5 mg and 15 mg prednisolone, respectively) in each 5 mL of formulation and is supplied in amber plastic bottles as follows:

Flo-Pred (prednisolone acetate oral suspension) 5 mg prednisolone per 5 mL (as 5.6 mg/ 5 mL of prednisolone acetate)

118 mL (NDC 51672- 1337-8)

Flo-Pred (prednisolone acetate oral suspension) 15 mg prednisolone per 5 mL (as 16.7 mg/ 5 mL of prednisolone acetate)

52 mL (NDC 51672-1338-3) 65 mL (NDC 51672-1338-6) 118 mL (NDC 51672-1338-8)

Dispense in the original container.
Do not transfer the bottle contents to other containers to prevent loss of the viscous formulation.

DO NOT REFRIGERATE.

Store between 20°-25°C (68°-77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
