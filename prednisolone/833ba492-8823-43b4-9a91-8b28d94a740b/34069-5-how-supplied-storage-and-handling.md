# HOW SUPPLIED

Prednisolone Sodium Phosphate Oral Solution is a colorless to light straw-colored, raspberry flavored solution containing 6.7 mg prednisolone sodium phosphate (5 mg prednisolone base) per 5 mL (teaspoonful).

NDC 13925-166-04

120 mL bottle

## Storage and Handling

Store at 4°-25°C (39°-77°F).
May be refrigerated.
Keep tightly closed and out of the reach of children.
