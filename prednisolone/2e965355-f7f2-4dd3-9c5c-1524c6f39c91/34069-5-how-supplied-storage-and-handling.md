# HOW SUPPLIED

Prednisolone Tablets, USP, 5 mg are white to off-white, round scored tablets, debossed with “CE” bisect “117” on one side and plain on the other side.
Tablets are supplied in bottles of 30 (NDC 62135-437-30).

Dispense in a well-closed container with child-resistant closure.

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
