# HOW SUPPLIED

Prednisolone Sodium Phosphate Ophthalmic Solution, USP 1% is supplied in a plastic squeeze bottle with a white cap and a controlled drop tip in the following size:

10 mL bottle - NDC 24208-715-10

## Storage:

Store between 15°-25°C (59°-77°F).

Protect from light.
Keep tightly closed.

DO NOT USE IF IMPRINTED NECKBAND IS NOT INTACT.

Keep out of reach of children.
