Unit Dose Cup 10 mL NDC 68999-526-10

20 Unit Dose Cups of 10 mL each NDC 68999-526-24

Store at controlled room temperature, between 20°C and 25°C (68° to 77°F) excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Protect from freezing.
