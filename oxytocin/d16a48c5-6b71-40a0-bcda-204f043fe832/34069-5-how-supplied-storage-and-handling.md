# HOW SUPPLIED

Oxytocin Injection, USP (synthetic) is supplied as follows:

NDC 0143-9743-25 Package of 25.
1 mL fill in a 2 mL vial, 10 USP Units per mL.

NDC 0143-9742-25 Package of 25.
10 mL fill in a 10 mL vial, 10 USP Units per mL.

Discard unused portion.

Use only if solution is clear and seal is intact.

Store at 20º to 25ºC (68º to 77ºF) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Do not permit to freeze.
