# HOW SUPPLIED

STERILE SINGLE USE VIAL FOR INTRAVENOUS INFUSION.

ALOPRIM (allopurinol sodium) for Injection, 30 mL flint glass vials with rubber stoppers each containing allopurinol sodium equivalent to 500 mg of allopurinol (white lyophilized powder), box of 1 (NDC 67457-187-50).

Store unreconstituted powder at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15°-30°C (59°-86°F) [see USP controlled room temperature].
