ALOPRIM (allopurinol) for Injection is supplied in 30 mL flint glass vials with rubber stoppers each containing 500 mg of allopurinol (white lyophilized powder) equivalent to 580.7 mg of allopurinol sodium.

Store unreconstituted powder at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
