Allopurinol has the following structural formula:

Allopurinol is known chemically as 1,5-dihydro-4 H-pyrazolo [3,4- d]pyrimidin-4-one.
It is a xanthine oxidase inhibitor which is administered orally.
Each scored white tablet contains 100 mg or 300 mg allopurinol and the inactive ingredients croscarmellose sodium, colloidal silicon dioxide, and magnesium stearate.
Its solubility in water at 37°C is 80.0 mg/dL and is greater in an alkaline solution.
