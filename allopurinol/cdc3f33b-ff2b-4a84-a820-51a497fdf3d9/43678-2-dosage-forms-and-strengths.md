# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Allopurinol Tablets, USP have functional scoring and are available in the following strengths:

- 100 mg: White to off-white, round, biconvex, scored tablet, debossed with “ CE” over “ 76” on one side and a score on the other side.
- 300 mg: White to off-white, round, biconvex, scored tablet, debossed with “ CE” over “ 77” on one side and a score on the other side.
