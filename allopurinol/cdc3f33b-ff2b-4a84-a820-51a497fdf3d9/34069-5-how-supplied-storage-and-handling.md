# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

How Supplied

Allopurinol Tablets, USP are available in multiple tablet strengths with functional scoring and package sizes as follows:

100 mg white to off-white, round, biconvex, scored tablet, debossed with “ CE” over “ 76” on one side and a score on the other side, bottles of 90 (NDC 62135-516-90) and bottles of 300 (NDC 62135-516-31).

300 mg white to off-white, round, biconvex, scored tablet, debossed with “ CE” over “ 77” on one side and a score on the other side, bottles of 90 (NDC 62135-517-90) and bottles of 300 (NDC 62135-517-31).

Storage and Handling

Store at 20°C to 25°C (USP Controlled Room Temperature) (68°F to 77°F) in a dry place.
Dispense in a tight container as defined in the USP.
