# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Allopurinol Tablets, USP are available containing 100 mg or 300 mg of allopurinol, USP.

- •The 100 mg tablets are white, round, functionally scored tablets debossed with M to the left of the score and 31 to the right of the score on one side of the tablet and blank on the other side.
- •The 300 mg tablets are white, round, functionally scored tablets debossed with M to the left of the score and 71 to the right of the score on one side of the tablet and blank on the other side.
