# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Allopurinol tablets, USP have functional scoring and are available in the following strengths:

- 100 mg: White, round, flat face, beveled edge tablets.
  The upper layer is bisected and debossed with “2083/V”.
  The lower layer is plain.
- 300 mg: Orange, round and biconvex tablets.
  The upper layer is bisected and debossed with “2084/V”.
  The lower layer is plain.
