# 11 DESCRIPTION

Allopurinol, USP is a xanthine oxidase inhibitor.
It has the following structural formula:

Allopurinol, USP is known chemically as 1, 5-dihydro-4H-pyrazolo [3, 4-d]pyrimidin-4-one and it has a molecular weight of 136.1 g/mol.
Soluble in solutions of potassium and sodium hydroxides, very slightly soluble in water and in alcohol; practically insoluble in chloroform and in ether.
It is a xanthine oxidase inhibitor which is administered orally.

Each scored white round-shaped tablet contains 100 mg allopurinol and the inactive ingredients colloidal silicon dioxide, lactose monohydrate, magnesium stearate, microcrystalline cellulose, sodium starch glycolate.

Each scored orange round-shaped tablet contains 300 mg allopurinol and the inactive ingredients colloidal silicon dioxide, FD&C Yellow No. 6 Lake, lactose monohydrate, magnesium stearate, microcrystalline cellulose, sodium starch glycolate.
