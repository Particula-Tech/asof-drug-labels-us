# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Allopurinol Tablets, USP are available in the following strengths:

- 100 mg: Round, flat-faced, beveled, scored, white tablets imprinted with “DAN DAN” on one side and “5543” on the other side.
- 300 mg: Round, standard convex, scored, orange tablets imprinted with “DAN DAN” on one side and “5544” on the other side.
