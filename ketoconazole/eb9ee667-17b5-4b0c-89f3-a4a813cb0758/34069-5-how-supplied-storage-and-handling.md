# 16. HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Extina Foam, 2% is supplied in 50 g (NDC 63032-051-50) and 100 g (NDC 63032-051-00) aluminum containers.

Store at controlled room temperature 68°–77°F (20°-25°C).
Do not store under refrigerated conditions.
Do not expose containers to heat, and/or store at temperatures above 120oF (49oC).
Do not store in direct sunlight.

Contents are flammable.

Contents under pressure.
Do not puncture and/or incinerate container.

Keep out of reach of children.
