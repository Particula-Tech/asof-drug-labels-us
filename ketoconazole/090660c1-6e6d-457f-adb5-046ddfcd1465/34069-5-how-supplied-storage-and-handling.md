# HOW SUPPLIED

NIZORAL® (ketoconazole) is available as white, scored tablets containing 200 mg of ketoconazole debossed "JANSSEN" and on the reverse side debossed "NIZORAL".
They are supplied in bottles of 100 tablets (NDC 50458-220-10).

Store at controlled room temperature 15°–25°C (59°–77°F).

Protect from moisture.

Keep out of reach of children.
