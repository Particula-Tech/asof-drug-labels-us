# HOW SUPPLIED

Each ketoconazole tablet USP contains ketoconazole, USP 200 mg available in bottles of 100 (NDC 64380-827-06) and 500 (NDC 64380-827-07).
The tablets are white to off white, round shaped flat-beveled debossed with "S" and "500" on the scored side and plain on the other side.

Store at 20° to 25° C (68° to 77° F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Protect from moisture.

Keep this and all medications out of reach of children.
