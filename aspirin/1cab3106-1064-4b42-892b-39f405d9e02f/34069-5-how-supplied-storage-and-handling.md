# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

ASCOMP with CODEINE capsules have a plain blue opaque cap with a yellow opaque body imprinted with "B 074" in black ink.
They are available in bottles of 100 capsules [NDC 79739-6094-1].

Store and Dispense

Below 25°C (77°F); tight container.
Protect from moisture.

Store ASCOMP with Codeine securely and dispose of properly [see Patient Counseling Information (17)].
