# 1INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Butalbital, Aspirin, Caffeine, and Codeine Phosphate Capsules, USP is indicated for the management of the symptom complex of tension (or muscle contraction) headache when non-opioid analgesic and alternative treatments are inadequate.

Limitations of Use

Because of the risks of addiction, abuse, and misuse with opioids, which can occur at any dosage of duration [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)], reserve Butalbital, Aspirin, Caffeine, and Codeine Phosphate Capsules, USP for use in patients for whom alternative treatment options [e.g., non-opioid analgesic or opioid combination products]:

• Have not been tolerated, or are not expected to be tolerated,

• Have not provided adequate analgesia or are not expected to provide adequate analgesia.

Butalbital, Aspirin, Caffeine, and Codeine Phosphate Capsules, USP should not be used for an extended period of time unless the pain remains severe enough to require an opioid analgesic and for which alternative treatment options continue to be inadequate.
