# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Aspirin and Extended-Release Dipyridamole Capsulesare available as a hard gelatin capsule, with a red colored cap and an ivory-colored body containing yellow colored extended-release pellets incorporating dipyridamole and a white to off white, film coated, circular bi-convex tablet incorporating immediate-release aspirin.
The capsule body is imprinted in black with ‘PAR’ on the cap and ‘730’on the body

Aspirin and Extended-Release Dipyridamole Capsulesare supplied in unit-of-use bottles of 60 capsules (NDC 49884-007-02).

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15°to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from excessive moisture.
