# HOW SUPPLIED

Butalbital, Aspirin, and Caffeine Capsules are dark green and light green in color, imprinted with logo "LANNETT" on the cap and "1552" on the body.
Each capsule contains: butalbital USP, 50 mg; aspirin USP, 325 mg; caffeine USP, 40 mg and is available as follows:

Bottles of 100 NDC 0527-1552-01

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP.
Use child-resistant closure.
Store below 25ºC (77ºF).

Protect from moisture.
