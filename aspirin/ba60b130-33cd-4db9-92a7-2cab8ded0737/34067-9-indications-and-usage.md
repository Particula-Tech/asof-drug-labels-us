# INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Butalbital, aspirin, and caffeine capsules are indicated for the relief of the symptom complex of tension (or muscle contraction) headache.
Evidence supporting the efficacy and safety of butalbital, aspirin, and caffeine capsules in the treatment of multiple recurrent headaches is unavailable.
Caution in this regard is required because butalbital is habit-forming and potentially abusable.
