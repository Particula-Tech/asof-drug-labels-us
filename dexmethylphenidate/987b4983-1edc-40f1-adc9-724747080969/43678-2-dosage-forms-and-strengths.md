# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Dexmethylphenidate hydrochloride tablets are available in 2.5 mg, 5 mg, and 10 mg tablets.

- 2.5 mg:Light blue colored, round, biconvex, beveled edge, uncoated, tablets debossed ‘376’ on one side and ‘S’ above ‘2.5’ on the other side.
- 5 mg:Light yellow colored, round, biconvex, beveled edge, uncoated, tablets debossed ‘378’ on one side and ‘S’ above ‘5’ on the other side.
- 10 mg:White, round, biconvex, beveled edge, uncoated, tablets debossed ‘379’ on one side and ‘S’ above ‘10’ on the other side.
