# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Focalin (dexmethylphenidate hydrochloride) tablets (D-shaped, embossed “D” on upper convex face and dosage strength on lower convex face) are available as follows:

- •2.5 mg tablets (NDC 66758-250-01) blue, supplied in bottles of 100
- •5 mg tablets (NDC 66758-251-01) yellow, supplied in bottles of 100
- •10 mg tablets (NDC 66758-252-01) white, supplied in bottles of 100

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F and 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Protect from light and moisture.
Dispense in tight, light-resistant container (USP).
