# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

XERESE is supplied in plastic-laminated aluminum tubes containing 2 gm or 5 gm of XERESE.
Each gram of XERESE contains 5% (w/w) acyclovir and 1% (w/w) hydrocortisone in an aqueous cream base.

NDC 0037-0501-02: 2 gm tubes

NDC 0037-0501-05: 5 gm tubes

Store at controlled room temperature [20°-25°C (68°-77°F); excursions permitted to 15°-30°C (59°-86°F).
Do not freeze.]
