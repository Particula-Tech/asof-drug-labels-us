# HOW SUPPLIED

Acyclovir Capsules USP 200 mg are available for oral administration as hard gelatin capsules with a white opaque body and an aqua blue opaque cap.
“APO 042” is imprinted on each capsule in black ink.
They are supplied as follows:

Bottles of 100 (60505-0042-6)

Bottles of 500 (60505-0042-5)

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature] and protect from moisture.
