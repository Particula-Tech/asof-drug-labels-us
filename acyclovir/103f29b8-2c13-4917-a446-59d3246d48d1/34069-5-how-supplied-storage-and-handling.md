# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Each gram of Acyclovir Cream contains 50 mg (equivalent to 5% w/w) of acyclovir in an aqueous cream base.
Acyclovir Cream is supplied as follows:

- 5 g tubes NDC 0093-3630-20

Store at or below 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
