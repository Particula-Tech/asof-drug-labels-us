# 13 NON-CLINICAL TOXICOLOGY

## 13.1 Carcinogenesis, Mutagenesis, Impairment of Fertility

Carcinogenesis

Acyclovir was not shown to be carcinogenic in mouse and rat bioassays at oral doses up to 450 mg/kg (approximately 1100 – 2200 times the maximum RHOD, on a mg/m2 basis, assuming 100% absorption).

Mutagenesis

Acyclovir was tested in 16 in vitro and in vivo genetic toxicity assays.
Acyclovir was found to be negative in the Ames test, positive in the in vitro mouse lymphoma assay (TK locus), and positive in the in vitro and in vivo assays for chromosomal effects.

Impairment of Fertility

In reproduction studies, acyclovir did not impair fertility or reproduction at oral doses up to 450 mg/kg/day in mice (1100 times the RHOD), or at subcutaneous doses of 25 mg/kg/day in rats (125 times the RHOD).
At a dose of 50 mg/kg/day in rats and rabbits (250 and 500 times the RHOD, respectively), implantation efficiency was decreased.
