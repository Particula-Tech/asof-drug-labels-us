# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

AVACLYR is available in a 3.5 g aluminum tube with a white high density polyethylene cap as a clear, colorless, sterile ophthalmic ointment for topical use containing 3% acyclovir active drug.
Each tube is packaged in an individual carton.

3.5 g tube (NDC 48102-028-35)

Store at 15°C to 25°C (59°F to 77°F).
