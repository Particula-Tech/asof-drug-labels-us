# 5 WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS

## 5.1 General

ZOVIRAX Cream should only be applied on the affected external aspects of the lips and face in patients with herpes labialis.
Because no data are available, application to human mucous membranes is not recommended.
ZOVIRAX Cream is intended for cutaneous use only and should not be used in the eye or inside the mouth or nose.

## 5.2 Contact Sensitization

ZOVIRAX Cream has a potential for irritation and contact sensitization [see Adverse Reactions (6.1)].

The effect of ZOVIRAX Cream has not been established in immunocompromised patients.
