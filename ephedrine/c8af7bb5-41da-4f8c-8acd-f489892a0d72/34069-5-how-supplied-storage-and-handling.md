# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Ephedrine sulfate injection, USP, 50 mg/mL, is supplied as follows:

| NDC | Strength | How Supplied |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0781-3269-95 | 50 mg/mL | 1 mL fill in 2 mL amber vial; for single-dose (supplied in packages of 10) |

Vial stoppers are not manufactured with natural rubber latex.
Store ephedrine sulfate injection, USP, 50 mg/mL, at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Store in carton until time of use.
For single-dose only.
Discard unused portion.
