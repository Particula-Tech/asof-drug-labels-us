# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Ephedrine Sulfate Injection, USP, 50 mg/mL, is supplied as follows:

| NDC | Strength | How Supplied |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 42023-216-25 | 50 mg/mL | 1 mL clear glass vial; for single use (supplied in packages of 25) |

Vial stoppers are not manufactured with natural rubber latex.

Store ephedrine sulfate injection, 50 mg/mL, at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F), with excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]
Protect from light.
Store in carton until time of use.
For single use only.
Discard unused portion.
