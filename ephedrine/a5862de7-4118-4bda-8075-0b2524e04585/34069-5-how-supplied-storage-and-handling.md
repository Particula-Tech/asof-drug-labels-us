# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Ephedrine sulfate injection USP, 50 mg/mL is a sterile, clear, colorless solution and is supplied as follows: 50 mg per mL 1 mL clear glass vial for Single-Dose, supplied NDC 55150-373-25 in a Carton of 25 The vial stopper is not made with natural rubber latex.
Store ephedrine sulfate injection USP, 50 mg/mL, at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F), excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Store in carton until time of use.
For single-dose only.
Discard unused portion.
