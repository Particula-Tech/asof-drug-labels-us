# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Ephedrine sulfate injection, USP is a clear, colorless, sterile solution for intravenous injection supplied as follows:

| NDC | Strength | How Supplied |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 55150-373-25 | 50 mg/mL of ephedrine sulfate equivalent to 38 mg/mL of ephedrine base | 1 mL clear glass vial; for single-dose (supplied in packages of 25) |

The vial stopper is not made with natural rubber latex.
Store ephedrine sulfate injection, USP at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Store in carton until time of use.
For single-dose only.
Discard unused portion.
