Store REZIPRES® at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

For single-dose Ampules.
If the Rezipres diluted solution is not used immediately, store at controlled room temperature at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) for up to 4 hours or refrigerate at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) for no more than 24 hours.
Discard the unused Rezipres diluted solution after 4 hours if stored at controlled room temperature or after 24 hours if refrigerated.

For single dose Vials, discard any unused portion.
Product is light sensitive.
Store in carton until time of use.
