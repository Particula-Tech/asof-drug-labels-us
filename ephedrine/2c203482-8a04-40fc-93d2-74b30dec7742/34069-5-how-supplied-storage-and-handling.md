# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Ephedrine Sulfate Injection 5 mg/mL (equivalent to 3.8 mg/mL ephedrine base), is a clear, colorless solution and is supplied as follows:

| NDC | Strength | How Supplied |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 42023-243-01 | 50 mg/10 mL (5 mg/mL) | 10 mL clear, glass, single-dose vial; (supplied in a package of 1) |
| 42023-253-05 | 25 mg/5 mL (5 mg/mL) | 5 mL single-dose prefilled syringe (supplied in a package of 5) |

Vial stoppers, syringe plunger stoppers and Luer lock tip caps are not manufactured with natural rubber latex.
Store Ephedrine Sulfate Injection, 5 mg/mL at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Store in carton until time of use.
For single use only.
Discard unused portion.

Protect from Light

Contains No Preservatives
