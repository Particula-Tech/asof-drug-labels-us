# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

REZIPRES® is a clear, colorless solution supplied as follows:

| NDC Number | Strength | Presentation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 39822-1420-1 | 47 mg/mL | 2 mL clear glass, single-dose ampule filled with 1 mL |
| 39822-1420-2 |  | 2 mL ampules filled with 1 mL packaged in a carton of 10 |
| 39822-1400-5 | 47 mg/5 mL (9.4 mg/mL) | 5 mL clear glass, single-dose ampule |
| 39822-1400-6 |  | 5 mL ampules packaged in a carton of 10 |
| 39822-1410-5 | 23.5 mg/5 mL (4.7 mg/mL) | 5 mL clear glass, single-dose ampule |
| 39822-1410-6 |  | 5 mL ampules packaged in a carton of 10 |

Store REZIPRES® at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
For single-dose only.
The diluted solution should not be held for more than 4 hours at room temperature or for more than 24 hours under refrigerated conditions.
Discard any unused portion.
