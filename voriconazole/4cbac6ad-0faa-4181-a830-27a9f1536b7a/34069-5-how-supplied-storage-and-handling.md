# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Voriconazole Tablets, 50 mg; white to off-white, circular biconvex film-coated tablets, debossed with "73" on one side and "G" on the other side.

Bottles of 30 with child-resistant closures (NDC 68462-572-30)

Bottles of 90 with child-resistant closures (NDC 68462-572-90)

Unit-dose carton of 30 tablets (3x10) (NDC 68462-572-13)

Voriconazole Tablets, 200 mg; white to off-white, oval shaped, film-coated tablets, debossed with "573" on one side and "G" on the other side.

Bottles of 30 with child-resistant closures (NDC 68462-573-30)

Bottles of 90 with child-resistant closures (NDC 68462-573-90)

Unit-dose carton of 30 tablets (3x10) (NDC 68462-573-13)

## 16.2 Storage

Voriconazole Tablets should be stored at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
