# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

- Voriconazole for oral suspension is contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity to voriconazole, USP or its excipients.
  There is no information regarding cross-sensitivity between voriconazole, USP and other azole antifungal agents.
  Caution should be used when prescribing voriconazole, USP to patients with hypersensitivity to other azoles.

- Co-administration of terfenadine, astemizole, cisapride, pimozide or quinidine with voriconazole, USP is contraindicated because increased plasma concentrations of these drugs can lead to QT prolongation and rare occurrences of torsade de pointes [see Drug Interactions (7) and Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].

- Co-administration of voriconazole, USP with sirolimus is contraindicated because voriconazole, USP significantly increases sirolimus concentrations [see Drug Interactions (7) and Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].

- Co-administration of voriconazole, USP with rifampin, carbamazepine and long-acting barbiturates is contraindicated because these drugs are likely to decrease plasma voriconazole, USP concentrations significantly [see Drug Interactions (7) and Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].

- Co-administration of standard doses of voriconazole, USP with efavirenz doses of 400 mg q24h or higher is contraindicated, because efavirenz significantly decreases plasma voriconazole, USP concentrations in healthy subjects at these doses.
  Voriconazole, USP also significantly increases efavirenz plasma concentrations [see Drug Interactions (7) and Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].

- Co-administration of voriconazole, USP with high-dose ritonavir (400 mg q12h) is contraindicated because ritonavir (400 mg q12h) significantly decreases plasma voriconazole, USP concentrations.
  Co-administration of voriconazole, USP and low-dose ritonavir (100 mg q12h) should be avoided, unless an assessment of the benefit/risk to the patient justifies the use of voriconazole, USP [see Drug Interactions (7) and Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].

- Co-administration of voriconazole, USP with rifabutin is contraindicated since voriconazole, USP significantly increases rifabutin plasma concentrations and rifabutin also significantly decreases voriconazole, USP plasma concentrations [see Drug Interactions (7) and Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].

- Co-administration of voriconazole, USP with ergot alkaloids (ergotamine and dihydroergotamine) is contraindicated because voriconazole, USP may increase the plasma concentration of ergot alkaloids, which may lead to ergotism [see Drug Interactions (7) and Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].

- Co-administration of voriconazole, USP with St. John’s Wort is contraindicated because this herbal supplement may decrease voriconazole, USP plasma concentration [see Drug Interactions (7) and Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].
