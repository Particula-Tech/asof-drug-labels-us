# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Tablets

Voriconazole 50 mg tablets; white, film-coated, round, debossed with "Pfizer" on one side and "VOR50" on the reverse.

Voriconazole 200 mg tablets; white, film-coated, capsule shaped, debossed with the letter "G" on one side and "VOR200" on the reverse.

Voriconazole 200 mg tablets; white, film-coated, capsule shaped, debossed with "Pfizer" on one side and "VOR200" on the reverse.

Powder for Oral Suspension

Voriconazole for Oral Suspension is supplied as a white to off-white powder in 100 mL high density polyethylene (HDPE) bottles.
Following reconstitution, the volume of white to off white suspension is 75 mL, providing a usable volume of 70 mL.
Each mL of the oral suspension contains 40 mg of voriconazole (200 mg of voriconazole per 5 mL).
