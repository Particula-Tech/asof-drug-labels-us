# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Medroxyprogesterone acetate is indicated only for the prevention of pregnancy.
The loss of bone mineral density (BMD) in women of all ages and the impact on peak bone mass in adolescents should be considered, along with the decrease in BMD that occurs during pregnancy and/or lactation, in the risk/benefit assessment for women who use Medroxyprogesterone acetate long-term [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
