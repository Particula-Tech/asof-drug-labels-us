# HOW SUPPLIED

Medroxyprogesterone Acetate Tablets USP are available as:

1. 2.5 mg: White, round, scored, biconvex tablet.
   Debossed with 555/872 on the scored side and stylized b on the other side, available in bottles of 100 (NDC 0555-0872-02) and 500 (NDC 0555-0872-04).
2. 5 mg: White, round, scored, biconvex tablet.
   Debossed with 555/873 on the scored side and stylized b on the other side, available in bottles of 100 (NDC 0555-0873-02) and 500 (NDC 0555-0873-04).
3. 10 mg: White, round, scored, biconvex tablet.
   Debossed with 555/779 on the scored side and stylized b on the other side, available in bottles of 100 (NDC 0555-0779-02) and 500 (NDC 0555-0779-04).

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP, with a child-resistant closure (as required).

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

KEEP THIS AND ALL MEDICATIONS OUT OF THE REACH OF CHILDREN.
