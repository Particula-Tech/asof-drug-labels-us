# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

YUSIMRY (adalimumab-aqvh) is supplied as a preservative-free, sterile, clear to slightly opalescent, colorless to slightly yellow solution for subcutaneous administration.
The following packaging configuration are available:

- YUSIMRY Pen Carton - 40mg/0.8 mL

YUSIMRY is supplied in a carton containing two dose trays.
Each dose tray consists of a single- dose pen, containing a 1 mL prefilled glass syringe with a fixed 1⁄2 inch needle, providing 40 mg/0.8 mL of YUSIMRY.
The needle cover is not manufactured from natural rubber latex.
The NDC number is 70114-220-02.

- Prefilled Syringe Carton - 40mg/0.8 mL

YUSIMRY is supplied in a carton containing two dose trays.
Each dose tray consists of a single-dose, 1 mL prefilled glass syringe with a fixed 1⁄2 inch needle, providing 40 mg/0.8 mL of YUSIMRY.
The needle cover is not manufactured from natural rubber latex.
The NDC number is 70114-210-02.

Storage and Stability

Do not use beyond the expiration date on the container.
YUSIMRY must be refrigerated at 36°F to 46°F (2°C to 8°C).
DO NOT FREEZE.
Do not use if frozen, even if it has been thawed.

Store in original carton until time of administration to protect from light.

If needed, for example when traveling, YUSIMRY may be stored at room temperature up to a maximum of 77°F (25°C) for a period of up to 14 days, with protection from light.
YUSIMRY should be discarded if not used within the 14-day period.
Record the date when YUSIMRY is first removed from the refrigerator in the spaces provided on the carton and dose pack.

Do not store YUSIMRY in extreme heat or cold.
