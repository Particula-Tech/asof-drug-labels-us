# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

HADLIMA is a clear to opalescent, and colorless to pale brown solution available as:

- •Autoinjector (HADLIMA PushTouch) Injection: 40 mg/0.8 mL in a single-dose prefilled autoinjector.
- •Prefilled syringe Injection: 40 mg/0.8 mL in a single-dose prefilled glass syringe.
