BEFORE YOU GET STARTED…

Store refrigerated at 36°F to 46°F (2°C to 8°C) in the original carton to protect from sunlight and indoor light until time of injection.
DO NOT FREEZE.

If needed, HADLIMA may be stored at room temperature up to 77°F (25°C) for up to 14 days, with protection from sunlight and indoor light.
Once stored at room temperature, do not place back in the refrigerator.
HADLIMA should be discarded if not used within the 14-day period.
Write the date removed from the refrigerator _____/_____/_____.

Do not use if medication has expired.

Do not remove the needle cap before you are ready to inject.

Each 0.8 mL single-dose prefilled syringe contains:

40 mg adalimumab-bwwd, citric acid monohydrate (0.544 mg), L-histidine (0.96 mg), L-histidine hydrochloride monohydrate (8.64 mg), polysorbate 20 (0.64 mg), sodium citrate dihydrate (1.6 mg), sorbitol (20 mg), and Water for Injection, USP
