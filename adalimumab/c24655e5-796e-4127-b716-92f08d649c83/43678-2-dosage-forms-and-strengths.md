# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Adalimumab-bwwd is a clear to opalescent, and colorless to pale brown solution available as:

- •Autoinjector (Adalimumab-bwwd autoinjector) Injection: 40 mg/0.8 mL in a single-dose prefilled autoinjector.
  Injection: 40 mg/0.4 mL in a single-dose prefilled autoinjector.
- •Prefilled Syringe Injection: 40 mg/0.8 mL in a single-dose prefilled glass syringe.
  Injection: 40 mg/0.4 mL in a single-dose prefilled glass syringe.
- •Single-Dose Institutional Use Vial Injection: 40 mg/0.8 mL in a single-dose, glass vial for institutional use only.
