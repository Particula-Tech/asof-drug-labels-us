# 9. DRUG ABUSE AND DEPENDENCE

## 9.1. Controlled Substance

Doxepin is not a controlled substance.

## 9.2. Abuse

Doxepin is not associated with abuse potential in animals or in humans.
Physicians should carefully evaluate patients for history of drug abuse and follow such patients closely, observing them for signs of misuse or abuse of doxepin (e.g., incrementation of dose, drug-seeking behavior).

## 9.3. Dependence

In a brief assessment of adverse events observed during discontinuation of doxepin following chronic administration, no symptoms indicative of a withdrawal syndrome were observed.
Thus, doxepin does not appear to produce physical dependence.
