# 3. DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Doxepin HCl Tablets is an immediate-release, oval-shaped, tablet for oral administration available in strengths of 3 mg and 6 mg.
The tablets are blue (3 mg) or green (6 mg) and are debossed with 3 or 6, respectively, on one side and SP on the other.
Doxepin HCl Tablets are not scored.
