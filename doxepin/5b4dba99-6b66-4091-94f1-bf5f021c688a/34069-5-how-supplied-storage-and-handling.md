# 16. HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1. How Supplied

Doxepin HCl Tablets 3 mg tablets are oval shaped, blue, identified with debossed markings of "3" on one side and "SP" on the other, and are supplied as:

|  | NDC 44183-103-30 | Bottle of 30 |
| --- | --- | --- |

Doxepin HCl Tablets 6 mg tablets are oval shaped, green, identified with debossed markings of "6" on one side and "SP" on the other, and are supplied as:

|  | NDC 44183-106-30 | Bottle of 30 |

## 16.2. Storage and Handling

Store at controlled room temperature 20° - 25°C (68° - 77°F), protected from light.
