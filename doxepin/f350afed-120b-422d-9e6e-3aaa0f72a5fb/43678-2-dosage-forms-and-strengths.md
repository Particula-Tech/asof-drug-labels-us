# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Oral solution: Each mL of oral solution contains 10 mg of doxepin as a clear, colorless solution for dilution prior to administration [see Dosage and Administration (2.8)].
Supplied as a 120 mL bottle with accompanying calibrated dropper with 5 mg, 10 mg, 15 mg, 20 mg, and 25 mg markings.
