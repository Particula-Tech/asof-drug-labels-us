# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Doxepin Hydrochloride Capsules, USP are available containing doxepin hydrochloride, USP equivalent to 10 mg, 25 mg, 50 mg, 75 mg or 100 mg of doxepin.

- The 10 mg capsules are hard gelatin capsule filled with white to off-white granular powder having “AMNEAL” printed on yellow opaque cap and “1166” printed on yellow opaque body with black ink.
- The 25 mg capsules are hard gelatin capsule filled with white to off-white granular powder having “AMNEAL” printed on yellow opaque cap and “1170” printed on white opaque body with black ink.
- The 50 mg capsules are hard gelatin capsule filled with white to off-white granular powder having “AMNEAL” printed on yellow opaque cap and “1171” printed on yellow opaque body with black ink.
- The 75 mg capsules are hard gelatin capsule filled with white to off-white granular powder having “AMNEAL” printed on green opaque cap and “1172” printed on green opaque body with black ink.
- The 100 mg capsules are hard gelatin capsule filled with white to off-white granular powder having “AMNEAL” printed on green opaque cap and “1173” printed on white opaque body with black ink.
