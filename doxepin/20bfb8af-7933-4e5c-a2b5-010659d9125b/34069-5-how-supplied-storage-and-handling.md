# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

How Supplied

Doxepin Hydrochloride Capsules, USP are available containing doxepin hydrochloride, USP equivalent to 10 mg, 25 mg, 50 mg, 75 mg or 100 mg of doxepin.

Doxepin Hydrochloride Capsules USP, 10 mg are supplied as hard gelatin capsule filled with white to off-white granular powder having “AMNEAL” printed on yellow opaque cap and “1166” printed on yellow opaque body with black ink.

They are available as follows:

Bottle of 30 Capsules: NDC 69238-1166-3

Bottle of 90 Capsules: NDC 69238-1166-9

Bottle of 1000 Capsules: NDC 69238-1166-1

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

Protect from light.

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP using a child-resistant closure.

PHARMACIST: Dispense a Medication Guide with each prescription.
