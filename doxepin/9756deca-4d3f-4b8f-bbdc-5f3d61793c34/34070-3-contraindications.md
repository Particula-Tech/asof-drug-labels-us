# CONTRAINDICATIONS

Because doxepin HCl has an anticholinergic effect and because significant plasma levels of doxepin are detectable after topical PRUDOXINTM Cream application, the use of PRUDOXINTM Cream is contraindicated in patients with untreated narrow angle glaucoma or a tendency to urinary retention.

PRUDOXINTM Cream is contraindicated in individuals who have shown previous sensitivity to any of its components.
