# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Mesalamine extended-release capsules are available as light blue opaque hard gelatin capsules containing 0.375 g mesalamine in a light blue opaque gelatin capsule with the letters “G” and “M” imprinted on either side of a black band and are available in bottles of 120 capsules (NDC 0093- 9224-89).

Storage:

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15° and 30°C (59° and 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
