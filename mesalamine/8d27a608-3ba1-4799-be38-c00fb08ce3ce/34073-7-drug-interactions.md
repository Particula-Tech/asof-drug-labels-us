# 7 DRUG INTERACTIONS

Based on in vitro studies, mesalamine extended-release capsules are not expected to inhibit the metabolism of drugs that are substrates of CYP1A2, CYP2C9, CYP2C19, CYP2D6, or CYP3A4.

## 7.1 Antacids

Because the dissolution of the coating of the granules in mesalamine extended-release capsules depends on pH, mesalamine extended-release capsules should not be co-administered with antacids.
