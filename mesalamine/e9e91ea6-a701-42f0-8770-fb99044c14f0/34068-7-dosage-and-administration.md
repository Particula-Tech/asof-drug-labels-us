# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

For the treatment of moderately active ulcerative colitis, the recommended dose of Asacol HD in adults is two 800 mg tablets to be taken three times daily with or without food, for a total daily dose of 4.8 g, for a duration of 6 weeks.
Asacol HD use beyond 6 weeks has not been evaluated.
Asacol HD should be swallowed whole without cutting, breaking, or chewing.
One Asacol HD 800 mg tablet has not been shown to be bioequivalent to two Asacol 400 mg tablets [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].
