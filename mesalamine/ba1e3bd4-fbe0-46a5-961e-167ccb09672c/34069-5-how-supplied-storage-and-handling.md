# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Mesalamine Delayed-Release Tablets, USP are available as red-brown, oval shaped, unscored, coated tablets containing 1.2 g mesalamine, and imprinted with black ink “WPI 2245” on one side and plain on the other side.

NDC 0591-2245-22 (Bottle of 120)

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
