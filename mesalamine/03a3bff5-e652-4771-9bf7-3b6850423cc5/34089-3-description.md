# 11 DESCRIPTION

Each Asacol HD delayed-release tablet for oral administration contains 800 mg of mesalamine, an aminosalicylate.
Asacol HD delayed-release tablets have an outer protective coat consisting of a combination of acrylic based resins, Eudragit S (methacrylic acid and methyl methacrylate copolymer (1:2), NF) and Eudragit L (methacrylic acid and methyl methacrylate copolymer (1:1), NF).
The inner coat consists of an acrylic based resin, Eudragit S, which dissolves at pH 7 or greater, releasing mesalamine in the terminal ileum and beyond for topical anti- inflammatory action in the colon.
