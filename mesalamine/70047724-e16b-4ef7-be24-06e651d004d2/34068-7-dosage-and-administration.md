# 2. DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

## 2.1. For the treatment of mildly to moderately active ulcerative colitis:

The recommended dose in adults is 2.4 g/day or 4.8 g/day (two or four 400mg tablets three times a day).
Treatment duration in controlled clinical trials was 6 weeks.

## 2.2 For the maintenance of remission of ulcerative colitis:

The recommended dosage in adults is 1.6 grams daily, in divided doses.
Treatment duration in the prospective, well-controlled trial was 6 months.
