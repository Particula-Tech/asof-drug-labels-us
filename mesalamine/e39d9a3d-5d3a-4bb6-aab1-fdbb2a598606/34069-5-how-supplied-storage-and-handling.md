# HOW SUPPLIED

PENTASA controlled-release 250 mg capsules are supplied in bottles of 240 capsules (NDC 54092-189-81).
Each green and blue capsule contains 250 mg of mesalamine in controlled-release beads.
PENTASA controlled-release capsules are identified with a pentagonal starburst logo and the number 2010 on the green portion and PENTASA 250 mg or S429 250 mg on the blue portion of the capsules.

PENTASA controlled-release 500 mg capsules are supplied in bottles of 120 capsules (NDC 54092-191-12).
Each blue capsule contains 500 mg of mesalamine in controlled-release beads.
PENTASA controlled-release capsules are identified with a pentagonal starburst logo and PENTASA 500 mg or S429 500 mg on the capsules.

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15-30°C (59-86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
