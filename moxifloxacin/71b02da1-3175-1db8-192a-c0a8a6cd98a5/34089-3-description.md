# 11 DESCRIPTION

Moxifloxacin hydrochloride USP is a synthetic antibacterial agent for oral administration.
Moxifloxacin, a fluoroquinolone, is available as the monohydrochloride salt of 1-cyclopropyl-7-[(S,S)-2,8-diazabicyclo[4.3.0]non-8-yl]-6-fluoro-8-methoxy-1,4-dihydro-4-oxo-3 quinoline carboxylic acid.
It is a slightly yellow to yellow powder or crystals with a molecular weight of 437.9.

## 11.1 Moxifloxacin Tablets, USP

Moxifloxacin Tablets, USP are available as film-coated tablets containing 400 mg moxifloxacin (equivalent to 436.30 mg moxifloxacin hydrochloride, USP).

Inactive ingredients: colloidal silicon dioxide, croscarmellose sodium, hypromellose, iron oxide red, iron oxide yellow, magnesium stearate, microcrystalline cellulose, polyethylene glycol 400, polysorbate 80, sodium stearyl fumarate, talc and titanium dioxide.
