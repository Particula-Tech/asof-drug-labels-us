# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 Moxifloxacin Hydrochloride Tablets

Moxifloxacin hydrochloride tablets 400 mg are available as Beige colored, capsule shaped, biconvex tablets debossed with ''on one side and ‘112’ on the other side.
They are supplied in bottles of 30, 100, 500, and unit dose package of 50’s (5 x 10’s).

Bottles of 30 NDC 55111-112-30

Bottles of 100 NDC 55111-112-01

Bottles of 500 NDC 55111-112-05

Unit dose package of 50 (5 x 10’s) NDC 55111-112-66

Store at 20-25°C (68-77°F); [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Avoid high humidity.
