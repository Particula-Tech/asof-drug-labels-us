# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 Moxifloxacin Hydrochloride Tablets

Moxifloxacin Hydrochloride Tablets are available as modified capsule shaped, dull red film-coated tablets containing 400 mg moxifloxacin.
The tablet is debossed with E-18 on one side and plain on the other side.
Bottles of 30 NDC 65862-603-30 Bottles of 1000 NDC 65862-603-99 Cartons of 50 (5 x 10) Unit-dose Tablets NDC 65862-603-55 Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Avoid high humidity.
