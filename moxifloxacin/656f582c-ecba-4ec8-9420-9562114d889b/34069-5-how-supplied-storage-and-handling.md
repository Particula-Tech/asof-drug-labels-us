# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 Moxifloxacin tablets, USP

Moxifloxacin tablets, USP are available as pink, biconvex, capsule shaped tablets debossed with “CE”on one side and “312”on the other side.

| Package | NDC Code |
| --- | --- |
| Bottles of 30: | 62135-014-30 |

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15 to 30°C (59 to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Avoid high humidity.
