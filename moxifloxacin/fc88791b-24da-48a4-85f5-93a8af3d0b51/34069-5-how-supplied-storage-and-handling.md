# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Moxifloxacin ophthalmic solution is supplied as a sterile ophthalmic solution in Alcon’s DROP-TAINER® dispensing system consisting of a natural low density polyethylene bottle and dispensing plug and tan polypropylene closure.
Tamper evidence is provided with a shrink band around the closure and neck area of the package.

3 mL in a 4 mL bottle - NDC 0781-7135-93

Storage: Store at 2°C-25°C (36°F-77°F).
