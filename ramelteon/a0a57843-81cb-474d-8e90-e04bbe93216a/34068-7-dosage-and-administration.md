# 2. DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

2.1 Dosage in Adults

The recommended dose of ramelteon tablets is 8 mg taken within 30 minutes of going to bed.
It is recommended that ramelteon tablets not be taken with or immediately after a high-fat meal.

The total ramelteon tablets dose should not exceed 8 mg per day.

## 2.2 Dosing in Patients with Hepatic Impairment

Ramelteon tablets are not recommended in patients with severe hepatic impairment.
Ramelteon tablets should be used with caution in patients with moderate hepatic impairment [see Warnings and Precaution (5.6), Clinical Pharmacology (12.4)].

## 2.3 Administration with Other Medications

Ramelteon tablets should not be used in combination with fluvoxamine.
Ramelteon tablets should be used with caution in patients taking other CYP1A2 inhibiting drugs [see Drug Interactions(7), Clinical Pharmacology (12.5)].
