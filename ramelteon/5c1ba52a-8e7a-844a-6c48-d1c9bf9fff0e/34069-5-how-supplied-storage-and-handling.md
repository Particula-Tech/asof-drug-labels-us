# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Ramelteon tablets, 8 mg are available as round, pale orange-yellow, biconvex film-coated tablets debossed with 'C' on one side and plain on the other side, in the following quantities:

| NDC 70700-272-30 | Bottles of 30 |
| --- | --- |
| NDC 70700-272-10 | Bottles of 100 |
| NDC 70700-272-05 | Bottles of 500 |

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Keep container tightly closed and protected from moisture and humidity.
