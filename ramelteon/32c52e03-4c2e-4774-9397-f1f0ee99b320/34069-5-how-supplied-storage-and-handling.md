# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Ramelteon Tablets are available as round, yellow, film coated tablets debossed with "I3" on one side and "5" on the other side in the following quantities:

| NDC 72319-005-01 | Bottles of 30 |
| --- | --- |
| NDC 72319-005-04 | Bottles of 100 |
| NDC 72319-005-05 | Bottles of 500 |

Store at 25°C(77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Keep container tightly closed and protected from moisture and humidity.
