# 16HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

ROZEREM is available as round, pale orange-yellow, film-coated, 8 mg tablets, with “TAK” and “RAM-8” printed on one side, in the following quantities:

NDC 64764-805-30 Bottles of 30 NDC 64764-805-10 Bottles of 100 NDC 64764-805-50 Bottles of 500

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP controlled room temperature].
Keep container tightly closed and protected from moisture and humidity.
