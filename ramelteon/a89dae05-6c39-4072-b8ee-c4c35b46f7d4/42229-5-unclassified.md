# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Ramelteon Tablets, 8 mg are available as orange colored, unscored, round, biconvex tablets, debossed with “WPI” on one side and plain on the other side, in the following quantities:

NDC 0591-2191-30 Bottles of 30

NDC 0591-2191-01 Bottles of 100

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Keep container tightly closed and protected from moisture and humidity.
