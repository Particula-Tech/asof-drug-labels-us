# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Ramelteon tablets, 8 mg are available as light yellow to beige-coloured, round, biconvex bevel edged, film-coated tablets, debossed with "1344"on one side and plain on other side and are supplied as follows:

NDC 70771-1495-3 in bottles of 30 tablets with child-resistant closure

NDC 70771-1495-1 in bottles of 100 tablets

NDC 70771-1495-5 in bottles of 500 tablets

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Keep container tightly closed and protected from moisture and humidity.
