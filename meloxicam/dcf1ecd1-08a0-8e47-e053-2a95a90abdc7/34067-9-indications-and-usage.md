# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

SYMBRAVO is indicated for the acute treatment of migraine with or without aura in adults.

Limitations of Use

- SYMBRAVO should only be used where a clear diagnosis of migraine has been established.
  If a patient has no response for the first migraine attack treated with SYMBRAVO, the diagnosis of migraine should be reconsidered before SYMBRAVO is administered to treat any subsequent attacks.
- SYMBRAVO is not indicated for the preventive treatment of migraine attacks.
- SYMBRAVO is not indicated for the treatment of cluster headache.
