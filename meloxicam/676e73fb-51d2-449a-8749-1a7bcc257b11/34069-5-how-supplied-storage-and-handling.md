# HOW SUPPLIED

MOBIC is available as a pastel yellow, round, biconvex, uncoated tablet containing meloxicam 7.5 mg or as a pastel yellow, oblong, biconvex, uncoated tablet containing meloxicam 15 mg.
The 7.5 mg tablet is impressed with the Boehringer Ingelheim logo on one side, and on the other side, the letter “M”.
The 15 mg tablet is impressed with the tablet code “15” on one side and the letter “M” on the other.
MOBIC is also available as a yellowish green tinged viscous oral suspension containing 7.5 mg meloxicam in 5 mL.

MOBIC tablets 7.5 mg is available as follows: NDC 0597-0029-01; Bottles of 100

MOBIC tablets 15 mg is available as follows: NDC 0597-0030-01; Bottles of 100

MOBIC oral suspension 7.5 mg/5 mL is available as follows: NDC 0597-0034-01; Bottles of 100 mL
