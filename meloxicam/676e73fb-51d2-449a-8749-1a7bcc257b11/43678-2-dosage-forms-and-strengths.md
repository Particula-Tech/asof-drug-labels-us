# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

MOBIC (meloxicam) Tablets:

- 7.5 mg: pastel yellow, round, biconvex, uncoated tablet containing meloxicam 7.5 mg.
  Impressed with the Boehringer Ingelheim logo on one side and the letter “M” on the other.
- 15 mg: pastel yellow, oblong, biconvex, uncoated tablet containing meloxicam 15 mg.
  Impressed with the tablet code “15” on one side and the letter “M” on the other.

MOBIC (meloxicam) Oral Suspension:

- yellowish green tinged viscous suspension containing 7.5 mg meloxicam per 5 mL.
