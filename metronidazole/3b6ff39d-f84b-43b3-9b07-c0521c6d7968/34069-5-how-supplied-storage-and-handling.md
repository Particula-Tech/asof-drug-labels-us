# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

NUVESSA is available in cartons containing one single-dose, prefilled disposable applicator delivering 5 g of vaginal gel containing approximately 65 mg of metronidazole: 5 g disposable applicator (NDC 0642-7466-05).

Store at 20-25°C (68-77°F); excursions permitted to 15°-30°C (59°-86°F) [see USP Controlled Room temperature].
Do not freeze.
Do not refrigerate.
