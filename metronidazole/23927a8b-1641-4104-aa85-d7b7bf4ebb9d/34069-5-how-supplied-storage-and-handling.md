# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

How Supplied

LIKMEZ 500 mg/5 mL is supplied as 200 mL of white to slightly brown suspension with characteristic strawberry peppermint flavor packed in white HDPE round bottle with a child‑resistant cap (NDC 81033-066-20).

Storage

Store between 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
Brief exposure to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) permitted [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Do not freeze.

Dispense in a tight container as defined in USP.
Discard 10 days after opening container.
