# WARNING: POTENTIAL FOR CARCINOGENICITY

Metronidazole has been shown to be carcinogenic in mice and rats [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
Avoid unnecessary use of LIKMEZ.
Reserve LIKMEZ for use in the following indications: trichomoniasis [see Indications and Usage (1.1)], amebiasis [see Indications and Usage (1.2)] and anaerobic bacterial infections [see Indications and Usage (1.3)].
