# 11 DESCRIPTION

METROGEL contains metronidazole, USP.
Chemically, metronidazole is 2-methyl-5-nitro-1 H-imidazole-1-ethanol.
The molecular formula for metronidazole is C6H9N3O3.

Metronidazole has a molecular weight of 171.16.
It is a white to pale yellow crystalline powder.
It is slightly soluble in alcohol and has solubility in water of 10 mg/mL at 20˚C.
Metronidazole belongs to the nitroimidazole class of compounds.

METROGEL is an aqueous gel; each gram contains 10 mg of metronidazole in a base of betadex, edetate disodium, hydroxyethyl cellulose, methylparaben, niacinamide, phenoxyethanol, propylene glycol, propylparaben and purified water.
