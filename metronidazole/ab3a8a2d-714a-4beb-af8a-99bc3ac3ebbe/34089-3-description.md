# 11 DESCRIPTION

METROGEL (metronidazole) topical gel, 1% is a nitroimidazole for topical use.
METROGEL is a clear, colorless to pale yellow, aqueous gel.
Each gram contains 10 mg of metronidazole.
Chemically, metronidazole is 2-methyl-5-nitro-1 Himidazole- 1-ethanol.
The molecular formula for metronidazole is C6H9N3O3.

Metronidazole has a molecular weight of 171.16.
It is a white to pale yellow crystalline powder.
It is slightly soluble in alcohol and has solubility in water of 10 mg/mL at 20°C.
Metronidazole belongs to the nitroimidazole class of compounds.
The inactive ingredients are betadex, edetate disodium, hydroxyethyl cellulose, methylparaben, niacinamide, phenoxyethanol, propylene glycol, propylparaben and purified water.
