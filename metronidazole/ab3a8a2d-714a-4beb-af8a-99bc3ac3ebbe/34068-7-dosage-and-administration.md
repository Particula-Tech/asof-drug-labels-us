# DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

Areas to be treated should be cleansed before application of METROGE®L (metronidazole gel), 1%.
Apply and rub in a thin film of METROGEL® (metronidazole gel), 1% once daily to entire affected area(s).
Patients may use cosmetics after application of METROEL® (metronidazole gel), 1%.
