# DESCRIPTION:

METROCREAM® Topical Cream contains metronidazole, USP, at a concentration of 7.5 mg per gram (0.75%) in an emollient cream consisting of benzyl alcohol, emulsifying wax, glycerin, isopropyl palmitate, purified water, sorbitol solution, lactic acid and/or sodium hydroxide to adjust pH.
Metronidazole is a member of the imidazole class of antibacterial agents and is classified therapeutically as an antiprotozoal and anti-bacterial agent.
Chemically, metronidazole is 2-methyl-5-nitro-1 H-imidazole-1-ethanol.
The molecular formula is C6H9N3O3 and molecular weight is 171.16.
