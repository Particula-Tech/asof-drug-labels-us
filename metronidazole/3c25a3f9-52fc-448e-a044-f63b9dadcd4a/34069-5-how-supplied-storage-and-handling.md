# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Metronidazole Gel is clear, colorless to pale yellow in color, and is supplied as follows: 55 gram pump - NDC 68308-663-55

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Storage Conditions: Store at controlled room temperature: 20˚ to 25˚C (68˚ to 77˚F), excursions permitted between 15˚ and 30˚C (59˚ and 86˚F).
