# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Each PYLERA capsule contains 140 mg of bismuth subcitrate potassium, 125 mg of metronidazole, and a smaller capsule inside containing 125 mg of tetracycline hydrochloride.
The capsules are white and opaque, with the Axcan Pharma logo printed on the body and “BMT” printed on the cap.
