# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

PYLERA is supplied as a white opaque capsule containing 140 mg bismuth subcitrate potassium, 125 mg metronidazole, and 125 mg tetracycline hydrochloride, with the APTALIS™ logo printed on the body and "BMT" printed on the cap.
PYLERA capsules are supplied as the 10 day Therapy pack containing 10 blister cards, with each card containing 12 PYLERA capsules for a total of 120 capsules.

NDC Number: 61269-380-12, Blister pack of 120.

Storage

Store at controlled room temperature [68° to 77°F or 20° to 25°C].
