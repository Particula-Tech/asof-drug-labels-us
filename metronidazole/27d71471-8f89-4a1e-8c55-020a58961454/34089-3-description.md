# 11 DESCRIPTION

VANDAZOLE (metronidazole vaginal gel, 0.75%) is the vaginal dosage form of the nitroimidazole antimicrobial metronidazole at a concentration of 0.75%.
Chemically, metronidazole is a 2-methyl-5-nitroimidazole-1-ethanol.

C6H9N3O3 M.W.
171.16

VANDAZOLE is a colorless to yellow gel, containing metronidazole at a concentration of 7.5 mg/g (0.75%).
The gel also contains edetate disodium, hypromellose, methylparaben, propylene glycol, propylparaben, purified water, and sodium hydroxide (to adjust pH).

Each applicator full of 5 grams of vaginal gel contains approximately 37.5 mg of metronidazole.
