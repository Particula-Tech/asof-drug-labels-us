# 16HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Store at 36oF to 46oF (2oC to 8oC).
Do not freeze.

Do not shake.
Protect from light; store Epogen in the carton until use.

Do not use Epogen that has been shaken or frozen.

Single-dose, Preservative-free Vial (in citrate-buffered formulation): 1 mL of solution contains 2000 (NDC 55513-126-10), 3000 (NDC 55513-267-10), 4000 (NDC 55513-148-10), or 10,000 Units (NDC 55513-144-10) of epoetin alfa.
Each strength is supplied in dispensing packs containing 10 single-dose vials.

Multiple-dose, Preserved Vial: 2 mL total volume (20,000 Units total; 10,000 Units/mL).
Each 1 mL of solution contains 10,000 Units (NDC 55513-283-10) of epoetin alfa, and is supplied in dispensing packs containing 10 multiple-dose vials.

Multiple-dose, Preserved Vial: 1 mL total volume (20,000 Units/mL).
Each 1 mL of solution contains 20,000 Units (NDC 55513-478-10) of epoetin alfa and is supplied in dispensing packs containing 10 multiple-dose vials.
