# 10 OVERDOSAGE

Epogen overdosage can cause hemoglobin levels above the desired level, which should be managed with discontinuation or reduction of Epogen dosage and/or with phlebotomy, as clinically indicated [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.2)].
Cases of severe hypertension have been observed following overdose with ESAs [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3)].
