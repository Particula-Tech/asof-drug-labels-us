# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Store at 36°F to 46°F (2°C to 8°C).
Do not freeze.

Do not shake.
Protect from light; store PROCRIT in the carton until use.

Do not use PROCRIT that has been shaken or frozen.
