# PRINCIPAL DISPLAY PANEL – 25 mg/100 mg

NDC 0378-0088-01

Carbidopa and Levodopa Extended-Release Tablets, USP 25 mg/ 100 mg

Rx only 100 Tablets

Each tablet contains: Carbidopa, USP 25 mg* *(Anhydrous equivalent) Levodopa, USP 100 mg

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP using a child-resistant closure.

Keep container tightly closed.

Keep this and all medication out of the reach of children.

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

Usual Dosage: See accompanying prescribing information.

Tablets should be swallowed without chewing or crushing.
