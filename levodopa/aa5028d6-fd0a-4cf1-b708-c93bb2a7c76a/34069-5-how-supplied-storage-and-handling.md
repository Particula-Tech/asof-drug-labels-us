# HOW SUPPLIED

Carbidopa and Levodopa Extended-release Tablets containing 25 mg of carbidopa and 100 mg of levodopa are available as purple, oval, unscored, biconvex tablets debossed with MYLAN on one side of the tablet and 88 on the other side of the tablet.
They are available as follows:

NDC 0378-0088-01 bottles of 100 tablets NDC 0378-0088-05 bottles of 500 tablets

Carbidopa and Levodopa Extended-release Tablets containing 50 mg of carbidopa and 200 mg of levodopa are available as purple, oval, scored, biconvex tablets debossed with MYLAN on one side of the tablet and 9 to the left of the score and 4 to the right of the score on the other side of the tablet.
They are available as follows:

NDC 0378-0094-01 bottles of 100 tablets NDC 0378-0094-05 bottles of 500 tablets

Dispense in a tight container as defined in the USP using a child-resistant closure.

Keep container tightly closed.

DO NOT STORE ABOVE 30°C (86°F).
