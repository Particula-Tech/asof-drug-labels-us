# HOW SUPPLIED

Carbidopa and Levodopa Extended-release Tablets are available containing 25 mg of carbidopa, USP and 100 mg of levodopa, USP or 50 mg of carbidopa, USP and 200 mg of levodopa, USP.

The 25 mg/100 mg tablets are purple, oval, unscored tablets debossed with MYLAN on one side of the tablet and 88 on the other side of the tablet.
They are available as follows:

NDC 0378-0088-01 bottles of 100 tablets NDC 0378-0088-05 bottles of 500 tablets

The 50 mg/200 mg tablets are purple, oval, scored tablets debossed with MYLAN on one side of the tablet and 9 to the left of the score and 4 to the right of the score on the other side of the tablet.
They are available as follows:

NDC 0378-0094-01 bottles of 100 tablets NDC 0378-0094-05 bottles of 500 tablets

Dispense in a tight, light resistant container as defined in the USP using a child-resistant closure.

Keep container tightly closed.

DO NOT STORE ABOVE 30°C (86°F).
