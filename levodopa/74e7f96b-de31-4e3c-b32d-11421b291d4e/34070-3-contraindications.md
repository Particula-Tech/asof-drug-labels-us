# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Carbidopa and levodopa extended-release capsules are contraindicated in patients:

- Currently taking a nonselective monoamine oxidase (MAO) inhibitor (e.g., phenelzine and tranylcypromine) or have recently (within 2 weeks) taken a nonselective MAO inhibitor.
  Hypertension can occur if these drugs are used concurrently [see Drug Interactions (7.1)].
