# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

INBRIJA is contraindicated in patients currently taking a nonselective monoamine oxidase (MAO) inhibitor (e.g., phenelzine and tranylcypromine) or who have recently (within 2 weeks) taken a nonselective MAO inhibitor.
Hypertension can occur if these drugs are used concurrently [see Drug Interactions (7.1)].
