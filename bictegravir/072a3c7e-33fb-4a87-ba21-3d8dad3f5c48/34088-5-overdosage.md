# 10 OVERDOSAGE

No data are available on overdose of BIXLENVO in patients.
If overdose occurs, monitor the patient for evidence of toxicity.
Treatment of overdose with BIXLENVO consists of general supportive measures including monitoring of vital signs as well as observation of the clinical status of the patient.
As BIC and LEN are highly bound to plasma proteins, they are unlikely to be significantly removed by dialysis.
