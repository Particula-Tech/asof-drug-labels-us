# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

BIKTARVY tablets are purplish brown, capsule-shaped, and film-coated with "GSI" debossed on one side and "9883" on the other side.
Each bottle contains 30 tablets (NDC 61958-2501-1), a silica gel desiccant, polyester coil, and is closed with a child-resistant closure.
Each BIKTARVY tablet contains 50 mg of bictegravir (BIC), 200 mg of emtricitabine (FTC), and 25 mg of tenofovir alafenamide (TAF).

Store below 30 °C (86 °F).

- Keep container tightly closed.
- Dispense only in original container.
