# PRINCIPAL DISPLAY PANEL – 250 mg Film-Coated

NDC 0378-6924-78

Abiraterone Acetate Tablets, USP 250 mg

Rx only 120 Film-Coated Tablets

Each film-coated tablet contains: Abiraterone acetate, USP 250 mg

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP using a child-resistant closure.

Keep container tightly closed.

Keep this and all medication out of the reach of children.

| Swallow whole tablet. Do not crush or chew. |
| --- |

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted in the range from 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Usual Dosage: See accompanying prescribing information.
