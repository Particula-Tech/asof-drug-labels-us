# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

## Instructions for Patients

Women of childbearing potential should be advised to avoid becoming pregnant while receiving treatment with Decitabine for Injection and for 1 month afterwards, and to use effective contraception during this time, [See Warnings and Precautions (5.3)].

Men should be advised not to father a child while receiving treatment with Decitabine for Injection, and for 2 months afterwards.
During these times, men with female partners of childbearing potential should use effective contraception [See Warnings and Precautions (5.4) andNonclinical Toxicology (13.1)].

Patients should be advised to monitor and report any symptoms of neutropenia, thrombocytopenia, or fever to their physician as soon as possible [See Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
