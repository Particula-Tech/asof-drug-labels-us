# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

## Instructions for Patients

Women of childbearing potential should be advised to avoid becoming pregnant while receiving treatment with DACOGEN and for 1 month afterwards, and to use effective contraception during this time, [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3)].

Men should be advised not to father a child while receiving treatment with DACOGEN, and for 2 months afterwards.
During these times, men with female partners of childbearing potential should use effective contraception [see Warnings and Precautions (5.4) and Nonclinical Toxicology (13.1)].

Patients should be advised to monitor and report any symptoms of neutropenia, thrombocytopenia, or fever to their physician as soon as possible [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
