# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Decitabine for injection is a sterile, white to almost white lyophilized powder for intravenous use supplied as:

NDC 55111-556-10, 50 mg single-dose vial individually packaged in a carton.

Store vials at 20°-25°C (68°-77°F); [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
