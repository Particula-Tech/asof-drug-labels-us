# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Decitabine for Injection 50 mg is white to almost white Lyophilized cake or powder in 20 mL Type-I clear glass vial with 20 mm igloo grey rubber stopper and sealed with 20 mm aluminum seal having blue color polypropylene disc.
NDC 72205-031-01, 50 mg single-dose vial individually packaged in a carton.
Storage Store vials at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15 to 30°C (59 to 86°F).
