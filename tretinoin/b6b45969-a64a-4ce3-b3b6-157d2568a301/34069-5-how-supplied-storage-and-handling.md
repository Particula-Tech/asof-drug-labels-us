# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Atralin (tretinoin) Gel, 0.05% is a translucent to opaque, pale yellow topical gel and available as:

- •45 g tube NDC 13548-070-45

Storage and Handling: Store at controlled room temperature 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) with excursions permitted between 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F).
Protect from freezing.
Keep out of reach of children.
