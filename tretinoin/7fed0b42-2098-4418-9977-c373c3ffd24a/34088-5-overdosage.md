# 10 OVERDOSAGE

In case of overdose with VESANOID, reversible signs of hypervitaminosis A (headache, nausea, vomiting, mucocutaneous symptoms) can appear.
Overdosage with other retinoids has been associated with transient headache, facial flushing, cheilosis, abdominal pain, dizziness and ataxia.
These symptoms have quickly resolved without apparent residual effects.

There is no specific treatment in the case of an overdose; however, it is important that the patient be treated in a special hematological unit.
