# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Tretinoin Capsules are available as:

| 10 mg: | Pink, oval, soft-gelatin capsule printed with black ‘T 10’ filled with yellow to orange, opaque, viscous suspension. |
| --- | --- |

NDC 68462-792-01 Bottle of 100 capsules with child-resistant closure

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68° F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° C to 30°C (59° F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Keep bottle tightly closed.
Protect from light.
