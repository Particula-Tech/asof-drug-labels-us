# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Tretinoin Gel Microsphere is a white to very pale yellow opaque gel.
Tretinoin Gel Microsphereis available in three strengths: 0.1%, 0.08% and 0.04%.

Each gram of Tretinoin Gel Microsphere, 0.1%, contains 1 mg of tretinoin.

Each gram of Tretinoin Gel Microsphere, 0.08%, contains 0.8 mg of tretinoin.

Each gram of Tretinoin Gel Microsphere, 0.04%, contains 0.4 mg of tretinoin.
