# 11 DESCRIPTION

Atralin Gel is a translucent to opaque, pale yellow topical gel containing 0.05% tretinoin, by weight.
Other components of this formulation are benzyl alcohol, butyl paraben, butylated hydroxytoluene, carbomer 940, ethyl paraben, fish collagen hydrolyzates, glycerin, iso-butyl paraben, methylparaben, octoxynol 9, phenoxyethanol, propylparaben, purified water, sodium hyaluronate, and trolamine.

Chemically, tretinoin is all-trans-retinoic acid, also known as (all-E)-3,7-dimethyl-9-(2,6,6-trimethyl-1-cyclohexen-1-yl)-2,4,6,8-nonatetraenoic acid.
It is a member of the retinoid family of compounds, and a metabolite of Vitamin A. Tretinoin has a molecular weight of 300.44.
