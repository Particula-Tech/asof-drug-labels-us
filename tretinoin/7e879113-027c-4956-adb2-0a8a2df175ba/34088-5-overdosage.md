# OVERDOSAGE

Retin-A Micro (tretinoin gel) microsphere, 0.1% and 0.04%, is intended for topical use only.
If medication is applied excessively, no more rapid or better results will be obtained and marked redness, peeling, or discomfort may occur.
Oral ingestion of large amounts of the drug may lead to the same side effects as those associated with excessive oral intake of Vitamin A.
