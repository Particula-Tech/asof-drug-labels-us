# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Macitentan Tablets, 10 mg are white to off-white colored, round-shaped, biconvex, film-coated tablet debossed with "MT" on one side and "10" on other side and are supplied as follows:

NDC 70710-1166-3 in bottles of 30 tablets with child-resistant closure

NDC 70710-1166-5 in carton of 15 (5 x 3) unit-dose tablets

Store at 20ºC to 25ºC (68ºF to 77ºF).
Excursions are permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F and 86°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Keep out of reach of children.
