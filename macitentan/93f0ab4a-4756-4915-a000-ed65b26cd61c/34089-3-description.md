# 11 DESCRIPTION

Macitentan is an endothelin receptor antagonist.
The chemical name of macitentan is N-[5-(4-Bromophenyl)-6-[2-[(5-bromo-2-pyrimidinyl)oxy]ethoxy]-4-pyrimidinyl]-N'-propylsulfamide.
It has a molecular formula of C19H20Br2N6O4S and a molecular weight of 588.27.

Macitentan is a white to off-white color solid that is soluble in dichloromethane, ethyl acetate and practically insoluble in water.
In the solid state macitentan is very stable, is not hygroscopic, and is not light sensitive.

Macitentan tablets are available as a 10 mg film-coated tablet for once daily oral administration.
The tablets include the following inactive ingredients: crospovidone, magnesium stearate, mannitol, microcrystalline cellulose, polysorbate 80, polyvinylpyrrolidone.
The tablets are film-coated with a coating material containing polyvinyl alcohol, soya lecithin, talc, titanium dioxide, and xanthan gum.
