# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Macitentan tablets are 10 mg, white to off white, round shaped, biconvex, film coated tablets debossed with ‘L1’ on one side and plain on the other side and supplied as follows:

Bottle of 30 NDC 42385-907-30 15 Tablets (1 x 15 Unit-Dose Blisters) NDC 42385-907-15

Store at 20º to 25ºC (68º to 77ºF); excursions permitted between 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

Keep out of reach of children.
