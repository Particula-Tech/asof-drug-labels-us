# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

## 4.1 Pregnancy

OPSUMIT may cause fetal harm when administered to a pregnant woman.
OPSUMIT is contraindicated in females who are pregnant.
OPSUMIT was consistently shown to have teratogenic effects when administered to animals.
If OPSUMIT is used during pregnancy, advise the patient of the potential risk to a fetus [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)and Use in Specific Populations (8.1)].

## 4.2 Hypersensitivity

OPSUMIT is contraindicated in patients with a history of a hypersensitivity reaction to macitentan or any component of the product [see Adverse Reactions (6.2)].
