# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

## 4.1 Pregnancy

OPSUMIT may cause fetal harm when administered to a pregnant woman.
OPSUMIT is contraindicated in females who are pregnant.
OPSUMIT was consistently shown to have teratogenic effects when administered to animals.
If OPSUMIT is used during pregnancy, apprise the patient of the potential hazard to a fetus [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1) and Use in Specific Populations (8.1)].
