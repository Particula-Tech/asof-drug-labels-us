# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Macitentan tablets are 10 mg white colored, round shaped, biconvex, film-coated tablets, debossed with "M" on one side and "10" on other side and supplied as follows: 30 tablets white high-density polyethylene bottle (NDC 0781-8135-31) Store at 20ºC to 25ºC (68ºF to 77ºF).
Excursions are permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F and 86°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Keep out of reach of children.
