# HOW SUPPLIED

Vials:

200 mg white, lyophilized powder in a 10–mL size sterile single use vial (No.
7501) NDC 0002–7501–01

1 g white, lyophilized powder in a 50–mL size sterile single use vial (No.
7502) NDC 0002–7502–01

Store at controlled room temperature 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
The USP has defined controlled room temperature as “A temperature maintained thermostatically that encompasses the usual and customary working environment of 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); that results in a mean kinetic temperature calculated to be not more than 25°C; and that allows for excursions between 15° and 30°C (59° and 86°F) that are experienced in pharmacies, hospitals, and warehouses.”
