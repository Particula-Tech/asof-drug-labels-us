# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Gemcitabine for Injection, USP is available in sterile single-use vials individually packaged in a carton containing:

200 mg white, lyophilized powder in a sterile single-use vial - NDC 55111-686-07.

1 g white, lyophilized powder in a sterile single-use vial – 55111-687-25.

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Unopened vials of gemcitabine for injection are stable until the expiration date indicated on the package when stored at 20°-25°C (68°-77°F); [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
[see Dosage and Administration (2.5 and 2.6)].
