# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

INFUGEM (gemcitabine in 0.9% sodium chloride injection) is a clear, colorless, sterile solution in a single-dose, premixed intravenous infusion bag with an aluminum overwrap.
The container closure is not made with natural rubber latex and is tamper evident.
It is available in presentations as described in Table 22.

INFUGEM is a cytotoxic drug.
Follow applicable special handling and disposal procedures.1

Unopened infusion bags of INFUGEM are stable until the expiration date indicated on the package when stored at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F and 86°F).
[see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Do not freeze as crystallization can occur.
