# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

How Supplied

INLEXZO (gemcitabine intravesical system) contains 225 mg gemcitabine.

INLEXZO (NDC# 57894-225-01) is available in a carton containing:

- One sterile single-dose of INLEXZO in two clear laminate sleeves and packaged in an inner pouch.
  The inner pouch and a desiccant are packaged in an outer foil pouch.
  The outside surfaces of the inner and outer pouches are not sterile.
- One sterile urinary catheter and one sterile stylet packaged together in a pouch.

Storage

Store in the original carton at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); with excursions permitted between 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Handling

INLEXZO is a hazardous drug.
Follow applicable special handling and disposal procedures.
