# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Gemcitabine Injection is supplied as follows:

| NDC | Gemcitabine Injection (38 mg per mL) | Package Factor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 25021-239-05 | 200 mg per 5.26 mL Single-Dose Vial | 1 vial per carton |
| 25021-239-26 | 1 gram per 26.3 mL Single-Dose Vial | 1 vial per carton |
| 25021-239-52 | 2 grams per 52.6 mL Single-Dose Vial | 1 vial per carton |

Gemcitabine Injection appears as a clear and colorless to light straw-colored solution.

Store refrigerated between 2° and 8°C (36° and 46°F).

Do not freeze.
Discard unused portion.

Sterile, Nonpyrogenic, Preservative-free.
The container closure is not made with natural rubber latex.

Gemcitabine Injection is a cytotoxic drug.
Follow applicable special handling and disposal procedures.1
