# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Gemcitabine Injection is supplied as follows:

| NDC | Gemcitabine Injection (38 mg per mL) | Package Factor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 25021-239-05 | 200 mg per 5.26 mL Single-Use Vial | 1 vial per carton |
| 25021-239-26 | 1 gram per 26.3 mL Single-Use Vial | 1 vial per carton |
| 25021-239-52 | 2 grams per 52.6 mL Single-Use Vial | 1 vial per carton |

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Unopened vials of Gemcitabine Injection are stable until the expiration date indicated on the package.
Store refrigerated between 2° and 8°C (36° and 46°F).
[See Dosage and Administration (2.6 and 2.7)].

Do not freeze.
Discard unused portion.

Sterile, Nonpyrogenic, Preservative-free.
The container closure is not made with natural rubber latex.
