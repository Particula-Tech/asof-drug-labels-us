# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Gemcitabine Injection is available in sterile single-use vials individually packaged in a carton as follows:

- 200 mg/5.26 mL (38 mg/mL), sterile solution in a single-use glass vial per package, NDC 0409-0183-01
- 1 g/26.3 mL (38 mg/mL), sterile solution in a single-use glass vial per package, NDC 0409-0181-01
- 2 g/52.6 mL (38 mg/mL), sterile solution in a single-use glass vial per package, NDC 0409-0182-01

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Unopened vials of Gemcitabine Injection are stable until the expiration date indicated on the package when stored at 2° to 8°C (36° to 46°F).
Do not freeze [see Dosage and Administration (2.5 and 2.6)].
