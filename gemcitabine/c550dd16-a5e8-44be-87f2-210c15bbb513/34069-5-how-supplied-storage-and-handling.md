# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Gemcitabine for Injection, USP is supplied as a sterile, lyophilized powder as follows:

| Product No. | NDC No. | Strength |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FK101210 | 63323-102-13 | 200 mg/vial | 10 mL single use vial packaged individually. |
| FK102550 | 63323-125-53 | 1 gram/vial | 50 mL single use vial packaged individually. |
| FK102600 | 63323-126-03 | 2 gram/vial | 100 mL single use vial packaged individually. |

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Unopened vials of gemcitabine are stable until the expiration date indicated on the package when stored at controlled room temperature 20º to 25º C (68º to 77ºF) and that allows for excursions between 15º and 30ºC (59º and 86ºF) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature] [see Dosage and Administration (2.5 and 2.6)].
This container closure is not made with natural rubber latex.
