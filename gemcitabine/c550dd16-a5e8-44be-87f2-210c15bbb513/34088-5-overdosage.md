# 10 OVERDOSAGE

There is no known antidote for overdoses of gemcitabine.
Myelosuppression, paresthesias, and severe rash were the principal toxicities seen when a single dose as high as 5,700 mg/m 2 was administered by intravenous infusion over 30 minutes every 2 weeks to several patients in a dose-escalation study.
In the event of suspected overdose, monitor with appropriate blood counts and provide supportive therapy, as necessary.
