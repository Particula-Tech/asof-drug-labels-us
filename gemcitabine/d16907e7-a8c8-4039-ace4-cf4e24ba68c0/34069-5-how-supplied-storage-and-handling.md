# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Gemcitabine for Injection, USP is available in sterile single-use vials individually packaged in a carton containing:

200 mg white to off-white, lyophilized powder in a 10-mL size sterile single-use vial - NDC 0409-0185-01

1 g white to off-white, lyophilized powder in a 50-mL size sterile single-use vial - NDC 0409-0186-01

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Unopened vials of Gemcitabine for Injection, USP are stable until the expiration date indicated on the package when stored at controlled room temperature 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) and that allows for excursions between 15° and 30°C (59° and 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature] [see Dosage and Administration (2.5 and 2.6)].
