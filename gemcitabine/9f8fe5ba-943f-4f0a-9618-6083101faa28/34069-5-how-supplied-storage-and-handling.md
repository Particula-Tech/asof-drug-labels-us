# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Gemcitabine for Injection, USP, is supplied as a clear tubular USP Type-I, sterile single use vial, lyophilized powder or cake as follows:

| NDC | Gemcitabine for Injection, USP | Package Factor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 68083-148-01 | 200 mg Single-use Vial | 1 vial per carton |
| 68083-149-01 | 1 gram Single-use Vial | 1 vial per carton |

Gemcitabine for Injection, USP is a white to off-white lyophilized powder.

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Unopened vials of Gemcitabine for Injection, USP are stable until the expiration date indicated on the package when stored at controlled room temperature 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) and that allows for excursions between 15° and 30°C (59° and 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature] [see Dosage and Administration (2.6 and 2.7)].

Sterile, Nonpyrogenic, Preservative-free, Lyophilized.

The container closure is not made with natural rubber latex.
