# 11 DESCRIPTION

Gemcitabine is a nucleoside metabolic inhibitor.
Gemcitabine hydrochloride, USP is 2’- deoxy-2’, 2’-difluorocytidine monohydrochloride (β-isomer) with the following structural formula:

The empirical formula for gemcitabine hydrochloride, USP is C9H11F2N3O4 • HCl.
It has a molecular weight of 299.66 g/mol.
Gemcitabine hydrochloride, USP is soluble in water, slightly soluble in methanol, and practically insoluble in ethanol and polar organic solvents.
Gemcitabine for Injection, USP is a sterile white to off-white lyophilized powder and available as 200 mg and 1 gram single-dose vials for intravenous use only.
Each 200 mg vial contains 200 mg gemcitabine (equivalent to 227.7 mg gemcitabine hydrochloride, USP), 200 mg mannitol and 12.5 mg sodium acetate.
Each 1 gram vial contains 1 gram gemcitabine (equivalent to 1.139 gram gemcitabine hydrochloride, USP), 1 gram mannitol, and 62.5 mg sodium acetate.
Hydrochloric acid and/or sodium hydroxide may have been added for pH adjustment.
