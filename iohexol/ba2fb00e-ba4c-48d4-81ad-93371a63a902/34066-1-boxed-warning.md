# WARNING: RISKS WITH INADVERTENT INTRATHECAL ADMINISTRATION

See prescribing information for full boxed warning.

FOR INTRAVENOUS USE ONLY.
Inadvertent intrathecal administration may cause death, convulsions/seizures, cerebral hemorrhage, coma, paralysis, arachnoiditis, acute renal failure, cardiac arrest, rhabdomyolysis, hyperthermia, and brain edema [see Warnings and precautions (5.1)].
