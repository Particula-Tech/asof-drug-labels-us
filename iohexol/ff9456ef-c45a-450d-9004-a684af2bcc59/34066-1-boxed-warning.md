# WARNING: RISKS ASSOCIATED WITH INTRATHECAL ADMINISTRATION OF IOHEXOL INJECTION 140 mg IODINE/mL and 350 mg IODINE/mL

Use only the iohexol, iodine concentrations and presentations recommended for intrathecal procedure [see Dosage and Administration (2.2, 2.8)].
Intrathecal administration of iohexol of a wrong iodine concentration, even if inadvertent, may cause death, convulsions, seizures, cerebral hemorrhage, coma, paralysis, arachnoiditis, acute renal failure, cardiac arrest, rhabdomyolysis, hyperthermia, and brain edema [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
