# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Fluticasone Propionate DISKUS 50 mcg is supplied as a disposable orange plastic inhaler containing a foil blister strip with 60 blisters.
The inhaler is packaged in a plastic-coated, moisture-protective foil pouch (NDC 66993-790-97).

Fluticasone Propionate DISKUS 100 mcg is supplied as a disposable orange plastic inhaler containing a foil blister strip with 60 blisters.
The inhaler is packaged in a plastic-coated, moisture-protective foil pouch (NDC 66993-791-97).

Fluticasone Propionate DISKUS 250 mcg is supplied as a disposable orange plastic inhaler containing a foil blister strip with 60 blisters.
The inhaler is packaged in a plastic-coated, moisture-protective foil pouch (NDC 66993-792-97).

Store at room temperature between 68°F and 77°F (20°C and 25°C); excursions permitted from 59°F to 86°F (15°C to 30°C) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Store in a dry place away from direct heat or sunlight.
Keep out of reach of children.

Fluticasone Propionate DISKUS should be stored inside the unopened moisture-protective foil pouch and only removed from the pouch immediately before initial use.
Discard Fluticasone Propionate DISKUS 6 weeks (50-mcg strength) or 2 months (100- and 250-mcg strengths) after opening the foil pouch or when the counter reads “0” (after all blisters have been used), whichever comes first.
The inhaler is not reusable.
Do not attempt to take the inhaler apart.
