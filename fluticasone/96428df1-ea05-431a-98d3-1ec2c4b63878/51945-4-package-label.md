BREO ELLIPTA

(fluticasone furoate and vilanterol inhalation powder)

50 mcg/25 mcg

Rx Only

FOR ORAL INHALATION ONLY

BREO ELLIPTA contains 2 foil strips of 30 blisters each.
Each blister on one strip contains 50 mcg of fluticasone furoate and lactose monohydrate.
Each blister on the other strip contains 25 mcg of vilanterol, magnesium stearate, and lactose monohydrate.

1 ELLIPTA Inhaler containing 30 doses (60 blisters total)
