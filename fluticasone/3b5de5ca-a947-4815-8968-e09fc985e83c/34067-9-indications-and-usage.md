# INDICATIONS AND USAGE

CUTIVATE® Cream is a medium potency corticosteroid indicated for the relief of the inflammatory and pruritic manifestations of corticosteroid-responsive dermatoses.
CUTIVATE® Cream may be used with caution in pediatric patients 3 months of age or older.
The safety and efficacy of drug use for longer than 4 weeks in this population have not been established.
The safety and efficacy of CUTIVATE® Cream in pediatric patients below 3 months of age have not been established.
