# INDICATIONS AND USAGE

CUTIVATE® (fluticasone propionate) Lotion is indicated for the relief of the inflammatory and pruritic manifestations of atopic dermatitis in patients 1 year of age or older.
The safety and efficacy of drug use for longer than 4 weeks in this population have not been established.
The safety and efficacy of CUTIVATE® Lotion in pediatric patients below 1 year of age have not been established.
