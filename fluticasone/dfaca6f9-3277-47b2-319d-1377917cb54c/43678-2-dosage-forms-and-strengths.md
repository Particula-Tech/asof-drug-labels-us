# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

ADVAIR HFA is an inhalation aerosol.
Each actuation delivers a combination of fluticasone propionate (45, 115, or 230 mcg) and salmeterol (21 mcg) from the actuator.
ADVAIR HFA is supplied in 8- and 12-g pressurized aluminum canisters containing 60 and 120 metered inhalations, respectively.
Each canister is fitted with a counter and a purple actuator with a light purple strapcap.
