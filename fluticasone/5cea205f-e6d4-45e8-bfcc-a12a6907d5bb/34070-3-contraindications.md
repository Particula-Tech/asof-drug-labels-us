# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

ArmonAir Digihaler is contraindicated:

- in the primary treatment of status asthmaticus or other acute episodes of asthma where intensive measures are required [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)].
- in patients with known severe hypersensitivity to milk proteins or who have demonstrated hypersensitivity to fluticasone propionate or any of the excipients [see Warnings and Precautions (5.6) and Description (11)].
