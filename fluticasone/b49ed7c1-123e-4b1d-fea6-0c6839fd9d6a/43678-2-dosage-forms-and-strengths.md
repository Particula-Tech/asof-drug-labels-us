# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

FLOVENT HFA is an inhalation aerosol.
Each actuation delivers 44, 110, or 220 mcg of fluticasone propionate from the actuator.
FLOVENT HFA 44 mcg is supplied in 10.6-g pressurized aluminum canisters, and FLOVENT HFA 110 mcg and FLOVENT HFA 220 mcg are supplied in 12-g pressurized aluminum canisters.
Each canister contains 120 metered inhalations and is fitted with a counter and a dark orange oral actuator with a peach strapcap.
