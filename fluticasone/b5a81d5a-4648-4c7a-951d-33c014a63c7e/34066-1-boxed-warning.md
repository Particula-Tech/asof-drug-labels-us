# WARNING: ASTHMA-RELATED DEATH

Long-acting beta2-adrenergic agonists (LABA), such as vilanterol, one of the active ingredients in TRELEGY ELLIPTA, increase the risk of asthma-related death.
Data from a large placebo-controlled U.S. trial that compared the safety of another LABA (salmeterol) with placebo added to usual asthma therapy showed an increase in asthma-related deaths in subjects receiving salmeterol.
This finding with salmeterol is considered a class effect of LABA [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].

The safety and efficacy of TRELEGY ELLIPTA in patients with asthma have not been established.
TRELEGY ELLIPTA is not indicated for the treatment of asthma [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
