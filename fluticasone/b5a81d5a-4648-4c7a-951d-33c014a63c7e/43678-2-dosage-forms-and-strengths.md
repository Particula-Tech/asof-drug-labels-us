# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Inhalation powder: Disposable light grey and beige plastic inhaler containing 2 foil blister strips of powder intended for oral inhalation only.
One strip contains fluticasone furoate (100 mcg per blister) and the other strip contains a blend of umeclidinium and vilanterol (62.5 mcg and 25 mcg per blister, respectively).
