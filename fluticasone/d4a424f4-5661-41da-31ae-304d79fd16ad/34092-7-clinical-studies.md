# 14 CLINICAL STUDIES

Perennial Nonallergic Rhinitis:Three randomized, double‑blind, parallel‑group, vehicle placebo‑controlled trials were conducted in 1,191 subjects to investigate regular use of FLONASE Nasal Spray in subjects with perennial nonallergic rhinitis.
These trials evaluated subject‑rated total nasal symptom scores (TNSS) that included nasal obstruction, postnasal drip, rhinorrhea in subjects treated for 28 days of double‑blind therapy and in 1 of the 3 trials for 6 months of open‑label treatment.
Two of these trials demonstrated that subjects treated with Flonase Nasal Spray (100 mcg twice daily) exhibited statistically significant decreases in TNSS compared with subjects treated with vehicle.
