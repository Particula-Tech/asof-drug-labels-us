# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

ARMONAIR RESPICLICK (fluticasone propionate) Inhalation Powder:

- a white multidose dry-powder inhaler (MDPI)
- each inhaler has a green cap and is packaged individually in a foil pouch with desiccant, one pouch per carton
- is available as noted in the table below

| STRENGTH | Actuations | Net Contents | NDC CODE |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ARMONAIR RESPICLICK 30 mcg (pediatric) | 60 | 0.46 grams | NDC 59310-320-06 |
| ARMONAIR RESPICLICK 55 mcg (low) | 60 | 0.9 grams | NDC 59310-705-06 |
| ARMONAIR RESPICLICK 113 mcg (medium) | 60 | 0.9 grams | NDC 59310-711-06 |
| ARMONAIR RESPICLICK 232 mcg (high) | 60 | 0.9 grams | NDC 59310-722-06 |

Each ARMONAIR RESPICLICK inhaler has a dose counter attached to the actuator.
Patients should never try to alter the numbers for the dose counter.
Discard the inhaler when the counter displays 0, 30 days after opening the foil pouch or after the expiration date on the product, whichever comes first.
The labeled amount of medication in each actuation cannot be assured after the counter displays 0, even though the inhaler is not completely empty and will continue to operate [see Patient Counseling Information (17)].

Store at room temperature (between 15ºC and 25ºC; 59ºF and 77ºF) in a dry place; excursions permitted from 59ºF to 86ºF (15ºC to 30ºC).
Avoid exposure to extreme heat, cold, or humidity.

Keep out of reach of children.

Store ARMONAIR RESPICLICK inside the unopened moisture‑protective foil pouch and remove from the pouch immediately before initial use.
Discard ARMONAIR RESPICLICK 30 days after opening the foil pouch or when the counter reads “0”, whichever comes first.
The inhaler is not reusable.
Do not attempt to take the inhaler apart.
