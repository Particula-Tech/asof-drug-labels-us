# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

## 2.1 Administration Instructions

- ARMONAIR RESPICLICK is for oral inhalation and does not require priming.
- Do not use ARMONAIR RESPICLICK with a spacer or volume holding chamber.

- Do not use more than 2 times every 24 hours.
  The safety and effectiveness of ARMONAIR RESPICLICK when administered in excess of recommended dosages have not been established.
- If symptoms arise between doses, an inhaled short-acting beta2-agonist should be used for immediate relief.

## 2.2 Recommended Dosage

Administer 1 inhalation of ARMONAIR RESPICLICK (fluticasone propionate) twice daily by oral inhalation (approximately 12 hours apart at the same time every day).
Rinse the mouth with water without swallowing after each dose.

Dosage Selection

The recommended starting dosage for ARMONAIR RESPICLICK is based on asthma severity and current asthma therapy and strength.

Adult and Pediatric Patients Aged 12 Years and Older:

- Patients with greater asthma severity, use the higher doses:
  - 1 inhalation of 113 mcg ARMONAIR RESPICLICK (113 mcg of fluticasone propionate) twice daily by oral inhalation; or
  - 1 inhalation of 232 mcg ARMONAIR RESPICLICK (232 mcg of fluticasone propionate) twice daily by oral inhalation.

- Patients not taking inhaled corticosteroids (ICS), with less severe asthma:
  - 1 inhalation of 55 mcg ARMONAIR RESPICLICK (55 mcg of fluticasone propionate) twice daily by oral inhalation.

- Patients switching to ARMONAIR RESPICLICK from another ICS:
  - 1 inhalation of low (55 mcg fluticasone propionate), medium (113 mcg fluticasone propionate), or high (232 mcg fluticasone propionate) ARMONAIR RESPICLICK twice daily by oral inhalation based on the strength of the previous ICS product and disease severity.

- The maximum recommended dosage is 232 mcg twice daily.

Pediatric Patients Aged 4 to 11 Years:

- Patients not taking inhaled corticosteroids (ICS), with less severe asthma:
  - 1 inhalation of 30 mcg ARMONAIR RESPICLICK (30 mcg of fluticasone propionate) twice daily by oral inhalation.

- Patients switching to ARMONAIR RESPICLICK from another ICS:
  - 1 inhalation of 30 mcg fluticasone propionate or 55 mcg fluticasone propionate ARMONAIR RESPICLICK twice daily by oral inhalation based on the strength of the previous ICS product and disease severity.

- Dosages above 55 mcg of ARMONAIR RESPICLICK twice daily by oral inhalation have not been established in patients aged 4 to 11 years.

General Dosing Information

The maximum benefit may not be achieved for up to 2 weeks or longer after starting treatment.
Individual patients will experience a variable time to onset and degree of symptom relief.
For patients who do not respond to lower doses of ARMONAIR RESPICLICK after 2 weeks of therapy, increasing the dose may provide additional asthma control.

If a previously effective dosage regimen of ARMONAIR RESPICLICK fails to provide adequate control of asthma, re-evaluate the therapeutic regimen, including patient compliance and inhaler technique, and consider additional therapeutic options (e.g., increasing the dose of ARMONAIR RESPICLICK with a higher strength, or adding additional controller therapies).
After asthma stability has been achieved, it is desirable to titrate to the lowest effective dosage to reduce the risk of adverse reactions.

## 2.3 Storing and Cleaning the Inhaler

- Keep the inhaler in a cool dry place.
- Routine maintenance is not required.
  If the mouthpiece needs cleaning, gently wipe the mouthpiece with a dry cloth or tissue as needed.
- Never wash or put any part of the inhaler in water.

## 2.4 Dose Counter

The ARMONAIR RESPICLICK inhaler has a dose counter.

- The number 60 is displayed (prior to use).
- The dose counter will count down each time the mouthpiece is opened and closed [see Patient Counseling Information (17)].
