# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Inhalation powder: an inhalation-driven, multidose dry powder inhaler (MDPI) for oral inhalation that meters 30 mcg, 55 mcg, 113 mcg, or 232 mcg of fluticasone propionate from the device reservoir and delivers 26 mcg, 51 mcg, 103 mcg, or 210 mcg of fluticasone propionate, respectively, from the mouthpiece per actuation.
The ARMONAIR RESPICLICK is a white inhaler with a green cap in a sealed foil pouch with desiccant.
