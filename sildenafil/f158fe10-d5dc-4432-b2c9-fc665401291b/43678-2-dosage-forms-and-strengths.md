# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

REVATIO Tablets

White, film-coated, round tablets engraved with "RVT20" containing sildenafil citrate equivalent to 20 mg of sildenafil.

REVATIO Injection

Single use vial containing 10 mg/12.5 mL of sildenafil.

REVATIO for Oral Suspension

White to off-white powders containing 1.57 g of sildenafil citrate (equivalent to 1.12 g of sildenafil) in a bottle intended for reconstitution.
Following reconstitution with 90 mL of water, the volume of the oral suspension is 112 mL and the oral suspension contains 10 mg/mL sildenafil.
A 2 mL oral syringe (with 0.5 mL and 2 mL dose markings) and a press-in bottle adaptor are also provided.
