# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

## 4.1 Use with Organic Nitrates

Do not use REVATIO in patients taking organic nitrates in any form, either regularly or intermittently.
Consistent with its known effects on the nitric oxide/cGMP pathway, sildenafil was shown to potentiate the hypotensive effects of nitrates.

## 4.2 Hypersensitivity Reactions

REVATIO is contraindicated in patients with a known hypersensitivity to sildenafil or any component of the tablet or injection.

Rare cases of hypersensitivity have been reported in association with the use of sildenafil including anaphylactic reaction/shock events and anaphylactoid reaction.
The majority of reported events were non-serious hypersensitivity reactions.
