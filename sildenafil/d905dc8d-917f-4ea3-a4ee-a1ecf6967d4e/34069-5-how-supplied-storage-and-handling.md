# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

VIAGRA (sildenafil citrate) is supplied as blue, rounded diamond shaped film-coated tablets containing sildenafil citrate equivalent to the nominally indicated amount of sildenafil and debossed on the obverse and reverse sides as follows:

|  | 25 mg | 50 mg | 100 mg |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Obverse | “VGR 25” | “VGR 50” | “VGR 100” |
| Reverse | “VIAGRA” | “VIAGRA” | “VIAGRA” |
| Bottle of 30 | NDC-58151-426-93 | NDC-58151-427-93 | NDC-58151-428-93 |
| Bottle of 100 | N/A | NDC-58151-427-01 | NDC-58151-428-01 |

Recommended Storage: Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15-30°C (59-86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
