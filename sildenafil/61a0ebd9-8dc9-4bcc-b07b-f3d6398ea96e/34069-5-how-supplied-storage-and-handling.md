# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Sildenafil Tablets, USP are supplied as white to off white, round shaped, film-coated tablets containing sildenafil citrate equivalent to the nominally indicated amount of sildenafil as follows:

| Sildenafil Tablets, USP |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Package Configuration | Strength | NDC | Debossing on Tablet |
| Bottle of 90 Tablets | 20 mg | 33342-536-10 | "N7" |
| 10’s Blister Pack | 20 mg | 33342-536-66 | "N7" |
| 6500’s TLP bulk pack | 20 mg | 33342-536-58 | "N7" |

Recommended Storage for Sildenafil Tablets,USP: Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Dispense in tight containers (USP).
