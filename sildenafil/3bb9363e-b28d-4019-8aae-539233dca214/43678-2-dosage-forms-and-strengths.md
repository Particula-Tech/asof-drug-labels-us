# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

REVATIO Tablets

White to off-white, film-coated, round tablets engraved with “RVT 20” on one side and “VLE” on the other side.
Each tablet contains sildenafil citrate equivalent to 20 mg of sildenafil.

REVATIO for Oral Suspension

White to slight pink powder containing 1.57 g of sildenafil citrate (equivalent to 1.12 g sildenafil) in a bottle for reconstitution to 10 mg/mL.
Following reconstitution with 90 mL of water, the total volume of the oral suspension is 112 mL.
A 2 mL oral dosing syringe (with 1 mL and 2 mL dose markings) and a press-in bottle adaptor are also provided.

REVATIO Injection

Single-dose vial containing 10 mg/12.5 mL (0.8 mg/mL) of sildenafil.
