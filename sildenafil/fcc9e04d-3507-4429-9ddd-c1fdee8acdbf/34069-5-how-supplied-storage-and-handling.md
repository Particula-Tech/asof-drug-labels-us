# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Sildenafil Tablets, USP 20 mg are blue colored, round, film-coated tablets, debossed with “CE” on one side and “109” on the other side and are supplied in bottles of 90 tablets.

Bottles of 90 NDC 62135-372-90

Store at

Recommended storage for sildenafil tablets: 20°-25°C (68°-77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
