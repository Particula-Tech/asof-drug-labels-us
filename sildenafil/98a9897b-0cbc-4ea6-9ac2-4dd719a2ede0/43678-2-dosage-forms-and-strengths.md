# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

SILDENAFIL CITRATE TABLETS are supplied as white to off-white, film-coated, rounded-diamond-shaped tablets containing sildenafil citrate equivalent to 25 mg, 50 mg, or 100 mg of sildenafil.
Tablets are debossed on one side with 25, 50 or 100 to indicate the dosage strengths.
