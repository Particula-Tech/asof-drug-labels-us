# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

SILDENAFIL CITRATE TABLETS (sildenafil citrate) are supplied as white to off-white, film-coated, rounded-diamond-shaped tablets containing sildenafil citrate equivalent to the nominally indicated amount of sildenafil and debossed on one side as follows:

|  | 25 mg | 50 mg | 100 mg |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tablet marking | 25 | 50 | 100 |
| Bottle of 30 | NDC-59762-0034-1 | NDC-59762-0035-1 | NDC-59762-0036-1 |
| Bottle of 100 | N/A | NDC-59762-0035-2 | NDC-59762-0036-2 |

Recommended Storage: Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15–30°C (59–86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
