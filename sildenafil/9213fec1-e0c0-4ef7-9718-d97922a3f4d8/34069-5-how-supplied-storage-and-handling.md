# 16HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

VYBRIQUE is supplied as oral films in individually sealed foil pouches in four dosage strengths.
Each carton contains 4 or 8 pouches.
The product is an opaque light blue, thin, flexible oral film with film imprint code, and characteristic lemon and grapefruit flavor with the following dimensions:

| Strengths(mg) of sildenafil | FilmImprint Code | Film Dimensions (shape) | NDC forCarton of4 Pouches | NDC forCarton of8 Pouches |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 25 mg | S25 | 30 mm x 15 mm (rectangular) | 71858-0205-5 | 71858-0205-6 |
| 50 mg | S50 | 30 mm x 30 mm (square) | 71858-0210-5 | 71858-0210-6 |
| 75 mg | S75 | 30 mm x 45 mm (rectangular) | 71858-0215-5 | 71858-0215-6 |
| 100 mg | S100 | 45 mm x 40 mm (rectangular) | 71858-0220-5 | 71858-0220-6 |

Recommended Storage:Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) with excursions permitted between 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
