# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

## 2.1 Recommended Dosage in Adults

The recommended dosage of LIQREV is 20 mg orally three times a day [see Clinical Studies (14).
A calibrated measuring device is recommended to measure and deliver the prescribed dose accurately.
Shake well for at least 10 seconds before use.

Pediatric use information is approved for Viatris Specialty LLC's, REVATIO (sildenafil) tablets.
However, because of Viatris Specialty LLC's marketing exclusivity rights, this drug product is not labeled with that information.
