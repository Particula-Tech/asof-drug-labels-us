# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

QVAR REDIHALER is contraindicated in:

- the primary treatment of status asthmaticus or other acute episodes of asthma where intensive measures are required [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)].

- in patients with known hypersensitivity to beclomethasone dipropionate or any of the ingredients in QVAR REDIHALER [see Warnings and Precautions (5.6)].
