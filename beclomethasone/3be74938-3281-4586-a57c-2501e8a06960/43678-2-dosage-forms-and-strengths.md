# 3. DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

QNASL Nasal Aerosol is a nonaqueous nasal spray solution.

Each actuation of QNASL 40 mcg Nasal Aerosol delivers 40 mcg of beclomethasone dipropionate and each actuation of QNASL 80 mcg Nasal Aerosol delivers 80 mcg of beclomethasone dipropionate.
Each strength is supplied in an 8.7 g canister containing 120 actuations; QNASL 40 mcg Nasal Aerosol is also supplied in a 4.9 g canister containing 60 actuations.
