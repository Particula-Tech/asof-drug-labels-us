# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Inhalation Aerosol: Beclomethasone dipropionate HFA inhalation aerosol is a pressurized, metered-dose aerosol with a dose counter intended for oral inhalation containing beclomethasone dipropionate in the following 2 strengths: Beclomethasone dipropionate HFA inhalation aerosol 40 mcg is supplied in an aluminum canister with a white plastic actuator with a dose counter and a brown dust cap.
Each actuation delivers 50 mcg of beclomethasone dipropionate from the valve and 40 mcg from the actuator.
Beclomethasone dipropionate HFA inhalation aerosol 40 mcg is available as a 120-inhalation/8.7 g canister.
Beclomethasone dipropionate HFA inhalation aerosol 80 mcg is supplied in an aluminum canister with a white plastic actuator with a dose counter and a maroon dust cap.
Each actuation delivers 100 mcg of beclomethasone dipropionate from the valve and 80 mcg from the actuator.
Beclomethasone dipropionate HFA inhalation aerosol 80 mcg is available as a 120-inhalation/8.7 g canister.
