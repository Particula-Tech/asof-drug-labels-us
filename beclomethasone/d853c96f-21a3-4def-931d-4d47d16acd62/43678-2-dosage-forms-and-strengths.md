# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Inhalation Aerosol: Beclomethasone dipropionate HFA inhalation aerosol is a pressurized, metered-dose aerosol with a dose counter intended for oral inhalation containing beclomethasone dipropionate, USP in the following 2 strengths:

Beclomethasone dipropionate HFA inhalation aerosol 40 mcg is supplied in a pressurized aluminum canister with a plastic actuator (beige actuator with light blue dust cap).
Each actuation delivers 50 mcg of beclomethasone dipropionate, USP from the valve and 40 mcg from the actuator.
Beclomethasone dipropionate HFA inhalation aerosol 40 mcg is available as a 120-inhalation/8.7 g canister.

Beclomethasone dipropionate HFA inhalation aerosol 80 mcg is supplied in a pressurized aluminum canister with a plastic actuator (dark pink actuator with gray dust cap).
Each actuation delivers 100 mcg of beclomethasone dipropionate, USP from the valve and 80 mcg from the actuator.
Beclomethasone dipropionate HFA inhalation aerosol 80 mcg is available as a 120-inhalation/8.7 g canister.
