# 11 DESCRIPTION

Zolpidem tartrate extended-release tablet, USP contains zolpidem tartrate, a gamma-aminobutyric acid (GABA) A agonist of the imidazopyridine class.
Zolpidem tartrate extended-release tablets, USP are available in 6.25 mg and 12.5 mg strengths for oral administration.

Chemically, zolpidem is N,N,6-trimethyl-2-p-tolylimidazo[1,2-a] pyridine-3-acetamide L-(+)-tartrate (2:1).

Zolpidem tartrate is a white to off-white crystalline powder that is sparingly soluble in water, alcohol, and propylene glycol.
It has a molecular weight of 764.88.

Zolpidem tartrate extended-release tablets, USP are available for oral administration as a film-coated tablet containing 6.25 mg and 12.5 mg of zolpidem tartrate.
Inactive ingredients consist of colloidal silicon dioxide, hypromellose, hydroxypropyl cellulose, lactose monohydrate, magnesium stearate, polyethylene glycol, red ferric oxide, talc, and titanium dioxide.

USP Dissolution test 4 used.
