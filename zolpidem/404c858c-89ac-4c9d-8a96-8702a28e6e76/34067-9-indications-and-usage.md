# INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Ambien CR (zolpidem tartrate extended-release tablets) is indicated for the treatment of insomnia, characterized by difficulties with sleep onset and/or sleep maintenance (as measured by wake time after sleep onset).
(See Clinical Pharmacology: Controlled trials supporting safety and efficacy.)

The clinical trials performed in support of efficacy were both 3 weeks in duration, although the final formal assessments of sleep latency and maintenance were performed after 2 weeks of treatment.
