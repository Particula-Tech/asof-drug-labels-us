# 3. DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Zolpidem tartrate extended-release tablets are available as extended-release tablets containing 6.25 mg or 12.5 mg of zolpidem tartrate for oral administration.
Tablets are not scored.

Zolpidem tartrate extended-release tablets, 6.25 mg are pink colored, round, biconvex, film-coated tablets debossed with "E61" on one side and "LU" on the other side.

Zolpidem tartrate extended-release tablets, 12.5 mg are blue colored, round, biconvex, film-coated tablets debossed with "E62" on one side and "LU" on the other side.
