# WARNING: COMPLEX SLEEP BEHAVIORS

Complex sleep behaviors including sleep-walking, sleep-driving, and engaging in other activities while not fully awake may occur following use of zolpidem tartrate extended-release tablets.
Some of these events may result in serious injuries, including death.
Discontinue zolpidem tartrate extended-release tablets immediately if a patient experiences a complex sleep behavior [see CONTRAINDICATIONS (4) and WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS (5.1)].
