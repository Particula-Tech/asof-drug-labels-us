# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Zolpidem Tartrate Capsules 7.5 mg are hard gelatin capsules with “ALM” printed axially on the opaque light green cap and “775” printed axially on the opaque white body, and available as:

NDC 52427-775-30, bottle of 30 capsules with child-resistant closure

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
