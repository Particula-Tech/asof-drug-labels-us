# DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

The dose of Ambien should be individualized.

The recommended dose for adults is 10 mg immediately before bedtime.

Downward dosage adjustment may be necessary when Ambien is administered with agents having known CNS-depressant effects because of the potentially additive effects.

Elderly or debilitated patients may be especially sensitive to the effects of Ambien (zolpidem tartrate).
Patients with hepatic insufficiency do not clear the drug as rapidly as normals.
An initial 5-mg dose is recommended in these patients (see Precautions).

The total Ambien dose should not exceed 10 mg.
