# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

INTERMEZZO is contraindicated in patients who have experienced complex sleep behaviors after taking INTERMEZZO [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].

INTERMEZZO is contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity to zolpidem.
Observed reactions with zolpidem include anaphylaxis and angioedema [see Warnings and Precautions (5.4)].
