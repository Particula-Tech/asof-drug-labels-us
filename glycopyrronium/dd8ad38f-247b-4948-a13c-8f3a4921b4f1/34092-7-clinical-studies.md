# 14 CLINICAL STUDIES

The evidence for the efficacy of neostigmine methylsulfate for the reversal of the effects of non­depolarizing neuromuscular blocking agents after surgery is derived from the published literature.
Randomized, spontaneous-recovery or placebo-controlled studies using similar efficacy endpoints evaluated a total of 404 adult and 80 pediatric patients undergoing various surgical procedures.
Patients had reductions in their recovery time from neuromuscular blockade with neostigmine methylsulfate treatment compared to spontaneous recovery.
