# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Glycopyrrolate tablets, USP 1 mg are bisected, compressed white, round tablets debossed “K” above the bisect and “400” below the bisect on one side of the tablet, and plain on the other side.
Available as

Bottles of 100 NDC 49884-065-01

Glycopyrrolate tablets, USP 2 mg are bisected, compressed white, round tablets debossed “K” above the bisect and “401” below the bisect on one side of the tablet, and plain on the other side.
Available as

Bottles of 100 NDC 49884-066-01

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Keep out of reach of children.

Dispense in a tight container.
