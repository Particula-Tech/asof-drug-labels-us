# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

## 2.1 Administration Overview

For oral inhalation only.
Do not swallow SEEBRI capsules, as the intended effects on the lungs will not be obtained.
SEEBRI capsules should only be used with the NEOHALER device [see Overdosage (10)].

## 2.2 Recommended Dosage

The recommended dosage is one capsule of SEEBRI (15.6 mcg glycopyrrolate) inhalation powder twice daily by oral inhalation using the NEOHALER device.
SEEBRI NEOHALER should be administered at the same time of the day, (1 capsule in the morning and 1 capsule in the evening), every day.
More frequent administration or a greater number of inhalations (more than 1 capsule twice -daily) of SEEBRI NEOHALER is not recommended.
