# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Glycopyrrolate injection, USP is a clear, colorless solution without preservative and is available as:

- 0.2 mg/mL single-dose prefilled disposable syringe packaged in 10s (NDC 0641-6212-10)
- 0.4 mg/2 mL (0.2 mg/mL) single-dose prefilled disposable syringe packaged in 10s (NDC 0641-6213-10)

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Sensitive to heat – Do not autoclave.
Discard unused portion.

Do not place syringe on a sterile field.
