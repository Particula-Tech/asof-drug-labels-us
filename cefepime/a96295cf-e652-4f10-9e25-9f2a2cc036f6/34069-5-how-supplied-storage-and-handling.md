# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

How Supplied

MAXIPIME for injection is supplied as follows: MAXIPIME for Injection in the dry state, is a white to pale yellow powder.
Constituted solution of MAXIPIME can range in color from pale yellow to amber.

Storage and Handling

MAXIPIME for injection in the dry state should be stored at 20 to 25° C (68 to 77° F) [see USP controlled room temperature.] and protected from light.
