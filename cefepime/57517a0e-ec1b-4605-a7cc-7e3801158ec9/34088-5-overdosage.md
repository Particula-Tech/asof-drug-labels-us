# 10 OVERDOSAGE

Carefully observe patients who receive an overdose of cefepime, and give supportive treatment as appropriate.
In the presence of renal impairment, hemodialysis, not peritoneal dialysis, is recommended to aid in the removal of cefepime from the body.
Accidental overdosing has occurred when large doses were given to patients with impaired renal function.
Symptoms of overdose include encephalopathy (disturbance of consciousness including confusion, hallucinations, stupor, and coma), myoclonus, seizures, nonconvulsive status epilepticus, and neuromuscular excitability [see Warnings and Precatuions (5.2)].
