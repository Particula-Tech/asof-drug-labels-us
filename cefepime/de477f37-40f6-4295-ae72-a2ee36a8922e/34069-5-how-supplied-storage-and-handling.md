# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

How Supplied

Cefepime for Injection, USP is supplied as follows:

Cefepime for Injection, USP in the dry state, is a white to pale yellow powder.
Constituted solutions of Cefepime for Injection, USP can range in color from pale yellow to amber.

| NDC | Cefepime for Injection, USP | Package Factor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 25021-121-20 | 1 gram* Single-Dose Vial | 10 vials per carton |
| 25021-122-50 | 2 gram* Single-Dose Vial | 10 vials per carton |
| *Based on cefepime activity. |  |  |

Storage and Handling

In the dry state store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15° and 30°C (59° and 86°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

Protect from light.
Retain in carton until time of use.
Discard unused portion.

Sterile, Nonpyrogenic, Preservative-free.
The container closure is not made with natural rubber latex.
