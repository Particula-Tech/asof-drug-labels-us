# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

How Supplied

Cefepime for Injection, USP is supplied as follows:

Cefepime for Injection, USP in the dry state, is a white to pale yellow powder.
Constituted solution of Cefepime for Injection, USP can range in color from pale yellow to amber.

| 320620 | NDC 63323-326-20 Unit of 10 | 1 gram* per vial | NDC 63323-326-21 |
| 324020 | NDC 63323-340-20 Unit of 10 | 2 grams* per vial | NDC 63323-340-21 |

*Based on cefepime activity.

Storage and Handling

Cefepime for Injection, USP in the dry state should be stored at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light.
Retain in carton until time of use.
Discard unused portion.
