# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

RITUXAN (rituximab) injection is a sterile, preservative-free, clear, colorless solution for intravenous infusion supplied as single-dose vials of 100 mg/10 mL (10 mg/mL) in cartons of one vial (NDC 50242-051-21) or cartons of 10 vials (NDC 50242-051-10) and single-dose vials of 500 mg/50 mL (10 mg/mL) in a carton of one vial (NDC 50242-053-06).

Store RITUXAN vials refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F).
RITUXAN vials should be protected from direct sunlight.
Do not freeze or shake.
