# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

RUXIENCE (rituximab-pvvr) injection is a sterile, preservative-free, clear to slightly opalescent, colorless to pale brownish-yellow solution for intravenous infusion supplied as a carton containing one 100 mg/10 mL (10 mg/mL) single-dose vial (NDC 0069-0238-01) or a carton containing one 500 mg/50 mL (10 mg/mL) single-dose vial (NDC 0069-0249-01).

Store RUXIENCE vials refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) in the original carton.
RUXIENCE vials should be protected from direct sunlight.
Do not freeze or shake.
