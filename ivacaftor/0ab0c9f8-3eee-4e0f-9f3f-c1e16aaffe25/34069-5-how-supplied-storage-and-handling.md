# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

KALYDECO™ (ivacaftor) is supplied as light blue, film-coated, capsule-shaped tablets containing 150 mg of ivacaftor.
Each tablet is printed with the characters "V 150" on one side and plain on the other, and is packaged as follows:

|  | 56-count carton (contains 4 individual blister cards of 14 tablets per card) | NDC 51167-200-01 |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  | 60-count bottle | NDC 51167-200-02 |

Store at 20-25ºC (68-77ºF); excursions permitted to 15-30ºC (59-86ºF) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
