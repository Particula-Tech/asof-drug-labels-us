# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Tablets: 150 mg; supplied as light blue, film-coated, capsule-shaped tablets containing 150 mg of ivacaftor.
Each tablet is printed with the characters "V 150" on one side and plain on the other.

Oral granules: Unit-dose packets containing 50 mg or 75 mg per packet; supplied as small, white to off-white granules and enclosed in unit-dose packets.
