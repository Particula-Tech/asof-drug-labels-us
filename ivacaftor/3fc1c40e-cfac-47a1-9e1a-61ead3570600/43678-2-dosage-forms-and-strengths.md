# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Tablets: 100 mg lumacaftor and 125 mg ivacaftor; supplied as pink, oval-shaped, film-coated, fixed-dose combination tablets containing 100 mg of lumacaftor and 125 mg of ivacaftor.
Each tablet is printed with the characters "1V125" in black ink on one side and plain on the other.

Tablets: 200 mg lumacaftor and 125 mg ivacaftor; supplied as pink, oval-shaped, film-coated, fixed-dose combination tablets containing 200 mg of lumacaftor and 125 mg of ivacaftor.
Each tablet is printed with the characters "2V125" in black ink on one side and plain on the other.

Oral granules: Unit-dose packets containing lumacaftor 100 mg/ivacaftor 125 mg or lumacaftor 150 mg/ivacaftor 188 mg per packet; supplied as small, white to off- white granules in unit-dose packets.
