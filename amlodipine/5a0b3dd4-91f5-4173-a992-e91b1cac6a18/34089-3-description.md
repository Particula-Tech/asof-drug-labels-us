# 11 DESCRIPTION

Amlodipine besylate tablets, USP is the besylate salt of amlodipine besylate, a long-acting calcium channel blocker.

Amlodipine besylate is chemically described as 3-Ethyl-5-methyl (±)-2-[(2-aminoethoxy)methyl]-4-(2-chlorophenyl)-1,4-dihydro-6-methyl-3,5-pyridinedicarboxylate, monobenzenesulphonate.
Its molecular formula is C20H25ClN2 O5 ·C6 H6 O3 S, and its structural formula is:

Amlodipine besylate is a white crystalline powder with a molecular weight of 567.1.
It is slightly soluble in water and sparingly soluble in ethanol.
Amlodipine besylate tablets, USP are formulated as white tablets equivalent to 2.5, 5, and 10 mg of amlodipine besylate for oral administration.
In addition to the active ingredient, amlodipine besylate, each tablet contains the following inactive ingredients: lactose monohydrate, magnesium stearate, microcrystalline cellulose, and corn starch.
