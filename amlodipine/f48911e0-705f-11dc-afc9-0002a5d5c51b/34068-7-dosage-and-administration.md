# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

The usual starting dose of Azor is 5/20 mg once daily.
The dosage can be increased after 1 to 2 weeks of therapy to a maximum dose of one 10/40 mg tablet once daily as needed to control blood pressure [see Clinical Studies (14.1)].

Dosage may be increased after 2 weeks.
The maximum recommended dose of Azor is 10/40 mg.
