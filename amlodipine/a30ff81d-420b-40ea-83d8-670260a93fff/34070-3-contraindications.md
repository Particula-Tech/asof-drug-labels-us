# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Do not use in patients with anuria, known hypersensitivity (e.g., anaphylaxis or angioedema) to telmisartan, amlodipine, indapamide, or to other sulfonamide-derived drugs, or to any other component of this product.

Do not co-administer aliskiren with Widaplik in patients with diabetes [see Drug Interactions (7.1)].
