# 11 DESCRIPTION

NORLIQVA is the besylate salt of amlodipine, a long-acting calcium channel blocker.

Amlodipine besylate is chemically described as 3-Ethyl-5-methyl (±)-2-[(2-aminoethoxy)methyl]-4-(2-chlorophenyl)-1,4-dihydro-6-methyl-3,5-pyridinedicarboxylate, monobenzenesulphonate.
Its empirical formula is C20H25CIN2O5•C6H6O3S, and its structural formula is:

Amlodipine besylate is a white crystalline powder with a molecular weight of 567.1.
It is slightly soluble in water and sparingly soluble in ethanol.
Each mL of NORLIQVA (amlodipine) oral solution contains 1 mg of amlodipine provided as 1.385 mg of amlodipine besylate and the following inactive ingredients: butylated hydroxyanisole, ethanol, glycerin, maltitol, and peppermint flavor.
Contains 4% v/v alcohol.
