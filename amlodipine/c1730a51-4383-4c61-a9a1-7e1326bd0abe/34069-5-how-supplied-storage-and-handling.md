# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

NORLIQVA (amlodipine) oral solution, 1 mg/mL is a pale straw colored solution with a peppermint flavor supplied in 150 mL amber glass bottles with a child-resistant closure.

NDC 46287-035-15

Storage

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursion permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Store and dispense in original packaging.
