# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Esmolol hydrochloride injection, Ready-to-use vials are supplied as:

| NDC 68083-211-25 | Esmolol hydrochloride injection 100 mg/10 mL (10 mg/mL) 10 mL single dose vial | Package Factor 25 vials per carton |
| --- | --- | --- |

## 16.2 Storage

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from freezing.
Avoid excessive heat.

Visually inspect the container.
