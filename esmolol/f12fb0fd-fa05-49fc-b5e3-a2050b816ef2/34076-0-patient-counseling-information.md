# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

Physicians should inform patients of the risks associated with Esmolol Hydrochloride in Sodium Chloride Injection:

- The most common adverse reactions are symptomatic hypotension (hyperhidrosis, dizziness) and asymptomatic hypotension.
- Inform patients or caregivers that there is a risk of hypoglycemia when Esmolol Hydrochloride in Sodium Chloride Injection is given to patients who are fasting or who are vomiting.
  Monitor for symptoms of hypoglycemia [see Warnings and Precautions (5.6)].
