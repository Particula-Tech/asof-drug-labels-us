# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Esmolol Hydrochloride Injection Esmolol hydrochloride injection is a clear, colorless to light yellow, sterile, nonpyrogenic solution of esmolol hydrochloride and is supplied as follows: 100 mg per 10 mL (10 mg/mL) 10 mL Single-Dose Vials in a Carton of 25 NDC 55150-194-10

## 16.2 Storage

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from freezing.
Avoid excessive heat.
The vial stopper is not made with natural rubber latex.
