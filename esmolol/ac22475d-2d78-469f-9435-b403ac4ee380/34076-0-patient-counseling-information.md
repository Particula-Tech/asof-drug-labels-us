# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

Physicians should inform patients of the risks associated with esmolol hydrochloride:

- The most common adverse reactions are symptomatic hypotension (hyperhidrosis, dizziness) and asymptomatic hypotension.
- Inform patients or caregivers that there is a risk of hypoglycemia when esmolol is given to patients who are fasting or who are vomiting.
  Monitor for symptoms of hypoglycemia.
  [See Warnings and Precautions (5.6)].
