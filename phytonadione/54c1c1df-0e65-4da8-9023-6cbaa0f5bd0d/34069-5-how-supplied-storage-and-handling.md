# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Phytonadione Tablets USP, 5 mg are supplied as light yellow to yellow colored, round, scored tablets, debossed with “AA” and “05” on either side of scoring and plain on the other side.

They are available as follows:

Bottles of 30: NDC 69238-1051-3

Bottles of 100: NDC 69238-1051-1

Storage

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Always protect phytonadione tablets, USP from light.

Store in tightly closed original container and carton until contents have been used.
