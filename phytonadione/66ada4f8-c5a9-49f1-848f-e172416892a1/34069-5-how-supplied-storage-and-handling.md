# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Phytonadione Tablets USP, 5 mg are supplied as yellow round shaped scored tablets, debossed with “AP” on top and “5” on bottom of the score-line and plain on the other side.

They are supplied as follows:

NDC 72134-008-01 bottles of 100

Storage

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Always protect phytonadione tablets, USP from light.

Store in tightly closed original container and carton until contents have been used.
