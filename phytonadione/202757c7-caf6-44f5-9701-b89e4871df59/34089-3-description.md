# 11 DESCRIPTION

Phytonadione is a vitamin K replacement, which is a clear, yellow to amber, viscous, odorless or nearly odorless liquid.
It is insoluble in water, soluble in chloroform and slightly soluble in ethanol.
It has a molecular weight of 450.70.

AquaMEPHYTON injection is a yellow, sterile, aqueous colloidal solution of vitamin K1, with a pH of 5.0 to 7.0, available for injection by the intravenous, intramuscular, and subcutaneous routes.
AquaMEPHYTON is available in 1 mg (2 mg/mL) and 10 mg (10 mg/mL) single-dose ampuls.
Each milliliter of AquaMEPHYTON contains the following inactive ingredients: 70 mg polyoxyethylated fatty acid derivative, 37.5 mg dextrose, 9 mg benzyl alcohol (preservative), and water for injection.
AquaMEPHYTON may contain glacial acetic acid for pH adjustment to 6.3 (5.0 – 7.0).
