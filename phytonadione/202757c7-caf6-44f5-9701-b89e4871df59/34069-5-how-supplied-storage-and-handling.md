# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

AquaMEPHYTON is a yellow, sterile, aqueous colloidal solution and is supplied in a package of 25 as follows:

| NDC No. | Container | Amount of AquaMEPHYTON® In Container | Volume | Concentration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 52565-092-05 | 1 mL single-dose ampul | 1 mg | 0.5 mL | 2 mg/mL |
| 52565-093-05 | 1 mL single-dose ampul | 10 mg | 1 mL | 10 mg/mL |

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect AquaMEPHYTON from light.
Store container in closed original carton until contents have been used.
