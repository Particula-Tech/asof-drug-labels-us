# 7 DRUG INTERACTIONS

Anticoagulants

Vitamin K1 Injection may induce temporary resistance to prothrombin-depressing anticoagulants, especially when larger doses of Vitamin K1 Injection are used.
Should this occur, higher doses of anticoagulant therapy may be needed when resuming anticoagulant therapy, or a change in therapy to a different class of anticoagulant may be necessary (i.e., heparin sodium).

Vitamin K1 Injection does not affect the anticoagulant action of heparin.
