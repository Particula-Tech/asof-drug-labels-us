# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

Advise the patient to read the FDA-approved patient labeling (Patient Information).
Inform the patient of the following important risks of Phytonadione Injectable Emulsion:

Serious Hypersensitivity Reactions Advise the patient and caregivers to immediately report signs of hypersensitivity after receiving Phytonadione Injectable Emulsion [see WARNING AND PRECAUSTIONS (5.1)].

Cutaneous Reactions

Advise the patient and caregivers to report the occurrence of new rashes after receiving Phytonadione Injectable Emulsion.
These reactions may be delayed for up to a year after treatment [see WARNING AND PRECAUTIONS (5.3)].
