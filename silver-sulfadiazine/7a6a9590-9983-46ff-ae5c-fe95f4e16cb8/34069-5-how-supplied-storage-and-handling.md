# HOW SUPPLIED

Silver Sulfadiazine Cream 1% is available in jars containing 50 g (NDC 59762-0131-6) and 400 g (NDC 59762-0131-4), and tubes containing 20 g (NDC 59762-0131-0), 25 g (NDC 59762-0131-2), 50 g (NDC 59762-0131-5) and 85 g (NDC 59762-0131-8).

Store at Controlled Room Temperature 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
