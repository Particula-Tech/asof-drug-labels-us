# HOW SUPPLIED

Lorazepam Injection, USP is available in the following dosage strengths in single-dose and multiple-dose vials:

2 mg per mL, NDC 0641-6044-25, 25 x 1 mL vial NDC 0641-6046-10, 10 x 10 mL vial

4 mg per mL, NDC 0641-6045-25, 25 x 1 mL vial NDC 0641-6047-10, 10 x 10 mL vial

For IM or IV injection.
Store in a refrigerator.
PROTECT FROM LIGHT.
Use carton to protect contents from light.
