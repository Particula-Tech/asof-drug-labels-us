# WARNING: RISKS FROM CONCOMITANT USE WITH OPIOIDS

- Concomitant use of benzodiazepines and opioids may result in profound sedation, respiratory depression, coma, and death (see WARNINGS, PRECAUTIONS, Clinically Significant Drug Interactions).

1. 1.Reserve concomitant prescribing of these drugs for use in patients for whom alternative treatment options are inadequate.
2. 2.Limit dosages and durations to the minimum required.
3. 3.Follow patients for signs and symptoms of respiratory depression and sedation.
