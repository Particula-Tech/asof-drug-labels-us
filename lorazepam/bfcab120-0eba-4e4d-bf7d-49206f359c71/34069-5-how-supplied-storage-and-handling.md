# HOW SUPPLIED

Lorazepam Tablets are available in the following dosage strengths:

0.5 mg, white, five-sided (shield shape) tablet with a raised "A" on one side and "BPI" and "63" impressed on reverse side.

NDC 68682-303-01 - Bottles of 100

NDC 68682-303-50 – Bottles of 500

NDC 68682-303-10 – Bottles of 1000

1 mg, white, five-sided (shield shape) tablet with a raised "A" on one side and "BPI" and "64" impressed on scored reverse side.

NDC 68682-304-01 - Bottles of 100

NDC 68682-304-50 – Bottles of 500

NDC 68682-304-10 - Bottles of 1000

2 mg, white, five-sided (rectangular pentagon) tablet with a raised "A" and impressed "2" on one side and "BPI" and "65" impressed on scored reverse side.

NDC 68682-305-01 - Bottles of 100

NDC 68682-305-50 – Bottles of 500

NDC 68682-305-10 – Bottles of 1000

BOTTLES:

Keep tightly closed.

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15°-30°C (59° – 86°F)

[see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Dispense in a tight container.
