# 13 NONCLINICAL TOXICOLOGY

## 13.1 Carcinogenesis, Mutagenesis, Impairment of Fertility

Carcinogenesis

No evidence of carcinogenic potential emerged in rats during an 18-month study with lorazepam.

Mutagenesis

No studies regarding mutagenesis have been performed.

Impairment of Fertility

Animal fertility studies have not been conducted with LOREEV XR.

## 13.2 Animal Toxicology and/or Pharmacology

Esophageal dilation occurred in rats treated with lorazepam for more than one year at 6 mg/kg/day.
The no-effect dose was 1.25 mg/kg/day (approximately 1.2 times the maximum recommended human dose of 10 mg/day based on mg/m2 body surface area).
The effect was reversible only when the treatment was withdrawn within two months of first observation of the phenomenon.
The clinical significance of this is unknown.
