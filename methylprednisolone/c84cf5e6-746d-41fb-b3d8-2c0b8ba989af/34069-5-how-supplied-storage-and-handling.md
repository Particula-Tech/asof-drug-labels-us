# HOW SUPPLIED

Methylprednisolone tablets, USP are available in the following strengths and package sizes:

4 mg (white, flat faced, oval shaped with a beveled edge.
The upper layer is quadrisected and debossed “42/16/V” with the “42/16” above the lengthwise bisect and the “V” in the lower righthand corner.
The lower layer is debossed “4”)

- Bottles of 100 NDC 0603-4593-21
- Unit of Use (21 tablets) NDC 0603-4593-15

Dispense in a tight light-resistant container as defined in the USP.

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
