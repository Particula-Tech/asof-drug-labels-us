# HOW SUPPLIED

BiDil Tablets contain 20 mg of isosorbide dinitrate plus 37.5 mg of hydralazine hydrochloride.
They are biconvex, approximately 8 mm in diameter, scored, film-coated, orange tablets debossed “20” on one side over the score and “N” on the other side.

NDC 12948-001-12 bottle of 180.

Keep bottles tightly closed.

Store at 25ºC (77ºF), excursions permitted to 15-30ºC (59-86ºF).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

Protect from light.
Dispense in a light-resistant, tight container.
