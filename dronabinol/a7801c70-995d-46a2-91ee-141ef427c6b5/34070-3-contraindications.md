# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

SYNDROS is contraindicated in patients:

- with a history of a hypersensitivity reaction to dronabinol.
  Reported hypersensitivity reactions to dronabinol include lip swelling, hives, disseminated rash, oral lesions, skin burning, flushing, throat tightness [see Adverse Reactions (6.2)].
- with a history of a hypersensitivity reaction to alcohol.
- who are receiving, or have recently received, disulfiram- or metronidazole-containing products within 14 days [see Warning and Precautions (5.3)].
  SYNDROS contains 50% (w/w) dehydrated alcohol and 5.5% (w/w) propylene glycol.
