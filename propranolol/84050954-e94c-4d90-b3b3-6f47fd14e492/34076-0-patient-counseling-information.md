# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

Patient Advice

Advise patients:

- Do not interrupt or discontinue using INDERAL XL without a physician’s advice.
- Patients with heart failure should consult their physician if they experience signs or symptoms of worsening heart failure such as weight gain or increasing shortness of breath.
- Inform diabetic patients that certain signs of hypoglycemia may be masked.
  Patients should report any changes in blood sugar levels to their physician.
