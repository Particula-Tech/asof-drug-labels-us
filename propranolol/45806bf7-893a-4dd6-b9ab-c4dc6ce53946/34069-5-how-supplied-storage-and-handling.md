# HOW SUPPLIED

INNOPRAN XL (propranolol hydrochloride) Extended Release Capsules

Each gray/white capsule, imprinted with “80”, 2 segmented bands, “InnoPran XL”, and the Reliant logo, contains 80 mg of propranolol hydrochloride in bottles of 30 (NDC 0173-0790-01), bottles of 100 (NDC 0173-0790-02).

Each gray/off-white capsule, imprinted with “120”, 3 segmented bands, “InnoPran XL”, and the Reliant logo, contains 120 mg of propranolol hydrochloride in bottles of 30 (NDC 0173-0791-01), bottles of 100 (NDC 0173-0791-02).

Store at 25ºC (77ºF); excursions permitted to 15 and 30ºC (59 and 86ºF) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature] in a tightly closed container.
