# 14 CLINICAL STUDIES

## 14.1 Hypertension

In a double-blind, parallel dose-response study in patients with mild-to-moderate hypertension (n=434), doses of INDERAL XL from 80 to 640 mg were taken once daily at approximately 10 p.m.
INDERAL XL significantly lowered sitting systolic and diastolic blood pressure when measurements were taken approximately 16 hours later.
The placebo-subtracted diastolic blood pressure effect for the 80 and 120 mg doses was -3.0 and -4.0 mm Hg, respectively.
Higher doses of INDERAL XL (160, 640 mg) had no additional blood-pressure lowering effect when compared with 120 mg.
The antihypertensive effects of INDERAL XL were seen in the elderly (≥65 years old) and men and women.
There were too few non-white patients to assess the efficacy of INDERAL XL in these patients.
