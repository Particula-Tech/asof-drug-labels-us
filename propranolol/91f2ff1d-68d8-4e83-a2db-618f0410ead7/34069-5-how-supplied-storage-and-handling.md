# HOW SUPPLIED

Each mL contains 1 mg of propranolol hydrochloride in Water for Injection.
The pH is adjusted with citric acid.
Supplied as: 1 mL vials in boxes of 10 (NDC 00143-9872-01).

Store at controlled room temperature 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
Protect from freezing or excessive heat.
