# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

INNOPRAN XL Extended Release Capsules are supplied as capsules containing either 80 mg (equivalent to 70.14 mg of propranolol) or 120 mg (equivalent to 105.21 mg of propranolol) of propranolol hydrochloride imprinted with “InnoPran XL”.
In addition, the 80 mg strength is a gray/white capsule imprinted with “80” and 2 segmented bands, while the 120 mg strength is a gray/off-white capsule imprinted with “120” and 3 segmented bands.
