# HOW SUPPLIED

Ondansetron Oral Solution, USP, a clear, colorless liquid with a characteristic strawberry odor, contains 5 mg of ondansetron HCI dihydrate equivalent to 4 mg of ondansetron per 5 mL in amber glass bottles of 50 mL with child-resistant closures (NDC 0054-0064-47).

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]
Protect from light.
Store bottles upright in cartons.
