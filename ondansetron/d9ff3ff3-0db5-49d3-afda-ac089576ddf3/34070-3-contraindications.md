# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

The concomitant use of apomorphine with ondansetron is contraindicated based on reports of profound hypotension and loss of consciousness when apomorphine was administered with ondansetron.

ZUPLENZ (ondansetron) oral soluble film is contraindicated for patients known to have hypersensitivity to the drug.
Anaphylactic reactions have been reported in patients taking ondansetron.
