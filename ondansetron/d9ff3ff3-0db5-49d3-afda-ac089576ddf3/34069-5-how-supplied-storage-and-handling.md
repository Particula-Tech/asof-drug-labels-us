# 16 HOW SUPPLIED / STORAGE AND HANDLING

ZUPLENZ (ondansetron) oral soluble film 4 mg and ZUPLENZ (ondansetron) oral soluble film 8 mg, are supplied as thin rectangular white opaque films in individual foil-foil sealed child resistant pouches.
Individual films are identified by “4 mg” or “8 mg”, according to the respective strengths, which is printed using pharmaceutical grade edible ink.

Individual pouches of ZUPLENZ 4 mg oral soluble film (NDC 43288-104-01) are packaged in boxes of 10 (NDC 43288-104-10).

Individual pouches of ZUPLENZ 8 mg oral soluble film (NDC 43288-108-01) are packaged in boxes of 10 (NDC 43288-108-10).

Store at controlled room temperature 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
Store pouches in cartons.
Keep product in pouch until ready to use.
