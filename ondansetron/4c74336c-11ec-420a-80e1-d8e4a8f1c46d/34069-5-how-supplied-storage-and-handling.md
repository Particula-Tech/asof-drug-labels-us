# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Ondansetron Orally Disintegrating Tablets, USP

4 mg (as 4 mg ondansetron base) are white, round, flat faced tablets debossed with " K1" on one side and plain on other side.

These are packaged as follows:

Bottles of 30 tablets NDC 62135-121-30

8 mg (as 8 mg ondansetron base) are white, round, flat faced tablets debossed with " K2" on one side and plain on other side

These are packaged as follows:

Bottles of 30 tablets NDC 62135-122-30

16 mg (as 16 mg ondansetron base) are white, round flat face radius edge scored tablets, debossed with " CE" above " 231" on one side and bisect on the other side.

These are packaged as follows:

Bottles of 30 tablets NDC 62135-878-30

24 mg (as 24 mg ondansetron base) are white, round flat face radius edged tablets, debossed with " CE" above " 232" on one side and plain on the other side.

These are packaged as follows:

Bottles of 30 tablets NDC 62135-879-30

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
