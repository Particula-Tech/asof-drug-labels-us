# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Ondansetron Orally Disintegrating Tablets, USP, are white, round, flat faced tablets available in the following strengths:

- 4 mg – debossed with " K1" on one side.
- 8 mg – debossed with " K2" on one side.
- 16 mg – radius edge scored tablets, debossed with " CE" above " 231" on one side and bisect on the other side.
- 24 mg - radius edged tablets, debossed with " CE" above " 232" on one side and plain on the other side.
