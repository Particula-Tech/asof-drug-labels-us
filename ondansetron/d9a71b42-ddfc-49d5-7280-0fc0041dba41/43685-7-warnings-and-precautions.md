# 5 WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS

## 5.1 Hypersensitivity Reactions

Hypersensitivity reactions, including anaphylaxis and bronchospasm, have been reported in patients who have exhibited hypersensitivity to other selective 5-HT3 receptor antagonists.

## 5.2 Electrocardiographic Changes

Rarely and predominantly with intravenous ondansetron, transient ECG changes including QT/QTc interval prolongation have been reported.

## 5.3 Masking of Progressive Ileus and Gastric Distension

The use of ZOFRAN in patients following abdominal surgery or in patients with chemotherapy-induced nausea and vomiting may mask a progressive ileus and gastric distention.

## 5.4 Effect on Peristalsis

ZOFRAN is not a drug that stimulates gastric or intestinal peristalsis.
It should not be used instead of nasogastric suction.
