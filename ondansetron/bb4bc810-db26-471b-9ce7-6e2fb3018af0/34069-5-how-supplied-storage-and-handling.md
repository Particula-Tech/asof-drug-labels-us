# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Ondansetron Injection, USP 2 mg/mL, is supplied as follows:

NDC 0143-9891-25 2 mL single dose vials (Carton of 25) NDC 0143-9890-01 20 mL multi dose vial (Carton of 1)

Storage: Store vials between 20° to 25ºC (68° to 77°F) [see USP controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light.
