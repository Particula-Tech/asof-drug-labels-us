# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Ondansetron Injection is contraindicated for patients known to have hypersensitivity (e.g., anaphylaxis) to this product or any of its components.
Anaphylactic reactions have been reported in patients taking ondansetron [see Adverse Reactions (6.2)].

The concomitant use of apomorphine with ondansetron is contraindicated based on reports of profound hypotension and loss of consciousness when apomorphine was administered with ondansetron.
