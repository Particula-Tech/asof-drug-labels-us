# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

How Supplied

Pemetrexed for Injection USP is a white or off-white lyophilized powder supplied in single-dose vials for reconstitution for intravenous infusion.

| NDC | Pemetrexed for Injection USP | Package Factor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 71288-166-10 | 100 mg Single-Dose Vial | 1 vial per carton |
| 71288-167-50 | 500 mg Single-Dose Vial | 1 vial per carton |

Storage and Handling

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Pemetrexed for Injection USP is a cytotoxic drug.
Follow applicable special handling and disposal procedures.
1

Sterile, Nonpyrogenic, Preservative-free.

The container closure is not made with natural rubber latex.
