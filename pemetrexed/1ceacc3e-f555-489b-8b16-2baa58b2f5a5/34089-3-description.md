# 11 DESCRIPTION

Pemetrexed is a folate analog metabolic inhibitor.
The drug substance, pemetrexed disodium hemipentahydrate, has the chemical name: N-[4-[2-(2-amino-4,7-dihydro-4-oxo- 1H-pyrrolo[2,3-d]pyrimidin-5-yl)ethyl]benzoyl]-L-glutamic acid disodium salt, hemipentahydrate with a molecular formula of C20H19O6N5Na2•2.5H2O and a molecular weight of 516.41.

Pemetrexed for Injection, USP is a sterile white or off-white lyophilized powder in single-dose vials to be reconstituted for intravenous infusion.
Each 100-mg vial of Pemetrexed for Injection, USP contains 100 mg pemetrexed (equivalent to 120.8 mg pemetrexed disodium hemipentahydrate) and 106 mg mannitol.
Each 500-mg vial of Pemetrexed for Injection, USP contains 500 mg pemetrexed (equivalent to 604.1 mg pemetrexed disodium hemipentahydrate) and 500 mg mannitol.
Hydrochloric acid and/or sodium hydroxide may have been added to adjust pH.
