## Storage

ALIMTA, pemetrexed for injection, should be stored at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15–30°C (59–86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Chemical and physical stability of reconstituted and infusion solutions of ALIMTA were demonstrated for up to 24 hours following initial reconstitution, when stored refrigerated, 2–8°C (36–46°F), or at 25°C (77°F), excursions permitted to 15–30°C (59–86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
When prepared as directed, reconstituted and infusion solutions of ALIMTA contain no antimicrobial preservatives.
Discard unused portion.

ALIMTA is not light sensitive.
