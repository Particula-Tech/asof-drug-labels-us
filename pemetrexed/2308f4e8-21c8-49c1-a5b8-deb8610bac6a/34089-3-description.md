# 11 DESCRIPTION

Pemetrexed is a folate analog metabolic inhibitor.
Pemetrexed diacid, the drug substance, has the chemical name N-[4-[2-(2-amino-4,7-dihydro-4-oxo-1H-pyrrolo[2,3-d]pyrimidin-5-yl)ethyl]benzoyl]-L-glumatic acid.
The molecular formula is C20H21N5O6 and the molecular weight is 427.41.

PEMFEXY (pemetrexed injection) for intravenous use is a sterile, clear, colorless to yellow or green-yellow solution.
Each mL contains: 25 mg pemetrexed diacid, 260 mg propylene glycol, up to 16.5-19.9 mg tromethamine, and water for injection.
Additional tromethamine not exceeding 19.9 mg/mL and/or hydrochloric acid may be added for pH adjustment.
