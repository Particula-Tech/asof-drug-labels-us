# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

How Supplied

Pemetrexed for Injection USP, is a white to either light-yellow or green-yellow lyophilized powder supplied in single-dose vials with ivory flip-off cap individually packaged in a carton for reconstitution for intravenous infusion.

NDC 43598-386-62: Carton containing one (1) single-dose vial of 100 mg pemetrexed.

NDC 43598-387-11: Carton containing one (1) single-dose vial of 500 mg pemetrexed.

NDC 43598-370-74: Carton containing one (1) single-dose vial of 1g pemetrexed.

Storage and Handling

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Pemetrexed for Injection, USP is a hazardous drug.
Follow applicable special handling and disposal procedures.1
