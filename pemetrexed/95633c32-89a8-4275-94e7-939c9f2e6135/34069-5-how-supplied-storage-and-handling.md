# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

How Supplied

Pemetrexed for Injection USP, is a white-to-light yellow or green-yellow lyophilized powder supplied in single-dose vials for reconstitution for intravenous infusion.

NDC 72338-100-01: Carton containing one (1) single-dose vial of 100 mg pemetrexed.

NDC 72338-101-01: Carton containing one (1) single-dose vial of 500 mg pemetrexed.

Storage and Handling

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15-30°C (59-86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Discard unused portion.

Pemetrexed for Injection USP is a cytotoxic drug.
Follow applicable special handling and disposal procedures. ﻿1
