# HOW SUPPLIED

VASERETIC Tablets 10-25 mg, are rust, oval shaped tablets, with one side "VASE 10-25" and scored and the other side scored.
Each tablet contains 10 mg of enalapril maleate and 25 mg of hydrochlorothiazide.
They are supplied as follows:

NDC 64455-146-01 bottles of 100 (with desiccant).

Storage: Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15-30°C (59-86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Keep container tightly closed.

Protect from moisture.

Dispense in a tight container as per USP, if product package is subdivided.
