# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

EPANED is supplied as a kit.

NDC 52652-1001-1

- One bottle containing 150 mg of EPANED (enalapril) Powder for Oral Solution in an HDPE bottle with child-resistant cap to provide 1 mg/mL final concentration after reconstitution.
- One bottle containing 150 mL of Ora-Sweet SF provided as the diluent for reconstitution.

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15-30°C (59-86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Do not freeze.

Keep container tightly closed.

Protect from moisture.
