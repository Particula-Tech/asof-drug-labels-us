# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

The dose is one drop of dorzolamide hydrochloride ophthalmic solution in the affected eye(s) three times daily.
Dorzolamide hydrochloride ophthalmic solution may be used concomitantly with other topical ophthalmic drug products to lo wer intraocular pressure.
If more than one topical ophthalmic drug is being used, the drugs should be ad ministered at least five minutes apart.
