# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

COSOPT PF is supplied in a foil pouch containing 15 low density polyethylene 0.2 mL single-use containers.

NDC 0006-3629-60, package of 60 single-use vials.

Store COSOPT PF at 20-25°C (68-77°F).
Do not freeze.

Store in the original pouch.
After the pouch is opened, store the remaining single-use containers in the foil pouch to protect from light.
Write down the date you open the foil pouch in the space provided on the pouch.
Discard any unused containers 15 days after first opening the pouch.
