# CONTRAINDICATIONS

COSOPT is contraindicated in patients with (1) reactive airway disease; (2) bronchial asthma; (3) a history of bronchial asthma; (4) severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (see WARNINGS); (5) sinus bradycardia; (6) sino-atrial block; (7) second or third degree atrioventricular block; (8) overt cardiac failure (see WARNINGS); (9) cardiogenic shock; or (10) hypersensitivity to any component of this product.
