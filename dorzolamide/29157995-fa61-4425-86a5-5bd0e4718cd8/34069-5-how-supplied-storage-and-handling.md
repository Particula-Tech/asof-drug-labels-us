# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Dorzolamide hydrochloride ophthalmic solution is a slightly opalescent, nearly colorless, slightly viscous solution.

Dorzolamide hydrochloride ophthalmic solution is supplied in a white LDPE plastic bottle, a natural dropper tip and an orange polypropylene cap as follows:

NDC 61314-019-10 10 mL, in a 10 mL bottle

Storage

Store dorzolamide hydrochloride ophthalmic solution at 2° to 25°C (36° to 77°F).
Protect from light.
After opening, dorzolamide hydrochloride ophthalmic solution can be used until the expiration date on the bottle.
