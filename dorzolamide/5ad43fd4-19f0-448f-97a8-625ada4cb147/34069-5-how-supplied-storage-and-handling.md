# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Dorzolamide hydrochloride ophthalmic solution USP, 2% is supplied in sterile white low density polyethylene round screw neck bottle with high density polyethylene orange screw cap.

10 mL in a 10 mL bottle NDC 62332-519-10

Storage

Store dorzolamide hydrochloride ophthalmic solution at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F).
Protect from light.
After opening, dorzolamide hydrochloride ophthalmic solution can be used until the expiration date on the bottle.
