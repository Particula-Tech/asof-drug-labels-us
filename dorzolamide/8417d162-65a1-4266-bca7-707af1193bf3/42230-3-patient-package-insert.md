# INSTRUCTIONS FOR USE

Dorzolamide Hydrochloride (HCl) Ophthalmic Solution 2%

Sterile

1. 1.Wash hands before each use.
2. 2.To open the bottle, remove the shrink neck band and unscrew the cap by turning counterclockwise.

1. 3.Tilt your head back and pull your lower eyelid down slightly to form a pocket between your eyelid and your eye.

1. 4.Invert the bottle, and instill drops as directed by your doctor.

1. 5.If your doctor has told you to use Dorzolamide HCl Ophthalmic Solution drops in both eyes, repeat Steps 3 and 4.
2. 6.Replace cap on bottle and turn until closed.

How should I store Dorzolamide HCl Ophthalmic Solution?

- •Store Dorzolamide HCl Ophthalmic Solution at 68° to 77°F (20° to 25°C).
- •Protect from light.
- •Safely throw away medicine that is out of date or no longer needed.

KEEP DORZOLAMIDE HCl OPHTHALMIC SOLUTION AND ALL MEDICINES OUT OF THE REACH OF CHILDREN.

Important information about using Dorzolamide HCl Ophthalmic Solution

- •If you have any eye or skin reactions, especially conjunctivitis or eyelid reactions to Dorzolamide HCl Ophthalmic Solution, stop using it and call your doctor right away.
- •If you have eye surgery or have a problem such as trauma or infection of your eye while using Dorzolamide HCl Ophthalmic Solution, call your doctor right away.
- •If you do not handle eye medicines the right way the medicine can become contaminated.
  If the tip of the dispenser touches your eye or areas around your eye, the tip can become contaminated with bacteria which can cause an eye infection and other serious problems including loss of eyesight.
- •If you use other eye medicines dropped onto the eye like Dorzolamide HCl Ophthalmic Solution, use the medicines at least 5 minutes before or after you use Dorzolamide HCl Ophthalmic Solution.
- •Dorzolamide HCl Ophthalmic Solution contains benzalkonium chloride which may be absorbed by soft contact lenses.
  If you wear contact lenses, remove them before you use your Dorzolamide HCl Ophthalmic Solution.
  You can place your contact lenses back into your eyes 15 minutes after using your Dorzolamide HCl Ophthalmic Solution.
