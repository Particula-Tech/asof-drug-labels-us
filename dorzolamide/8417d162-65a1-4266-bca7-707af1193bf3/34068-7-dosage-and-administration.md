# DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

The dose is one drop of Dorzolamide HCl Ophthalmic Solution in the affected eye(s) three times daily.

Dorzolamide HCl Ophthalmic Solution may be used concomitantly with other topical ophthalmic drug products to lower intraocular pressure.
If more than one topical ophthalmic drug is being used, the drugs should be administered at least ten minutes apart.
