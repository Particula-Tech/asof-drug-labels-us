# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Dorzolamide HCl Ophthalmic Solution is supplied sterile in a white low density polyethylene (LDPE) bottle with a controlled drop tip and an orange polypropylene cap in the following sizes:

NDC 24208-485-05, 5 mL, in a 7.5 mL bottle.

NDC 24208-485-10, 10 mL, in a 10 mL bottle.

Storage

Store Dorzolamide HCl Ophthalmic Solution at 20-25°C (68-77°F).
Protect from light.
