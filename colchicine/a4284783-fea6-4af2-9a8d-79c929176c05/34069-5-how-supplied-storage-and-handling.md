# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Colchicine tablets, USP 0.6 mg are purple colored, capsule shaped, film, coated tablets debossed with “C6” on one side and scored on other side.

Bottles of 30 NDC 67877-589-30

Bottles of 60 NDC 67877-589-60

Bottles of 100 NDC 67877-589-01

Bottles of 250 NDC 67877-589-86

Bottles of 500 NDC 67877-589-05

Bottles of 1000 NDC 67877-589-10

Carton of 10 [10 (1 x 10 Unit-dose) Tablets] NDC 67877-589-33 Carton of 30 [30 (1 x 10 Unit-dose) Tablets] NDC 67877-589-84

## 16.2 Storage

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light.
DISPENSE IN TIGHT, LIGHT-RESISTANT CONTAINER.
