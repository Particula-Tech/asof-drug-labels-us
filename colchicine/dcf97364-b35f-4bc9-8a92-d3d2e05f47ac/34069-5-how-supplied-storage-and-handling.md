# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Colchicine tablets, USP 0.6 mg are purple, film-coated, capsule-shaped tablets debossed with "G 02" on one side and scored on the other side.
Bottles of 30 NDC 70010-002-03 Bottles of 100 NDC 70010-002-01

Bottles of 1000 NDC 70010-002-10

## 16.2 Storage

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light.

DISPENSE IN TIGHT, LIGHT-RESISTANT CONTAINER.
