# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

GLOPERBA (colchicine) Oral Solution is a slightly hazy, red liquid with a cherry odor and the strength of 0.6 mg/5 mL.
GLOPERBA (150 mL) is provided in a white, oblong, high density polyethylene bottle with a child-resistant cap.

150 mL: NDC 69557-222-01

## 16.2 Storage

Store at 20°-25°C (68° - 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°-30°C (59°-86°F).
