# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Patients with renal or hepatic impairment should not be given GLOPERBA in conjunction with drugs that inhibit both CYP3A4 and P-gp [see Drug Interactions (7)].
Combining these dual inhibitors with colchicine in patients with renal or hepatic impairment has resulted in life threatening or fatal colchicine toxicity.

Patients with both renal and hepatic impairment should not be given GLOPERBA.
