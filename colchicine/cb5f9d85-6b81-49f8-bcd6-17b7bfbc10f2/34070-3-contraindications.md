# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Patients with renal or hepatic impairment should not be given MITIGAREwith drugs that inhibit both P-glycoprotein and CYP3A4 inhibitors [see Drug Interactions (7)].
Combining these dual inhibitors with colchicine in patients with renal or hepatic impairment has resulted in life-threatening or fatal colchicine toxicity.

Patients with both renal and hepatic impairment should not be given MITIGARE.
