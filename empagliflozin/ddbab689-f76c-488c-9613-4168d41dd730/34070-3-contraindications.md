# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

GLYXAMBI is contraindicated in patients with:

- Severe renal impairment, end-stage renal disease, or dialysis [see Use in Specific Populations (8.6)].
- A history of hypersensitivity reaction to linagliptin, such as anaphylaxis, angioedema, exfoliative skin conditions, urticaria, or bronchial hyperreactivity [see Warnings and Precautions (5.9) and Adverse Reactions (6)].
- History of serious hypersensitivity reaction to empagliflozin.
