# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Diovan (valsartan) is available as tablets containing valsartan 40 mg, 80 mg, 160 mg, or 320 mg.
All strengths are packaged in bottles as described below.

The 40 mg tablets are scored on one side and ovaloid with beveled edges.
The 80 mg, 160 mg, and 320 mg tablets are unscored and almond-shaped with beveled-edges.

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F and 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Protect from moisture.

Dispense in tight container (USP).
