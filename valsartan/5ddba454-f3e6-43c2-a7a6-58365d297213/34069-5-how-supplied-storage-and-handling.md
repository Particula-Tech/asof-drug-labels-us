# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Diovan (valsartan) is available as tablets containing valsartan 40 mg, 80 mg, 160 mg, or 320 mg.
All strengths are packaged in bottles as described below.

The 40 mg tablets are scored on one side and ovaloid with bevelled edges.
80 mg, 160 mg, and 320 mg tablets are unscored and almond-shaped with bevelled-edges.

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C-30°C (59°F-86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Protect from moisture.

Dispense in tight container (USP).
