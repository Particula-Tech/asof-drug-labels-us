# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Valsartan tablets USP are available as:

- 40 mg tablets are yellow colored, capsule shaped, biconvex, film coated tablets, with a scoreline on one side, debossed with 'C' on one side of scoreline and 'I' on the other side of scoreline and plain on the other side.
- 80 mg tablets are peach colored, round, biconvex, film coated tablets, debossed with 'C3' on one side and 'C' on other side.
- 160 mg tablets are yellow colored, oval shaped, biconvex, film coated tablets, debossed with 'C4' on one side and 'C' on other side.
- 320 mg tablets are yellow colored, oval shaped, biconvex, film coated tablets, debossed with 'C5' on one side and 'C' on other side.
