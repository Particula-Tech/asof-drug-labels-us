# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Valsartan Tablets USP are available as: 40 mg tablets are yellow, capsule shaped, biconvex, film-coated with a functional scoreline on one side, debossed with 'C' on one side of scoreline and 'I' on other side of scoreline and plain on the other side.
80 mg tablets are peach, round, biconvex, film-coated, debossed with 'C3'on one side and 'C' on other side.
160 mg tablets are yellow, oval shaped, biconvex, film-coated, debossed with 'C4' on one side and 'C' on the other side.
320 mg tablets are yellow, oval shaped, biconvex, film-coated, debossed with 'C5' on one side and 'C' on the other side.
