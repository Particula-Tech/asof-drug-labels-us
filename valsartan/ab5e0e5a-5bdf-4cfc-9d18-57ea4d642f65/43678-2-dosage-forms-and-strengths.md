# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

40 mg are yellow colored, film-coated, oval-shaped tablets debossed with ' RX121' on one side and break line on the other side.

80 mg are yellowish brown colored, film-coated, oval-shaped tablets debossed with ' RX124' on one side and plain on the other side.

160 mg are pink colored, film-coated, oval-shaped tablets debossed with ' RX125' on one side and plain on the other side.

320 mg are brown colored, film-coated, oval-shaped tablets debossed with ' RX126' on one side and plain on the other side.
