# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Diovan (valsartan) is available as capsules containing valsartan 80 mg or 160 mg.
All strengths are packaged in bottles and unit dose blister packages (10 strips of 10 capsules) as described below.

| Capsule | Color | Imprint | NDC |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  | Light pink and |  | Bottle | Blister |
| 80 mg | Light grey | CG FZF | 0083-4000-01 | 0083-4000-61 |
| 160 mg | Dark grey | CG GOG | 0083-4001-01 | 0083-4001-61 |

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15-30°C (59 - 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Protect from moisture.

Dispense in tight container (USP).
