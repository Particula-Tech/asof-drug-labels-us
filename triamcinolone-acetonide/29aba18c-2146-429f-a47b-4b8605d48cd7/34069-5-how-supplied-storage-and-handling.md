STORAGE

To maintain expiry period, refrigerate the ZILRETTA single-dose kit (36°-46°F; 2°-8°C) before use.

If refrigeration is unavailable, store the ZILRETTA single-dose kit in the sealed, unopened kit at temperatures not exceeding 77°F (25°C) for up to six weeks and then discard.
Do not expose the ZILRETTA single-dose kit to temperatures above 77°F (25°C).

Do not freeze.
Store vials in carton.
