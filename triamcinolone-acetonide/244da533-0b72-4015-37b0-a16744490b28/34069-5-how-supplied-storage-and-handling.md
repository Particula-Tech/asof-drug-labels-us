# HOW SUPPLIED

Azmacort Inhalation Aerosol contains 60 mg triamcinolone acetonide in a 20 gram package which delivers at least 240 actuations.
It is supplied with a white plastic actuator, a white plastic spacer-mouthpiece and patient's leaflet of instructions: box of one.
NDC 0074–3014–60.
Each actuation delivers 200 mcg triamcinolone acetonide from the valve and 75 mcg from the spacer-mouthpiece under defined in vitro test conditions.

Avoid spraying in eyes.

For best results, the canister should be at room temperature before use.

Shake well before using.

CONTENTS UNDER PRESSURE.
Do not puncture.
Do not use or store near heat or open flame.
Exposure to temperatures above 120°F may cause bursting.
Never throw canister into fire or incinerator.
Keep out of reach of children unless otherwise prescribed.
Store at Controlled Room Temperature 20 to 25°C (68 to 77°F) [see USP].
