# HOW SUPPLIED

KENALOG®-10 Injection (triamcinolone acetonide injectable suspension, USP) is supplied in 5 mL multiple-dose vials (NDC 0003-0494-20) providing 10 mg triamcinolone acetonide per mL.

## Storage

Store at controlled room temperature, 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); protect from temperatures below 20°C (68°F).
Store vial in carton to protect from light.
Store vial upright.
