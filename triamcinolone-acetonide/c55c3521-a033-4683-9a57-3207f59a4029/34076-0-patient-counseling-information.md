# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

Corticosteroid-Related Effects

Advise patients that they may develop elevated intraocular pressure following treatment, which may need to be managed with medication or surgery.

When to Seek Physician Advice

Advise patients that if the eye becomes red, sensitive to light, painful, or develops a change in vision, they should seek immediate care from an ophthalmologist.
