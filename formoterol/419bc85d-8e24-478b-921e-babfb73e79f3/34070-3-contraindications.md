# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Use of a long-acting beta2-adrenergic agonists (LABA), including formoterol fumarate inhalation solution, without an inhaled corticosteroid is contraindicated in patients with asthma [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
Formoterol fumarate inhalation solution is not indicated for the treatment of asthma.
