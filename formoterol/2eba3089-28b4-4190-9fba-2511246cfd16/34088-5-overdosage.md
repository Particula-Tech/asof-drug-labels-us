# 10 OVERDOSAGE

DUAKLIR PRESSAIR contains both aclidinium and formoterol fumarate; therefore, the risks associated with overdosage for the individual components described below apply to DUAKLIR PRESSAIR.
The most common symptoms are blurred vision, dry mouth, nausea, muscle spasms, tremor, headache, palpitations, and systolic hypertension.

Treatment of overdosage consists of discontinuation of DUAKLIR PRESSAIR together with institution of appropriate symptomatic and/or supportive therapy.
