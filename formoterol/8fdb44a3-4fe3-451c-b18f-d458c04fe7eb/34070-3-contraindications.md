# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Use of a long-acting beta2-adrenergic agonist (LABA), including formoterol fumarate, one of the active ingredients in DUAKLIR PRESSAIR, without an inhaled corticosteroid is contraindicated in patients with asthma [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
DUAKLIR PRESSAIR is not indicated for the treatment of asthma.

DUAKLIR PRESSAIR is contraindicated in patients with:

- •Severe hypersensitivity to milk proteins [see Warnings and Precautions (5.5)].
- •Hypersensitivity to aclidinium bromide, formoterol fumarate, or to any component of the product [see Warnings and Precautions (5.5)].
