# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Inhalation Aerosol: BEVESPI AEROSPHERE is a pressurized metered dose inhaler that delivers 9 mcg of glycopyrrolate and 4.8 mcg of formoterol fumarate per inhalation.
Two inhalations equal one dose.
BEVESPI AEROSPHERE contains 28 or 120 inhalations per canister.
The canister has an attached dose indicator and is supplied with a white plastic actuator with an orange dust cap.
