# 11 DESCRIPTION

Sterile Indomethacin for Injection for intravenous administration is lyophilized indomethacin for injection.
Each vial of indomethacin for injection contains 1 mg indomethacin; 0.29 mg monobasic sodium phosphate, 0.41 mg dibasic sodium phosphate and not more than 0.24 mg of sodium hydroxide (used for converting indomethacin base to the sodium salt) as a white to yellow lyophilized powder or plug.
Variations in the size of the lyophilized plug and the intensity of color have no relationship to the quality or amount of indomethacin present in the vial.

The reconstituted solution, pH 6.0 to 7.5, is clear, slightly yellow and essentially free from visible particles.
The pH of the product does not meet the USP monograph [FD&C Act Chapter V, SEC. 501.
[21USC §351](b)].

Indomethacin is designated chemically as 1-(4-chlorobenzoyl)-5-methoxy-2-methyl-1H-indole-3-acetic acid.
The structural formula is:

C19H16ClNO4 M.W.
