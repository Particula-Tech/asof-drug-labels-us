# 13 NONCLINICAL TOXICOLOGY

In rats and mice, oral indomethacin 4 mg/kg/day given during the last three days of gestation caused a decrease in maternal weight gain and some maternal and fetal deaths.
An increased incidence of neuronal necrosis in the diencephalon in the live-born fetuses was observed.
At 2 mg/kg/day, no increase in neuronal necrosis was observed as compared to the control groups.
Administration of 0.5 or 4 mg/kg/day during the first three days of life did not cause an increase in neuronal necrosis at either dose level.

Pregnant rats, given 2 mg/kg/day and 4 mg/kg/day during the last trimester of gestation, delivered offspring whose pulmonary blood vessels were both reduced in number and excessively muscularized.
These findings are similar to those observed in the syndrome of persistent pulmonary hypertension of the neonate.
