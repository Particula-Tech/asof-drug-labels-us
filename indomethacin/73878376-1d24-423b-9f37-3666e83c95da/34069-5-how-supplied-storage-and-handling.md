# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

INDOCIN (indomethacin) Oral Suspension, 25 mg per 5 mL, is an off-white suspension with a pineapple coconut mint flavor.
It is supplied as follows:

NDC 69344-101-01 in bottles of 237 mL

Storage

Store below 30°C (86°F).
Avoid temperatures above 50°C (122°F).
Do not freeze.
