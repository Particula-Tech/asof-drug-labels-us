# HOW SUPPLIED

Indomethacin capsules USP are available containing either 25 mg or 50 mg of indomethacin, USP.

The 25 mg capsule is a size ‘3’ two piece opaque green hard gelatin capsule imprinted with ‘G406’ on body and ‘G’ on cap, filled with white-to off-white granular powder.
They are available as follows:

Bottles of 100 capsules NDC 68462-406-01 Bottles of 1000 capsules NDC 68462-406-10

The 50 mg capsule is a size ‘1’ two piece opaque green hard gelatin capsules imprinted with ‘G302’ on body and ‘G’ on cap filled with white to off-white granular powder.
They are available as follows:

Bottles of 100 capsules NDC 68462-302-01 Bottles of 500 capsules NDC 68462-302-05
