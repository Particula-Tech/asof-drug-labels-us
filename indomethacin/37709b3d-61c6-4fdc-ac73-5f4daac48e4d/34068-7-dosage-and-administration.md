# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

## 2.1 General Dosing Instructions

Carefully consider the potential benefits and risks of TIVORBEX and other treatment options before deciding to use TIVORBEX.
Use the lowest effective dosage for the shortest duration consistent with individual patient treatment goals [see Warnings and Precautions (5)].

For treatment of mild to moderate acute pain, the dosage is 20 mg three times daily or 40 mg two or three times daily.

## 2.2 Non-Interchangeability with Other Formulations of Indomethacin

Different dose strengths and formulations of oral indomethacin are not interchangeable.
This difference should be taken into consideration when changing strengths or formulations.
