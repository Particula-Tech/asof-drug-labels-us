Each single-dose prefilled autoinjector provides a 1.67 mg dose of atropine base (equivalent to 2 mg atropine sulfate) in a self-contained unit designed for self or caregiver administration.

Each 2 mg Atropine autoinjector delivers atropine in 0.7 mL of sterile pyrogen-free solution containing the inactive ingredients: citric acid and sodium citrate (buffer), glycerin 12.47 mg, and phenol 2.8 mg.
The pH range is 4.1–4.5.

After the 2 mg Atropine autoinjector has been activated, the empty container should be disposed of properly (see DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION).
It cannot be refilled, nor can the protruding needle be retracted.

Atropine, an anticholinergic agent (muscarinic antagonist), occurs as white crystals, usually needle-like, or as a white, crystalline powder.
It is highly soluble in water with a molecular weight of 289.38.
Atropine, a naturally occurring belladonna alkaloid, is a racemic mixture of equal parts of d- and l‑hyoscyamine; its activity is due almost entirely to the levo isomer of the drug.
