# DRUG ABUSE AND DEPENDENCE

MOTOFEN® (difenoxin hydrochloride with atropine sulfate) tablets are a Schedule IV controlled substance.

Addiction to (dependence on) difenoxin hydrochloride is theoretically possible at high dosage.
Therefore, the recommended dosage should not be exceeded.
Because of the structural and pharmacological similarities of difenoxin hydrochloride to drugs with a definite addiction potential, MOTOFEN® should be administered with considerable caution to patients who are receiving addicting drugs, to individuals known to be addiction prone, or to those in whom histories suggest may increase the dosage on their own initiative.
