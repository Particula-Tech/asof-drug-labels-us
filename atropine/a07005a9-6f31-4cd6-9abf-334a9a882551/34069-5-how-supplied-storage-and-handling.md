# HOW SUPPLIED

MOTOFEN® is available as a white, dye-free, five-sided, scored tablet with “0500” on the scored side and “V” on the other side.
Each tablet contains 1.0 mg difenoxin (as the hydrochloride salt) and 0.025 mg atropine sulfate.
Supplied in bottles of 100 tablets (NDC 0187-0500-02).

Store at 20°-25°C (68°-77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
