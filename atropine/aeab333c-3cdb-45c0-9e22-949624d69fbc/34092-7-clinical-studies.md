# 14 CLINICAL STUDIES

Topical administration of Atropine Sulfate Ophthalmic Solution, USP 1% results in mydriasis and/or cycloplegia with efficacy demonstrated in both adults and children.
The maximum effect for mydriasis is achieved in about 30–40 minutes after administration, with recovery after approximately 7–10 days.
The maximum effect for cycloplegia is achieved within 60–180 minutes after administration, with recovery after approximately 7–12 days.
