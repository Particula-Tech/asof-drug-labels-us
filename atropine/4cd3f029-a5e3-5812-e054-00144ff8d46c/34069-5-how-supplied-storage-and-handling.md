# HOW SUPPLIED

MOTOFEN® is available as a white, dye-free, five-sided, scored tablet with “0200” on the scored side and "M" on the other side.
Each tablet contains 1 mg difenoxin and 0.025 mg atropine sulfate.
Supplied in bottles of 100 tablets (NDC 54766-200-10).
Store at 20°-25°C (68°-77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
