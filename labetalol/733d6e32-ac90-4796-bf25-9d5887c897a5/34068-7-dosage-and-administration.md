# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

## 2.1 Recommended Dosage

Labetalol Hydrochloride dosage must be individualized.

The recommended initial dosage of labetalol hydrochloride is 100 mg twice daily.
Adjust dosage in increments of 100 mg twice daily at 2- to 3-day intervals based on response.
The recommended maintenance dosage of labetalol hydrochloride is between 200 and 400 mg twice daily.
