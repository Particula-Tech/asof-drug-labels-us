# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

EVOTAZ™ (atazanavir and cobicistat) is indicated in combination with other antiretroviral agents for the treatment of human immunodeficiency virus (HIV-1) infection in adults.

Limitation of Use:

- •Use of EVOTAZ in treatment-experienced patients should be guided by the number of baseline primary protease inhibitor resistance substitutions [see Microbiology (12.4)].
