# 10 OVERDOSAGE

There have been no reports of overdosage in the AVEED clinical trials.
There is 1 report of acute overdosage with use of an approved injectable testosterone product: this subject had serum testosterone levels of up to 11,400 ng/dL with a cerebrovascular accident.

Treatment of overdosage would consist of discontinuation of AVEED together with appropriate symptomatic and supportive care.
