# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Aveed should not be used in any of the following patients:

- Men with carcinoma of the breast or known or suspected carcinoma of the prostate [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3)].
- Women who are or may become pregnant, or who are breastfeeding.
  Testosterone can cause fetal harm when administered to a pregnant woman.
  Aveed may cause serious adverse reactions in nursing infants.
  Exposure of a female fetus or nursing infant to androgens may result in varying degrees of virilization [see Use in Specific Populations (8.1, 8.3)].
- Men with known hypersensitivity to Aveed or any of its ingredients (testosterone undecanoate, refined castor oil, benzyl benzoate).
