# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

- Vogelxo is contraindicated in men with carcinoma of the breast or known or suspected carcinoma of the prostate [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
- Vogelxo is contraindicated in women who are pregnant.
  Vogelxo can cause virilization of the female fetus when administered to a pregnant woman.
  Pregnant women need to be aware of the potential for skin transfer of testosterone from men treated with Vogelxo.
  If a pregnant woman is exposed to Vogelxo, she should be apprised of the potential hazard to the fetus [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2) and Use in Specific Populations (8.1)].
