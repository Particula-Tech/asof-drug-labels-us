# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

- ANDRODERM is contraindicated in men with carcinoma of the breast or known or suspected carcinoma of the prostate [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
- ANDRODERM is contraindicated in women who are pregnant.
  Testosterone can cause virilization of the female fetus when administered to a pregnant woman.
  If a pregnant woman is exposed to ANDRODERM, she should be apprised of the potential hazard to the fetus [see Use in Specific Populations (8.1)].
