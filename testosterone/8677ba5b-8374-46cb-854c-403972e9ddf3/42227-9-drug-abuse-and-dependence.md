# 9 DRUG ABUSE AND DEPENDENCE

## 9.1 Controlled Substance

AndroGel 1.62% contains testosterone, a Schedule III controlled substance as defined under the Anabolic Steroids Control Act.

## 9.2 Abuse

Anabolic steroids, such as testosterone, are abused.
Abuse is often associated with adverse physical and psychological effects.

## 9.3 Dependence

Although drug dependence is not documented in individuals using therapeutic doses of anabolic steroids for approved indications, dependence is observed in some individuals abusing high doses of anabolic steroids.
In general, anabolic steroid dependence is characterized by any three of the following:

- Taking more drug than intended
- Continued drug use despite medical and social problems
- Significant time spent in obtaining adequate amounts of drug
- Desire for anabolic steroids when supplies of the drugs are interrupted
- Difficulty in discontinuing use of the drug despite desires and attempts to do so
- Experience of a withdrawal syndrome upon discontinuation of anabolic steroid use
