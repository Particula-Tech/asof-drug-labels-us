# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

AndroGel 1.62% is supplied in a metered-dose pump that delivers 20.25 mg of testosterone per complete pump actuation.
The pump is composed of plastic and stainless steel and an LDPE/aluminum foil inner liner encased in rigid plastic with a polypropylene cap.
The metered-dose pump is capable of dispensing 60 metered pump actuations.
One pump actuation delivers 1.25 g of gel.

NDC 0051-8462-33

Store at controlled room temperature 20°-25°C (68°-77°F); excursions permitted to 15°- 30°C (59°- 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Used AndroGel 1.62% pumps should be discarded in household trash in a manner that prevents accidental application or ingestion by children or pets.
