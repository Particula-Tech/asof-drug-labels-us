# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

- Testosterone gel 1% is contraindicated in men with carcinoma of the breast or known or suspected carcinoma of the prostate [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1), Adverse Reactions (6.1), and Nonclinical Toxicology (13.1)].
- Testosterone gel 1% is contraindicated in women who are pregnant.
  Testosterone gel 1% can cause virilization of the female fetus when administered to a pregnant woman.
  Pregnant women need to be aware of the potential for transfer of testosterone from men treated with testosterone gel 1%.
  If a pregnant woman is exposed to testosterone gel 1%, she should be apprised of the potential hazard to the fetus [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2) and Use in Specific Populations (8.1)].
