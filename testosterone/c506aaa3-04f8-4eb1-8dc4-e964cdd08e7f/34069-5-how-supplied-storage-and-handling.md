# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

AndroGel is supplied in non-aerosol, metered-dose pumps.
The pump is composed of plastic and stainless steel and an LDPE/aluminum foil inner liner encased in rigid plastic with a polypropylene cap.
Each 88 g AndroGel Pump in the twin package is capable of dispensing 75 g or 60 metered 1.25 g doses.

AndroGel is also supplied in unit-dose aluminum foil packets in cartons of 30.
Each packet of 2.5 g or 5 g gel contains 25 mg or 50 mg testosterone, respectively.

| NDC Number | Package Size |
| --- | --- |
| 0051-8488-88 | 2 x 75 g pumps (each pump dispenses 60 metered 1.25 g doses) |
| 0051-8425-30 | 30 packets (2.5 g per packet) |
| 0051-8450-30 | 30 packets (5 g per packet) |

Keep AndroGel out of the reach of children.

Storage

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Disposal

Used AndroGel pumps or used AndroGel packets should be discarded in household trash in a manner that prevents accidental application or ingestion by children or pets.
