# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

XYOSTED injection is available as 0.5 mL of a sterile, preservative-free and nonpyrogenic, clear, colorless to yellow solution containing testosterone enanthate.
It is supplied in a single-dose syringe assembled in an autoinjector for subcutaneous administration.
XYOSTED is available in three dosage strengths:

50 mg/0.5 mL

75 mg/0.5 mL

100 mg/0.5 mL
