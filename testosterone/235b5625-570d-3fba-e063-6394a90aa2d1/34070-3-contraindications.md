# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

AZMIRO is contraindicated in: • Known hypersensitivity to AZMIRO or to any of its components [see Description (11)].
Hypersensitivity, including skin manifestations and anaphylactoid reactions have been reported [see Adverse Reactions (6.2)]. • Men with carcinoma of the breast or known or suspected carcinoma of the prostate gland [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3)]. • Women who are pregnant.
Testosterone can cause virilization of the female fetus when administered to a pregnant woman [see Use in Specific Populations (8.1)].
