Store at 20-25°C (68-77°F).
[See USP controlled room temperature.]
Apply to skin immediately upon removal from the protective pouch.
Do not store outside the pouch provided.
Damaged systems should not be used.
The drug reservoir may be burst by excessive pressure or heat.
Discard systems in household trash in a manner that prevents accidental application or ingestion by children, pets or others.
