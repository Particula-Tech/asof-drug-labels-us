# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

- AXIRON is contraindicated in men with carcinoma of the breast or known or suspected carcinoma of the prostate [see Warnings and Precaution (5.1)].

AXIRON is contraindicated in women who are, or who may become pregnant, or who are breastfeeding.
AXIRON may cause fetal harm when administered to a pregnant woman.
AXIRON may cause serious adverse reactions in nursing infants.
If a pregnant woman is exposed to AXIRON, she should be apprised of the potential hazard to the fetus.
[See Use in Specific Populations (8.1, 8.3)].
