# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

AXIRON (testosterone) topical solution is available as a metered-dose pump containing 110 mL of solution.
The pump is capable of dispensing 90 mL of solution in 60 metered pump actuations.
One pump actuation delivers 30 mg of testosterone in 1.5 mL of solution.
Each metered-dose pump is supplied with an applicator.
Neither the bottle nor the applicator cup contains latex.

NDC 0002-1975-90

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Keep AXIRON out of reach of children.

Store at 25°C (77°F).
Excursions are permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F).
See USP Controlled Room Temperature.

Used AXIRON bottles and applicators should be discarded in household trash in a manner that prevents accidental exposure of children or pets.
