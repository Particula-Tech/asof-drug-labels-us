# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

- •TYRUKO is contraindicated in patients who have or have had progressive multifocal leukoencephalopathy (PML) [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
- •TYRUKO is contraindicated in patients who have had a hypersensitivity reaction to natalizumab products or any of the ingredients in TYRUKO.
  Observed reactions range from urticaria to anaphylaxis [see Warnings and Precautions (5.5)].
