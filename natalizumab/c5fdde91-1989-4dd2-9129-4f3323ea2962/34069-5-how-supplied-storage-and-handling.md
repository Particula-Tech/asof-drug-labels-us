# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

TYSABRI (natalizumab) injection, a sterile, preservative-free, colorless and clear to slightly opalescent solution for dilution prior to intravenous infusion, is supplied as one 300 mg/15 mL (20 mg/mL) single-dose vial per carton (NDC 64406-008-01).

TYSABRI is available only through registered infusion centers participating in the TOUCH® Prescribing Program.
To locate these infusion centers, contact Biogen at 1-800-456-2255.

TYSABRI single-dose vials must be refrigerated between 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F).
Do not use beyond the expiration date stamped on the carton and vial label.
DO NOT SHAKE OR FREEZE.
Protect from light.

Store diluted TYSABRI solution refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) [see Dosage and Administration (2.3)]
