# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

- TYSABRI is contraindicated in patients who have or have had progressive multifocal leukoencephalopathy (PML) [see Boxed Warning, Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
- TYSABRI should not be administered to a patient who has had a hypersensitivity reaction to TYSABRI.
  Observed reactions range from urticaria to anaphylaxis [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3)].
