# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Daptomycin for Injection is supplied as a sterile pale yellow to light brown lyophilized cake or powder in a single-dose 20-mL vial containing 500 mg of daptomycin: Package of 1 (NDC 67457-813-50)

Store original packages at refrigerated temperatures, 2º to 8ºC (36º to 46ºF); avoid excessive heat [see Dosage and Administration (2.7)].
