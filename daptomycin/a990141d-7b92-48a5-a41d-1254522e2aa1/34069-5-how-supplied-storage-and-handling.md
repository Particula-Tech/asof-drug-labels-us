# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Daptomycin for injection is supplied as a sterile pale yellow to light brown lyophilized cake in a single-dose vial.

| Product No. | NDC No. |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 350710 | 63323-371-10 | 500 mg per vial, packaged individually. |

Store original packages at refrigerated temperatures, 2° to 8°C (36° to 46°F); avoid excessive heat [see Dosage and Administration (2.7)].

The container closure is not made with natural rubber latex.
