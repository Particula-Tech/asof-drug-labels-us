# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

CUBICIN RF (daptomycin for injection) is supplied as a sterile pale yellow to light brown lyophilized powder in a single-dose 10 mL vial containing 500 mg of daptomycin: Package of 1 (NDC 67919-012-02).

Store original packages at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature] [see Dosage and Administration (2.7)].
