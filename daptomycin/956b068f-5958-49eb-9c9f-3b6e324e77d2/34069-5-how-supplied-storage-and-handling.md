# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Daptomycin for Injection is supplied as a sterile pale yellow to light brown lyophilized cake or powder in a single-dose vial containing 500 mg of daptomycin: Package of 1 (NDC 16729-435-05).

Store original packages at refrigerated temperatures, 2 to 8°C (36 to 46°F); avoid excessive heat [see Dosage and Administration (2.7)].
