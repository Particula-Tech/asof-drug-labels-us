# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

CUBICIN (daptomycin for injection) is supplied as sterile pale yellow to light brown lyophilized cake in a single-use 10-mL vial containing 500 mg of daptomycin: Package of 1 (NDC 67919-011-01)

Store original packages at refrigerated temperatures, 2 to 8°C (36 to 46°F); avoid excessive heat.
