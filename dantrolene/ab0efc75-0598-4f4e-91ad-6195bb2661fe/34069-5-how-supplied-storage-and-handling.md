# HOW SUPPLIED

Dantrolene Sodium for Injection, USP (NDC 0143-9297-06) is available in vials containing a sterile lyophilized mixture of 20 mg dantrolene sodium, 3,000 mg mannitol, and sufficient sodium hydroxide to yield a pH of approximately 9.5 when reconstituted with 60 mL sterile water for injection USP (without a bacteriostatic agent).

Store reconstituted solution at 20° to 25°C (68º to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Protect from direct light.

Store unreconstituted product at 20° to 25°C (68º to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Avoid prolonged exposure to light.
