# DESCRIPTION

Revonto is a sterile, non-pyrogenic, lyophilized formulation of dantrolene sodium for injection.
Revonto is supplied in 65 mL vials containing 20 mg dantrolene sodium, 3000 mg mannitol, and sufficient sodium hydroxide to yield a pH of approximately 9.5 when reconstituted with 60 ml sterile water for injection USP (without a bacteriostatic agent).

Dantrolene sodium is classified as a direct-acting skeletal muscle relaxant.
Chemically, dantrolene sodium is hydrated 1-[[[5-(4-nitrophenyl)-2-furanyl] methylene] amino]-2,4- imidazolidinedione sodium salt.
