# HOW SUPPLIED

Tablets STROMECTOL 3 mg are white, round, flat, bevel-edged tablets coded MSD on one side and 32 on the other side.
They are supplied as follows:

NDC 0006-0032-20 unit dose packages of 20.

NDC 0006-0032-01 unit dose packages of 20.

## Storage

Store at temperatures below 30°C (86°F).
