# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Fingolimod capsules 0.5 mgare bright yellow cap/opaque white body size ‘4’ hard gelatin capsule imprinted with ‘H’ on cap with black ink and ‘F7’ on body with blue ink, filled with white to off white powder.
Fingolimod capsules are supplied as follows: Bottle of 30 capsules NDC 31722-889-30 Blister card of 10 Unit-dose capsules NDC 31722-889-31 Blister pack of 100 (10 x 10) Unit-dose capsules NDC 31722-889-32

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Fingolimod capsules should be stored at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from moisture.
