# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Fingolimod capsules are available as:

- •0.5 mg, size 3 hard gelatin capsules with a light yellow to yellow opaque cap and off-white to white opaque body, imprinted with a Glenmark Logo 'G' on the cap and '559' on the capsule body in black ink containing white to off-white powder.
