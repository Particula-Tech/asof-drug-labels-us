# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Doxylamine succinate and pyridoxine hydrochloride delayed-release tablets are white to off white, round, biconvex film coated tablets, plain on both sides containing 10 mg doxylamine succinate, USP and 10 mg pyridoxine hydrochloride, USP.
The tablets are printed with “2132” on one side with black ink and plain on the other.
