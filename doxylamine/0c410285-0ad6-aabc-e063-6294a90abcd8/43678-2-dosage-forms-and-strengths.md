# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Doxylamine succinate and pyridoxine hydrochloride delayed‑release tablets are white, round, film-coated tablets containing 10 mg doxylamine succinate, USP and 10 mg pyridoxine hydrochloride, USP.
The tablets are imprinted on one side with “ DP 1” in black ink and plain on other side.
