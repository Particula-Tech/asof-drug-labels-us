# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

## 4.1 Severe Renal Impairment

Capecitabine Tablets, USP is contraindicated in patients with severe renal impairment (creatinine clearance below 30mL/min [Cockroft and Gault]) [see Use in Specific Populations (8.7) and Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].

## 4.2 Hypersensitivity

Capecitabine Tablets, USP is contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity to capecitabine or to any of its components.
Capecitabine Tablets, USP is contraindicated in patients who have a known hypersensitivity to 5-fluorouracil.
