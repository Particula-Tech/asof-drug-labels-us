# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Ropivacaine Hydrochloride Injection, USP is a clear colorless, and preservative-free solution, available in single-dose containers in 2 mg/mL (0.2%), 5 mg/mL (0.5%) and 10 mg/mL (1%) concentration.

Storage

Solutions should be stored at 20 °C to 25 °C (68 °F to 77 °F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
