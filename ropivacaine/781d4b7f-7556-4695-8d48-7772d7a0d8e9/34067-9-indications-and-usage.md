# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Ropivacaine Hydrochloride is indicated for the production of local or regional anesthesia for surgery and for acute pain management.

1. Surgical Anesthesia: epidural block for surgery including cesarean section; major nerve block; local infiltration
2. Acute Pain Management: epidural continuous infusion or intermittent bolus, e.g., postoperative or labor; local infiltration
