# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

NAROPIN (ropivacaine hydrochloride) Injection is a clear colorless, and preservative-free solution, available in single-dose containers in 2 mg/mL (0.2%), 5 mg/mL (0.5%), 7.5 mg/mL (0.75%) and 10 mg/mL (1%) concentrations.

Storage

Solutions should be stored at 20ºC to 25°C (68ºF to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
