# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Ropivacaine Hydrochloride Injection USP is a clear colorless, and preservative-free solution available in single dose infusion bags.
Storage Solutions should be stored at 20 ºC to 25 °C (68 ºF to 77 °F); [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Ropivacaine Hydrochloride Injection single-dose, ready-to-use, polypropylene flexible bags.
The flexible bag container is not made with natural rubber latex or polyvinyl chloride (PVC), Non-DEHP.
