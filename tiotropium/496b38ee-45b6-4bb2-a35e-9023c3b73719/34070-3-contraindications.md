# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Tiotropium bromide inhalation powder is contraindicated in patients with a hypersensitivity to tiotropium, ipratropium, or any components of this product [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)].
In clinical trials and postmarketing experience with tiotropium bromide inhalation powder, immediate hypersensitivity reactions, including angioedema (including swelling of the lips, tongue, or throat), itching, or rash have been reported [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)].
