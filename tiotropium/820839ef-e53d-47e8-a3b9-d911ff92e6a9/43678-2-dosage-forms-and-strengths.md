# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Inhalation Powder: SPIRIVA HANDIHALER consists of SPIRIVA capsules containing tiotropium powder for oral inhalation and a HANDIHALER device.
SPIRIVA capsules contain 18 mcg of tiotropium in a light green, hard gelatin capsule with TI 01 printed on one side and Boehringer Ingelheim company logo on the other side.
The HANDIHALER device is only intended for use with the SPIRIVA capsules.
