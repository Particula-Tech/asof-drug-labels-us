# DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

SPIRIVA capsules must not be swallowed as the intended effects on the lungs will not be obtained.
The contents of the capsules are only for oral inhalation and should only be used with the HandiHaler inhalation device (see OVERDOSAGE section).

The recommended dosage of SPIRIVA HandiHaler (tiotropium bromide inhalation powder) is the inhalation of the contents of one SPIRIVA capsule, once-daily, with the HandiHaler inhalation device (see Patient’s Instructions for Use).

No dosage adjustment is required for geriatric, hepatically-impaired, or renally-impaired patients.
However, patients with moderate to severe renal impairment given SPIRIVA HandiHaler should be monitored closely (see CLINICAL PHARMACOLOGY, Pharmacokinetics, Special Populations and PRECAUTIONS).
