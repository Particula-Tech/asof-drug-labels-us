# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

## 1.1 Maintenance Treatment of Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease

SPIRIVA RESPIMAT (tiotropium bromide) is indicated for the long-term, once-daily, maintenance treatment of bronchospasm associated with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), including chronic bronchitis and emphysema.
SPIRIVA RESPIMAT is indicated to reduce exacerbations in COPD patients.

Important Limitation of Use: SPIRIVA RESPIMAT is NOT indicated for the relief of acute bronchospasm.

## 1.2 Maintenance Treatment of Asthma

SPIRIVA RESPIMAT is a bronchodilator indicated for the long-term, once-daily, maintenance treatment of asthma in patients 6 years of age and older.

Important Limitation of Use: SPIRIVA RESPIMAT is NOT indicated for the relief of acute bronchospasm.
