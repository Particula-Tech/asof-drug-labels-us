# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

SPIRIVA RESPIMAT consists of a SPIRIVA RESPIMAT inhaler and an aluminum cylinder (SPIRIVA RESPIMAT cartridge) containing tiotropium bromide (as the monohydrate).
The SPIRIVA RESPIMAT cartridge is only intended for use with the SPIRIVA RESPIMAT inhaler.

Each actuation from the SPIRIVA RESPIMAT inhaler delivers 2.5 mcg of tiotropium (equivalent to 3.124 mcg of tiotropium bromide monohydrate) from the mouthpiece.
Two actuations equal one dose (5 mcg).
