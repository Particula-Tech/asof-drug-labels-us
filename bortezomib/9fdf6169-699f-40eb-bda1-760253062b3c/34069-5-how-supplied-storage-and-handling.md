# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Bortezomib for Injection is supplied as individually cartoned 5 mL or 10 mL vials containing 1 mg or 2.5 mg of bortezomib, respectively, as a white to off-white cake or powder.

| Unit of Sale | Strength |
| --- | --- |
| NDC 0409-1704-01 1 Single-dose vial in a carton | 1 mg/vial |
| NDC 0409-1703-01 1 Single-dose vial in a carton | 2.5 mg/vial |

Unopened vials may be stored at controlled room temperature 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted from 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Retain in original package to protect from light.

Follow guidelines for handling and disposal for hazardous drugs, including the use of gloves and other protective clothing to prevent skin contact1.
