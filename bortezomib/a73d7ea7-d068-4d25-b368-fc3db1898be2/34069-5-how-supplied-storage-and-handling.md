# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Bortezomib for Injection (bortezomib) for Injection is supplied as individually cartoned 10 mL vials containing 3.5 mg of bortezomib as a white to off-white cake or powder.

NDC 72338-200-01

3.5 mg single-dose vial

Unopened vials may be stored at controlled room temperature 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted from 15 to 30°C (59 to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Retain in original package to protect from light.
Discard unused portion.

Follow guidelines for handling and disposal for hazardous drugs, including the use of gloves and other protective clothing to prevent skin contact1.
