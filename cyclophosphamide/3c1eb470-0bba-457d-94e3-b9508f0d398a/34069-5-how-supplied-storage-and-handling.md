# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Cyclophosphamide Injection is a colorless to slightly yellow clear solution available in clear glass vials for single-dose use.
Discard unused portion.

Cyclophosphamide Injection:

NDC 55150-270-01 500 mg/2.5 mL (200 mg/mL) in 30 mL vial, carton of 1

NDC 55150-271-01 1 g/5 mL (200 mg/mL) in 50 mL vial, carton of 1

Storage:

Store vials at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F).

Cyclophosphamide is a hazardous drug.
Follow special handling and disposal procedures.1
