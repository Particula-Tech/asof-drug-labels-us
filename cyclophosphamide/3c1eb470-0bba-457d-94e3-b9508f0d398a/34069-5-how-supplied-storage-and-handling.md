# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Cyclophosphamide Injection is a colorless to slightly yellow clear solution available in clear glass vials for multiple-dose use.
Cyclophosphamide Injection: NDC 55150-270-99 500 mg/2.5 mL (200 mg/mL) in 30 mL vial, carton of 1 NDC 55150-271-99 1 g/5 mL (200 mg/mL) in 50 mL vial, carton of 1 NDC 55150-272-01 2 g/10 mL (200 mg/mL) in 100 mL vial, carton of 1 Storage: Store vials refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F).
Cyclophosphamide is a hazardous drug.
Follow special handling and disposal procedures.1 The vial stopper is not made with natural rubber latex.
