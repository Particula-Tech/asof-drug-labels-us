# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Cyclophosphamide Capsules

25 mg, blue opaque capsules (Identified 54 006)

NDC 0054-0382-25: Bottle of 100 capsules.

50 mg, blue opaque capsules (Identified 54 881)

NDC 0054-0383-25: Bottle of 100 capsules.

## Storage

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (between 59°F and 86°F).

Cyclophosphamide is an antineoplastic product.
Follow special handling and disposal procedures.1
