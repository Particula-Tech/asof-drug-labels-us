# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Each 25 mg capsule for oral administration contains 25 mg of cyclophosphamide.
It is a blue opaque capsule with “54 006” printed on the capsule in black ink, containing a white to off-white powder.

Each 50 mg capsule for oral administration contains 50 mg of cyclophosphamide.
It is a blue opaque capsule with “54 881” printed on the capsule in black ink, containing a white to off-white powder.
