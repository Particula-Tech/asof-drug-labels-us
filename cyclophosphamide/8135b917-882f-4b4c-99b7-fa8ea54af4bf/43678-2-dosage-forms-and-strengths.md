# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Capsules:

- •25 mg capsule for oral administration contains 25 mg of cyclophosphamide, USP.
  It is a blue/blue opaque capsule with “54 006” printed in black ink on the cap and body, containing a white to off-white powder.
- •50 mg capsule for oral administration contains 50 mg of cyclophosphamide, USP.
  It is a blue/blue opaque capsule with “54 881” printed in black ink on the cap and body, containing a white to off-white powder.
