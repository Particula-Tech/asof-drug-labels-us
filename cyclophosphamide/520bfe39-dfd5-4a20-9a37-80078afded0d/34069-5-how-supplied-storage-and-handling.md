# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Cyclophosphamide for Injection, USP is a sterile white powder containing cyclophosphamide, USP and is supplied in vials for single dose use.
It is available as follows:

500 mg/vial

1 Vial in Carton: NDC 70121-1238-1

1 g/vial

1 Vial in Carton: NDC 70121-1239-1

2 g/vial

1 Vial in Carton: NDC 70121-1240-1

Store vials at or below 25°C (77°F).
During transport or storage of cyclophosphamide vials, temperature influences can lead to melting of the active ingredient, cyclophosphamide [see Dosage and Administration (2.3)].

Cyclophosphamide is an antineoplastic product.
Follow special handling and disposal procedures.1
