# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Cyclophosphamide for Injection, USP (lyophilized powder) is a sterile white lyophilized cake or powder containing cyclophosphamide and mannitol and is supplied in vials for single dose use.

| Cyclophosphamide for Injection, USP |  |
| --- | --- |
| NDC 39822-0250-1 | 500 mg vial, carton of 1 |
| NDC 39822-0255-1 | 1 g vial, carton of 1 |
| NDC 39822-0260-1 | 2 g vial, carton of 1 |

Store vials at or below 25°C (77°F).
During transport or storage of cyclophosphamide vials, temperature influences can lead to melting of the active ingredient, cyclophosphamide [see Dosage and Administration (2.3)].

Cyclophosphamide is an antineoplastic product.
Follow special handling and disposal procedures.
