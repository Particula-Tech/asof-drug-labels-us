# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Cyclophosphamide Injection is a 200 mg/mL sterile clear colorless solution ready-to-dilute solution containing cyclophosphamide, USP.

Cyclophosphamide Injection

Store the vials refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F).

Store diluted solutions of cyclophosphamide according to Table 1 [seeDosage and Administration (2.4)].
