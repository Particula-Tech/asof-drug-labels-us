# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

FRINDOVYXTM is a ready-to-dilute clear, colorless to yellow sterile solution containing cyclophosphamide and is supplied in multiple-dose vial.

FRINDOVYXTM Injection

| NDC Number | Strength | Vial Presentation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 83831-119-01 | 500 mg/mL | Multiple-dose vial, carton of 1 |
| 83831-120-02 | 1 g/2 mL | Multiple-dose vial, carton of 1 |
| 83831-121-04 | 2 g/4 mL | Multiple-dose vial, carton of 1 |

Store vials refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F).

Store diluted solutions of cyclophosphamide according to Table 1 [see Dosage and Administration (2.3)].

Cyclophosphamide is a hazardous product.
Follow special handling and disposal procedures.1
