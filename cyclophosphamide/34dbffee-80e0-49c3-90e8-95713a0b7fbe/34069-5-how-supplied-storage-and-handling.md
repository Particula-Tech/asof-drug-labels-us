# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Cyclophosphamide Injection is a 200 mg/mL clear or colorless ready-to-dilute sterile solution containing cyclophosphamide, USP.

Cyclophosphamide Injection

| NDC Number | Presentation | Pack Factor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0338-9777-01 | 500 mg per 2.5 mL Multiple-Dose Vial | 1 vial per carton |
| 0338-9779-01 | 1,000 mg per 5 mL Multiple-Dose Vial | 1 vial per carton |

The container closure is not made with natural rubber latex.

Store the vials refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F).

Cyclophosphamide is a hazardous product.
Follow special handling and disposal procedures.
