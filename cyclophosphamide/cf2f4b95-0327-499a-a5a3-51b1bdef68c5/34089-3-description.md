# 11 DESCRIPTION

Cyclophosphamide is an alkylating drug.
It is a synthetic antineoplastic drug chemically related to the nitrogen mustards.
The chemical name for cyclophosphamide is 2-[bis(2-chloroethyl)amino]tetrahydro-2H-1,3,2-oxazaphosphorine 2-oxide monohydrate, and has the following structural formula:

Cyclophosphamide is a white crystalline powder with the molecular formula C7H15Cl2N2O2P•H2O and a molecular weight of 279.1.
Cyclophosphamide is soluble in water and freely soluble in alcohol.

Cyclophosphamide Injection, 100 mg/mL is a sterile ready-to-dilute, clear, colorless to pale-yellow solution in a multiple-dose vial available as 500 mg/5 mL, 1,000 mg/10 mL, and 2,000 mg/20 mL strengths.

- •500 mg vial contains 534.5 mg cyclophosphamide monohydrate equivalent to 500 mg cyclophosphamide, 2.92 g dehydrated alcohol (equivalent to 73.9 % v/v) and 0.96 g propylene glycol.
- •1,000 mg vial contains 1,069 mg cyclophosphamide monohydrate equivalent to 1,000 mg cyclophosphamide, 5.85 g dehydrated alcohol (equivalent to 73.9 % v/v) and 1.92 g propylene glycol.
- •2,000 mg vial contains 2,138 mg cyclophosphamide monohydrate equivalent to 2,000 mg cyclophosphamide, 11.69 g dehydrated alcohol (equivalent to 73.9 % v/v) and 3.84 g propylene glycol.
