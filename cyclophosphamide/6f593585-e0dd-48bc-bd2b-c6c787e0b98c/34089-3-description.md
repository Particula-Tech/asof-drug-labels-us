# 11 DESCRIPTION

Cyclophosphamide is an alkylating drug.
It is a synthetic antineoplastic drug chemically related to the nitrogen mustards.
The chemical name for cyclophosphamide is (±)-2-[Bis(2-chloroethyl)amino]tetrahydro-2H-1,3,2-oxazaphosphorine 2-oxide monohydrate, and has the following structural formula:

Cyclophosphamide is a white crystalline powder with the molecular formula C7H15Cl2N2O2 P•H2O and a molecular weight of 279.1.
Cyclophosphamide is soluble in water, saline, or ethanol.
The pKa of cyclophosphamide is 5.7.

Cyclophosphamide Injection is a clear, colorless to slight yellow sterile solution available as 500 mg/mL, 1 g/2 mL, and 2 g/4 mL in single dose vials for dilution prior to intravenous administration.

- 500 mg/mL vial contains 534.5 mg cyclophosphamide monohydrate, equivalent to 500 mg cyclophosphamide and 500 mg dehydrated alcohol (equivalent to 63% v/v).
- 1 g/2 mL vial contains 1069.0 mg cyclophosphamide monohydrate equivalent to 1 g cyclophosphamide and 1000 mg dehydrated alcohol (equivalent to 63% v/v).
- 2 g/4 mL vial contains 2138.0 mg cyclophosphamide monohydrate, equivalent to 2 g cyclophosphamide and 2000 mg dehydrated alcohol (equivalent to 63% v/v).
