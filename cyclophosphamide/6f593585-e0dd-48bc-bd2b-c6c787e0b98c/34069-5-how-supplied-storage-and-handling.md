# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Cyclophosphamide Injection is a ready-to-dilute clear, colorless to slight yellow sterile solution containing cyclophosphamide and is supplied in single-dose vials.

Cyclophosphamide Injection

| NDC Number | Strength | Vial Presentation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NDC 83831-116-01 | 500 mg/mL | single-dose vial, carton of 1 |
| NDC 83831-117-02 | 1 g/2 mL | single-dose vial, carton of 1 |
| NDC 83831-118-04 | 2 g/4 mL | single-dose vial, carton of 1 |

Store vials refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F).

Cyclophosphamide is a hazardous product.
Follow special handling and disposal procedures.1
