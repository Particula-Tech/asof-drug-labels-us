# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Cyclophosphamide capsules are contraindicated in patients with:

• A history of severe hypersensitivity reactions to cyclosphosphamide, any of its metabolites, or to other components of the product.
Anaphylactic reactions including death have been reported with cyclophosphamide.
Cross-sensitivity with other alkylating agents can occur. • In patients with urinary outflow obstruction [see WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS (5.2)].
