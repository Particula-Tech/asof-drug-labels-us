# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Solifenacin Succinate tablets are supplied as round, biconvex, film-coated tablets, available in bottles and unit dose blister packages as follows:

- Each 5 mg tablet is yellow colored and debossed with a ‘G’ on one side and ‘51’ on other side and is available as follows:
- Bottle of 30 NDC 68462-386-30 Bottle of 90 NDC 68462-386-90 Unit Dose Pack of 100 NDC 68462-386-14
- Each 10 mg tablet is pink colored and debossed with ‘G’ on one side and ‘52’ on other side and is available as follows:
- Bottle of 30 NDC 68462-387-30 Bottle of 90 NDC 68462-387-90 Unit Dose Pack of 100 NDC 68462-387-14

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
