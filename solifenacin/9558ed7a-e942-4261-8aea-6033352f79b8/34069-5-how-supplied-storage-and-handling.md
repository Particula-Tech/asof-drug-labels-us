# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Solifenacin succinate tablets are supplied as circular, biconvex, film-coated tablets.

Each 5 mg tablet is light yellow colored with “S1” debossed on one side and plain on the other side and is available as follows:

Bottle of 30 with child-resistant closure NDC 27241-037-03

Bottle of 90 with child-resistant closure NDC 27241-037-09

Each 10 mg tablet is light pink colored with “S2” debossed on one side and plain on the other side and is available as follows:

Bottle of 30 with child-resistant closure NDC 27241-038-03

Bottle of 90 with child-resistant closure NDC 27241-038-09

Store at 25ºC (77ºF) with excursions permitted from 15ºC to 30ºC (59°F to 86ºF) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
