# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Solifenacin succinate tablets are supplied as round, film-coated tablets, available in bottles as follows: Each 5 mg tablet is light yellow colored, round shaped, biconvex, film-coated tablets, debossed with 'S 5' on one side and plain on other side and is available as follows: Bottle of 30 NDC 72205-020-30 Bottle of 90 NDC 72205-020-90 Bottle of 1000 NDC 72205-020-99 Each 10 mg tablet is light pink colored, round shaped, biconvex, film-coated tablets, debossed with 'S 10' on one side and plain on other side and is available as follows: Bottle of 30 NDC 72205-021-30 Bottle of 90 NDC 72205-021-90 Bottle of 1000 NDC 72205-021-99 Store at 25ºC (77ºF) with excursions permitted from 15ºC to 30ºC (59°F to 86ºF) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
