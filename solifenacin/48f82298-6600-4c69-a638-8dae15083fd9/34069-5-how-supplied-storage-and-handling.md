# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Solifenacin succinate tablets 5 mg are creamish to light yellow, round, film-coated tablets, debossed with “L” on one side and “431” on other side.
They are supplied as follows:

NDC 62332-192-30 Bottle of 30 NDC 62332-192-90 Bottle of 90 NDC 62332-192-91 Bottle of 1000 NDC 62332-192-08 Carton of 80 (10 x 8) unit-dose tablets

Solifenacin succinate tablets 10 mg are light pink, round, film-coated tablets, debossed with “L” on one side and “432” on other side.
They are supplied as follows:

NDC 62332-193-30 Bottle of 30 NDC 62332-193-90 Bottle of 90 NDC 62332-193-91 Bottle of 1000 NDC 62332-193-08 Carton of 80 (10 x 8) unit-dose tablets

Store at 25ºC (77ºF) with excursions permitted from 15ºC to 30ºC (59°F to 86ºF) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
