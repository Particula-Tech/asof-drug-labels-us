# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

VESIcare LS is contraindicated in patients:

- •With gastric retention [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3)],
- •With uncontrolled narrow-angle glaucoma [see Warnings and Precautions (5.5)], and
- •Who have demonstrated hypersensitivity to solifenacin succinate or the inactive ingredients in VESIcare LS oral suspension.
  Reported adverse reactions have included anaphylaxis and angioedema [see Adverse Reactions (6.2)].
