# OVERDOSAGE

## Acute

Overdosage with VESIcare can potentially result in severe anticholinergic effects and should be treated accordingly.
The highest VESIcare dose given to human volunteers was a single 100 mg dose.

## Chronic

Intolerable anticholinergic side effects (fixed and dilated pupils, blurred vision, failure of heel-to-toe exam, tremors and dry skin) occurred on day 3 in normal volunteers taking 50 mg daily (5 times the maximum recommended therapeutic dose) and resolved within 7 days following discontinuation of drug.

## Treatment of Overdosage

In the event of overdose with VESIcare, treat with gastric lavage and appropriate supportive measures.
ECG monitoring is also recommended.
