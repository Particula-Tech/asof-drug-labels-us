# HOW SUPPLIED

Caffeine Citrate Oral Solution, USP is available as a clear, colorless, sterile, non-pyrogenic, preservative-free, aqueous solution in colorless glass vials.

The vials contain 3 mL of solution at a concentration of 20 mg/mL caffeine citrate (60 mg/vial) equivalent to 10 mg/mL caffeine base (30 mg/vial).

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F)[see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
