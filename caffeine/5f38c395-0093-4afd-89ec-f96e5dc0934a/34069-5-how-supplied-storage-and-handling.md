Caffeine Citrate Injection USP, 60 mg per 3 mL is available as clear, colorless, aqueous solutions in 5 mL clear glass vials.

The injection vials contain 3 mL solution at a concentration of 20 mg per mL caffeine citrate (60 mg per vial) equivalent to 10 mg per mL caffeine base (30 mg per vial).
