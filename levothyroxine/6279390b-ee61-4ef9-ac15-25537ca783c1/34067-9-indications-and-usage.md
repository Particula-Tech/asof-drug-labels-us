# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Levothyroxine Sodium Injection is indicated for the treatment of myxedema coma.

Limitations of Use: The relative bioavailability between Levothyroxine Sodium Injection and oral levothyroxine products has not been established.
Caution should be used when switching patients from oral levothyroxine products to Levothyroxine Sodium Injection as accurate dosing conversion has not been studied.
