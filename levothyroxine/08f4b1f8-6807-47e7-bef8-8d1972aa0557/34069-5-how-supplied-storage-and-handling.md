# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Levothyroxine Sodium Injection is a clear, colorless solution available as follows:

| NDC | Total Strength per Total Volume | Concentration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 63323-885-10 | 100 mcg per 5 mL | 20 mcg per mL |
| 63323-890-10 | 200 mcg per 5 mL | 40 mcg per mL |
| 63323-895-10 | 500 mcg per 5 mL | 100 mcg per mL |

Protect from light in the original vial in a carton and store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
The unopened vial may be stored for up to 24 hours exposed to indoor lighting outside of the carton.
The drug product is preservative free.
Discard any unused portion.
