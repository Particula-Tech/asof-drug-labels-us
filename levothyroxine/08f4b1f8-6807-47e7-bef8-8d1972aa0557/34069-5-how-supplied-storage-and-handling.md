Protect from light in the original vial in a carton and store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
The unopened vial may be stored for up to 24 hours exposed to indoor lighting outside of the carton.
The drug product is preservative free.
Discard any unused portion.
