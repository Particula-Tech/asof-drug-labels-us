Each carton contains 30 tablets with 2 blister packs.
Each blister pack contains 15 tablets placed in individual cavities.

Store between 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F), with excursions permitted between 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F).
Protect from light and moisture, avoid heat.
Do not separate the individual cavities containing the tablet from the intact blister and do not remove the individual tablets from blister packaging until ready to use.
