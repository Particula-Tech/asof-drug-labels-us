# 11 DESCRIPTION

Levothyroxine sodium tablets USP is L-thyroxine (T4) and contains synthetic crystalline L-3,3',5,5' tetraiodothyronine sodium salt.
Synthetic T4 is chemically identical to that produced in the human thyroid gland.
Levothyroxine (T4) sodium has an empirical formula of C15H10I4N NaO4•H2O, molecular weight of 798.85 (anhydrous), and structural formula as shown:

Levothyroxine sodium tablets USP for oral administration are supplied in the following strengths: 25 mcg, 50 mcg, 75 mcg, 88 mcg, 100 mcg, 112 mcg, 125 mcg, 137 mcg, 150 mcg, 175 mcg, 200 mcg, and 300 mcg.
Each levothyroxine sodium tablets USP contains the inactive ingredients corn starch, croscarmellose sodium, magnesium stearate, mannitol and sodium bicarbonate.
