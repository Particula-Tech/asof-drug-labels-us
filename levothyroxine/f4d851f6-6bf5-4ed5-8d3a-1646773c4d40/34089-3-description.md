# 11 DESCRIPTION

TIROSINT (levothyroxine sodium) capsules for oral use contain synthetic L-3,3',5,5'-tetraiodothyronine sodium salt [levothyroxine (T4) sodium].
Synthetic T4 is chemically identical to that produced in the human thyroid gland.
Levothyroxine (T4) sodium has an empirical formula of C15H10I4NNaO4 • x H2O (where x = 5), molecular weight of 798.86 g/mol (anhydrous), and structural formula as shown:

TIROSINT (levothyroxine sodium) capsules are amber-colored, round/biconvex capsules containing a viscous amber-colored liquid.

The inactive ingredients in TIROSINT are gelatin, glycerin and water.
