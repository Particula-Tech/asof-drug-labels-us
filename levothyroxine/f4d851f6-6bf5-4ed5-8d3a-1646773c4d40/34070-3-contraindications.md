# CONTRAINDICATIONS

Levothyroxine is contraindicated in patients with untreated subclinical (suppressed serum TSH level with normal T3 and T4 levels) or overt thyrotoxicosis of any etiology and in patients with acute myocardial infarction.
Levothyroxine is contraindicated in patients with uncorrected adrenal insufficiency since thyroid hormones may precipitate an acute adrenal crisis by increasing the metabolic clearance of glucocorticoids (see PRECAUTIONS).
TIROSINT is contraindicated in patients with hypersensitivity to any of the inactive ingredients in TIROSINT capsules (See DESCRIPTION, Inactive Ingredients).

TIROSINT is also contraindicated for anyone who may be unable to swallow a capsule (e.g., infants, small children).
