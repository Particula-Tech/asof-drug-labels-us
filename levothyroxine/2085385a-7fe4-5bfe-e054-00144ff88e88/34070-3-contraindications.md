# CONTRAINDICATIONS

Levothyroxine is contraindicated in patients with untreated subclinical (suppressed serum TSH level with normal T 3 and T 4 levels) or overt thyrotoxicosis of any etiology and in patients with acute myocardial infarction.
Levothyroxine is contraindicated in patients with uncorrected adrenal insufficiency since thyroid hormones may precipitate an acute adrenal crisis by increasing the metabolic clearance of glucocorticoids (see PRECAUTIONS).
Levo-T® is contraindicated in patients with hypersensitivity to any of the inactive ingredients in Levo-T® (see DESCRIPTION, Inactive Ingredients.)
