# 11 DESCRIPTION

Levothyroxine Sodium for Injection contains synthetic crystalline levothyroxine (L-thyroxine) sodium salt.

Levothyroxine Sodium for Injection is a sterile, preservative-free lyophilized powder.
Each vial contains: 100 mcg, 200 mcg or 500 mcg of levothyroxine sodium (the active ingredient), and the excipients dibasic sodium phosphate heptahydrate 0.944 mg; mannitol 3.0 mg; and sodium hydroxide for pH adjustment at the range of pH value 9.5-11.5.
