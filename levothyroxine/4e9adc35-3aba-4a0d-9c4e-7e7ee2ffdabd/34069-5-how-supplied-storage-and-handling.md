# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Levothyroxine Sodium for Injection, a lyophilized powder in a single-dose amber-colored vial containing Levothyroxine Sodium available in the following strengths:

| Strength | Reconstituted Concentration | Each |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 100 mcg per vial | 20 mcg per mL | NDC 70511-111-10 One Single-Dose Vial per Carton |
| 200 mcg per vial | 40 mcg per mL | NDC 70511-112-10 One Single-Dose Vial per Carton |
| 500 mcg per vial | 100 mcg per mL | NDC 70511-113-10 One Single-Dose Vial per Carton |

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Protect from light and store dry product at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Reconstituted drug product is preservative free and stable for 4 hours.
Discard any unused portion.

This container closure is not made with natural rubber latex.
