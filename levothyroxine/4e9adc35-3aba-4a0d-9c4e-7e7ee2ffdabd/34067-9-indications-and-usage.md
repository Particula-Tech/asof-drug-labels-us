# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Levothyroxine Sodium for Injection is indicated for the treatment of myxedema coma.
Important Limitations of Use: The relative bioavailability between Levothyroxine Sodium for Injection and oral levothyroxine products has not been established.
Caution should be used when switching patients from oral levothyroxine products to Levothyroxine Sodium for Injection as accurate dosing conversion has not been studied.
