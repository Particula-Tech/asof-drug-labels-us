# 11 DESCRIPTION

TIROSINT-SOL (levothyroxine sodium) oral solution contains synthetic L-3,3',5,5'-tetraiodothyronine sodium salt [levothyroxine (T4) sodium].
Synthetic T4 is chemically identical to that produced in the human thyroid gland.
Levothyroxine (T4) sodium has an empirical formula of C15H10I4NNaO4 ∙ x H2O (where x = 5), molecular weight of 798.86 g/mol (anhydrous), and structural formula as shown:

TIROSINT-SOL oral solution is a clear, colorless to slightly yellow solution supplied in a 1 mL white, non-transparent, unit-dose ampule and is available in the following strengths (mcg/mL): 13, 25, 50, 75, 88, 100, 112, 125, 137, 150, 175, 200.

The inactive ingredients in TIROSINT-SOL are glycerol and water.
