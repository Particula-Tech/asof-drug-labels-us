# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

ARIDOL use is contraindicated in:

- Patients with known hypersensitivity to mannitol or to the gelatin used to make the capsules
- Patients with conditions that may be compromised by induced bronchospasm or repeated spirometry maneuvers.
  Some examples include: aortic or cerebral aneurysm, uncontrolled hypertension, recent myocardial infarction or cerebral vascular accident [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)].
