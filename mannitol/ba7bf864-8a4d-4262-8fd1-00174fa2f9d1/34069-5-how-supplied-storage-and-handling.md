# HOW SUPPLIED

Mannitol Intravenous (Mannitol Injection, USP) is supplied in single-dose containers as follows:

| Unit of Sale | Concentration | Each |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NDC 0409-7715-03 NDC 0990-7715-03 Case containing 12 | 100 g/500 mL (200 mg/mL) | NDC 0409-7715-13 NDC 0990-7715-13 500 mL Flexible Container |
| NDC 0409-7715-02 NDC 0990-7715-02 Case containing 24 | 50 g/250 mL (200 mg/mL) | NDC 0409-7715-12 NDC 0990-7715-12 250 mL Flexible Container |

ICU Medical is transitioning NDC codes from "0409" to "0990" labeler code.
Both NDC codes are expected to be in the market for a period of time.

NOTE: Crystals may form in mannitol solutions especially if the solutions are chilled.
To dissolve crystals in the flexible container, warm the unit to 70°C with agitation.
Heat solution by using a dry-heat cabinet with overwrap intact.
The use of a water bath is not recommended.
Cool to body temperature or less before administering.
When infusing mannitol, the administration set should include a filter.

Protect from freezing.
Store at 20 to 25°C (68 to 77°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]
