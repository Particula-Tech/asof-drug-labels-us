# HOW SUPPLIED

Sorbitol-Mannitol Irrigation is supplied in single-dose 3000 mL flexible irrigation container (NDC No. 0409–7981–08).

Store at 20 to 25°C (68 to 77°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]
Protect from freezing.
