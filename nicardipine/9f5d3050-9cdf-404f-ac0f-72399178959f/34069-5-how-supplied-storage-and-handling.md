# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Nicardipine hydrochloride injection, USP is a sterile, non-pyrogenic, clear, yellow colored solution and is available as follows: 25 mg per 10 mL (2.5 mg/mL): 10 mL Single-Dose Vials in a Carton of 10 NDC 55150-183-11

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Freezing does not adversely affect the product, but avoid exposure to elevated temperatures.
Protect from light.
Store vials in carton until used.
The vial stopper is not made with natural rubber latex.
