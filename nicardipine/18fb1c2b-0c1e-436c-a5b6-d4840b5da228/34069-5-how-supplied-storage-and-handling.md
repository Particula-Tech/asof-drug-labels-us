# 16 HOW SUPPLIED / STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Nicardipine hydrochloride injection is available in packages as follows:

| NDC | Strength | Packaged |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0143-9689-10 | 25 mg /10 mL Single-Use Vial (2.5 mg/mL) | 10 vials of 10 mL |

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at controlled room temperature 20º to 25ºC (68º to 77ºF) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Freezing does not adversely affect the product, but exposure to elevated temperatures should be avoided.
Protect from light.
Store vials in carton until used.
