# HOW SUPPLIED

Nicardipine Hydrochloride Capsules 20 mg are available as hard gelatin capsules with an opaque light green cap and opaque light green body imprinted ‘ANI 205’ in black ink.
They are supplied in bottles of 90 (NDC 62559-205-90).

Nicardipine Hydrochloride Capsules 30 mg are available as hard gelatin capsules with an opaque green cap and opaque light green body imprinted ‘ANI 206’ in black ink.
They are supplied in bottles of 90 (NDC 62559-206-90).

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature] and dispense in light-resistant containers.
