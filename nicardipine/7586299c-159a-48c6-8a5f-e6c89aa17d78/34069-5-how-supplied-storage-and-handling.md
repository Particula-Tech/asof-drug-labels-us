# HOW SUPPLIED

CARDENE® SR 30-mg capsules are available in opaque pink-pink hard gelatin capsules.
The capsule cap is printed with CARDENE SR 30 mg and the capsule body is printed with ROCHE.
These are supplied in bottles of 60 (NDC 0004-0180-22) and bottles of 200 (NDC 0004-0180-91).

CARDENE® SR 45-mg capsules are available in opaque powder blue-powder blue hard gelatin capsules.
The capsule cap is printed with CARDENE SR 45 mg and the capsule body is printed with ROCHE.
These are supplied in bottles of 60 (NDC 0004-0181-22) and bottles of 200 (NDC 0004-0181-91).

CARDENE® SR 60-mg capsules are available in opaque light blue-white hard gelatin capsules.
The capsule cap is printed with CARDENE SR 60 mg and the capsule body is printed with ROCHE.
These are supplied in bottles of 60 (NDC 0004-0182-22).

Store bottles at 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) and dispense in light-resistant containers, such as the manufacturer's original container.
