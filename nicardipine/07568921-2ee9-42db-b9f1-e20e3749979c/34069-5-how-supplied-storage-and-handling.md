(nicardipine hydrochloride) is available in packages of 10 ampuls of 10 mL as follows:

25 mg (2.5 mg/mL), NDC 67286-0812-3.

Store at controlled room temperature 20º to 25ºC (68º to 77ºF).

Freezing does not adversely affect the product, but exposure to elevated temperatures should be avoided.

Protect from light.
Store ampuls in carton until used.
