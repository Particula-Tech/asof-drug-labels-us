# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Store at controlled room temperature, 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F).
PROTECT FROM LIGHT.

Handling and Disposal

Handle and dispose of Otrexup consistent with recommendations for handling and disposal of cytotoxic drugs.1
