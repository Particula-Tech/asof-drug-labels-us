# WARNING: EMBRYO-FETAL TOXICITY, HYPERSENSITIVITY REACTIONS AND SEVERE ADVERSE REACTIONS

- Methotrexate can cause embryo-fetal toxicity, including fetal death.
  For non-neoplastic diseases, Jylamvo is contraindicated in pregnancy.
  For neoplastic diseases, advise females and males of reproductive potential to use effective contraception during and after treatment with Jylamvo [see Contraindications (4), Warnings and Precautions (5.1), Use in Specific Populations (8.1, 8.3)].
- Jylamvo is contraindicated in patients with a history of severe hypersensitivity reactions to methotrexate, including anaphylaxis [Contraindications (4), Warnings and Precautions (5.2)].
- Serious adverse reactions, including death, have been reported with methotrexate.
  Closely monitor for infections and adverse reactions of the bone marrow, gastrointestinal tract, liver, lungs, skin, and kidneys.
  Withhold or discontinue Jylamvo as appropriate [Warnings and Precautions (5.3, 5.4, 5.5, 5.6, 5.7, 5.8, 5.11)].
