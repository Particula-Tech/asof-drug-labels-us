# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

XATMEP is a clear yellow to orange oral solution that contains 2.5 mg of methotrexate per milliliter (equivalent to 2.74 mg of methotrexate sodium/mL).
It is packaged in a high-density polyethylene (HDPE) bottle with a child‑resistant cap and tamper-evident seal.
Each bottle contains 120 mL.

NDC 52652-2001-1

Prior to dispensing to the patient, keep XATMEP refrigerated (2°C to 8°C/36°F to 46°F).

Avoid freezing and excessive heat.
After dispensing, patients may store XATMEP at room temperature (20°C to 25°C/68°F to 77°F) for up to 60 days; excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
