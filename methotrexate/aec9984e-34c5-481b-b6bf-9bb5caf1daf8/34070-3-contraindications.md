# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

XATMEP is contraindicated in the following:

- •Pregnancy in patients with non-malignant diseases.
  XATMEP can cause embryo-fetal toxicity and fetal death when administered during pregnancy [see Warnings and Precautions (5.9), Use in Specific Populations (8.1)].
- •Patients with severe hypersensitivity to methotrexate [see Warnings and Precautions (5.7), Adverse Reactions (6.1, 6.2)].
