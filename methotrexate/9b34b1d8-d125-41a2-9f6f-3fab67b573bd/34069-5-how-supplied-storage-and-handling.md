# HOW SUPPLIED

Methotrexate Tablets USP contain an amount of methotrexate sodium equivalent to 2.5 mg of methotrexate.

2.5 mg: Yellow, oval-shaped, scored tablet.
Debossed with stylized b/572 on one side.
Available in bottles of 36 and 100.

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP, with a child-resistant closure (as required).

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Protect from light.
