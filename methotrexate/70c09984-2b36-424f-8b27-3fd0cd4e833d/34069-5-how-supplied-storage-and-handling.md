# HOW SUPPLIED

Methotrexate Tablets contain an amount of methotrexate sodium equivalent to 2.5 mg of methotrexate and are orange, scored, round tablets debossed with M above the score and 14 below the score and the other side blank.
They are available as follows:

NDC 0378-0014-01 bottles of 100 tablets

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[See USP for Controlled Room Temperature.]

Protect from light.

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP using a child-resistant closure.
