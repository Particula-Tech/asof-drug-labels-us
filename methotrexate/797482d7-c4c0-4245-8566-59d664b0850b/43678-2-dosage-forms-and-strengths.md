# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

RediTrex contains methotrexate in a sterile, preservative-free, solution in a pre-filled syringe (in a needle safety device) with a 29 gauge ½ inch needle for a single subcutaneous injection.
RediTrex solution is yellow in color.
RediTrex is available at a concentration of 25 mg/mL to administer the following doses of methotrexate solution:

- 7.5 mg
- 10 mg
- 15 mg
- 17.5 mg
- 20 mg
- 22.5 mg
- 25 mg
