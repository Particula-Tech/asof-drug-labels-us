Methotrexate is an antimetabolite used in the treatment of certain neoplastic diseases, severe psoriasis, and adult rheumatoid arthritis.

Chemically methotrexate is N-[4-[[(2,4-diamino-6-pteridinyl)methyl]methylamino]benzoyl]-L-glutamic acid.

Methotrexate Tablets for oral administration are available in bottles of 36, bottles of 100, and unit dose cartons of 100 blisters.
Methotrexate Tablets contain an amount of methotrexate sodium equivalent to 2.5 mg of methotrexate and the following inactive ingredients: lactose, magnesium stearate, pregelatinized starch NF, sodium hydroxide, and sodium starch glycolate.
