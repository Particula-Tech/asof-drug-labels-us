Methotrexate is an antimetabolite used in the treatment of certain neoplastic diseases, severe psoriasis, and adult rheumatoid arthritis.

Chemically methotrexate is N-[4-[[(2,4-diamino-6-pteridinyl)methyl]methyl-amino]benzoyl]-L-glutamic acid.

Methotrexate Sodium Tablets USP, for oral administration, are available as 2.5 mg tablets.
Methotrexate Sodium Tablets USP contain an amount of methotrexate sodium equivalent to 2.5 mg of methotrexate and the following inactive ingredients: lactose, magnesium stearate, pregelatinized starch NF, sodium hydroxide, and sodium starch glycolate.
