# HOW SUPPLIED

Methotrexate Tablets USP 2.5 mg (contain an amount of methotrexate sodium equivalent to 2.5 mg of methotrexate)

Yellow, round, scored tablets (Identified 54 323)

NDC 0054-8550-25: Unit dose, 10 tablets per strip, 10 strips per shelf pack, 10 shelf packs per shipper.

NDC 0054-4550-15: Bottles of 36 tablets.

NDC 0054-4550-25: Bottles of 100 tablets.

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

PROTECT FROM LIGHT.

Dispense with a child-resistant closure in a well-closed container as defined in the USP/NF.
