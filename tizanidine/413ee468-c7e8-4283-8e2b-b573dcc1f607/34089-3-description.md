# DESCRIPTION

Zanaflex® (tizanidine hydrochloride) is a centrally acting α2-adrenergic agonist.
Tizanidine HCl (tizanidine) is a white to off-white, fine crystalline powder, which is odorless or with a faint characteristic odor.
Tizanidine is slightly soluble in water and methanol; solubility in water decreases as the pH increases.
Its chemical name is 5-chloro-4-(2-imidazolin-2-ylamino)-2,1,3-benzothiodiazole hydrochloride.

Zanaflex Capsules® are supplied as 2, 4, and 6 mg capsules and Zanaflex® tablets are supplied as 4 mg tablets for oral administration.
Zanaflex Capsules® are composed of the active ingredient, tizanidine hydrochloride (2.29 mg equivalent to 2 mg tizanidine base, 4.58 mg equivalent to 4 mg tizanidine base, and 6.87 mg equivalent to 6 mg tizanidine base), and the inactive ingredients, hydroxypropyl methyl cellulose, silicon dioxide, sugar spheres, titanium dioxide, gelatin, and colorants.

Zanaflex® tablets are composed of the active ingredient, tizanidine hydrochloride (4.58 mg equivalent to 4 mg tizanidine base), and the inactive ingredients, silicon dioxide colloidal, stearic acid, microcrystalline cellulose and anhydrous lactose.
