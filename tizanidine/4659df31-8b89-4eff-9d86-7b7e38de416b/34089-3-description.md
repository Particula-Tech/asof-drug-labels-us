# DESCRIPTION

Tizanidine hydrochloride is a centrally acting α2-adrenergic agonist.
Tizanidine HCl (tizanidine) is a white to off-white, fine crystalline powder, odorless or with a faint characteristic odor.
Tizanidine is slightly soluble in water and methanol; solubility in water decreases as the pH increases.
Its chemical name is 5-chloro-4-(2-imidazolin- 2-ylamino)-2,1,3-benzothiodiazole hydrochloride.

Tizanidine hydrochloride tablets are supplied as 2 mg and 4 mg tablets for oral administration.
Tizanidine hydrochloride tablets are composed of the active ingredient, tizanidine hydrochloride (2.288 mg equivalent to 2 mg tizanidine base and 4.576 mg equivalent to 4 mg tizanidine base), and the inactive ingredients, silicon dioxide colloidal, stearic acid, microcrystalline cellulose, lactose monohydrate and anhydrous lactose.
