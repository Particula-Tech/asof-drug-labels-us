4 mg Bottles of 30 NDC 57664-503-83 4 mg Bottles of 100 NDC 57664-503-88 4 mg Bottles of 150 NDC 57664-503-89 4 mg Bottles of 500 NDC 57664-503-13 4 mg Bottles of 1000 NDC 57664-503-18
