# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.2 Tizanidine Tablets, USP

Tizanidine Tablets, USP 2 mg are available as; white to off-white, round, flat beveled, uncoated tablets debossed with product code “502” on one side, and bisecting score on the other.

2 mg Bottles of 150 NDC 57664-502-89

2 mg Bottles of 1000 NDC 57664-502-18

Tizanidine Tablets, USP 4 mg are available as; white to off-white, round, flat beveled, uncoated tablets debossed with product code “503” on one side, and quadrisecting score on the other.

4 mg Bottles of 150 NDC 57664-503-89

4 mg Bottles of 1000 NDC 57664-503-18

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Dispense in containers with child resistant closure.
