# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Ontralfy is contraindicated in patients:

- taking strong CYP1A2 inhibitors [see Drug Interactions (7.1)].
- with a history of hypersensitivity to tizanidine or the ingredients in Ontralfy.
  Symptoms have included anaphylaxis and angioedema [see Warnings and Precautions (5.5)]
