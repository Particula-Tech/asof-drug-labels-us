# DESCRIPTION

Tizanidine hydrochloride is a centrally acting α2-adrenergic agonist.
Tizanidine HCl (tizanidine) is a white to off-white, fine crystalline powder, which is odorless or with a faint characteristic odor.
Tizanidine is slightly soluble in water and methanol; solubility in water decreases as the pH increases.
Its chemical name is 5-chloro-4-(2-imidazolin-2-ylamino)-2,1,3-benzothiodiazole hydrochloride.

Tizanidine tablets USP are supplied as 2 and 4 mg tablets for oral administration.

Tizanidine tablets USP are composed of the active ingredient, tizanidine hydrochloride USP (2.288 mg equivalent to 2 mg tizanidine base and 4.576 mg equivalent to 4 mg tizanidine base), and the inactive ingredients, colloidal silicon dioxide, stearic acid, microcrystalline cellulose and anhydrous lactose.
