# 11 DESCRIPTION

Tizanidine Tablets contains tizanidine hydrochloride as the active ingredient, which is a central alpha2-adrenergic agonist.
Its chemical name is 5-chloro-4-(2-imidazolin-2-ylamino)-2,1,3- benzothiadiazole monohydrochloride.
It has a molecular formula of C9H8ClN5S-HCl and a molecular weight of 290.2.

Tizanidine hydrochloride is a white to off-white, fine crystalline powder, which is odorless or with a faint characteristic odor.
Tizanidine hydrochloride is slightly soluble in water and methanol; solubility in water decreases as the pH increases

Tizanidine tablets, are for oral administration and contain 2 or 4 mg tizanidine (equivalent to 2.288 mg and 4.576 mg tizanidine hydrochloride, respectively), and the inactive ingredients anhydrous lactose, colloidal silicon dioxide, microcrystalline cellulose, and stearic acid.
