# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Tizanidine is contraindicated in patients: taking strong CYP1A2 inhibitors [see Drug Interactions (7.1)]. with a history of hypersensitivity to tizanidine or the ingredients in Tizanidine.
Symptoms have included anaphylaxis and angioedema [see Warnings and Precautions (5.5)].
