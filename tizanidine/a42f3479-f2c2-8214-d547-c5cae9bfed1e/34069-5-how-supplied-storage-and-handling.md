# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.2 Tizanidine Tablets

Tizanidine Tablets USP, 2 mg are white to off white, oval, flat, beveled edged tablets debossed with “R179” on one side and “bisecting score” on other side.
The tablets are available in bottles of 30, 150, 300 and 1000.

Bottles of 30 NDC 55111-179-30

Bottles of 150 NDC 55111-179-15

Bottles of 300 NDC 55111-179-03

Bottles of 1000 NDC 55111-179-10

Tizanidine Tablets USP, 4 mg are white to off white, oval, flat, beveled edged tablets debossed with “R180” on one side and “quadrisecting score” on other side.
The tablets are available in bottles of 30, 150, 300 and 1000.

Bottles of 30 NDC 55111-180-30

Bottles of 150 NDC 55111-180-15

Bottles of 300 NDC 55111-180-03

Bottles of 1000 NDC 55111-180-10

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15-30°C (59-86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Dispense in containers with child resistant closure.
