# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Ezetimibe tablets, USP 10 mg are white to off white, capsule shaped, flat faced with beveled edge, uncoated tablets, debossed with "10" on one side and plain on other side.
They are supplied as follows:

NDC 16729-433-10 bottles of 30 NDC 16729-433-15 bottles of 90 NDC 16729-433-01 bottles of 100 NDC 16729-433-16 bottles of 500 NDC 16729-433-17 bottles of 1,000.

Storage

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15 to 30°C (59 to 86°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]
Protect from moisture.
