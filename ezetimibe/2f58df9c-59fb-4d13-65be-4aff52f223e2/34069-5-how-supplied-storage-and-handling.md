Storage

Store at 20-25°C (68-77°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]
Keep container tightly closed.

Storage of 10,000, 5000, and 2500 count bottles

Store bottle of 10,000 VYTORIN 10/10 and 10/20, 5000 VYTORIN 10/40, and 2500 VYTORIN 10/80 capsule-shaped tablets at 20-25°C (68-77°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]
Store in original container until time of use.
When product container is subdivided, repackage into a tightly-closed, light-resistant container.
Entire contents must be repackaged immediately upon opening.
