# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Each sofosbuvir and velpatasvir tablet contains 400 mg of sofosbuvir and 100 mg of velpatasvir, is pink, diamond-shaped, film-coated, debossed with "ASE" on one side and "9761" on the other.
Each carton contains 28 tablets (2 blister cards each containing 14 tablets) (NDC 72626-2701-1).

Store below 30 °C (86 ºF).
