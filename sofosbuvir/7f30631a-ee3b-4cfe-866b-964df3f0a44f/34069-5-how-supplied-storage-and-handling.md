# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Each EPCLUSA tablet contains 400 mg of sofosbuvir and 100 mg of velpatasvir, is pink, diamond-shaped, film-coated, debossed with "GSI" on one side and "7916" on the other.
Each bottle contains 28 tablets (NDC 61958-2201-1), polyester coil, and is closed with a child-resistant closure.

Store below 30 °C (86 °F).
Dispense only in original container.
