# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

SOVALDI tablets are yellow, capsule-shaped, film-coated tablets containing 400 mg sofosbuvir debossed with "GSI" on one side and "7977" on the other side.
Each bottle contains 28 tablets (NDC 61958-1501-1), a silica gel desiccant, polyester coil and is closed with a child-resistant closure.

Store at room temperature below 30 °C (86 °F).

- Dispense only in original container
- Do not use if seal over bottle opening is broken or missing
