# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Ledipasvir and sofosbuvir tablets (90 mg/400 mg) are orange, diamond-shaped, film-coated, debossed with "ASE" on one side and "9875" on the other side of the tablet.
Each blister pack contains 28 tablets (2 blister cards each containing 14 tablets) (NDC 72626-2601-1).

Store at room temperature below 30 °C (86 °F).
