# POST MARKETING EXPERIENCE

The following serious adverse reactions have been reported since approval and use of zonisamide worldwide.
These reactions are reported voluntarily from a population of uncertain size; therefore, it is not possible to estimate their frequency or establish a causal relationship to drug exposure.

Acute pancreatitis, rhabdomyolysis, increased creatine phosphokinase, drug reaction with eosinophilia and systemic symptoms (DRESS), acute myopia and secondary angle closure glaucoma, and hyperammonemia and encephalopathy (see WARNINGS).

To report SUSPECTED ADVERSE REACTIONS, contact Exelan Pharmaceuticals at 1-866-604-3268 or the FDA at 1-800-FDA-1088 or www.fda.gov/medwatch.
