# DESCRIPTION

Zonisamide USP is an antiseizure drug chemically classified as a sulfonamide and unrelated to other antiseizure agents.
The active ingredient is zonisamide USP, 1,2-benzisoxazole-3-methanesulfonamide.
The empirical formula is C8H8N2O3S with a molecular weight of 212.23.
Zonisamide USP is a white powder, pKa = 10.2, and is moderately soluble in water (0.80 mg/mL) and 0.1 N HCl (0.50 mg/mL).
