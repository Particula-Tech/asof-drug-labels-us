# HOW SUPPLIED

ZONEGRAN is available as 25 mg and 100 mg two-piece hard gelatin capsules.
The capsules are printed in black with "Eisai" and "ZONEGRAN 25" or "ZONEGRAN 100," respectively.
ZONEGRAN is available in bottles of 100 with strengths and colors as follows:

| Dosage Strength | Capsule Colors | NDC # |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 25 mg | White opaque body with white opaque cap. | 62856-681-10 |
| 100 mg | White opaque body with red opaque cap. | 62856-680-10 |

Store at 25°C (77°F), excursions permitted to 15-30°C (59-86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature], in a dry place and protected from light.
