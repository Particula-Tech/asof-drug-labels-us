# HOW SUPPLIED

Zonisamide capsules are available as 25 mg, 50 mg and 100 mg two-piece hard gelatin capsules.
The capsules are printed in black with product code on cap and body “258”, “259” and “260”, respectively.
Zonisamide capsules are available in bottles of 30, 100, 500 and 1000 with strengths and colors as follows:
