# DESCRIPTION

Zonisamide is an antiseizure drug chemically classified as a sulfonamide and unrelated to other antiseizure agents.
The active ingredient is zonisamide, 1,2-benzisoxazole-3-methanesulfonamide.
The molecular formula is C8H8N2O3S with a molecular weight of 212.23.
Zonisamide is a white to off white crystalline powder, pKa=10.2, and is moderately soluble in water (0.80 mg/mL) and 0.1 N HCl (0.50 mg/mL).

Zonisamide capsules contain 25 mg or 50 mg or 100 mg zonisamide.

The capsule is printed with black pharmaceutical ink.
