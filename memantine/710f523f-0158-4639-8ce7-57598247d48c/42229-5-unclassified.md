# 9 DRUG ABUSE AND DEPENDENCE

Memantine is not a controlled substance.
Memantine is a low to moderate affinity uncompetitive NMDA antagonist that did not produce any evidence of drug-seeking behavior or withdrawal symptoms upon discontinuation in 3,254 patients who participated in clinical trials at therapeutic doses.
Post marketing data, outside the U.S., retrospectively collected, has provided no evidence of drug abuse or dependence.
