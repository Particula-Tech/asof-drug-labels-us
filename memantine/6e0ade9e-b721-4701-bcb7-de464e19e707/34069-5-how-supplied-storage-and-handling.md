# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Memantine hydrochloride tablets, USP 5 mg are tan, capsule-shaped, film-coated tablets with "12" debossed on one side and "832" on the other side.
They are supplied as follows:

1. Bottle of 60, NDC 0832-1112-60

Memantine hydrochloride tablets, USP 10 mg are gray, capsule-shaped, film-coated tablets with "13" debossed on one side and "832" on the other side.
They are supplied as follows:

1. Bottle of 60, NDC 0832-1113-60

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
