# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Memantine hydrochloride tablets 5 mg: capsule-shaped, film-coated tablets are tan, with the strength “5” debossed on one side and “FL” on the other side.

Memantine hydrochloride tablets 10 mg: capsule-shaped, film-coated tablets are gray, with the strength “10” debossed on one side and “FL” on the other side.
