# WARNING: CIGARETTE SMOKING AND SERIOUS CARDIOVASCULAR EVENTS

Cigarette smoking increases the risk of serious cardiovascular events from combination hormonal contraceptive (CHC) use.
This risk increases with age, particularly in women over 35 years of age, and with the number of cigarettes smoked.
For this reason, CHCs, including etonogestrel and ethinyl estradiol vaginal ring, should not be used by women who are over 35 years of age and smoke.
[See Contraindications (4).]

What is the most important information I should know about etonogestrel and ethinyl estradiol vaginal ring?

Do not use etonogestrel and ethinyl estradiol vaginal ring if you smoke cigarettes and are over 35 years old.
Smoking increases your risk of serious cardiovascular side effects (heart and blood vessel problems) from combination hormonal contraceptives (CHCs), including death from heart attack, blood clots or stroke.
This risk increases with age and the number of cigarettes you smoke.
