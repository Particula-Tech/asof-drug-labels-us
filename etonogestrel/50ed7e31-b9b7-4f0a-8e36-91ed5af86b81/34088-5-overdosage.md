# OVERDOSAGE

Insertion of multiple rods has been reported.
Overdosage may result if more than one IMPLANON™ (etonogestrel implant) rod is in place.
In case of suspected overdose, IMPLANON™ should be removed.
It is important to remove the IMPLANON™ rod or other contraceptive implant(s) before inserting a new IMPLANON™ rod.
