# HOW SUPPLIED

One IMPLANON™ (etonogestrel implant) package consists of a single rod implant containing 68 mg etonogestrel that is 4 cm in length and 2 mm in diameter.
IMPLANON™ is pre-loaded in the needle of a disposable applicator.
The applicator consists of acrylonitrile-butadiene-styrene body with a stainless steel needle and a polypropylene shield.
The sterile applicator containing IMPLANON™ is packed in a blister pack.

NDC 0052-0272-01

## STORAGE

Store IMPLANON™ (etonogestrel implant) at 25°C (77° F); excursions permitted to 15–30°C (59–86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light.
Avoid storing IMPLANON™ in direct sunlight or at temperatures above 30°C (86° F).
