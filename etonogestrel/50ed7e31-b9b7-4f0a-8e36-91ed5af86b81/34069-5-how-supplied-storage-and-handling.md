# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

One IMPLANON package consists of a single implant containing 68 mg etonogestrel that is 4 cm in length and 2 mm in diameter, which is pre-loaded in the needle of a disposable applicator.
The sterile applicator containing the implant is packed in a blister pack.

NDC 0052-0272-01

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store IMPLANON (etonogestrel implant) at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15°-30°C (59°-86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light.
Avoid storing IMPLANON in direct sunlight or at temperatures above 30°C (86°F).
