# Pharmacodynamics

Duration of analgesia was evaluated using a pinprick test in 40 adult volunteers.
The median duration of analgesia was 11 hours.
There was no difference between the 30- minute and 60-minute PLIAGLIS application periods with respect to the mean for time to return of sensation.
However, 55% of PLIAGLIS treated subjects still reported diminished sensation at the end of the 13-hour study period.
