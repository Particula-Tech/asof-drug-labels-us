# HOW SUPPLIED

Lidocaine Ointment USP, 5% is available in:

30 g tubes with a child resistant cap (NDC 51672-3008-2)

50 g jar for Health/Professional Institutional use only, not for household use (NDC 51672-3008-3)

NOTE - JARS ARE FILLED BY WEIGHT NOT VOLUME.
KEEP CONTAINER CLOSED AT ALL TIMES WHEN NOT IN USE.

Store at controlled room temperature 15° - 30°C (59° - 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from freezing.
