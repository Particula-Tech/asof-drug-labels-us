# CONTRAINDICATIONS

Hypersensitivity reactions, including anaphylactic reactions, have been reported with lidocaine.
Lidocaine hydrochloride is contraindicated in patients with a history of hypersensitivity to local anesthetics of the amide type.

Lidocaine is contraindicated in patients with Stokes-Adams syndrome, Wolff-Parkinson-White syndrome, or with severe degrees of sinoatrial, atrioventricular, or intraventricular block.
