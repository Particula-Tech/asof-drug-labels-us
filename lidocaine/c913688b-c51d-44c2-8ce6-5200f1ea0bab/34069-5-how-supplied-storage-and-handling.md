# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

NDC 53329-200-57 Zingo™ (lidocaine hydrochloride monohydrate) powder intradermal injection system contains 0.5 mg of sterile lidocaine hydrochloride monohydrate.
Zingo™ is a single-use device packaged in an individual foil/clear pouch placed inside a bubble-wrap sleeve.
Twelve poucheddevices are placed in labeled cartons.

Cartons are stored at controlled room temperature (15–30°C, 59–86°F).
