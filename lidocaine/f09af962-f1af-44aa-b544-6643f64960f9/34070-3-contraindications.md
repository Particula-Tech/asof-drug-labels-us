# CONTRAINDICATIONS

Xylocaine is contraindicated in patients with a known history of hypersensitivity to local anesthetics of the amide type.
Xylocaine should not be used in patients with Stokes-Adams syndrome, Wolff-Parkinson-White syndrome, or with severe degrees of sinoatrial, atrioventricular, or intraventricular block in the absence of an artificial pacemaker.
