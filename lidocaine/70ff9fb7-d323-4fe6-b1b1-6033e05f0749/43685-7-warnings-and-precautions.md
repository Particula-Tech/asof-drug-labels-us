# 5 WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS

## 5.1 For Topical Ophthalmic Use

AKTEN is not for injection or intraocular administration.

## 5.2 Corneal Injury Due to Insensitivity

Patients should not touch the eye for at least 10 to 20 minutes after using AKTEN as accidental injuries can occur due to insensitivity of the eye.

## 5.3 Corneal Opacification

Prolonged use of a topical ocular anesthetic may produce permanent corneal opacification and ulceration with accompanying visual loss.

## 5.4 For Administration by Healthcare Provider

AKTEN is indicated for administration under the direct supervision of a healthcare provider.

AKTEN is not intended for patient self-administration.
