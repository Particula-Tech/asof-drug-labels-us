# INDICATIONS AND USAGE

4% Xylocaine-MPF (lidocaine HCl) Sterile Solution is indicated for the production of topical anesthesia of the mucous membranes of the respiratory tract or the genitourinary tract.
It may be injected transtracheally to anesthetize the larynx and trachea, and it may be administered by retrobulbar injection to provide anesthesia for ophthalmic surgery.
