# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Lidocaine Hydrochloride Injection, USP is a clear and colorless solution available as:

Preservative-Free

1% (50 mg/5 mL) (10 mg/mL): 5 mL Single-Dose Vials packaged in 25s (NDC 0143-9595-25)

2% (100 mg/5 mL) (20 mg/mL): 5 mL Single-Dose Vials packaged in 25s (NDC 0143-9594-25)

Storage: Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Discard unused portion.
