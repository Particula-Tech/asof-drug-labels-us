# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

BONDLIDO (lidocaine topical system) 10% is a rectangular topical system with blue printing on one side and release liner on other.
It is available as the following:

Carton of 28 topical systems, packaged into individual child-resistant pouches.

NDC 83708-111-28

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light.
