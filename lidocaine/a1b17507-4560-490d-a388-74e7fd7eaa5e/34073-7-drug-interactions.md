# 7 DRUG INTERACTIONS

## 7.1 Antiarrhythmic Drugs

When ZTLIDO is used in patients receiving Class I antiarrhythmic drugs (such as tocainide and mexiletine), the toxic effects are additive and potentially synergistic.
Consider risk/benefit during concomitant use.

## 7.2 Local Anesthetics

When ZTLIDO is used concomitantly with other products containing local anesthetic agents, the effects are additive.
Consider the amount of drug absorbed from all formulations when local anesthetic agents are administered concomitantly.
