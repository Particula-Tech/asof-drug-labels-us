# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Paricalcitol capsules are available as 1 mcg, 2 mcg, and 4 mcg capsules.
Paricalcitol Capsules 1 mcg are gray colored opaque, oval shaped soft gelatin capsule imprinted as “1” with black edible ink containing clear colorless to pale yellow colored oily liquid.
Bottles of 30 NDC 65862-936-30 Paricalcitol Capsules 2 mcg are orange brown colored opaque oval shaped soft gelatin capsule imprinted as “P2” with black edible ink containing clear colorless to pale yellow colored oily liquid.
Bottles of 30 NDC 65862-937-30 Paricalcitol Capsules 4 mcg are gold colored opaque, oval shaped soft gelatin capsule imprinted as “P4” with black edible ink containing clear colorless to pale yellow colored oily liquid.
Bottles of 30 NDC 65862-938-30 Storage Store paricalcitol capsules at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
