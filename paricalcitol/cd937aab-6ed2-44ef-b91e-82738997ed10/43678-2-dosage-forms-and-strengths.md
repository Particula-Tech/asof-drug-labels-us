# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Paricalcitol capsules are available as 1 mcg, 2 mcg, and 4 mcg soft gelatin capsules.

- 1 mcg: oval, gray soft gelatin capsules imprinted with “A8”
- 2 mcg: oval, brown soft gelatin capsules imprinted with “A83”
- 4 mcg: oval, light yellow soft gelatin capsules imprinted with “A84”
