# 7 DRUG INTERACTIONS

## 7.1 Strong CYP3A Inhibitors

Paricalcitol is partially metabolized by CYP3A.
Paricalcitol blood levels will be increased when paricalcitol is co-administered with strong CYP3A inhibitors.
If a patient initiates or discontinues therapy with a strong CYP3A inhibitor, monitor both PTH and serum calcium more frequently and adjust Paricalcitol Injection dose as required [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].
