Storage

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Excursions permitted between 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F).
Discard unused portion of the single-dose vial.
The opened (in use) multiple-dose vial should be stored at room temperature 20°C to 25°C (68° to 77°F) and protected from light.
Discard seven days after opening.
