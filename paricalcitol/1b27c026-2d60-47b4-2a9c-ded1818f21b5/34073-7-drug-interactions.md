# 7 DRUG INTERACTIONS

Since paricalcitol is partially metabolized by CYP3A, exposure of paricalcitol will increase while dosing paricalcitol with strong CYP3A inhibitors including ketoconazole, atazanavir, clarithromycin, indinavir, itraconazole, nefazodone, nelfinavir, ritonavir, saquinavir, telithromycin or voriconazole.
Dose adjustment of Zemplar Capsules may be required, and iPTH and serum calcium concentrations should be closely monitored if a patient initiates or discontinues therapy with a strong CYP3A4 inhibitor [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].

Drugs that impair intestinal absorption of fat-soluble vitamins, such as cholestyramine, may interfere with the absorption of Zemplar Capsules.
