# HOW SUPPLIED

Paricalcitol injection, USP is available as a sterile, clear, colorless, aqueous solution for intravenous injection.
It is available as follows:

Each 1 mL single-dose vial contains 2 mcg of paricalcitol, USP, NDC 70121-1033-1; carton of 25 vials (NDC 70121-1033-5), carton of 10 vials (NDC 70121-1033-7)

Each 1 mL single-dose vial contains 5 mcg of paricalcitol, USP, NDC 70121-1034-1; carton of 25 vials (NDC 70121-1034-5), carton of 10 vials (NDC 70121-1034-7)

Each 2 mL single-dose vial contains 10 mcg of paricalcitol, USP, NDC 70121-1035-1; carton of 25 vials (NDC 70121-1035-5), carton of 10 vials (NDC 70121-1035-7)

Each 2 mL multi-dose vial contains 10 mcg of paricalcitol, USP, NDC 70121-1036-1; carton of 25 vials (NDC 70121-1036-5), carton of 10 vials (NDC 70121-1036-7)

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
