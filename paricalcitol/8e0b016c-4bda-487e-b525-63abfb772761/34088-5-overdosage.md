# 10 OVERDOSAGE

Overdosage of paricalcitol may lead to hypercalcemia, hypercalciuria, and hyperphosphatemia [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].

The treatment of acute overdosage should consist of supportive measures and discontinuation of drug administration.
Serum calcium levels should be measured until normal.

Paricalcitol is not significantly removed by dialysis.
