# 16 HOW SUPPLIED

Alymsys (bevacizumab-maly) injection is a clear to slightly opalescent, colorless to pale brown, sterile solution for intravenous infusion supplied in a carton containing single-dose vial in the following strengths and packaging configurations:

- 100 mg/4 mL (25 mg/mL): carton of one vial (NDC 85006-1754-1).
- 400 mg/16 mL (25 mg/mL): carton of one vial (NDC 85006-1755-1).

Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) in the original carton until time of use to protect from light.
Do not freeze or shake the vial or carton.
