# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

## 4.1 Ocular or Periocular Infections

LYTENAVA is contraindicated in patients with ocular or periocular infections.

## 4.2 Active Intraocular Inflammation

LYTENAVA is contraindicated in patients with active intraocular inflammation.

## 4.3 Hypersensitivity

LYTENAVA is contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity to bevacizumab products or any of the ingredients in LYTENAVA.
Hypersensitivity reactions may manifest as severe intraocular inflammation.
