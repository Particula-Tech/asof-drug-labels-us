# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

Advise patients that in the days following LYTENAVA administration, patients are at risk of developing endophthalmitis, retinal detachment, and intraocular inflammation.
If the eye becomes red, sensitive to light, painful, or develops a change in vision, advise the patient to seek immediate care from an ophthalmologist [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1, 5.3)].

Patients may experience temporary visual disturbances after an intravitreal injection with LYTENAVA and the associated eye examinations [see Adverse Reactions (6)].
Advise patients not to drive or use machinery until visual function has recovered sufficiently.
