# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

VEGZELMA (bevacizumab-adcd) injection is a clear to opalescent, colorless to pale brown, sterile solution for intravenous infusion supplied in a single-dose vial packaged within cartons in the following strengths and packaging configurations:

- 100 mg/4 mL (25 mg/mL): carton of one vial (NDC 72606-011-01); carton of 10 vials (NDC 72606-011-10).
- 400 mg/16 mL (25 mg/mL): carton of one vial (NDC 72606-012-01); carton of 10 vials (NDC 72606-012-10).

Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) in the original carton until time of use to protect from light.
Do not freeze or shake the vial or carton.
