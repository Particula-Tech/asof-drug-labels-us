# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

MVASI (bevacizumab-awwb) injection is a clear to slightly opalescent, colorless to pale yellow, sterile solution for intravenous infusion supplied as single-dose vials in the following strengths: 100 mg/4 mL (NDC 55513-206-01) and 400 mg/16 mL (NDC 55513-207-01).
Each carton contains one vial.

Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) in the original carton until time of use to protect from light.
Do not freeze or shake the vial or carton.
