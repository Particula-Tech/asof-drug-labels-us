# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

- Varenicline tablets, 0.5 mg are white to off-white, capsule shaped, biconvex, film-coated tablets, debossed with "ZV1" on one side and plain on other side.
- Varenicline tablets, 1 mg are light blue to blue, capsule shaped, biconvex, film-coated tablets, debossed with "ZV2" on one side and plain on other side.
