# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Varenicline tablets is supplied for oral administration in two strengths: a 0.5 mg, white to off-white, capsule shaped, biconvex, film coated tablet, debossed with 'Ml' on one side and plain on other side and a 1 mg, light blue to blue, capsule shaped, biconvex, film coated tablet, debossed with 'M2' on one side and plain on other side.

Varenicline tablets is supplied in the following package configurations:

|  | Description | NDC |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starting Month Box: | 0.5 mg x 11 tablets and 1 mg x 42 Tablets | NDC 85742-017-17 |
| Starting 4-week card: | 0.5 mg × 11 tablets and 1 mg × 42 tablets | NDC 85742-017-17 |
| Continuing Month Box: | 1 mg x 56 tablets | NDC 85742-016-16 |
| Continuing 4-week card: | 1 mg × 56 tablets | NDC 85742-016-16 |
| Bottles | 0.5 mg - bottle of 56 tablets | NDC 85742-015-15 |
| 1 mg - bottle of 56 tablets | NDC 85742-016-15 |  |

Store at 25ºC (77ºF); excursions permitted to 15–30ºC (59–86ºF) (see USP Controlled Room Temperature).
