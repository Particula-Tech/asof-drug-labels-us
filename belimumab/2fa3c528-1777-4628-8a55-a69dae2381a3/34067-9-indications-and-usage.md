# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

BENLYSTA ® (belimumab) is indicated for the treatment of adult patients with active, autoantibody-positive, systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE) who are receiving standard therapy.

Limitations of Use

The efficacy of BENLYSTA has not been evaluated in patients with severe active lupus nephritis or severe active central nervous system lupus.
BENLYSTA has not been studied in combination with other biologics or intravenous cyclophosphamide.
Use of BENLYSTA is not recommended in these situations.
