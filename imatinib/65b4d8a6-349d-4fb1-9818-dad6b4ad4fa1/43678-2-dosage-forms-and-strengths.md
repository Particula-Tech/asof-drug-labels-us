# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Imatinib Mesylate Tablets, 400 mg film-coated tablets: Light orange colored film-coated tablets, capsule shaped, biconvex with beveled edges debossed with “ CE” and “ 139” on either side of the break line and plain on the other side.
