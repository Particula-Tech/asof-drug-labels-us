# HOW SUPPLIED

Neomycin and Polymyxin B Sulfates and Dexamethasone Ophthalmic Suspension is supplied as a sterile ophthalmic suspension in a white, low density polyethylene dispenser with a pink, polypropylene cap as follows:

5 mL in 8 mL bottle NDC 69918-515-05

Storage: Store upright at 8°C to 27°C (46°F to 80°F).

After opening, Neomycin and Polymyxin B Sulfates and Dexamethasone Ophthalmic Suspension can be used until the expiration date on the bottle.
