Each syringe contains sterile lyophilized microspheres of leuprolide acetate incorporated in a biodegradable lactic acid/glycolic acid copolymer.
When mixed with 1 milliliter of accompanying diluent, LUPRON DEPOT-PED is administered as a single intramuscular injection.

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15–30°C (59–86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature]
