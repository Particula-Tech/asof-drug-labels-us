# 14 CLINICAL STUDIES

## 14.1 LUPRON DEPOT-PED–1 Month

In children with central precocious puberty (CPP), therapeutic doses of LUPRON DEPOT-PED reduce stimulated and basal gonadotropins to prepubertal levels.
Testosterone and estradiol are also reduced to prepubertal levels in males and females respectively.
Reduction of gonadotropins and sex steroids allow a return to age-appropriate physical and psychological growth and development.
The following effects have been noted with the chronic administration of leuprolide: cessation of menses (in girls), normalization and stabilization of linear growth and bone age advancement, stabilization of clinical signs and symptoms of puberty.

In a study of 22 children with central precocious puberty, doses of LUPRON DEPOT-PED were given every 4 weeks and plasma levels were determined according to weight categories as summarized below:
