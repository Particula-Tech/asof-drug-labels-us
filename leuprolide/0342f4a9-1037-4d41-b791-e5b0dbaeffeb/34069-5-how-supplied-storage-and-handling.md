# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE HANDLING

## 16.1 How supplied

ELIGARD® is available in a single use kit of a two syringe-mixing system in the following strengths:

ELIGARD® 7.5 mg – NDC 62935-752-75 ELIGARD® 22.5 mg – NDC 62935-222-05 ELIGARD® 30 mg – NDC 62935-302-30 ELIGARD® 45 mg – NDC 62935-452-45

## 16.2 Storage:

Store at 2 - 8 °C (35.6 – 46.4 °F)
