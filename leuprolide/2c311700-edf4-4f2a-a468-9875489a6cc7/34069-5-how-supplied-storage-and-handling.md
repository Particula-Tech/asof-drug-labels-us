# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

For injectable suspension, 45 mg of leuprolide acetate (NDC 62935-163-60) supplied in a carton that contains:

- Tray containing pre-connected syringe system and desiccant pack
- Prescribing information
- Sterile safety needle and cap (located under the tray in carton)

Store refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F).

Once outside the refrigerator, this product may be stored in its original packaging at room temperature 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) for up to eight weeks prior to reconstitution and administration.
