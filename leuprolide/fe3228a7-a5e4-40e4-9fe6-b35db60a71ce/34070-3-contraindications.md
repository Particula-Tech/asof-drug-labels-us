# 4. CONTRAINDICATIONS

## 4.1 Hypersensitivity

ELIGARD® is contraindicated in patients with hypersensitivity to GnRH, GnRH agonist analogs or any of the components of ELIGARD®.
Anaphylactic reactions to synthetic GnRH or GnRH agonist analogs have been reported in the literature.

## 4.2. Pregnancy

ELIGARD may cause fetal harm when administered to a pregnant woman.
Expected hormonal changes that occur with ELIGARD treatment increase the risk for pregnancy loss and fetal harm when administered to a pregnant women [see Use in Specific Populations (8.1)].
ELIGARD is contraindicated in women who are or may become pregnant.
If this drug is used during pregnancy, or if the patient becomes pregnant while taking this drug, the patient should be apprised of the potential hazard to the fetus.
