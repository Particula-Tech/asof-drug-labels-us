# HOW SUPPLIED

Leuprolide acetate injection is a sterile solution supplied in a 2.8 mL multiple- dose vial.
The vial is packaged as follows: 14 Day Patient Administration Kit with 14 disposable syringes and 28 alcohol swabs, NDC 42023-259-00 and vial carton, NDC 42023-259-01.

Store below 77°F (25°C).
Do not freeze.
Protect from light; store vial in carton until use.
