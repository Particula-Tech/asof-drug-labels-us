# 16HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Butalbital, Acetaminophen, Caffeine, and Codeine Phosphate Capsules are dark blue, opaque cap with a gray, opaque body, and body is imprinted with “B 073” in black ink.

Bottles of 100 (NDC 79739-6670-1)

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]; and Dispense in a tight, light resistant container.

Store Butalbital, Acetaminophen, Caffeine, and Codeine Phosphate Capsules securely and dispose of properly [see Patient Counseling Information (17)].

Rx only
