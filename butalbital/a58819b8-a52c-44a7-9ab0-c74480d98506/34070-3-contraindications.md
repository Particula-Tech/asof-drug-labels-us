# CONTRAINDICATIONS

Butalbital, Acetaminophen, Caffeine, and Codeine Phosphate Capsules, USP is contraindicated under the following conditions:

- Post-operative pain management in children who have undergone tonsillectomy and/or adenoidectomy.
- Hypersensitivity or intolerance to acetaminophen, caffeine, butalbital, or codeine.
- Patients with porphyria.
