# HOW SUPPLIED

Butalbital and acetaminophen tablets, each tablet of which contains butalbital 50 mg (Warning: May be habit-forming) and acetaminophen 325 mg, are supplied in bottles of 100 tablets.
Tablets are uncoated, white, capsule-shaped and are debossed “MIA/106”.

## Storage

Protect from light and moisture.
Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container with a child-resistant closure.
