# 10 OVERDOSAGE

Reports of overdoses with Oseltamivir Phosphate for Oral Suspension have been received from clinical trials and during postmarketing experience.
In the majority of cases reporting overdose, no adverse reactions were reported.
Adverse reactions reported following overdose were similar in nature to those observed with therapeutic doses of Oseltamivir Phosphate for Oral Suspension [see Adverse Reactions (6)].
