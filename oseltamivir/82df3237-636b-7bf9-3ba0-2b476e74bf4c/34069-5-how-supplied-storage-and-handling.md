# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Oseltamivir Phosphate for Oral Suspension

Supplied as a white to off-white granular powder in a glass bottle.
After constitution, the powder blend produces a white to off-white tutti-frutti–flavored oral suspension.
After constitution with 55 mL of water, each bottle delivers a usable volume of 60 mL of oral suspension equivalent to 360 mg oseltamivir base (6 mg/mL).
(NDC 47781-384-26)

Storage

Store dry powder at 25ºC (77ºF); excursions permitted to 15º to 30ºC (59º to 86ºF) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Store constituted suspension under refrigeration for up to 17 days at 2º to 8ºC (36º to 46ºF).
Do not freeze.
Alternatively, store constituted suspension for up to 10 days at 25ºC (77ºF); excursions permitted to 15º to 30ºC (59º to 86ºF) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
