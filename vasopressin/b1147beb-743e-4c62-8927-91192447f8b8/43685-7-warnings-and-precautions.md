# 5 WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS

## 5.1 Worsening Cardiac Function

Use in patients with impaired cardiac response may worsen cardiac output.

## 5.2 Reversible Diabetes Insipidus

Patients may experience reversible diabetes inspidus, manifested by the development of polyuria, a dilute urine, and hypernatremia, after cessation of treatment with vasopressin.
Monitor serum electrolytes, fluid status and urine output after vasopressin discontinuation.
Some patients may require readministration of vasopressin or administration of desmopressin to correct fluid and electrolyte shifts.
