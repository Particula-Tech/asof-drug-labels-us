# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Each Bismuth Subcitrate Potassium, Metronidazole and Tetracycline Hydrochloride Capsules contains 140 mg of bismuth subcitrate potassium, 125 mg of metronidazole, and a smaller capsule inside containing 125 mg of tetracycline hydrochloride.
The capsules are white and opaque, with “BMT” printed on the cap.
