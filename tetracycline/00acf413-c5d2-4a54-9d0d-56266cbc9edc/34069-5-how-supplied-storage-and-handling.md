# HOW SUPPLIED

Tetracycline Hydrochloride Capsules, USP are available as:

250 mg: Blue opaque cap and yellow opaque Body, size '2', cap and body imprinted B906 in black ink.

Available in bottles of:

Bottle of 30 Count – NDC 51991-906-33

Bottle of 100 Count – NDC 51991-906-01

Bottle of 500 Count – NDC 51991-906-05

500 mg: Blue opaque cap and Blue opaque Body, size '0', cap and body imprinted B907 in black ink.

Available in bottles of:

Bottle of 30 Count – NDC 51991-907-33

Bottle of 100 Count – NDC 51991-907-01

Bottle of 500 Count – NDC 51991-907-05

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP.
Use child-resistant closure (as required).

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
