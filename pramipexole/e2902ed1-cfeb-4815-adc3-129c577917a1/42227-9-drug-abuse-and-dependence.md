# 9 DRUG ABUSE AND DEPENDENCE

## 9.1 Controlled Substance

Pramipexole is not a controlled substance.

## 9.2 Abuse and Dependence

Pramipexole has not been systematically studied in animals or humans for its potential for abuse, tolerance, or physical dependence.
However, in a rat model of cocaine self-administration, pramipexole had little or no effect.
