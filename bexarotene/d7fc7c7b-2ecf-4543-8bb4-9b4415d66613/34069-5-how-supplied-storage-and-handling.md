# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Bexarotene capsules 75 mg are supplied as white, oblong soft gelatin capsules imprinted “A125” with black ink, in high density polyethylene bottles with child-resistant closures.
They are available as follows:

Bottles of 100 capsules: NDC 69238-1250-1

Store at 2° to 25°C (36° to 77°F).
Avoid exposing to high temperatures and humidity after the bottle is opened.
Protect from light.
