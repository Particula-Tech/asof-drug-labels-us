# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Bexarotene capsules are supplied as 75 mg white to off-white, oblong soft gelatin capsules printed with B75 in black ink, in high density polyethylene bottles with child-resistant closures.

Bottles of 100 capsules: NDC 0378-6955-01

Store at 2° to 25°C (36° to 77°F).
Avoid exposing to high temperatures and humidity after the bottle is opened.
Protect from light.
