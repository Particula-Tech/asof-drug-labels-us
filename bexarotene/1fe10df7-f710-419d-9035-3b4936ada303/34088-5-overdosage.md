# OVERDOSAGE

Doses up to 1000 mg/m2/day of Targretin® capsules have been administered in short-term studies in patients with advanced cancer without acute toxic effects.
Single doses of 1500 mg/kg and 720 mg/kg were tolerated without significant toxicity in rats and dogs, respectively.
These doses are approximately 30 and 50 times, respectively, the recommended human dose on a mg/m2 basis.

No clinical experience with an overdose of Targretin® capsules has been reported.
Any overdose with Targretin® capsules should be treated with supportive care for the signs and symptoms exhibited by the patient.
