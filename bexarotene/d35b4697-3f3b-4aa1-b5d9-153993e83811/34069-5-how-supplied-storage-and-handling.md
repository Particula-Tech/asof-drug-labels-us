# HOW SUPPLIED

Targretin gel is supplied in tubes containing 60 g (600 mg active bexarotene).

60 g tube NDC 0187-5525-60

Store at 25°C (77°F); with excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Avoid exposing to high temperatures and humidity after the tube is opened.
Protect from light.
