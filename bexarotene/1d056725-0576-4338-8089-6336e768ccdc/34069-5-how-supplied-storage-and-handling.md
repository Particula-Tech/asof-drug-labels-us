# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

TARGRETIN® (bexarotene) Capsules are supplied as 75 mg off-white, oblong soft gelatin capsules, imprinted with “Targretin”, in high density polyethylene bottles with child-resistant closures.

Bottles of 100 capsules: NDC 0187-5526-75

Store at 2° to 25°C (36° to 77°F).
Avoid exposing to high temperatures and humidity after the bottle is opened.
Protect from light.
