# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

## 4.1 Pregnancy

TARGRETIN can cause fetal harm when administered to a pregnant female.
TARGRETIN is a member of the retinoid class of drugs that is associated with birth defects in humans and is contraindicated in females who are pregnant.
Bexarotene was also teratogenic and caused developmental mortality when administered orally to pregnant rats.
If this drug is used during pregnancy or if the patient becomes pregnant while taking this drug, the patient should be advised of the potential risk to a fetus.

## 4.2 Hypersensitivity

TARGRETIN Capsules are contraindicated in patients with a known serious hypersensitivity to bexarotene or other components of the product.
