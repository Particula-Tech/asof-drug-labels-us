# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Bexarotene Capsules are supplied as 75 mg off-white, oblong soft gelatin capsules, imprinted "US285" in black ink.
They are supplied as follows:

Bottles of 100 capsules: NDC 0832-0285-00

Store at 2° to 25°C (36° to 77°F).
Avoid exposing to high temperatures and humidity after the bottle is opened.
Protect from light.
