# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

SELZENTRY film-coated tablets are available as follows:

150 and 300 mg tablets are blue, biconvex, oval film-coated tablets debossed with "Pfizer" on one side and "MVC 150" or "MVC 300" on the other.

Bottle packs 150 mg tablets

- 60 tablets (NDC 0069-0807-60)

Bottle packs 300 mg tablets

- 60 tablets (NDC 0069-0808-60)

SELZENTRY film-coated tablets should be stored at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted between 15° and 30°C (59°–86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Shelf life is 24 months.
