# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Maraviroc tablets (film-coated) are available as follows:

150-mg and 300-mg tablets are blue, biconvex, oval, film-coated tablets debossed with "RP 150" and "RP 300", respectively, on one side and plain on the other.

150-mg tablets: Bottle of 60 tablets (NDC 42858-246-06).

300-mg tablets: Bottle of 60 tablets (NDC 42858-247-06).

Maraviroc tablets (film-coated) should be stored at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
