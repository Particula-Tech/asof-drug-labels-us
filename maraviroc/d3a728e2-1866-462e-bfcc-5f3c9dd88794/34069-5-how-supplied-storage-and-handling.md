# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Maraviroc film-coated tablets are available as follows: 150 mg white to off-white colored, oval, biconvex, film coated tablets debossed with 'J' on one side and '62' on the other side.
Bottle of 60 tablets (NDC 72865-231-60) 300 mg white to off-white colored, oval, biconvex, film coated tablets debossed with 'J' on one side and '63' on the other side.
Bottle of 60 tablets (NDC 72865-232-60) Maraviroc film-coated tablets should be stored at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
