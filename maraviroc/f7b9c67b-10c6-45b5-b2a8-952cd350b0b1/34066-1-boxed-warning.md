# WARNING: HEPATOTOXICITY

Hepatotoxicity has been reported with use of Maraviroc.
Severe rash or evidence of a systemic allergic reaction (e.g., fever, eosinophilia, or elevated IgE) prior to the development of hepatotoxicity may occur.
Patients with signs or symptoms of hepatitis or allergic reaction following use of Maraviroc should be evaluated immediately [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
