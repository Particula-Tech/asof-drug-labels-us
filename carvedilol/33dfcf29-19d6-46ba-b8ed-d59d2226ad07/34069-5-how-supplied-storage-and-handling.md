# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Carvedilol Tablets are available as follows:

3.125 mg- White, oval shaped, film-coated tablet engraved with 254 on one side and plain on the other side contains 3.125 mg of Carvedilol.
Tablets are supplied as follows: NDC 76385-110-01 in bottles of 100 tablets NDC 76385-110-50 in bottles of 500 tablets

6.25 mg- White, oval shaped, film-coated tablet engraved with 255 on one side and plain on the other side contains 6.25 mg of Carvedilol.
Tablets are supplied as follows: NDC 76385-111-01 in bottles of 100 tablets NDC 76385-111-50 in bottles of 500 tablets

12.5 mg- White, oval shaped, film-coated tablet engraved with 256 on one side and plain on the other side, and contains 12.5 mg of Carvedilol.
Tablets are supplied as follows: NDC 76385-112-01 in bottles of 100 tablets NDC 76385-112-50 in bottles of 500 tablets

25 mg- White, oval shaped, film-coated tablet engraved with 257 on one side and plain on the other side contains 25 mg of Carvedilol.
Tablets are supplied as follows: NDC 76385-113-01 in bottles of 100 bottles NDC 76385-113-50 in bottles of 500 tablets

Store at 25°C (77°F); Excursions permitted to 15°-30°C (59°-86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from moisture.
Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP.
