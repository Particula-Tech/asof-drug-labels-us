# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Carvedilol tablets, 3.125 mg, are available as white, film-coated, elliptical-shaped tablets, debossed with "93" on one side and "51" on the other.
They are available in bottles of 100.

Carvedilol tablets, 6.25 mg, are available as white, film-coated, elliptical-shaped tablets, debossed with "93" on one side and "135" on the other.
They are available in bottles of 100.

Carvedilol tablets, 12.5 mg, are available as white, film-coated, elliptical-shaped tablets, debossed with "93" on one side and "7295" on the other.
They are available in bottles of 100.

Carvedilol tablets, 25 mg, are available as white, film-coated, elliptical-shaped tablets, debossed with "93" on one side and "7296" on the other.
They are available in bottles of 100.

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from moisture.

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP, with a child-resistant closure (as required).
