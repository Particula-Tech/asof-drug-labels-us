# 11 DESCRIPTION

Carvedilol, USP is a nonselective β-adrenergic blocking agent with α1-blocking activity.
It is (±)-1-(carbazol-4-yloxy)-3-[[2-(o-methoxyphenoxy)ethyl]amino]-2-propanol.
Carvedilol, USP is a racemic mixture with the following structure:

C24H26N2O4 M.W.
406.47

Carvedilol tablets, USP are white, elliptical-shaped, film-coated tablets containing 3.125 mg, 6.25 mg, 12.5 mg, or 25 mg of carvedilol, USP.
Inactive ingredients consist of colloidal silicon dioxide, crospovidone, hypromellose 2910, lactose monohydrate, magnesium stearate, polyethylene glycol 400, polysorbate 80, povidone, and titanium dioxide.

Carvedilol, USP is a white or nearly white, crystalline powder.
Slightly soluble in alcohol; practically insoluble in water and in dilute acids.

Carvedilol tablets, USP meet USP Dissolution Test 2.
