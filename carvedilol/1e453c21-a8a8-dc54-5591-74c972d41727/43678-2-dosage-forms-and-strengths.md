# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

The round, film coated tablets are available in the following strengths: 3.125 mg– yellow colored, debossed with R and 252, 6.25 mg– white colored, debossed with R and 253, 12.5 mg– white colored, debossed with R and 254, and 25 mg– white colored, debossed with R and 255.
