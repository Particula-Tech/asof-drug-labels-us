# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

The white, oval, film-coated tablets are available in the following strengths: 3.125 mg–engraved with 39 and SB, in bottles of 100; 6.25 mg–engraved with 4140 and SB, in bottles of 100; 12.5 mg–engraved with 4141 and SB, in bottles of 100; 25 mg–engraved with 4142 and SB, in bottles of 100.
The 6.25 mg, 12.5 mg, and 25 mg tablets are TILTAB tablets.

- 3.125 mg 100’s: NDC 0007-4139-20
- 6.25 mg 100’s: NDC 0007-4140-20
- 12.5 mg 100’s: NDC 0007-4141-20
- 25 mg 100’s: NDC 0007-4142-20

Store below 30°C (86°F).
Protect from moisture.
Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container.
