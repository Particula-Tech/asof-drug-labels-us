# HOW SUPPLIED

Nutropin AQ® [somatropin (rDNA origin) injection] is supplied as either 10 mg (approximately 30 International Units) of sterile liquid somatropin per vial, a 10 mg (approximately 30 International Units) of sterile liquid somatropin per pen cartridge.

Each vial carton contains one single vial containing 2 mL of Nutropin AQ® [somatropin (rDNA origin) injection] 10 mg/2 mL (5 mg/mL).
DC 50242‑022‑20.

Each pen cartridge carton contains one single pen cartridge containing 2 mL of Nutropin AQ® [somatropin (rDNA origin) injection] 10 mg/2 mL (5 mg/mL).
DC 50242‑043‑14.
