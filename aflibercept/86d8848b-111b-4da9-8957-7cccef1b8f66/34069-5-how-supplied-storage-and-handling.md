# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

EYLEA HD (aflibercept) is a clear to slightly opalescent, colorless to pale yellow solution supplied in the following presentations [see Dosage and Administration (2.5) and (2.6)].
Each vial is for single eye use only.
Discard unused portion.

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Refrigerate EYLEA HD at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F).
Do not freeze.
Do not use beyond the date stamped on the carton and container label.
Store in the original carton until time of use to protect from light.
