CONTRAINDICATIONS

4.1 Ocular or Periocular Infections

AHZANTIVE is contraindicated in patients with ocular or periocular infections.

4.2 Active Intraocular Inflammation

AHZANTIVE is contraindicated in patients with active intraocular inflammation.

4.3 Hypersensitivity

AHZANTIVE is contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity to aflibercept or any of the excipients in AHZANTIVE.
Hypersensitivity reactions may manifest as rash, pruritus, urticaria, severe anaphylactic/anaphylactoid reactions, or severe intraocular inflammation.
