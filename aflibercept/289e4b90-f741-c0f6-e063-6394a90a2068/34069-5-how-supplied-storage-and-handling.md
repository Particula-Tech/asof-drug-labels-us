16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

16.1 How Supplied

Each vial is for single eye use only.
Discard unused portion.

AHZANTIVE (aflibercept-mrbb) injection is a clear, colorless to pale yellow solution and is supplied in the following presentation [see Dosage and Administration (2.1), (2.6) and (2.7)].

16.2 Storage and Handling

Refrigerate AHZANTIVE at 2°C to 8ºC (36°F to 46ºF).
Do not freeze.
Do not use beyond the date stamped on the carton and container label.
Store in the original carton until time of use to protect from light.
