# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

In the days following YESAFILI administration, patients are at risk of developing endophthalmitis, retinal detachment, or retinal vasculitis with or without occlusion.
If the eye becomes red, sensitive to light, painful, or develops a change in vision, advise patients and/or caregivers to seek immediate care from an ophthalmologist [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].

Patients may experience temporary visual disturbances after an intravitreal injection with YESAFILI and the associated eye examinations [see Adverse Reactions (6)].
Advise patients not to drive or use machinery until visual function has recovered sufficiently.
