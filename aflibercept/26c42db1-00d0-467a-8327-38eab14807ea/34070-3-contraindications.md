# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

## 4.1 Ocular or Periocular Infections

OPUVIZ is contraindicated in patients with ocular or periocular infections.

## 4.2 Active Intraocular Inflammation

OPUVIZ is contraindicated in patients with active intraocular inflammation.

## 4.3 Hypersensitivity

OPUVIZ is contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity to aflibercept or any of the excipients in OPUVIZ.
Hypersensitivity reactions may manifest as rash, pruritus, urticaria, severe anaphylactic/anaphylactoid reactions, or severe intraocular inflammation.
