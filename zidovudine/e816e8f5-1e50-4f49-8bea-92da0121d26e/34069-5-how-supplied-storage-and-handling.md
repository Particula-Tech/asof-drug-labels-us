# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Lamivudine and Zidovudine Tablets, USP containing 150 mg lamivudine USP and 300 mg zidovudine USP are white to off-white, scored, capsule shaped, biconvex, film coated tablets, debossed with 'I' on one side and '115' on other side.
They are available as follows: Bottle of 60 Tablets (NDC 31722-739-60) Bottle of 500 Tablets (NDC 31722-739-05) Unit Dose Pack of 100 (10 x 10) (NDC 31722-739-31) Store at 20 to 25°C (68 to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature]
