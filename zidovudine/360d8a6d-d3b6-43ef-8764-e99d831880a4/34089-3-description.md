# DESCRIPTION

RETROVIR is the brand name for zidovudine (formerly called azidothymidine [AZT]), a pyrimidine nucleoside analogue active against HIV.
RETROVIR IV Infusion is a sterile solution for intravenous infusion only.
Each mL contains 10 mg zidovudine in Water for Injection.
Hydrochloric acid and/or sodium hydroxide may have been added to adjust the pH to approximately 5.5.
RETROVIR IV Infusion contains no preservatives.

Zidovudine is a white to beige, odorless, crystalline solid with a molecular weight of 267.24 and a solubility of 20.1 mg/mL in water at 25°C.
