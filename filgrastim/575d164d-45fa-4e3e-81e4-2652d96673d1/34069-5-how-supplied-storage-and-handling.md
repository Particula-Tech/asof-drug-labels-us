# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

NYPOZI (filgrastim-txid) injection is a clear, colorless to slightly yellowish, preservative-free solution supplied as prefilled syringes:

Single-dose‚ preservative-free, prefilled syringes with 29 gauge, half inch needle with a BD UltraSafe Passive™ Needle Guard, containing 300 mcg/0.5 mL of filgrastim-txid.

- Carton of 1 prefilled syringe (NDC 69097-430-67)
- Carton of 10 prefilled syringes (NDC 69097-430-96)

Single-dose‚ preservative-free, prefilled syringes with 29 gauge, half inch needle with a BD UltraSafe Passive™ Needle Guard, containing 480 mcg/0.8 mL of filgrastim-txid.

- Carton of 1 prefilled syringe (NDC 69097-429-67)
- Carton of 10 prefilled syringes (NDC 69097-429-96)

Storage:

Store NYPOZI in the refrigerator at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) in the original carton to protect from light.
Do not leave NYPOZI in direct sunlight.
Avoid shaking.
Do not freeze.
Transport via pneumatic tube has not been studied.
Prior to injection‚ NYPOZI may be allowed to reach room temperature for a maximum of 24 hours.
