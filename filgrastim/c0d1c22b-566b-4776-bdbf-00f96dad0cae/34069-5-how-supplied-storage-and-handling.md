# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

ZARXIO injection is a clear, colorless to slightly yellowish, preservative-free solution supplied as:

Vial:

The vial is supplied in a carton containing one single-dose vial providing:

- •300 mcg/mL of filgrastim-sndz solution (NDC 61314-246-71)
- •480 mcg/1.6 mL (300 mcg/mL) of filgrastim-sndz solution (NDC 61314-266-73)

The vial stopper is not made with natural rubber latex.

Prefilled syringe (BD UltraSafe Passive® Needle Guard):

Single-dose prefilled syringe with a 29 gauge, ½ inch needle and with BD UltraSafe Passive® Needle Guard, containing 300 mcg/0.5 mL of filgrastim-sndz solution.

- •Pack of 1 prefilled syringe (NDC 61314-318-63)
- •Pack of 10 (multipack) prefilled syringes (NDC 61314-318-69)

Single-dose prefilled syringe with a 29 gauge, ½ inch needle and with BD UltraSafe Passive® Needle Guard, containing 480 mcg/0.8 mL of filgrastim-sndz solution.

- •Pack of 1 prefilled syringe (NDC 61314-326-63)
- •Pack of 10 (multipack) prefilled syringes (NDC 61314-326-69)

The prefilled syringe with safety guard is not made with natural rubber latex.

Storage:

Store ZARXIO in the refrigerator at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) in the original pack to protect from light.
Do not leave ZARXIO in direct sunlight.
Avoid shaking.
Avoid freezing; if frozen, thaw in the refrigerator before administration.
Discard ZARXIO if frozen more than once.
