# 14 CLINICAL STUDIES

## 14.1 Ocular Inflammation and Pain

Clinical efficacy was evaluated in 2 multi-centered, randomized, double-masked, parallel group, placebo-controlled US trials in which subjects requiring cataract surgery were assigned to receive bromfenac ophthalmic solution or vehicle.
Patients undergoing cataract surgery self-administered bromfenac ophthalmic solution or vehicle twice daily, beginning 1 day prior to surgery, continuing the day of surgery and for 14 days after surgery.
Clearance of ocular inflammation was assessed on Days 1, 8, 15, and 29 using slit lamp biomicroscopy.
The primary efficacy endpoint was the proportion of subjects with anterior chamber cell (ACC) grade 0 at Day 15.
The secondary efficacy endpoint was the proportion of subjects who were pain free after cataract surgery as assessed using a Visual Analog Scale.
