# 14 CLINICAL STUDIES

## 14.1 Ocular inflammation and pain following cataract surgery

Clinical efficacy was evaluated in three randomized, double-masked, placebo-controlled trials in which subjects requiring cataract surgery were assigned to bromfenac or placebo.
Patients were dosed with one drop per eye starting the day before surgery and continuing for 14 days.
The primary endpoint was clearing of ocular inflammation by day 15.
An additional efficacy endpoint was the number of patients who were pain free on day 1 after cataract surgery.

In 2 of the 3 studies, bromfenac ophthalmic solution had statistically significant higher incidence of completely clearing inflammation (46-47% vs. 25-29%) and also had a statistically significant higher incidence of subjects that were pain free at day 1 post cataract surgery (83-89% vs. 51-71%).
