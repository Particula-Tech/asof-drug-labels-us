# HOW SUPPLIED

Levocarnitine Oral Solution USP is supplied in 118 mL (4 FL.
OZ.) multiple-dose plastic containers.
The multiple-dose containers are packaged 24 per case (NDC 64980-503-12).
Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursion permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Rx only.
