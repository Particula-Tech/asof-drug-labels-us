# OVERDOSAGE

There have been no reports of toxicity from levocarnitine overdosage.
Levocarnitine is easily removed from plasma by dialysis.
The intravenous LD 50of levocarnitine in rats is 5.4 g/kg and the oral LD 50of levocarnitine in mice is 19.2 g/kg.
Large doses of levocarnitine may cause diarrhea.
