# HOW SUPPLIED

Levocarnitine tablets, USP are supplied as white, round compressed tablets debossed “Cor” over “160” on one side and other side is plain.
They are supplied as follows: Blister pack of 10 tablets, packaged in boxes of 90 tablets (NDC 16571-762-09) Store at 20o to 25oC (68o to 77oF) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
KEEP THIS AND ALL DRUGS OUT OF THE REACH OF CHILDREN.
