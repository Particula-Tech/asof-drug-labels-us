# HOW SUPPLIED

Levocarnitine Tablets, USP are supplied as 330 mg white to off white, round, standard biconvex, uncoated tablets debossed with N492 on one side and plain on the other side in individual blisters, packaged in boxes of 90 (NDC 70954-492-10).
Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Levocarnitine Oral Solution, USP is a clear cherry flavored solution supplied in 118 mL (4 fl. oz.) amber colored HDPE bottles (NDC 70954-139-10).
Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Levocarnitine Oral Solution, USP (Sugar Free) is a clear cherry flavored solution supplied in 118 mL (4 fl. oz.) white colored HDPE bottles (NDC 70954-140-10).
Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
