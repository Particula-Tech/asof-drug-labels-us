# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 Donepezil Hydrochloride Tablets, USP

Donepezil Hydrochloride Tablets, USP 5 mg are white, round, biconvex, film-coated tablets debossed on one side with SG and 139 on the other side.

NDC 50228-139-30: Bottles of 30 tablets

NDC 50228-139-10: Bottles of 1000 tablets

Donepezil Hydrochloride Tablets, USP 10 mg are yellow, round, biconvex, film-coated tablets debossed on one side with SG and 140 on the other side.

NDC 50228-140-30: Bottles of 30 tablets

NDC 50228-140-10: Bottles of 1000 tablets

Storage: Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F).
