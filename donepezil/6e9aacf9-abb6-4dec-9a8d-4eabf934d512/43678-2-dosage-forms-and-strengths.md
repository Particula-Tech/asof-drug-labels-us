# 3. DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Donepezil hydrochloride tablets, USP are supplied as film-coated, round biconvex tablets containing 5 mg or 10 mg of donepezil hydrochloride.

The 5 mg tablets are white, round, biconvex, film coated tablets debossed on one side with SG and 139 on the other side.

The 10 mg tablets are yellow, round, biconvex, film coated tablets debossed on one side with SG and 140 on the other side.
