# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

- The 5 mg tablets are white to off-white, circular, biconvex, film-coated tablets debossed with ‘X’ on one side and ‘11’ on the other side.
- The 10 mg tablets are yellow colored, circular, biconvex, film-coated tablets debossed with ‘X’ on one side and ‘12’ on the other side.
