# 8. USE IN SPECIFIC POPULATIONS

## 8.1. Pregnancy

Pregnancy Category C: Teratology studies conducted in pregnant rats at doses up to 16 mg/kg/day (approximately 13 times the maximum recommended human dose on a mg/m2 basis) and in pregnant rabbits at doses up to 10 mg/kg/day (approximately 16 times the maximum recommended human dose on a mg/m2 basis) did not disclose any evidence for a teratogenic potential of donepezil.
However, in a study in which pregnant rats were given up to 10 mg/kg/day (approximately 8 times the maximum recommended human dose on a mg/m2 basis) from day 17 of gestation through day 20 postpartum, there was a slight increase in still births and a slight decrease in pup survival through day 4 postpartum at this dose; the next lower dose tested was 3 mg/kg/day.
There are no adequate or well-controlled studies in pregnant women.
ARICEPT® should be used during pregnancy only if the potential benefit justifies the potential risk to the fetus.

## 8.3. Nursing Mothers

It is not known whether donepezil is excreted in human breast milk.
ARICEPT® has no indication for use in nursing mothers.

## 8.4. Pediatric Use

There are no adequate and well-controlled trials to document the safety and efficacy of ARICEPT® in any illness occurring in children.

## 8.5. Geriatric Use

Alzheimer's disease is a disorder occurring primarily in individuals over 55 years of age.
The mean age of patients enrolled in the clinical studies with ARICEPT® was 73 years; 80% of these patients were between 65 and 84 years old, and 49% of patients were at or above the age of 75.
The efficacy and safety data presented in the clinical trials section were obtained from these patients.
There were no clinically significant differences in most adverse events reported by patient groups ≥ 65 years old and < 65 years old.
