# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

## 4.1 History of Clozapine-induced Agranulocytosis or Severe Granulocytopenia

VERSACLOZ is contraindicated in patients with a history of clozapine-induced agranulocytosis or severe granulocytopenia [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].

## 4.2 Hypersensitivity

VERSACLOZ is contraindicated in patients with a history of hypersensitivity to clozapine (e.g., photosensitivity, vasculitis, erythema multiforme, or Stevens-Johnson Syndrome) or any other component of VERSACLOZ [see Adverse Reactions (6.2)].
