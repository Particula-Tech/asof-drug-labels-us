PRINCIPAL DISPLAY PANEL - 25 mg

NDC 0378-0825-01

CLOZAPINE TABLETS, USP 25 mg

100 TABLETS (Rx only)

Each tablet contains: Clozapine, USP....
25 mg

Usual Adult Dosage: See accompanying prescribing information.

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP using a child-resistant closure.

Keep container tightly closed.

Keep this and all medication out of the reach of children.

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

Drug dispensing should not ordinarily exceed a weekly supply.
If a patient is eligible for White Blood Cell (WBC) count and Absolute Neutrophil Count (ANC) testing every 2 weeks, then a two- week supply of clozapine can be dispensed.
If a patient is eligible for WBC count and ANC testing every 4 weeks, then a four-week supply of clozapine can be dispensed.
Dispensing should be contingent upon the WBC count and ANC test results.
