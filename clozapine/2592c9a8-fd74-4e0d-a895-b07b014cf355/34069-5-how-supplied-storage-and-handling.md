# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Oral Suspension

Free-flowing, yellow suspension (50 mg/mL) in amber bottle containing 100 mL.
Each box contains 1 x 1 mL oral syringe, 1 x 9 mL oral syringe and 1 bottle adaptor.

NDC No. 52817-601-38

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store VERSACLOZ at room temperature between 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursion permitted between 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Do not refrigerate or freeze.
Protect from light.
Shake well for 10 seconds before use.

The suspension is stable for 100 days after initial bottle opening.
Keep out of reach of children
