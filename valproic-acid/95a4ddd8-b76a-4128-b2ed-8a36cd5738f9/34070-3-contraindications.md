# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

- Stavzor should not be administered to patients with hepatic disease or significant hepatic dysfunction.
  [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
- Stavzor is contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity to the drug.
  [see Warnings and Precautions (5.10)].
- Stavzor is contraindicated in patients with known urea cycle disorders [see Warnings and Precautions (5.4)].
