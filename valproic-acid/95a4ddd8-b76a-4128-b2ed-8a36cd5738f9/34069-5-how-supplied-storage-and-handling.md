# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Stavzor (valproic acid) delayed-release capsules are supplied as:

- 125-mg orange-colored, oval-shaped capsules with NVN in black print: Bottles of 100 (NDC 68968-3125-1)
- 250-mg orange-colored, oval-shaped capsules with NVN1 in black print: Bottles of 100 (NDC 68968-3250-1) Bottles of 10 (NDC 68968-3250-6)
- 500-mg orange-colored, oval-shaped capsules with NVN2 in black print: Bottles of 100 (NDC 68968-3500-1) Bottles of 10 (NDC 68968-3500-6)

Store at 25° C (77° F); excursions permitted to 15° - 30° C (59° - 86 ° F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]
