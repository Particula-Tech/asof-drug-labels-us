# DESCRIPTION

Divalproex sodium is a stable co-ordination compound comprised of sodium valproate and valproic acid in a 1:1 molar relationship and formed during the partial neutralization of valproic acid with 0.5 equivalent of sodium hydroxide.
Chemically it is designated as sodium hydrogen bis(2-propylpentanoate).

Divalproex sodium occurs as a white to off white powder with a characteristic odor.

Divalproex sodium delayed-release tablets are for oral administration.
Divalproex sodium delayed-release tablets are supplied in three dosage strengths containing divalproex sodium equivalent to 125 mg, 250 mg, or 500 mg of valproic acid.
