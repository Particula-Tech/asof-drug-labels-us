# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Depakote Sprinkle Capsules (divalproex sodium coated particles in capsules), 125 mg, are white opaque and blue, and are supplied in bottles of 100 (NDC 0074-6114-13) and Abbo-Pac® unit dose packages of 100 (NDC 0074-6114-11).

Recommended Storage

Store capsules below 77°F (25°C).
