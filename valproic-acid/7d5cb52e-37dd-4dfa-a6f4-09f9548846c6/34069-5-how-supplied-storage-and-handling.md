# HOW SUPPLIED

Valproic Acid Syrup USP is available as a red, cherry flavored syrup containing the equivalent of 250 mg valproic acid per 5 mL as the sodium salt in bottles of 16 fluid ounces (473 mL), NDC 0121-0675-16, and in 5 mL unit dose containers, NDC 0121-4675-05.

Store at controlled room temperature, 20°-25°C (68°-77°F).
