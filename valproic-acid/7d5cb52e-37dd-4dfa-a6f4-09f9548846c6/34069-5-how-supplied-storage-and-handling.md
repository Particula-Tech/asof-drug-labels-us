# HOW SUPPLIED

Valproic Acid Oral Solution USP is available as a red, cherry flavored solution containing the equivalent of 250 mg valproic acid per 5 mL as the sodium salt in bottles of 16 fluid ounces (473 mL), NDC 0121-0675-16, 5 mL unit dose containers, NDC 0121-4675-05 and 10 mL unit dose containers, NDC 0121-4675-10.

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
