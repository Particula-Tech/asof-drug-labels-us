# 11 DESCRIPTION

Valproate sodium is the sodium salt of valproic acid designated as sodium 2-propylpentanoate.
Valproate sodium has the following structure:

Valproate sodium occurs as an essentially white and odorless, crystalline, deliquescent powder.

Valproate sodium injection, USP is available in 5 mL single dose vials for intravenous infusion only.
Each mL contains valproate sodium equivalent to 100 mg valproic acid, edetate disodium 0.4 mg, and water for injection to volume.
The pH is adjusted to 7.6 with sodium hydroxide and/or hydrochloric acid.
The solution is clear and colorless.
