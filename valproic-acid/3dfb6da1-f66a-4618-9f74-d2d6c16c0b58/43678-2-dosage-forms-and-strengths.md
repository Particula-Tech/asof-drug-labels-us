# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Valproate sodium injection, equivalent to 100 mg of valproic acid per mL, is a clear, colorless solution in 5 mL single use vials, available in trays of 10 vials.

Recommended storage: Store vials at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Preservative Free.
Unused portion of container should be discarded.
