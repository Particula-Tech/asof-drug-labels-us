# WARNINGS: LIFE THREATENING ADVERSE REACTIONS

See full prescribing information for complete boxed warning. • Hepatotoxicity, including fatalities, usually during the first 6 months of treatment.
Children under the age of two years and patients with mitochondrial disorders are at higher risk.
Monitor patients closely, and perform serum liver testing prior to therapy and at frequent intervals thereafter (5.1) • Fetal Risk, particularly neural tube defects, other major malformations, and decreased IQ (5.2, 5.3, 5.4) • Pancreatitis, including fatal hemorrhagic cases (5.5)
