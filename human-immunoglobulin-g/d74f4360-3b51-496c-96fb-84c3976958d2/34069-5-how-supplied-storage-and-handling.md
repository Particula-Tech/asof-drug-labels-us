# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

ASCENIV is supplied in a single-use, tamper-evident vial.
The components used in the packaging for ASCENIV are not made with natural rubber latex.
ASCENIV is supplied in 50 mL size containing 5 grams of protein (NDC 69800-0250-1).

- Store at 2 to 8°C (36 to 46°F) for up to 36 months from the date of manufacture.
  Do not freeze.
- Product may be stored up to 4 weeks at ≤ 25° C (77° F). After storage at room temperature product must be used or discarded.
