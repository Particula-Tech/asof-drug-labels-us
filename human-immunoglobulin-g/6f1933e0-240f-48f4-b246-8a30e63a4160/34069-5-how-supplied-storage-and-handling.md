# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

QIVIGY is supplied in single-dose, tamper evident vials containing the labeled amount of functionally active IgG.
The vial labels incorporate integrated hangers.
The components used in the packaging for QIVIGY are not made with natural rubber latex.

Each product presentation includes a package insert and the components are listed in Table 6.

Storage

Do not use QIVIGY after the expiration date which is stated on the carton and label after "EXP."
The expiration date refers to the last day of that month.

Store QIVIGY at 2 °C - 8 °C (36 °F - 46 °F) for up to 36 months.

Keep the vial stored in the outer carton in order to protect from light.

Do not freeze.
