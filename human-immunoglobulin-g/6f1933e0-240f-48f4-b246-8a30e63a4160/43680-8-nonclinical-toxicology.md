# 13 NONCLINICAL TOXICOLOGY

## 13.1 Carcinogenesis, mutagenesis, impairment of fertility

No animal studies were conducted to evaluate the carcinogenic or mutagenic effect of QIVIGY or its effects on fertility.

## 13.2 Animal toxicology and/or pharmacology

An acute animal toxicology study was conducted to evaluate possible toxicity of QIVIGY before clinical trial.
There were no toxicological concerns for the therapeutic use of QIVIGY.
