# CONTRAINDICATIONS

Sulfamethoxazole and Trimethoprim Oral Suspension is contraindicated in patients with a known hypersensitivity to trimethoprim or sulfonamides and in patients with documented megaloblastic anemia due to folate deficiency.
Sulfamethoxazole and Trimethoprim Oral Suspension is also contraindicated in pregnant patients and nursing mothers, because sulfonamides pass the placenta and are excreted in the milk and may cause kernicterus.
Sulfamethoxazole and Trimethoprim Oral Suspension is contraindicated in infants less than 2 months of age.
