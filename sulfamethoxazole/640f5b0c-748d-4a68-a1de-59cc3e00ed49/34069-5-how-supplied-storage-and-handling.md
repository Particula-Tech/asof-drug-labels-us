# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Sulfamethoxazole and Trimethoprim Injection, USP is supplied as follows:

| NDC Numbers | Sulfamethoxazole, USPStrength | Trimethoprim, USPStrength | Pack Size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
