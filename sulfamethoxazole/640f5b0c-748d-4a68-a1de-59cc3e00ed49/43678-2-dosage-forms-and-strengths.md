# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Sulfamethoxazole and Trimethoprim Injection USP, is a clear, colorless to slight yellow, sterile solution available as an injection containing 800 mg/10 mL (80 mg/mL) of sulfamethoxazole, USP and 160 mg/10 mL (16 mg/mL) of trimethoprim, USP in 10 mL multiple-dose vials.
