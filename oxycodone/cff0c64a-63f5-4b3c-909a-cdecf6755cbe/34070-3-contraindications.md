# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

OXAYDO is contraindicated in patients with respiratory depression in unmonitored settings and in the absence of resuscitative equipment.

OXAYDO is contraindicated in any patient who has or is suspected of having paralytic ileus.

OXAYDO is contraindicated in patients with acute or severe bronchial asthma or hypercarbia.

OXAYDO is contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity to oxycodone, oxycodone salts, or any components of the product.
