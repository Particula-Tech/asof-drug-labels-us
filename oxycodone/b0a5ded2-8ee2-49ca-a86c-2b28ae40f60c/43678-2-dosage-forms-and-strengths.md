# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

XTAMPZA ER capsules contain yellow to light brown microspheres, have an outer opaque capsule with colors as identified below and are available in five dosage strengths:

- Each 9 mg extended-release capsule has an ivory cap printed with "XTAMPZA ER" and a white body printed with "9 mg"; each 9 mg capsule contains the equivalent of 10 mg oxycodone HCl
- Each 13.5 mg extended-release capsule has a Swedish orange cap printed with "XTAMPZA ER" and a white body printed with "13.5 mg"; each 13.5 mg capsule contains the equivalent of 15 mg oxycodone HCl
- Each 18 mg extended-release capsule has a rich yellow cap printed with "XTAMPZA ER" and a white body printed with "18 mg"; each 18 mg capsule contains the equivalent of 20 mg oxycodone HCl
- Each 27 mg extended-release capsule has a light gray cap printed with "XTAMPZA ER" and a white body printed with "27 mg"; each 27 mg capsule contains the equivalent of 30 mg oxycodone HCl
- Each 36 mg extended-release capsule has a flesh color cap printed with "XTAMPZA ER" and a white body printed with "36 mg"; each 36 mg capsule contains the equivalent of 40 mg oxycodone HCl
