# HOW SUPPLIED

Prolixin Decanoate (Fluphenazine Decanoate Injection, USP) is available in 25 mg/mL:

| 1 mL Unimatic® single dose syringe | NDC 0003-0569-02 Each syringe is supplied with a 20-gauge 1-1/2 inch needle. |
| --- | --- |
| 5 mL Multiple dose vial | NDC 0003-0569-15 At time of manufacture, the air in the vials is replaced by nitrogen. |

## Storage

Store at room temperature; avoid freezing and excessive heat.
Protect from light.
