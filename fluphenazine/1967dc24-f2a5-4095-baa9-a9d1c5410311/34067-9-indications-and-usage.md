# INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Fluphenazine Decanoate Injection, USP is a long-acting parenteral antipsychotic drug intended for use in the management of patients requiring prolonged parenteral neuroleptic therapy (e.g., chronic schizophrenics).

Fluphenazine Decanoate Injection, USP has not been shown effective in the management of behavioral complications in patients with mental retardation.
