# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

- Dapagliflozin tablets 5 mg are white to off-white colored, round-shaped, film-coated tablets, debossed with "1380" on one side and plain on the other side.
- Dapagliflozin tablets 10 mg are white to off-white colored, oval-shaped, film-coated tablets, debossed with "1381" on one side and plain on the other side.
