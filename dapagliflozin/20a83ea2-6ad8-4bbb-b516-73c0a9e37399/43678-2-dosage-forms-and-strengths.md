# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Tablets:

- Dapagliflozin tablets 5 mg are yellow colored, biconvex, round shaped, film-coated tablets with "120" engraved on one side and plain on another side.
- Dapagliflozin tablets 10 mg are yellow colored, biconvex, diamond-shaped, film-coated tablets with "121" engraved on one side and plain on another side.
