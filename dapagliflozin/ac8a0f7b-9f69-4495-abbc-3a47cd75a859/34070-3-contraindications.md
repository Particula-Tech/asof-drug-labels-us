# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

XIGDUO XR is contraindicated in patients with:

- •Moderate to severe renal impairment (e.g., serum creatinine levels ≥1.5 mg/dL for men, ≥1.4 mg/dL for women, or eGFR <60 mL/min/1.73 m2 or CrCl <60 mL/min), which may also result from conditions such as cardiovascular collapse (shock), acute myocardial infarction, and septicemia [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3)].
- •History of a serious hypersensitivity reaction to dapagliflozin or hypersensitivity to metformin hydrochloride [see Adverse Reactions (6.1)].
- •Acute or chronic metabolic acidosis, including diabetic ketoacidosis, with or without coma.
  Diabetic ketoacidosis should be treated with insulin.
