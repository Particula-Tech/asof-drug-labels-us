# HOW SUPPLIED

BISOPROLOL FUMARATE is supplied as 5 mg and 10 mg tablets.

The 5 mg tablet is pink colored, biconvex, round, film coated tablets debossed with UL on one side and scored on the other side with 5 debossed on either side of the score.

Bottles of 30: NDC 29300-126-13

Bottles of 100: NDC 29300-126-01

The 10 mg tablet is white colored, biconvex, round, film coated tablets debossed with UL on one side and 10 on the other side.

Bottles of 30: NDC 29300-127-13

Bottles of 100: NDC 29300-127-01

Store at 200 to 250C (680 to 770F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Protect from moisture.

Dispense in tight, light-resistant containers.
