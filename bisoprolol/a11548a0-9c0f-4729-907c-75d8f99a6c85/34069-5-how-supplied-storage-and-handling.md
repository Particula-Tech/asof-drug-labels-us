# HOW SUPPLIED

ZEBETA® (bisoprolol fumarate) is supplied as 5 mg and 10 mg tablets.

The 5 mg tablet is pink, heart-shaped, film-coated, vertically scored in half on both sides, with an engraved stylized b/stylized b on one side and 6/0 on the reverse side, supplied as follows:

| 30 Tablets | Unit-of-use | NDC 51285-060-01 |
| --- | --- | --- |

The 10 mg tablet is white, heart-shaped, film-coated, with an engraved stylized b on one side and 61 on the reverse side, supplied as follows:

| 30 Tablets | Unit-of-use | NDC 51285-061-01 |
| --- | --- | --- |

Store at 20o to 25o C (68o to 77oF) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from moisture.
Dispense in tight containers.
