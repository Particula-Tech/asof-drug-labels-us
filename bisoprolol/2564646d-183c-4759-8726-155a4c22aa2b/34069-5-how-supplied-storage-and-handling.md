# HOW SUPPLIED

Bisoprolol fumarate is supplied as 5 mg and 10 mg tablets.
Bisoprolol Fumarate Tablets, USP 5 mg are pink colored, circular, biconvex, film coated tablets, debossed with ‘C and break line’ on one side and ‘86’ on the other side.
Bottles of 30 NDC 65862-086-30 Bottles of 100 NDC 65862-086-01 Bisoprolol Fumarate Tablets, USP 10 mg are white, circular, biconvex, film coated tablets, debossed with ‘C’ on one side and ‘87’ on the other side.
Bottles of 30 NDC 65862-087-30 Bottles of 100 NDC 65862-087-01 Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light and moisture.
Dispense in tight, light-resistant containers as defined in the USP.
