# 8 USE IN SPECIFIC POPULATIONS

## 8.1 Pregnancy

Pregnancy Category C: There are no adequate and well-controlled studies in pregnant women.
AVONEX should be used during pregnancy only if the potential benefit justifies the potential risk to the fetus.

In pregnant monkeys given interferon beta at 100 times the recommended weekly human dose (based upon a body surface area [mg/m2] comparison), no teratogenic or other adverse effects on fetal development were observed.
Abortifacient activity was evident following 3 to 5 doses at this level.
No abortifacient effects were observed in monkeys treated at 2 times the recommended weekly human dose (based upon mg/m2).

## 8.3 Nursing Mothers

It is not known whether AVONEX is excreted in human milk.

## 8.4 Pediatric Use

Safety and effectiveness in pediatric patients have not been established.

## 8.5 Geriatric Use

Clinical studies of AVONEX did not include sufficient numbers of patients aged 65 and over to determine whether they respond differently than younger patients.
