# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Tofacitinib Tablets 5 mg are white to off-white, round, biconvex, film-coated tablets debossed with “TI” on one side and “5” on other side.

Bottles of 60 NDC 59651-459-60

Tofacitinib Tablets 10 mg are blue, round, biconvex, film-coated tablets debossed with “TI” on one side and “10” on other side.

Bottles of 60 NDC 59651-460-60

Store at 20º to 25ºC (68º to 77ºF); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Do not repackage.
