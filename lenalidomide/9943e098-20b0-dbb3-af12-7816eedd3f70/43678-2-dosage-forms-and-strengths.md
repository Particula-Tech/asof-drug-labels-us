# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Lenalidomide 5 mg, 10 mg, 15 mg and 25 mg capsules will be supplied through the Lenalidomide REMS program.

Lenalidomide is available in the following capsule strengths:

5 mg: Hard gelatin capsule with white opaque body and white opaque cap.
Imprinted “APO L5” in black ink.

10 mg: Hard gelatin capsule with ivory opaque body and blue green opaque cap.
Imprinted “APO L10” in black ink.

15 mg: Hard gelatin capsule with white opaque body and powder blue opaque cap.
Imprinted “APO L15” in black ink.

25 mg: Hard gelatin capsule with white opaque body and white opaque cap.
Imprinted “APO L25” in black ink.
