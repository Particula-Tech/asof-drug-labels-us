# 10. OVERDOSAGE

There is no known antidote for overdoses with Jakafi.
Single doses up to 200 mg have been given with acceptable acute tolerability.
Higher than recommended repeat doses are associated with increased myelosuppression including leukopenia, anemia and thrombocytopenia.
Appropriate supportive treatment should be given.

Hemodialysis is not expected to enhance the elimination of Jakafi.
