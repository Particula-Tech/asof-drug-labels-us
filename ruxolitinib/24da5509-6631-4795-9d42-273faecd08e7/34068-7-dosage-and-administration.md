# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

Instruct patients to apply a thin layer of OPZELURA twice daily to affected areas of up to 20% body surface area.
Do not use more than 60 grams per week.

OPZELURA is for topical use only.
OPZELURA is not for ophthalmic, oral, or intravaginal use.

Stop using when signs and symptoms (e.g., itch, rash, and redness) of atopic dermatitis resolve.
If signs and symptoms do not improve within 8 weeks, patients should be re-examined by their healthcare provider.
