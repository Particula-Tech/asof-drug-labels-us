# 11 DESCRIPTION

Pirfenidone belongs to the chemical class of pyridone.
Pirfenidone is available as film-coated tablets containing 267 mg and 801 mg pirfenidone.
Pirfenidone has a molecular formula of C12H11NO and a molecular weight of 185.23.
Pirfenidone has the following structural formula, which has been referred to as 5-methyl-1-phenyl-2-1(H)-pyridone or 5-methyl-1-phenyl-2-(1H)-pyridone.

Pirfenidone is a white to pale yellow, non-hygroscopic powder.
It is freely soluble in dichloromethane, methanol, soluble in ethanol and practically insoluble in water.
The melting point is approximately 109°C.
Pirfenidone tablets contain pirfenidone and the following inactive ingredients: croscarmellose sodium, colloidal silicon dioxide, hydroxypropyl cellulose, magnesium stearate, microcrystalline cellulose, polyvinyl alcohol, titanium dioxide, macrogol (polyethylene glycol) and talc.
