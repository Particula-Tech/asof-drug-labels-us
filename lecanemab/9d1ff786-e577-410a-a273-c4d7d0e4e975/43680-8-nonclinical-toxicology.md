# 13NONCLINICAL TOXICOLOGY

## 13.1Carcinogenesis, Mutagenesis, Impairment of Fertility

Carcinogenesis

Carcinogenicity studies have not been conducted.

Mutagenesis

Genotoxicity studies have not been conducted.

Impairment of Fertility

No studies in animals have been conducted to assess the effects of lecanemab-irmb on male or female fertility.
No adverse effects on male or female reproductive organs were observed in a 39-week intravenous toxicity study in monkeys administered lecanemab-irmb weekly at doses up to 100 mg/kg.
The highest dose tested was associated with plasma exposures (Cave) approximately 27 times that in humans at the recommended human dose (10 mg/kg every two weeks).
