# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

ULTRAM ER (tramadol hydrochloride) Extended-release tablets are supplied in the following package and dose strength forms:

100 mg tablets (round, convex, white to off-white, non-scored) imprinted with “100” over “ER” on one side in black ink Bottle of 30 tablets – NDC 50458-0653-30

200 mg tablets (round, convex, white to off-white, non-scored) imprinted with “200” over “ER” on one side in black ink Bottle of 30 tablets – NDC 50458-0655-30

300 mg tablets (round, convex, white to off-white, non-scored) imprinted with “300” over “ER” on one side in black ink Bottle of 30 tablets – NDC 50458-0657-30

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15-30°C (59 - 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
