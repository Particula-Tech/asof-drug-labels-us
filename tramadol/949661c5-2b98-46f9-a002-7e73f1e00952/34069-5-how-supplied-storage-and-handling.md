# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

QDOLO (tramadol hydrochloride) oral solution is supplied as a clear, grape flavored liquid containing tramadol HCl, 5 mg/mL.
Supplied in 16 oz (473 mL) white, opaque plastic bottles with a child-resistant cap, NDC 71511-301-16

Dispense in a tight container.
Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to30°C (59°F to 86°F).
[see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Store QDOLO securely and dispose of properly [see Patient Counseling Information (17)].
