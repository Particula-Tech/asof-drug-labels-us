# CONTRAINDICATIONS

ULTRAM® should not be administered to patients who have previously demonstrated hypersensitivity to tramadol, any other component of this product or opioids.
ULTRAM® is contraindicated in any situation where opioids are contraindicated, including acute intoxication with any of the following: alcohol, hypnotics, narcotics, centrally acting analgesics, opioids or psychotropic drugs.
ULTRAM® may worsen central nervous system and respiratory depression in these patients.
