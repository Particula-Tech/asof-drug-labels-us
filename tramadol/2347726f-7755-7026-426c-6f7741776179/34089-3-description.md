# 11 DESCRIPTION

ConZip™ (tramadol hydrochloride) capsules is a centrally acting synthetic analgesic in an extended-release oral formulation.
The chemical name for tramadol hydrochloride is (±)cis-2-[(dimethylamino)methyl]-1-(3-methoxyphenyl) cyclohexanol hydrochloride.
Its structural formula is:

Figure 1

The molecular weight of tramadol hydrochloride is 299.8.
It is a white, bitter, crystalline and odorless powder that is readily soluble in water and ethanol and has a pKa of 9.41.
The n-octanol/water log partition coefficient (logP) is 1.35 at pH 7.
ConZip™ capsules contain a total dose of tramadol HCl 100, 200 and 300 mg in a combination of immediate-release and extended-release components.

| Dosage | Immediate-release | Extended-release |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 100 mg | 25 mg | 75 mg |
| 200 mg | 50 mg | 150 mg |
| 300 mg | 50 mg | 250 mg |

ConZip™ capsules are white in color.
Inactive ingredients include gelatin, titanium dioxide, shellac, FD & C Blue #2 aluminum lake (E132), D & C Red #7 calcium lake (E180), D & C Yellow #10 aluminum lake, lactose monohydrate 200 mesh, microcrystalline cellulose, povidone K30, corn starch, sodium starch glycolate, magnesium stearate, sucrose stearate, hypromellose, talc, polysorbate 80, Eudragit NE 30D, and simethicone emulsion.
