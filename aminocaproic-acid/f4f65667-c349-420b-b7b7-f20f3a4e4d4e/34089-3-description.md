# DESCRIPTION

Aminocaproic Acid Injection, USP is 6-aminohexanoic acid, which acts as an inhibitor of fibrinolysis.
The structural formula is:

The molecular formula is: C6H13NO2

Aminocaproic Acid Injection, USP is soluble in water, acid and alkaline solutions; it is sparingly soluble in methanol and practically insoluble in chloroform.

Aminocaproic Acid Injection, USP, for intravenous injection, is a sterile pyrogen-free solution containing 250 mg/mL of Aminocaproic Acid with Benzyl Alcohol 0.9%, as a preservative, and Water for Injection q.s.
The pH is adjusted to approximately 6.8 with Hydrochloric Acid and/or Sodium Hydroxide.
