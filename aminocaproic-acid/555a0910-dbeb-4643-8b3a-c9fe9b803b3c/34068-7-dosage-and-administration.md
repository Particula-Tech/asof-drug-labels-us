# DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

An identical dosage regimen may be followed by administering AminocaproicAcid Tablets as follows:

For the treatment of acute bleeding syndromes due to elevated fibrinolytic activity, it is suggested that 5 Aminocaproic Acid Tablets, 1000 mg or 10 Aminocaproic Acid Tablets, 500 mg (5 g) be administered during the first hour of treatment, followed by a continuing rate of 1 Aminocaproic Acid Tablets, 1000 mg or 2 Aminocaproic Acid Tablets, 500 mg (1 g) per hour.
This method of treatment would ordinarily be continued for about 8 hours or until the bleeding situation has been controlled.
