# HOW SUPPLIED

Aminocaproic Acid Oral Solution USP, 0.25 g/mL

Each mL of raspberry-flavored oral solution contains 0.25 g/mL of aminocaproic acid.

8 Fl.
Oz.
(236.5 mL) Bottle - NDC 0054-1100-58

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Dispense in a tight container with a child-resistant closure.
Do Not Freeze.

Aminocaproic Acid Tablets USP, 500 mg

Each round, white tablet, engraved with XP on one side and scored on the other with A to the left of the score and 10 on the right, contains 500 mg of aminocaproic acid.

Bottle of 30 – NDC 0054-1150-13

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Dispense in a tight container with a child-resistant closure.
Do Not Freeze.

Aminocaproic Acid Tablets USP, 1,000 mg

Each oblong, white tablet, engraved with XP on one side and scored on the other with A to the left of the score and 20 on the right, contains 1,000 mg of aminocaproic acid.

Bottle of 30 – NDC 0054-1110-13

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Dispense in a tight container with a child-resistant closure.
Do Not Freeze.
