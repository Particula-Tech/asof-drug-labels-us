# 8.4 Pediatric Use

HYFTORTMGel, 0.2% is not indicated for children less than 3 years of age.

HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2% has been studied in children 3 years and older.

The use of HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2% is not recommended in children below 3 years of age, since the safety and efficacy of HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2% have not been established in this age group.

The long-term safety and effects of HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2% on the developing immune system are unknown [see Warnings and Precautions (5) and Indications and Usage (1)].

Three studies were conducted involving a total of about 63 patients 3-18 years of age: two 12-week randomized vehicle-controlled studies and one open-label safety study conducted for up to two and a half years duration.

The most common adverse events associated with HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2% application in the long term safety study until 52 weeks (50 pediatric patients) were dry skin and application site irritation (26.0% each, 13 patients), pruritus (6.0%, 3 patients), and acne (12.0%, 6 patients).
In the open-label safety studies, the incidence of adverse events, including infections, did not increase with increased duration of study drug exposure or amount of Gel used.

## 8.5 Geriatric Use

Studies of HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2% in geriatric patients have not been conducted.

## 8.6 Patients with Hepatic Impairment

The effect of hepatic impairment on the pharmacokinetics of topically administered sirolimus has not been evaluated but dose-adjustment is not expected to be needed.

## 8.7 Patients with Renal Impairment

The effect of renal impairment on the pharmacokinetics of topically administered sirolimus has not been evaluated.
However, there is minimal (2.3%) renal excretion of the drug or its metabolites in healthy volunteers following oral administration.
On the basis of this information dose-adjustment is not expected to be needed.

## 10 OVERDOSAGE

HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2% is not for oral use.
Oral ingestion of HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2% may lead to adverse effects associated with systemic administration of sirolimus.
If oral ingestion occurs, medical advice should be sought.
