MEDICATION GUIDE

HYFTORTMGel, 0.2%

(sirolimus)

Read the Medication Guide every time you or a family member receives HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2%.
There may be new information.
This Medication Guide does not take the place of talking to your doctor about your medical condition or treatment.
If you have questions about HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2%, ask your doctor or pharmacist.

What is the most important information I should know about HYFTORTMGel, 0.2%?

The safety of using HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2% for a long period of time is not known.

Because of this concern:

- Use HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2% only on areas of your skin that have angiofibroma.
- Do not use HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2% on a child under 3 years old.

HYFTORTMGel, 0.2% is:

- for use on children aged 3 years and adults.

Talk to your doctor for more information.

What is HYFTORTMGel, 0.2%?

HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2% is a prescription medicine used on the skin (topical) applied to angiofibroma.
HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2% is in a class of medicines called topical mTOR inhibitors.
It is for adults and children 3 years of age and older

Who should not use HYFTORTMGel, 0.2%?

HYFTORTMGel, 0.2% should not be used:

- on children younger than 3 years of age.
- if you are allergic to HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2% or its ingredients.
  See the end of this Medication Guide for a complete list of ingredients.
- In pregnant women
- Applied to bleeding or ulcerated tumors

What should I tell my doctor before starting HYFTORTMGel, 0.2%?

Before you start using HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2%, you and your doctor should talk about all of your medical conditions, including if you:

- have any infection on your skin.
- have been told you have a weakened immune system or have cancer.
- are pregnant, breastfeeding, or planning to become pregnant.

Tell your doctor about all the medicines you take and skin products you use including prescription and nonprescription medicines, vitamins, and herbal supplements.

Know the medicines you take.
Keep a list of them with you to show your doctor and pharmacist each time you get a new medicine.

How should I use HYFTORTMGel, 0.2%?

- Use HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2% exactly as prescribed.
- Use HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2% only on areas of your skin that have angiofibroma.

- Call your doctor if:
  - your symptoms get worse with HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2%.
  - you get an infection on your skin.

To apply HYFTOR:

- Wash your hands before applying HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2%.
- Apply a thin layer of HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2% twice daily to angiofibroma affected areas.
- Use the smallest amount of HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2% to cover the angiofibroma.
- If you are a patient or caregiver applying HYFTORTM Gel.
  0.2% to a patient,, wash your hands with soap and water after applying HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2%.
  This should remove any gel left on the hands.
- Do not bathe, shower, or swim immediately after applying HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2% as this could wash off the gel.
- You can use sunscreen and moisturizers with HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2%.
  Make sure you check with your doctor first about the products that are right for you.
  If you use sunscreen or moisturizers, apply them after HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2%.

What should I avoid while using HYFTORTMGel, 0.2%?

- Do not use ultraviolet light therapy, sun lamps, or tanning beds during treatment with HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2%.
- Limit sun exposure during treatment with HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2%.
  Ask your doctor what types of protection from the sun you should use.
- Do not cover the skin being treated with bandages, dressings or wraps.
- Avoid getting HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2% in the eyes or mouth.
  Do not swallow HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2.

What are the possible side effects of HYFTORTMGel, 0.2%?

Please read the first section of this Medication Guide.

The most common side effects of HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2% at the skin application site are dermal irritation of the skin.

Other side effects.

Talk to your doctor if you have a skin infection or if side effects continue or bother you.

These are not all the side effects with HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2%.
Ask your doctor or pharmacist for more information.

Call your doctor for medical advice about side effects.
You may report side effects to FDA at 1-800-FDA-1088

How should I store HYFTORTMGel, 0.2%?

- Store HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2% under refrigeration at 36-46°F (2-8°C).
- Do not leave HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2% out of refrigeration, greater than 46°F (8°C).
- Do not leave HYFTORTM Gel 0.2% at extreme temperatures, above 104°F(40°C).
- Keep HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2% away from an open flame.
- Make sure the cap on the tube is tightly closed after each use.
- Keep HYFTORTM Gel, 0.2% and all medicines out of the reach of children.
