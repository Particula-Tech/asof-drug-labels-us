# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Pyridostigmine bromide tablets, USP, 30 mg, are round, white tablets imprinted with the letters "PBT."

Immediate Container: Twenty-one (21) tablets individually sealed in a blister or strip package which is supplied in a protective sleeve.

NDC: 72458-128-01 NSN 6505-01-178-7903 (refrigerator) NDC: 72458-128-03 NSN 6505-01-676-2174 (controlled room temperature)

The NSN refers to the actual unit that is ordered from supply (if someone orders 1 of this stock number they will get one package of 10 blister packs).

Storage

Store refrigerated (2°C and 8°C) (36°F to 46°F) Or Store at controlled room temperature between 20°C and 25°C (68°F-77°F) for up to 5 years Protect from light.
Discard the contents of the blister package after removal from controlled room temperature for more than a total of 3 months.
Do not use after the 5-year expiration date provided on the package.
