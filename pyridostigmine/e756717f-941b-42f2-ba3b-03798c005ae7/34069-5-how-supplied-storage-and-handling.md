# HOW SUPPLIED

Tablets, are available as white, flat-faced tablets containing 30 mg pyridostigmine bromide, USP in bottles of 21 (NDC 58657-810-21) and cartons of 30 (3 x 10 blister packs - NDC 58657-810-30) and cartons of 210 tablets (21 blister cards of 10 tablets - NDC 58657-810-10).
Each tablet is White to off white, round, flat tablet, debossed IT above bisect and 106 below bisect on one side, other side is plain.

Store pyridostigmine bromide tablets, USP at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° -30°C (59° -86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Keep pyridostigmine bromide tablets, USP in a dry place with the silica gel enclosed
