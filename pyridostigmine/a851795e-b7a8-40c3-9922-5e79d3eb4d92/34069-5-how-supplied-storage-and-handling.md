# HOW SUPPLIED

Syrup, 60 mg pyridostigmine bromide per teaspoonful (5 mL) and 5% alcohol – bottles of 16 fluid ounces (1 pint) (NDC 0187-3012-20).

Tablets, 60 mg pyridostigmine bromide each – bottles of 100 (NDC 0187-3010-30) and 500 (NDC 0187-3010-40).

Timespan Tablets, 180 mg pyridostigmine bromide each – bottles of 30 (NDC 0187-3013-30).

Note: Because of the hygroscopic nature of the Timespan Tablets, mottling may occur.
This does not affect their efficacy.

Store Mestinon Syrup, Tablets, and Timespan Tablets at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C-30°C (59°F-86F).
