# HOW SUPPLIED

Extended-release Tablets are available as light straw colored capsule-shaped tablets containing 180 mg Pyridostigmine bromide in bottles of 30 (NDC 64980-220-03).
Each tablet is engraved "NM 180" on one side and is single-scored on the other.
Note: Because of the hygroscopic nature of the Extended-release Tablets, mottling may occur.
This does not affect their efficacy.
Store Pyridostigmine Bromide Extended-Release Tablets, 180 mg at 25°C (77°F), excursions permitted to 15°C-30°C (59°F-86°F).
Keep Pyridostigmine Bromide Extended-Release Tablets, 180 mg in a dry place with the silica gel enclosed.
