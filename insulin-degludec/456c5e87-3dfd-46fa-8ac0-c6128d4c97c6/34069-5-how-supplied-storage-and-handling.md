## 16.2 Recommended Storage

Dispense in the original sealed carton with the enclosed Instructions for Use.

Store TRESIBA vials in the original carton to protect from light.
Unused TRESIBA should be stored in a refrigerator (36ºF to 46ºF [2ºC to 8ºC]).
Do not store in the freezer or directly adjacent to the refrigerator cooling element.
Do not freeze.
Do not use TRESIBA if it has been frozen.
