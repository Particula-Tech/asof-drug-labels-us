# 11 DESCRIPTION

TRESIBA (insulin degludec injection) is a long-acting basal human insulin analog for subcutaneous injection.
Insulin degludec is produced by a process that includes expression of recombinant DNA in Saccharomyces cerevisiae followed by chemical modification.

Insulin degludec differs from human insulin in that the amino acid threonine in position B30 has been omitted and a side-chain consisting of glutamic acid and a C16 fatty acid has been attached (chemical name: LysB29(Nε-hexadecandioyl-γ-Glu) des(B30) human insulin).
Insulin degludec has a molecular formula of C274H411N65O81S6 and a molecular weight of 6103.97.

TRESIBA is a sterile, aqueous, clear, and colorless solution that contains insulin degludec 100 units/mL (U-100) or 200 units/mL (U-200).

For the 100 units/mL solution, each mL contains 100 units (600 nmol) of insulin degludec and glycerol (19.6 mg), metacresol (1.72 mg), phenol (1.50 mg), zinc (32.7 mcg), and Water for Injection, USP.

For the 200 units/mL solution, each mL contains 200 units (1200 nmol) of insulin degludec and glycerol (19.6 mg), metacresol (1.72 mg), phenol (1.50 mg), zinc (71.9 mcg), and Water for Injection, USP.

TRESIBA has a pH of approximately 7.6.
Hydrochloric acid or sodium hydroxide may be added to adjust pH.
