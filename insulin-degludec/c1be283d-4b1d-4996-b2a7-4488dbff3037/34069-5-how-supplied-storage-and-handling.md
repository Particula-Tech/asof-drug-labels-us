## 16.2 Recommended Storage

Dispense in the original sealed carton with the enclosed Instructions for Use.

Store Insulin Degludec vials in the original carton to protect from light.
Store unused Insulin Degludec in a refrigerator (36ºF to 46ºF [2ºC to 8ºC]).
Do not store in the freezer or directly adjacent to the refrigerator cooling element.
Do not freeze.
Do not use Insulin Degludec if it has been frozen.
