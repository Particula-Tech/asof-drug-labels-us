# DESCRIPTION

Each tablet of Phenergan contains 12.5 mg, 25 mg, or 50 mg promethazine HCl.
The inactive ingredients present are lactose, magnesium stearate, and methylcellulose.
Each dosage strength also contains the following:

12.5 mg–FD&C Yellow 6 and saccharin sodium; 25 mg–saccharin sodium; 50 mg–FD&C Red 40.

Each rectal suppository of Phenergan contains 12.5 mg, 25 mg, or 50 mg promethazine HCl with ascorbyl palmitate, silicon dioxide, white wax, and cocoa butter.
Phenergan Suppositories are for rectal administration only.

Promethazine HCl is a racemic compound; the empirical formula is C17H20N2S•HCl and its molecular weight is 320.88.
