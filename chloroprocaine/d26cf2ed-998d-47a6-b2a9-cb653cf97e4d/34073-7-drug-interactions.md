# 7 DRUG INTERACTIONS

Concurrent administration of vasopressor drugs (for the treatment of hypotension related to obstetric blocks) and ergot-type oxytocic drugs may cause severe, persistent hypertension or cerebrovascular accidents.

The para-aminobenzoic acid metabolite of chloroprocaine inhibits the action of sulfonamides.
Therefore, avoid use in any condition in which a sulfonamide drug is being employed [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].

No studies have been performed on interactions between chloroprocaine and class III antiarrhythmics (e.g., amiodarone).
Carefully monitor these patients for cardiovascular effects.

The combination of various local anesthetics may result in additive effects affecting the cardiovascular system and the central nervous system.
Monitor these patients for signs and symptoms of local anesthetic toxicity.
