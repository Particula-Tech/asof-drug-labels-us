# HOW SUPPLIED

NESACAINE (chloroprocaine HCl Injection, USP) with preservatives is supplied as follows:

1% solution (NDC 0186-0971-66) in 30 mL multiple dose vials

2% solution (NDC 0186-0972-66) in 30 mL multiple dose vials

NESACAINE-MPF (chloroprocaine HCl Injection, USP) without preservatives and without EDTA is supplied as follows:

2% solution (NDC 0186-0991-66) in 20 mL single dose vials

3% solution (NDC 0186-0992-66) in 20 mL single dose vials

Keep from freezing.
Protect from light.
Store at controlled room temperature 15−30°C (59−86°F).
