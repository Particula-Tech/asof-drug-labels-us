# HOW SUPPLIED

Amitriptyline hydrochloride tablets, USP for oral administration are available as:

10 mg: Light orange to orange, round film coated tablet debossed with 'LS' on one side and '201' on other side and supplied as:

NDC 70756-201-1 1 bottles of 100 with child-resistant closure

NDC 70756-201-1 2 bottles of 1000

25 mg: Light green to green, round film coated tablet debossed with 'LS' on one side and '202' on other side and supplied as:

NDC 70756-202-1 1 bottles of 100 with child-resistant closure

NDC 70756-202-1 2 bottles of 1000

50 mg: Light brown to brown, round film coated tablet debossed with 'LS' on one side and '203' on other side and supplied as:

NDC 70756-203-1 1 bottles of 100 with child-resistant closure

NDC 70756-203-1 2 bottles of 1000

75 mg: Light blue to blue, round film coated tablet debossed with 'LS' on one side and '204' on other side and supplied as:

NDC 70756-204-1 1 bottles of 100 with child-resistant closure

100 mg: Light orange to orange, round film coated tablet debossed with 'LS' on one side and '205' on other side and supplied as:

NDC 70756-205-1 1 bottles of 100 with child-resistant closure

150 mg: Light brown to brown, capsules shaped film coated tablet debossed with 'LS' on one side and '206' on other side and supplied as:

NDC 70756-206-1 1 bottles of 100 with child-resistant closure

Store at 20° - 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP

KEEP OUT OF THE REACH OF CHILDREN.
