# HOW SUPPLIED

Amitriptyline hydrochloride tablets, USP for oral administration are available as:

10 mg: Light pink to pink, round film coated tablet debossed with 'LS' on one side and '201' on other side and supplied as:

NDC 70756-201-11 bottles of 100 with child-resistant closure

NDC 70756-201-12 bottles of 1000

25 mg: Light green to green, round film coated tablet debossed with 'LS' on one side and '202' on other side and supplied as:

NDC 70756-202-11 bottles of 100 with child-resistant closure

NDC 70756-202-12 bottles of 1000

50 mg: Light brown to brown, round film coated tablet debossed with 'LS' on one side and '203' on other side and supplied as:

NDC 70756-203-11 bottles of 100 with child-resistant closure

NDC 70756-203-12 bottles of 1000

75 mg: Light blue to blue, round film coated tablet debossed with 'LS' on one side and '204' on other side and supplied as:

NDC 70756-204-11 bottles of 100 with child-resistant closure

100 mg: Light orange to orange, round film coated tablet debossed with 'LS' on one side and '205' on other side and supplied as:

NDC 70756-205-11 bottles of 100 with child-resistant closure

150 mg: White to off white, capsules shaped film coated tablet debossed with 'LS' on one side and '206' on other side and supplied as:

NDC 70756-206-11 bottles of 100 with child-resistant closure

Store at 20° - 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP

Keep this and all medication out of the reach of children.
