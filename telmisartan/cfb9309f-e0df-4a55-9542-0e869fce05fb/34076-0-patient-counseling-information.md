# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

See FDA-approved Patient Labeling (17.2).

## 17.1 Pregnancy

Inform female patients of childbearing age about the consequences of exposure to drugs that act on the renin-angiotensin system.
Advise these patients to report pregnancies to their physicians as soon as possible [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
