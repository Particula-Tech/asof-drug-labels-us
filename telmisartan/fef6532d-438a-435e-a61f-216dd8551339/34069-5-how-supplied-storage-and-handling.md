# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Telmisartan Tablets USP, 20 mg are white to off-white, round, flat faced beveled edged uncoated tablets, debossed with ‘H’ on one side and ‘74’ on the other side.
3 x 10 Unit-dose Tablets NDC 65862-867-03 Telmisartan Tablets USP, 40 mg are white to off-white, oval, biconvex uncoated tablets, debossed with ‘H’ on one side and ‘75’ on the other side.
3 x 10 Unit-dose Tablets NDC 65862-868-03 Telmisartan Tablets USP, 80 mg are white to off-white, oval, biconvex uncoated tablets, debossed with ‘H’ on one side and ‘73’ on the other side.
3 x 10 Unit-dose Tablets NDC 65862-869-03 Storage Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from moisture.
Tablets should not be removed from blisters until immediately before administration.
