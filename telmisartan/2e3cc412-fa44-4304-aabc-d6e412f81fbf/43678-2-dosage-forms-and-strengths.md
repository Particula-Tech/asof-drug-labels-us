# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

- 20 mg are mottled light brown to mottled brown-colored, round-shaped, flat face beveled edge, uncoated tablets debossed with ‘471’ on one side and plain on the other side.
- 40 mg are mottled light brown to mottled brown-colored, oblong-shaped, biconvex, uncoated tablets debossed with ‘472’ on one side and plain on the other side.
- 80 mg are mottled light brown to mottled brown-colored, oblong-shaped, biconvex, uncoated tablets debossed with ‘473’ on one side and plain on the other side.
