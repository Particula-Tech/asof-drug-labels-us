## 17.1 Pregnancy

Female patients of childbearing age should be told about the consequences of exposure to telmisartan during pregnancy.
Discuss treatment options with women planning to become pregnant.
Patients should be asked to report pregnancies to their physicians as soon as possible [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
