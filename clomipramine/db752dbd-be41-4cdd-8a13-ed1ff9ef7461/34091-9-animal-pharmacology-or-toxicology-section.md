# ANIMAL TOXICOLOGY

Phospholipidosis and testicular changes commonly associated with tricyclic compounds have been observed with clomipramine.
In chronic rat studies, changes related to clomipramine consisted of systemic phospholipidosis, alteration in the testes (atrophy, mineralization) and secondary changes in other tissues.
In addition cardiac thrombosis and dermatitis/keratitis were observed in rats treated for 2 years at doses which were 24 and 10 times the maximum recommended human daily dose (MRHD), respectively, on a mg/kg basis, and 4 and 1.5 times the MRHD, respectively on a mg/m2 basis.
