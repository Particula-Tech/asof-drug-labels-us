# DESCRIPTION

Clomipramine Hydrochloride Capsules, USP is an antiobsessional drug that belongs to the class (dibenzazepine) of pharmacologic agents known as tricyclic antidepressants.
Clomipramine Hydrochloride Capsules, USP is available as capsules of 25, 50, and 75 mg for oral administration.

Clomipramine hydrochloride, USP is a white to slightly yellow crystalline powder.
It is freely soluble in water and methylene chloride; soluble in alcohol.
