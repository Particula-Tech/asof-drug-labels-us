# DESCRIPTION

Anafranil®, (clomipramine hydrochloride capsules USP), is an antiobsessional drug that belongs to the class (dibenzazepine) of pharmacologic agents known as tricyclic antidepressants.
Anafranil is available as capsules of 25, 50, and 75 mg for oral administration.

Clomipramine hydrochloride USP is a white to off-white crystalline powder.
It is freely soluble in water, in methanol, and in methylene chloride, and insoluble in ethyl ether and in hexane.
