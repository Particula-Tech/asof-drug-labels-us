# HOW SUPPLIED

Ipratropium bromide inhalation solution is a clear, colorless solution containing 2.5 mL, packaged in cartons as listed below:

NDC 62135-830-84, 6 foil pouches, each containing 5 vials, total 30 vials per carton

Each vial is made from a low density polyethylene (LDPE) resin.

Storage and Handling

Store between 15° C and 30° C (59° F and 86° F). Protect from light.
Store in pouch until time of use.
