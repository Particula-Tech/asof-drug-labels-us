# OVERDOSAGE

Acute overdose by inhalation is unlikely since ipratropium bromide is not well absorbed systemically after inhalation or oral administration.
Oral median lethal doses of ipratropium bromide were greater than 1001 mg/kg in mice (approximately 20,000 times the maximum recommended daily inhalation dose in adults on a mg/m2 basis); 1,663 mg/kg in rats (approximately 66,000 times the maximum recommended daily inhalation dose in adults on a mg/m2 basis); and 400 mg/kg in dogs (approximately 53,000 times the maximum recommended daily inhalation dose in adults on a mg/m2 basis).
