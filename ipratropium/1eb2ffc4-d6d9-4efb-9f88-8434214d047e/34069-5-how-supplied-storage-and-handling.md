# HOW SUPPLIED

ATROVENT Nasal Spray 0.06% is supplied in a white high density polyethylene (HDPE) bottle fitted with a metered nasal spray pump, a green safety clip to prevent accidental discharge of the spray, and a clear plastic dust cap.
It contains 16.6 g of product formulation, 165 sprays, each delivering 42 mcg of ipratropium bromide per spray (70 µL), or 10 days of therapy at the maximum recommended dose (two sprays per nostril four times a day) (NDC 0597-0086-76).

Store tightly closed at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15°- 30°C (59°-86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Avoid freezing.
Keep out of reach of children.
Do not spray in the eyes.
