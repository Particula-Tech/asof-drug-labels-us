# HOW SUPPLIED

Ipratropium bromide nasal solution 0.06% (Nasal Spray) is supplied in a white high density polyethylene (HDPE) bottle fitted with a metered nasal spray pump, a safety clip to prevent accidental discharge of the spray, and a clear plastic dust cap.
It contains 16.6 g of product formulation, 165 sprays, each delivering 42 mcg of ipratropium bromide per spray (70 μL), or 10 days of therapy at the maximum recommended dose (two sprays per nostril four times a day).
