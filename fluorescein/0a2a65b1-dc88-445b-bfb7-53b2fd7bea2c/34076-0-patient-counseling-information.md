# 17. PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

After administration of Fluorescein sodium, skin will attain a temporary yellowish discoloration.
Urine attains a bright yellow color.
Discoloration of the skin usually fades in 6 to 12 hours and usually fades in urine in 24 to 36 hours.
[see WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS AND ADVERSE REACTIONS (6.1)].
