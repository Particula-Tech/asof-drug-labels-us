## 8.4 Pediatric Use

The safety and effectiveness of Fluorescein Sodium and Benoxinate Hydrochloride Ophthalmic Solution, 0.3%/0.4% have been established for pediatric patients.
Use of Fluorescein Sodium and Benoxinate Hydrochloride Ophthalmic Solution, 0.3%/0.4% is supported in pediatric patients by evidence from adequate and well controlled studies.

## 8.5 Geriatric Use

No overall differences in safety or effectiveness have been observed between elderly and younger patients.
