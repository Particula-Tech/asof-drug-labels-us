# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Fluorescein Sodium and Benoxinate Hydrochloride Ophthalmic Solution, 0.3%/0.4% is supplied as a sterile, aqueous, topical ophthalmic solution with a fill volume of 5 mL in a 6 mL amber glass bottle and a black polypropylene cap with a sterilized rubber dropper bulb and glass pipette.

NDC 24208-734-05

Storage: Store in a refrigerator at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F).
Protect from light.
After opening, Fluorescein Sodium and Benoxinate Hydrochloride Ophthalmic Solution, 0.3%/0.4% can be stored up to one month if stored at room temperature or until the expiration date on the bottle if stored in refrigerated conditions.
Keep tightly closed.
