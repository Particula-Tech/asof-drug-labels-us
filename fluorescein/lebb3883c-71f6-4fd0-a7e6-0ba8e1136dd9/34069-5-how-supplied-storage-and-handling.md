# HOW SUPPLIED

FLUORESCITE®(fluorescein injection, USP) 10% is supplied in a single-use 5 mL glass vial with a gray FluroTec coated chlorobutyl (latex free) stopper and purple flip-off aluminum seal.
It contains a sterile, red-orange solution of fluorescein sodium.

## Storage

Store at 2°- 25°C (36°- 77°F).

Do Not Freeze
