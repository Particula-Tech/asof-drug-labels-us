# HOW SUPPLIED

Furadantin Oral Suspension is available in: NDC 59630-450-60 glass amber bottle of 60 mL NDC 59630-450-16 glass amber bottle of 470 mL Avoid exposure to strong light which may darken the drug.

It should be dispensed in glass amber bottles.
