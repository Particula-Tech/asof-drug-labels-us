# HOW SUPPLIED:

Furadantin Oral Suspension is available in: NDC 59630-450-16 glass amber bottle of 470 mL Avoid exposure to strong light which may darken the drug.
It is stable when stored between 20˚-25˚C (68˚-77˚F); excursions permitted to 15˚-30˚C (59˚-86˚F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from freezing.
It should be dispensed in glass amber bottles.
Keep out of reach of children.
Rx only.
