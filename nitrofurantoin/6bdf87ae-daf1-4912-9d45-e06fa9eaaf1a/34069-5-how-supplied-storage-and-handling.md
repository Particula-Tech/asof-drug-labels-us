# HOW SUPPLIED

Furadantin Oral Suspension is available in:

NDC 59630-450-08 glass amber bottle of 230 mL

Avoid exposure to strong light which may darken the drug.
It is stable when stored between 20°-25°C (68°-77°F); excursions permitted to 15-30°C (59-86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from freezing.
Shake vigorously.
Dispense in tight, light-resistant, plastic (PET) or glass container.
Use within 30 days.

Keep out of reach of children.

Rx only.
