# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

How Supplied Nitrofurantoin oral suspension (25 mg/5 mL) is available in: NDC 16571-740-24 PET amber bottle of 230 mL Nitrofurantoin oral suspension (50 mg/5 mL) is available in: NDC 16571-888-06 PET amber bottle of 60 mL Storage and Handling Avoid exposure to strong light which may darken the drug.
It is stable when stored between 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from freezing.
Shake vigorously.
Dispense in tight, light-resistant, plastic (PET) or glass container.
Use within 30 days.
