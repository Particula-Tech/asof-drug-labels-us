# HOW SUPPLIED

Nitrofurantoin oral suspension, USP (a clear, yellow colored, tutti fruity flavored suspension) containing 25 mg nitrofurantoin, USP in each teaspoonful (5 mL) is available as follows:

230 mL in an 8 oz amber bottle.

NDC 65162-689-88

Avoid exposure to strong light which may darken the drug.
It is stable when stored between 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from freezing.
Shake vigorously.
Dispense in tight, light-resistant, amber glass bottles.
Use within 30 days.

Keep out of reach of children.

Rx only.
