# STORAGE

Store unopened vials at 20°-25°C (68°-77°F); excursions permitted to 15°-30°C (59°-86°F) (see USP Controlled Room Temperature).

Acetylcysteine does not contain an antimicrobial agent, and care must be taken to minimize contamination of the sterile solution.
Dilutions of acetylcysteine should be used freshly prepared and utilized within one hour.
If only a portion of the solution in a vial is used, store the remaining undiluted portion in a refrigerator and use within 96 hours.
