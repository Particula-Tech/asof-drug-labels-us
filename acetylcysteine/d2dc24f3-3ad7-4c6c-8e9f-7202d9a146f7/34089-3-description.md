# DESCRIPTION

MUCOMYST brand of acetylcysteine is for inhalation (mucolytic agent) or oral administration (acetaminophen antidote), and available as sterile, unpreserved solutions (not for injection).
The solutions contain 20% (MUCOMYST-20) or 10% (MUCOMYST-10) acetylcysteine, with edetate disodium in purified water.
Sodium hydroxide is added to adjust pH to 7.
Acetylcysteine is the N-acetyl derivative of the naturally-occurring amino acid, cysteine.
The compound is a white crystalline powder with the molecular formula C5H9NO3S, a molecular weight of 163.2, and chemical name of N-acetyl-L-cysteine.
