# HOW SUPPLIED:

Acetylcysteine Solution, USP is available in rubber stoppered glass vials containing 30 mL.
The 20% solution may be diluted to a lesser concentration with either Sodium Chloride for Injection, Sodium Chloride for Inhalation, Sterile Water for Injection, or Sterile Water for Inhalation.

Acetylcysteine is sterile, not for injection and can be used for inhalation (mucolytic agent) or oral administration (acetaminophen antidote).

STORAGE

Store at 20° - 25°C (68° - 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° - 30°C (59° - 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Store in refrigerator 2° - 8°C (36° - 46°F) after opening.

Acetylcysteine does not contain an antimicrobial agent, and care must be taken to minimize contamination of the sterile solution.
Dilutions of acetylcysteine should be used freshly prepared and utilized within one hour.
If only a portion of the solution in a vial is used, store the remaining undiluted portion in a refrigerator and use within 96 hours.
