# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

Hypersensitivity Reactions Advise patients and caregivers that hypersensitivity reactions related to administration and infusion may occur during and after acetylcysteine injection treatment, including hypotension, wheezing, shortness of breath and bronchospasm [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
For specific treatment information regarding the clinical management of acetaminophen overdose, please contact your regional poison center at 1-800-222-1222, or alternatively, a special health professional assistance line for acetaminophen overdose at 1-800-525-6115.
