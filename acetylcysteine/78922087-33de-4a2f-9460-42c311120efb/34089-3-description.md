# 11 DESCRIPTION

Acetylcysteine is an antidote for the treatment of acetaminophen overdose.
It is the N-acetyl derivative of the naturally-occurring amino acid, cysteine.
Acetylcysteine is a white crystalline powder that is freely soluble in water, alcohol, practically insoluble in chloroform and in ether with the molecular formula C5H9NO3S, a molecular weight of 163.2, and chemical name of N-acetyl-L-cysteine.
