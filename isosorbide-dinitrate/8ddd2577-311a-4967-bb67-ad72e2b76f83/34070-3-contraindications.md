# CONTRAINDICATIONS

Isordil Titradose is contraindicated in patients who are allergic to isosorbide dinitrate or any of its ingredients.

Do not use Isordil Titradose in patients who are taking certain drugs for erectile dysfunction (phosphodiesterase inhibitors), such as sildenafil, tadalafil, or vardenafil.
Concomitant use can cause severe hypotension, syncope, or myocardial ischemia.

Do not use Isordil Titradose in patients who are taking the soluble guanylate cyclase stimulator riociguat.
Concomitant use can cause hypotension.
