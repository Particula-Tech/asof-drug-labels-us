# CONTRAINDICATIONS

The use of Ampicillin is contraindicated in individuals with a history of serious hypersensitivity reactions (e.g., anaphylaxis or Stevens-Johnson syndrome) to ampicillin or to other beta-lactam antibacterial drugs.
Ampicillin is also contraindicated in infections caused by penicillinase-producing organisms.

Ampicillin is contraindicated in patients with a previous history of cholestatic jaundice/hepatic dysfunction associated with treatment with ampicillin.
