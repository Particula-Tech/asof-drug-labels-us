# DESCRIPTION

Ampicillin trihydrate is a semisynthetic penicillin derived from the basic penicillin nucleus, 6- aminopenicillanic acid.
Ampicillin is designated chemically as (2S, 5R, 6R)-6-[(R)-2-Amino-2-phenylacetamido]-3,3- dimethyl-7-oxo-4-thia-1-azabicyclo [3.2.0] heptane-2- carboxylic acid.
Its structural formula is:

1. Molecular formula: C16H19N3O4S · 3 H2O Molecular weight: 403.5

Ampicillin capsules, USP for oral administration provide ampicillin trihydrate equivalent to 250 mg and 500 mg ampicillin.
Inactive ingredients: black iron oxide, gelatin, magnesium stearate, titanium dioxide, shellac, propylene glycol, ammonium hydroxide, and potassium hydroxide.
