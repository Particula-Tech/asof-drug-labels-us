# HOW SUPPLIED

Ampicillin for injection, USP for intramuscular or intravenous injection.
It is a white to off-white crystalline powder supplied in vials containing ampicillin sodium equivalent to 250 mg, 500 mg, 1 gram or 2 grams ampicillin per vial.

Storage Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect the constituted solution from freezing.
