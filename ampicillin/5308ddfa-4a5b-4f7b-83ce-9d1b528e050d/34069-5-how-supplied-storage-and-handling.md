# HOW SUPPLIED

Ampicillin for Injection, USP, Pharmacy Bulk Package, contains ampicillin sodium equivalent to 10 grams ampicillin per bottle.

NDC 0781-9409-95 10 gram Pharmacy Bulk Package – Packaged in 10s

## Storage

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Protect the constituted solution from freezing.
