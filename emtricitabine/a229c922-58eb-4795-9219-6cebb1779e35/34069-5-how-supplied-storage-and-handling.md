# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Emtricitabine Capsules 200mg are size '1' capsules with sky blue cap imprinted with "EMT" in black and white body imprinted with "200" in black.

Emtricitabine Capsules, 200mg are available as follows:

Bottles of 30 capsules (NDC 69097-642-02)

Bottles of 1000 capsules (NDC 69097-642-15)

- Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F - 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
- Dispense only in original container.
  Keep container tightly closed.
