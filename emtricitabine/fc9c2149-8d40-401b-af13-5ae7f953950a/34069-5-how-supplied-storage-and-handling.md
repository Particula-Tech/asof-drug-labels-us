# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Efavirenz, emtricitabine and tenofovir disoproxil fumarate tablets are available as follows:

600 mg/200 mg/300 mg - light-pink to pink, unscored, film-coated, modified capsule-shaped tablets, debossed with “TV” on one side of the tablet and with “C72” on the other side of the tablet, in bottles of 30 (NDC 0093-5234-56).

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

- Keep container tightly closed.
- Dispense only in original container.
