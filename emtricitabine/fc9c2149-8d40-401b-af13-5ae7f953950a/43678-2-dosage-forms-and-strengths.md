# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Efavirenz, emtricitabine and tenofovir disoproxil fumarate tablets are light-pink to pink, unscored, film-coated, modified capsule-shaped, debossed with “TV” on one side of the tablet and with “C72” on the other side of the tablet.
Each tablet contains 600 mg of efavirenz, 200 mg of emtricitabine, and 300 mg of tenofovir disoproxil fumarate (equivalent to 245 mg of tenofovir disoproxil).
