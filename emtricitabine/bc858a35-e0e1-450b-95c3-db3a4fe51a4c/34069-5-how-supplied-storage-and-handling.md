# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Emtricitabine and tenofovir disoproxil fumarate tablets are available as follows: 200 mg of emtricitabine and 300 mg of tenofovir disoproxil fumarate (equivalent to 245 mg of tenofovir disoproxil) are white capsule shaped, film-coated tablets debossed with "L-24" on one side and plain on the other surface.
Bottle of 30 NDC 33342-106-07 Store below 30°C (86° F). • Keep container tightly closed • Dispense only in original container
