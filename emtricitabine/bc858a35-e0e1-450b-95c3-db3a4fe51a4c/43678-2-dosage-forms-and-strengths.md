# 3 DOSAGE FORMS & STRENGTHS

Emtricitabine and tenofovir disoproxil fumarate are available in single dose strength: • Tablet: 200 mg of emtricitabine and 300 mg of tenofovir DF (equivalent to 245 mg of tenofovir disoproxil): white, capsule shaped, film coated, debossed with “L-24” on one side and with “701” on the other side.
