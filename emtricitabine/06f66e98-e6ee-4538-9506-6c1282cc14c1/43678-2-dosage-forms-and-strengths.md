# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Each DESCOVY tablet contains 200 mg of emtricitabine (FTC) and 25 mg of tenofovir alafenamide (TAF) (equivalent to 28 mg of tenofovir alafenamide fumarate).
The tablets are blue, rectangular-shaped, film-coated, debossed with "GSI" on one side and "225" on the other side.
