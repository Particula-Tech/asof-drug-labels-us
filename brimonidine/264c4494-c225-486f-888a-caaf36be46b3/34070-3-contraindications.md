# 4CONTRAINDICATIONS

## 4.1 Neonates and Infants (Pediatric Patients Younger than 2 Years Old)

ALPHAGAN P is contraindicated in neonates and infants (pediatric patients younger than 2 years old) [see Use in Specific Populations (8.4)].

## 4.2 Hypersensitivity Reactions

ALPHAGAN P is contraindicated in patients who have exhibited a hypersensitivity reaction to any component of this medication in the past.
