# 2DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

The recommended dosage is one drop of ALPHAGAN P in the affected eye(s) three times daily, approximately 8 hours apart.
ALPHAGAN P may be used concomitantly with other topical ophthalmic drug products to lower intraocular pressure.
If more than one topical ophthalmic product is to be used, the different products should be instilled at least 5 minutes apart.
