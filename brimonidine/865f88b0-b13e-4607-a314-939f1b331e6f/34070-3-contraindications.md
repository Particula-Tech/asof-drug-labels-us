# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

## 4.1 Neonates and Infants (under the age of 2 years)

- Brimonidine Tartrate Ophthalmic Solution is contraindicated in neonates and infants (under the age of 2 years) [see (8) USE IN SPECIFIC POPULATIONS].

## 4.2 Hypersensitivity Reactions

Brimodine Tartrate Ophthalmic Solution is contraindicated in patients who have exhibited a hypersensitivity reaction to any component of this medication in the past [see Adverse Reactions (6.1) and (6.2)].
