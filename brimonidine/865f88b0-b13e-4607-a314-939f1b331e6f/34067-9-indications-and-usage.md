# INDICATIONS AND USAGE:

Brimonidine tartrate ophthalmic solution 0.2% is indicated for lowering intraocular pressure in patients with open-angle glaucoma or ocular hypertension.
The lOP lowering efficacy of brimonidine tartrate ophthalmic solution 0.2% diminishes over time in some patients.
This loss of effect appears with a variable time of onset in each patient and should be closely monitored.
