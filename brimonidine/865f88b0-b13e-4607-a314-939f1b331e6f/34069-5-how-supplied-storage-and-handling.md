# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Brimonidine Tartrate Ophthalmic Solution 0.2% is supplied sterile in a plastic bottle with a purple cap and controlled drop tip in the following sizes:

5 mL - NDC 24208-411-05

10 mL - NDC 24208-411-10

15 mL - NDC 24208-411-15

Storage: Store between 15° - 25°C (59°-77°F).

KEEP OUT OF REACH OF CHILDREN.
