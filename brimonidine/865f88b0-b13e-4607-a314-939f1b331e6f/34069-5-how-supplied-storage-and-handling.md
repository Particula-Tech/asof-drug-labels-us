# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Brimonidine tartrate ophthalmic solution 0.2% is supplied sterile in a plastic bottle with a purple cap and controlled drop tip in the following sizes:

5 mL - NDC 24208-411-05 10 mL - NDC 24208-411-10 15 mL - NDC 24208-411-15

Storage:Store between 15°C to 25°C (59°F to 77°F).

Keep out of reach of children.
