# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

## 4.1 Neonates and Infants (Pediatric Patients Younger than 2 Years Old)

Brimonidine tartrate ophthalmic solution 0.1% and 0.15% is contraindicated in neonates and infants (pediatric patients younger than 2 years old) [see Use in Specific Populations (8.4)].

## 4.2 Hypersensitivity Reactions

Brimonidine tartrate ophthalmic solution 0.1% and 0.15% is contraindicated in patients who have exhibited a hypersensitivity reaction to any component of this medication in the past.
