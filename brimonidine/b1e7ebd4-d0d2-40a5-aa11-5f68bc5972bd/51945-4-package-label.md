Brimonidine Tartrate Opthalmic Solution

FOR TOPICAL OPTHALMIC USE ONLY

Each mL of Brimonidine tartrate ophthalmic solution, 0.15% contains: Active: brimonidine tartrate 0.15% (1.5 mg/mL); Inactives: povidone; boric acid; sodium borate; calcium chloride; magnesium chloride; potassium chloride; mannitol; sodium chloride; POLYQUAD* 0.001% (0.01 mg/mL); purified water; with hydrochloric acid and/or sodium hydroxide to adjust pH.

USUAL DOSAGE: One drop three times daily to affected eye(s), approximately eight hours apart.

Note: Store at 15°- 25°C (59° - 77°F).
