# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

## 4.1 Neonates and Infants (Pediatric Patients Younger than 2 Years Old)

Brimonidine tartrate ophthalmic solution, 0.15% is contraindicated in neonates and infants (pediatric patients younger than 2 years old) [see Use in Specific Populations (8.4)].

## 4.2 Hypersensitivity Reactions

Brimonidine tartrate ophthalmic solution, 0.15% is contraindicated in patients with hypersensitivity to any component of this product [see Adverse Reactions (6.1) and (6.2)].
