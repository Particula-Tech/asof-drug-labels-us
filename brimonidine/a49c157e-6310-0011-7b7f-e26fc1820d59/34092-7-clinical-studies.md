# 14 CLINICAL STUDIES

Two clinical trials of 3 months duration were conducted in patients with open-angle glaucoma or ocular hypertension to compare the IOP-lowering effect of SIMBRINZA (brinzolamide/brimonidine tartrate ophthalmic suspension) 1%/0.2% dosed three times daily to individually administered 1% brinzolamide three times daily and 0.2% brimonidine tartrate three times daily.

The IOP-lowering effect of SIMBRINZA ophthalmic suspension was 1 to 3 mmHg greater than monotherapy with either 1% brinzolamide or 0.2% brimonidine tartrate throughout the duration of the trials.
