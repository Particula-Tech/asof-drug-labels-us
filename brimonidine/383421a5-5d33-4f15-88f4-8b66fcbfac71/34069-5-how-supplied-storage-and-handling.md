# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Brimonidine tartrate ophthalmic solution, 0.2% is supplied sterile, in white opaque LDPE plastic bottles and natural nozzle with purple tear-off HDPE caps as follows:

5 mL in 5mL bottle NDC 14445-400-05 10 mL in 10 mL bottle NDC 14445-400-10 15 mL in 15 mL bottle NDC 14445-400-15.
STORAGE: Store between 20°- 25°C (68°- 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
KEEP OUT OF THE REACH OF CHILDREN.
TEAR AND DISCARD RING BEFORE USE Do not use if tear-off ring is broken or missing.
