# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

Apply a pea-size amount once daily to each of the five areas of the face: central forehead, chin, nose, each cheek.
MIRVASO topical gel should be applied smoothly and evenly as a thin layer across the entire face avoiding the eyes and lips.

Wash hands after applying MIRVASO topical gel.

MIRVASO topical gel is not for oral, ophthalmic, or intravaginal use.
