# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Sacubitril/valsartan tablets are available as unscored, oval shaped, biconvex, film-coated tablets, containing 24 mg of sacubitril and 26 mg of valsartan; 49 mg of sacubitril and 51 mg of valsartan; and 97 mg of sacubitril and 103 mg of valsartan.
All strengths are packaged in bottles as described below.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sacubitril/Valsartan |  | ”M” and | Bottle of 60 | Bottle of 180 | Bottle of 500 | Bottle of 1000 |
| 24 mg/26 mg | Purple | S1 | 280-60 | 280-18 | 280-01 | 280-02 |
| 49 mg/51 mg | Light yellow | S2 | 281-60 | 281-18 | 281-01 | 281-02 |

Store at 25°C (77°F), excursions permitted between 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from moisture.
