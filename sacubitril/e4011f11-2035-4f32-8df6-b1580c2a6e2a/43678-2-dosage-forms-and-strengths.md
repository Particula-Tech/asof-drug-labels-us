# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Sacubitril and valsartan tablets are supplied as unscored, oval shaped, film-coated tablets in the following strengths:

Sacubitril and valsartan tablets 24/26 mg, (sacubitril 24 mg and valsartan 26 mg) are violet white and debossed with “SAV” on one side and “001” on the other side.

Sacubitril and valsartan tablets 49/51 mg, (sacubitril 49 mg and valsartan 51 mg) are pale yellow and debossed with “SAV” on one side and “002” on the other side.

Sacubitril and valsartan tablets 97/103 mg, (sacubitril 97 mg and valsartan 103 mg) are pink to light pink and debossed with “SAV” on one side and “003” on the other side.
