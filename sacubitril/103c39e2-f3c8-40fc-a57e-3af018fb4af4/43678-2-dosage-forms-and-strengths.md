# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Sacubitril and valsartan tablets are supplied as oval shaped, bioconvex, film-coated tablets in the following strengths: Sacubitril and valsartan tablets 24 mg/26 mg, (sacubitril 24 mg and valsartan 26 mg) are White to off-white and debossed with “J13” on one side and “Plain” on the other side.
Sacubitril and valsartan tablets 49 mg/51 mg, (sacubitril 49 mg and valsartan 51 mg) are Light pink and debossed with “J14” on one side and “Plain” on the other side.
Sacubitril and valsartan tablets 97 mg/103 mg, (sacubitril 97 mg and valsartan 103 mg) are White to off-white and debossed with “J15” on one side and “Plain” on the other side.
