# HOW SUPPLIED

Levetiracetam Oral Solution 100 mg/mL is a clear, colorless, grape-flavored liquid.
It is supplied in 16 fl. oz. white HDPE bottles (NDC 0472-0235-16).

Storage

Store at 25 °C (77 °F); excursions permitted to 15-30 °C (59-86 °F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

PHARMACIST: Dispense in tight, light-resistant container with a child-resistant closure.
