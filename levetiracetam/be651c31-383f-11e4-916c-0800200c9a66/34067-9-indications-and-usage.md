# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

## 1.1 Partial-Onset Seizures

SPRITAM is indicated for the treatment of partial-onset seizures in patients 4 years of age and older weighing more than 20 kg.

## 1.2 Myoclonic Seizures in Patients with Juvenile Myoclonic Epilepsy

SPRITAM is indicated as adjunctive therapy for the treatment of myoclonic seizures in patients 12 years of age and older with juvenile myoclonic epilepsy.

## 1.3 Primary Generalized Tonic-Clonic Seizures

SPRITAM is indicated as adjunctive therapy for the treatment of primary generalized tonic‑clonic seizures in patients 6 years of age and older with idiopathic generalized epilepsy.
