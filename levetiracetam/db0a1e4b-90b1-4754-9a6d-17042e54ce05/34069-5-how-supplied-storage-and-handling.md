# HOW SUPPLIED

Levetiracetam tablets are supplied as follows:

Levetiracetam 250 mg tablets are blue, oblong-shaped, scored, film-coated tablets debossed with "OL" and "250" on one side.
They are supplied in white HDPE BOTTLE, PLASTICs containing 120 tablets (NDC 67850-011-01).

Levetiracetam 500 mg tablets are yellow, oblong-shaped, scored, film-coated tablets debossed with "OL" and "500" on one side.
They are supplied in white HDPE BOTTLE, PLASTICs containing 120 tablets (NDC 67850-012-01).

Levetiracetam 750 mg tablets are dark pink, oblong-shaped, scored, film-coated tablets debossed with "OL" and "750" on one side.
They are supplied in white HDPE BOTTLE, PLASTICs containing 120 tablets (NDC 67850-013-01).

Levetiracetam 1000 mg tablets are white, oblong-shaped, scored, film-coated tablets debossed with "OL" and "1000" on one side.
They are supplied in white HDPE BOTTLE, PLASTICs containing 60 tablets (NDC 67850-014-02).

## Pharmacist Dispensing

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container with a child-resistant closure.
Provide Patient Information.

## Storage

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15 to 30°C (59 to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
