# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Levetiracetam in Sodium Chloride Injection is a clear, colorless, sterile solution that is available in a single-dose 100 mL dual port bag with an aluminum over wrap.
The container closure is not made with natural rubber latex.
It is available in the following presentations:

| 10 bags per carton | 44567-500-10 |  |

## 16.2 Storage

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
