# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

KEPPRA XR 500 mg tablets are white, oblong-shaped, film-coated tablets imprinted with "UCB 500XR" in red on one side.
They are supplied in white HDPE bottles containing 60 tablets (NDC 50474-598-66).

KEPPRA XR 750 mg tablets are white, oblong-shaped, film-coated tablets imprinted with "UCB 750XR" in red on one side.
They are supplied in white HDPE bottles containing 60 tablets (NDC 50474-599-66).

## 16.2 Storage

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15-30°C (59-86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
