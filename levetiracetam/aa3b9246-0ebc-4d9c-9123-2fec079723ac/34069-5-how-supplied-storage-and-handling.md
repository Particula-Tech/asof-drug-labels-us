# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

KEPPRA (levetiracetam) 500 mg/5 mL injection is a clear, colorless, sterile solution.
It is supplied in single-use 5 mL vials, available in cartons of 10 vials (NDC 50474-002-63).

## 16.2 Storage

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15-30°C (59-86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
