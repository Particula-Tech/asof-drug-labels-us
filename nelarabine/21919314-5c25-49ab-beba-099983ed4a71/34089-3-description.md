# 11 DESCRIPTION

Nelarabine Injection is a prodrug of the cytotoxic deoxyguanosine analogue, 9-β-D-arabinofuranosylguanine (ara-G).

The chemical name for nelarabine is 2-amino-9-β-D-arabinofuranosyl-6-methoxy-9H-purine.
It has the molecular formula C11H15N5O5and a molecular weight of 297.27.

Nelarabine is slightly soluble in water and melts with decomposition between 209°C and 214°C.

Nelarabine Injection is supplied as a clear, colorless, sterile solution in glass single-dose vials.
Each vial contains 250 mg of nelarabine (5 mg nelarabine per mL) and the inactive ingredient sodium chloride, USP (4.5 mg per mL) in 50 mL Water for Injection, USP.
Nelarabine Injection is intended for intravenous infusion.
Hydrochloric acid and sodium hydroxide may have been used to adjust the pH.
The solution pH ranges from 5.0 to 7.0.
