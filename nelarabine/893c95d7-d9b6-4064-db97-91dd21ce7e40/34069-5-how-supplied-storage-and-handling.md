# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

ARRANON Injection is supplied as a clear, colorless, sterile solution in Type I, clear glass vials with a gray bromobutyl rubber stopper (not made with natural rubber latex) and a red snap-off aluminum seal.
Each vial contains 250 mg of nelarabine (5 mg nelarabine per mL) and the inactive ingredient sodium chloride (4.5 mg per mL) in 50 mL Water for Injection, USP.
Vials (NDC 0007-4401-01) are available in the following carton size:

NDC 0007-4401-06 (package of 6)

Store at 25° C (77° F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30° C (59° to 86° F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
