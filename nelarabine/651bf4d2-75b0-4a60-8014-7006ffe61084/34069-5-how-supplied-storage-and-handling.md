# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Nelarabine Injection is supplied as a clear, colorless, sterile solution in Type I, clear glass single-dose vials with a gray bromobutyl rubber stopper (not made with natural rubber latex) and an aluminum seal with a red snap-off cap.
Each vial contains 250 mg of nelarabine (5 mg nelarabine per mL) and the inactive ingredient sodium chloride (4.5 mg per mL) in 50 mL Water for Injection, USP.

Single-dose Vials are available in the following carton sizes:

NDC 68083-233-01 (single-dose vial)

NDC 68083-233-06 (package of 6)

Store at 20° to 25°C (68°-77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
