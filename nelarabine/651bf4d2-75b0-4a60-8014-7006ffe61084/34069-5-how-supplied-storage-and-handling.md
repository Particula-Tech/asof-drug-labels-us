# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Nelarabine Injection is supplied as a clear, colorless, sterile solution in Type I, clear glass single-dose vials with a gray bromobutyl rubber stopper (not made with natural rubber latex) and an aluminum flip-off seal.
Single-dose Vials are available in 250 mg/50 mL (5 mg/mL) strength and the following carton sizes: NDC 68083-233-01 (single-dose vial) NDC 68083-233-06 (package of 6) Discard Unused Portion.
Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
