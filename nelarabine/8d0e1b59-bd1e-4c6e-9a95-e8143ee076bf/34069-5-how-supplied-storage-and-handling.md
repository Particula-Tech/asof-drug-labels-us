# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Nelarabine Injection, 250 mg/50 mL (5 mg/mL) is supplied as a sterile, clear, colorless solution filled in Type I, clear glass single-dose vials with a gray chlorobutyl rubber stopper and a red flip-off aluminum seal.

It is available as follows:

50 mL single-dose vials (NDC 70121-1743-1) are available in the following carton sizes:

NDC 70121-1743-1 (package of 1).

NDC 70121-1743-4 (package of 6).

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Discard unused portion.

The vial stoppers are not made with natural rubber latex.
