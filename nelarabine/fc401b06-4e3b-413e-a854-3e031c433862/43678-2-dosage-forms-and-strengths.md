# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Nelarabine Injection is supplied as a clear, colorless, sterile solution in Type 1, clear glass single-dose vials with a grey chlorobutyl rubber stopper (not made with natural rubber latex) and an aluminum seal with a white snap-off cap.
Available as:

- 250 mg/50 mL (5 mg/mL) single-dose vial
