# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Capsules

- 100 mg: white, opaque hard gelatin capsules printed with “IP 101” with black ink on both cap and body and filled with white to off-white powder.
- 300 mg: buff, opaque hard gelatin capsules printed with “IP 102” with black ink on both cap and body and filled with white to off-white powder.
- 400 mg: light caramel, opaque hard gelatin capsules printed with “IP 103” with black ink on both cap and body and filled with white to off-white powder.
