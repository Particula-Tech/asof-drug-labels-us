# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Gabapentin capsules USP, 100 mg are supplied as white, opaque hard gelatin capsules printed with “IP 101” with black ink on both cap and body and filled with white to off-white powder.

They are available as follows:

Bottles of 30: NDC 65162-101-03 Bottles of 100: NDC 65162-101-10 Bottles of 500: NDC 65162-101-50 Bottles of 1000: NDC 65162-101-11

Gabapentin capsules USP, 300 mg are supplied as buff, opaque hard gelatin capsules printed with “IP 102” with black ink on both cap and body and filled with white to off-white powder.

They are available as follows:

Bottles of 30: NDC 65162-102-03 Bottles of 100: NDC 65162-102-10 Bottles of 500: NDC 65162-102-50 Bottles of 1000: NDC 65162-102-11

Gabapentin capsules USP, 400 mg are supplied as light caramel, opaque hard gelatin capsules printed with “IP 103” with black ink on both cap and body and filled with white to off-white powder.

They are available as follows:

Bottles of 30: NDC 65162-103-03 Bottles of 100: NDC 65162-103-10 Bottles of 500: NDC 65162-103-50 Bottles of 1000: NDC 65162-103-11

Store gabapentin capsules at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Dispense in tight (USP), child-resistant containers.
