# INDICATIONS AND USAGE

## Postherpetic Neuralgia

Gabapentin capsules are indicated for the management of postherpetic neuralgia in adults.

## Epilepsy

Gabapentin capsules are indicated as adjunctive therapy in the treatment of partial seizures with and without secondary generalization in patients over 12 years of age with epilepsy.
Gabapentin capsules are also indicated as adjunctive therapy in the treatment of partial seizures in pediatric patients age 3 to 12 years.
