# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

## 4.1 Pregnancy and Premenopausal Women

Anastrozole may cause fetal harm when administered to a pregnant woman and offers no clinical benefit to premenopausal women with breast cancer.
Anastrozole tablet is contraindicated in women who are or may become pregnant.
There are no adequate and well-controlled studies in pregnant women using anastrozole tablets.
If anastrozole tablet is used during pregnancy, or if the patient becomes pregnant while taking this drug, the patient should be apprised of the potential hazard to a fetus or potential risk for loss of the pregnancy.
[see Use in Specific Populations (8.1)]

## 4.2 Hypersensitivity

Anastrozole tablet is contraindicated in any patient who has shown a hypersensitivity reaction to the drug or to any of the excipients.
Observed reactions include anaphylaxis, angioedema, and urticaria.
[see Adverse Reactions (6.2)]
