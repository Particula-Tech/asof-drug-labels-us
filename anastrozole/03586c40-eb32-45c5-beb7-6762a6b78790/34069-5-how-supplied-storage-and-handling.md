# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Anastrozole tablets, USP 1 mg are white to off-white, round biconvex, film coated tablets, with “AHI” debossing on one side and plain on other side and are supplied as follows:

Bottles of 30 tablets with a child-resistant closure (NDC 16729-035-10)

Bottles of 90 tablets with a child-resistant closure (NDC 16729-035-15)

Bottles of 500 tablets (NDC 16729-035-16)

Bottles of 1000 tablets (NDC 16729-035-17)

Storage

Store at controlled room temperature, 20 to 25°C (68 to 77°F) [see USP].
Preserve in tight container.
