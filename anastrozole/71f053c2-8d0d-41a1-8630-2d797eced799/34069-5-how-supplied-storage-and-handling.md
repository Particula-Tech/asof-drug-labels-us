# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Anastrozole Tablets USP 1 mg are white, biconvex, film-coated tablets, debossed with ‘A1’ on one side and plain on the other side.

Bottles of 30 NDC 59651-236-30

Bottles of 90 NDC 59651-236-90

Storage

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
