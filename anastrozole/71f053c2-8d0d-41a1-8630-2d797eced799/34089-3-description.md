# 11 DESCRIPTION

Anastrozole tablets USP for oral administration contain 1 mg of anastrozole USP, a non-steroidal aromatase inhibitor.
It is chemically described as 1,3-Benzenediacetonitrile, a, a, a′, a′-tetramethyl-5-(1H-1,2,4-triazol-1-ylmethyl).
Its molecular formula is C17H19N5and its structural formula is:

Anastrozole USP is a white to off-white powder with a molecular weight of 293.4.
Anastrozole is freely soluble in methanol, acetone, alcohol, and tetrahydrofuran, and very soluble in acetonitrile.

Each tablet contains as inactive ingredients: hypromellose, lactose monohydrate, magnesium stearate, polyethylene glycol, povidone, sodium starch glycolate and titanium dioxide.
