# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Anastrozole Tablets, USP are available as follows:

1 mg – White to off-white, film-coated, round-shaped tablets, debossed with “TEVA” on one side of the tablet and with “A10” on the other, in bottles of 30 (NDC 0093-7536-56).

Storage

Store at controlled room temperature, 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP, with a child-resistant closure (as required).

Keep this and all medications out of the reach of children.
