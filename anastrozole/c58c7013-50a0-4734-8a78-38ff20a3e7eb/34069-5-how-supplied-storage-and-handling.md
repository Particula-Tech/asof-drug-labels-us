# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Anastrozole tablets are available as follows:

1 mg – White to off-white, film-coated, round-shaped tablets, debossed with “TEVA” on one side of the tablet and with “A10” on the other, in bottles of 30.

Storage

Store at controlled room temperature, 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
