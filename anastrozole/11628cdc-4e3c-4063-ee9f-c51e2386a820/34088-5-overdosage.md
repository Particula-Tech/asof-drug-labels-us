# OVERDOSAGE

Clinical trials have been conducted with ARIMIDEX, up to 60 mg in a single dose given to healthy male volunteers and up to 10 mg daily given to postmenopausal women with advanced breast cancer; these dosages were well tolerated.
A single dose of ARIMIDEX that results in life-threatening symptoms has not been established.
In rats, lethality was observed after single oral doses that were greater than 100 mg/kg (about 800 times the recommended human dose on a mg/m2 basis) and was associated with severe irritation to the stomach (necrosis, gastritis, ulceration, and hemorrhage).

In an oral acute toxicity study in the dog the median lethal dose was greater than 45 mg/kg/day.

There is no specific antidote to overdosage and treatment must be symptomatic.
In the management of an overdose, consider that multiple agents may have been taken.
Vomiting may be induced if the patient is alert.
Dialysis may be helpful because ARIMIDEX is not highly protein bound.
General supportive care, including frequent monitoring of vital signs and close observation of the patient, is indicated.
