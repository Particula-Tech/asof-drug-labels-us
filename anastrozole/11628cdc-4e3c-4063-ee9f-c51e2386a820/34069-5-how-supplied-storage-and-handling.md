# HOW SUPPLIED

White, biconvex, film-coated tablets containing 1 mg of anastrozole.
The tablets are impressed on one side with a logo consisting of a letter “A” (upper case) with an arrowhead attached to the foot of the extended right leg of the “A” and on the reverse with the tablet strength marking “Adx 1”.
These tablets are supplied in bottles of 30 tablets (NDC 0310-0201-30).

## Storage

Store at controlled room temperature, 20-25°C (68-77°F) [see USP].
