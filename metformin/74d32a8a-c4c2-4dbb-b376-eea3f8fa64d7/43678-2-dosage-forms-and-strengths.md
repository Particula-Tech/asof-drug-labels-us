# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Metformin Hydrochloride Extended-Release Tablets 500 mg are available as blue, film-coated, oval-shaped tablets debossed with “GMZ” on one side and “500” on the other side.

Metformin Hydrochloride Extended-Release Tablets 1000 mg are available as white, film-coated, oval-shaped tablets with “M1000” on one side.
