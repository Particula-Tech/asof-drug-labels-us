# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Metformin Hydrochloride Extended-Release Tablets are supplied as:

| 500 mg | Bottles of 100 | NDC 68682-021-50 | white, film-coated, oval-shaped, extended-release tablets with “M500” on one side. |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1,000 mg | Bottles of 90 | NDC 68682-018-90 | white, film-coated, oval-shaped, extended-release tablets with “M1000” on one side. |

- Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
