# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Metformin Hydrochloride Extended-Release Tablets 500 mg are available as white, film-coated, oval-shaped tablets with “M500” on one side.

Metformin Hydrochloride Extended-Release Tablets 1000 mg are available as white, film-coated, oval-shaped tablets with “M1000” on one side.

They are supplied as follows:

| NDC Code | Strength | Package |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 68682-021-50 | 500 mg | Bottles of 100 |
| 68682-018-90 | 1000 mg | Bottles of 90 |

- Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
