# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Metformin hydrochloride extended-release tablets USP, 500 mg are oval white tablets, with "OE" debossed on one side and "584" debossed on the other side.

They are available as follows:

| Bottles of 100 | NDC 51224-007-50 |
| --- | --- |
| Bottles of 500 | NDC 51224-007-60 |
| Bottles of 1000 | NDC 51224-007-70 |

Metformin hydrochloride extended-release tablets USP, 750 mg are white, capsule shaped tablets, with "OE" debossed on one side and "585" debossed on the other side.

They are available as follows:

| Bottles of 100 | NDC 51224-107-50 |
| --- | --- |
| Bottles of 500 | NDC 51224-107-60 |

## 16.2 Storage

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted from 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

Dispense in light-resistant containers.
