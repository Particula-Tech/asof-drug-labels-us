# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Metformin hydrochloride tablets, USP are available as:

- Tablets: 500mg –White, oval-shaped, film-coated tablets, debossed " CE 63" on one side and plain on the other side.
- Tablets: 625mg –White, oval-shaped, film-coated tablets, debossed " CE 64" on one side and plain on the other side.
- Tablets: 750mg –White, oval-shaped, film-coated tablets, debossed " CE 65" on one side and plain on the other side.
- Tablets: 850mg –White, oval-shaped, film-coated tablets, debossed " CE 66" on one side and plain on the other side.
- Tablets: 1000mg– White, oval-shaped, scored on both sides, film-coated tablets, debossed " CE 67" on one side and plain on the other side.
