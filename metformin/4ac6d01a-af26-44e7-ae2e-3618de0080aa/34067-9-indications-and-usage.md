# INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Metformin hydrochloride tablets as monotherapy, are indicated as an adjunct to diet and exercise to improve glycemic control in patients with type 2 diabetes.
Metformin hydrochloride tablets are indicated in patients 10 years of age and older.
Metformin hydrochloride tablets may be used concomitantly with a sulfonylurea or insulin to improve glycemic control in adults (17 years of age and older).
