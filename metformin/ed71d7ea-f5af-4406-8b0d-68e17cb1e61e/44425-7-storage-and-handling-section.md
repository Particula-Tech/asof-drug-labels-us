# STORAGE

Store at 20º to 25°C (68º to 77°F) Excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Keep tightly closed (protect from moisture).
Protect from light.
Avoid excessive heat and humidity.
