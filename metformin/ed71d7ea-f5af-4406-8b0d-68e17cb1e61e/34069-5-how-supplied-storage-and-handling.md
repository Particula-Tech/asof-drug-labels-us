# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

FORTAMET® (metformin hydrochloride) Extended-release Tablets are supplied as biconvex-shaped, film-coated extended-release tablets containing 500 mg or 1000 mg of metformin hydrochloride.

NDC 59630-574-60: 500 mg extended-release, white-colored, unscored tablets imprinted with Andrx logo and 574 on one side: bottles of 60.

NDC 59630-575-60: 1000 mg extended-release, white-colored, unscored tablets imprinted with Andrx logo and 575 on one side: bottles of 60.

## 16.2 Storage

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Keep tightly closed (protect from moisture).

Protect from light.

Avoid excessive heat and humidity.

KEEP THIS AND ALL MEDICATIONS OUT OF THE REACH OF CHILDREN.
