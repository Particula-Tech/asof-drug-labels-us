## 16.2 Storage

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature] excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F).
Avoid excessive heat and humidity.

Keep tightly closed (protect from moisture).
Protect from light.
