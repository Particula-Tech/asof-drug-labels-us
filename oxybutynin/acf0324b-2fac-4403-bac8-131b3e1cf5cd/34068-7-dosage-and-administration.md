# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

The contents of one sachet of GELNIQUE should be applied once daily to dry, intact skin on the abdomen, upper arms/shoulders, or thighs.
Application sites should be rotated.
Application of GELNIQUE should not be made to the same site on consecutive days [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].

GELNIQUE is for topical application only and should not be ingested.
