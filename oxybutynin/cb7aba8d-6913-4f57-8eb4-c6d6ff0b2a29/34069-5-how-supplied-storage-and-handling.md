# HOW SUPPLIED

Oxybutynin chloride tablets, USP are available as follows:

5 mg – Very pale blue, round, biconvex, scored tablets.
Debossed with PLIVA 456 on one side and scored on the other side.
Available in bottles of 100 (NDC 50111-456-01), 500 (NDC 50111-456-02), and 1,000 (NDC 50111-456-03) tablets.

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP, with a child-resistant closure (as required).

Store at 20º to 25ºC (68º to 77ºF) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Keep this and all medications out of the reach of children.
