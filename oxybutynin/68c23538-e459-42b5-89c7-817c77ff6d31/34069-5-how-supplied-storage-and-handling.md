# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Oxybutynin Chloride Extended Release Tablets are available in three dosage strengths, 5 mg (pale yellow), 10 mg (pink), and 15 mg (gray) and are imprinted on one side with "5 XL", "10 XL", or "15 XL" with black ink.
Oxybutynin Chloride Extended Release Tablets are supplied in bottles of 100 tablets.

## 16.1 Storage

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15–30°C (59–86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from moisture and humidity.
