# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Oxybutynin Chloride Extended Release Tablets are contraindicated in patients with urinary retention, gastric retention and other severe decreased gastrointestinal motility conditions, uncontrolled narrow-angle glaucoma.

Oxybutynin Chloride Extended Release Tablets are also contraindicated in patients who have demonstrated hypersensitivity to the drug substance or other components of the product.
There have been reports of hypersensitivity reactions, including anaphylaxis and angiodema.
