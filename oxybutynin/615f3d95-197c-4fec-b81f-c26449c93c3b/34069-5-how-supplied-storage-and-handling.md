# HOW SUPPLIED

Oxybutynin Chloride Syrup USP (5 mg/5 mL) is supplied in bottles of one pint (473 mL).
Oxybutynin Chloride Syrup (5 mg/5 mL) is a light bluish-green liquid with a characteristic raspberry flavor.

Pharmacist: Dispense in tight, light-resistance container as defined in the USP.
Store at controlled room temperature 15ºC-30ºC (59ºF- 86ºF).

Rx only.
