# OVERDOSAGE

The symptoms of overdosage with oxybutynin chloride may be any of those seen with other anticholinergic agents.
Symptoms may include signs of central nervous system excitation (e.g., restlessness, tremor. irritability, convulsions, delirium, hallucinations), flashing, fever, nausea, vomiting, tachycardia, hypotension or hypertension, respiratory failure, paralysis, and coma.

In the event of an overdosage or exaggerated response, treatment should be symptomatic and supportive.
Maintain respiration and induce emesis or perform gastric lavage (emesis is contraindicated in precomatose, convulsive, or psychotic state).
Activated charcoal may be administered as well as a cathartic.
Physostigmine may be considered to reverse symptoms of anticholinergic intoxication.
Hyperpyrexia may be treated symptomatically with ice bags or other cold applications and alcohol sponges.
