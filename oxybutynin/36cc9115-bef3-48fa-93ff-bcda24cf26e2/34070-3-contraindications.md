# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

DITROPAN XL® is contraindicated in patients with urinary retention, gastric retention and other severe decreased gastrointestinal motility conditions, uncontrolled narrow-angle glaucoma.

DITROPAN XL® is also contraindicated in patients who have demonstrated hypersensitivity to the drug substance or other components of the product.
There have been reports of hypersensitivity reactions, including anaphylaxis and angiodema.
