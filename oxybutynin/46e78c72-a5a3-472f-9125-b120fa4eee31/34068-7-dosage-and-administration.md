# DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

Oral Solution

Adults: The usual dose is one teaspoon (5 mg/5 mL) of oral solution two to three times a day.
The maximum recommended dose is one teaspoon (5 mg/5 mL) of oral solution four times a day.
A lower starting dose of 2.5 mg two or three times a day is recommended for the frail elderly.

Pediatric patients over 5 years of age: The usual dose is one teaspoon (5 mg/5 mL) of oral solution two times a day.
The maximum recommended dose is one teaspoon (5 mg/5mL) of oral solution three times a day.
