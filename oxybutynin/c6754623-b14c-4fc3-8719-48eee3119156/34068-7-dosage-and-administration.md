# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

The recommended dosage is three pumps of GELNIQUE 3% (84 mg/day) applied once daily to clean, dry, intact skin on the abdomen, or upper arms/shoulders, or thighs.
Apply immediately after actuating the dose.
Application sites may be rotated to reduce the potential for local site reactions [see Adverse Reactions (6.1)].
GELNIQUE 3% is for topical application only and should not be ingested.

Wash hands immediately after product application.
Patients should cover the application site with clothing after the gel has dried if direct skin-to-skin contact at the application site is anticipated [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3)].
