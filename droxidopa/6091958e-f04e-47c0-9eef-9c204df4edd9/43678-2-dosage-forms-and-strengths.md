# 3 DOSAGE FORMS & STRENGTHS

Droxidopa capsules are available in 100 mg, 200 mg, and 300 mg strengths as specified below.

- 100 mg: Hard gelatin capsules with "DRO" on the white opaque body and "100" on the light blue to blue cap.
- 200 mg: Hard gelatin capsules with "DRO" on the white opaque body and "200" on the light yellow to yellow cap
- 300 mg: Hard gelatin capsules with "DRO" on the white opaque body and "300" on the light green to green cap
