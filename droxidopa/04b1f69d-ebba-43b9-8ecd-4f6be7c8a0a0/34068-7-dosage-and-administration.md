# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

## 2.1 Dosing Information

The recommended starting dose of droxidopa capsules are 100 mg, taken orally three times daily: upon arising in the morning, at midday, and in the late afternoon at least 3 hours prior to bedtime (to reduce the potential for supine hypertension during sleep).
Administer droxidopa capsules consistently, either with food or without food.
Take droxidopa capsule whole.
Titrate to symptomatic response, in increments of 100 mg three times daily every 24 to 48 hours up to a maximum dose of 600 mg three times daily (i.e., a maximum total daily dose of 1,800 mg).

Monitor supine blood pressure prior to initiating droxidopa capsules and after increasing the dose.
Patients who miss a dose of droxidopa capsules should take their next scheduled dose.
