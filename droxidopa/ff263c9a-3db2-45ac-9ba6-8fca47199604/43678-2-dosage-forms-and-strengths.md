# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Droxidopa capsules are available in 100 mg, 200 mg, and 300 mg strengths as specified below.

- 100 mg: Light blue opaque cap and blue opaque body, hard gelatin capsule shell imprinted “DROH” on cap and “100” on the body filled with white to off white granules.
- 200 mg: Light yellow opaque cap and blue opaque body, hard gelatin capsule shell imprinted “DROH” on cap and “200” on the body filled with white to off white granules.
- 300 mg: Light green opaque cap and blue opaque body, hard gelatin capsule shell imprinted “DROH” on cap and “300” on the body filled with white to off white granules.
