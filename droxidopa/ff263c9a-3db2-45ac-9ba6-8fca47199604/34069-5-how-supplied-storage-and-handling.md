# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Droxidopa capsules are supplied in the following dosage strengths:

Droxidopa Capsules, 100 mg are light blue opaque cap and blue opaque body, hard gelatin capsule shell size “3” imprinted “DROH” on cap and “100” on the body filled with white to off white granules.

Bottles of 90 NDC 59651-375-90

Droxidopa Capsules, 200 mg are light yellow opaque cap and blue opaque body, hard gelatin capsule shell size “2” imprinted “DROH” on cap and “200” on the body filled with white to off white granules.

Bottles of 90 NDC 59651-376-90

Droxidopa Capsules, 300 mg are light green opaque cap and blue opaque body, hard gelatin capsule shell size “1” imprinted “DROH” on cap and “300” on the body filled with white to off white granules.

Bottles of 90 NDC 59651-377-90

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Droxidopa capsules should be stored at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container.
