# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

BLOXIVERZ (Neostigmine Methylsulfate Injection, USP) is available in the following:

| NDC No. | Strength | Vial Size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 51754-1210-3 | 0.5 mg/mL | 10 mL multiple-dose vials (supplied in packages of 10) |
| 51754-1220-3 | 1 mg/mL | 10 mL multiple-dose vials (supplied in packages of 10) |
| 51754-1200-3 | 1 mg/mL | 5 mL single-dose prefilled syringe (supplied in packages of 10) |

The vial stopper is not made with natural rubber latex.

BLOXIVERZ should be stored at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) (see USP Controlled Room Temperature).
Protect from light.
Store in carton until time of use.
