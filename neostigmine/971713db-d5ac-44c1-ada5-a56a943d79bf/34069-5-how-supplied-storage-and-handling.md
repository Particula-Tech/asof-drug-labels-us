# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Neostigmine methylsulfate injection, USP is a clear, colourless solution free from visible particles in a glass vial.
It is available as follows:

| NDC No. | Strength | Vial Size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 71839-105-24 | 0.5 mg/mL | 10 mL multiple-dose vials (supplied in packages of 24) |
| 71839-106-24 | 1 mg/mL | 10 mL multiple-dose vials (supplied in packages of 24) |

The vial stopper is not made with natural rubber latex.
Neostigmine Methylsulfate Injection, USP should be stored at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) (see USP Controlled Room Temperature).
Protect from light.
Store in carton until time of use.
