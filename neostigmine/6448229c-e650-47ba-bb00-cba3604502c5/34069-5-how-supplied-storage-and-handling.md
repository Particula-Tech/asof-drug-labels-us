# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Neostigmine Methylsulfate Injection is available in 10 mL multiple dose amber glass vials containing a clear, colorless solution of 0.5 mg/mL (NDC 63323-413-10) or 1 mg/mL (NDC 63323-415-10) of neostigmine methylsulfate injection supplied in packages of 10 vials.

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature] and protect from light.
Store vials in tray until ready for use.

This container closure is not made with natural rubber latex.
