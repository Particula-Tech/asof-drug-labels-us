# 16. HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Neostigmine Methylsulfate Injection, USP is a clear, colorless liquid available in the following:

| NDC No. | Strength | Vial Size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0641-6264-10 | 0.5 mg/mL | 10 mL multiple dose vials supplied in packages of 10 |
| 0641-6265-10 | 1 mg/mL | 10 mL multiple dose vials supplied in packages of 10 |

The vial stopper is not made with natural rubber latex.

Neostigmine Methylsulfate Injection, USP should be stored at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Protect from light.
Store in carton until time of use.
