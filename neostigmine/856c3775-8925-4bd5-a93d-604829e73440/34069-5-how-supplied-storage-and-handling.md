# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Neostigmine methylsulfate injection, USP is a clear, colorless, sterile, nonpyrogenic solution intended for intravenous use.
It is available as follows:

0.5 mg/mL:

10 mL, Multiple-dose Vial: NDC 70121-1478-1

10 Vials in a Carton: NDC 70121-1478-7

1 mg/mL:

10 mL, Multiple-dose Vial: NDC 70121-1479-1

10 Vials in a Carton: NDC 70121-1479-7

The vial stopper is not made with natural rubber latex.

Neostigmine methylsulfate injection, USP should be stored at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light.
Store in carton until time of use.
