# OVERDOSAGE

Treatment of overdosage should be supportive and individualized.
Beta-adrenergic stimulation and/or parenteral administration of calcium solutions (calcium chloride) have been effectively used in treatment of deliberate overdosage with oral Verapamil.
Clinically significant hypotensive reactions or high-degree AV block should be treated with vasopressor agents or cardiac pacing, respectively.
Asystole should be handled by the usual measures including isoproterenol hydrochloride, other vasopressor agents, or cardiopulmonary resuscitation (see Suggested Treatment of Acute Cardiovascular Adverse Reactions).
