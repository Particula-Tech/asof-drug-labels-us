# DESCRIPTION

Verapamil hydrochloride is a calcium antagonist or slow-channel inhibitor available as a sterile solution for intravenous injection in 5-mg (2 ml) and 10-mg (4 ml) vials.
Each form contains Verapamil HCl 2.5 mg/ml and sodium chloride 8.5 mg/ml in water for injection.
Hydrochloric acid and/or sodium hydroxide is used for pH adjustment.
The pH of the solution is between 4.1 and 6.0.

The structural formula of verapamil HCl is given below:

Benzeneacetonitrile, α-[3-[[2-(3,4-dimethoxyphenyl)ethyl] methylamino]propyl]-3,4-dimethoxy-α-(1-methylethyl) hydrochloride

Verapamil hydrochloride is an almost white, crystalline powder, practically free of odor, with a bitter taste.
It is soluble in water, chloroform, and methanol.
Verapamil hydrochloride is not chemically related to other antiarrhythmic drugs.
