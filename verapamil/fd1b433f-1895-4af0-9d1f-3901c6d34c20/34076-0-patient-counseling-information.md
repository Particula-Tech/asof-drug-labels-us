# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

- THE CONTENTS OF THE Verelan PM CAPSULE SHOULD NOT BE CRUSHED OR CHEWED.
  Verelan PM CAPSULES ARE TO BE SWALLOWED WHOLE OR THE ENTIRE CONTENTS OF THE CAPSULE SPRINKLED ONTO APPLESAUCE [see Dosage and Administration (2.2)].
- When the sprinkle method of administration is prescribed, explain the details of the proper technique to the patient.
  [See Dosage and Administration (2.2)].
