# ADVERSE REACTIONS

The following reactions were reported with intravenous Verapamil use in controlled U.S. clinical trials involving 324 patients:

Cardiovascular: Symptomatic hypotension (1.5%); bradycardia (1.2%); severe tachycardia (1.0%).
The worldwide experience in open clinical trials in more than 7,900 patients was similar.

Central nervous system effects: Dizziness (1.2%); headache (1.2%).
Although rare, cases of seizures during verapamil injection has been reported.

Gastrointestinal: Nausea (0.9%); abdominal discomfort (0.6%).

In rare cases of hypersensitivity, broncho/laryngeal spasm accompanied by itch and urticaria has been reported.

The following reactions were reported in a few patients: emotional depression, rotary nystagmus, sleepiness, vertigo, muscle fatigue, or diaphoresis.

Suggested Treatment of Acute Cardiovascular Adverse Reactions*

The frequency of these adverse reactions was quite low, and experience with their treatment has been limited.
