# HOW SUPPLIED

Verapamil hydrochloride tablets, USP are available as follows:

40 mg - Round, unscored, light peach film-coated tablets, debossed with WATSON 404 and are available in bottles of 100 (NDC 0591-0404-01).

80 mg - Round, white, scored film-coated tablets, debossed with WATSON 343 and are available in bottles of 100 (NDC 0591-0343-01), 500 (NDC 0591-0343-05), and 1000 (NDC 0591-0343-10).

120 mg - Round, white, scored film-coated tablets, debossed with WATSON 345 and are available in bottles of 100 (NDC 0591-0345-01), 500 (NDC 0591-0345-05), and 1000 (NDC 0591-0345-10).

Bottles of 100 are supplied with child-resistant closures.

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container with child-resistant closure.

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
