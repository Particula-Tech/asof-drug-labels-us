# DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

Desonide Ointment should be applied to the affected area as a thin film from two to four times daily depending on the severity of the condition.

As with other corticosteroids, therapy should be discontinued when control is achieved.
If no improvement is seen within 2 weeks, reassessment of diagnosis may be necessary.

Desonide Ointment should not be used with occlusive dressings.
