# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Tenofovir disoproxil fumarate tablets, 300 mg, are light blue, oval shaped, film-coated tablet, debossed “CE 54” on one side, plain on the other side and containing 300 mg of tenofovir disoproxil fumarate, which is equivalent to 245 mg of tenofovir disoproxil.
They are supplied as follows:

Bottles of 30s (NDC 62135-466-30)

Store tenofovir disoproxil fumarate tablets at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F), excursions permitted from 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) (See USP Controlled Room Temperature).

Keep the bottle tightly closed.
Dispense only in original container.
Do not use if seal over bottle opening is broken or missing.
