# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Tenofovir disoproxil fumarate tablets 300 mg contain 300 mg of tenofovir disoproxil fumarate, which is equivalent to 245 mg of tenofovir disoproxil.
The tablets are light blue, oval shaped, film-coated tablets, debossed “CE 54” on one side, and plain on the other side.
