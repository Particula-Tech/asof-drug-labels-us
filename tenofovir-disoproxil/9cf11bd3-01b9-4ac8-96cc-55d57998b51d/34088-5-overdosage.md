# 10 OVERDOSAGE

If overdose occurs, the patient must be monitored for evidence of toxicity, and standard supportive treatment applied as necessary.

Tenofovir is efficiently removed by hemodialysis with an extraction coefficient of approximately 54%.
Following a single 300 mg dose of tenofovir disoproxil fumarate tablet, a four-hour hemodialysis session removed approximately 10% of the administered tenofovir dose.
