# 3 DOSAGE FORMS & STRENGTHS

Tenofovir disoproxil fumarate is available as tablets.

300 mg Tablets: 300mg of TDF, which is (equivalent to 245 mg of tenofovir disoproxil).
The tablets are blue coloured, oval shaped, biconvex, film coated tablets debossed with 'CL 77' on one side and plain on other side.
