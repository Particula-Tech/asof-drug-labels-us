# 3 DOSAGE FORMS & STRENGTHS

Tenofovir disoproxil fumarate is available as tablets.
Tenofovir disoproxil fumarate tablets contain 300 mg of tenofovir disoproxil fumarate, which is equivalent to 245 mg of tenofovir disoproxil.
The tablets are white, almond shaped, film coated debossed with ‘H’ on one side and ‘123’ on other side.
