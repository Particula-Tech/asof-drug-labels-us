# WARNING: POSTTREATMENT EXACERBATION OF HEPATITIS

Severe acute exacerbations of hepatitis have been reported in HBV-infected patients who have discontinued anti-hepatitis B therapy, including tenofovir disoproxil fumarate.
Hepatic function should be monitored closely with both clinical and laboratory follow-up for at least several months in patients who discontinue anti-hepatitis B therapy, including tenofovir disoproxil fumarate.
If appropriate, resumption of anti-hepatitis B therapy may be warranted [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
