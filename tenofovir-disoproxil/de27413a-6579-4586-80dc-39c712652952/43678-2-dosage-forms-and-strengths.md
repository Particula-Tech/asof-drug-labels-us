# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Tenofovir disoproxil fumarate is available as tablets.
Each tablet contains 300 mg of tenofovir disoproxil fumarate, which is equivalent to 245 mg of tenofovir disoproxil.
The tablets are light blue capsule shaped biconvex film coated tablet debossed with 'C533' on one side and plain on other side.
