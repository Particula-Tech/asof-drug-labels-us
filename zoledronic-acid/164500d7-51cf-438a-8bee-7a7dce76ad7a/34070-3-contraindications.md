# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

## 4.1 Hypocalcemia

[See Warnings and Precautions (5.2).]

## 4.2 Renal Failure

Zoledronic Acid Injection is contraindicated in patients with creatinine clearance < 35 mL/min and in those with evidence of acute renal impairment due to an increased risk of renal failure [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3)]

## 4.3 Hypersensitivity to Zoledronic Acid or Any Components of Zoledronic Acid Injection

Hypersensitivity reactions including rare cases of urticaria, angioedema, and anaphylactic reaction/shock have been reported [see Post-Marketing Experience (6.2)].
