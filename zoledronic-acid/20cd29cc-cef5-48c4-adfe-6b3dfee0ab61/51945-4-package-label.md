Zoledronic Acid Injection

4 mg/5 mL (0.8 mg/mL)

For Intravenouous Infusion after Dilution

Sterile

Not for direct injection

Dose must be diluted

Do not mix with calcium- containing infusion solutions

Rx only Single-Dose Vial

Each vial contains: Active: Zoledronic acid monohydrate 4.264 mg (equivalent to 4 mg zoledronic acid, USP on an anhydrous basis).
Inactives: Mannitol USP 220 mg, Sodium Citrate USP 24 mg and Water for Injection q.s. to 5 mL.
The pH of the injection solution is 5.7 to 6.7.

Zoledronic acid injection diluted with infusion media, may be stored under refrigeration at 2° to 8°C (36° to 46°F).

Total time between dilution and infusion media and end of administration must not exceed 24 hours.

Discard unused portion.

Usual Dosage: See package insert for Preparation of Solution.

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
