# 16. HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Zoledronic acid injection 4 mg per 5 mL single-use vial of concentrate

Each 5 mL vial contains 4.264 mg zoledronic acid monohydrate, equivalent to 4 mg zoledronic acid on an anhydrous basis, 220 mg of mannitol, USP, water for Injection, and 24 mg of sodium citrate, USP.
