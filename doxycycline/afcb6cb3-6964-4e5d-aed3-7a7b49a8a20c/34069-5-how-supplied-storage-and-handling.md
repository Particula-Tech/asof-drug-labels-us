# HOW SUPPLIED

MONODOX® 50 mg Capsules have a white opaque body with a yellow opaque cap.
The capsule bears the inscription “MONODOX 50” in brown and “M 260” in brown.
Each capsule contains doxycycline monohydrate equivalent to 50 mg doxycycline.

MONODOX® 50 mg is available in:

Bottles of 100 capsules......................................................NDC 55515-260-06

MONODOX® 100 mg Capsules have a yellow opaque body with a brown opaque cap.
The capsule bears the inscription “MONODOX 100” in white and “M 259” in brown.
Each capsule contains doxycycline monohydrate equivalent to 100 mg of doxycycline.

MONODOX® 100 mg is available in:

Bottles of 50 capsules........................................................NDC 55515-259-04

Bottles of 250 capsules......................................................NDC 55515-259-07

STORE AT CONTROLLED ROOM TEMPERATURE 15°C TO 30°C (59°F TO 86°F).

PROTECT FROM LIGHT.
