# HOW SUPPLIED

Doxycycline Hyclate Capsules USP (equivalent to 50 mg doxycycline) are opaque blue and opaque white capsules imprinted “DAN 5535” on the body and cap, supplied in bottles of 50 (NDC 0591-5535-50).

Doxycycline Hyclate Capsules USP (equivalent to 100 mg doxycycline) are opaque blue capsules imprinted “DAN 5440” on the body and cap, supplied in bottles of 50 (NDC 0591-5440-50) and 500 (NDC 0591-5440-05).

Doxycycline Hyclate Tablets USP (equivalent to 100 mg doxycycline) are round, film-coated, light orange, unscored tablets imprinted “DAN” and “5553” supplied in bottles of 50 (NDC 0591-5553-50) and 500 (NDC 0591-5553-05).

Store below 30°C (86°F).

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container with child-resistant closure.
