# HOW SUPPLIED

Doxycycline hyclate capsules USP (equivalent to 50 mg doxycycline) are opaque blue and opaque white capsules imprinted "DAN 5535" supplied in bottles of 50.

Doxycycline hyclate capsules USP (equivalent to 100 mg doxycycline) are opaque blue capsules imprinted "DAN 5440" supplied in bottles of 50 and 500.

Doxycycline hyclate tablets USP (equivalent to 100 mg doxycycline) are round, film-coated, light orange tablets imprinted "DAN" and "5553" supplied in bottles of 50 and 500.

Store below 30°C (86°F).

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container with child-resistant closure.
