# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

LYMEPAK tablets contain 100 mg of doxycycline (equivalent to 115 mg doxycycline hyclate).
The tablets are green, round, film-coated tablets engraved with LP-1 on one side.

NDC # 62135-596-86: is supplied as a child-resistant blister card containing 14 tablets NDC # 62135-596-87: Unit of use carton containing 3 blister cards, total 42 tablets

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light and moisture.
