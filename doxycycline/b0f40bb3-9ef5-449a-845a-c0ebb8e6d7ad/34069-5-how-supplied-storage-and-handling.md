# HOW SUPPLIED

Periostat® (white tablet imprinted with a PS20) containing doxycycline hyclate equivalent to 20 mg doxycycline.
Bottle of 60 (NDC 64682-008-01), Bottle of 100 (NDC 64682-008-02) and Bottle of 1000 (NDC 64682-008-03).

## Storage

All products are to be stored at controlled room temperatures of 15°C - 30°C (59°F - 86°F) and dispensed in tight, light-resistant containers (USP).
