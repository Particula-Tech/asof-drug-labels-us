# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

ACTICLATE® (doxycycline hyclate USP) Tablets, 75 mg are round, convex, light-teal, film-coated, tablets with "75" debossed on one side of the tablet and "AQ101" debossed on the other.

ACTICLATE® (doxycycline hyclate USP) Tablets, 150 mg are oval-shaped, convex, mossy-green, film-coated tablets.
Each side of the functionally scored tablet has two parallel score lines for splitting into 3 equal portions with "A" debossed on each portion of one side of the tablet, and no debossing on the other.
