# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

DORYX® (Doxycycline Hyclate Delayed-Release Tablets, USP), 75 mg are white, oval scored tablets containing yellow pellets and debossed with “D|7” on one face and plain on the other.
Each tablet contains specially coated pellets of doxycycline hyclate equivalent to 75 mg of doxycycline.
