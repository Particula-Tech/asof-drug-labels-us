# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

EYSUVIS (loteprednol etabonate ophthalmic suspension) 0.25% is a sterile ophthalmic suspension.
It is supplied in a white, low-density polyethylene dropper bottle with a linear low-density polyethylene tip, a pink high-density polyethylene cap, and a white low-density polyethylene tamper-evident overcap in the following size:

8.3 mL in a 10 mL bottle (NDC 71571-333-83)

Storage and Handling

Do not use if tamper-evident overcap seal is not intact.

The white tamper-evident overcap can be thrown away.
Retain the pink cap and keep the bottle tightly closed when not in use.

Store upright at 15°C to 25°C (59°F to 77°F).
Do not freeze.
After opening, EYSUVIS can be used until the expiration date on the bottle.
