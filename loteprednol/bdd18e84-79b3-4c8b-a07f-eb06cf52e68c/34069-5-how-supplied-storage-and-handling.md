# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

INVELTYS (loteprednol etabonate ophthalmic suspension) 1% is a sterile ophthalmic suspension.
It is supplied in a white low-density polyethylene bottle, with a natural low-density polyethylene tip, a pink polypropylene cap and a tamper-evident seal imprinted around the neck in the following size:

2.8 mL in a 5 mL bottle (NDC 0065-9307-04)

Storage and Handling

Do not use if tamper-evident seal is damaged

Store upright at 15°C to 25°C (59°F to 77°F).
Do not freeze.
