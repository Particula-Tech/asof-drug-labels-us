# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

Administration

Instruct the patient to shake the bottle for one to two seconds before using.

Risk of Contamination

Advise patients not to allow the dropper tip to touch any surface, as this may contaminate the suspension.

Risk of Secondary Infection

If pain develops, or redness, itching, or inflammation becomes aggravated, the patient should be advised to consult a physician.

Contact Lens Wear

Advise patients that the preservative in INVELTYS may be absorbed by soft contact lenses.
Contact lenses should be removed prior to instillation of INVELTYS and may be reinserted after 15 minutes following administration.
