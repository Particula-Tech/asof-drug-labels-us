# HOW SUPPLIED

Irbesartan tablets USP are available as white to off-white film coated, oval tablets, debossed on one side with “ML 94”, “ML 95” and “ML 96” respectively for 75mg, 150mg and 300mg tablets and plain on the other side.
Unit-of-use bottles contain 30 or 500 tablets and blister packs contain 100 tablets or 90 tablets, as follows:

Storage Store at 20° C - 25° C (68° F - 77° F); excursions permitted to 15° C - 30° C (59° F - 86° F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
