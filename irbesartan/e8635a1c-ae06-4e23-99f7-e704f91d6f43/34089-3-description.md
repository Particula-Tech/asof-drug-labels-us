# DESCRIPTION

Irbesartan USP is an angiotensin II receptor (AT 1 subtype) antagonist.
Irbesartan USP is a non-peptide compound, chemically described as a 2-butyl-3-[p-(o-1H-tetrazol-5-ylphenyl)benzyl]-1,3-diazaspiro[4.4]non-1-en-4-one.
Its empirical formula is C 25 H 28 N 6 O, and the structural formula: Irbesartan USP is a white to off-white crystalline powder with a molecular weight of 428.5.
It is a nonpolar compound with a partition coefficient (octanol/water) of 10.1 at pH of 7.4.
Irbesartan USP is slightly soluble in alcohol and methylene chloride and practically insoluble in water.
Irbesartan tablets USP are available for oral administration in unscored, film-coated tablets containing 75 mg, 150 mg, or 300 mg of irbesartan USP.
Inactive ingredients include: carboxymethylcellulose calcium, povidone, colloidal silicon dioxide, sodium starch glycolate, talc and magnesium stearate.
The film coating comprises of hypromellose, lactose monohydrate, titanium dioxide and polyethylene glycol.
