# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Irbesartan Tablets, USP 75 mg are available as white to off-white, round, flat beveled edge tablets, debossed with “CE”on one side and “283”on the other side.

Bottles of 30 tablets – NDC 62135-976-30

Bottles of 90 tablets – NDC 62135-976-90

Irbesartan Tablets, USP 150 mg are available as white to off-white, round, flat beveled edge tablets, debossed with “CE”on one side and “284”on the other side.

Bottles of 30 tablets – NDC 62135-977-30

Bottle of 90 tablets – NDC 62135-977-90

Irbesartan Tablets, USP 300 mg are available as white to off-white, oblong, standard concave tablets, debossed with “CE”on one side and “285”on the other side.

Bottles of 30 tablets – NDC 62135-978-30

Bottle of 90 tablets – NDC 62135-978-90

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
