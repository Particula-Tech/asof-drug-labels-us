# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Irbesartan tablets USP 75 mg are available as white to off-white, biconvex, oval tablets, debossed with ‘447’ on one side and ‘C’ on other.
Irbesartan tablets USP 150 mg are available as white to off-white, biconvex, oval tablets, debossed with ‘448’ on one side and ‘C’ on other.
Irbesartan tablets USP 300 mg are available as white to off-white, biconvex, oval tablets, debossed with ‘449’ on one side and ‘C’ on other.
