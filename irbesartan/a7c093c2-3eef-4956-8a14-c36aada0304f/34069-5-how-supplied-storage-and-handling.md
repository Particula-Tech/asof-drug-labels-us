# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Irbesartan Tablets USP are available as white to off-white, oval shaped, uncoated tablets, debossed with a LU on one side and a code on the other (see Table below).
Unit-of-use bottles contain 30 or 90 or 500 tablets as follows:

|  | 75 mg | 150 mg | 300 mg |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Debossing | M11 | M12 | M13 |
| Bottle of 30 | 68180-410-06 | 68180-411-06 | 68180-412-06 |
| Bottle of 90 | 68180-410-09 | 68180-411-09 | 68180-412-09 |
| Bottle of 500 | - | 68180-411-02 | 68180-412-02 |

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
