# INDICATIONS AND USAGE

## Hypertension

Irbesartan Tablets USP are indicated for the treatment of hypertension.
It may be used alone or in combination with other antihypertensive agents.

## Nephropathy in Type 2 Diabetic Patients

Irbesartan Tablets USP are indicated for the treatment of diabetic nephropathy with an elevated serum creatinine and proteinuria (>300 mg/day) in patients with type 2 diabetes and hypertension.
In this population, irbesartan reduces the rate of progression of nephropathy as measured by the occurrence of doubling of serum creatinine or end-stage renal disease (need for dialysis or renal transplantation) (see CLINICAL PHARMACOLOGY: Clinical Studies).
