# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

25 mg Tablets: White, round, biconvex film-coated tablets debossed with “RDY” on one side and “291” on the other side.
Each tablet contains 35 mg of sumatriptan succinate, USP equivalent to 25 mg of sumatriptan.

50 mg Tablets: White, round, biconvex film-coated tablets debossed with “RDY” on one side and “292” on the other side.
Each tablet contains 70 mg of sumatriptan succinate, USP equivalent to 50 mg of sumatriptan.

100 mg Tablets: White, capsule shaped, biconvex film-coated tablets debossed with “RDY” on one side and “293” on the other side.
Each tablet contains 140 mg of sumatriptan succinate, USP equivalent to 100 mg of sumatriptan.
