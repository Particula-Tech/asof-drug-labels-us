# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

The recommended dose of TOSYMRA is 10 mg given as a single spray in one nostril.

The maximum cumulative dose that may be given in a 24-hour period is 30 mg, with doses of TOSYMRA separated by at least 1 hour.
TOSYMRA may also be given at least 1 hour following a dose of another sumatriptan product.
