INSTRUCTIONS FOR USE OF SUMATRIPTAN AUTOINJECTOR

Important things that you need to know

This device is called an Sumatriptan autoinjector.
Here we use the shorter name 'autoinjector'.

1.
Read all of the instructions carefully before using this autoinjector.

2.
Follow these step-by-step instructions every time you use the autoinjector, ask your doctor or pharmacist.

3.
Only use each autoinjector once - do not try to use more than once.

ABOUT THE AUTOINJECTOR

The parts of the Autoinjector are shown in this picture

1 GETTING READY

1.1 Wash your hands.

1.2 Choose an area with an adequate fatty tissue layer usually the outside of the thigh.

1.3 Clean the skin area to be injected with alcohol or a new sterile swab (see Figure A).

1.4 Take the Sumatriptan Autoinjector out of the package.

1.5 Look in the medicine window on the autoinjector.

• Before injection, to check that the liquid is clear (see Figure B).

• If it is difficult to see what is in the window, hold the autoinjector upto the light and check.

• After injection, the blue plunger rod completely fills the medicine window (see Figure C).

• If the blue plunger rod can be seen through the medicine window, the device is spent and cannot be used again.

1.6 Pull the gray cap off the autoinjector.

• Do not twist the cap.

• Pull it straight off (see Figure D)

1.7 Look inside the cap, check that the gray needle cover is inside (see Figure E).Do not use the autoinjector if the gray needle cover is not inside the cap.

1.8 Do not try to put the cap back.

If you try to put it back, this will damage the needle.
You are now ready to inject the medicine, go to step 2.1.

2 INJECTING THE MEDICINE

2.
Without pressing the blue button, push the autoinjector firmly against your skin until you feel the stop point (see Figure F).

• Pushing to the stop point unlocks the button.

• As long as the autoinjector is firmly pressed against the skin, the safety lock is deactivated; the autoinjector could fire unintentionally if the blue button is pressed by accident.

• Do not attempt to re-engage the safety lock at any time

• Keep the autoinjector pressed against your skin for the next steps.

2.2 Keep pushing the autoinjector against your skin then firmly press down the blue button on the top of the autoinjector until it will not go further (see Figure G).

• You will hear a click, this indicates that the injection has started (see Figure G).

• If the injection did not start, release the blue button, make sure the autoinjector is pushed down against the skin and push down harder on the blue button.

2.3 Do not take the autoinjector off your skin.

• Monitor the injection through the medicine window to make sure that the entire dose is injected.
The blue plunger will move down the window, completely fill it, and stop moving when the injection is done (see Figure H).

• When the injection is done, keep holding the autoinjector against the skin for 5 seconds (see Figure I). If you take the autoinjector off before, not all of the medicine will be injected.

2.4 Carefully take the Autoinjector off your skin (see Figure J).

• The protective sleeve automatically covers the needle.
It is then locked and the needle is protected.

3 WHAT TO DO AFTER THE INJECTION

3.1 If you notice a spot of blood at the injection site, dab away with a cotton ball or tissue paper.
Do not rub the injection site.
If needed, you may cover the injection site with a bandage.

3.2 Visually check that there is no liquid left at the bottom of the medicine window.
If there is liquid, consult your doctor or pharmacist.

4 DISPOSE THE AUTOINJECTOR

4.1 Discard the whole Sumatriptan Autoinjector after use and discard the cap.

• Put your used injection Autoinjector in a FDA-cleared sharps disposal container right away after use (see Figure K).

If you do not have a FDA-cleared sharps disposal container, you may use a household container that is:

o made of a heavy-duty plastic,

o can be closed with a tight-fitting, puncture-resistant lid, without sharps being able to come out, upright and stable during use,

o leak-resistant, and

o properly labeled to warn of hazardous waste inside the container.

• When your sharps disposal container is almost full, you will need to follow your community guidelines for the right way to dispose of your sharps disposal container.
There may be state or local laws about how you should throw away used needles and cartridges.
For more information about safe sharps disposal, and for specific information about sharps disposal in the state that you live in, go to the FDA's website at: http://www.fda.gov/safesharpsdisposal.

• Do not dispose of your used sharps disposal container in your household trash unless your community guidelines permit this.
Do not recycle your used sharps disposal container.

Do not try to reuse the autoinjector.
To avoid any injury, never try to touch the needle.
