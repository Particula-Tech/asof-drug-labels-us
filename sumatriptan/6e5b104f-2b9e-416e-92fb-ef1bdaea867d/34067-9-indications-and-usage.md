# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

ZEMBRACE SymTouch is indicated for the acute treatment of migraine with or without aura in adults.

Limitations of Use:

- Use only if a clear diagnosis of migraine has been established.
  If a patient has no response to the first migraine attack treated with ZEMBRACE SymTouch, reconsider the diagnosis before ZEMBRACE SymTouch is administered to treat any subsequent attacks.
- ZEMBRACE SymTouch injection is not indicated for the prevention of migraine attacks.
