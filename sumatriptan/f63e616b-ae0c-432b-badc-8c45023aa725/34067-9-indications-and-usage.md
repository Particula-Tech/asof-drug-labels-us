# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Sumatriptan Nasal Spray is indicated for the acute treatment of migraine with or without aura in adults.

Limitations of Use:

- •Use only if a clear diagnosis of migraine headache has been established.
  If a patient has no response to the first migraine attack treated with Sumatriptan Nasal Spray, reconsider the diagnosis of migraine before Sumatriptan Nasal Spray is administered to treat any subsequent attacks.
- •Sumatriptan is not indicated for the prevention of migraine attacks.
- •Safety and effectiveness of Sumatriptan Nasal Spray have not been established for cluster headache.
