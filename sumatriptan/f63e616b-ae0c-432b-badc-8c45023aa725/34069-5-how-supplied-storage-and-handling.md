# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Sumatriptan Nasal Spray 5 mg (NDC 66993-081-69) and 20 mg (NDC 66993-082-69) are each supplied in boxes of 6 nasal spray devices.
Each unit dose spray supplies 5 mg and 20 mg, respectively, of sumatriptan.

Store between 2°C and 30°C (36°F and 86°F).
Protect from light.
