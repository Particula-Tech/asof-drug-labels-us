# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

IMITREX injection is indicated in adults for (1) the acute treatment of migraine, with or without aura, and (2) the acute treatment of cluster headache.

Limitations of Use:

- •Use only if a clear diagnosis of migraine or cluster headache has been established.
  If a patient has no response to the first migraine or cluster headache attack treated with IMITREX injection, reconsider the diagnosis before IMITREX injection is administered to treat any subsequent attacks.
- •IMITREX injection is not indicated for the prevention of migraine or cluster headache attacks.
