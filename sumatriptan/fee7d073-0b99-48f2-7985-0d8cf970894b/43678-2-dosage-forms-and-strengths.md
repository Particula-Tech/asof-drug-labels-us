# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

- •Injection: 4-mg and 6-mg single-dose prefilled syringe cartridges for use with the IMITREX STATdose Pen.
  Each 0.5 mL injection contains 5.6 mg or 8.4 mg of sumatriptan succinate equivalent to 4 or 6 mg of sumatriptan, respectively.
- •Injection: 6-mg single-dose vial.
  Each 0.5 mL injection contains 8.4 mg of sumatriptan succinate equivalent to 6 mg of sumatriptan.
