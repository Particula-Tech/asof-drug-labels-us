# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Sumatriptan tablets are indicated for the acute treatment of migraine with or without aura in adults.

Limitations of Use:

- •Use only if a clear diagnosis of migraine headache has been established.
  If a patient has no response to the first migraine attack treated with sumatriptan tablets, reconsider the diagnosis of migraine before sumatriptan tablets are administered to treat any subsequent attacks.
- •Sumatriptan tablets are not indicated for the prevention of migraine attacks.
- •Safety and effectiveness of sumatriptan tablets have not been established for cluster headache.
