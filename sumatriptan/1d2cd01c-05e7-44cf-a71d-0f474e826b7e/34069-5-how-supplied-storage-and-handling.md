# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Sumatriptan Tablets, USP are available containing 35 mg, 70 mg or 140 mg of sumatriptan succinate, USP equivalent to 25 mg, 50 mg or 100 mg of sumatriptan, respectively.

The 25 mg tablets are white, film-coated, round, unscored tablets debossed with M on one side of the tablet and S4 on the other side.
They are available as follows:

NDC 0378-5630-59 bottles of 9 tablets

The 50 mg tablets are white, film-coated, round, unscored tablets debossed with M over S7 on one side of the tablet and blank on the other side.
They are available as follows:

NDC 0378-5631-59 bottles of 9 tablets

The 100 mg tablets are white, film-coated, round, unscored tablets debossed with M over S12 on one side of the tablet and blank on the other side.
They are available as follows:

NDC 0378-5632-59 bottles of 9 tablets

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

Protect from light.

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP using a child-resistant closure.

PHARMACIST: Dispense the accompanying Patient Information Leaflet to each patient.
