# HOW SUPPLIED

Sumatriptan Succinate Tablets, 25 mg are white to off-white, round, biconvex uncoated tablets, debossed with ‘C’ on one side and ‘32’ on other side.
Unit-dose package of 9 Tablets NDC 65862-146-36 Sumatriptan Succinate Tablets, 50 mg are white to off-white, capsule shaped, biconvex uncoated tablets, debossed with ‘C’ on one side and ‘33’ on other side.
Unit-dose package of 9 Tablets NDC 65862-147-36 Sumatriptan Succinate Tablets, 100 mg are white to off-white, capsule shaped, biconvex uncoated tablets, debossed with ‘C’ on one side and ‘34’ on other side.
Unit-dose package of 9 Tablets NDC 65862-148-36 Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
