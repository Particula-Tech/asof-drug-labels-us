# ANIMAL TOXICOLOGY

## Corneal Opacities

Dogs receiving oral sumatriptan developed corneal opacities and defects in the corneal epithelium.
Corneal opacities were seen at the lowest dosage tested, 2 mg/kg/day, and were present after 1 month of treatment.
Defects in the corneal epithelium were noted in a 60-week study.
Earlier examinations for these toxicities were not conducted and no-effect doses were not established; however, the relative exposure at the lowest dose tested was approximately 5 times the human exposure after a 100 mg oral dose.
There is evidence of alterations in corneal appearance on the first day of intranasal dosing to dogs.
Changes were noted at the lowest dose tested, which was approximately one half the maximum single human oral dose of 100 mg on a mg/m 2 basis.
