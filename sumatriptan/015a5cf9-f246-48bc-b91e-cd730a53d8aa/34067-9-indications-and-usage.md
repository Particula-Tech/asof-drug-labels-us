# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

TOSYMRA is indicated for the acute treatment of migraine with or without aura in adults.

Limitations of Use:

- Use only if a clear diagnosis of migraine has been established.
  If a patient has no response to the first migraine attack treated with TOSYMRA, reconsider the diagnosis before TOSYMRA is administered to treat any subsequent attacks.
- TOSYMRA is not indicated for the preventive treatment of migraine.
- TOSYMRA is not indicated for the treatment of cluster headache.
