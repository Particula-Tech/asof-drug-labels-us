# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Metoprolol tartrate tablets, USP are supplied as:

25 mg tablet – white round shaped, film coated tablets debossed with ‘C over 73’ on one side and deep break line on other side.

50 mg tablet – pink round shaped, film coated tablets debossed with ‘C over 74’ on one side and deep break line on other side.

100 mg tablet – light blue round shaped, film coated tablets debossed with ‘C over 75’ on one side and deep break line on other side.
