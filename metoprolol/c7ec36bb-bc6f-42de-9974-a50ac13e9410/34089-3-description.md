# 11 DESCRIPTION

Metoprolol Tartrate Injection, USP is a selective beta1-adrenoreceptor blocking agent, available in 5 mL and oversized 10 mL vials for intravenous administration.
Each vial contains a sterile solution of Metoprolol Tartrate USP, 5 mg, and Sodium Chloride, USP, 45 mg, and Water for Injection, USP.
Metoprolol Tartrate, USP is (±)-1-(Isopropylamino)-3-[p-(2methoxyethyl)phenoxy]-2-propanol L-(+)-tartrate (2:1) salt, and its structural formula is:

Metoprolol Tartrate USP is a white, practically odorless, crystalline powder with a molecular weight of 684.82.
It is very soluble in water; freely soluble in methylene chloride, in chloroform, and in alcohol; slightly soluble in acetone; and insoluble in ether.
