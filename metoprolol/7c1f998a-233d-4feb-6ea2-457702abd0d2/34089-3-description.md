# DESCRIPTION

Metoprolol tartrate injection is a sterile solution containing metoprolol tartrate, a selective beta1-adrenoreceptor blocking agent, available in 5 mL vials for intravenous administration.
Each vial contains a sterile solution of metoprolol tartrate USP, 5 mg and sodium chloride USP, 45 mg.

Metoprolol tartrate is a white, practically odorless, crystalline powder with a molecular weight of 684.83.
Its molecular formula is (C15H25NO3)2 • C4H6O6.
It is very soluble in water; freely soluble in methylene chloride, in chloroform, and in alcohol; slightly soluble in acetone; and insoluble in ether.
