# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

- Advise patients and caregivers of the need to maintain dietary restriction of tyrosine and phenylalanine when taking Orfadin to treat hereditary tyrosinemia type 1 [see Warnings and Precautions (5)].
- Advise patients and caregivers to report promptly unexplained eye symptoms, rash, jaundice, or excessive bleeding [see Warnings and Precautions (5) and Adverse Reactions (6)].
