# 7 DRUG INTERACTIONS

## 7.1 Interaction with CYP2C9 Substrates

If NITYR is co-administered with drugs that are metabolized by CYP2C9, additional monitoring may be warranted because of a potential for increased systemic exposure of these drugs [seeClinical Pharmacology (12.3)].
The risk is dependent upon the particular 2C9 substrate and its adverse reaction profile.
