# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

## 2.1 Recommended Dosage

The recommended dosage of HARLIKU is 2 mg administered orally, once daily.

Administer HARLIKU with or without food [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].

Missed Dose If a dose of HARLIKU is missed, do not administer two doses at once to make up for a missed dose.
Take the next dose at the scheduled time.
