# INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Carefully consider the potential benefits and risks of etodolac extended-release tablets, USP and other treatment options before deciding to use etodolac extended-release tablets, USP.
Use the lowest effective dose for the shortest duration consistent with individual patient treatment goals (see WARNINGS).

Etodolac extended-release tablets, USP are indicated:

* For relief of signs and symptoms of juvenile arthritis

* For relief of the signs and symptoms of rheumatoid arthritis

* For relief of the signs and symptoms of osteoarthritis
