# INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Carefully consider the potential benefits and risks of etodolac capsules and other treatment options before deciding to use etodolac capsules.
Use the lowest effective dose for the shortest duration consistent with individual patient treatment goals (see WARNINGS).

Etodolac capsules are indicated:

- •For acute and long-term use in the management of signs and symptoms of the following:

1.
Osteoarthritis

2.
Rheumatoid arthritis

- •For the management of acute pain
