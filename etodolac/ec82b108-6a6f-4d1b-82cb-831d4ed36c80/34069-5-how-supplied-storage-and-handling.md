# HOW SUPPLIED

Etodolac Capsules and Tablets, USP are available as:

Etodolac Capsules, USP

200 mg capsules (pink capsules with "ETO 200 MG" imprinted in black on cap and body)

- -in bottles of 100, NDC 51672-4016-1

300 mg capsules (light pink capsules with "ETO 300 MG" imprinted in black on cap and body)

- -in bottles of 100, NDC 51672-4017-1

Store at 20°-25°C (68°-77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature], protected from moisture.

Etodolac Tablets, USP

400 mg tablets (peach, oval shape, film coated, unscored tablet with "T88" engraved on one side)

- -in bottles of 100, NDC 51672-4018-1

500 mg tablets (blue, oval shape, film coated, unscored tablet with "TARO" engraved on one side and "89." engraved on the other side)

- -in bottles of 100, NDC 51672-4036-1

Store at 20°-25°C (68°-77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Store tablets in original container until ready to use.
Dispense in light-resistant container.
