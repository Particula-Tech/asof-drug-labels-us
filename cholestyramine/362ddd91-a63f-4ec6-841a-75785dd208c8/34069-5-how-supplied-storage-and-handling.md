# HOW SUPPLIED

QUESTRAN ® Powder (Cholestyramine for Oral Suspension USP) is available in cans containing 378 grams and in cartons of sixty 9 gram packets.
Four grams of anhydrous cholestyramine resin are contained in 9 grams of QUESTRAN Powder.
The 378 g can includes a 15 cc scoop.
The scoop is not interchangeable with scoops from other products.
