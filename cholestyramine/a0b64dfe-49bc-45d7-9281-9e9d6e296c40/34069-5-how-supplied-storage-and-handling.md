# HOW SUPPLIED

Cholestyramine for oral suspension, USP orange flavor is available in cartons of sixty 9 gram sachets and in HDPE bottles containing 378 grams.
Nine grams of cholestyramine for oral suspension, USP contain 4 grams of anhydrous cholestyramine resin.

NDC # 27241-134-36 Carton of 60 sachets

NDC # 27241-134-51 HDPE bottle, 378 g (containing a scoop that is not interchangeable with scoops from other products)

Storage: Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted from 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
