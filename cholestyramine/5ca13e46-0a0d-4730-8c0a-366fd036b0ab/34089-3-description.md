# DESCRIPTION

QUESTRAN® (Cholestyramine for Oral Suspension, USP), the chloride salt of a basic anion exchange resin, a cholesterol lowering agent, is intended for oral administration.
Cholestyramine resin is quite hydrophilic, but insoluble in water.
The cholestyramine resin in QUESTRAN is not absorbed from the digestive tract.
Four grams of anhydrous cholestyramine resin is contained in 9 grams of QUESTRAN POWDER.
Four grams of anhydrous cholestyramine resin is contained in 6.4 grams of QUESTRAN LIGHT.
