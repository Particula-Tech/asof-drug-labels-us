# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Risperidone Oral Solution, USP, 1 mg/mL, (NDC 65162-673-84) is supplied in 30 mL bottles with a calibrated (in milligrams and milliliters) pipette.
The minimum calibrated volume is 0.25 mL, while the maximum calibrated volume is 3 mL.

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Risperidone oral solution, USP 1 mg/mL should be stored at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light and freezing.

Keep out of reach of children.
