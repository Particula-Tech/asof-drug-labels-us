# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Risperidone oral solution is contraindicated in patients with a known hypersensitivity to either risperidone or paliperidone, or to any of the excipients in the risperidone oral solution formulation.
Hypersensitivity reactions, including anaphylactic reactions and angioedema, have been reported in patients treated with risperidone and in patients treated with paliperidone.
Paliperidone is a metabolite of risperidone.
