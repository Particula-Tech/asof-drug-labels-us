# DRUG ABUSE AND DEPENDENCE

## Controlled Substance Class

RISPERDAL® CONSTA® (risperidone) is not a controlled substance.

## Physical and Psychological Dependence

RISPERDAL® CONSTA® has not been systematically studied in animals or humans for its potential for abuse, tolerance, or physical dependence.
Because RISPERDAL® CONSTA® is to be administered by health care professionals, the potential for misuse or abuse by patients is low.
