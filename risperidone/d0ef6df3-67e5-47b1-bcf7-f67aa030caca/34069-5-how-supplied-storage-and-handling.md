# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Risperidone Orally Disintegrating Tablets 0.25 mg, are white, round, flat-faced beveled edge tablets debossed “212” on one side and “P on the other side.

Risperidone Orally Disintegrating Tablets 0.5 mg, are white, round, flat-faced beveled edge tablets debossed “311” on one side and “P on the other side.

Risperidone Orally Disintegrating Tablets 2 mg, are white, round, flat-faced beveled edge tablets debossed “401” on one side and “P on the other side.

Risperidone Orally Disintegrating Tablets 3 mg, are white, round, flat-faced beveled edge tablets debossed “402” on one side and “P” on the other side.

Risperidone Orally Disintegrating Tablets 4 mg, are white, round, flat-faced beveled edge tablets debossed “403” on one side and “P” on the other side.

Risperidone Orally Disintegrating Tablets 0.25 mg are packaged in cartons of 30 tablets with 5 blister packs of 6 (3 x 2) tablets.
Risperidone Orally Disintegrating Tablets 0.5 mg are packaged in cartons of 28 tablets with 7 blister packs of 4 (2x2) tablets, and on cartons of 30 tablets with 5 blister packs of 6(3x2) tablets.
Risperidone Orally Disintegrating Tablets 2 mg, 3 mg and 4 mg are packaged in cartons of 28 tablets with 7 blister packs of 4 (2 x 2) tablets.

Storage and Handling

Risperidone Orally Disintegrating Tablets should be stored at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Keep out of reach of children.
