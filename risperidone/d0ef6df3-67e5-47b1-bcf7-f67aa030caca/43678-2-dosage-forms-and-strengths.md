# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Risperidone Orally Disintegrating Tablets are available in the following strengths: 0.25 mg,

0.5 mg, 1 mg, 2 mg, 3 mg, and 4 mg.
All are round shaped, white in color and imprinted with “P” on one side and either “212”, “311”, “315”, “401”, “402”, or “403” on the other side according to their respective strengths.
