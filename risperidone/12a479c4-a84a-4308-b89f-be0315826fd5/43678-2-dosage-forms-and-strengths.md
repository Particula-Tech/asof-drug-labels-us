# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Risperidone Tablets, USP are available in the following strengths and colors: 0.25 mg (light orange), 0.5 mg (orange), 1 mg (white), 2 mg (white), 3 mg (white), and 4 mg (white).
The 0.25 mg and 0.5 mg tablets are round, and the 1 mg, 2 mg, 3 mg, and 4 mg tablets are circular.
All are biconvex film coated tablets, debossed with “CE” on one side and 128, 129, 130, 131, 132 and 133, respectively, on the other side.
