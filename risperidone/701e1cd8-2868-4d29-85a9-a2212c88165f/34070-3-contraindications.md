# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Risperidone Tablets, Oral Solution, or Orally Disintegrating Tablets are contraindicated in patients with a known hypersensitivity to either risperidone or paliperidone, or to any of the excipients in the Risperidone Tablets, Oral Solution, or Orally Disintegrating Tablets formulations.
Hypersensitivity reactions, including anaphylactic reactions and angioedema, have been reported in patients treated with risperidone and in patients treated with paliperidone.
Paliperidone is a metabolite of risperidone.
