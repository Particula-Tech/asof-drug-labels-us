# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Risperidoneorally disintegrating tablets 0.5 mg, 1 mg, 2 mg, 3 mg and 4 mg are available as pink colored, round, flat faced, beveled edged tablets.

0.5 mg tablet is debossed with “ RE" on one side and "8” on the other side, 1 mg tablet is debossed with “RE" on one side and "9” on the other side, 2 mg tablet is debossed with “RE" on one side and "10” on the other side, 3 mg tablet is debossed with “RE" and "43” on one side and plain on the other side and 4 mg tablet is debossed with “RE" and "44” on one side and plain on the other side.
