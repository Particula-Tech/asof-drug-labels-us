# 11 DESCRIPTION

Risperidone orally disintegrating tablets contains risperidone, USP a psychotropic agent belonging to the chemical class of benzisoxazole derivatives.
The chemical designation is 3-[2-[4-(6-fluoro-1,2-benzisoxazol-3-yl)-1-piperidinyl]ethyl]-6,7,8,9-tetrahydro-2-methyl-4H-pyrido[1,2-a]pyrimidin-4-one.
Its molecular formula is C 23H27FN4O2 and its molecular weight is 410.49.
The structural formula is:

Risperidone, USP is a white to off white powder.
It is practically insoluble in water, soluble in methylene chloride, and sparingly soluble in alcohol.

Risperidone orally disintegrating tablets are available as 0.5 mg, 1 mg, 2 mg, 3 mg and 4 mg pink tablets.
Inactive ingredients are aspartame *, colloidal silicon dioxide, croscarmellose sodium, low substituted hydroxypropyl cellulose, magnesium carbonate, magnesium stearate, mannitol, menthol, flavor peppermint, ferric oxide red, saccharin sodium and talc.
