# 11 DESCRIPTION

Sodium oxybate, a CNS depressant, is the active ingredient in sodium oxybate oral solution.
The chemical name for sodium oxybate is sodium 4-hydroxybutyrate.
The molecular formula is C4H7NaO3, and the molecular weight is 126.09 g/mole.
The chemical structure is:

Sodium oxybate is a white to off-white, powder that is very soluble in aqueous solutions.
Each mL of sodium oxybate oral solution contains 0.5 g of sodium oxybate (equivalent to 0.413 g/mL of oxybate) in USP Purified Water, neutralized to pH 7.5 with malic acid.
