# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

- •Xyrem is contraindicated in patients being treated with sedative hypnotic agents [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
- •Patients should not drink alcohol when using Xyrem [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
- •Xyrem is contraindicated in patients with succinic semialdehyde dehydrogenase deficiency [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].
  This is a rare disorder of inborn error of metabolism variably characterized by mental retardation, hypotonia, and ataxia.
