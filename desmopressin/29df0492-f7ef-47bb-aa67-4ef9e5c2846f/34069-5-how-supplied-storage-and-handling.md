# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Desmopressin acetate injection is available as a sterile solution supplied as 4 mcg/ml in cartons of ten 1 ml single-dose, type 1 glass ampules (NDC 69918-899-10) and 40 mcg/10 ml (4 mcg/ml) in 10 ml multiple-dose vials, type 1 glass vial with rubber stopper and a flip off seal, (NDC 69918-901-10), each containing 4 mcg desmopressin acetate per ml.

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store refrigerated 2° to 8°C (36° to 46°F).
