# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Desmopressin acetate injection 4 mcg/mL is available as a sterile solution in cartons of ten 1 mL single-dose ampules (NDC 69918-899-10) and in 10 mL multiple-dose vials (NDC 69918-901-10), each containing 4 mcg desmopressin acetate per mL.

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store refrigerated 2° to 8°C (36° to 46°F).
