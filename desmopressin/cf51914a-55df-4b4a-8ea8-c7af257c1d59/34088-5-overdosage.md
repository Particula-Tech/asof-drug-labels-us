# 10 OVERDOSAGE

Overdosage of desmopressin leads to an increased risk of prolonged fluid retention and hyponatremia.
Signs of overdosage may include nausea, headache, drowsiness, confusion, and rapid weight gain due to fluid retention [See Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].

In case of overdosage, NOCDURNA must be discontinued, serum sodium assessed, and hyponatremia treated appropriately.
