# HOW SUPPLIED

DDAVP Nasal Spray is available in a 5-mL bottle with spray pump delivering 50 sprays of 10 mcg (NDC 0075-2452-01).
Desmopressin acetate is also available as DDAVP Rhinal Tube, a refrigerated product with 2.5 mL per bottle, packaged with two rhinal tube applicators per carton (NDC 0075-2450-01).

Store at Controlled Room Temperature 20 to 25°C (68 to 77°F) [see USP].
STORE BOTTLE IN UPRIGHT POSITION.

Keep out of the reach of children.
