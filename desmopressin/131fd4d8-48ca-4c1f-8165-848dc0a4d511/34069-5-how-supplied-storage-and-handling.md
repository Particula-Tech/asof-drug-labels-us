# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Desmopressin acetate injection 4 mcg/mL is available as a sterile solution in a 10 mL multiple-dose vial (NDC 69918-901-12), containing 4 mcg desmopressin acetate per mL.

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store refrigerated 2° to 8°C (36° to 46°F).
