# 14 CLINICAL STUDIES

The effectiveness of DESMODA was supported by dose response studies of desmopressin acetate tablets in patients with diabetes insipidus.
These studies demonstrated that oral doses of 0.025 mg to 0.4 mg of desmopressin acetate tablets produced clinically significant antidiuretic effects.
In patients treated with desmopressin acetate tablets, doses of 0.1 mg to 0.2 mg produced optimal antidiuretic effects lasting up to eight hours [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.2)].
