# 10 OVERDOSAGE

Signs of overdose may include confusion, drowsiness, continuing headache, problems with passing urine and rapid weight gain due to fluid retention [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
In case of overdosage, reduce the dosage, decrease the frequency of administration, or discontinue the DESMODA.
There is no known specific antidote for desmopressin acetate.
The patient should be observed and treated with appropriate symptomatic therapy.
