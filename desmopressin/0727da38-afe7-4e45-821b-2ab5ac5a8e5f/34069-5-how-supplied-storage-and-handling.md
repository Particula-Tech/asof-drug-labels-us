# HOW SUPPLIED

DDAVP Injection 4 mcg/mL is available as a sterile solution in cartons of ten 1 mL single-dose ampules (NDC 0075-2451-01) and in 10 mL multiple-dose vials (NDC 0075-2451-53), each containing 4.0 mcg DDAVP per mL.

Store refrigerated 2 to 8°C (36 to 46°F).

Keep out of the reach of children.
