# CONTRAINDICATIONS

Desmopressin acetate injection 4 mcg/mL is contraindicated in individuals with known hypersensitivity to desmopressin acetate or to any of the components of desmopressin acetate injection 4 mcg/mL .
Desmopressin acetate injection is contraindicated in patients with moderate to severe renal impairment (defined as a creatinine clearance below 50ml/min).
Desmopressin acetate injection is contraindicated in patients with hyponatremia or a history of hyponatremia.
