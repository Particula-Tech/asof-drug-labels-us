# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Desmopressin acetate injection, USP is available as a sterile solution supplied as 40 mcg/10 mL (4 mcg/mL) in 10 mL multiple-dose vials, type 1 glass vial with rubber stopper and flip off seal, (NDC 62756-529-40), each containing 4 mcg desmopressin acetate, USP per mL.

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store refrigerated 2° to 8°C (36° to 46°F).
Preserve in tight containers, protected from light.
