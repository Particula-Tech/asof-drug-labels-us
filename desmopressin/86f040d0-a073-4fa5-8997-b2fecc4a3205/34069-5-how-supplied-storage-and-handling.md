# HOW SUPPLIED

DDAVP Rhinal Tube is available in a 2.5 mL bottle, packaged with two rhinal tube applicators per carton (NDC 55566-2400-0).
Also available in a 5.0 mL pump bottle with spray pump delivery 50 doses of 10 mcg (NDC 55566-2500-0).

Store refrigerated 2 to 8°C (36 to 46°F).
When travelling, closed bottles will maintain stability for 3 weeks when stored at controlled room temperature, 20 to 25°C (68 to 77°F).

Keep out of the reach of children.
