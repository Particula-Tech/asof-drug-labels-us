# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

STIMATE is available as a 2.5 mL bottle containing an aqueous solution with the spray pump delivering 25 sprays of 150 mcg (0.1 mL) (NDC 55566-3000-0).

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at Controlled Room Temperature 20ºC to 25ºC (68ºF to 77ºF) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Discard STIMATE six months after being opened.
Store bottle in upright position.
