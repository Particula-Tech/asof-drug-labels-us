# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

- STIMATE is contraindicated in patients with:
  - Known hypersensitivity to desmopressin acetate or to any of the components of STIMATE.
    Severe allergic reactions and anaphylaxis have been reported [see Warnings and Precautions (5.4)].
  - Hyponatremia or a history of hyponatremia [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
  - Type IIB von Willebrand's disease [see Warnings and Precautions (5.5)
  - Known or suspected syndrome of inappropriate antidiuretic hormone (SIADH) secretion [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
