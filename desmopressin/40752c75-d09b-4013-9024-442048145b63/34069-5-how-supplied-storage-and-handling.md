# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Desmopressin Acetate Injection, USP is supplied as follows:

| NDC | Desmopressin Acetate Injection, USP (4 mcg per mL) | Package Factor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 25021-461-10 | 40 mcg per 10 mL Multi-Dose Vial | 1 vial per carton |

Desmopressin Acetate Injection, USP is a clear, colorless solution, free from particulate matter filled in a type 1 glass vial with rubber stopper and a flip off seal.

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store refrigerated between 2° and 8°C (36° and 46°F).

Keep out of the reach of children.

Sterile, Nonpyrogenic.
The container closure is not made with natural rubber latex.
