# HOW SUPPLIED

DDAVP Rhinal Tube is available in a 2.5 mL bottle, packaged with two rhinal tube applicators per carton (NDC 0075-2450-01).
Also available in a 5.0 mL pump bottle with spray pump delivering 50 doses of 10 mcg (NDC 0075-2452-01).

Store refrigerated 2 to 8°C (36 to 46°F).
When traveling, closed bottles will maintain stability for 3 weeks when stored at controlled room temperature, 20 to 25°C (68 to 77°F).

Keep out of the reach of children.

MILITARY: DDAVP Rhinal Tube, 1 × 2.5 mL (NSN 6505-01-145-6338).
