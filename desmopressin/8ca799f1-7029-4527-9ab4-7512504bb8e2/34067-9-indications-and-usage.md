# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Desmopressin nasal spray solution is indicated as antidiuretic replacement therapy in the management of central diabetes insipidus in adults and pediatric patients 4 years of age and older.

Limitations of Use: Desmopressin nasal spray solution is not indicated for:

1. • Treatment of nephrogenic diabetes insipidus, • Treatment of primary nocturnal enuresis [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)], • Use in patients with conditions that compromise the intranasal route of administration (e.g., severe nasal congestion and blockage, nasal mucosa atrophy, severe atrophic rhinitis, recent nasal surgery such as transsphenoidal hypophysectomy) [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)], • Use in patients with an impaired level of consciousness, • Use in patients requiring doses less than 10 mcg or doses that are not multiples of 10 mcg [see Dosage Forms and Strengths (3)].
