# HOW SUPPLIED

Folic Acid Tablets, USP 1 mg are 5/16”, scored, round, yellow to orange color tablets imprinted “EP 127” and supplied in bottles of 100 (NDC 69315-127-01) and 1000.
(NDC 69315-127-10) Dispense in well-closed container with child-resistant closure.
Store at controlled room temperature 20°-25°C (68°-77°F).
