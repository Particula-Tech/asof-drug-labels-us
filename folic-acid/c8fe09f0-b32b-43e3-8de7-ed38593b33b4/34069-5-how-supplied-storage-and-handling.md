# HOW SUPPLIED

Folic Acid Tablets, USP 1 mg are scored, round, yellow tablets imprinted “AN” and “361” on one side and supplied in bottles of 10, 100, 500 and 1000.

Dispense in well-closed container with child-resistant closure.

Store at controlled room temperature 20°-25°C (68°-77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
