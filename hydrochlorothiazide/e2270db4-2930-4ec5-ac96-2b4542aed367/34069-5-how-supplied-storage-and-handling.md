# HOW SUPPLIED

Hydrochlorothiazide Tablets USP, 25 mg are available as round, flat face, light orange, beveled edge tablets, bisected and debossed "2083" on one side and “TEVA” on the other side, containing 25 mg hydrochlorothiazide USP, packaged in bottles of 100 (NDC 0172-2083-60) and 1000 (NDC 0172-2083-80) tablets.

Hydrochlorothiazide Tablets USP, 50 mg are available as round, flat face, light orange, beveled edge tablets, bisected and debossed "2089" on one side and “TEVA” on the other side, containing 50 mg hydrochlorothiazide USP, packaged in bottles of 100 (NDC 0172-2089-60) and 1000 (NDC 0172-2089-80) tablets.

Dispense in a well-closed container as defined in the USP, with a child-resistant closure (as required).

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

KEEP THIS AND ALL MEDICATIONS OUT OF THE REACH OF CHILDREN.
