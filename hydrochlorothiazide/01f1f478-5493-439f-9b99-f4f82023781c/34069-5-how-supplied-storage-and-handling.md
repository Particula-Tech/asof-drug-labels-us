# HOW SUPPLIED

Hydrochlorothiazide Tablets USP, 25 mg are light pink colored, round shaped, flat faced beveled edge uncoated tablets, debossed with ‘D’ and ‘27’ on one side separated by scoring and plain on the other side.
Bottles of 100 NDC 65862-133-01 Bottles of 1000 NDC 65862-133-99 Hydrochlorothiazide Tablets USP, 50 mg are light pink colored, round shaped, flat faced beveled edge uncoated tablets, debossed with ‘D’ and ‘28’ on one side separated by scoring and plain on the other side.
Bottles of 100 NDC 65862-134-01 Bottles of 1000 NDC 65862-134-99 PHARMACIST: Dispense in a well-closed container as defined in the USP.
Use child-resistant closure (as required).

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
