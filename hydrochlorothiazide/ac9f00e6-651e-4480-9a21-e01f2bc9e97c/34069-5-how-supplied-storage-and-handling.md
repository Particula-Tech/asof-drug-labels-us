# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

AVALIDE® (irbesartan-hydrochlorothiazide) 150/12.5 mg and 300/12.5 mg tablets are peach, biconvex, and oval with a heart debossed on one side and "2775" or "2776" on the reverse side.
The 300/25 mg film-coated tablet is pink, biconvex, and oval with a heart debossed on one side and "2788" on the reverse side.
AVALIDE® Tablets are supplied as follows:

| Irbesartan (mg) | HCTZ (mg) | NDC 0087-xxxx-xx for unit of use |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bottle of 30 | Bottle of 90 |  |  |
| 150 | 12.5 | 2775-31 | 2775-32 |
| 300 | 12.5 | 2776-31 | 2776-32 |
| 300 | 25 | 2788-31 | 2788-32 |

## 16.2 Storage

Store at 25° C (77° F); excursions permitted to 15° C - 30° C (59° F - 86° F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
