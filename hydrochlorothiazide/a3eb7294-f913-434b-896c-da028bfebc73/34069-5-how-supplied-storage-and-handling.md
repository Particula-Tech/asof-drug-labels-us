# HOW SUPPLIED

Hydrochlorothiazide Capsules are #4 Teal Opaque/White Opaque two piece hard gelatin capsules imprinted with WATSON 347 and 12.5 mg in black ink.
They are supplied in bottles of 100 and 500.
Bottles of 100 are supplied with child resistant closures.

Store at 20°- 25°C (68°-77°F).
[See USP controlled room temperature.]
Protect from light, moisture, freezing, -20°C (-4°F).
Keep container tightly closed.
