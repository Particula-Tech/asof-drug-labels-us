# HOW SUPPLIED

Hydrochlorothiazide Capsules are #4 Teal Opaque/White Opaque two piece hard gelatin capsules imprinted with WATSON 347 and 12.5 mg in black ink.
They are supplied in bottles of 100 and 500 with child resistant closures.

Storage: Keep container tightly closed.
Protect from light, moisture, freezing, -20°C (-4°F) and store at room temperature 15°-30°C (59°-86°F)
