# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

## 4.1 Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors (MAOIs)

The use of MAOIs intended to treat psychiatric disorders with escitalopram oxalate, USP or within 14 days of stopping treatment with escitalopram oxalate, USP is contraindicated because of an increased risk of serotonin syndrome.
The use of escitalopram oxalate, USP within 14 days of stopping an MAOI intended to treat psychiatric disorders is also contraindicated [see Dosage and Administration (2.5) and Warnings and Precautions (5.2)].

Starting escitalopram oxalate, USP in a patient who is being treated with MAOIs such as linezolid or intravenous methylene blue is also contraindicated because of an increased risk of serotonin syndrome [see Dosage and Administration (2.6) and Warnings and Precautions (5.2)].

## 4.2 Pimozide

Concomitant use in patients taking pimozide is contraindicated [see Drug Interactions (7.10)].

## 4.3 Hypersensitivity to Escitalopram or Citalopram

Escitalopram oxalate, USP is contraindicated in patients with a hypersensitivity to escitalopram or citalopram or any of the inactive ingredients in escitalopram oxalate oral solution.
