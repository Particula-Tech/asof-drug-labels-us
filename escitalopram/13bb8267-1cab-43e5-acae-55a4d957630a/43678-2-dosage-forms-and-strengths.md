# 3DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Tablets

Lexapro tablets are film-coated, round tablets containing escitalopram in strengths equivalent to 5 mg, 10 mg and 20 mg escitalopram base.
The 10 and 20 mg tablets are scored.
Imprinted with "FL" on one side and either "5", “10”, or “20” on the other side according to their respective strengths.

Oral Solution

Lexapro oral solution contains escitalopram equivalent to 1 mg/mL escitalopram base (not currently being marketed).
