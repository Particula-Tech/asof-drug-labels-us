# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

How Supplied

Tablets

Each film coated round tablet contains escitalopram oxalate equivalent to the labeled amount of escitalopram as follows

5 mg Tablets: White to off-white, round, film coated tablets, debossed with "LU" on one side and "W21" on the other side

Bottles of 100 NDC 68180-137-01

10 mg Tablets: White to off-white, round, film coated tablets, debossed with 'L' and 'U' either side of the scoreline on one side and 'W22' on the other side.

Bottles of 100 NDC 68180-135-01

20 mg Tablets: White to off-white, round, film coated tablets, debossed with 'L' and 'U' either side of the scoreline on one side and 'W23' on the other side.

Bottles of 100 NDC 68180-136-01

Storage and Handling

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
