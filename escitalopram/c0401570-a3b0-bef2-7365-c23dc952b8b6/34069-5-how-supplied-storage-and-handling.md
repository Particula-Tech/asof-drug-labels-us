# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

How Supplied

15 mg capsules:

Hard shell gelatin capsules, with “ALM” printed axially on the light blue opaque cap and “795” printed axially on the blue opaque body.
All printing is in black ink.

NDC 52427-795-30, Bottle of 30 capsules with a child-resistant closure

Storage and Handling

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
