# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Escitalopram Capsules are contraindicated in patients:

- Taking, or within 14 days of stopping, MAOIs, including linezolid or intravenous methylene blue, because of an increased risk of serotonin syndrome [see Dosage and Administration (2.6), Warnings and Precautions (5.2), Drug Interactions (7)].
- Taking pimozide because of the risk of QT prolongation [see Drug Interactions (7)].
- With known hypersensitivity to escitalopram or citalopram or any of the inactive ingredients in Escitalopram Capsules.
