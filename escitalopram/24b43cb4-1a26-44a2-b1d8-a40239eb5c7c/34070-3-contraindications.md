# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Escitalopram is contraindicated in patients: • taking MAOIs with escitalopram or within 14 days of stopping treatment with escitalopram because of an increased risk of serotonin syndrome.
The use of escitalopram within 14 days of stopping an MAOI intended to treat psychiatric disorders is also contraindicated [see Dosage and Administration (2.7) and Warnings and Precautions (5.2)].
Starting escitalopram in a patient who is being treated with MAOIs such as linezolid or intravenous methylene blue is also contraindicated because of an increased risk of serotonin syndrome [see Dosage and Administration (2.6) and Warnings and Precautions (5.2)]. • taking pimozide [see Drug Interactions (7)]. • with a hypersensitivity to escitalopram or citalopram or any of the inactive ingredients in escitalopram.
