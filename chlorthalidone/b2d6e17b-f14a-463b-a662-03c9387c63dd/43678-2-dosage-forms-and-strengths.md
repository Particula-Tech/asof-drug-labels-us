# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Edarbyclor is supplied in the following dosage strengths:

- 40/12.5 mg: pale red, round, biconvex, film-coated tablets, approximately 9.7 mm in diameter, with "A/C" and "40/12.5" imprinted on one side.
  Each tablet contains 40 mg of azilsartan medoxomil and 12.5 mg of chlorthalidone.
- 40/25 mg: light red, round, biconvex, film-coated tablets, approximately 9.7 mm in diameter, with "A/C" and "40/25" imprinted on one side.
  Each tablet contains 40 mg of azilsartan medoxomil and 25 mg of chlorthalidone.
