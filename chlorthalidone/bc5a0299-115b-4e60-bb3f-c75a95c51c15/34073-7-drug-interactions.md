# 7 DRUG INTERACTIONS

Effect of chlorthalidone on other drugs Chlorthalidone may add to or potentiate the action of other antihypertensive drugs.
Chlorthalidone and related drugs may increase the responsiveness to tubocurarine.
Lithium renal clearance is reduced by chlorthalidone, increasing the risk of lithium toxicity.
Monitor serum lithium levels during concomitant use.
