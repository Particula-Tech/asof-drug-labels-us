# DESCRIPTION

Chlorthalidone is an oral antihypertensive/diuretic.
It is a monosulfamyl diuretic that differs chemically from thiazide diuretics in that a double-ring system is incorporated in its structure.
It is 2-chloro-5(1-hydroxy-3-oxo-1- isoindolinyl) benzenesulfonamide with the following structural formula:

Molecular Formula: C14H11ClN2O4S

Molecular weight: 338.76

Chlorthalidone, USP is practically insoluble in water, in ether, and in chloroform; soluble in methanol; slightly soluble in ethanol.

Chlorthalidone tablets are available containing either 25 mg or 50 mg of chlorthalidone USP and the following inactive ingredients: colloidal silicon dioxide, microcrystalline cellulose, D&C Yellow #10, sodium starch glycolate, pregelatinized starch, stearic acid and other inactive ingredients.
The 50 mg tablet also contains FD&C Blue #1.
