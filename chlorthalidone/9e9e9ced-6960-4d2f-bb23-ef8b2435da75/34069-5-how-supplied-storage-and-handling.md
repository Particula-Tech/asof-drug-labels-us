# HOW SUPPLIED

Chlorthalidone Tablets, USP are available containing 25 mg or 50 mg of Chlorthalidone, USP.

The 25 mg tablets are white to off-white, round, tablet debossed with “AC 145” on one side and plain on other side.
They are available as follows:

NDC 43598-719-01 bottles of 100 tablets

NDC 43598-719-10 bottles of 1000 tablets

The 50 mg tablets are white to off-white, round, scored tablet debossed “AC” and “146” on one side separated by horizontal and plain on other side.
They are available as follows:

NDC 43598-720-01 bottles of 100 tablets

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[See USP for Controlled Room Temperature.]

Protect from light.

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP using a child-resistant closure.
