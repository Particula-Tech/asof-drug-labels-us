# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

HEMICLOR 12.5 mg tablets are supplied as white to off-white, modified rectangle shaped tablets debossed with “I” on one side and plain on the other side.
HEMICLOR tablets are supplied as bottles of 30 tablets, 90 tablets, 100 tablets and 1,000 tablets.

NDC Number: 50742-285-30, Bottles of 30.

NDC Number: 50742-285-90, Bottles of 90.

NDC Number: 50742-285-01, Bottles of 100.

NDC Number: 50742-285-10, Bottles of 1,000.

Storage

Store at 20ºC to 25ºC (68ºF to 77ºF) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP using a child-resistant closure.
