# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Arformoterol tartrate inhalation solution is contraindicated in patients with a history of hypersensitivity to arformoterol, racemic formoterol or to any other components of this product.

Use of a LABA, including arformoterol tartrate inhalation solution, without an inhaled cortisteroid is contraindicated in patients with asthma [see Warnings and Precautions (5)].
Arformoterol tartrate inhalation solution is not indicated for the treatment of asthma.
