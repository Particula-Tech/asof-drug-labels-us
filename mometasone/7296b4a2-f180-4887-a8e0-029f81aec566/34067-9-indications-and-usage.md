# INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Mometasone furoate topical solution USP, 0.1%, is a medium potency corticosteroid indicated for the relief of the inflammatory and pruritic manifestations of corticosteroid-responsive dermatoses.
Since safety and efficacy of mometasone furoate topical solution USP, 0.1% have not been established in pediatric patients below 12 years of age, its use in this age group is not recommended.
(see PRECAUTIONS - Pediatric Use).
