# 14 CLINICAL STUDIES

The safety and efficacy of the Mometasone Furoate Cream 0.1% for the treatment of corticosteroid-responsive dermatoses were evaluated in two randomized, double-blind, vehicle-controlled clinical trials, one in psoriasis and one in atopic dermatitis.
A total 366 subjects (12-81 years of age), of whom 177 received Mometasone Furoate Cream 0.1% and 181 subjects received vehicle cream, were evaluated in these trials.
Mometasone Furoate Cream 0.1% or the vehicle cream were applied once daily for 21 days.
The two trials showed Mometasone Furoate Cream 0.1% is effective in the treatment of psoriasis and atopic dermatitis.
