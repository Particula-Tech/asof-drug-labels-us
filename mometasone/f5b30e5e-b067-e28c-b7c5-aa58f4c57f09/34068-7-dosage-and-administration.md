# 2 DOSAGE & ADMINISTRATION

Apply a thin film of Mometasone Furoate, USP Ointment 0.1% to the affected skin areas once daily.
Therapy should be discontinued when control is achieved.
If no improvement is seen within 2 weeks, reassessment of diagnosis may be necessary.
Safety and efficacy of Mometasone Furoate, USP Ointment 0.1% in pediatric patients for more than 3 weeks of use have not been established [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1) and Use in Specific Populations (8.4)].
Mometasone Furoate, USP Ointment 0.1% should not be used with occlusive dressings unless directed by a physician.
Mometasone Furoate, USP Ointment 0.1% should not be applied in the diaper area if the child still requires diapers or plastic pants, as these garments may constitute occlusive dressing.
Mometasone Furoate, USP Ointment 0.1% is for topical use only.
It is not for oral, ophthalmic, or intravaginal use.
Avoid use on the face, groin, or axillae.
