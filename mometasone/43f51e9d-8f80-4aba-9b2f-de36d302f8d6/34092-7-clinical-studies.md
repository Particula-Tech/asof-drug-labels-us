# 14 CLINICAL STUDIES

The safety and efficacy of the ELOCON Cream for the treatment of corticosteroid-responsive dermatoses were evaluated in two randomized, double-blind, vehicle-controlled clinical trials, one in psoriasis and one in atopic dermatitis.
A total 366 subjects (12-81 years of age), of whom 177 received ELOCON Cream and 181 subjects received vehicle cream, were evaluated in these trials.
ELOCON Cream or the vehicle cream were applied once daily for 21 days.

The two trials showed ELOCON Cream is effective in the treatment of psoriasis and atopic dermatitis.
