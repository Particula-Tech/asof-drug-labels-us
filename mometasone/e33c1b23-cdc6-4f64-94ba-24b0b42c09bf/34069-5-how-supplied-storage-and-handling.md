# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

A carton of SINUVA contains one SINUVA (1350 mcg mometasone furoate) implant inside of a Crimper and one disposable Delivery System packaged in a foil pouch.
The SINUVA Sinus Implant is 20 mm in length and 34 mm in expanded diameter (Figure 1).

Store the SINUVA Sinus Implant at 20° C– 25°C (68°F–77°F); excursions permitted at 15°C –30°C (59°F– 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

The NDC code for the SINUVA Sinus Implant is 10599-003-01.
