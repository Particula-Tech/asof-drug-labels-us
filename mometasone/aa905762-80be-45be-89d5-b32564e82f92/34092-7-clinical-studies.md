# 14 CLINICAL STUDIES

The safety and efficacy of ELOCON Ointment, 0.1% for the treatment of corticosteroid-responsive dermatoses was demonstrated in two vehicle-controlled trials, one in psoriasis and one in atopic dermatitis.
A total of 218 subjects received ELOCON Ointment (109 subjects) or the vehicle ointment applied once daily for 21 days.
