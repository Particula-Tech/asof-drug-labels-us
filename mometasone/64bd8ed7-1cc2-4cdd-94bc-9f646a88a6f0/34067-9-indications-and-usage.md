# INDICATIONS AND USAGE

ELOCON® Lotion, 0.1% is a medium potency corticosteroid indicated for the relief of the inflammatory and pruritic manifestations of corticosteroid-responsive dermatoses.
Since safety and efficacy of ELOCON Lotion, 0.1% have not been established in pediatric patients below 12 years of age, its use in this age group is not recommended (see PRECAUTIONS, Pediatric Use section).
