# 14 CLINICAL STUDIES

The safety and efficacy of ELOCON Lotion, 0.1% for the treatment of corticosteroid-responsive dermatoses was demonstrated in two vehicle-controlled trials, one in scalp psoriasis and one in seborrheic dermatitis.
A total of 405 subjects (age range: 12-95 years) received ELOCON Lotion (205 subjects) or the vehicle lotion applied once daily for 21 days.
