# 14 CLINICAL STUDIES

The safety and efficacy of mometasone furoate ointment, 0.1% for the treatment of corticosteroid-responsive dermatoses was demonstrated in two vehicle-controlled trials, one in subjects with psoriasis and one in subjects with atopic dermatitis.
These trials included a total of 218 subjects with 109 subjects applying mometasone furoate ointment and 109 subjects applying vehicle ointment once daily for 21 days.
