# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

SAPHNELO (anifrolumab-fnia) injection is a sterile, preservative-free, clear to opalescent, colorless to slightly yellow solution for intravenous infusion.
It is packaged in a 2 mL clear glass vial containing 300 mg/2 mL (150 mg/mL) of anifrolumab-fnia.

SAPHNELO is available in a carton containing one single-dose vial (NDC-0310-3040-00).

Store in a refrigerator at 36°F to 46°F (2°C to 8°C) in the original carton to protect from light.

Do not freeze.
Do not shake.
