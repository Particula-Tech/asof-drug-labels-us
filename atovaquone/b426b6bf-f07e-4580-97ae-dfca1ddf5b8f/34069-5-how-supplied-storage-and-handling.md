# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

MEPRON oral suspension (bright yellow, citrus-flavored) containing 750 mg atovaquone per 5 mL.

- •Bottle of 210 mL with child-resistant cap (NDC 0173-0665-18).
  Store at 15°C to 25°C (59°F to 77°F).
  Do not freeze.
  Dispense in tight container as defined in USP.
- •5-mL child‑resistant foil pouch ‑ unit dose pack of 42 (NDC 0173-0547-00).
  Store at 15°C to 25°C (59°F to 77°F).
  Do not freeze.
