# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

MEPRON suspension (bright yellow, citrus-flavored) containing 750 mg atovaquone in 5 mL.

- •Bottle of 210 mL with child-resistant cap (NDC 0173-0665-18).
  Store at 15° to 25°C (59° to 77°F).
  Do not freeze.
  Dispense in tight container as defined in USP.
- •5‑mL child‑resistant foil pouch ‑ unit dose pack of 42 (NDC 0173-0547-00).
  Store at 15° to 25°C (59° to 77°F).
  Do not freeze.
