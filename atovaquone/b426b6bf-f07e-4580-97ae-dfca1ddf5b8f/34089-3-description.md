# 11 DESCRIPTION

MEPRON (atovaquone oral suspension) is a quinone antimicrobial drug.
The chemical name of atovaquone is trans-2-[4-(4-chlorophenyl)cyclohexyl]-3-hydroxy-1,4-naphthalenedione.
Atovaquone is a yellow crystalline solid that is practically insoluble in water.
It has a molecular weight of 366.84 and the molecular formula C22H19ClO3.

MEPRON oral suspension is a formulation of micro‑fine particles of atovaquone.

Each 5 mL of MEPRON oral suspension contains 750 mg of atovaquone and the inactive ingredients benzyl alcohol, flavor, poloxamer 188, purified water, saccharin sodium, and xanthan gum.
