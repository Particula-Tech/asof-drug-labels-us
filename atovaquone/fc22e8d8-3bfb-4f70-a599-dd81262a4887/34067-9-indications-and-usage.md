# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

## 1.1 Prevention of Malaria

MALARONE® is indicated for the prophylaxis of Plasmodium falciparum malaria, including in areas where chloroquine resistance has been reported.

## 1.2 Treatment of Malaria

MALARONE is indicated for the treatment of acute, uncomplicated P. falciparum malaria.
MALARONE has been shown to be effective in regions where the drugs chloroquine, halofantrine, mefloquine, and amodiaquine may have unacceptable failure rates, presumably due to drug resistance.
