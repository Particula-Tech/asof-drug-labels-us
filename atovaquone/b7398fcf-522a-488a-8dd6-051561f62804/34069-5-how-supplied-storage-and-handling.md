# HOW SUPPLIED

Atovaquone Suspension (bright yellow, citrus flavored) containing 750 mg atovaquone in each teaspoonful (5 mL).

Bottle of 210 mL with child-resistant cap (NDC 65162-693-88).

Store at 15° to 25°C (59° to 77°F).
DO NOT FREEZE.
Dispense in tight container as defined in USP.
