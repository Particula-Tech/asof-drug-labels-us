# 11 DESCRIPTION

Atovaquone oral suspension is a quinone antimicrobial drug.
The chemical name of atovaquone is 1,4-Naphthalenedione, 2-[4-(4-chlorophenyl)cyclohexyl]-3-hydroxy-, trans.
Atovaquone USP is a yellow colored powder that is freely soluble in tetrahydrofuran, soluble in chloroform and sparingly soluble in acetone.
It has a molecular weight of 366.84 and the molecular formula C 22H 19ClO 3.

Atovaquone oral suspension, USP is a formulation of micro-fine particles of atovaquone USP.
Each 5 mL of atovaquone oral suspension, USP contains 750 mg of atovaquone USP and the inactive ingredients benzyl alcohol, flavor, hypromellose, poloxamer, purified water, saccharin sodium, and xanthan gum.
