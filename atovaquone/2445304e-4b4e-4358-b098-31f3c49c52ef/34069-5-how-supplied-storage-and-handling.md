# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Atovaquone oral suspension, USP is a yellow homogenous suspension containing 750 mg atovaquone USP per 5 mL. • Bottle of 210 mL with child-resistant cap (NDC 68554-3129-0).
Store at 15° to 25°C (59° to 77°F).
Do not freeze.
Dispense in tight container as defined in USP.
