# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

Important Administration Instructions • Advise patients to take the prescribed dose of atovaquone oral suspension as directed. • Advise patients to take their daily doses of atovaquone oral suspension with food, as food will significantly improve the absorption of the drug [see Dosage and Administration (2.3)]. • Advise patients to shake atovaquone oral suspension gently before use each time [see Dosage and Administration (2.3)].
Severe Cutaneous Adverse Reactions (SCARs) Advise patients about the signs and symptoms of serious skin manifestations.
Instruct patients to stop taking atovaquone oral suspension immediately and promptly report the first signs or symptoms of skin rash, mucosal lesions, or any other sign of hypersensitivity [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3)].
Lactation Inform women with HIV-1 that the potential risks of breastfeeding include HIV-1 transmission (in infants without HIV-1) [see Use in Specific Populations (8.2)].
