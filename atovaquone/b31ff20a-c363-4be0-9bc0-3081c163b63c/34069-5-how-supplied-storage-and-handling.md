# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Atovaquone oral suspension, USP (bright yellow, citrus-flavored) containing 750 mg atovaquone per 5 mL.

- Bottle of 210 mL with child-resistant cap (NDC 10702-223-21).
  Store at 15°C to 25°C (59°F to 77°F).
  Do not freeze.Dispense in tight container as defined in USP.
