# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Atovaquone Oral Suspension, USP is a bright yellow, tutti frutti flavored, oral suspension containing 750 mg of atovaquone USP in 5 mL.
It is supplied as follows:

1. NDC 68462-421-21 Bottle of 210 mL with child-resistant cap NDC 68462-421-69 Carton of 42 sachets (5 mL unit-dose)

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Do not freeze.
