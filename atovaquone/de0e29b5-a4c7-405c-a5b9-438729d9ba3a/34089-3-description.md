# 11 DESCRIPTION

Atovaquone Oral Suspension, USP is a quinone antimicrobial drug.
The chemical name of atovaquone is trans-2-[4-(4-chlorophenyl)cyclohexyl]-3-hydroxy-1,4-naphthalenedione.
Atovaquone is a yellow crystalline solid that is practically insoluble in water.
It has a molecular weight of 366.84 and the molecular formula C 22H 19ClO 3.

Atovaquone Oral Suspension, USP is a formulation of micro-fine particles of atovaquone.

Each 5 mL of atovaquone oral suspension contains 750 mg of atovaquone and the inactive ingredients benzyl alcohol, fruity flavor, poloxamer 188, purified water, saccharin sodium, and xanthan gum.
