# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Atovaquone Oral Suspension, USP (bright yellow suspension with fruity flavor) containing 750 mg atovaquone per 5 mL.

- Bottle of 210 mL with child-resistant cap (NDC 62135-528-09)
- Unit Dose Cup 5 mL (NDC 62135-528-45)
- 20 Unit Dose Cups of 5 mL each (NDC 62135-528-24)

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
Do not freeze.
Dispense in tight container as defined in USP.
