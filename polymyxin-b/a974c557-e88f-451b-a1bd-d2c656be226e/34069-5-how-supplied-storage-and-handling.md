# HOW SUPPLIED

Polymyxin B for Injection, USP is a sterile, white lyophilized cake or powder and is supplied as follows: 500,000 Units per Vial 10 Vials in a Carton NDC 55150-234-10 Storage recommendations: Before reconstitution: Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light.
Retain in carton until time of use.
After reconstitution: Product must be stored under refrigeration, between 2° to 8°C (36° to 46°F) and any unused portion should be discarded after 72 hours.
The vial stoppers are not made with natural rubber latex.
