# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Codeine Sulfate

15 mg Tablet: white, round, uncoated tablet, scored on one side and debossed "27" on the other side.

Unit dose, 25 tablets per blister card NDC 0527-1727-91: 4 Blister Cards per Carton

30 mg Tablet: white, round, uncoated tablet, scored on one side and debossed "98" on the other side and are supplied in:

Unit dose, 25 tablets per blister card NDC 0527-1698-91: 4 Blister Cards per Carton NDC 0527-1698-01: Bottles of 100 Tablets

60 mg Tablet: white, round, uncoated tablet, scored on one side and debossed "99" on the other side and are supplied in: NDC 0527-1699-01: Bottles of 100 Tablets

Storage

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Protect from moisture and light.

Dispense in well-closed container as defined in the USP/NF.
Blisters are not child-resistant.
Use child-resistant closure if dispensing to outpatient.

All opioids are liable to diversion and misuse both by the general public and healthcare workers and should be handled accordingly.
