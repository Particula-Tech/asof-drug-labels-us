# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Each 15 mg tablet for oral administration contains 15 mg of codeine sulfate.
It is a white, round, uncoated tablet, scored on one side, and debossed "27" on the other side.

Each 30 mg tablet for oral administration contains 30 mg of codeine sulfate.
It is a white, round, uncoated tablet, scored on one side, and debossed "98" on the other side.

Each 60 mg tablet for oral administration contains 60 mg of codeine sulfate.
It is a white, round, uncoated tablet, scored on one side, and debossed "99" on the other side.
