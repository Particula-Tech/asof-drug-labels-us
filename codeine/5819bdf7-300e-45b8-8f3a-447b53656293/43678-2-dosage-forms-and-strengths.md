# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Each 15 mg tablet for oral administration contains 15 mg of Codeine Sulfate, USP.
It is a white, round, uncoated tablet, scored on one side, debossed “15” on the scored side and “LCI” on the other side.

Each 30 mg tablet for oral administration contains 30 mg of Codeine Sulfate, USP.
It is a white, round, uncoated tablet, scored on one side, debossed “30” on the scored side and “LCI” on the other side.

Each 60 mg tablet for oral administration contains 60 mg of Codeine Sulfate, USP.
It is a white, round, uncoated tablet, scored on one side, debossed “1699” on the scored side and “LCI” on the other side.
