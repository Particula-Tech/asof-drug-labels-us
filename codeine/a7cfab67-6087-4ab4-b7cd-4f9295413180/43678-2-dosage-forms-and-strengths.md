# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Extended-release tablets: Each tablet contains 54.3 mg of codeine phosphate (equivalent to 40 mg of codeine); and 8 mg of chlorpheniramine maleate (equivalent to 5.6 mg of chlorpheniramine).
Each tablet is white to off-white, uncoated, round, debossed with MP on one side and CC on the other side [see Description (11)].
