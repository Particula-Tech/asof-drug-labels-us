# HOW SUPPLIED

Penicillamine tablets, USP (Titratable Tablets) are white colored, oval shaped, film coated tablets with single score line on both sides.
On one side, tablets are debossed with "LU" on left and "L17" on right side of the score line and plain on other side.

Bottles of 100 Tablets NDC 70748-153-01

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Protect from moisture.

Dispense in a tight container.
