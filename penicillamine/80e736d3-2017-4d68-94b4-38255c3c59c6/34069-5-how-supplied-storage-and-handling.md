# HOW SUPPLIED

CUPRIMINE Capsules, USP 250 mg, are pale yellow to ivory opaque hard gelatin capsules imprinted with “ATON 705” on the cap and “CUPRIMINE” on the body.
They are supplied as follows:

NDC 25010-705-15 in bottles of 100

## Storage

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Dispense in a tightly closed container.
Keep container tightly closed.
