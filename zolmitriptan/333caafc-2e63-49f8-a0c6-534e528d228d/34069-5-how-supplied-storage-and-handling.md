# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Zolmitriptan tablet, USP 2.5 mg Tablets – Yellow colored, round, biconvex film-coated tablets debossed with “497” on one side and functionally-scored on the other side and are supplied in cartons containing a blister pack of 6 tablets (NDC 68462-497-76).

Zolmitriptan tablet, USP 5 mg Tablets – Pink colored, round, biconvex film-coated tablets debossed with “498” on one side and plain on the other side and are supplied in cartons containing a blister pack of 3 tablets (NDC 68462-498-33).

Store zolmitriptan tablets at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light and moisture.
