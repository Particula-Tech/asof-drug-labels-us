# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

2.5 mg Tablets – Yellow colored, round, biconvex film-coated tablets debossed with 497 on one side and deep breakline on other side and are supplied in cartons containing a blister pack of 6 tablets (NDC 68462-497-76).

5 mg Tablets – Pink colored, round, biconvex film-coated tablets debossed with 498 on one side and plain on other side and are supplied in cartons containing a blister pack of 3 tablets (NDC 68462-498-33).

Store zolmitriptan tablets at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light and moisture.
