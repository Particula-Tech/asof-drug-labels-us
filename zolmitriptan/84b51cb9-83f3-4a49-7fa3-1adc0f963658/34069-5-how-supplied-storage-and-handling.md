# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

The ZOMIG Nasal Spray device is a blue colored plastic device with a gray protection cap, labeled to indicate the nominal dose.
Each ZOMIG Nasal Spray device administers a single dose of ZOMIG.

ZOMIG Nasal Spray is supplied as a clear to pale yellow solution of zolmitriptan, buffered to a pH 5.0.
Each ZOMIG Nasal Spray device contains 2.5 mg or 5 mg of zolmitriptan in a 100 µL unit dose aqueous buffered solution containing citric acid, anhydrous, USP, disodium phosphate dodecahydrate USP and purified water USP.

2.5 mg ZOMIG® Nasal Spray is supplied in boxes of 6 single‑use nasal spray units.
(NDC 64896-682-51)

5 mg ZOMIG® Nasal Spray is supplied in boxes of 6 single‑use nasal spray units.
(NDC 64896-681-51).

Each ZOMIG® Nasal Spray single dose unit spray supplies 2.5 and 5 mg, respectively, of zolmitriptan.
The ZOMIG® Nasal Spray unit must be discarded after use.

Store at controlled room temperature, 20‑25°C (68‑77°F) [see USP].
