# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Zolmitriptan is indicated for the acute treatment of migraine with or without aura in adults

Limitations of Use

- Only use zolmitriptan if a clear diagnosis of migraine has been established.
  If a patient has no response to zolmitriptan treatment for the first migraine attack, reconsider the diagnosis of migraine before zolmitriptan is administered to treat any subsequent attacks.
- Zolmitriptan is not indicated for the prevention of migraine attacks.
- Safety and effectiveness of zolmitriptan have not been established for cluster headache.
