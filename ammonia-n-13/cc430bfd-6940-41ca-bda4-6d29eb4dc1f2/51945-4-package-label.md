Ammonia N 13 Injection Diagnostic – For Intravenous Use Only

Caution: Rx Only NDC#: 52768-100-30

Sterile, Non-pyrogenic Multiple-Dose Vial

Lot #: ________________

Calibration: Date ________________ Time________________ (EOS*)

Expiration Date/Time: ________________ / ________________ (Expires 60 minutes after EOS*)

Activity @ EOS*: ____________ mCi in ____________ mL = ____________ mCi/mL

Each mL contains 0.139 GBq to 9.620 GBq (3.75 mCi to 260 mCi), of no carrier added ammonia N 13 @ EOS* and 9 mg of sodium chloride.
Store upright in a shielded container at 25°C (77°F), excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F).
Aseptically withdraw and handle doses.
Do not use if cloudy or contains particulate matter.
[13N] Half-life = 9.96 minutes.
Calculate correct dosage from date and time of calibration.
