# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Ammonia N 13 Injection USP is packaged in 10 mL multiple dose glass vial containing between 6.94 GBq to 29.6 GBq (18.75 mCi to 800 mCi) of [13N] ammonia, at the end of synthesis (EOS) reference time, in 0.9% sodium chloride injection solution, in a total volume ranging between 5-10 mL.
The recommended dose of radioactivity (10 mCi-20 mCi) is associated with a theoretical mass dose of 0.5 picomoles-1.0 picomoles (8.47 picograms-16.94 picograms) of Ammonia.

Storage

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15-30°C (59-86°F).Use the solution within 60 minutes of the End of Synthesis (EOS) calibration.
