# 16 HOW SUPPLIED

Ammonia N 13 Injection is packaged in 10 mL multiple dose glass vials or 5 or 10 mL single dose disposable plastic syringes containing between 1.11 GBq to 11.1 GBq (30 mCi to 300 mCi) of [13N] ammonia, at the end of synthesis (EOS) reference time, in 0.9% sodium chloride injection solution in approximately 8 mL volume.
The recommended dose of radioactivity (10-20 mCi) is associated with a theoretical mass dose of 0.5- 1 picomoles (8.47 - 16.94 picograms) of Ammonia.
