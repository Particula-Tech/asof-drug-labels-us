# 13 NONCLINICAL TOXICOLOGY

## 13.1 Carcinogenesis, Mutagenesis, Impairment of Fertility

Long term animal studies have not been performed to evaluate the carcinogenic potential of Ammonia N 13 Injection.
Genotoxicity assays and impairment of male and female fertility studies with Ammonia N 13 Injection have not been performed.
