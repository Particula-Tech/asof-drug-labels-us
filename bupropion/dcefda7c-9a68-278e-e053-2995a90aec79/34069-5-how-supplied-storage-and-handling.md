# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

AUVELITY (dextromethorphan hydrobromide / bupropion hydrochloride) extended-release tablets are film-coated, round, bilayer tablets with debossing on one side.
AUVELITY is supplied in the following strengths and package configurations:

- Dextromethorphan hydrobromide 30 mg/bupropion hydrochloride 105 mg tablets are white with “AXS” above “30” debossed on one side:

Bottles of 21 tablets, NDC 81968-030-21

- Dextromethorphan hydrobromide 45mg/bupropion hydrochloride 105 mg tablets are beige with “45/105” debossed on one side:

Bottles of 30 tablets, NDC 81968-045-30

Bottles of 60 tablets, NDC 81968-045-60

Store AUVELITY in original bottle at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F), excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
