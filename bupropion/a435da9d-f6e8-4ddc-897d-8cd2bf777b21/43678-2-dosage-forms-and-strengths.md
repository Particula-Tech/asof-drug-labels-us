# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Wellbutrin XL extended-release tablets, 150 mg of bupropion hydrochloride, are creamy-white to pale yellow, round tablets imprinted with “Wellbutrin XL 150” in black ink on one side and the other side plain.

Wellbutrin XL extended-release tablets, 300 mg of bupropion hydrochloride, are creamy-white to pale yellow, round tablets imprinted with “Wellbutrin XL 300” in black ink on one side and the other side plain.
