# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Bupropion Hydrobromide Extended-Release Tablets, 174 mg of bupropion hydrobromide, are white to off-white, round tablets printed on one side with black ink “BR” over “174” in bottles of 30 tablets (NDC 68682-580-30).

Bupropion Hydrobromide Extended-Release Tablets, 348 mg of bupropion hydrobromide, are white to off-white, round tablets printed on one side with black ink “BR” over “348” in bottles of 30 tablets (NDC 68682-581-30).

Bupropion Hydrobromide Extended-Release Tablets, 522 mg of bupropion hydrobromide, are white to off-white, round tablets printed on one side with black ink “BR” over “522” in bottles of 30 tablets (NDC 68682-582-30).

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
