# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Bupropion hydrochloride extended-release tablets (XL), 450 mg of bupropion hydrochloride, USP, are white to off-white, oblong-shaped tablets printed with the “BUP450” on one side supplied in bottles of 30 tablets (NDC 24979-248-06).

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
