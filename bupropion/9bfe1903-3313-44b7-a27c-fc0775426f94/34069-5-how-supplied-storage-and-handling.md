# HOW SUPPLIED

WELLBUTRIN XL Extended-Release Tablets, 150 mg of bupropion hydrochloride, are creamy-white to pale yellow, round, tablets printed with “WELLBUTRIN XL 150” in bottles of 30 (NDC 64455-730-30) and 90 (NDC 64455-730-90).

WELLBUTRIN XL Extended-Release Tablets, 300 mg of bupropion hydrochloride, are creamy-white to pale yellow, round, tablets printed with “WELLBUTRIN XL 300” in bottles of 30 (NDC 64455-731-30).

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15-30°C (59-86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
