# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Bupropion hydrochloride extended-release tablets (SR), 200 mg of bupropion hydrochloride, are pale orange, film-coated, round, convex tablets debossed with "555" on one side and plain on the other side.

| Bottles of 60 | NDC 0115-5445-13 |
| --- | --- |
| Bottles of 180 | NDC 0115-5445-19 |

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light and moisture.
