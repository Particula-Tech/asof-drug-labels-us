# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Bupropion hydrochloride extended-release tablets (XL), 150 mg of bupropion hydrochloride, USP are white to off-white, round, bi-convex, film-coated tablets with “WPI” over “3331” printed in black ink in bottles of 30 tablets (NDC 0591-3331-30), bottles of 90 tablets (NDC 0591-3331-19) and bottles of 500 tablets (NDC 0591-3331-05).

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
