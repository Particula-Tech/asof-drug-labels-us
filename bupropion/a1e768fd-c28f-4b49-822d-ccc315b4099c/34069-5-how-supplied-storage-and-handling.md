# HOW SUPPLIED

Bupropion Hydrochloride Extended-Release Tablets (XL), 300 mg of bupropion hydrochloride, are white to off-white, round, film-coated tablets with WPI over 3332 printed in black ink in bottles of 30 tablets (NDC 0591-3332-30) and bottles of 1000 tablets (NDC 0591-3332-10).

## Storage

Store at 20°-25°C (68°-77°F).
