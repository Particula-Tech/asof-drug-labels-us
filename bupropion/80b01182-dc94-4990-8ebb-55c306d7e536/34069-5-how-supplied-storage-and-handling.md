# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

FORFIVO XL Extended-Release Tablets, 450 mg of bupropion hydrochloride, are white to off-white, oblong-shaped tablets printed with the “Forfivo” logo on one side supplied in bottles of 30 tablets (NDC 24979-247-06).

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
