# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

ZYBAN sustained‑release tablets, 150 mg of bupropion hydrochloride, are purple, round, biconvex, film‑coated tablets printed with “ZYBAN 150” in the ZYBAN Advantage Pack containing 1 bottle of 60 (NDC 0173-0556-01) tablets.

Store at room temperature, 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F and 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light and moisture.
