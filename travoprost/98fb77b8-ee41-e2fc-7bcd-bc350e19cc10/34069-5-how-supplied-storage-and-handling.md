# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Travoprost ophthalmic solution, USP (ionic buffered solution) 0.004% is a sterile, isotonic, buffered, preserved, aqueous solution of travoprost (0.04 mg/mL).

Travoprost ophthalmic solution, USP (ionic buffered solution) 0.004% is supplied as a 2.5 mL solution and a 5 mL solution in a 5 mL translucent polypropylene ophthalmic bottle with a translucent polypropylene ophthalmic dropper and a turquoise, opaque polypropylene cap.

2.5 mL fill NDC 60505-0593-4

5 mL fill NDC 60505-0593-1

Storage: Store at 2°C to 25°C (36°F to 77°F).

After opening, travoprost ophthalmic solution, USP (ionic buffered solution) 0.004% can be used until the expiration date on the bottle.
