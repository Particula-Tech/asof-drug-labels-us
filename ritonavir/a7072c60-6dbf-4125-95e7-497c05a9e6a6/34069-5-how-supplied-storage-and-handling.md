# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Lopinavir and Ritonavir Tablets, USP are available in the following strengths and package sizes:

Lopinavir and Ritonavir Tablets USP, 100 mg/25 mg: Yellow colored, film coated oval shaped biconvex tablets debossed with “LA59” on one side and plain on other side.

Bottles of 60 NDC 42385-933-60

Lopinavir and Ritonavir Tablets USP, 200 mg/50 mg: Yellow colored, film coated oval shaped biconvex tablets debossed with “LA58” on one side and plain on other side.

Bottles of 120 NDC 42385-934-12

Recommended Storage:

Store lopinavir and ritonavir tablets, USP at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP controlled room temperature].
Dispense in original container or USP equivalent tight container.

For patient use: exposure of this product to high humidity outside the original container or USP equivalent tight container for longer than 2 weeks is not recommended.
