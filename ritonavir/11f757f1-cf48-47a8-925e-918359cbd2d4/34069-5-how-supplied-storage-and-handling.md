# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Ritonavir Tablets USP, 100 mg are white to off-white, film-coated, ovaloid tablets, debossed with ‘Z’ on one side and ‘24’ on other side.
Bottles of 30 NDC 65862-687-30 Bottles of 100 NDC 65862-687-01 Bottles of 500 NDC 65862-687-05 Bottles of 1,000 NDC 65862-687-99 Recommended Storage Store at or below 30°C (86°F).
Exposure to temperatures up to 50°C (122°F) for seven days permitted.
Dispense in original container or USP equivalent tight container (75 mL, 200 mL, 1300 mL and 2000 mL).
For patient use: exposure of this product to high humidity outside the original or USP equivalent tight container (75 mL, 200 mL, 1300 mL and 2000 mL) for longer than 2 weeks is not recommended.
