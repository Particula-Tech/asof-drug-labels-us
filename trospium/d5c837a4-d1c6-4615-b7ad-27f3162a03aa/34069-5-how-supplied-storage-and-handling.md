# HOW SUPPLIED

Trospium chloride tablets 20 mg (brownish yellow, round biconvex film-coated tablets, debossed with ‘L’ on one side and '1' on other side) are supplied as follows:

Bottles of 30 NDC 68462-461-30 Bottles of 60 NDC 68462-461-60 Bottles of 500 NDC 68462-461-05 Bottles of 1000 NDC 68462-461-10

Store at controlled room temperature 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
Excursions permitted to 15° to 30° C (59° to 86° F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Protect from light and moisture.

Rx only
