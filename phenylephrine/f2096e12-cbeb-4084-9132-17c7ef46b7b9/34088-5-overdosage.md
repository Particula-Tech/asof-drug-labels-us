# 10 OVERDOSAGE

Overdose of phenylephrine hydrochloride injection 10 mg per mL can cause a rapid rise in blood pressure.
Symptoms of overdose include headache, vomiting, hypertension, reflex bradycardia, and cardiac arrhythmias including ventricular extrasystoles and ventricular tachycardia, and may cause a sensation of fullness in the head and tingling of the extremities.
Consider using an α-adrenergic antagonist.
