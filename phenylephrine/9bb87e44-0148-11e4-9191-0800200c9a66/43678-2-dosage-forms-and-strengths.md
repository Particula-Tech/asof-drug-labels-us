# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

VAZCULEP (phenylephrine hydrochloride) Injection, 10 mg/mL, is available in three vial sizes:

- Injection: 10 mg/mL in a single dose 1 mL vial (10 mg of phenylephrine hydrochloride per vial)
- Injection: 10 mg/mL in Pharmacy Bulk Package 5 mL vial (50 mg of phenylephrine hydrochloride per vial) that will provide five 1 mL single doses
- Injection: 10 mg/mL in Pharmacy Bulk Package 10 mL vial (100 mg of phenylephrine hydrochloride per vial) that will provide ten 1 mL single doses
