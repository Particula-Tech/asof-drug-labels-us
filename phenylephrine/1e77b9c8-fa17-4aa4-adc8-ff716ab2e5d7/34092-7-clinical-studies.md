# 14 CLINICAL STUDIES

Increases in systolic and mean blood pressure following administration of phenylephrine were observed in 42 literature-based studies in the perioperative setting, including 26 studies where phenylephrine was used in low- risk (ASA 1 and 2) pregnant women undergoing neuraxial anesthesia during cesarean delivery, 3 studies in non- obstetric surgery under neuraxial anesthesia, and 13 studies in patients undergoing surgery under general anesthesia.
Mean arterial blood pressure increases were also observed in two double-blind, active-controlled studies in patients with septic shock.
