# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Phenylephrine hydrochloride injection USP, 10 mg/mL is available in following vial sizes:

- Injection: 10 mg/mL in Pharmacy Bulk Package 5 mL vial (50 mg of phenylephrine hydrochloride, USP per vial) that will provide five 1 mL single-doses
- Injection: 10 mg/mL in Pharmacy Bulk Package 10 mL vial (100 mg of phenylephrine hydrochloride, USP per vial) that will provide ten 1 mL single-doses
