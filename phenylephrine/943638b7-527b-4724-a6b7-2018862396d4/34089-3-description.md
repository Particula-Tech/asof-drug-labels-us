# 11 DESCRIPTION

Phenylephrine is an alpha-1 adrenergic receptor agonist.
Phenylephrine hydrochloride injection, USP 10 mg per mL is a sterile, nonpyrogenic solution for intravenous use.
It must be diluted before administration as an intravenous bolus or continuous intravenous infusion.

Phenylephrine hydrochloride is soluble in water and ethanol, and insoluble in chloroform and ethyl ether.
Phenylephrine hydrochloride injection, USP 10 mg per mL is sensitive to light.
Each mL contains: phenylephrine hydrochloride 10 mg, sodium chloride 3.5 mg, sodium citrate dihydrate 4 mg, citric acid monohydrate 1 mg, and sodium metabisulfite 2 mg in water for injection.
The pH is adjusted with sodium hydroxide and/or hydrochloric acid if necessary.
The pH range is 3.5-5.5.
