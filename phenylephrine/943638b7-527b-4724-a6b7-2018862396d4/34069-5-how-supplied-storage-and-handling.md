# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Phenylephrine Hydrochloride Injection, USP, 10 mg per mL, is a clear, colorless solution supplied as follows:

| NDC | Phenylephrine Hydrochloride Injection, USP (10 mg per mL) | Package Factor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 71288-808-76 | 50 mg per 5 mL Pharmacy Bulk Package | 10 vials per carton |
| 71288-808-77 | 100 mg per 10 mL Pharmacy Bulk Package | 1 vial per carton |

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F).
[see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Protect from light.
Store in carton until time of use.

The diluted solution should not be held for more than 4 hours at room temperature or for more than 24 hours under refrigerated conditions.
Discard any unused portion.

Sterile, Nonpyrogenic.

The container closure is not made with natural rubber latex.
