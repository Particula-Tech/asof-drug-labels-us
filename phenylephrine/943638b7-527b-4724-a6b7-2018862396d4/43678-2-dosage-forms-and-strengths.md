# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Phenylephrine hydrochloride injection, USP 10 mg per mL is available in two vial sizes:

- Injection: 10 mg per mL in Pharmacy Bulk Package 5 mL vial (50 mg of phenylephrine hydrochloride per vial) that will provide five 1 mL single doses
- Injection: 10 mg per mL in Pharmacy Bulk Package 10 mL vial (100 mg of phenylephrine hydrochloride per vial) that will provide ten 1 mL single doses
