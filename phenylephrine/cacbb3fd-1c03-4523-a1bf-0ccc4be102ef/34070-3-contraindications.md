# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

## 4.1 Cardiac and Endocrine Disease

Phenylephrine hydrochloride ophthalmic solution 10% is contraindicated in patients with hypertension or thyrotoxicosis.
Phenylephrine hydrochloride ophthalmic solution 2.5% should be used in these patients.

## 4.2 Pediatric Patients Less Than 1 Year of Age

Phenylephrine hydrochloride ophthalmic solution 10% is contraindicated in pediatric patients less than 1 year of age due to the increased risk of systemic toxicity.
Phenylephrine hydrochloride ophthalmic solution 2.5% should be used in these patients [See Dosage and Administration (2.2)].
