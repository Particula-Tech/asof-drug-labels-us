# 13 NONCLINICAL TOXICOLOGY

## 13.1 Carcinogenesis, Mutagenesis, Impairment of Fertility

Carcinogenesis: Long-term animal studies that evaluated the carcinogenic potential of orally administered phenylephrine hydrochloride in F344/N rats and B6C3F1 mice were completed by the National Toxicology Program using the dietary route of administration.
There was no evidence of carcinogenicity in mice administered approximately 270 mg/kg/day (131 times the maximum recommended daily dose of < 10 mg/day) or rats administered approximately 50 mg/kg/day (48 times the maximum recommended daily dose of < 10 mg/day) based on body surface area comparisons.

Mutagenesis: Phenylephrine hydrochloride tested negative in the in vitro bacterial reverse mutation assay (S.typhimurium strains TA98, TA100, TA1535 and TA1537), the in vitro chromosomal aberrations assay, the in vitro sister chromatid exchange assay, and the in vivo rat micronucleus assay.
Positive results were reported in only one of two replicates of the in vitro mouse lymphoma assay.

Impairment of Fertility: Studies to evaluate the effect of phenylephrine on fertility have not been conducted.
