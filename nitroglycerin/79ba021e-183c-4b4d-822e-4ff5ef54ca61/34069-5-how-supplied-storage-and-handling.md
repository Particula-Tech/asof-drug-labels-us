# HOW SUPPLIED

NITROSTAT is supplied as white, round, flat-faced tablets in 3 strengths (0.3 mg, 0.4 mg, and 0.6 mg) in bottles containing 100 tablets each, with color-coded labels, and in color-coded Patient Convenience Packages of 4 bottles of 25 tablets each.

| 0.3 mg: | Coded "N" on one side and "3" on the other. NDC 0071-0417-24—Bottle of 100 tablets |
| --- | --- |
| 0.4 mg: | Coded "N" on one side and "4" on the other. NDC 0071-0418-13—Convenience Package NDC 0071-0418-24—Bottle of 100 tablets |
| 0.6 mg: | Coded "N" on one side and "6" on the other. NDC 0071-0419-24—Bottle of 100 tablets |

Store at Controlled Room Temperature 20°–25°C (68°–77°F) [see USP].
