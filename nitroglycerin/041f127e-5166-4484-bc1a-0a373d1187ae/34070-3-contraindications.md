# CONTRAINDICATIONS

Allergic reactions to organic nitrates are extremely rare, but they do occur.
Nitroglycerin is contraindicated in patients who are allergic to it.

Sublingual nitroglycerin therapy is contraindicated in patients with early myocardial infarction, severe anemia, increased intracranial pressure, and those with a known hypersensitivity to nitroglycerin.

Administration of nitroglycerin tablets is contraindicated in patients who are using sildenafil citrate since sildenafil citrate has been shown to potentiate the hypotensive effects of organic nitrates.
