# DESCRIPTION:

Nitroglycerin is 1,2,3-propanetriol trinitrate, an organic nitrate whose structural formula is:

whose empiric formula is C3H5N3O9, and whose molecular weight is 227.09.
The organic nitrates are vasodilators, active on both arteries and veins.

Nitroglycerin Injection, USP is a clear, practically colorless additive solution for intravenous infusion after dilution.
Each mL contains: Nitroglycerin 5 mg, Alcohol 30% (v/v), Propylene Glycol 30%, and Water for Injection q.s. pH (range 3.0 to 6.5) may have been adjusted with Sodium Hydroxide and/or Hydrochloric Acid.

The solution is sterile, non-pyrogenic, and nonexplosive.
