# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

- For injection: 150 mg white to pale yellow lyophilized powder with a cake-like appearance in a single-dose vial.
- For injection: 420 mg white to pale yellow lyophilized powder with a cake-like appearance in a multiple-dose vial.
- For injection: 420 mg white to pale yellow lyophilized powder with a cake-like appearance in a multiple-dose vial co-packaged with vial of 20 mL Bacteriostatic Water for Injection (BWFI), USP, to be used as a diluent.
