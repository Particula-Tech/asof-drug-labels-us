# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

HERZUMA (trastuzumab-pkrb) for Injection 420 mg/vial is supplied in a multiple-dose vial as a white to pale yellow lyophilized sterile powder, under vacuum.
Each carton contains one multiple-dose vial of HERZUMA and one vial (20 mL) of Bacteriostatic Water for Injection (BWFI), USP, containing 1.1% benzyl alcohol as a preservative.

NDC 63459-305-47.

## 16.2 Storage

Store HERZUMA vials in the refrigerator at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) until time of reconstitution.
