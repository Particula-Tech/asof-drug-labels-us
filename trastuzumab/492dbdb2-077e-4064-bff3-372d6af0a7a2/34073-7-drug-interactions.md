# 7 DRUG INTERACTIONS

Anthracyclines

Patients who receive anthracycline after stopping Herceptin may be at increased risk of cardiac dysfunction because of trastuzumab's estimated long washout period [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].
If possible, avoid anthracycline-based therapy for up to 7 months after stopping Herceptin.
If anthracyclines are used, closely monitor the patient's cardiac function.
