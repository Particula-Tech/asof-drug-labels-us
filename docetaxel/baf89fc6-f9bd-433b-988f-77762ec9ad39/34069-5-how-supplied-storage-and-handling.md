# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

DOCIVYXTM Injection is supplied in a multiple-dose vial as a sterile, pyrogen-free, non-aqueous, pale-yellow to brownish-yellow solution.

DOCIVYXTM (docetaxel) Injection, 10 mg/mL, is available as carton of one multiple-dose vial in the following strengths:

20 mg/2 mL (NDC: 83831-128-02)

80 mg/8 mL (NDC: 83831-129-08)

160 mg/16 mL (NDC: 83831-130-16)

## 16.2 Storage

Store between 2°C and 25°C (36°F and 77°F).
Retain in the original package to protect from light.

Freezing does not adversely affect the product.

After first use and following multiple needle entries and product withdrawals, DOCIVYX multiple-dose vials are stable for up to 28 days when stored between 2°C and 25°C (36°F and 77°F) and protected from light.

## 16.3 Handling and Disposal

DOCIVYX is a hazardous drug.
Follow applicable special handling and disposal procedures.
