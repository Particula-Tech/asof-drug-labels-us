# 3 DOSAGE FORMS & STRENGTHS

One-vial Docetaxel Injection Concentrate

Docetaxel Injection Concentrate is available as 20 mg/mL, 80 mg/4 mL, 140 mg/7 mL and 160 mg/8 mL.
Each mL contains 20 mg docetaxel, citric acid anhydrous (6 mg), Povidone (kollidon 12 PF) (100 mg), polysorbate 80 (424 mg) and ethanol (400 mg).
