# 3. DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Docetaxel Injection 80 mg/2 mL

Docetaxel Injection 80 mg/2 mL: 80 mg docetaxel in 2 mL polysorbate 80 and Diluent for Docetaxel Injection 80 mg (13% (w/v) polyethylene glycol 400 in water for injection).
Both items are in a blister pack in one carton.

Docetaxel Injection 20 mg/0.5 mL Docetaxel Injection 20 mg/0.5 mL: 20 mg docetaxel in 0.5 mL polysorbate 80 and Diluent for Docetaxel Injection 20 mg (13% (w/v) polyethylene glycol 400 in water for injection).
Both items are in a blister pack in one carton.
