# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Docetaxel is contraindicated in patients with:

- neutrophil counts of <1500 cells/mm3 [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3)].
- a history of severe hypersensitivity reactions to docetaxel or to other drugs formulated with polysorbate 80.
  Severe reactions, including anaphylaxis, have occurred [see Warnings and Precautions (5.5)].
