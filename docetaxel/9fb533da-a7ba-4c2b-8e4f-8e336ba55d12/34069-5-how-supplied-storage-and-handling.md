# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Docetaxel Injection, USP is a non-aqueous, clear, colorless to pale yellow solution, and is supplied as follows:

| NDC | Docetaxel Injection, USP (10 mg per mL) | Package Factor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 71288-144-08 71288-144-16 | 80 mg per 8 mL (10 mg per mL) Multi-Dose Vial 160 mg per 16 mL (10 mg per mL) Multi-Dose Vial | 1 vial per carton 1 vial per carton |

## 16.2 Storage

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

Retain in the original package to protect from light.
Freezing does not adversely affect the product.

After first use and following multiple needle entries and product withdrawals, Docetaxel Injection, USP multi-dose vials are stable for up to 28 days when stored between 2° and 8°C (36° and 46°F) and protected from light.

## 16.3 Handling and Disposal

Docetaxel Injection, USP is a hazardous drug.
Follow applicable special handling and disposal procedures.1
