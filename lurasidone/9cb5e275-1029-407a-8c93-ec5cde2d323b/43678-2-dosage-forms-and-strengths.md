# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Lurasidone hydrochloride tablets are available in the following shape and color (Table 1) with respective one-sided debossing.

Table 1: Lurasidone Hydrochloride Tablets Presentations

| Tablet Strength | Tablet Color/Shape | Tablet Markings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 20 mg | white to off-white round biconvex | Plain on one side and debossed "64" on other side |
| 40 mg | white to off-white round biconvex | Plain on one side and debossed "65" on other side. |
| 60 mg | white to off-white oblong | Plain on one side and debossed “509” on other side. |
| 80 mg | pale green oval | Plain on one side and debossed "466" on other side. |
| 120 mg | white to off-white oval | Plain on one side and debossed "465" on other side. |
