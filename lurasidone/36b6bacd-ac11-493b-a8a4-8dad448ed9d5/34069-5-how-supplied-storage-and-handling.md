# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Lurasidone hydrochloride tablets are white to off-white, round (20 mg or 40 mg), white to off-white, capsule shape (60 mg), light-green to green, oval (80 mg) or white to off-white, oval (120 mg) and identified with strength- specific two-sided debossing, “20” on one side and “L” on other side (20 mg), “40” on one side and “L” on other side (40 mg), “60” on one side and “L” on other side (60mg), “80” on one side and “L” on other side (80 mg) or "120” on one side and “L” on other side (120 mg).

Storage

Store lurasidone hydrochloride tablets at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
