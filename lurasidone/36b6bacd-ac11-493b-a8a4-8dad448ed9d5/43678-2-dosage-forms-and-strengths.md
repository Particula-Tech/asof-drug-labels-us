# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Lurasidone hydrochloride tablets are available in the following shape and color (Table 1) with respective two sided debossing.

Table 1: Lurasidone Hydrochloride Tablet Presentations

| Tablet Strength | Tablet Color/Shape | Tablet Markings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 20 mg | white to off-white round | “20” on one side and “L” on other side |
| 40 mg | white to off-white round | “40” on one side and “L” on other side |
| 60 mg | white to off-white capsule | “60" on one side and “L” on other side |
| 80 mg | Light-green to green oval | “80” on one side and “L” on other side |
| 120 mg | white to off-white oval | “120” on one side and “L” on other side |
