# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

LATUDA is contraindicated in the following:

- Any patient with a known hypersensitivity to lurasidone HCl or any components in the formulation.
  Angioedema has been observed with lurasidone [see Adverse Reactions (6.1)].
- Concomitant use with strong CYP3A4 inhibitors (e.g., ketoconazole) [see Drug Interactions (7.1)].
- Concomitant use with strong CYP3A4 inducers (e.g., rifampin) [see Drug Interactions (7.1)].
