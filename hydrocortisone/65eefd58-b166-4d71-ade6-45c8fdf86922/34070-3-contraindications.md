# CONTRAINDICATIONS

SOLU-CORTEF Sterile Powder is contraindicated in systemic fungal infections and patients with known hypersensitivity to the product and its constituents.

Intramuscular corticosteroid preparations are contraindicated for idiopathic thrombocytopenic purpura.

SOLU-CORTEF Sterile Powder is contraindicated for intrathecal administration.
Reports of severe medical events have been associated with this route of administration.
