# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

KHINDIVI is indicated as replacement therapy in pediatric patients 5 years of age and older with adrenocortical insufficiency.

Limitations of Use

KHINDIVI is not approved for increased dosing during periods of stress or acute events.
Use a different hydrocortisone-containing drug product for stress dosing [(see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
