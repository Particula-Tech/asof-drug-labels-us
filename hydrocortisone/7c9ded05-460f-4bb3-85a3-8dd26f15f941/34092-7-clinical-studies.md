# 14 CLINICAL STUDIES

## 14.2 Pediatric Atopic Dermatitis

In a multicenter, randomized, vehicle-controlled trial of 264 pediatric subjects 3 months to 18 years of age with mild to moderate atopic dermatitis, Locoid Lipocream or vehicle was applied twice daily for up to four weeks.
Treatment success was assessed at day 29 (after 28 days of treatment) and was defined as the proportion of patients who achieved both "clear" or "almost clear" and at least a two grade improvement from baseline on a 5-point Physician's Global Assessment (PGA) scale.
