# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

Locoid Lotion is not for oral, ophthalmic, or intravaginal use.

Apply a thin layer to the affected skin areas two times daily.
Rub in gently.

Therapy should be discontinued when control is achieved.
If no improvement is seen within 2 weeks, reassessment of the diagnosis may be necessary.
The safety of Locoid Lotion has not been established beyond 4 weeks of use.

Locoid Lotion should not be used with occlusive dressings or applied in the diaper area unless directed by a physician.
