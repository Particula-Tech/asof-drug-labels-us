# 14 CLINICAL STUDIES

In a multicenter, randomized, vehicle-controlled trial of 284 pediatric subjects 3 months to 18 years of age with mild to moderate atopic dermatitis, LOCOID Lotion or vehicle was applied twice daily for up to 4 weeks.
Treatment success was assessed at day 29 (after 28 days of treatment) and was defined as the proportion of patients who achieved both “clear” or “almost clear” and at least a 2-grade improvement from baseline on a 5-point Physician’s Global Assessment (PGA) scale.
Study results are shown in Table 3.

Another multicenter, randomized, double-blind study compared twice-daily treatment with LOCOID Lotion (n=151) to vehicle (n=150) for 3 or 4 weeks in adult subjects (ages 18 years or older) having atopic dermatitis, including those with mild or moderate severity.
Results favored LOCOID Lotion over vehicle.
