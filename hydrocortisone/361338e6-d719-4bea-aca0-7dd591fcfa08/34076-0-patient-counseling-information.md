# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

Advise the patient and/or caregiver to read the FDA-approved patient labeling (Patient Information).

Inform patients and/or caregivers of the following:

1. 1.Discontinue therapy when control is achieved unless directed otherwise by the physician.
2. 2.If no improvement is seen within two weeks, contact the physician.
3. 3.Avoid contact with the eyes.
4. 4.Do not use with occlusive dressing unless directed by the physician.
5. 5.Report any signs or symptoms of local or systemic adverse reactions to the physician.
6. 6.Do not treat diaper dermatitis.
   Do not apply PANDEL in the diaper area as diapers or plastic pants may constitute occlusive dressings.
7. 7.Do not use on the face, underarms, or groin areas unless directed by the physician.
8. 8.Advise a woman to use PANDEL on the smallest area of skin and for the shortest duration possible while breastfeeding.
