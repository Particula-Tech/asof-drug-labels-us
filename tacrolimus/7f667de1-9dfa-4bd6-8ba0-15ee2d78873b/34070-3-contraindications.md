# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

PROGRAF is contraindicated in patients with a hypersensitivity to tacrolimus.
PROGRAF injection is contraindicated in patients with a hypersensitivity to HCO-60 (polyoxyl 60 hydrogenated castor oil).
Hypersensitivity symptoms reported include dyspnea, rash, pruritus, and acute respiratory distress syndrome [see Adverse Reactions (6)].
