# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

PROGRAF is available in the following dosage forms and strengths:

| Capsules | Oblong, hard capsule for oral administration contains anhydrous tacrolimus USP as follows: • 0.5 mg, light-yellow color, imprinted in red “0.5 mg” on the capsule cap and “607” on capsule body • 1 mg, white color, imprinted in red “1 mg” on the capsule cap and “617” on capsule body • 5 mg, grayish-red color, imprinted with white “5 mg” on the capsule cap and “657” on capsule body |
| --- | --- |
| Injection | 1 mL ampule for intravenous infusion contains anhydrous tacrolimus USP, 5 mg/mL |
| For Oral Suspension | Unit-dose packets with white granules for oral suspension contains anhydrous tacrolimus USP: •0.2 mg•1 mg |
