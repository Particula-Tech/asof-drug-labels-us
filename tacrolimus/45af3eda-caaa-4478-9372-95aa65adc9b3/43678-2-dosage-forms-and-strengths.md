# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Tacrolimus Capsules, USP are available containing the equivalent of 0.5 mg, 1 mg or 5 mg of anhydrous tacrolimus, USP.

- •The 0.5 mg capsule is a hard-shell gelatin capsule with a light orange opaque cap and a gray opaque body filled with white to off-white powder.
  The capsule is axially printed with MYLAN over 2045 in black ink on both the cap and the body.
- •The 1 mg capsule is a hard-shell gelatin capsule with a light blue opaque cap and a gray opaque body filled with white to off-white powder.
  The capsule is axially printed with MYLAN over 2046 in black ink on both the cap and the body.
- •The 5 mg capsule is a hard-shell gelatin capsule with a rubine red opaque cap and a gray opaque body filled with white to off-white powder.
  The capsule is axially printed with MYLAN over 2047 in black ink on both the cap and the body.
