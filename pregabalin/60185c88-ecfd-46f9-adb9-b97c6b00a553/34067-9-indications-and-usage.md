# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

LYRICA is indicated for:

## 1.1 Management of neuropathic pain associated with diabetic peripheral neuropathy

## 1.2 Management of postherpetic neuralgia

## 1.3 Adjunctive therapy for adult patients with partial onset seizures

## 1.4 Management of fibromyalgia

## 1.5 Adjunctive Treatment of Generalized Anxiety Disorder

LYRICA is indicated as an adjunctive treatment of generalized anxiety disorder (GAD).

Generalized anxiety disorder is defined by the DSM-IV as excessive anxiety and worry, occurring more days than not, for at least 6 months.
The excessive anxiety and worry must be difficult to control and causes significant distress or impairment in normal functioning.
It is associated with at least 3 of the following 6 symptoms: restlessness or feeling keyed up or on edge, being easily fatigued, difficulty concentrating or mind going blank, irritability, muscle tension, and sleep disturbance.
