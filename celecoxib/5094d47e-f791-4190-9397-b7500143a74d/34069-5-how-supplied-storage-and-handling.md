# 16. HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

VYSCOXA (celecoxib) oral suspension, 10 mg/mL celecoxib is a white, opaque, unflavored suspension, supplied as:

| NDC Number | Size |
| --- | --- |
| 15370-501-16 | 473 mL Bottle |

Storage

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from Light.
Shake well before use.
Discard 21 days after opening.
