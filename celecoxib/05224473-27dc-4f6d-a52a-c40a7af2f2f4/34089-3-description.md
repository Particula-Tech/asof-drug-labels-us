# 11 DESCRIPTION

Celecoxib capsules are a nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug, available as capsules containing 50 mg, 100 mg, 200 mg and 400 mg celecoxib, USP for oral administration.
The chemical name is 4-[5-(4-methylphenyl)-3-(trifluoromethyl)-1H-pyrazol-1-yl] benzenesulfonamide and is a diaryl-substituted pyrazole.
It has the following chemical structure:

C17H14F3N3O2S M.W.
381.38

Celecoxib, USP is a white to off-white powder with a pKa of 11.1 (sulfonamide moiety).
Celecoxib, USP is hydrophobic (log P is 3.5) and is practically insoluble in aqueous media at physiological pH range.

The inactive ingredients in celecoxib capsules include: sodium lauryl sulfate, FD&C Blue No.1, black iron oxide, shellac, dehydrated alcohol, gelatin, lactose monohydrate, crospovidone, povidone, titanium dioxide and magnesium stearate.
The 50 mg, 200 mg and 400 mg capsules also contain D&C Yellow No. 10.
The 50 mg, 100 mg and 200 mg capsules also contain D&C Red No. 28.
The 400 mg capsule also contains FD&C Yellow No. 6.
