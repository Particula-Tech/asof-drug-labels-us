# 11 DESCRIPTION

ELYXYB is an oral solution of celecoxib, a nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug.
Each unit dose of ELYXYB contains 120 mg of celecoxib.
Celecoxib is a white or almost white, crystalline or amorphous powder with a pKa of 11.
Celecoxib is hydrophobic (log P is 3.0) and practically insoluble in water.
Celecoxib is chemically designated as p-[5- p-tolyl-3-(trifluoromethyl) pyrazol-1-yl] benzenesulfonamide.
The empirical formula for celecoxib is C 17H 14F 3N 3O 2S, and the molecular weight is 381.37.
