# 11 DESCRIPTION

Celecoxib is chemically designated as 4-[5-(4-methylphenyl)- 3-(trifluoromethyl)-1H-pyrazol-1-yl] benzenesulfonamide and is a diaryl-substituted pyrazole.

Celecoxib oral capsules contain either 50 mg, 100 mg, 200 mg or 400 mg of celecoxib, together with inactive ingredients including: black iron oxide, croscarmellose sodium, FD&C blue #1, FD&C red #40, gelatin, lactose monohydrate, magnesium stearate, potassium hydroxide, povidone, propylene glycol, shellac, sodium lauryl sulphate, titanium dioxide, red iron oxide and yellow iron oxide.
