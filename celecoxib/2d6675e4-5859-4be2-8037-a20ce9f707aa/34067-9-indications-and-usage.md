# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

CELEBREX is indicated

## 1.1 Osteoarthritis (OA)

1. For the management of the signs and symptoms of OA [see Clinical Studies (14.1)].

## 1.2 Rheumatoid Arthritis (RA)

1. For the management of the signs and symptoms of RA [see Clinical Studies (14.2)].

## 1.3 Juvenile Rheumatoid Arthritis (JRA)

1. For the management of the signs and symptoms of JRA in patients 2 years and older [see Clinical Studies (14.3)].

## 1.4 Ankylosing Spondylitis (AS)

1. For the management of the signs and symptoms of AS [see Clinical Studies (14.4)].

## 1.5 Acute Pain

1. For the management of acute pain in adults [see Clinical Studies (14.5)].

## 1.6 Primary Dysmenorrhea

1. For the management of primary dysmenorrhea [see Clinical Studies (14.5)].
