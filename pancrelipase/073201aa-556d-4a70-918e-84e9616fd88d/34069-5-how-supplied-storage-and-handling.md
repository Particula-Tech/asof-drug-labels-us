Storage and Handling

CREON must be stored at room temperature 15°C to 25°C (59°F to 77°F) and protected from moisture.
Temperature excursions are permitted between 25°C to 40°C (77°F to 104°F) for up to 30 days.
CREON should be discarded if exposed to higher temperature and moisture conditions higher than 70%.

After opening, keep bottle tightly closed between uses to protect from moisture.
Keep the desiccant in the bottle if present.

Store and dispense CREON in the original container.

Do not crush CREON delayed-release capsules or the capsule contents [see Dosage and Administration (2.1)].
