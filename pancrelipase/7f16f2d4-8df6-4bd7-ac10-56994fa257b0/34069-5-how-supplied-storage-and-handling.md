Storage and Handling

Original container:

Store at room temperature, 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) and protect from moisture.
Brief excursions permitted to 15°C to 40°C (59°F to104°F) for 24 hours.
After opening, keep bottle tightly closed between uses to protect from moisture.

Repackaged HDPE container:

Dispense in tight container (USP).
Store at up to 30°C (86°F) for up to 6 months and protect from moisture.
Brief excursions permitted to 15°C to 40°C (59°F to 104°F) for up to 30 days.
Protect from moisture.
After opening, keep bottle tightly closed between uses to protect from moisture.

Zenpep is dispensed in bottles containing a desiccant.
