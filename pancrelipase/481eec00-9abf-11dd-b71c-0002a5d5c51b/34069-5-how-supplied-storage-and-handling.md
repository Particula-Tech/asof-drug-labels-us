Storage and Handling

- Store PERTZYE at room temperature 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F), excursions permitted to 15°C to 40°C (59°F to 104°F) for up to 30 days.
  PERTZYE hard gelatin capsules should be stored in a dry place in the original container or equivalent airtight container.
  After opening, keep the container tightly closed between uses to protect from moisture.
- PERTZYE is dispensed in bottles containing a desiccant.
