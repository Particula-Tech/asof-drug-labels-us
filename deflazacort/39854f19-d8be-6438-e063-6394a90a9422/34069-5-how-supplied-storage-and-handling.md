# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

PYQUVI Oral Suspension

- 22.75 mg/mL is a white to off-white suspension.
  Supplied as 13 mL in a 30 mL bottle packaged with one bottle adapter and two 1 mL oral dispensers.
  NDC 73289-0089-1

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

Discard any unused PYQUVI Oral Suspension remaining after 1 month of first opening the bottle.
