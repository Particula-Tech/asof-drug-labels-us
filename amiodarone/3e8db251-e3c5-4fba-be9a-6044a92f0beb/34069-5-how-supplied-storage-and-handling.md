# HOW SUPPLIED

Amiodarone Hydrochloride Tablets, 200 mg are white to off-white, round-shaped, flat beveled-edge, uncoated tablets with bisect on one side and other side is plain; one side of bisect is debossed with 'ZE' and other side is debossed with '65' and are supplied as follows:

NDC 65841-631-06 in bottle of 30 tablets

NDC 65841-631-14 in bottle of 60 tablets

NDC 65841-631-05 in bottle of 500 tablets

NDC 65841-631-10 in bottle of 1000 tablets

NDC 65841-631-77 in unit-dose blister cartons of 100 (10 x 10) unit-dose tablets

Storage:

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light.

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container.
