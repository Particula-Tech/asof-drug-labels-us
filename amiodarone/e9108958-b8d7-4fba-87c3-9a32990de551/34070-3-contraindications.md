# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

NEXTERONE is contraindicated in patients with:

1. •Known hypersensitivity to any of the components of NEXTERONE Premixed Injection, including iodine [see Warnings and Precautions (5.8)].
2. •Cardiogenic shock.
3. •Marked sinus bradycardia.
4. •Second- or third-degree atrioventricular (AV) block unless a functioning pacemaker is available.
