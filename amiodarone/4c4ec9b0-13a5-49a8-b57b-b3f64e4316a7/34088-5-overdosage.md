# 10 OVERDOSAGE

There have been cases, some fatal, of amiodarone hydrochloride overdose.

Monitor the patient's cardiac rhythm and blood pressure, and, if bradycardia ensues, a β-adrenergic agonist or a pacemaker may be used.
Treat hypotension with inadequate tissue perfusion with positive inotropic and vasopressor agents.
Neither Pacerone nor its metabolite is dialyzable.
