# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Amiodarone HCl Injection, 50 mg/mL, is available in:

150 mg/3 mL - single-use vial - NDC #67457-153-03, packages of 10.

450 mg/9 mL - single-use vial - NDC #67457-153-09, packages of 10.

900 mg/18 mL - single-use vial - NDC #67457-153-18, packages of 1.

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]
Protect from light and excessive heat.
Use carton to protect contents from light until used.
Stoppers do not contain natural rubber latex.
