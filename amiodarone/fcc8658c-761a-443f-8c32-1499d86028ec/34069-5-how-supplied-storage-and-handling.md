# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/ STORAGE AND HANDLING

Amiodarone HCl Injection, 50 mg/mL, is available in:

150 mg/3 mL - 3 mL single use vial - NDC #67457-153-03, packages of 10.
450 mg/9 mL - 9 mL single use vial - NDC #67457-153-09, packages of 10.
900 mg/18 mL - 18 mL single use vial - NDC #67457-153-18, packages of 1.

Store at 20° - 25°C (68° - 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° - 30°C (59° - 86°F).
See USP Controlled Room Temperature.
Protect from light and excessive heat.
Use carton to protect contents from light until used.
Stoppers do not contain natural rubber latex.
