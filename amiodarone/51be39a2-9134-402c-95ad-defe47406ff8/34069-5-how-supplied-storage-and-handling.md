# HOW SUPPLIED

Cordarone® (amiodarone HCl) Tablets are available in bottles of 60 tablets as follows:

200 mg, NDC 0008-4188-04, round, convex-faced, pink tablets with a raised “C” and marked “200” on one side, with reverse side scored and marked “WYETH” and “4188.”

Keep tightly closed.

Store at Controlled Room Temperature, 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).

Protect from light.

Dispense in a light-resistant, tight container.
