# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/ STORAGE AND HANDLING

Amiodarone Hydrochloride Injection, 50 mg/mL, is available in cartons of 10 vials, 3 mL each, as follows:

50 mg per mL, NDC 0143-9875-10

Store at 20º-25ºC (68º-77ºF) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Protect from light and excessive heat.

Use carton to protect contents from light until time of use.
