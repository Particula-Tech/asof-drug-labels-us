# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 Levofloxacin Tablets

Levofloxacin Tablets, 250 mg are terra pink colored capsule shaped, biconvex film-coated tablets, debossed with ‘13’ on one side and ‘T’ on the other side.
Bottles of 50 NDC 65862-536-50 Bottles of 1000 NDC 65862-536-99 10 x 10 Unit-dose Tablets NDC 65862-536-10 Levofloxacin Tablets, 500 mg are peach colored capsule shaped, biconvex film-coated tablets, debossed with ‘12’ on one side and ‘T’ on the other side.
Bottles of 50 NDC 65862-537-50 Bottles of 1000 NDC 65862-537-99 10 x 10 Unit-dose Tablets NDC 65862-537-10 Levofloxacin Tablets, 750 mg are white capsule shaped, biconvex film-coated tablets, debossed with ‘11’ on one side and ‘T’ on the other side.
Bottles of 20 NDC 65862-538-20 Bottles of 500 NDC 65862-538-05 10 x 10 Unit-dose Tablets NDC 65862-538-10 Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature] in well-closed containers.
