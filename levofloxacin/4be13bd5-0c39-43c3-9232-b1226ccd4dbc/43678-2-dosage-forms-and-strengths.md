# 3. DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Levofloxacin Tablets 250, 500, and 750 mg are film coated:

- 250 mg terra cotta pink, oblong shaped, film-coated tablets, imprinted 'L021' (in black ink) on one side and plain on the other side.
- 500 mg peach, oblong shaped, film-coated tablets, imprinted 'L022' (in black ink) on one side and plain on the other side.
- 750 mg white, capsule-shaped, film-coated tablets, imprinted 'L023' (in black ink) on one side and plain on the other side.
