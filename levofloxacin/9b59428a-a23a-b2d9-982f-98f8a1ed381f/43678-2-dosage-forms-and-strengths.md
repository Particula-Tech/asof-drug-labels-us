# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Levofloxacin tablets USP, 250 mg are white colored, modified capsule shaped, biconvex, film coated tablets debossed with ‘RDY’ on one side and ‘279’ on other side.

Levofloxacin tablets USP, 500 mg are orange colored, modified capsule shaped, biconvex, film coated tablets debossed with ‘RDY’ on one side and ‘280’ on other side.

Levofloxacin tablets USP, 750 mg are yellow colored, modified capsule shaped, biconvex, film coated tablets debossed with ‘RDY’ on one side and ‘281’ on other side.
