# 10 OVERDOSAGE

A limited number of accidental overdoses of octreotide in adults have been reported.
The doses ranged from 2,400 mcg/day to 6,000 mcg/day administered by continuous infusion or subcutaneously 1,500 mcg three times a day.
Adverse reactions in some patients included arrhythmia, hypotension, cardiac arrest, brain hypoxia, pancreatitis, hepatitis steatosis, hepatomegaly, lactic acidosis, flushing, diarrhea, lethargy, weakness, and weight loss.

If overdose occurs, contact Poison Control (1-800-222-1222) for latest recommendations.
