PRINCIPAL DISPLAY PANEL - 50 mcg

NDC 67457-239-01

Subcutaneous Injection See Insert

Octreotide Acetate Injection

50 mcg (base)/1 mL (0.05 mg/mL)

Needle shield may contain latex

Rx Only 10 x 1 mL Single Dose Unit-of-Use Syringes with a fixed 27 gauge 1/2 inch needle

Usual Dosage: See accompanying prescribing information.

Storage: REFRIGERATE at 2° to 8°C (36° to 46°F).

Protect from light.

Retain in carton until time of use.

Discard after initial use.

Each mL contains:

Octreotide (as acetate)..........................
50 mcg L-lactic acid 3 mg, sodium chloride 7 mg, sodium hydroxide to adjust pH, and water for injection, q.s.
