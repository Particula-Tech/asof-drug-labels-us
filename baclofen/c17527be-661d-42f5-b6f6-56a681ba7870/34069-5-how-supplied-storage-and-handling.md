# HOW SUPPLIED

Baclofen Tablets, USP 10 mg are off-white, scored, oval-shaped tablets debossed "2265" on one side and "V" on the other.
Supplied in bottles of 10, 100, 500 and 1000.

Baclofen Tablets, USP 20 mg are off-white, scored, capsule-shaped tablets debossed "2266" on one side and "V" on the other.
Supplied in bottles of 10, 100, 500 and 1000.
