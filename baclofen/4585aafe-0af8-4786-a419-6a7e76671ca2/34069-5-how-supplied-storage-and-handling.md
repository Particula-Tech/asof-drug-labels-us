# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Baclofen oral suspension contains 25 mg per 5 mL (5 mg/mL) baclofen.
It is a concentrated orange to yellow-colored, grape-flavored suspension and is supplied in high-density polyethylene (HDPE) bottles with white, polypropylene, child-resistant closures with a foam liner and heat induction layered inner seal.

120 mL, NDC 52536-600-11

300 mL, NDC 52536-600-12

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15° and 30° (59° and 86° F) (see USP Controlled Room Temperature).

Discard unused portion 2 months after first opening.
