# DESCRIPTION

Lioresal, baclofen USP, is a muscle relaxant and antispastic, available as 10-mg and 20-mg tablets for oral administration.

Baclofen USP is a white to off-white, odorless or practically odorless crystalline powder, with a molecular weight of 213.66.
It is slightly soluble in water, very slightly soluble in methanol, and insoluble in chloroform.

Inactive Ingredients.
Cellulose compounds, magnesium stearate, povidone, and starch.
