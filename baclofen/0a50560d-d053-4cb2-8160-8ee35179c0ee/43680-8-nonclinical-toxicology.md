# 13 NONCLINICAL TOXICOLOGY

## 13.1 Carcinogenesis, Mutagenesis, Impairment of Fertility

No increase in tumors was seen in rats receiving baclofen orally for two years at approximately 30 - 60 times on a mg/ kg basis, or 10 - 20 times on a mg/m2 basis, the maximum oral dose recommended for human use.
Mutagenicity assays with baclofen have not been performed.
