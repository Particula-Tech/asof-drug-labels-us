# HOW SUPPLIED

Baclofen Tablets, 10 mg are available as white, round tablets with functional scoring; one side scored and debossed with "BAC" above the score and "10" below the score, and the other side unscored and debossed with "U-S".
Tablets are supplied in bottles of 90 (NDC 0832-1054-90), bottles of 100 (NDC 0832-1054-11), bottles of 500 (NDC 0832-1054-15), and bottles of 1000 (NDC 0832-1054-10).

Baclofen Tablets, 20 mg are available as white, round tablets with functional scoring; one side scored and debossed "BAC" above the score and "20" below the score, and the other side unscored and debossed with "U-S".
Tablets are supplied in bottles of 90 (NDC 0832-1055-90), bottles of 100 (NDC 0832-1055-11), bottles of 500 (NDC 0832-1055-15), and bottles of 1000 (NDC 0832-1055-10).

Dispense in a tight container as defined in the USP.

Keep out of reach of children.

Store at 20°-25°C (68°-77°F).
Excursions permitted to 15°-30°C (59°-86°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
