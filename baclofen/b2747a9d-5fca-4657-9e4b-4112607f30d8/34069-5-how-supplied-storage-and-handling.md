# HOW SUPPLIED

Baclofen Tablets, USP 10 mg are available as white, round tablets with functional scoring; one side scored and debossed with "BAC" above the score and "10" below the score, and the other side unscored and debossed with "U-S".
They are supplied as follows:

Bottles of 90 with a child-resistant closure, NDC 0832-1054-90

Bottles of 100, NDC 0832-1054-11

Bottles of 500, NDC 0832-1054-15

Bottles of 1,000, NDC 0832-1054-10

Baclofen Tablets, USP 20 mg are available as white, round tablets with functional scoring; one side scored and debossed "BAC" above the score and "20" below the score, and the other side unscored and debossed with "U-S".
They are supplied as follows:

Bottles of 90 with a child-resistant closure, NDC 0832-1055-90

Bottles of 100, NDC 0832-1055-11

Bottles of 500, NDC 0832-1055-15

Bottles of 1,000, NDC 0832-1055-10

Pharmacist: Dispense in a tight container as defined in the USP with a child-resistant closure.

Keep out of reach of children.

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
