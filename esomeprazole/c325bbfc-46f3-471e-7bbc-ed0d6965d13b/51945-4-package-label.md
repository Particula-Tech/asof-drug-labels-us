# PACKAGE/LABEL PRINCIPAL DISPLAY PANEL – 20 mg/Vial

NDC 0186-6020-01 Discard unused portion.

Rx only

NEXIUM® I.V.

(esomeprazole magnesium)

For Injection

For Intravenous Use Only.

20 mg mg/vial*

*Each vial contains esomeprazole sodium 21.3 mg, equivalent to esomeprazole 20 mg.

Edetate disodium 1.5 mg and sodium hydroxide for pH adjustment.
Intravenous injection:

After reconstitution with 5 mL of 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection, USP, the resulting solution

will contain esomeprazole 4 mg/mL.
Intravenous infusion: After reconstitution in 50 mL or

0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection, USP, Lactated Ringer’s Injection, USP or 5% Dextrose

Injection, USP, the resulting solution will contain esomeprazole 0.4 mg/mL.
