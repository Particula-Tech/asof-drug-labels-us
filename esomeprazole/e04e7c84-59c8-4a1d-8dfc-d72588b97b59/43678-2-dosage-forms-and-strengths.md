# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Esomeprazole Magnesium Delayed-Release Capsules, USP are available containing 22.25 mg or 44.50 mg of esomeprazole magnesium, USP equivalent to 20 mg or 40 mg of esomeprazole, respectively.

- •The 20 mg capsules are hard gelatin capsules with a white opaque cap and white opaque body filled with white to off-white colored pellets.
  The capsules are axially printed with M150 in black ink on the cap and body.
- •The 40 mg capsules are hard gelatin capsules with a white opaque cap and white opaque body filled with white to off-white colored pellets.
  The capsules are axially printed with M151 in black ink on the cap and body.
