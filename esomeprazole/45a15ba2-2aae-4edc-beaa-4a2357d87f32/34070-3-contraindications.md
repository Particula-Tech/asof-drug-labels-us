# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Esomeprazole sodium for injection is contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity to substituted benzimidazoles or to any component of the formulation.
Hypersensitivity reactions may include anaphylaxis, anaphylactic shock, angioedema, bronchospasm, acute interstitial nephritis, and urticaria [see Adverse Reactions (6)].
