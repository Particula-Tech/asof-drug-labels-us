# INDICATIONS & USAGE

## Hypertension

ATACAND is indicated for the treatment of hypertension.
It may be used alone or in combination with other antihypertensive agents.

## Heart Failure

ATACAND is indicated for the treatment of heart failure (NYHA class II-IV) in patients with left ventricular systolic dysfunction (ejection fraction ≤ 40%) to reduce cardiovascular death and to reduce heart failure hospitalizations.
(See Clinical Trials.)
ATACAND also has an added effect on these outcomes when used with an ACE inhibitor.
