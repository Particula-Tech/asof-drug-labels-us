# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Candesartan cilexetil tablets USP, 32 mg are light pink, round, biconvex, uncoated mottled tablets debossed with ‘L171’on one side and scoring on other side.
They are supplied as follows:

NDC 62332-060-30 bottle of 30 tablets NDC 62332-060-90 bottle of 90 tablets NDC 62332-060-10 carton of 100 (10 x 10) unit dose tablets

Storage Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Keep container tightly closed.
