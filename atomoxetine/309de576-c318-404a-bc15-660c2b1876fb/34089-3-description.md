# 11 DESCRIPTION

Atomoxetine is a selective norepinephrine reuptake inhibitor.
Atomoxetine hydrochloride is the R(-) isomer as determined by x-ray diffraction and its chemical designation is (-)-N-Methyl-3-phenyl-3-(o-tolyloxy)-propylamine hydrochloride and its molecular formula is C17H21NO•HCl, which corresponds to a molecular weight of 291.82.
The chemical structure is:

Atomoxetine hydrochloride is a white to practically white solid, which has a solubility of 27.8 mg/mL in water.

STRATTERA (atomoxetine) capsules are for oral administration only.

Each STRATTERA capsule contains 10 mg, 18 mg, 25 mg, 40 mg, 60 mg, 80 mg, or 100 mg of atomoxetine (equivalent to 11.4 mg, 20.6 mg, 28.6 mg, 45.7 mg, 68.6 mg, 91.4 mg and 114.3 mg of atomoxetine hydrochloride, respectively).
The capsules also contain pregelatinized starch and dimethicone.
The capsule shells contain gelatin, sodium lauryl sulfate, and one or more of the following inactive ingredients:

FD&C Blue No. 2, synthetic yellow iron oxide, titanium dioxide, red iron oxide.
The capsules are imprinted with edible black ink.
