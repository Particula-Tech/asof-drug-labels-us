# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Each capsule contains atomoxetine hydrochloride equivalent to 18 mg (golden-colored cap imprinted with "ZA 68" and opaque white colored body imprinted with "18 mg"); 25 mg (opaque blue colored cap imprinted with "ZA 69" and opaque white colored body imprinted with "25 mg"); 40 mg (opaque blue colored cap imprinted with "ZA 70" and opque blue colored body imprinted with "40 mg"); 60 mg (opaque blue colored cap imprinted with "ZA 71" and golden-colored body imprinted with "60 mg"); 80 mg (opaque brown colored cap imprinted with "ZA 72" and white-colored body imprinted with "80 mg"); 100 mg (opaque brown colored cap imprinted with "ZA 73" and opaque brown colored body imprinted with "100 mg") of atomoxetine.
