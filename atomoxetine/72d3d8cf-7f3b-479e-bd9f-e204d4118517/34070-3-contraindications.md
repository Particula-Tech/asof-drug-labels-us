# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

ATONCY is contraindicated in patients:

- With known hypersensitivity reaction to ATONCY or other components of ATONCY.
  Hypersensitivity reactions that occurred with ATONCY use included anaphylaxis, angioneurotic edema, urticaria, and rash [see Warnings and Precautions (5.8)].
- Taking, or within 14 days of stopping, a monoamine oxidase inhibitor (MAOI) [see Drug Interactions (7)].
- With narrow angle glaucoma.
  In clinical trials, ATONCY use was associated with an increased risk of mydriasis.
- With pheochromocytoma or a history of pheochromocytoma.
  Serious reactions, including elevated blood pressure and tachyarrhythmia, have been reported in patients with pheochromocytoma or a history of pheochromocytoma who received ATONCY.
- With severe cardiac or vascular disorders whose condition would be expected to deteriorate if they had clinically important increase in blood pressure or heart rate (e.g., 15 to 20 mm Hg in blood pressure or 20 beats per minute in heart rate) [see Warnings and Precautions (5.4)].
