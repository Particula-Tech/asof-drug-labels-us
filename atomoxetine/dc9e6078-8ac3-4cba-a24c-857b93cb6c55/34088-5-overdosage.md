# OVERDOSAGE

## Human Experience

There is limited clinical trial experience with STRATTERA overdose and no fatalities were observed.
During postmarketing, there have been reports of acute and chronic overdoses of STRATTERA.
No fatal overdoses of STRATTERA alone have been reported.
The most commonly reported symptoms accompanying acute and chronic overdoses were somnolence, agitation, hyperactivity, abnormal behavior, and gastrointestinal symptoms.
Signs and symptoms consistent with sympathetic nervous system activation (e.g., mydriasis, tachycardia, dry mouth) have also been observed.

## Management of Overdose

An airway should be established.
Monitoring of cardiac and vital signs is recommended, along with appropriate symptomatic and supportive measures.
Gastric lavage may be indicated if performed soon after ingestion.
Activated charcoal may be useful in limiting absorption.
Because atomoxetine is highly protein-bound, dialysis is not likely to be useful in the treatment of overdose.
