# DESCRIPTION

DIPRIVAN® (propofol) Injectable Emulsion, USP is a sterile, nonpyrogenic emulsion containing 10 mg/mL of propofol suitable for intravenous administration.
Propofol is chemically described as 2,6‑diisopropylphenol.
The structural formula is:

C12H18O M.W.
178.27

Propofol is slightly soluble in water and, thus, is formulated in a white, oil-in-water emulsion.
The pKa is 11.
The octanol/water partition coefficient for propofol is 6761:1 at a pH of 6 to 8.5.
In addition to the active component, propofol, the formulation also contains soybean oil (100 mg/mL), glycerol (22.5 mg/mL), egg lecithin (12 mg/mL); and disodium edetate (0.005%); with sodium hydroxide to adjust pH.
The DIPRIVAN Injectable Emulsion, USP is isotonic and has a pH of 7 to 8.5.
