# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Propofol Injectable Emulsion, USP is available in single-patient use vials as follows:

200 mg of propofol per 20 mL of an oil-in-water emulsion (10 mg per mL), in a 20 mL vial

500 mg of propofol per 50 mL of an oil-in-water emulsion (10 mg per mL), in a 50 mL vial

1,000 mg of propofol per 100 mL of an oil-in-water emulsion (10 mg per mL), in a 100 mL vial
