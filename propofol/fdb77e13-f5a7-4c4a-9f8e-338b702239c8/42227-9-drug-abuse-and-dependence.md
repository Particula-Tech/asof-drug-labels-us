# DRUG ABUSE AND DEPENDENCE

There are reports of the abuse of propofol for recreational and other improper purposes, which have resulted in fatalities and other injuries.
Instances of self-administration of propofol by healthcare professionals have also been reported, which have resulted in fatalities and other injuries.
Inventories of propofol should be stored and managed to prevent the risk of diversion, including restriction of access and accounting procedures as appropriate to the clinical setting.
