# HOW SUPPLIED

Propofol Injectable Emulsion, USP Vials containing 10 mg/mL of propofol, is available as follows:

20 mL single dose vial in cartons of 10.
NDC 0641-6194-10.

50 mL single-patient infusion vial in cartons of 20.
NDC 0641-6195-20.

100 mL single-patient infusion vial in cartons of 10.
NDC 0641-6196-10.

Propofol undergoes oxidative degradation, in the presence of oxygen, and is therefore packaged under nitrogen to eliminate this degradation path.

Store between 4° to 25°C (40° to 77°F).
DO NOT FREEZE.
Shake well before use.
