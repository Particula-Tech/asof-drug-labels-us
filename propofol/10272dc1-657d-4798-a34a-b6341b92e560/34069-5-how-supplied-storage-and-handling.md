# HOW SUPPLIED

Propofol injectable emulsion, USP is a white, oil-in-water homogeneous emulsion free of large oil drops.
It is available as follows:

| Strength | NDC No. |
| --- | --- |
| 1% 200 mg per 20 mL (10 mg/mL propofol) | 20 mL ready-to-use single-patient infusion vial in packages of 10 (23155-345-41) and in packages of 20 (23155-345-44) |
| 1% 500 mg per 50 mL (10 mg/mL propofol) | 50 mL ready-to-use single-patient infusion vial in packages of 20 (23155-345-42) |
| 1% 1,000 mg per 100 mL (10 mg/mL propofol) | 100 mL ready-to-use single-patient infusion vial in packages of 10 (23155-345-43) |

Propofol undergoes oxidative degradation, in the presence of oxygen, and is therefore packaged under nitrogen to eliminate this degradation path.

Store between 4° to 25°C (40° to 77°F).
Do not freeze.
Shake well before use.
