# DESCRIPTION:

Propofol injectable emulsion, USP is a sterile, nonpyrogenic emulsion containing 10 mg/mL of propofol, USP suitable for intravenous administration.
Propofol, USP is chemically described as 2,6–diisopropylphenol.
The structural formula is:

C12H18O M.W.
178.27

Propofol, USP is slightly soluble in water and, thus, is formulated in a white, oil-in-water emulsion.
The pKa is 11.
The octanol/water partition coefficient for propofol, USP is 6761:1 at a pH of 6 to 8.5.
In addition to the active component, propofol, USP, the formulation also contains soybean oil, USP (100 mg/mL), glycerol, USP (22.5 mg/mL), egg lecithin, NF (12 mg/mL); and disodium edetate anhydrous, USP (0.05 mg/mL); with sodium hydroxide, NF to adjust pH.
Propofol injectable emulsion, USP is isotonic and has a pH of 6.0 to 8.5.
