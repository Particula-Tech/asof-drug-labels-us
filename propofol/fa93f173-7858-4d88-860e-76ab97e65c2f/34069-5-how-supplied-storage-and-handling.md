| 25021-608-51 | 1,000 mg per 100 mL Single-Patient Infusion Vial | 10 vials per carton |

Propofol injectable emulsion, USP is available in ready-to-use 20 mL vials, 50 mL infusion vials and 100 mL infusion vials containing 10 mg/mL of propofol, USP.

Propofol, USP undergoes oxidative degradation, in the presence of oxygen, and is therefore packaged under nitrogen to eliminate this degradation path.

## Storage Conditions

Store between 4° to 25°C (40° to 77°F).
Do not freeze.
Shake well before use.

Discard unused portion.

Sterile, Nonpyrogenic.
