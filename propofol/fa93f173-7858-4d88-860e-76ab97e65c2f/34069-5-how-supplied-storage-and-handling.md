# HOW SUPPLIED

Propofol Injectable Emulsion, USP is available as follows:

| NDC | Propofol Injectable Emulsion, USP (10 mg per mL) | Package Factor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 25021-608-20 | 200 mg per 20 mL Single-Patient Infusion Vial | 25 vials per carton |
| 25021-608-50 | 500 mg per 50 mL Single-Patient Infusion Vial | 20 vials per carton |
| 25021-608-51 | 1,000 mg per 100 mL Single-Patient Infusion Vial | 10 vials per carton |

Propofol Injectable Emulsion, USP is available in ready-to-use 20 mL infusion vials, 50 mL infusion vials and 100 mL infusion vials containing 1% (10 mg per mL) of propofol, USP.

Propofol, USP undergoes oxidative degradation, in the presence of oxygen, and is therefore packaged under nitrogen to eliminate this degradation path.

## Storage Conditions

Store between 4° to 25°C (40° to 77°F).
Do not freeze.
Shake well before use.

Discard unused portion.

Sterile, Nonpyrogenic.
The container closure is not made with natural rubber latex.
