# DRUG ABUSE AND DEPENDENCE

There are reports of the abuse of propofol for recreational and other improper purposes, which have resulted in fatalities and other injuries.
Instances of self-administration of DIPRIVAN Injectable Emulsion by health care professionals have also been reported, which have resulted in fatalities and other injuries.
Inventories of DIPRIVAN Injectable Emulsion should be stored and managed to prevent the risk of diversion, including restriction of access and accounting procedures as appropriate to the clinical setting.
