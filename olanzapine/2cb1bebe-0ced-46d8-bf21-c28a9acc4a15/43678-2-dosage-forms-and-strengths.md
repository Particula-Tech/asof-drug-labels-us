# 3. DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

The 10 mg tablets are yellow colored, round, biconvex, uncoated tablets, debossed with "10" on one side and "169" on other side.
The 15 mg tablets are yellow colored, oval shaped, biconvex, uncoated tablets, debossed with "15" on one side and "1170" on other side.
The 20 mg tablets are yellow colored, round, biconvex, uncoated tablets, debossed with "20" on one side and "1171" on other side.
