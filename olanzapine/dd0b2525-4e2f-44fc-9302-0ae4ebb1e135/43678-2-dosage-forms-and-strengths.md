# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Olanzapine orally disintegrating tablets are available as:

| 5 mg: | Yellow, round, flat-faced, beveled-edge, unscored tablets, debossed with “TV” over “U1” on one side and “5” on the other side. |
| --- | --- |
| 10 mg: | Yellow, round, flat-faced, beveled-edge, unscored tablets, debossed with “TV” over “U2” on one side and “10” on the other side. |
| 15 mg: | Yellow, round, flat-faced, beveled-edge, unscored tablets, debossed with “TV” over “U7” on one side and “15” on the other side. |
| 20 mg: | Yellow, round, flat-faced, beveled-edge, unscored tablets, debossed with “TV” over “U4” on one side and “20” on the other side. |
