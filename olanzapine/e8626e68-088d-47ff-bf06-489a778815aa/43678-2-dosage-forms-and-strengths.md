# 3 DOSAGE FORMS & STRENGTHS

Olanzapine tablets USP 2.5 mg are white to off white colored, film-coated, round shaped, biconvex tablets, imprinted “2.5” with black ink on one side and plain on the other side.

Olanzapine tablets USP 5 mg are white to off white colored film-coated, round shaped, biconvex tablets, imprinted “5” with black ink on one side and plain on the other side.

Olanzapine tablets USP 7.5 mg are white to off white colored, film-coated, round shaped, biconvex tablets, imprinted “7.5” with black ink on one side and plain on the other side.

Olanzapine tablets USP 10 mg are white to off white colored, film-coated, round shaped, biconvex tablets, imprinted “10” with black ink on one side and plain on the other side.

Olanzapine tablets USP 15 mg are blue colored, film-coated, elliptical shaped, biconvex tablets, imprinted “15” with black ink on one side and plain on the other side.

Olanzapine tablets USP 20 mg are pink colored, film-coated, elliptical shaped, biconvex tablets, imprinted “20” with black ink on one side and plain on the other side.

Tablets are not scored.
