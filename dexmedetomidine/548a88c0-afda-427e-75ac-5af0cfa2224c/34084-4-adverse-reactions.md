# ADVERSE REACTIONS

Adverse event information is derived from the placebo-controlled, continuous infusion trials of dexmedetomidine for sedation in the ICU setting in which 387 patients received Precedex®.
Overall, the most frequently observed treatment-emergent adverse events included hypotension, hypertension, nausea, bradycardia, fever, vomiting, hypoxia, tachycardia and anemia (see Table 5).
