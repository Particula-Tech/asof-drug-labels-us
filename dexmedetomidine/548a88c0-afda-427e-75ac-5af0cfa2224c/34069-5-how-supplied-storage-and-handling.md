# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Precedex (dexmedetomidine hydrochloride) injection, 200 mcg/2 mL (100 mcg/mL) is available in 2 mL clear glass vial.
Vials are intended for single use only.

| NDC No. | Container | Size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0409-1638-02 | Vial | 2 mL |

Store at controlled room temperature, 25°C (77°F) with excursions allowed from 15 to 30°C (59 to 86°F).
