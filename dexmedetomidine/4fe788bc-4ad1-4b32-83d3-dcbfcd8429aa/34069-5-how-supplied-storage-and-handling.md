# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Dexmedetomidine hydrochloride injection, 200 mcg (dexmedetomidine)/2 mL (100 mcg (dexmedetomidine) /mL) is available as clear and colorless solution in 2 mL clear glass vial.
Vials are intended for single dose only.
Discard unused portion.
