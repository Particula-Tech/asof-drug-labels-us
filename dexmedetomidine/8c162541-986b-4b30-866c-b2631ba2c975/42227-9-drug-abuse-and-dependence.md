# 9 DRUG ABUSE AND DEPENDENCE

## 9.1 Controlled Substance

Dexmedetomidine hydrochloride is not a controlled substance.

## 9.3 Dependence

The dependence potential of dexmedetomidine has not been studied in humans.
However, since studies in rodents and primates have demonstrated that dexmedetomidine exhibits pharmacologic actions similar to those of clonidine, it is possible that dexmedetomidine may produce a clonidine-like withdrawal syndrome upon abrupt discontinuation [see Warnings and Precautions (5.5)].
