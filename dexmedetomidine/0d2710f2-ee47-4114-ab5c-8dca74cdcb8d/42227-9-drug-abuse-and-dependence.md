# 9 DRUG ABUSE AND DEPENDENCE

## 9.3 Dependence

The dependence potential of dexmedetomidine HCl has not been studied in humans.
However, since studies in rodents and primates have demonstrated that dexmedetomidine HCl exhibits pharmacologic actions similar to those of clonidine, it is possible that dexmedetomidine HCl may produce a clonidine-like withdrawal syndrome upon abrupt discontinuation [see Warnings and Precautions (5.5)].
