# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

For the unsupervised setting, IGALMI is a blue rectangular sublingual film containing on its surface two darker blue spots and D12 imprinted in black in a dose strength of 120 mcg dexmedetomidine.

For the healthcare supervised setting, IGALMI is a blue rectangular sublingual film containing on its surface two darker blue spots in dose strengths of 120 mcg and 180 mcg dexmedetomidine.
