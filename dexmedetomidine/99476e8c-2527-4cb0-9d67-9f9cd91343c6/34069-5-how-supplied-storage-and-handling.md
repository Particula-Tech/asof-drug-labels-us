# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

How Supplied

IGALMI (dexmedetomidine) sublingual film is supplied as a blue rectangular sublingual film, containing on its surface two darker blue spots in dose strengths of 120 mcg and 180 mcg and is packaged as individual films in heat-sealed foil pouches in 10-count and 30-count films per carton.
The NDC number for each packaging configuration is:

- 120 mcg 10-count NDC # 81092-1120-1
- 120 mcg 30-count NDC # 81092-1120-3
- 180 mcg 10-count NDC # 81092-1180-1
- 180 mcg 30-count NDC # 81092-1180-3

Storage and Handling

Store at controlled room temperature, 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
Excursions permitted from 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F).
See USP Controlled Room Temperature.

Keep IGALMI in the foil pouch until ready to administer.
