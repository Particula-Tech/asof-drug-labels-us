| What is the most important information I should know about IGALMI? IGALMI can cause serious side effects, including: Low blood pressure (hypotension), slow heart rate (bradycardia), and fainting (syncope). Your healthcare provider will check your blood pressure and heart rate before and after you take IGALMI. Tell your healthcare provider right away if you feel dizzy, lightheaded, or faint.Drowsiness or sleepiness can occur. Do not drive, operate machinery, or do anything that requires alertness for at least 8 hours after taking IGALMI.QT prolongation (heart rhythm changes). IGALMI may cause changes in your heart rhythm. Tell your healthcare provider if you have heart problems or take medicines that affect your heart rhythm. |  |  |
| What is IGALMI? IGALMI is a prescription medicine used for the acute treatment of agitation in adults with: Schizophrenia, Schizoaffective Disorder orBipolar I or II disorder. IGALMI is given as a sublingual film (placed under your tongue) or buccal film (placed behind your lower lip). |  |  |
| Who should not take IGALMI? Do not take IGALMI if you: Have been told by your healthcare provider not to use sublingual or buccal medicinesHave certain heart rhythm problems (including prolonged QT interval) or very low blood pressure |  |  |
| Before taking IGALMI, tell your healthcare provider if you: Have heart problems, slow heart rate, or low blood pressureHave had fainting spellsHave liver problemsHave low potassium or magnesium levelsAre 65 years of age or olderAre pregnant or plan to become pregnantAre breastfeeding or plan to breastfeedTake any other medicines — especially medicines that can: Lower blood pressure or heart rateProlong the QT interval (affect heart rhythm)Cause drowsiness or sedation (such as opioids, sleeping pills, or alcohol) |  |  |
| How should I take IGALMI? Take IGALMI exactly how your healthcare provider directs you to For acute agitation when they occur IGALMI can be taken 1 time as needed. You should not take more than 1 IGALMI in 12 hours.To take IGALMI Use clean dry hands when opening the pouch Cut the pouch open with scissors on the dotted line Place the film under your tongue or behind your lower lip. While the film is dissolving: Do not chew or swallow it.Do not eat or drink for: 15 minutes after sublingual (under-the-tongue) use, or1 hour after buccal (behind-the-lip) use. After taking IGALMI: Stay sitting or lying down if you feel lightheaded or dizzyYou should not drive or operate machinery for at least 8 hours after your dose. Take IGALMI immediately after opening the pouch. Do not store IGALMI outside the pouch. |  |  |
| What are the possible side effects of IGALMI? IGALMI may cause serious side effects, including: Low blood pressure (hypotension)Slow heart rate (bradycardia)Heart rhythm changes (QT prolongation)Drowsiness or sleepinessFainting (syncope) The most common side effects of IGALMI include: Sleepiness or drowsinessNumbness or tingling in the mouth (oral paresthesia)Dry mouthDizzinessLow blood pressure (hypotension) These are not all the possible side effects of IGALMI. Call your doctor for medical advice about side effects. You may report side effects to the FDA at 1-800-FDA-1088 or www.fda.gov/medwatch. |  |  |
| How Should I Store IGALMI? Store IGALMI films at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).Keep films in their sealed foil pouches until ready to use.Keep out of reach of children.Dispose of unused films properly — do not flush them. |  |  |
| General information about the safe and effective use of IGALMI: Medicines are sometimes prescribed for purposes other than those listed in a Patient Information leaflet. Do not use IGALMI for a condition for which it was not prescribed. Do not give IGALMI to other people, even if they have the same symptoms that you have. It may harm them. You can ask your pharmacist or healthcare provider for information about IGALMI that is written for health professionals. |  |  |
