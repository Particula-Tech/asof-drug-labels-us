# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Dexmedetomidine Injection USP, 200 mcg/2 mL (100 mcg/mL) is clear and colorless and is available in:

NDC 67457-251-02 2 mL in a 3 mL single-dose vial, cartons of 25

The strength is based on the dexmedetomidine base.
Vials are intended for single-dose only.

Discard unused portion.

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
