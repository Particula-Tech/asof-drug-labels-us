# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

EMBLAVEO (aztreonam and avibactam) for injection is supplied as a white to slightly yellow sterile lyophilized powder for reconstitution in a single-dose, sterile, clear glass vial containing:

2 g aztreonam and avibactam (1.5 grams aztreonam and 0.5 grams avibactam (equivalent to 0.542 grams of avibactam sodium)).
