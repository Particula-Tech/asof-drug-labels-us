# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Acetaminophen injection is a sterile, clear, colorless, non-pyrogenic, preservative free, isotonic formulation of acetaminophen intended for intravenous infusion.
Each 100 mL bag contains 1000 mg acetaminophen (10 mg/mL) and 65 mL bag contains 650 mg acetaminophen (10 mg/mL).
