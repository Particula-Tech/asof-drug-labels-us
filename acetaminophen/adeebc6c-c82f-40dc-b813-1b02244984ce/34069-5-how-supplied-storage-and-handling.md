# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Acetaminophen injection is supplied in a 100 mL glass vial containing 1000 mg acetaminophen (10 mg/mL) in cartons of 24 vials.
NDC 24201-110-24 Acetaminophen injection should be stored at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
For single-dose only.
The product should be used within 6 hours after opening.
Do not refrigerate or freeze.
