# 11 DESCRIPTION

Acetaminophen is a non-salicylate antipyretic and non-opioid analgesic agent.
Its chemical name is N-acetyl-p-aminophenol.
Its structural formula is:

|  | M.W. 151.16 |
| --- | --- |

Acetaminophen Injection, for intravenous use, is a sterile, clear, colorless, non-pyrogenic, isotonic formulation of acetaminophen intended for intravenous infusion.
Each 100 mL contains 1,000 mg of Acetaminophen, USP; 3,670 mg of mannitol, 10 mg of cysteine, and hydrochloric acid and sodium hydroxide as pH adjusters, in water for injection.
Acetaminophen Injection has a pH between 5.0 and 6.3, and an osmolality of approximately 290 mOsm/kg.
