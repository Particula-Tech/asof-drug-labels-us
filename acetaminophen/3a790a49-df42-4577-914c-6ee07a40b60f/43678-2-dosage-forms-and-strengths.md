# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Immediate-release tablet.

- Capsule-shaped white tablet debossed with “KP201” on one side and “445” on the opposite side contains 4.08 mg benzhydrocodone (equivalent to 4.45 mg benzhydrocodone hydrochloride) and 325 mg acetaminophen.
- Capsule-shaped white tablet debossed with “KP201” on one side and blank on the opposite side contains 6.12 mg benzhydrocodone (equivalent to 6.67 mg benzhydrocodone hydrochloride) and 325 mg acetaminophen.
- Capsule-shaped white tablet debossed with “KP201” on one side and “890” on the opposite side contains 8.16 mg benzhydrocodone (equivalent to 8.90 mg benzhydrocodone hydrochloride) and 325 mg acetaminophen.
