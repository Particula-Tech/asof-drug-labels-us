# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

APADAZ is contraindicated in patients with:

- Significant respiratory depression [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3)]
- Acute or severe bronchial asthma in an unmonitored setting or in the absence of resuscitative equipment [see Warnings and Precautions (5.8)]
- Known or suspected gastrointestinal obstruction, including paralytic ileus [see Warnings and Precautions (5.14)]
- Hypersensitivity to hydrocodone or acetaminophen, or any other component of this product (e.g., anaphylaxis) [see Warnings and Precautions (5.13), Adverse Reactions (6)]
