# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Atorvastatin calcium tablets are supplied as white to off-white, oval, biconvex film-coated tablets of atorvastatin calcium containing 10, 20, 40 and 80 mg atorvastatin.

10 mg tablets

Atorvastatin calcium tablets, 10 mg, are available for oral administration as white to off-white, oval, biconvex film-coated tablets, engraved “APO” on one side, “A10” on the other side.
