# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Atorvastatin calcium tablets of 10 mg are white to off-white, capsule shaped, biconvex, film coated tablets debossed ‘RDY’ on one side and ‘121’ on other side and are supplied in bottles of 30’s, 60’s, 90’s and 500’s.

Bottles of 30 NDC 55111-121-30

Bottles of 60 NDC 55111-121-60

Bottles of 90 NDC 55111-121-90

Bottles of 500 NDC 55111-121-05
