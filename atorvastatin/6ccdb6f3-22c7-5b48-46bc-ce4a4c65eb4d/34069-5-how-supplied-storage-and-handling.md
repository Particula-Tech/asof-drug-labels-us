# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Atorvastatin calcium tablets of 80 mg are white to off-white, oval shaped, biconvex, film coated tablets debossed ‘RDY’ on one side and ‘124’ on other side and are supplied in bottles of 30’s, 60’s, 90’s and 500’s.

Bottles of 30 NDC 55111-124-30

Bottles of 60 NDC 55111-124-60

Bottles of 90 NDC 55111-124-90

Bottles of 500 NDC 55111-124-05

Storage

Store atorvastatin calcium tablets at 20° - 25°C (68° - 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
