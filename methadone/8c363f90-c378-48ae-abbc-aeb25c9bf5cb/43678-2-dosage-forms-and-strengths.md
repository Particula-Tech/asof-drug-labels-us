# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Each 5 mL of clear or nearly clear orange colored Methadone Hydrochloride Oral Solution USP contains methadone hydrochloride USP 5 mg or 10 mg.
The concentration of the 5 mg per 5 mL solution is 1 mg/mL and the concentration of the 10 mg per 5 mL solution is 2 mg/mL.
