# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

1. Methadone Hydrochloride Oral Solution is contraindicated in patients with:
2. •Significant respiratory depression.
3. •Acute or severe bronchial asthma in an unmonitored setting or in the absence of resuscitative equipment.
4. •Known or suspected paralytic ileus.
5. •Hypersensitivity (e.g., anaphylaxis) to methadone [see Adverse Reactions (6)].
