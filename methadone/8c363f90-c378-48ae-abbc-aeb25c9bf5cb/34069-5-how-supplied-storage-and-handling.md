# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 Storage and Handling

1. Methadone Hydrochloride Oral Solution USP contains methadone which is a controlled substance.
   Like fentanyl, morphine, oxycodone, hydromorphone, and oxymorphone, methadone is controlled under Schedule II of the Federal Controlled Substances Act.
   Methadone may be targeted for theft and diversion by criminals [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
2. Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP.
3. Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15º to 30ºC (59º to 86ºF) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
