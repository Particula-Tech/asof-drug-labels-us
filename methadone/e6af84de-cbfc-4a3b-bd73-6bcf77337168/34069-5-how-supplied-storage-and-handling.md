# HOW SUPPLIED

DISKETS® Dispersible Tablets

(Methadone Hydrochloride Tablets for Oral Suspension, USP), 40 mg

40 mg peach cross-scored tablets (Identified 54 883).

NDC 0054-4538-25: Bottles of 100 dispersible tablets.

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15°-30°C (59°-86°F) [see

USP Controlled Room Temperature].

DISKETS, if dispensed, must be packaged in child-resistant containers

and kept out of reach of children to prevent accidental ingestion.
