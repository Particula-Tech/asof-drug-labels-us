# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 Storage and Handling

DISKETS Dispersible Tablets contain methadone which is a controlled substance.
Like fentanyl, morphine, oxycodone, hydromorphone, and oxymorphone, methadone is controlled under Schedule II of the Federal Controlled Substances Act.
DISKETS Dispersible Tablets may be targeted for theft and diversion by criminals [see Warnings and Precautions (5.4)].

DISKETS Dispersible Tablets, if dispensed, must be packaged in child-resistant containers and kept out of reach of children to prevent accidental ingestion.

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]
