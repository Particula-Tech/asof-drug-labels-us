# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Each 40 mg dispersible tablet for oral administration following dispersion in a liquid contains: 40 mg of methadone hydrochloride and is a pillow-shaped, light-pinkish orange compressed dispersible tablet.
DISKETS are cross-scored, allowing for flexible dosage adjustment.
Each tablet may be broken or cut in half to yield two 20-mg doses, or in quarters to yield four 10-mg doses.
