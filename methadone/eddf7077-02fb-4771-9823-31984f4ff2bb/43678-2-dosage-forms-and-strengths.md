# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Methadone hydrochloride tablets are available in 5 mg and 10 mg dosage strengths.
The 5 mg tablets are white to off-white, modified rectangle shaped convex tablets and are debossed with a score between “57” and “55” on one side and on the other side.
The 10 mg tablets are white to off-white, modified rectangle shaped convex tablets and are debossed with a score between “57” and “71” on one side and on the other side.
