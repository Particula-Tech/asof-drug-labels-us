# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 Storage and Handling

Methadone hydrochloride tablets contain methadone which is a controlled substance.
Like fentanyl, morphine, oxycodone, hydromorphone, and oxymorphone, methadone is controlled under Schedule II of the Federal Controlled Substances Act.
Methadone hydrochloride tablets may be targeted for theft and diversion by criminals [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container (as defined in USP) with a child-resistant closure.

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
