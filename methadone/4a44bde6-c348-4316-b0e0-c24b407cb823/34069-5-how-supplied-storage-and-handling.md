# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Methadone Hydrochloride Tablets, USP

10 mg tablets are white, round, biconvex tablets, scored on one side and debossed with "N" above the score and "128" below the score and plain on the other side.

NDC 66689-836-99: Bottles of 100 Tablets

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F), with excursions permitted between 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

Store Methadone Hydrochloride Tablets securely and dispose of properly [see PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION (17)]
