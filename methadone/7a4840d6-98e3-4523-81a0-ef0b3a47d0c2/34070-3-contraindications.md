# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

DOLOPHINE Tablets are contraindicated in patients with:

1. • Significant respiratory depression [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)]
2. • Acute or severe bronchial asthma in an unmonitored setting or in the absence of resuscitative equipment [see Warnings and Precautions (5.7)]
3. • Known or suspected gastrointestinal obstruction, including paralytic ileus [see Warnings and Precautions (5.12)]
4. • Hypersensitivity (e.g., anaphylaxis) to methadone [see Adverse Reactions (6)].
