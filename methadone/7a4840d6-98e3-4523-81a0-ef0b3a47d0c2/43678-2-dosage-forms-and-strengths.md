# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

DOLOPHINE Tablets are available in 5 mg and 10 mg dosage strengths.
The 5 mg tablets are round, white and are debossed with tablet identifier “54 162” on one side and scored on the other side.
The 10 mg tablets are round, white and are debossed with tablet identifier “54 549” on one side and scored on the other side.
