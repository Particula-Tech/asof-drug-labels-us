# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

DOLOPHINE tablets are available in 5 mg and 10 mg dosage strengths.
The 5 mg tablets are white, round, biconvex tablets, scored on one side and product identification “54 162” debossed on the other side.
The 10 mg tablets are white, round, biconvex tablets, scored on one side and product identification “54 549” debossed on the other side.
