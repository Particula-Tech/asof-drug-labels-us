# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Oral Concentrate:

- •Each mL of clear, colorless Methadone Hydrochloride Oral Concentrate, USP (Intensol™) contains 10 mg methadone hydrochloride, USP (equivalent to 8.95 mg of methadone).
  The concentration of Methadone Hydrochloride Oral Concentrate, USP (Intensol™) is 10 mg/mL.
