# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

METHADOSE™ Dispersible Tablet for oral administration following dispersion in a liquid: 40 mg of methadone hydrochloride and is a white round dispersible tablet.

Methadone Hydrochloride Tablets (dispersible, orange flavored) for oral administration following dispersion in a liquid: 40 mg of methadone hydrochloride and is an orange rectangular dispersible tablet.

Methadone hydrochloride tablets for oral suspension are cross-scored, allowing for flexible dosage adjustment.
Each tablet may be broken or cut in half to yield two 20-mg doses, or in quarters to yield four 10-mg doses.
