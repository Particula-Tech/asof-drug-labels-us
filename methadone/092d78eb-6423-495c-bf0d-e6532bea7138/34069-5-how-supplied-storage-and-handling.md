# HOW SUPPLIED

Methadone Hydrochloride Injection, USP, 10mg/mL is available in: NDC 67457-217-20 20mL Multi-Dose Vials: One Vial per package.

Protect from light.
Store in carton until contents have been used.

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions are permitted to 15°-30°C (59°-86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature]
