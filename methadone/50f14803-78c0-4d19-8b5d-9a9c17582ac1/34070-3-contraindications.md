# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Methadone Hydrochloride Tablets are contraindicated in patients with:

- •Significant respiratory depression [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)].
- •Acute or severe bronchial asthma in an unmonitored setting or in the absence of resuscitative equipment [see Warnings and Precautions (5.10)].
- •Known or suspected gastrointestinal obstruction, including paralytic ileus [see Warnings and Precautions (5.14)].
- •Hypersensitivity (e.g., anaphylaxis) to methadone [see Adverse Reactions (6)].
