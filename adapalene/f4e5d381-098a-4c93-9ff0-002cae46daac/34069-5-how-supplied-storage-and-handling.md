# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

EPIDUO FORTE (adapalene and benzoyl peroxide) gel 0.3%/2.5% is white to very pale yellow in color and opaque in appearance, and is supplied as follows:

15 gram pump NDC 0299-5906-15 30 gram pump NDC 0299-5906-30 45 gram pump NDC 0299-5906-45 60 gram pump NDC 0299-5906-60 70 gram pump NDC 0299-5906-70

Storage and handling • Store at controlled room temperature 20–25°C (68–77°F) with excursions permitted to 15°–30°C (59°–86°F) [see USP controlled room temperature]. • Protect from light. • Keep out of reach of children. • Keep away from heat.
