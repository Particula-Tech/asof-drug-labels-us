# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

How Supplied

CABTREO (clindamycin phosphate, adapalene, and benzoyl peroxide) topical gel is a white to off-white, opaque topical gel.
CABTREO contains 1.2% clindamycin phosphate, 0.15% adapalene, and 3.1% benzoyl peroxide and is supplied as follows:

- 20 g pump (NDC 0187-0006-10)
- 50 g pump (NDC 0187-0006-25)

Storage and Handling

- Prior to Dispensing:Store CABTREO in a refrigerator between 2° to 8°C (36° to 46°F) until dispensed to the patient.
  Dispense CABTREO with a 10-week expiration date.
- After Dispensing:Store CABTREO at room temperature at or below 25°C (77°F).
- Do not freeze.
- Keep away from heat.
- Store pump upright.
