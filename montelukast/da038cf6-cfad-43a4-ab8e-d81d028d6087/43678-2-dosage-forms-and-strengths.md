# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

- Montelukast sodium tablets USP, 10 mg are light brown colored, round, biconvex film coated tablets debossed with "1081" on one side and "10 MG" on other side.
- Montelukast sodium chewable tablets USP, 4mg are pink colored, oval biconvex shaped, uncoated tablets, debossed with '1079' on one side and '4 MG' on other side.
- Montelukast sodium chewable tablets USP, 5mg are pink colored, round shaped, uncoated tablets, debossed with '1080' on one side and '5 MG' on other side.
- Montelukast sodium oral granules USP, 4 mg are white to off white granules with 500 mg net weight, packed in a child-resistant foil packet.
