# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

-

Halobetasol Propionate Lotion 0.05 % is white to off-white lotion.
It is supplied in an oval tapered white high-density polyethylene bottle with a white polypropylene disc cap.
Each bottle contains 60 ml (59 g) of Halobetasol Propionate Lotion 0.05%.

NDC 85437-008-60 ml (59 g) bottle

Store at 25'C (77'F); excursions permitted to 15°G and 30°G (59° F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Do not freeze.
