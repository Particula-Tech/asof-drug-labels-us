# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

ULTRAVATE lotion, 0.05 % is white to off-white lotion.
It is supplied in an oval tapered white high-density polyethylene bottle with a white polypropylene disc cap.
Each bottle contains 60 mL (59 g) of ULTRAVATE lotion.

NDC 10631-122-04 60 mL (59 g) bottle

1. NDC 10631-122-51 120 mL (2-60 mL/59 g bottles)

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15ºC and 30ºC (59ºF to 86ºF) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Do not freeze.
