# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Latanoprost ophthalmic solution, 0.005% (50 mcg/mL) is a clear, isotonic, buffered, preserved colorless solution.
It is supplied as a 2.5 mL solution in a 5 mL natural LDPE bottle with LDPE nozzle and turquoise color HDPE cap with a tamper-evident ring.
2.5 mL fill, 0.005% (50 mcg/mL)

Package of 1 bottle NDC 65862-872-25

Storage: Protect from light.
Store unopened bottle(s) under refrigeration at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F).
During shipment to the patient, the bottle may be maintained at temperatures up to 40°C (104°F) for a period not exceeding 8 days.
Once a bottle is opened for use, it may be stored at room temperature up to 25°C (77°F) for 6 weeks.
