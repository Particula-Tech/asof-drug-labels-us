# 8 USE IN SPECIFIC POPULATIONS

## 8.1 Pregnancy

Risk Summary

There are no well controlled studies in pregnant women.
Limited human data from small case series and case reports are insufficient to inform risk of adverse outcome(s) in pregnant women exposed to XALATAN.

In animal reproduction studies administration of latanoprost to pregnant rabbits caused fetotoxicity at a dose that was approximately 80 times the maximum human dose (see Data).

XALATAN should be used during pregnancy only if the potential benefit justifies the potential risk to the fetus.

In the U.S. general population, the estimated background risk of major birth defects and miscarriage in clinically recognized pregnancies is 2 to 4% and 15 to 20%, respectively.This information regarding the background risks of major birth defects and miscarriage in the U.S. general population, regardless of drug exposure is required to be included here per CFR § 201.57(c)(9)(i)(B).

Data

Animal Data

Reproduction studies have been performed in pregnant rats and rabbits.
In rabbits, an incidence of 4 of 16 dams had no viable fetuses at a dose that was approximately 80 times the maximum human dose, and the highest nonembryocidal dose in rabbits was approximately 15 times the maximum human dose.

## 8.2 Lactation

Risk Summary

It is not known whether this drug or its metabolites are excreted in human milk.
Because many drugs are excreted in human milk, caution should be exercised when XALATAN is administered to a nursing woman.

The developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding should be considered along with the mother's clinical need for XALATAN and any potential adverse effects on the breastfed child from XALATAN or from the underlying maternal condition.This statement is being included per CFR § 201.57(c)(9)(ii)(A)(1).

## 8.4 Pediatric Use

Safety and effectiveness in pediatric patients have not been established.

## 8.5 Geriatric Use

No overall differences in safety or effectiveness have been observed between elderly and younger patients.
