# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Paroxetine HCL CR extended‑release tablets, USP are available as:

• 12.5 mg yellow, round tablets, one face is plain and the other face is engraved with “12.5”.

• 25 mg pink, round tablets, one face is plain and the other face is engraved with “25”.

• 37.5 mg blue, round tablets, one face is plain and the other face is engraved with “37.5”.
