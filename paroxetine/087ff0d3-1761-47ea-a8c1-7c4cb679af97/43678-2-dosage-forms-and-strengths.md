# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Paroxetine HCL CR extended‑release tablets are available as:

- 12.5 mg yellow, round tablets, One face is plain and the other is engraved with “12.5”.
- 25 mg pink, round tablets, One face is plain and the other face is engraved with “25”.
- 37.5 mg blue, round tablets, One face is plain and the other face is engraved with “37.5”.
