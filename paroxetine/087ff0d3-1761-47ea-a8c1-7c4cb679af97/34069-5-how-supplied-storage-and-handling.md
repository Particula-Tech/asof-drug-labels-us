# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Paroxetine HCL CR is supplied as an enteric-coated controlled-release, round tablet, as follows:

12.5-mg yellow tablets

NDC 60505-1316-3 Bottles of 30 (One face is plain and the other is engraved with 12.5)

25-mg pink tablets

NDC 60505-1317-3 Bottles of 30 (One face is plain and the other face is engraved with 25)

37.5 mg blue tablets

NDC 60505-1318-3 Bottles of 30 (One face is plain and the other is engraved with 37.5)

Store at or below 25°C (77°F); [see USP].
