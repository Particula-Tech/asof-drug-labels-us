# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

## 2.1 Dosage Information

The recommended dosage of BRISDELLE for the treatment of moderate to severe VMS is 7.5 mg once daily, at bedtime, with or without food.

## 2.2 Use of BRISDELLE Before or After a Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitor (MAOI)

Wait at least 14 days after discontinuation of an MAOI before initiating therapy with BRISDELLE.
Conversely, allow at least 14 days after stopping BRISDELLE before starting an MAOI [see Contraindications (4.1), Warnings and Precautions (5.2) and Drug Interactions (7.3)].
