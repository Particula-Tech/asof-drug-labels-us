# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Paroxetine capsules are available as 7.5 mg pink capsules printed with black edible ink with “BRISDELLE” and “7.5 mg” on each capsule.

NDC 0574-0279-30, 30 count bottle

Store at 20°-25°C (68°-77°F); excursions permitted to 15°-30°C (59°-86°F).
Protect from light and humidity.
