# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

PAXIL CR is supplied as an enteric film-coated, controlled-release, round tablet, as follows:

12.5 mg yellow tablets

NDC 60505-4377-3 Bottles of 30 (one face is plain and the other is engraved with 12.5)

25 mg pink tablets

NDC 60505-4378-3 Bottles of 30 (one face is plain and the other is engraved with 25)

37.5 mg blue tablets

NDC 60505-4379-3 Bottles of 30 (one face is plain and the other is engraved with 37.5)

Store at or below 25° C (77° F) [see USP].
