# DESCRIPTION

Lisinopril is an oral long-acting angiotensin converting enzyme inhibitor.
Lisinopril, a synthetic peptide derivative, is chemically described as (S)-1-[N2-(1-carboxy-3-phenylpropyl)-L-lysyl]-L-proline dihydrate.

Lisinopril USP is a white to off-white, crystalline powder, with a molecular weight of 441.53.
It is soluble in water and sparingly soluble in methanol and practically insoluble in ethanol.
Lisinopril is supplied as 2.5 mg, 5 mg, 10 mg, 20 mg, 30 mg, and 40 mg tablets for oral administration.
Inactive Ingredients: 2.5 mg tablets – dibasic calcium phosphate, magnesium stearate, mannitol, starch (maize) and pregelatinized starch (maize).
5 mg tablets – dibasic calcium phosphate, magnesium stearate, mannitol, starch (maize), pregelatinized starch (maize) and ferric oxide red.
10 mg, 20 mg, 30 mg, and 40 mg tablets - dibasic calcium phosphate, magnesium stearate, mannitol, starch (maize), pregelatinized starch (maize) and ferric oxide yellow.
