# DESCRIPTION

Lisinopril is an oral long-acting angiotensin converting enzyme inhibitor.
Lisinopril, a synthetic peptide derivative, is chemically described as (S)-1-[N 2 -(1-carboxy-3-phenylpropyl)-L-lysyl]-L-proline dihydrate.
Its empirical formula is C 21 H 31 N 3 O 5 .
2H 2 O and its structural formula is: Lisinopril is a white to off-white, crystalline powder, with a molecular weight of 441.53.
It is soluble in water and sparingly soluble in methanol and practically insoluble in ethanol.
Lisinopril is supplied as 2.5 mg, 5 mg, 10 mg, 20 mg, 30 mg, and 40 mg tablets for oral administration.
Inactive Ingredients: 2.5 mg tablets – dibasic calcium phosphate, magnesium stearate, mannitol, starch and pregelatinized starch.
5 mg tablets – dibasic calcium phosphate, magnesium stearate, mannitol, starch, pregelatinized starch and ferric oxide red.
10, 20, 30, and 40 mg tablets - dibasic calcium phosphate, magnesium stearate, mannitol, starch, pregelatinized starch and ferric oxide yellow.
