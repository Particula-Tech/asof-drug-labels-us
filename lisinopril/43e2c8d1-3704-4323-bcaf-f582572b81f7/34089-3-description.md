# DESCRIPTION

Lisinopril is an oral long-acting angiotensin converting enzyme inhibitor.
Lisinopril, a synthetic peptide derivative, is chemically described as (S)-1-[N2-(1-Carboxy-3-phenylpropyl)-L-lysyl]-L-proline dihydrate.

Lisinopril is a white to off-white, crystalline powder, with a molecular weight of 441.53.
It is soluble in water and sparingly soluble in methanol and practically insoluble in ethanol.

Lisinopril is supplied as 2.5 mg, 5 mg, 10 mg, 20 mg, 30 mg and 40 mg tablets for oral administration.

Each tablet for oral administration contains 2.5 mg, 5 mg, 10 mg, 20 mg, 30 mg, or 40 mg of lisinopril USP.
In addition, each tablet contains the following inactive ingredients: colloidal silicon dioxide, dibasic calcium phosphate, FD&C Blue #2 Aluminum Lake (10 mg only), FD&C Yellow #5 Aluminum Lake (20 mg, 30 mg, and 40 mg, only), magnesium stearate, mannitol, pregelatinized starch.
