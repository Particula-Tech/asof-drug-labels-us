# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

2.5 mg are white to off-white, capsule-shaped tablets, imprinted with ‘H 144’ on one side and plain on the other side.

5 mg are yellow, capsule-shaped tablets, imprinted with ‘H 145’ on one side and plain on the other side.

10 mg are light pink, capsule-shaped tablets, imprinted with ‘H 146’ on one side and plain on the other side.

20 mg are dark pink, capsule-shaped tablets, imprinted with ‘H 147’ on one side and plain on the other side.

30 mg are red, capsule-shaped tablets, imprinted with ‘H 148’ on one side and plain on the other side.

40 mg are yellow, capsule-shaped tablets, imprinted with ‘H 149’ on one side and plain on the other side.
