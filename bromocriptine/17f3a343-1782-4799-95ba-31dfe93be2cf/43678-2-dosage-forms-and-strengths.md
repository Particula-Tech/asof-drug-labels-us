# 3 DOSAGE FORMS & STRENGTHS

Tablets: 2.5 mg of bromocriptine, off-white, round, flat-faced, beveled-edge tablets, debossed “E” above the score and “280” below the score on one side and debossed “2.5” on the other side.

Capsules: 5 mg of bromocriptine, hard gelatin capsule size 3 with caramel opaque cap imprinted “102” on the cap and white opaque body, imprinted with “PARLODEL” over “5 mg” on the body.
