# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Bromocriptine Mesylate Tablets, USP 2.5 mg

Tablets contain 2.5 mg of bromocriptine and are off-white, round, flat-faced, beveled-edge tablets, debossed “E” above the score and “280” below the score on one side and debossed “2.5” on the other side.

- Bottles of 30 ……….NDC 70954-978-10
- Bottles of 100………NDC 70954-978-20

Bromocriptine Mesylate Capsules, USP 5 mg

- Bottles of 30……..…NDC 70954-951-10
- Bottles of 100………NDC 70954-951-20

Store and Dispense

Store at 68°F to 77°F (20°C to 25°C); excursions permitted to 59°F to 86°F (15°C to 30°C) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Protect from light.
Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container.
