# 7 DRUG INTERACTIONS

## 7.1 Antiepileptic Drugs

When using doses of oxcarbazepine greater than 1200 mg/day during adjunctive therapy, a decrease in the dose of phenytoin may be required.

Strong inducers of cytochrome P450 enzymes (i.e., carbamazepine, phenytoin and phenobarbital) have been shown to decrease the plasma levels of MHD (29% to 40%).

## 7.2 Hormonal Contraceptives

Concurrent use of oxcarbazepine with hormonal contraceptives may render these contraceptives less effective [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].
Studies with other oral or implant contraceptives have not been conducted.
