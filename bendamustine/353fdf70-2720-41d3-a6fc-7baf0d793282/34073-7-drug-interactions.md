# 7 DRUG INTERACTIONS

## 7.1 Effect of Other Drugs on GOBARDEN

CYP1A2 Inhibitors

The coadministration of GOBARDEN with CYP1A2 inhibitors may increase bendamustine plasma concentrations and may result in increased incidence of adverse reactions with GOBARDEN [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].
Consider alternative therapies that are not CYP1A2 inhibitors during treatment with GOBARDEN.

CYP1A2 Inducers

The coadministration of GOBARDEN with CYP1A2 inducers may decrease bendamustine plasma concentrations and may result in decreased efficacy of GOBARDEN [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].
Consider alternative therapies that are not CYP1A2 inducers during treatment with GOBARDEN.
