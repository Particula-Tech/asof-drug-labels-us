# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Bendamustine Hydrochloride Injection is contraindicated in patients with a known hypersensitivity (e.g., anaphylactic and anaphylactoid reactions) to bendamustine, polyethylene glycol 400, polyethylene glycol 300, or butylated hydroxytoluene.
