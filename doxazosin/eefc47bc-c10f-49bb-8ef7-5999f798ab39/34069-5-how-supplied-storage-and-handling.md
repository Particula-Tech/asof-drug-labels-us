# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

CARDURA (doxazosin) is available as tablets for oral administration.
Each tablet contains doxazosin mesylate equivalent to 1 mg (white), 2 mg (yellow or white), 4 mg (orange or white) or 8 mg (green or white) of doxazosin as the free base.
