# HOW SUPPLIED

Doxazosin Tablets USP are supplied as follows:

1 mg (doxazosin as free base): White to off-white, round, biconvex compressed tablet, engraved with 590 over bisect on one side and TV on the other side.
They are available in bottles of 100 tablets (NDC 0093-2070-01).

2 mg (doxazosin as free base): White to off-white, capsule-shaped, biconvex compressed tablet, engraved with 593 left of bisect on one side and TV on the other side.
They are available in bottles of 100 tablets (NDC 0093-2069-01).

4 mg (doxazosin as free base): White to off-white, modified diamond-shaped, biconvex compressed tablet, engraved with 596 over bisect on one side and TV on the other side.
They are available in bottles of 100 tablets (NDC 0093-2068-01).

8 mg (doxazosin as free base): White to off-white, round, biconvex compressed tablet, engraved TV over bisect 598 (TV/598) on one side and 8 on the other side.
They are available in bottles of 100 tablets (NDC 0093-2067-01).

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Dispense in a well-closed container.

KEEP THIS AND ALL MEDICATIONS OUT OF THE REACH OF CHILDREN.
