# HOW SUPPLIED

Doxazosin tablets USP, 1 mg (doxazosin as free base), are white to off-white, round, biconvex compressed tablet, engraved with 590 over bisect on one side and TV on the other side.
They are supplied as follows:

NDC 0093-2070-01 bottles of 100’s

NDC 0093-2070-05 bottles of 500’s

NDC 0093-2070-93 unit-dose boxes of 100’s (10 x 10s)

Doxazosin tablets USP, 2 mg (doxazosin as free base), are white to off-white, capsule-shaped, biconvex compressed tablet, engraved with 593 left of bisect on one side and TV on the other side.
They are supplied as follows:

NDC 0093-2069-01 bottles of 100’s

NDC 0093-2069-05 bottles of 500’s

NDC 0093-2069-93 unit-dose boxes of 100’s (10 x 10s)

They are supplied as follows:

NDC 0093-2068-01 bottles of 100’s

NDC 0093-2068-05 bottles of 500’s

NDC 0093-2068-93 unit-dose boxes of 100’s (10 x 10s)

They are supplied as follows:

NDC 0093-2067-01 bottles of 100’s

NDC 0093-2067-05 bottles of 500’s
