# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Febuxostat tablets 40 mg are white or almost white round film-coated tablets, debossed with “C33” on one side and blank on the other side and supplied as:

| NDC Number | Size |
| --- | --- |
| 0527-2244-32 | Bottle of 30 Tablets |

Febuxostat tablets 80 mg are white or almost white capsule-shaped film-coated tablets, debossed with “C32” on one side and blank on the other side and supplied as:

| NDC Number | Size |
| --- | --- |
| 0527-2248-32 | Bottle of 30 Tablets |

Protect from light.
Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
