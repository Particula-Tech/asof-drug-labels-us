Febuxostat is a xanthine oxidase inhibitor.
The active ingredient in Febuxostat Tablets is 2-[3-cyano-4-(2-methylpropoxy) phenyl]-4-methylthiazole-5-carboxylic acid hemihydrate, with a molecular weight of 325.39.

Febuxostat is a non-hygroscopic, white to yellowish crystalline powder that is soluble in dimethylformamide; slightly soluble in methanol; and insoluble in water.

Febuxostat Tablets for oral use contain the active ingredient, febuxostat, and are available in two dosage strengths, 40 mg and 80 mg.
