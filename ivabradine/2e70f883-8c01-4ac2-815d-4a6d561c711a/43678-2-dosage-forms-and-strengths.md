# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Tablets

Ivabradine tablets 5 mg: Light salmon-colored, capsule-shaped, film-coated scored tablet debossed with "I5" divided by score on one side and plain on other side.
The tablet is scored and can be divided into equal halves to provide a 2.5 mg dose.

Ivabradine tablets 7.5 mg: Light salmon-colored, round-shaped, film-coated tablet debossed with "I" on one side & 7.5 on other side.
