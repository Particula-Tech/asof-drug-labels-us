# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Ivabradine tablets 5 mg: orange colored, capsule shaped, film-coated tablets, functionally scored with debossing "M" & "I" on one side and "2" on other side.
The tablet is scored and can be divided into equal halves to provide a 2.5 mg dose.
7.5 mg: orange colored, round shaped, film-coated tablets debossed with “MI” on one side and “1” on the other side.
