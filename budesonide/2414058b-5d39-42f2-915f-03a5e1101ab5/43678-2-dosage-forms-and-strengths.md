# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Budesonide inhalation suspension is available in two strengths, each containing 2 mL: 0.25 mg/2 mL and 0.5 mg/2 mL.
Budesonide inhalation suspension is supplied in sealed aluminum foil envelopes containing one plastic strip of five single-dose vials.
There are 30 vials in a carton.
Each single-dose vial contains 2 mL of sterile liquid suspension.
