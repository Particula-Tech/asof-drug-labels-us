# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Budesonide inhalation suspension is available in one strength, containing 2 mL: 1 mg/2 mL.
Budesonide inhalation suspension is supplied in sealed aluminum foil envelopes containing one plastic strip of five single-dose ampules.
There are 30 ampules in a carton.
Each single-dose ampule contains 2 mL of sterile liquid suspension.
