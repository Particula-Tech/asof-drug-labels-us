# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

- •160 mcg/9 mcg/4.8 mcg (budesonide 160 mcg, glycopyrrolate 9 mcg, and formoterol fumarate 4.8 mcg) per inhalation.
- •160 mcg/18 mcg/4.8 mcg (budesonide 160 mcg, glycopyrrolate 18 mcg, and formoterol fumarate 4.8 mcg) per inhalation.
