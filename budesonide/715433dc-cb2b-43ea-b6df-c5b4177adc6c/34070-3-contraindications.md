# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

BREZTRI AEROSPHERE is contraindicated in the following conditions:

- •Primary treatment of status asthmaticus or other acute episodes of COPD or asthma where intensive measures are required [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)].
- •Hypersensitivity to budesonide, glycopyrrolate, formoterol, or any of the excipients [see Warnings and Precautions (5.11) and Description (11)].
