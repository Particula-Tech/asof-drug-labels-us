# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

TARPEYO (budesonide) delayed release capsules 4 mg, are white opaque- coated capsules marked with “CAL10 4 MG” in black ink on the body of the capsule.
They are supplied as follows:

NDC 81749-004-01: Bottles of 120 capsules.
Child-resistant cap.

Store at 20-25°C (68 - 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Keep container tightly closed.
Protect from moisture.
