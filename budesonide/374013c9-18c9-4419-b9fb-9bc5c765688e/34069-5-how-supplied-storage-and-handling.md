# 16 HOW SUPPLIED / STORAGE AND HANDLING

Budesonide inhalation suspension is supplied in sealed aluminum foil envelopes containing one plastic strip of five single-dose ampules or in individually wrapped single-dose ampules, together with patient instructions for use.
There are 30 ampules in a carton.
Each single-dose ampule contains 2 mL of sterile liquid suspension.

Budesonide inhalation suspension is available in two strengths, each containing 2 mL:

NDC 62135-822-84 0.25 mg/2 mL 6 foil pouches, each containing 5 ampules, total 30 ampules per carton

NDC 62135-823-84 0.5 mg/2 mL 6 foil pouches, each containing 5 ampules, total 30 ampules per carton

Budesonide inhalation suspension should be stored upright at controlled room temperature 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [see USP], and protected from light.
When an envelope has been opened, the shelf life of the unused ampules is 2 weeks when protected.
After opening the aluminum foil envelope, the unused ampules should be returned to the aluminum foil envelope to protect them from light.
Any opened ampule must be used promptly.
Gently shake the ampule using a circular motion before use.
Keep out of reach of children.
Do not freeze.
