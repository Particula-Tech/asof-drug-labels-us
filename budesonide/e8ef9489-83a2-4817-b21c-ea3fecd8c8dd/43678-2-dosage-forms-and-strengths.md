# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Budesonide inhalation suspension is available in two strengths, each containing 2 mL: 0.25 mg/2 mL and 0.5 mg/2mL.

Budesonide inhalation suspension is supplied in sealed aluminum foil envelopes containing one plastic strip of five single-dose ampules together with patient instructions for use.
There are 30 ampules in a carton.
Each single-dose ampule contains 2 mL of sterile liquid suspension.
