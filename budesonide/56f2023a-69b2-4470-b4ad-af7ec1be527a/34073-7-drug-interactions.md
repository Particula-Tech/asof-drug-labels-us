# 7 DRUG INTERACTIONS

## 7.1 CYP3A4 Inhibitors

Budesonide is a substrate for CYP3A4.
Concomitant use of budesonide with CYP3A4 inhibitors (e.g., ketoconazole, itraconazole, ritonavir, indinavir, saquinavir, erythromycin, cyclosporine, grapefruit juice) can increase systemic budesonide concentrations [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].
Avoid concomitant use of CYP3A4 inhibitors, including grapefruit juice, with EOHILIA [see Dosage and Administration (2.2)].
