# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

FAVLYXA™ Injection is a sterile colorless to light yellow clear solution available in single-dose vials and multiple-dose vials, individually packaged in a carton as follows:

| Strength | Carton Contains | NDC |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 250 mg/10 mL (25 mg/mL) 2,500 mg/100 mL (25 mg/mL) | One single-dose vial One multiple-dose vial | 83831-143-0183831-164-10 |

## 16.2 Storage

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted from 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F).
[see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light.
Retain in carton until time of use.
Fluorouracil is a hazardous drug.
Follow applicable special handling and disposal procedures [see References (15)].
