# WARNING: SERIOUS ADVERSE REACTIONS OR DEATH IN PATIENTS WITH COMPLETE DPD DEFICIENCY

Increased Risk of Serious Adverse Reactions or Death in Patients with Complete DPD Deficiency

Test patients for genetic variants of DPYD prior to initiating fluorouracil unless immediate treatment is necessary.
Avoid use of fluorouracil in patients with certain homozygous or compound heterozygous DPYD variants that result in complete DPD deficiency [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
