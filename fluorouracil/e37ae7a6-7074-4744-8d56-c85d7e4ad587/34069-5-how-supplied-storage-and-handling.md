# 16 HOW SUPPLIED / STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Tolak (fluorouracil) Cream, 4% containing 40 mg of fluorouracil per gram of white cream is available in a 40 gram tube (NDC 64370-421-40).

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at 25°C (77°F), with excursion permitted from 15°C to 30°C (59°F - 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Do not freeze.
