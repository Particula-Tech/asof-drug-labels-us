# DESCRIPTION

FLUOROPLEX® (fluorouracil) 1% Topical Cream is an antineoplastic/antimetabolite product for dermatological use.
Fluorouracil has the empirical formula C4H3FN2O2 and a molecular weight of 130.08.
It is sparingly soluble in water and slightly soluble in alcohol.
The pH is approximately 8.5.
