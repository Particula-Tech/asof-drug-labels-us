# DESCRIPTION

Leucovorin Calcium Tablets contain either 5 mg or 25 mg leucovorin as the calcium salt of N-[4-[[(2-amino-5-formyl-1, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8-hexahydro-4-oxo-6-pteridinyl)-methyl]amino]benzoyl]-L-glutamic acid.
This is equivalent to 5.40 mg or 27.01 mg of anhydrous leucovorin calcium.
In addition each tablet contains the following inactive ingredients: colloidal silicon dioxide, croscarmellose sodium, lactose monohydrate, magnesium stearate, and microcrystalline cellulose.
The 25mg tablet also contains D&C yellow no. 10 and FD&C blue no. 1.

Leucovorin is a water soluble form of reduced folate in the folate group; it is useful as an antidote to drugs which act as folic acid antagonists.
These tablets are intended for oral administration only.
The structural formula of leucovorin calcium is:

C20H21CaN7O7 Molecular Weight: 511.51
