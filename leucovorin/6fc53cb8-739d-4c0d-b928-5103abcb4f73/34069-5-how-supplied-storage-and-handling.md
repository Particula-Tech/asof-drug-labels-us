# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

WELLCOVORIN (leucovorin calcium) tablets, USP contain:

- •5 mg of leucovorin: off white‑colored, imprinted with “WELLCOVORIN” and “5” and supplied in bottles of 20 and 100 or unit dose packs of 50.
- •25 mg of leucovorin: peach‑colored, imprinted with “WELLCOVORIN” and “25” and supplied in bottles of 25 or unit dose packs of 10.

WELLCOVORIN 5 mg tablets and 25 mg tablets are not manufactured or marketed by GSK.

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at 15°C to 25°C (59°F to 77°F).
Protect from light and moisture.
