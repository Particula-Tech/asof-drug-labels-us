# 6 ADVERSE REACTIONS

The following clinically significant adverse reactions are described elsewhere in the labeling:

- •Hypersensitivity Reactions [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].

The following adverse reactions have been identified during postapproval use of leucovorin (d,l‑leucovorin) or levoleucovorin (l‑leucovorin).
Because these reactions are reported voluntarily from a population of uncertain size, it is not always possible to reliably estimate their frequency or establish a causal relationship to drug exposure.

- •Dermatologic: Pruritus, rash.
- •Respiratory: Dyspnea.
- •Other Clinical Events: Rigors, temperature change.

Safety information for the treatment of FOLR1-CFTD with oral leucovorin is limited.
The available evidence is based on published case reports [see Clinical Studies (14.1)].
