# 5 WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS

## 5.1 Hypersensitivity Reactions

Hypersensitivity reactions, including anaphylactic reactions and urticaria, have been reported following the administration of leucovorin.
Leucovorin is contraindicated for the treatment of folic acid antagonist or DHFR inhibitor toxicity in patients with a history of a severe hypersensitivity reaction and for the treatment of FOLR1-CFTD in patients with a history of any hypersensitivity reaction to leucovorin, levoleucovorin, folic acid, or any component of leucovorin [see Contraindications (4)].
Withhold or permanently discontinue leucovorin based on the severity of hypersensitivity.
