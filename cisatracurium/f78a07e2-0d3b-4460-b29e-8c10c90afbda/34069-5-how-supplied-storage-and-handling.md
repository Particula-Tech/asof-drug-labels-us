Storage

Cisatracurium Besylate Injection, USP should be refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) in the tray to preserve potency.
Protect from light.
DO NOT FREEZE.
Upon removal from refrigeration to room temperature storage conditions (25°C/77°F), use Cisatracurium Besylate Injection, USP within 21 days even if re-refrigerated.

The container closure is not made with natural rubber latex.
