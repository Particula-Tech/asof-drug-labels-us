# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

NIMBEX injection is available as a clear solution in the following strengths:

- 10 mg of cisatracurium per 5 mL (2 mg/mL) in single-dose vials (equivalent to 2.68 mg/mL cisatracurium besylate)
- 20 mg of cisatracurium per 10 mL (2 mg/mL) and benzyl alcohol as a preservative in multiple-dose vials (equivalent to 2.68 mg/mL cisatracurium besylate).
- 200 mg of cisatracurium per 20 mL (10 mg/mL) in single-dose vials (equivalent to 13.38 mg/mL cisatracurium besylate); intended only for administration as an infusion in a single patient in the ICU.
