Storage and Handling

Capsules

Store at 20-25°C (68-77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

For Oral Suspension

Store unopened pouch at 20-25°C (68-77°F); excursions permitted between 15-30°C (between 59-86°F).
Store in the original container.
Do not open pouch until ready for use.

Once prepared, if suspension is not used immediately, store refrigerated [between 36°F-46°F (2°C-8°C)] for up to 72 hours prior to use.
When ready to use, the mixture can be kept at room temperature [between 68°F-77°F (20°C-25°C)] for up to 3 hours.
