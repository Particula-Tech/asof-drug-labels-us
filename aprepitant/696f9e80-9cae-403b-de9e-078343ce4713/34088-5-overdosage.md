# OVERDOSAGE

No specific information is available on the treatment of overdosage with EMEND.
Single doses up to 600 mg of aprepitant were generally well tolerated in healthy subjects.
Aprepitant was generally well tolerated when administered as 375 mg once daily for up to 42 days to patients in non-CINV studies.
In 33 cancer patients, administration of a single 375-mg dose of aprepitant on Day 1 and 250 mg once daily on Days 2 to 5 was generally well tolerated.

Drowsiness and headache were reported in one patient who ingested 1440 mg of aprepitant.

In the event of overdose, EMEND should be discontinued and general supportive treatment and monitoring should be provided.
Because of the antiemetic activity of aprepitant, drug-induced emesis may not be effective.

Aprepitant cannot be removed by hemodialysis.
