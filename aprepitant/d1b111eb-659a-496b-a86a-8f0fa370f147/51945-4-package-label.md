# PRINCIPAL DISPLAY PANEL - 10 Vial Carton

NDC 47426-401-10

How Supplied: APONVIE injectable emulsion is supplied in a carton containing 10 sterile, single-dose glass vials of 32 mg aprepitant per 4.4 mL for intravenous administration.

Recommended Dosage: See Prescribing Information.

APONVIE® (aprepitant) injectible emulsion

32 mg per 4.4 mL (7.2 mg/mL)

For Intravenous Use Only

10 x 5 mL single-dose vials - Discard unused portion of vial.

Storage: Must be refrigerated, 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F).
Do not freeze.

Rx Only
