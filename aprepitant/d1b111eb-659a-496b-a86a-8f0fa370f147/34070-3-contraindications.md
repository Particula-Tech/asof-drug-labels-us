# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

APONVIE is contraindicated in patients:

- with a history of hypersensitivity to aprepitant or any component of the product [see Description (11)].
  Hypersensitivity reactions have included anaphylaxis [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
- taking pimozide.
  Inhibition of CYP3A4 by aprepitant could result in elevated plasma concentrations of pimozide, which is a CYP3A4 substrate, potentially causing serious or life-threatening reactions, such as QT prolongation, a known adverse reaction of pimozide [see Drug Interactions (7.1)].
