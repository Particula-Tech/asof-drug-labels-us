# 10 OVERDOSAGE

There is no specific information on the treatment of overdosage with aprepitant.

In the event of overdose, CINVANTI should be discontinued and general supportive treatment and monitoring should be provided.
Because of the antiemetic activity of CINVANTI, drug-induced emesis may not be effective in cases of CINVANTI overdosage.

Aprepitant is not removed by hemodialysis.
