# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

CINVANTI is contraindicated in patients:

- who are hypersensitive to any component of the product [see Description (11)].
  Hypersensitivity reactions including anaphylaxis have been reported [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2), Adverse Reactions (6.2)].
- taking pimozide.
  Inhibition of CYP3A4 by aprepitant could result in elevated plasma concentrations of pimozide, which is a CYP3A4 substrate, potentially causing serious or life-threatening reactions, such as QT prolongation, a known adverse reaction of pimozide [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
