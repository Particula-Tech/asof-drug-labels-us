# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Product No.: 103450 NDC No.: 68817-134-50 100 mg of paclitaxel in a single-use vial, individually packaged in a carton.

## 16.2 Storage

Store the vials in original cartons at 20° C to 25° C (68° F to 77°F).
Retain in the original package to protect from bright light.

## 16.3 Handling and Disposal

Procedures for proper handling and disposal of anticancer drugs should be considered.
Several guidelines on this subject have been published [see References (15)].
There is no general agreement that all of the procedures recommended in the guidelines are necessary or appropriate.
