# HOW SUPPLIED

Product No. 103450

NDC No. 68817-134-50 100 mg of paclitaxel in a single use vial, individually packaged in a carton.

## Storage

Store the vials in original cartons at 20° C to 25° C (68° F to 77°F).
Retain in the original package to protect from bright light.

## Handling and Disposal

Procedures for proper handling and disposal of anticancer drugs should be considered.
Several guidelines on this subject have been published.1-8 There is no general agreement that all of the procedures recommended in the guidelines are necessary or appropriate.
