# HOW SUPPLIED

Paclitaxel Injection, USP (6 mg/mL) is supplied in the following:

| NDC No. | Fill volume | Package |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 68083-178-01 | 5 mL fill in 5 mL Multiple-dose vial | One Multiple-dose vial in one carton |
| 68083-179-01 | 16.7 mL fill in 20 mL Multiple-dose vial | One Multiple-dose vial in one carton |
| 68083-180-01 | 50 mL fill in 50 mL Multiple-dose vial | One Multiple-dose vial in one carton |

Storage: Store the vials in original cartons between 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]
Retain in the original package to protect from light.
