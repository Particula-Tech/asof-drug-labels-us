# 7 DRUG INTERACTIONS

## 7.1 Effect of Other Drugs on Paclitaxel Protein-Bound Particles for Injectable Suspension

The metabolism of paclitaxel is catalyzed by CYP2C8 and CYP3A4.
Caution should be exercised when administering protein bound paclitaxel concomitantly with medicines known to inhibit or induce either CYP2C8 or CYP3A4 [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].
