# HOW SUPPLIED

Paclitaxel Injection (6 mg/mL) is supplied in the following:

NDC No. Container Size

61703-342-09 Multiple-dose Vial 30 mg/5 mL

61703-342-22 Multiple-dose Vial 100 mg/16.7 mL

61703-342-50 Multiple-dose Vial 300 mg/50 mL

Storage: Store the vials in original cartons between 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
Retain in the original package to protect from light.

Handling and Disposal: Procedures for proper handling and disposal of anticancer drugs should be considered.
Several guidelines on this subject have been published.1-7 There is no general agreement that all of the procedures recommended in the guidelines are necessary or appropriate.
