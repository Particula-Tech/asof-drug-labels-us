# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

At bedtime, squeeze a pea-sized amount of medication onto one fingertip, dot onto the chin, cheeks, nose, and forehead, then gently rub over the entire face.
ZIANA® Gel should be kept away from the eyes, the mouth, angles of the nose, and mucous membranes.

ZIANA Gel is not for oral, ophthalmic, or intravaginal use.
