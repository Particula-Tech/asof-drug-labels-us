# CLINICAL PHARMACOLOGY

## Pharmacokinetics

Following a single intravaginal application of ClindesseTM cream to twenty healthy women, the mean (range) AUC0-inf and Cmax estimates were 175 (38.6 to 541) ng/mL∙hr and 6.6 (0.8 to 39) ng/mL, respectively.
The mean Cmax of clindamycin for ClindesseTM was approximately 0.3%, 0.1%, and 7.6% of that observed after the administration of a 150 mg Cleocin® oral capsule (2.5 μg/mL), a 600 mg Cleocin® intravenous injection (10.9 μg/mL), and a single dose of 100 mg of Cleocin® Vaginal Cream (86.5 ng/mL), respectively.
The peak serum concentration of clindamycin was attained approximately 20 hours post dosing for Clindesse™.
