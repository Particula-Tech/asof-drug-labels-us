# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Evoclin (clindamycin phosphate) Foam, 1% is supplied as follows:

- 50 g aluminum can NDC 40076-061-50
- 100 g aluminum can NDC 40076-061-00

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at controlled room temperature between 68°F to 77°F (20°C to 25°C).

Flammable.
Avoid fire, flame or smoking during and immediately following application.

Contents under pressure.
Do not puncture or incinerate.
Do not expose to heat or store at temperature above 120°F (49°C).

Keep out of reach of children.
