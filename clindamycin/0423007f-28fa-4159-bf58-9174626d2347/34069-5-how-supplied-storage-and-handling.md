# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

XACIATO vaginal gel, 2% is a clear, colorless, viscous gel supplied in a carton containing one 8-gram tube of vaginal gel and one vaginal applicator.
One single-dose, user-filled disposable applicator delivers 5 g of gel containing 100 mg of clindamycin.
NDC 78206-189-01.

Store at 68ºF to 77ºF (20ºC to 25ºC); excursions permitted to 59ºF to 86ºF (15ºC to 30ºC).
[See USP Controlled Room temperature].
