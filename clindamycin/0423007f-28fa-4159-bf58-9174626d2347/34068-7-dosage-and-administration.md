# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

The recommended dosage of XACIATO is one applicatorful (5 g of vaginal gel containing 100 mg of clindamycin) administered once intravaginally as a single dose at any time of the day [see Clinical Studies (14)].
Place the used tube with any remaining gel and used applicator in the container box and deposit in a trash container after use [see Instructions for Use].

XACIATO is not for ophthalmic, dermal, or oral use.
