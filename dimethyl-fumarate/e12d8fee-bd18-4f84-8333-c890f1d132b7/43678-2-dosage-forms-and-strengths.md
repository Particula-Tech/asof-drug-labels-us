# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Dimethyl fumarate delayed-release capsules is available as hard gelatin delayed-release capsules containing 120 mg or 240 mg of dimethyl fumarate.
The 120 mg capsules have an opaque green cap printed with "DMF" and opaque white body printed with" 120" in black ink.
The 240 mg capsules have an opaque green cap printed with "DMF" and opaque green body printed with "240" in black ink.
