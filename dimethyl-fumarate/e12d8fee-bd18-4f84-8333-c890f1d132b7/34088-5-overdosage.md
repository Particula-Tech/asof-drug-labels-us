# 10 OVERDOSE

Cases of overdose with dimethyl fumarate delayed-release capsules have been reported.
The symptoms described in these cases were consistent with the known adverse event profile of dimethyl fumarate delayed-release capsules.

There are no known therapeutic interventions to enhance elimination of dimethyl fumarate delayed-release capsules nor is there a known antidote.
In the event of overdose, initiate symptomatic supportive treatment as clinically indicated.
