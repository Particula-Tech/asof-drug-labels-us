# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Dimethyl fumarate delayed-release capsules are available as hard gelatin delayed-release capsules containing 120 mg or 240 mg of dimethyl fumarate.

120 mg: White to off white colored mini tablets filled in size '0' empty hard gelatin capsule shell with opaque green colored cap and opaque white body imprinted 'CIPLA 322' on cap with black ink.

240 mg: White to off white colored mini tablets filled in size '0' empty hard gelatin capsule shell with opaque green colored cap and body imprinted 'CIPLA 323' on cap with black ink.
