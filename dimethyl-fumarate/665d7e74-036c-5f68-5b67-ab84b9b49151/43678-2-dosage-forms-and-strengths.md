# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

TECFIDERA is available as hard gelatin delayed-release capsules containing 120 mg or 240 mg of dimethyl fumarate.
The 120 mg capsules have a green cap and white body, printed with “BG-12 120 mg” in black ink on the body.
The 240 mg capsules have a green cap and a green body, printed with “BG-12 240 mg” in black ink on the body.
