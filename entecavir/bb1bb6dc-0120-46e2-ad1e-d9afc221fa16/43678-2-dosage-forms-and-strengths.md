# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

- •Entecavir 0.5 mg film-coated tablets, USP, are white to off-white, triangular-shaped, and debossed with “S” on one side and “064” on the other side.
- •Entecavir 1 mg film-coated tablets, USP, are pink, triangular-shaped, and debossed with “S” on one side and “065” on the other side.
