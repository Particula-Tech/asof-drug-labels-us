# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

- Entecavir tablets, USP 0.5 mg are white to off-white, oval, film-coated, biconvex bevel edged, unscored tablets, debossed with “AN” on one side and “446” on the other side.
- Entecavir tablets, USP 1 mg are pink, oval, film-coated, biconvex bevel edged, unscored tablets, debossed with “AN” on one side and “449” on the other side.
