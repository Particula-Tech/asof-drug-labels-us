# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Entecavir tablets USP, 0.5 mg are white to off-white, round-shaped, biconvex with beveled edge, film-coated tablets, debossed with "920" on one side and plain on the other side and are supplied as follows:

NDC 70771-1019-3 in bottle of 30 tablets

NDC 70771-1019-9 in bottle of 90 tablets

NDC 70771-1019-1 in bottle of 100 tablets

NDC 70771-1019-4 in unit-dose blister cartons of 100 (10 x 10) unit-dose tablets

Entecavir tablets USP, 1 mg are light-pink to pink, round-shaped, biconvex with beveled edge, film-coated tablets, debossed with "921" on one side and plain on the other side and are supplied as follows:

NDC 70771-1020-3 in bottle of 30 tablets

NDC 70771-1020-9 in bottle of 90 tablets

NDC 70771-1020-1 in bottle of 100 tablets

NDC 70771-1020-4 in unit-dose blister cartons of 100 (10 x 10) unit-dose tablets

Storage

Entecavir tablets should be stored in a tightly closed container at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Protect from light.
