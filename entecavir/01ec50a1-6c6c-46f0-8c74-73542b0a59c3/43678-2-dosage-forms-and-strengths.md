# 3 DOSAGE FORMS & STRENGTHS

- Entecavir tablets, 0.5 mg are white to off white, triangle shaped, biconvex, film-coated tablets, debossed with 'J' on one side and'110' on the other side.
- Entecavir tablets, 1 mg are pink, triangle shaped, biconvex, film-coated tablets, debossed with'J' on one side and '111' on the other side.
