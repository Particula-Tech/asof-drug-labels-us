# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

• Entecavir tablets USP, 0.5 mg are white to off white, triangle shaped, biconvex, film-coated tablets, debossed with ‘J’ on one side and ‘110ʼ on the other side. • Entecavir tablets USP, 1 mg are pink, triangle shaped, biconvex, film-coated tablets, debossed with ‘J’ on one side and ‘111’ on the other side.
