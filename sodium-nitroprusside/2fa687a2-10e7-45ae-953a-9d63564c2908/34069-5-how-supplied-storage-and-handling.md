Sodium nitroprusside injection is supplied in amber-colored, single-dose 50 mg/2 mL Fliptop Vials.
It is available as follows:

2 mL, Single-dose Vial in Carton: NDC 70121-1189-1

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

To protect sodium nitroprusside injection from light, it should be stored in its carton until it is used.
