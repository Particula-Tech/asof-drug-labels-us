# HOW SUPPLIED

Sodium Nitroprusside Injection, USP is supplied in amber-colored, single-dose 50 mg in 2 mL vials (25 mg/mL) NDC 67457-839-02.

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

To protect sodium nitroprusside injection from light, it should be stored in its carton until it is used.

Discard unused portion.
