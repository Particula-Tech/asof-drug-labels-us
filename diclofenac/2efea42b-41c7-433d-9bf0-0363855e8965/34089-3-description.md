# DESCRIPTION

VOLTAREN® (diclofenac sodium enteric-coated tablets) is a benzene-acetic acid derivative.
VOLTAREN is available as delayed-release (enteric-coated) tablets of 75 mg (light pink) for oral administration.
Diclofenac sodium is a white or slightly yellowish crystalline powder and is sparingly soluble in water at 25°C.
The chemical name is 2-[(2,6-dichlorophenyl)amino] benzeneacetic acid, monosodium salt.
The molecular weight is 318.14 g/mol.
