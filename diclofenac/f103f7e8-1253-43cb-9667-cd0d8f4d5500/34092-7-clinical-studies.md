# CLINICAL STUDIES

Efficacy of Flector® Patch was demonstrated in two of four studies of patients with minor sprains, strains, and contusions.
Patients were randomly assigned to treatment with the Flector® Patch, or a placebo patch identical to the Flector® Patch minus the active ingredient.
In the first of these two studies, patients with ankle sprains were treated once daily for a week.
In the second study, patients with sprains, strains and contusions were treated twice daily for up to two weeks.
Pain was assessed over the period of treatment.
Patients treated with the Flector® Patch experienced a greater reduction in pain as compared to patients randomized to placebo patch as evidenced by the responder curves presented below.
