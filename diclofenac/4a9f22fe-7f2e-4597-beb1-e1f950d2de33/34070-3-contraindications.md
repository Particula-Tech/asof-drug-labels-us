# 4. CONTRAINDICATIONS

PENNSAID is contraindicated in patients with a known hypersensitivity to diclofenac sodium or any other component of PENNSAID.

PENNSAID is contraindicated in patients who have experienced asthma, urticaria, or allergic-type reactions after taking aspirin or other NSAIDs.
Severe, rarely fatal, anaphylactic-like reactions to NSAIDs have been reported in such patients [see Warnings and Precautions (5.7, 5.10)].

PENNSAID is contraindicated in the setting of coronary artery bypass graft (CABG) surgery [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
