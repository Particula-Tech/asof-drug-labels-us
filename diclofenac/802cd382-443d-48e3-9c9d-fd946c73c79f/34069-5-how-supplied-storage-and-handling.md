# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

PENNSAID (diclofenac sodium) topical solution 2% w/w, is supplied as a clear, colorless to faintly pink or orange solution containing 20 mg of diclofenac sodium per gram of solution, in a white polypropylene-dose pump bottle with a clear cap.
Each pump actuation delivers 20 mg of diclofenac sodium in 1 gram of solution.

| 112 g bottle | NDC # 75987-040-05 |

Storage

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
