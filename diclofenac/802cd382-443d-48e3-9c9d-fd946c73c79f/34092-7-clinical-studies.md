# 14 CLINICAL STUDIES

## 14.1 Study in Osteoarthritis of the Knee

PENNSAID

The use of PENNSAID for the treatment of pain of osteoarthritis of the knee was evaluated in a single double-blind controlled trial conducted in the US, involving patients treated with PENNSAID at a dose of 2 pumps twice a day for 4 weeks.
PENNSAID was compared to topical vehicle, applied directly to the study knee.
In this trial, patients treated with PENNSAID experienced a greater reduction in the Western Ontario and McMaster Universities Osteoarthritis Index (WOMAC) pain subscale compared to patients treated with vehicle.
