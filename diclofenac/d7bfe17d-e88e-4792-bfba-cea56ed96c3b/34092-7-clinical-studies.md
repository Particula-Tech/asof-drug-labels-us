# 14 CLINICAL STUDIES

## 14.1 Strains, Sprains, and Contusions

Efficacy of DICLOFENAC EPOLAMINE TOPICAL SYSTEM 1.3% was demonstrated in two of four studies of patients with minor sprains, strains, and contusions.
Patients were randomly assigned to treatment with the DICLOFENAC EPOLAMINE TOPICAL SYSTEM 1.3%, or a placebo identical to the DICLOFENAC EPOLAMINE TOPICAL SYSTEM 1.3% minus the active ingredient.
In the first of these two studies, patients with ankle sprains were treated once daily for a week.
In the second study, patients with sprains, strains and contusions were treated twice daily for up to two weeks.
Pain was assessed over the period of treatment.
