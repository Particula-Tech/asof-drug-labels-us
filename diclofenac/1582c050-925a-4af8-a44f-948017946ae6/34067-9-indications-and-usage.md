# INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Carefully consider the potential benefits and risks of Cataflam® (diclofenac potassium immediate-release tablets) and other treatment options before deciding to use Cataflam.
Use the lowest effective dose for the shortest duration consistent with individual patient treatment goals (see WARNINGS).

Cataflam is indicated:

- For treatment of primary dysmenorrhea
- For relief of mild to moderate pain
- For relief of the signs and symptoms of osteoarthritis
- For relief of the signs and symptoms of rheumatoid arthritis
