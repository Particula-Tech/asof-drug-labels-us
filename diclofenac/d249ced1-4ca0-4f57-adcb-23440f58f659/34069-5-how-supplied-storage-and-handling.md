# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

CAMBIA (diclofenac potassium) 50 mg, is a white to off-white, buffered, flavored powder for oral solution, supplied as one or more sets of three perforated co-joined individual dose packets.
Each individual packet is designed to deliver a dose of 50 mg diclofenac potassium when mixed in water.

NDC 13913-012-01 Individual CAMBIA Packets NDC 13913-012-03 Boxes of nine (9) CAMBIA Packets

Storage

Store at 25°C (77°F).
Excursions permitted from 15°C-30°C (59°F-86°F).
