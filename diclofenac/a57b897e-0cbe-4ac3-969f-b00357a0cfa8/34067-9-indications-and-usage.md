# INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Carefully consider the potential benefits and risks of diclofenac sodium delayed-release and other treatment options before deciding to use diclofenac sodium delayed-release.
Use the lowest effective dose for the shortest duration consistent with individual patient treatment goals (see WARNINGS).

Diclofenac sodium delayed-release tablets are indicated:

• For relief of signs and symptoms of osteoarthritis

• For relief of signs and symptoms of rheumatoid arthritis

• For acute or long-term use in the relief of signs and symptoms of ankylosing spondylitis
