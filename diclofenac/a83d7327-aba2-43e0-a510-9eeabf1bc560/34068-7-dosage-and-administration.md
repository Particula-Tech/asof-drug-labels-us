# DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

Carefully consider the potential benefits and risks of Flector® Patch and other treatment options before deciding to use Flector® Patch.
Use the lowest effective dose for the shortest duration consistent with individual patient treatment goals (seeWARNINGS).

The recommended dose of Flector® Patch is one (1) patch to the most painful area twice a day.

Flector® patch should not be applied to damaged or non-intact skin.

Flector® patch should not be worn when bathing or showering.

## HANDLING AND DISPOSAL

Patients and caregivers should wash their hands after applying, handling or removing the patch.
Eye contact should be avoided.
