# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

PENNSAID (diclofenac sodium) topical solution 1.5% w/w. is supplied as a clear, colorless to faintly pink-orange solution containing 16.05 mg of diclofenac sodium per mL of solution, in a white high density polyethylene bottle with a white low-density dropper cap.

NDC Number & Size

150 mL bottle NDC # 69451-300-15

Storage Store at room temperature 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
