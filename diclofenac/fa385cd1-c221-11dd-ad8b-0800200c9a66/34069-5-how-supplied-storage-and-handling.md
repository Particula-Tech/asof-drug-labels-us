# 16. HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Zipsor 25 mg is supplied as translucent, pale yellow, liquid-filled capsules printed with ”X592” in black ink.
Bottles of 100 Capsules NDC# 66479-592-10.

Store at 25ºC (77ºF); excursions permitted to 15ºC-30ºC (59ºF-86ºF).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature]

Protect from moisture.

Dispense in tight container (USP).
