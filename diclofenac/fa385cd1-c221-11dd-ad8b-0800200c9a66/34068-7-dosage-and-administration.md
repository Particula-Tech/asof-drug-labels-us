# 2. DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

## 2.1 Initiating Therapy

For treatment of mild to moderate acute pain, the dosage is 25 mg four times a day.
Use the lowest effective dose for the shortest duration consistent with individual patient treatment goals.

## 2.2 Non-Interchangeability with Other Formulations of Diclofenac

Different formulations of oral diclofenac are not bioequivalent even if the milligram strength is the same.
Therefore, it is not possible to convert dosing from any other formulation of diclofenac to Zipsor.
The only approved dosing regimen for Zipsor is 25 mg four times a day.
