# 11. DESCRIPTION

Zipsor (diclofenac potassium) Liquid Filled Capsule is a benzeneacetic acid derivative NSAID.
Zipsor is available as liquid-filled capsules of 25 mg for oral administration.
The chemical name is 2-[(2,6- dichlorophenyl) amino] benzeneacetic acid monopotassium salt.
The molecular weight is 334.24.

The inactive ingredients in Zipsor include ProSorb® (a proprietary combination of polyethylene glycol 400, glycerin, sorbitol, povidone, polysorbate 80, and hydrochloric acid), isopropyl alcohol, and mineral oil.
The capsule shells contain gelatin, sorbitol, isopropyl alcohol, glycerin, and mineral oil.
The imprinting on the gelatin capsules is black edible ink.
