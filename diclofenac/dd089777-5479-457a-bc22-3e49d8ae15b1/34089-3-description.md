# 11 DESCRIPTION

Dyloject Injection is a clear, colorless solution for intravenous administration.
Each mL of aqueous solution contains 37.5 mg of diclofenac sodium (34.8 mg of diclofenac), 333 mg hydroxypropyl betadex (HPβCD), 5 mg monothioglycerol, sodium hydroxide and/or hydrochloric acid for pH adjustment, and water for injection.

Diclofenac sodium, a nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug, is designated chemically as benzeneacetic acid, 2-[(2,6-dichlorophenyl)amino] monosodium salt, with a molecular formula of C14H10Cl2NNaO2.
The molecular weight is 318.13.
