# HOW SUPPLIED

The Flector® Patch is supplied in resealable envelopes, each containing 5 patches (10 cm x 14 cm), with 6 envelopes per box (NDC 60793-411-30).
Each individual patch is embossed with “FLECTOR PATCH DICLOFENAC EPOLAMINE TOPICAL PATCH 1.3%”.

- Each patch contains 180 mg of diclofenac epolamine in an aqueous base (13 mg of active per gram of adhesive or 1.3%).
- The product is intended for topical use only.
- Keep out of reach of children and pets.
- The ENVELOPES SHOULD BE SEALED AT ALL TIMES
- WHEN NOT IN USE.
- Store at 25ºC (77ºF); excursions permitted to 15º-30ºC

(59º-86ºF).
