# OVERDOSAGE

There is limited experience with overdose of Flector® Patch.
In clinical studies, the maximum single dose administered was one Flector® Patch containing 180 mg of diclofenac epolamine.
There were no serious adverse events.
Should systemic side effects occur due to incorrect use or accidental overdose of this product, the general measures recommended for intoxication with non-steroidal antiinflammatory drugs should be taken.
