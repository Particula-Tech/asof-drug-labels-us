IMPORTANT: Reseal after Opening NDC 60793-411-05 FLECTOR PATCH (diclofenac epolamine topical patch) 1.3% Rx Only 5 PATCHES (10MC x 14 CM EACH) Change patch once every 12 hours.
Fold used patches so that the adhesive side sticks to itself and safely discard used patches where children and pets cannot get to them.
FLECTOR PATCH INACTIVE INGREDIENTS: 1,3-butylene glycol, dihydroxyaluminium aminoacetate, disodium edetate, D-sorbitol, fragrance (Dalin PH), gelatin, kaolin, methylparaben, polysorbate 80, povidone, propylene glycol, propylparaben, sodium carboxymethylcellulose, sodium polyacrylate, tartaric acid, titanium dioxide, and purified water.
DOSAGE: For dosage and full prescribing information, read accompanying product information.
STORAGE: Store at 25C (77F); excursions permitted to 15-30C (59-85F).
WARNING: Envelope is not child resistant.
Keep used and unused patches out of reach of children and pets.
Avoid contact of FLECTOR Patch with your eyes, nose, or mouth.
If contact with your eyes occurs, rinse right away with water or saline.
Talk with your doctor if eye irritation continues.
DIRECTIONS FOR USE: Refer to Full Directions Before Using.
Wash hands throughly after applying, handling, or removing the patch.

1. Wash and Dry site: Wash the skin where you will apply FLECTOR Patch with soap and water.
   Do not use a moisturizing soap, lotion, astringent, or other skin care product where you will be applying the patch.
   Be sure skin is completely dry before applying FLECTOR Patch.
2. Cut: Cut envelope along the dotted line, making sure not to cut the zipper seal just below it.
3. Open and Reseal: Pull apart the zipper seal.
   Remove the FLECTOR Patch.
   IMPORTANT: RESEAL ENVELOPE by squeezing the zipper seal together.
   The patches inside will dry out if the envelope is left open.
4. Remove Clear Liner: There is a clear liner covering the sticky side of the patch.
   To remove it, fold over one corner of the patch.
   Gently rub the folded corner between your finger and thumb to separate the patch and liner.
   Remove the entire liner before applying the patch to the skin.
5. Apply: FLECTOR Patch should be applied to intact skin only.
   Apply one FLECTOR Patch to the most painful area.
   Firmly press the patch in place, especially around all four edges.
   Change the patch every 12 hours.
   Remove the patch if irritation occurs.
   (See full prescribing information).
6. Fold and Discard: Fold used patches so that the adhesive side sticks to itself.
   Throw away used patches where children and pets cannot get them.
   Discard unused patches 3 months after opening envelope.
