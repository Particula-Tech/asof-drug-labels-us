# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

- Hypersensitivity to ertugliflozin, metformin, or any excipient in SEGULORMET, reactions such as angioedema or anaphylaxis have occurred [see Adverse Reactions (6.2)].
- Patients with severe renal impairment (eGFR less than 30 mL/min/1.73 m2), end stage-renal disease (ESRD), or on dialysis [see Use in Specific Populations (8.6)].
- Acute or chronic metabolic acidosis, including diabetic ketoacidosis, with or without coma.
