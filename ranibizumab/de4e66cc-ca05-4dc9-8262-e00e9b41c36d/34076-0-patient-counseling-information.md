# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

Advise patients that in the days following LUCENTIS administration, patients are at risk of developing endophthalmitis and retinal vasculitis with or without occlusion.
If the eye becomes red, sensitive to light, painful, or develops a change in vision, advise the patient to seek immediate care from an ophthalmologist [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1, 5.5)].
