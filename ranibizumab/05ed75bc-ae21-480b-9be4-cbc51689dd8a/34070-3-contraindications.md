# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

## 4.1 Ocular or Periocular Infections

NUFYMCO is contraindicated in patients with ocular or periocular infections.

## 4.2 Hypersensitivity

NUFYMCO is contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity to ranibizumab products or any of the excipients in NUFYMCO.
Hypersensitivity reactions may manifest as severe intraocular inflammation.
