# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

NUFYMCO (ranibizumab-leyk) injection is a clear and colorless to pale yellow solution supplied in:

- Each NUFYMCO 0.5 mg carton (NDC 70710-2173-1) contains a single-dose glass vial with a BLUE CAP designed to deliver 0.05 mL of 10 mg/mL ranibizumab-leyk solution.
- Each NUFYMCO 0.3 mg carton (NDC 70710-2172-1) contains a single-dose glass vial with a WHITE CAP designed to deliver 0.05 mL of 6 mg/mL ranibizumab-leyk solution.

EACH CARTON IS FOR SINGLE-EYE USE ONLY.

NUFYMCO should be refrigerated at 2℃ to 8ºC (36℉ to 46ºF).
DO NOT FREEZE.
Do not use beyond the expiry date stamped on the label.
Protect NUFYMCO vials from light and store in the original carton until time of use.
