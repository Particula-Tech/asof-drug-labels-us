# 11 DESCRIPTION

PHEBURANE (sodium phenylbutyrate) oral pellets is a nitrogen binding agent.
Sodium phenylbutyrate is a white or yellowish-white powder, freely soluble in water and in methanol, and practically insoluble in methylene chloride.
It is known chemically as sodium 4-phenylbutanoate with a molecular weight of 186.19 and the molecular formula C 10H 11NaO 2.

Structural formula:

Each bottle of PHEBURANE contains 84 g of sodium phenylbutyrate (equivalent to 74 g of phenylbutyrate) in 174 g of oral pellets.
Each gram of pellets contains 483 mg of sodium phenylbutyrate (equivalent to 423 mg of phenylbutyrate).
PHEBURANE contains the following inactive ingredients: ethylcellulose, hypromellose, polyethylene glycol 1500, povidone K25, and sugar spheres.
