# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Nebivolol is available as tablets for oral administration containing nebivolol hydrochloride equivalent to 2.5, 5, 10, and 20 mg of nebivolol.

Nebivolol Tablets are round, flat beveled, differentiated by color and debossed with “G” on one side and with the number “15” (2.5 mg), “616” (5 mg), “617” (10 mg) or “618” (20 mg) on the other side.
