# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Nebivolol is available as tablets for oral administration containing nebivolol hydrochloride equivalent to 2.5, 5, 10, and 20 mg of nebivolol.

Nebivolol tablets are uncoated round-shaped, beveled edge, biconvex, unscored, mottled, differentiated by color and are debossed with “NL” on one side and the number of mg (2.5, 5, 10, or 20) on the other side.
