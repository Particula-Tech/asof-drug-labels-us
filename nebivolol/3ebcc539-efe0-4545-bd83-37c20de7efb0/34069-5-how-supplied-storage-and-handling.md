# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Nebivolol Tablets are available for oral administration containing nebivolol hydrochloride equivalent to 2.5, 5, 10, and 20 mg of nebivolol.

2.5 mg tablets: White to off-white, round, unscored tablets debossed with “A4” on one side with the other side blank, are supplied in the following package size: Bottles of 30 NDC 62559-275-30 Bottles of 2300 NDC 62559-275-83

5 mg tablets: White to off-white, round, unscored tablets debossed with “A49” on one side with the other side blank, are supplied in the following package sizes: Bottles of 30 NDC 62559-276-30 Bottles of 90 NDC 62559-276-90 Bottles of 1100 NDC 62559-276-71

10 mg tablets: White to off-white, round, unscored tablets debossed with “A50” on one side with the other side blank, are supplied in the following package sizes: Bottles of 30 NDC 62559-277-30 Bottles of 90 NDC 62559-277-90 Bottles of 500 NDC 62559-277-05

20mg tablets: White to off-white, round, unscored tablets debossed with “A51” on one side with the other side blank, are supplied in the following package sizes: Bottles of 30 NDC 62559-278-30 Bottles of 90 NDC 62559-278-90 Bottles of 500 NDC 62559-278-05

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP using a child-resistant closure.
