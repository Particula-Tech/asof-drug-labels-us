# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Nebivolol is available as tablets for oral administration containing nebivolol hydrochloride equivalent to 2.5, 5, 10, and 20 mg of nebivolol.
Nebivolol tablets are supplied in the following strengths and package configurations: Nebivolol tablets, 2.5 mg are white to off-white, triangular biconvex tablets debossed with ‘J’ on one side and ‘8’ on other side.
