# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Nebivolol is available as tablets for oral administration containing nebivolol hydrochloride equivalent to 2.5, 5, 10, and 20 mg of nebivolol.
Nebivolol tablets, 2.5 mg are white to off-white, triangular biconvex tablets debossed with ‘J’ on one side and ‘8’ on other side.
Nebivolol tablets, 5 mg are light orange, triangular biconvex tablets debossed with ‘J’ on one side and ‘9’ on other side.
Nebivolol tablets, 10 mg are light peach color, triangular shaped biconvex tablets debossed with ‘J’ on one side and ‘10’ on other side.
Nebivolol tablets, 20 mg are white to off-white, triangular biconvex tablets debossed with ‘J’ on one side and ‘11’ on other side.
