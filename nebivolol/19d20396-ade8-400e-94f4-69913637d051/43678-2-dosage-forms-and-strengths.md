# 3 DOSAGE FORMS & STRENGTHS

Nebivolol is available as tablets for oral administration containing nebivolol hydrochloride equivalent to 2.5, 5, 10, and 20 mg of nebivolol.

Nebivolol tablets are white to off white, round, biconvex, unscored tablet debossed with "C50" (for 2.5 mg), "C51"(for 5 mg), "C52"(for 10 mg), "C53"(for 20 mg) on one side and plain on the other side.
