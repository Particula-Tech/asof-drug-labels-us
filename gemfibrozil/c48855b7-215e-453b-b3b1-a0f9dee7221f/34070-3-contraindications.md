# CONTRAINDICATIONS

1. 1.Hepatic or severe renal dysfunction, including primary biliary cirrhosis.
2. 2.Preexisting gallbladder disease (see WARNINGS).
3. 3.Hypersensitivity to gemfibrozil.
4. 4.Combination therapy of gemfibrozil with simvastatin (see WARNINGS and PRECAUTIONS).
5. 5.Combination therapy of gemfibrozil with repaglinide (see PRECAUTIONS).
6. 6.Combination therapy of gemfibrozil with dasabuvir (see PRECAUTIONS).
7. 7.Combination therapy of gemfibrozil with selexipag (see PRECAUTIONS).
