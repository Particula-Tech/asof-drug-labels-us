# OVERDOSAGE

There have been reported cases of overdosage with gemfibrozil.
In one case, a 7-year-old child recovered after ingesting up to 9 grams of gemfibrozil.
Symptoms reported with overdosage were abdominal cramps, abnormal liver function tests, diarrhea, increased CPK, joint and muscle pain, nausea and vomiting.
Symptomatic supportive measures should be taken, should an overdose occur.
