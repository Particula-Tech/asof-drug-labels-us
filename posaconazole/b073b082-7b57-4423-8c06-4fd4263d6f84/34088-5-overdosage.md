# 10. OVERDOSAGE

During the clinical trials, some patients received posaconazole up to 1600 mg/day with no adverse reactions noted that were different from the lower doses.
In addition, accidental overdose was noted in one patient who took 1200 mg BID for 3 days.
No related adverse reactions were noted by the investigator.

Posaconazole is not removed by hemodialysis.
