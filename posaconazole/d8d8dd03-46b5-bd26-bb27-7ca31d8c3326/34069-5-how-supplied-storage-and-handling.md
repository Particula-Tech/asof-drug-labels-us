# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Posaconazole delayed-release tablets, 100 mg are available as brown, capsule shaped, bevelled edge, film-coated tablet, debossed with 470 on one side and plain on other side and free from physical defects.

Bottles of 60 NDC 43598-470-60

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
