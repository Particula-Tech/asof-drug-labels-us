# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

## 1.1 Prophylaxis of Invasive Aspergillus and Candida Infections

Posaconazole delayed-release tablets are indicated for prophylaxis of invasive Aspergillus and Candida infections in patients who are at high risk of developing these infections due to being severely immunocompromised, such as hematopoietic stem cell transplant (HSCT) recipients with graft-versus-host disease (GVHD) or those with hematologic malignancies with prolonged neutropenia from chemotherapy.

Posaconazole delayed-release tablets are indicated in patients 13 years of age and older.
