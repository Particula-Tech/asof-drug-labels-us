# 13 NONCLINICAL TOXICOLOGY

## 13.1 Carcinogenesis, Mutagenesis, Impairment of Fertility

Subcutaneous administration of up to 100 mg/kg of tobramycin (3.2x an adult human dose of 5 mg/kg/day based on body surface area) did not affect mating behavior or cause impairment of fertility in male or female rats.
