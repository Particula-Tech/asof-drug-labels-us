# INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Tobramycin Ophthalmic Solution is a topical antibiotic indicated in the treatment of external infections of the eye and its adnexa caused by susceptible bacteria.
Appropriate monitoring of bacterial response to topical antibiotic therapy should accompany the use of Tobramycin Ophthalmic Solution.
Clinical studies have shown tobramycin to be safe and effective for use in children.
