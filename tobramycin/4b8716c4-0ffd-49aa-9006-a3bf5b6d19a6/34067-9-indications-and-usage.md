# INDICATIONS AND USAGE

TOBREX (tobramycin ophthalmic solution) 0.3% is a topical antibiotic indicated in the treatment of external infections of the eye and its adnexa caused by susceptible bacteria.
Appropriate monitoring of bacterial response to topical antibiotic therapy should accompany the use of TOBREX.
Clinical studies have shown tobramycin to be safe and effective for use in children.
