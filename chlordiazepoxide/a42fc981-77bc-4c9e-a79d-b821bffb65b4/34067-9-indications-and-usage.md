# INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Chlordiazepoxide Hydrochloride and Clidinium Bromide is indicated to control emotional and somatic factors in gastrointestinal disorders.
Chlordiazepoxide Hydrochloride and Clidinium Bromide may also be used as adjunctive therapy in the treatment of peptic ulcer and in the treatment of the irritable bowel syndrome (irritable colon, spastic colon, mucous colitis) and acute enterocolitis.
