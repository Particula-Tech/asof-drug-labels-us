# HOW SUPPLIED

Chlordiazepoxide and Amitriptyline Hydrochloride Tablets, USP are available containing 5 mg of chlordiazepoxide, and 12.5 mg of amitriptyline (as the hydrochloride salt) or 10 mg of chlordiazepoxide, and 25 mg of amitriptyline (as the hydrochloride salt).

The 5 mg/12.5 mg tablets are green, film-coated, round, biconvex, beveled edge tablets debossed with MYLAN on one side of the tablet and 211 on the other side.
They are available as follows:

NDC 0378-0211-01 bottles of 100 tablets NDC 0378-0211-05 bottles of 500 tablets

The 10 mg/25 mg tablets are white, film-coated, round, biconvex, beveled edge tablets debossed with MYLAN on one side of the tablet and 277 on the other side.
They are available as follows:

NDC 0378-0277-01 bottles of 100 tablets NDC 0378-0277-05 bottles of 500 tablets

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[See USP for Controlled Room Temperature.]

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP using a child-resistant closure.

PHARMACIST: Dispense a Medication Guide with each prescription.
