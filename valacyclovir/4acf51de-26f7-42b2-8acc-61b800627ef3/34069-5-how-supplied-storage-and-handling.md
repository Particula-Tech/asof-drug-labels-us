# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Valacyclovir Tablets USP, 500 mg are blue, film-coated, capsule shaped tablets with "F 82" on one side and plain on the otherside.
Bottles of 30 NDC 16714-698-01 Bottles of 90 NDC 16714-698-03 Bottles of 100 NDC 16714-698-02 Valacyclovir Tablets USP, 1 gram are blue, film-coated, capsule shaped tablets with a partial scorebar on both sides containing "F" on one side and "8" and "3" on the otherside.

Bottles of 30 NDC 16714-697-01 Bottles of 90 NDC 16714-697-03 Bottles of 100 NDC 16714-697-02 Store at 20o to 25oC (68o to 77oF).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]
Dispense in a well-closed container as defined in the USP.
