# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Valacyclovir tablets are contraindicated in patients who have had a demonstrated clinically significant hypersensitivity reaction [e.g., anaphylaxis, severe cutaneous adverse reactions (SCARs)] to valacyclovir, acyclovir, or any component of the formulation [see Warnings and Precautions (5.4), Adverse Reactions (6.3)].
