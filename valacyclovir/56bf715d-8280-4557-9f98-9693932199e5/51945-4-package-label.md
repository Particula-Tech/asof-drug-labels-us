# PRINCIPAL DISPLAY PANEL – 500 mg

NDC 0378-4275-93

Valacyclovir Tablets, USP 500 mg

Rx only 30 Tablets

Each film-coated tablet contains 500 mg of valacyclovir, on the anhydrous basis, equivalent to 556.2 mg of valacyclovir hydrochloride, USP (hydrous) per tablet.

Usual Dosage: See accompanying prescribing information.

Keep this and all medication out of the reach of children.

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

Do not use if safety seal under cap is broken or missing.
