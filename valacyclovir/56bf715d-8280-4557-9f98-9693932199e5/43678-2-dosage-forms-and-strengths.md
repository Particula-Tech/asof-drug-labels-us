# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Valacyclovir Tablets, USP are available containing valacyclovir hydrochloride, USP equivalent to 500 mg or 1 gram of valacyclovir.

- •The 500 mg tablets are white, film-coated, oval shaped tablets imprinted with M122 in black ink on one side and blank on the other side.
- •The 1 gram tablets are white, film-coated, oval shaped tablets with a partial break line on both sides, imprinted with M123 in black ink on one side of the tablet and blank on the other side.
