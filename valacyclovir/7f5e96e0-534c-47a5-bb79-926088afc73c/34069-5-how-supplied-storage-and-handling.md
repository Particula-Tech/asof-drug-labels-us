# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Valacyclovir Tablets USP, 500 mg are blue, film-coated, capsule shaped tablets, debossed with 'I' on one side and '86' on other side.
They are supplied in Bottle of 30 (NDC 31722-704-30) Bottle of 60 (NDC 31722-704-60) Bottle of 90 (NDC 31722-704-90) Bottle of 100 (NDC 31722-704-01) Bottle of 500 (NDC 31722-704-05) Blister pack of 10x10's (Alu-PVC) (NDC 31722-704-31) Blister pack of 10x10's (Alu-PVC/PVdC) (NDC 31722-704-32) Valacyclovir Tablets USP, 1 gm are white to off-white, film-coated, capsule shaped tablets, debossed with 'I' on one side and '87' on other side with partial scorebar on both sides.
They are supplied in Bottle of 30 (NDC 31722-705-30) Bottle of 60 (NDC 31722-705-60) Bottle of 90 (NDC 31722-705-90) Bottle of 100 (NDC 31722-705-01) Bottle of 500 (NDC 31722-705-05) Storage: Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Dispense in a well-closed container as defined in the USP.
