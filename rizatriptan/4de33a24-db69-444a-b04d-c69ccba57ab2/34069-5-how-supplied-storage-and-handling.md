# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Rizatriptan benzoate orally disintegrating tablets, 5 mg (equivalent to 7.265 mg rizatriptan benzoate USP) are white to off-white, round, flat, beveled-edged, uncoated orally disintegrating tablets engraved with ‘467’ on one side and plain on other side with a peppermint flavor.
Each orally disintegrating tablet is individually packaged in a blister card inside an aluminum pouch (sachet).
They are supplied as follows:

NDC 68462-467-74, 4 x unit of use carton of 3 orally disintegrating tablets (12 tablets total).

NDC 68462-467-06, 6 x unit of use carton of 3 orally disintegrating tablets (18 tablets total).

Rizatriptan benzoate orally disintegrating tablets, 10 mg (equivalent to 14.53 mg rizatriptan benzoate USP) are white to off-white, round, flat, beveled-edged, uncoated orally disintegrating tablets engraved with ‘468’ on one side and plain on other side with a peppermint flavor.
They are packaged as 1 tablet per blister card.
Each orally disintegrating tablet is individually packaged in a blister card inside an aluminum pouch (sachet).
They are supplied as follows:

NDC 68462-468-74, 4 x unit of use carton of 3 orally disintegrating tablets (12 tablets total).

NDC 68462-468-06, 6 x unit of use carton of 3 orally disintegrating tablets (18 tablets total).

## Storage

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP using a child-resistant closure.

PHARMACIST: Dispense a Patient Information Leaflet with each prescription.
