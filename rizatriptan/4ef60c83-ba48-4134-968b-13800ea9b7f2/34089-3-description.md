# 11 DESCRIPTION

Rizatriptan benzoate tablets contain rizatriptan benzoate, a selective 5-hydroxytryptamine1B/1D (5-HT1B/1D) receptor agonist.

Rizatriptan benzoate is described chemically as: N,N-dimethyl-5-(1H-1,2,4-triazol-1-ylmethyl)-1H-indole-3-ethanamine monobenzoate and its structural formula is:

C15H19N5•C7H6O2 M.W.
391.47

The molecular weight of the free base is 269.4.
Rizatriptan benzoate is a white to almost white, crystalline powder that is soluble in water at about 42 mg per mL (expressed as free base) at 25°C.

Rizatriptan benzoate tablets are available for oral administration in strengths of 5 and 10 mg (corresponding to 7.265 mg or 14.53 mg of the benzoate salt, respectively).
Each compressed tablet contains the following inactive ingredients: lactose monohydrate, magnesium stearate, microcrystalline cellulose, pregelatinized corn starch, and sodium starch glycolate.
