# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Rizatriptan benzoate tablets USP are supplied as follows:

5 mg (base) – white to off-white, capsule-shaped tablets, one side of the tablet debossed with the number “93”, the other side debossed with the number “7471”, in unit dose boxes of 18 (3 cards x 6 tablets), NDC 0093-7471-43.

10 mg (base) – white to off-white, capsule-shaped tablets, one side of the tablet debossed with the number “93”, the other side debossed with the number “7472”, in unit dose boxes of 18 (3 cards x 6 tablets), NDC 0093-7472-43.

Storage

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
