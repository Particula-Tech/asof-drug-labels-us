# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Rizatriptan benzoate tablets are supplied as follows:

5 mg (base) – white to off-white, capsule-shaped tablets, one side of the tablet debossed with the number "93", the other side debossed with the number "7471", in unit dose boxes of 18 (6x3).

10 mg (base) – white to off-white, capsule-shaped tablets, one side of the tablet debossed with the number "93", the other side debossed with the number "7472", in unit dose boxes of 18 (6x3).

Storage

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
