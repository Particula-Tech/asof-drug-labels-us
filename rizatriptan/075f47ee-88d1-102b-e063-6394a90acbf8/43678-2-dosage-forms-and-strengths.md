# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Rizatriptan Benzoate Tablets USP

- 5 mg tablets are a pale pink-colored, circular, flat, beveled-edge uncoated tablets debossed with ‘X’ on one side and ‘13’ on other side.
  The tablets may be mottled.
- 10 mg tablets are a pale pink-colored, circular, flat, beveled-edge uncoated tablets debossed with ‘X’ on one side and ‘14’ on other side.
  The tablets may be mottled.
