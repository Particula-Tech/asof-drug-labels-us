# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Paliperidone Extended-Release Tablets are available containing 1.5 mg, 3 mg, 6 mg or 9 mg of paliperidone, USP.

The 1.5 mg tablets are brown, film-coated, round, unscored tablets imprinted with M over PD1 in black ink on one side of the tablet and blank on the other side.
They are available as follows:

NDC 72819-157-03 bottles of 30 tablets

The 3 mg tablets are white, film-coated, round, unscored tablets imprinted with M over PD3 in black ink on one side of the tablet and blank on the other side.
They are available as follows:

NDC 72819-158-03 bottles of 30 tablets

The 6 mg tablets are light beige, film-coated, round, unscored tablets imprinted with M over PD6 in black ink on one side of the tablet and blank on the other side.
They are available as follows:

NDC 72819-159-03 bottles of 30 tablets

The 9 mg tablets are pink, film-coated, round, unscored tablets imprinted with M over PD9 in black ink on one side of the tablet and blank on the other side.
They are available as follows:

NDC 72819-160-03 bottles of 30 tablets

Storage and Handling: Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

Protect from moisture.

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP using a child-resistant closure.

Keep out of reach of children.
