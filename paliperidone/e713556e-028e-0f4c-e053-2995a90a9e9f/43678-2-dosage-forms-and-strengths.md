# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Paliperidone Extended-Release Tablets are available in the following strengths and colors 3 mg(white), 6 mg(light biege) or 9 mg(pink) of paliperidone, USP.

- The 3 mg tablets are white, film-coated, round, unscored tablets imprinted with Mover PD3in black ink on one side of the tablet and blank on the other side.
- The 6 mg tablets are light beige, film-coated, round, unscored tablets imprinted with Mover PD6in black ink on one side of the tablet and blank on the other side.
- The 9 mg tablets are pink, film-coated, round, unscored tablets imprinted with Mover PD9in black ink on one side of the tablet and blank on the other side.
