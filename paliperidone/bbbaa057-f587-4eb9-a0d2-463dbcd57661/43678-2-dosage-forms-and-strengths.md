# 3. DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Paliperidone Extended-Release Tablets are available in the following strengths and colors: 1.5 mg (orange-brown), 3 mg (white), 6 mg (beige), and 9 mg (pink).
All tablets are circular shaped, biconvex, beveled edged, coated tablet plain on one side and with either "032", "033", "034", or "035" printed in black ink on other side.
