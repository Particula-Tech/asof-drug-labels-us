# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

INVEGA SUSTENNA ®is contraindicated in patients with a known hypersensitivity to either paliperidone or risperidone, or to any of the excipients in the INVEGA SUSTENNA ®formulation.
Hypersensitivity reactions, including anaphylactic reactions and angioedema, have been reported in patients treated with risperidone and in patients treated with paliperidone.
Paliperidone palmitate is converted to paliperidone, which is a metabolite of risperidone.
