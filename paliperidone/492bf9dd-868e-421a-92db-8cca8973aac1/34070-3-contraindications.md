# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

ERZOFRI is contraindicated in patients with a known hypersensitivity to either paliperidone or risperidone, or to any of the excipients in the ERZOFRI formulation.
Hypersensitivity reactions, including anaphylactic reactions and angioedema, have been reported in patients treated with risperidone and in patients treated with paliperidone [see Adverse Reactions (6.1, 6.2)].
