# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Paliperidone extended-release tablets are available as:

1.5 mg- Light beige to beige film coated, round cylindrical biconvex tablets printed with “15” in black ink.

3 mg- Light pink to pink film coated, round cylindrical biconvex tablets printed with “3” in black ink.

6 mg- Light beige to beige film coated, round cylindrical biconvex tablets printed with “6” in black ink.

9 mg- Light yellow to yellow film coated, round cylindrical biconvex tablets printed with “9” in black ink.
