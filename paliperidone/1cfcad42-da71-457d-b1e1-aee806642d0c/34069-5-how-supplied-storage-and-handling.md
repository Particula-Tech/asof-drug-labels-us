# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Paliperidone extended-release tablets are available in the following strengths

1.5 mg extended-release tablets are light beige to beige film coated, round cylindrical biconvex tablets printed with “15” in black ink.
They are available as follows:

Bottles of 30 NDC 31722-317-30

3 mg extended-release tablets are light pink to pink film coated, round cylindrical biconvex tablets printed with “3” in black ink.
They are available as follows:

Bottles of 30 NDC 31722-318-30

6 mg extended-release tablets are light beige to beige film coated, round cylindrical biconvex tablets printed with “6” in black ink.
They are available as follows:

Bottles of 30 NDC 31722-319-30

9 mg extended-release tablets are light yellow to yellow film coated, round cylindrical biconvex tablets printed with “9” in black ink.
They are available as follows:

Bottles of 30 NDC 31722-320-30

Storage and Handling

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from moisture.

Keep out of reach of children.
