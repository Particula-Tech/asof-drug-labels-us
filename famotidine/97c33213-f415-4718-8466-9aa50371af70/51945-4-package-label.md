Each 50 mL contains: 20 mg famotidine, USP, 450 mg sodium chloride, USP, and 6.8 mg L-aspartic acid. pH may have been adjusted with additional L-aspartic acid and/or sodium hydroxide. pH 5.7 to 6.4
