# HOW SUPPLIED

Famotidine tablets, USP 20 mg are yellow color, round shaped, film-coated tablets debossed with "C119" on one side and plain on the other and are supplied in bottles of 30's, 100's, and 1000's counts.

Bottles of 30 NDC 55111-119-30

Bottles of 100 NDC 55111-119-01

Bottles of 1000 NDC 55111-119-10
