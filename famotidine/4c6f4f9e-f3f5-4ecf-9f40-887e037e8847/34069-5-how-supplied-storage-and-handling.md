# HOW SUPPLIED

Famotidine Tablets USP are available as beige, round, unscored, film-coated tablets, debossed with

and the figure “20” on one side and “5728” on the reverse side containing 20 mg famotidine, packaged in bottles of 100, 500, 1000 tablets and unit dose blisters packaged in cartons of 100.

Famotidine Tablets USP are available as tan, round, unscored, film-coated tablets, debossed with

and the figure “40” on one side and "5729" on the reverse side containing 40 mg famotidine, packaged in bottles of 30, 100, 500, 1000 tablets and unit dose blisters packaged in cartons of 100.

PHARMACIST: Dispense in a well-closed, light-resistant container as defined in the USP.
Use child-resistant closure (as required).

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Avoid storage of famotidine tablets at temperatures above 40°C (104°F).
