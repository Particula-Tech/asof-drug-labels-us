# 10 OVERDOSAGE

Adverse reactions reported in cases of overdosage are similar to the adverse reactions reported with use of the recommended dosage [see Adverse Reactions (6)].
In the event of overdosage, treatment should be symptomatic and supportive.
In the event of overdosage, monitor the patient, and employ supportive therapy.
Due to low binding to plasma proteins, famotidine is eliminated by hemodialysis.
There is limited experience on the usefulness of hemodialysis as a treatment for famotidine overdosage.

Consider contacting the Poison Help line (1-800-222-1222) or a medical toxicologist for additional overdose management recommendations.
