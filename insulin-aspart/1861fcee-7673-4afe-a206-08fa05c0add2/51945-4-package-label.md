NovoLog® Mix 70/30

FlexPen® Prefilled Pen

(insulin aspart protamine and

insulin aspart) injectable suspension

For Single Patient Use Only

100 units/mL (U-100)

5×3 mL prefilled pens

For subcutaneous use.

Shake carefully before using.
