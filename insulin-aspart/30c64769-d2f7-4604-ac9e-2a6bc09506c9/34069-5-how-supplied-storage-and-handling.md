# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Garzulys (insulin aspart-fsan) injection 100 units/mL (U-100) is available as a clear and colorless solution and is supplied as follows:

| Garzulys (insulin aspart-fsan) Injection 100 units/mL (U-100) | Package Size | NDC |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10 mL Multiple-Dose Vial | 1 vial per carton | 71288-904-10 |
| 3 mL prefilled, Single-Patient-Use pen | 5 pens per carton | 71288-903-81 |

*Garzulys Prefilled Pen can be used with disposable needles, such as, NovoFine or NovoFine Plus.

The Garzulys Prefilled Pen dials in 1-unit increments.

Dispense in the original sealed carton with the enclosed Instructions for Use.

Sterile, Nonpyrogenic.
The container closure is not made with natural rubber latex.

## 16.2 Recommended Storage

Dispense in the original sealed carton with the enclosed Instructions for Use.
Store unused Garzulys in a refrigerator between 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F).
Do not freeze Garzulys and do not use Garzulys if it has been frozen.
Do not expose Garzulys to excessive heat or light.
Do not withdraw Garzulys into a syringe and store for later use.

Always remove and discard the needle after each injection from the Garzulys Prefilled Pen and store without a needle attached.

The storage conditions are summarized in the following table:

Table 9.

*For insulin pump use, the total in-use time is 19 days, including 7 days pump in-use time.

Storage in External Insulin Pump:

Change Garzulys in the pump reservoir at least every 7 days or according to the pump user manual instructions for Garzulys or NOVOLOG, whichever is shorter, or after exposure to temperatures that exceed 37°C (98.6°F).

Storage of Diluted Garzulys

Garzulys diluted with Insulin Diluting Medium for Garzulys to a concentration equivalent to U-10 or equivalent to U-50 prepared as indicated under Dosage and Administration (2.2) may remain in patient use at temperatures up to 30°C (86°F) for 28 days.

Storage of Garzulys in Intravenous Infusion Fluids

Infusion bags prepared as indicated under Dosage and Administration (2.2) are stable at room temperature for 24 hours.
Some insulin will be initially adsorbed to the material of the infusion.
