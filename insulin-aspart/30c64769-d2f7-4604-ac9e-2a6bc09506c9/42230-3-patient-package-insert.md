| Do not share your Garzulys prefilled pen with other people, even if the needle has been changed. You may give other people a serious infection, or get a serious infection from them. |  |  |
| What is Garzulys? Garzulys is a man-made insulin that is used to control high blood sugar in adults and children with diabetes mellitus. |  |  |
| Who should not take Garzulys? Do not take Garzulys if you: are having an episode of low blood sugar (hypoglycemia).have an allergy to insulin aspart products or any of the ingredients in Garzulys. |  |  |
| Before taking Garzulys, tell your healthcare provider about all your medical conditions including, if you are: pregnant, planning to become pregnant, or are breastfeeding. Tell your healthcare provider about all the medicines you take, including new prescriptions or over-the-counter medicines, vitamins, or herbal supplements.Before you start taking Garzulys, talk to your healthcare provider about low blood sugar and how to manage it. |  |  |
| How should I take Garzulys? Read the Instructions for Use that come with your Garzulys.Take Garzulys exactly as your healthcare provider tells you to.Garzulys starts acting fast. You should eat a meal within 5 to 10 minutes after you take your dose of Garzulys.Know the type and strength of insulin you take. Do not change the type of insulin you take unless your healthcare provider tells you to. The amount of insulin and the best time for you to take your insulin may need to change if you take different types of insulin.Check your blood sugar levels. Ask your healthcare provider what your blood sugars should be and when you should check your blood sugar levels.Do not reuse or share your needles with other people. You may give other people a serious infection or get a serious infection from them.Garzulys can be injected under the skin (subcutaneously) of your stomach area (abdomen), buttocks, upper legs (thighs) or upper arms, or by continuous infusion under the skin (subcutaneously) through an insulin pump into an area of your body recommended in the instructions that come with your insulin pump.Change (rotate) your injection sites within the area you choose with each dose to reduce your risk of getting lipodystrophy (pits in skin or thickened skin) and localized cutaneous amyloidosis (skin with lumps) at the injection sites. Do not use the exact same spot for each injection.Do not inject where the skin has pits, is thickened, or has lumps.Do not inject where the skin is tender, bruised, scaly or hard, or into scars or damaged skin. |  |  |
| What should I avoid while taking Garzulys? While taking Garzulys do not: Drive or operate heavy machinery, until you know how Garzulys affects you.Drink alcohol or use prescription or over-the-counter medicines that contain alcohol. |  |  |
| What are the possible side effects of Garzulys? Garzulys may cause serious side effects that can lead to death, including: Low blood sugar (hypoglycemia). Signs and symptoms that may indicate low blood sugar include: |  |  |
| dizziness or light-headednesssweatingconfusionheadache | blurred visionslurred speechshakinessfast heart beat | anxiety, irritability, or mood changeshunger |
| Your insulin dose may need to change because of: |  |  |
| change in level of physical activity or exerciseweight gain or loss | increased stressillness | change in diet |
| Other common side effects of Garzulys may include: low potassium in your blood (hypokalemia), reactions at the injection site, itching, rash, serious allergic reactions (whole body reactions), skin thickening or pits at the injection site (lipodystrophy), weight gain, and swelling of your hands and feet. Get emergency medical help if you have: trouble breathing, shortness of breath, fast heartbeat, swelling of your face, tongue, or throat, sweating, extreme drowsiness, dizziness, confusion. These are not all the possible side effects of Garzulys. Call your doctor for medical advice about side effects. You may report side effects to FDA at 1-800-FDA-1088. |  |  |
| General information about the safe and effective use of Garzulys. Medicines are sometimes prescribed for purposes other than those listed in a Patient Information leaflet. You can ask your pharmacist or healthcare provider for information about Garzulys that is written for health professionals. Do not use Garzulys for a condition for which it was not prescribed. Do not give Garzulys to other people, even if they have the same symptoms that you have. It may harm them. |  |  |
