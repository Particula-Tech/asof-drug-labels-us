# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Teriflunomide Tablets are available containing 7 mg or 14 mg of teriflunomide.

The 7 mg tablets are light blue, film-coated, round, unscored tablets debossed with A on one side and 7 on the other side.
They are available as follows:

NDC 0378-0627-93 bottles of 30 tablets

The 14 mg tablets are light yellow, film-coated, round, unscored tablets debossed with A on one side and 14 on the other side.
They are available as follows:

NDC 0378-0628-93 bottles of 30 tablets

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP using a child-resistant closure.

PHARMACIST: Dispense a Medication Guide with each prescription.
