# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Teriflunomide tablets are available as 7 mg and 14 mg tablets.
The 7 mg tablet is light green to green, round shaped film-coated tablet debossed with “N” on one side and with “7” on the other side.
The 14 mg tablet is light blue to blue, round shaped film-coated tablet debossed with “N” on one side and with “14” on the other side.
