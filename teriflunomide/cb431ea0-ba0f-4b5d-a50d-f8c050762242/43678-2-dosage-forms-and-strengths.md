# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Teriflunomide is available as 7 mg and 14 mg tablets.

The 14 mg tablet is a white to off-white, biconvex, round film-coated tablet debossed with ‘G’ on one side and ‘42’ on the other side.
Each tablet contains 14 mg of teriflunomide.

The 7 mg tablet is a light yellow to yellow, biconvex, round film-coated tablet debossed with ‘G’ on one side and ‘43’ on the other side.
Each tablet contains 7 mg of teriflunomide.
