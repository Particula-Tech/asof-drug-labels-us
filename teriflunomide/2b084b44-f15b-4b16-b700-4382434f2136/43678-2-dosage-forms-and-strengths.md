# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Teriflunomide tablets are available as 7 mg and 14 mg tablets.

The 7 mg tablet is very light greenish-bluish grey to pale greenish-blue, hexagonal film-coated tablet debossed with “L” on one side and “597” on other side.
Each tablet contains 7 mg of teriflunomide.

The 14 mg tablet is pale blue to pastel blue, pentagonal film-coated tablet debossed with “L” on one side and “598” on other side.
Each tablet contains 14 mg of teriflunomide.
