# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Rivaroxaban film-coated tablets, USP are available in the strengths and packages listed below:

2.5 mg tablets are yellow, round shape, biconvex coated tablet.
Engraved "R" over "2.5" on one side, plain on the other side.

The tablets are supplied in the packages listed:

NDC 60505-6256-6 Bottle containing 60 tablets

NDC 60505-6256-5 Bottle containing 500 tablets

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Keep out of the reach of children.
