# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Rivaroxaban Tablets, USP are available in the strengths and packages listed below: • 2.5 mg tablets are Light yellow colour, circular, biconvex, film coated tablets debossed with “J 7” on one side and plain on the other side.
The tablets are supplied in the packages listed:

NDC 33342-488-09 Bottle containing 60 tablets NDC 33342-488-57 Bottle containing 180 tablets NDC 33342-488-98 Blister package containing 60 tablets (10 blister cards containing 6 tablets each)

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Keep out of the reach of children.
