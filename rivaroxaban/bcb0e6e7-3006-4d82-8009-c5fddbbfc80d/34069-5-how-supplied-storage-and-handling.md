# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Rivaroxaban tablets, USP are available in the strength and packages listed below:

Rivaroxaban Tablets USP, 2.5 mg are light orange, round, biconvex film-coated tablets with ‘2.5’ on one side and ‘M’ on other side.
The tablets are supplied in the packages listed:

Bottles of 60 NDC 59651-849-60

Bottles of 180 NDC 59651-849-18

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Keep out of the reach of children.
