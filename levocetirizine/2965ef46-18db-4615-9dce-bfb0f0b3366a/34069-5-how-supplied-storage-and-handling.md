# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Levocetirizine dihydrochloride tablets are white to off-white, film-coated, scored, oval, biconvex, debossed with "L9CZ 5" on the unscored side and nothing on the scored side, and contain 5 mg levocetirizine dihydrochloride.
They are supplied in the following HDPE bottle configurations:

Levocetirizine dihydrochloride oral solution is a clear, colorless liquid containing 0.5 mg of levocetirizine dihydrochloride per mL.

Oral solution in 150 mL glass bottles (NDC 45802-680-28)

## Storage:

Store at 20 to 25°C (68 to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15 to 30°C

(59 to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
