# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

## 1.1 Perennial Allergic Rhinitis

XYZAL is indicated for the relief of symptoms associated with perennial allergic rhinitis in children 6 months to 2 years of age.

## 1.2 Chronic Idiopathic Urticaria

XYZAL is indicated for the treatment of the uncomplicated skin manifestations of chronic idiopathic urticaria in adults and children 6 months of age and older.
