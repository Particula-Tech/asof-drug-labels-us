# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Levocetirizine dihydrochloride tablets are white film coated, scored, round shaped, standard concave tablets debossed with ‘161’ on one side and ‘H’ on other side.
They are supplied in Bottles of 30 Tablets (NDC 31722-551-30) Bottles of 90 Tablets (NDC 31722-551-90) Bottles of 180 Tablets (NDC 31722-551-18) Bottles of 500 Tablets (NDC 31722-551-05) Bottles of 1000 Tablets (NDC 31722-551-10) Storage: Store at 20 to 25° C (68 to 77° F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
