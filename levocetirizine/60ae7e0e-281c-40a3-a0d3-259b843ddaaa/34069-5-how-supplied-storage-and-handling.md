# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Levocetirizine dihydrochloride tablets are white, oval, film coated biconvex tablets, one side embossed with ‘G’ breakline ‘G’ and the other side plain and contains 5 mg levocetirizine dihydrochloride.
They are supplied in HDPE bottles.

90 tablets (NDC 68462-346-90)

## Storage

Store at 20-25°C (68-77°F); excursions permitted to 15-30°C (59-86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
