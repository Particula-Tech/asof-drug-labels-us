# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

The use of XYZAL is contraindicated in:

- Patients with known hypersensitivity to levocetirizine or any of the ingredients of XYZAL, or to cetirizine.
  Observed reactions range from urticaria to anaphylaxis [see Adverse Reactions (6.2)].
- Patients with end-stage renal disease (CLCR < 10 mL/min) and patients undergoing hemodialysis.
- Pediatric patients 6 to 11 years of age with impaired renal function [see Use in Specific Populations (8.4)].
