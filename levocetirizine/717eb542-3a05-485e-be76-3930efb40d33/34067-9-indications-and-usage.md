# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

## 1.1 Perennial Allergic Rhinitis

Levocetirizine dihydrochloride tablets are indicated for the relief of symptoms associated with perennial allergic rhinitis in children 6 years of age and older.

## 1.2 Chronic Idiopathic Urticaria

Levocetirizine dihydrochloride tablets

are indicated for the treatment of the uncomplicated skin manifestations of chronic idiopathic urticaria in adults and children 6 years of age and older.
