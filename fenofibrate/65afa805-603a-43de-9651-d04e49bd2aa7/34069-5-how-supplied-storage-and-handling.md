# HOW SUPPLIED

TRIGLIDE (fenofibrate) tablets, are available as a tablet in two strengths:

50 mg round off-white tablets debossed with “FH 50” are available in bottles of 90 tablets (NDC 59630-480-90). in bottles of 30 tablets (NDC 59630-480-30).

160 mg round off-white tablets debossed with “FH 160” are available in bottles of 90 tablets (NDC 59630-485-90). in bottles of 30 tablets (NDC 59630-485-30).

Storage: Store at 20-25°C (68-77°F); excursions permitted between 15-30°C (59-86°F).[See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light and moisture.
