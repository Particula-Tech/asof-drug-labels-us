# CONTRAINDICATIONS

LIPOFEN® is contraindicated in patients who exhibit hypersensitivity to fenofibrate.

LIPOFEN® is contraindicated in patients with hepatic or severe renal dysfunction, including primary biliary cirrhosis, and patients with unexplained persistent liver function abnormality.

LIPOFEN® is contraindicated in patients with preexisting gallbladder disease (see WARNINGS).
