# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

- NDC 59630-480-30: bottles of 30 tablets.
  50 mg, off-white round tablets, debossed "FH 50".
- NDC 59630-485-30: bottles of 30 tablets.
  160 mg, off-white round tablets, debossed "FH 160".

Store at 20°-25°C (68°-77°F); excursions permitted to 15°-30°C (59°-86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light and moisture.
Store tablets only in the moisture protective container.
