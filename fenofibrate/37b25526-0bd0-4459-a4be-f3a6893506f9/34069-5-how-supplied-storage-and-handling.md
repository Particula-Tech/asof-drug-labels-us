# 16. HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Fenofibrate Capsules are available in two strengths:

- 43 mg capsules, imprinted with "43" and a segmented band, on the light green cap and "ANTARA" and "LUPIN" on the white to off-white body, available in bottles of 30 (NDC # 68180-130-06).
- 130 mg capsules, imprinted with "130" and a segmented band, on the dark green cap and "ANTARA" and "LUPIN" on the white body, available in bottles of 30 (NDC # 68180-131-06).

Storage: Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15 to 30°C (59 to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature] in a tightly closed container.
