# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

- Fenofibrate is contraindicated in patients with severe renal dysfunction, including those receiving dialysis [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)]
- Fenofibrate is contraindicated in patients with active liver disease, including those with primary biliary cirrhosis and unexplained persistent liver function abnormalities [see Warnings and Precautions(5.1)]
- Fenofibrate is contraindicated in patients with gallbladder disease [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)]
- Fenofibrate is contraindicated in nursing mothers [see Use in Specific Populations (8.3)]
- Fenofibrate is contraindicated in patients who have a known hypersensitivity to fenofibrate, such as severe skin rashes, including Stevens-Johnson syndrome and toxic epidermal necrolysis.
