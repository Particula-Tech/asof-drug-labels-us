# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Tasigna (nilotinib) is indicated for the treatment of chronic phase and accelerated phase Philadelphia chromosome positive chronic myelogenous leukemia (CML) in adult patients resistant or intolerant to prior therapy that included imatinib.
The effectiveness of Tasigna is based on hematologic and cytogenetic response rates [See Clinical Studies (14)].
There are no controlled trials demonstrating a clinical benefit, such as improvement in disease-related symptoms or increased survival.
