# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

## 1.1 Newly Diagnosed Ph+ CML-CP

Tasigna (nilotinib) is indicated for the treatment of adult patients with newly diagnosed Philadelphia chromosome positive chronic myeloid leukemia (Ph+ CML) in chronic phase.
The effectiveness of Tasigna is based on major molecular response and cytogenetic response rates [see Clinical Studies (14.1)].
The study is ongoing and further data will be required to determine long-term outcome.

## 1.2 Resistant or Intolerant Ph+ CML-CP and CML-AP

Tasigna is indicated for the treatment of chronic phase and accelerated phase Philadelphia chromosome positive chronic myelogenous leukemia (Ph+ CML) in adult patients resistant or intolerant to prior therapy that included imatinib.
The effectiveness of Tasigna is based on hematologic and cytogenetic response rates [see Clinical Studies (14.2)].
