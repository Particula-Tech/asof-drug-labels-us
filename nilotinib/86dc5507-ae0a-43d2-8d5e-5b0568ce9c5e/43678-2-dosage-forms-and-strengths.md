# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

• Nilotinib capsules, 150 mg are opaque pink cap and opaque pink body size “1” hard gelatin capsules imprinted with “H” on cap and “23” on body, filled with slightly yellow to yellowish granular powder.

• Nilotinib capsules, 200 mg are opaque white cap and opaque white body size “0” hard gelatin capsules imprinted with “H” on cap and “24” on body, filled with slightly yellow to yellowish granular powder.
