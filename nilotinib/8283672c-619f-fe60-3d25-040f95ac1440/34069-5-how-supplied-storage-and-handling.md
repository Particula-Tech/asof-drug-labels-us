# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Nilotinib capsules, 50 mg are available for oral administration as hard gelatin capsules with yellow opaque body and light orange opaque cap imprinted “APO N50” in black ink.
They are supplied as follows:

Bottles of 120 NDC 60505-3803-7

Nilotinib capsules, 150 mg are available for oral administration as hard gelatin capsules with red opaque body and red opaque cap imprinted “APO N150” in black ink.
They are supplied as follows:

Bottles of 60 NDC 60505-3801-8 Bottles of 180 NDC 60505-3801-7 Bottles of 500 NDC 60505-3801-5 Carton of 4 blister packs of (4x28) NDC 60505-3801-2 Blisters of 28 capsules NDC 60505-3801-0

Nilotinib capsules, 200 mg are available for oral administration as hard gelatin capsules with yellow opaque body and yellow opaque cap imprinted “APO N200” in maroon ink.
They are supplied as follows:

Bottles of 60 NDC 60505-3802-8 Bottles of 180 NDC 60505-3802-7 Bottles of 500 NDC 60505-3802-5 Carton of 4 blister packs of (4x28) NDC 60505-3802-2 Blisters of 28 capsules NDC 60505-3802-0

Each blister pack contains two blister cards of 14 capsules each, for dosing two in the morning and two in the evening at 12 hour intervals over a 7 day period.

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted from 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
