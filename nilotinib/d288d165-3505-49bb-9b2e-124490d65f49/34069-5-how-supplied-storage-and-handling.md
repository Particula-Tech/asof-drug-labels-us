# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

DANZITEN (nilotinib) 71 mg tablets are pink, coated, oblong tablets, debossed with “N5” on one side and plain on other side.
DANZITEN (nilotinib) 95 mg tablets are yellow, coated, oblong tablets, debossed with “N2” on one side and plain on other side.
DANZITEN (nilotinib) 71 mg and 95 mg tablets are supplied in blister packs.
