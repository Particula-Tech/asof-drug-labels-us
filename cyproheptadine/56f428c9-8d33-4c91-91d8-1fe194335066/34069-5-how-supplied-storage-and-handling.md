# HOW SUPPLIED

Cyproheptadine Hydrochloride Tablets USP, 4 mg are available as white to off- white, round, flat-faced, beveled-edged tablets, debossed “4” on left side, “9” on right side of the scoring on one side and plain on the other side, containing 4 mg of cyproheptadine HCl packaged in bottles of 100 (NDC 50742-190-01) and 1000 (NDC 50742-190-10) tablets.
Tablets have functional scoring.

PHARMACIST: Dispense in a well-closed container as defined in the USP with a child-resistant closure (as required).

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
