# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Timolol Maleate Ophthalmic Solution 0.5% is supplied in white LDPE bottles with 15 mm PP yellow caps and 15 mm LLDPE white dropper tips as follows:

NDC 82260-045-05 5 mL in 10 mL container

NDC 82260-045-25 2.5 mL in 7.5 mL container

Storage:

Store at 15°C to 25°C (59°F to 77°F).
After opening, Timolol Maleate Ophthalmic Solution can be used until the expiration date on the bottle.
