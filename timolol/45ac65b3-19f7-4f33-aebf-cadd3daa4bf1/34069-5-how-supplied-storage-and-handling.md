# HOW SUPPLIED

TIMOPTIC® (timolol maleate ophthalmic solution) is a clear, colorless to light yellow solution.

TIMOPTIC 0.25% timolol equivalent is supplied in a white low density polyethylene (LDPE) bottle with a controlled drop tip and a yellow polypropylene cap as follows:

NDC 24208-812-05: 5 mL in a 7.5 mL capacity bottle

TIMOPTIC 0.5% timolol equivalent is supplied in a white low density polyethylene (LDPE) bottle with a controlled drop tip and a yellow polypropylene cap as follows:

NDC 24208-813-05: 5 mL in a 7.5 mL capacity bottle NDC 24208-813-10: 10 mL in a 10 mL capacity bottle

Storage

Store at 15°C to 25°C (59°F to 77°F).
Protect from freezing.
Protect from light.
After opening, TIMOPTIC can be used until the expiration date on the bottle.
