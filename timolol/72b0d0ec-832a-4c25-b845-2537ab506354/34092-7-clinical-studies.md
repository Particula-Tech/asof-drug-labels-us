# 14 CLINICAL STUDIES

In controlled, double-masked, multicenter clinical studies, Timolol GFS administered once daily was compared to equivalent concentrations of TIMOPTIC* (timolol maleate ophthalmic solution) [Merck and Co., Inc.] administered twice daily.
Timolol GFS once daily was shown to be equally effective in lowering intraocular pressure as the equivalent concentration of TIMOPTIC administered twice daily.

The effect of timolol in lowering intraocular pressure was evident for 24 hours with a single dose of Timolol GFS.
Repeated observations over a three-month study period indicate that the intraocular pressure-lowering effect of Timolol GFS was consistent.
