# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Timolol GFS, 0.25% timolol equivalent and 0.5% timolol equivalent, are both supplied as either a 2.5 mL or 5 mL solution in a 5 mL white polyethylene bottle with a natural polyethylene dropper tip and a yellow polypropylene overcap.
Tamper evidence is provided with a shrink band around the closure and neck area of the DROP-TAINER® package.

- 0.25% 2.5 mL fill NDC 61314-224-25 5 mL fill NDC 61314-224-05
- 0.5% 2.5 mL fill NDC 61314-225-25 5 mL fill NDC 61314-225-05

Storage and Handling

Store at 2º to 25ºC (36º to 77ºF).

Protect from light.
