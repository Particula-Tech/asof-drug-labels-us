# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Timolol GFS, 0.25% timolol equivalent and 0.5% timolol equivalent, are both supplied as either a 2.5 mL or 5 mL solution in a 5 mL white polyethylene bottle with a natural polyethylene dropper tip and a yellow polypropylene overcap.
Tamper evidence is provided with a shrink band around the closure and neck area of the bottle.

• 0.25% 2.5 mL fill NDC 61314-224-25

5 mL fill NDC 61314-224-05

• 0.5% 2.5 mL fill NDC 61314-225-25

5 mL fill NDC 61314-225-05

Storage and Handling

Store at 2° to 25°C (36° to 77°F).

Protect from light.
