# HOW SUPPLIED

Timolol GFS is a colorless to nearly colorless, slightly opalescent, and slightly viscous solution supplied in a DROP-TAINER® package system.

Timolol GFS, 0.25% timolol equivalent and 0.5% timolol equivalent, are both supplied as either a 2.5 mL or 5 mL solution in a 5 mL white polyethylene bottle with a natural polyethylene dropper tip and a yellow polypropylene overcap.
Tamper evidence is provided with a shrink band around the closure and neck area of the package.
