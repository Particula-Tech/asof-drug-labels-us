# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

Instill one drop of Timolol GFS (either 0.25% or 0.5%) in the affected eye(s) once daily.
Shake once before each use.

Timolol GFS may be used alone or in combination with other intraocular pressure lowering medications.
Concomitant topical ophthalmic medications should be administered at least 10 minutes before instilling Timolol GFS.
