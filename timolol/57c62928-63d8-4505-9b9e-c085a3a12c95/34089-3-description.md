# DESCRIPTION

Timolol maleate is a nonselective beta-adrenergic receptor blocking agent.
The chemical name for timolol maleate is (S)-1-[(1,1-dimethylethyl)amino]-3-[[4-(4-morpholinyl)-1,2,5-thiadiazol-3-yl]oxy]-2-propanol (Z)-2-butenedioate (1:1) salt.
It possesses an asymmetric carbon atom in its structure and is provided as the levo isomer.
Its molecular formula is C13H24N4O3S•C4H4O4 and its structural formula is:

Timolol maleate has a molecular weight of 432.50.
It is a white, odorless, crystalline powder which is soluble in water, methanol, and alcohol.

Timolol maleate is supplied as tablets containing 5 mg, 10 mg and 20 mg timolol maleate for oral administration.
Inactive ingredients are: colloidal silicon dioxide, croscarmellose sodium, magnesium stearate, microcrystalline cellulose, pregelatinized starch, sodium lauryl sulfate, FD&C Blue #2 aluminum lake, and D&C Yellow #10 aluminum lake.
