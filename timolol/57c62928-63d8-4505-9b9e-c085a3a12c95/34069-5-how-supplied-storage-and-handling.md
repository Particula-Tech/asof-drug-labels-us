# HOW SUPPLIED

Timolol Maleate Tablets, USP are available containing 5 mg, 10 mg and 20 mg of timolol maleate, USP.

The 5 mg tablets are green, round, flat-faced, unscored tablets debossed with M over 55 on one side of the tablet and blank on the other side.
They are available as follows:

NDC 0378-0055-01 bottles of 100 tablets

The 10 mg tablets are green, round, flat-faced, scored tablets debossed with M above the score and 221 below the score on one side of the tablet and blank on the other side.
They are available as follows:

NDC 0378-0221-01 bottles of 100 tablets

The 20 mg tablets are green, capsule-shaped, scored tablets debossed with M to the left of the score and 715 to the right of the score on one side of the tablet and blank on the other side.
They are available as follows:

NDC 0378-0715-01 bottles of 100 tablets

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

Protect from light.

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP using a child-resistant closure.
