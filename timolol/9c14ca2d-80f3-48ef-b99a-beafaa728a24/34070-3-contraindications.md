# CONTRAINDICATIONS

Timolol Maleate Ophthalmic Solution is contraindicated in patients with (1) bronchial asthma; (2) a history of bronchial asthma; (3) severe chronic obstructive pulmonary disease [see]; (4) sinus bradycardia; (5) second or third degree atrioventricular block; (6) overt cardiac failure [see]; (7) cardiogenic shock; or (8) hypersensitivity to any component of this product.
