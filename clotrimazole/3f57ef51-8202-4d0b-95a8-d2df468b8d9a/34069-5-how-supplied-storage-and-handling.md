# HOW SUPPLIED

Clotrimazole lozenges, white discoid, uncoated tablets are supplied in bottles of 70 and 140.
Each lozenge is debossed with "PAD" over "0107" on one side and plain on the other side.

|  | Strength | NDC Code | Lozenge Identification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bottles of 70: | 10 mg | 0574-0107-70 | PAD 0107 |
| Bottles of 140: | 10 mg | 0574-0107-14 | PAD 0107 |

Store below 86°F (30°C).

Avoid freezing.
