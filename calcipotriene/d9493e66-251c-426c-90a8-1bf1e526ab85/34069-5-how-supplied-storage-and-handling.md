# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Calcipotriene and Betamethasone Dipropionate Ointment is off-white to yellow in color, available in collapsible tubes of:

60 gram (NDC 66993-938-61) 100 gram (NDC 66993-938-65)

## 16.2 Storage

Store Calcipotriene and Betamethasone Dipropionate Ointment between 20°C - 25°C (68°F - 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C - 30°C (59°F - 86°F).
[See USP controlled room temperature.]

## 16.3 Handling

Keep out of reach of children.
