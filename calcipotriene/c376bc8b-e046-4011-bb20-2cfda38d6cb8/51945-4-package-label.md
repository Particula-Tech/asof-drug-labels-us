0.005%

Description: Each gram of SoriluxTM Foam contains 50 mcg/g calcipotriene.
The foam also contains cetyl alcohol, dibasic sodium phosphate, edetate disodium, isopropyl myristate, light mineral oil, polyoxyl 20 cetostearyl ether, propylene glycol, purified water, stearyl alcohol, dl-α-tocopherol, white petrolatum, and is pressurized with a hydrocarbon (propane, n-butane, and isobutane) propellant.

Dosage: Use only as prescribed by your physician.
See package insert for full prescribing information.

Warning: FLAMMABLE, AVOID FIRE, FLAME, OR SMOKING DURING AND IMMEDIATELY FOLLOWING APPLICATION.

Contents under pressure.
Do not puncture and/or incinerate container.
Do not expose containers to heat, and/or store at temperatures above 120°F (49°C).
Do not store in direct sunlight.

Avoid contact with eyes.
Keep out of reach of children.

Do not store in the refrigerator or freezer.
Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15-30°C (59-86°F).

NOT FOR OPHTHALMIC, ORAL, OR INTRAVAGINAL USE.
