# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

SORILUX (calcipotriene) Foam, 0.005%, is supplied as follows:

60 g aluminum can NDC 0145-2130-06

120 g aluminum can NDC 0145-2130-07

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

- Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15–30°C (59–86°F).
- FLAMMABLE.
  AVOID FIRE, FLAME, OR SMOKING DURING AND IMMEDIATELY FOLLOWING APPLICATION.
  Contents under pressure.
  Do not puncture or incinerate.
  Do not expose to heat or store at temperatures above 120°F (49°C).
- Avoid contact with eyes.
- Keep out of reach of children.
