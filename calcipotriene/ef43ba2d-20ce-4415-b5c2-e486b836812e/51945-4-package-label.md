TACLONEX® (calcipotriene 0.005% and betamethasone dipropionate 0.064%) Ointment Rx Only For Topical Use Only Net Wt.
100 g

Each gram contains 52.18 mcg of calcipotriene hydrate (equivalent to 50 mcg of calcipotriene) and 0.643 mg of betamethasone dipropionate (equivalent to 0.5 mcg of betamethasone) in an ointment base of mineral oil, PPG-15 stearyl ether, dl-alpha tocopherol and white petrolatum.

Store Taclonex Ointment between 20 - 25° C (68 - 77° F); excursions permitted between 15 - 30° C (59 - 86° F). Keep out of reach of children.
Usual Dosage: Apply once daily, or as directed by physician.
