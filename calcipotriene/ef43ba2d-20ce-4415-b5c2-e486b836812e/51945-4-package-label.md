# PRINCIPAL DISPLAY PANEL - CARTON 60 G

NDC 50222-227-04

TACLONEX® (calcipotriene and betamethasone dipropionate) Ointment, 0.005%/0.064% Rx Only For Topical Use Only Net Wt.
60 g

Each gram contains 52.18 mcg of calcipotriene hydrate (equivalent to 50 mcg of calcipotriene) and 0.643 mg of betamethasone dipropionate (equivalent to 0.5 mg of betamethasone) in an off-white to yellow paraffin ointment base of mineral oil, polyoxypropylene stearyl ether, all-rac-alpha-tocopherol, butylhydroxytoluene and white petrolatum.

Store Taclonex® Ointment between 20 - 25° C (68 - 77° F); excursions permitted between 15 - 30° C (59 - 86° F). Keep out of reach of children.
Usual Dosage: Apply once daily, or as directed by physician.
