# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Taclonex® Ointment is off-white to yellow in color, available in collapsible tubes of:

60 gram (NDC 50222-227-04)

100 gram (NDC 50222-227-81)

## 16.2 Storage

Store Taclonex®Ointment between 20 - 25° C (68 - 77° F); excursions permitted between 15 - 30° C (59 - 86° F).

[See USP controlled room temperature.]

## 16.3 Handling

Keep out reach of children.
