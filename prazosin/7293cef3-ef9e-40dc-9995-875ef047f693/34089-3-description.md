# DESCRIPTION

Prazosin hydrochloride, a quinazoline derivative, is the first of a new chemical class of antihypertensives.
It is the hydrochloride salt of 1-(4-amino-6,7-dimethoxy-2-quinazolinyl)-4-(2-furoyl) piperazine and its structural formula is:

C19H21N5O4•HCl M.W.
419.87

It is a white, crystalline substance, slightly soluble in water and isotonic saline.

Each capsule for oral administration, contains prazosin hydrochloride, USP equivalent to 1 mg, 2 mg or 5 mg of prazosin.
Inactive ingredients include: anhydrous lactose, magnesium stearate, and pregelatinized starch.
Additional inactive ingredients for the gelatin capsule include: 1 mg (Ivory): D&C Yellow No. 10 and titanium dioxide; 2 mg (Pink): FD&C Blue No. 1, FD&C Red No. 40, D&C Red No. 28, and titanium dioxide; 5 mg (Blue): FD&C Blue No. 1 and titanium dioxide.
