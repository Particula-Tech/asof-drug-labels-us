# HOW SUPPLIED

Prazosin hydrochloride capsules USP are available as follows:

1 mg: An ivory opaque capsule, filled with white powder, imprinted with “93” and “4067” on both cap and body, containing prazosin hydrochloride, USP, equivalent to 1 mg of prazosin, packaged in bottles of 100, 250, and 1000 capsules.

2 mg: A pink opaque capsule, filled with white powder, imprinted with “93” and “4068” on both cap and body, containing prazosin hydrochloride, USP, equivalent to 2 mg of prazosin, packaged in bottles of 100, 250, and 1000 capsules.

5 mg: A blue capsule, filled with white powder, imprinted with “93” and “4069” on both cap and body, containing prazosin hydrochloride, USP, equivalent to 5 mg of prazosin, packaged in bottles of 100, 250, and 500 capsules.

Dispense in a well-closed, light-resistant container as defined in the USP.
Use child-resistant closure.

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light and moisture.
