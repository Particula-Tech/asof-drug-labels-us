# HOW SUPPLIED

Desoximetasone cream USP, 0.05% is supplied in:

15 gram (NDC 51672-1271-1), 30 gram (NDC 51672-1271-2), 60 gram (NDC 51672-1271-3), and 100 gram (NDC 51672-1271-7) tubes.

Desoximetasone cream USP, 0.25% is supplied in:

15 gram (NDC 51672-1270-1), 30 gram (NDC 51672-1270-2), 60 gram (NDC 51672-1270-3), and 100 gram (NDC 51672-1270-7) tubes.

Desoximetasone gel USP, 0.05% is supplied in:

15 gram (NDC 51672-1261-1), 30 gram (NDC 51672-1261-2) and 60 gram (NDC 51672-1261-3) tubes.

Store at controlled room temperature between 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F), excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F).
