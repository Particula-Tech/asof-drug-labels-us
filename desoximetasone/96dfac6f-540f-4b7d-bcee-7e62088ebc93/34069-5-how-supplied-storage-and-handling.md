# HOW SUPPLIED

Topicort® (desoximetasone ointment USP) 0.05% is supplied in:

15 gram tubes (NDC 51672-5263-1) and 60 gram tubes (NDC 51672-5263-3).

Store at controlled room temperature between 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F), excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F).
