# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Deferiprone Tablets (three times a day), 1,000 mg with functional scoring

Deferiprone Tablets (three times a day) are white to off-white, capsule shaped film coated scored tablets, with debossed bisect separating "A" and "67'' on one side and plain on other side.
They are provided in HDPE bottles.

1,000 mg film-coated tablets, 50 tablets NDC 75907-348-50

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Keep the bottle tightly closed to protect from moisture.
