# 10 OVERDOSAGE

No cases of acute overdose have been reported.
There is no specific antidote to deferiprone tablets overdose.

Neurological disorders such as cerebellar symptoms, diplopia, lateral nystagmus, psychomotor slowdown, hand movements and axial hypotonia have been observed in children treated with 2.5 to 3 times the recommended dose for more than one year.
The neurological disorders progressively regressed after deferiprone discontinuation.
