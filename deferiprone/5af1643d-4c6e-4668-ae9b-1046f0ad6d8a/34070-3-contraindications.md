# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Deferiprone is contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity to deferiprone or to any of the excipients in the formulations.
The following reactions have been reported in association with the administration of deferiprone: Henoch-Schönlein purpura; urticaria; and periorbital edema with skin rash [see Adverse Reactions (6.2)].
