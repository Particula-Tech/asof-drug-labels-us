# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Deferiprone Tablets:

500 mg, white to off-white, modified capsule shaped, biconvex, film coated tablet, debossed with 54 [score] 422 on one side and plain on the other side.
They are provided in a 100 count and 300 count HDPE bottle with a child-resistant cap.

NDC 0054-0576-25: Bottle of 100 Tablets

NDC 0054-0576-28: Bottle of 300 Tablets

Storage:

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
