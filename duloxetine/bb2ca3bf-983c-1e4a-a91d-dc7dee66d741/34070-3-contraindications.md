# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Duloxetine Delayed-Release Capsules are contraindicated in patients

- who are using or within 14 days of stopping an MAOI intended to treat psychiatric disorders because of an increased risk of serotonin syndrome.
  Allow at least 5 days after stopping Duloxetine Delayed-Release Capsules prior to initiation of an MAOI to treat psychiatric disorders.
  [see Dosage and Administration (2.7), Warnings and Precautions (5.4)].
- who are using other MAOIs such as linezolid or intravenous methylene blue because of an increased risk of serotonin syndrome [see Dosage and Administration (2.8), Warnings and Precautions (5.4)].
