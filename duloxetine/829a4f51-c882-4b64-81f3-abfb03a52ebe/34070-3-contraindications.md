# 4. CONTRAINDICATIONS

## 4.1 Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors (MAOIs)

The use of MAOIs intended to treat psychiatric disorders with duloxetine or within 5 days of stopping treatment with duloxetine is contraindicated because of an increased risk of serotonin syndrome.
The use of duloxetine within 14 days of stopping an MAOI intended to treat psychiatric disorders is also contraindicated [see DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION (2.5) and WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS (5.4)].

Starting duloxetine in a patient who is being treated with MAOIs such as linezolid or intravenous methylene blue is also contraindicated because of an increased risk of serotonin syndrome [see DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION (2.6) and WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS (5.4)].

## 4.2 Uncontrolled Narrow-Angle Glaucoma

In clinical trials, duloxetine use was associated with an increased risk of mydriasis; therefore, its use should be avoided in patients with uncontrolled narrow-angle glaucoma [see WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS (5.13)].
