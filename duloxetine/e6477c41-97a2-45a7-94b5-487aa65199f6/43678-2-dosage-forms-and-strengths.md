# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Duloxetine is available as delayed release capsules:

20 mg opaque green capsules printed “Dulox” on cap and “20 mg” on body in black ink.

30 mg white and blue capsules printed “Dulox” on cap and “30 mg” on body in black ink.

60 mg green and blue capsules printed “Dulox” on cap and “60 mg” on body in white ink.
