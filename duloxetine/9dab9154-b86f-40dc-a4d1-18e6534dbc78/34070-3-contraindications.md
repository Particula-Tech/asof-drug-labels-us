# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

## 4.1 Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors (MAOIs)

The use of MAOIs intended to treat psychiatric disorders with duloxetine delayed-release or within 5 days of stopping treatment with duloxetine delayed-release is contraindicated because of an increased risk of serotonin syndrome.
The use of duloxetine delayed-release within 14 days of stopping an MAOI intended to treat psychiatric disorders is also contraindicated [see Dosage and Administration (2.5) and Warnings and Precautions (5.4)].

Starting duloxetine delayed-release in a patient who is being treated with MAOIs such as linezolid or intravenous methylene blue is also contraindicated because of an increased risk of serotonin syndrome [see Dosage and Administration (2.6) and Warnings and Precautions (5.4)].

## 4.2 Uncontrolled Narrow-Angle Glaucoma

In clinical trials, duloxetine delayed-release use was associated with an increased risk of mydriasis; therefore, its use should be avoided in patients with uncontrolled narrow-angle glaucoma [see Warnings and Precautions (5.13)].
