# 3. DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Duloxetine delayed-release capsules, USP are available as:

- 20 mg: Green-colored cap printed with "241" and white-colored body printed with "20 mg".
- 30 mg: Blue-colored cap printed with "242"and green-colored body printed with "30 mg".
- 60 mg: Blue-colored cap printed with "243" and white-colored body printed with "60 mg".
