# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

- •Advise patients to take spironolactone oral suspension consistently with respect to food.
- •Patients who receive spironolactone oral suspension should avoid potassium supplements and foods containing high levels of potassium, including salt substitutes.
- •Pregnancy: Advise a pregnant woman of the potential risk to a fetus.
  Advise females of reproductive potential to inform their prescriber of a known or suspected pregnancy [see Use in Specific Populations (8.1)]
