# DESCRIPTION

Spironolactone tablets USP, for oral administration contain 25 mg, 50 mg, or 100 mg of the aldosterone antagonist spironolactone, 17-hydroxy-7α-mercapto-3-oxo-17α-pregn-4-ene-21-carboxylic acid γ-lactone acetate, which has the following structural formula:

Spironolactone is practically insoluble in water, soluble in alcohol, and freely soluble in benzene and in chloroform.

Inactive ingredients include lactose monohydrate, dibasic calcium phosphate, povidone, peppermint oil, purified talc, pregelatinised starch, colloidal anhydrous silica, magnesium stearate, hypromellose, polyethylene glycol 400, titanium dioxide and iron oxide yellow.
In addition, iron oxide red (50 mg and 100 mg tablets) is included in the film coating of specific strengths.
