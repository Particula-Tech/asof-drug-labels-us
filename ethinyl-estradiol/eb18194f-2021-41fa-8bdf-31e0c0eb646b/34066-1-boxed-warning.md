# WARNING: CIGARETTE SMOKING AND SERIOUS CARDIOVASCULAR EVENTS

Cigarette smoking increases the risk of serious cardiovascular events from combination hormonal contraceptive (CHC) use.
This risk increases with age, particularly in females over 35 years of age, and with the number of cigarettes smoked.
For this reason, CHC should not be used by females who are over 35 years of age and smoke.
[See Contraindications (4) and Warnings and Precautions (5.5)].
