# HOW SUPPLIED

Norethindrone Acetate and Ethinyl Estradiol Tablets USP, 1 mg/0.02 mg are packaged in a carton of three pouches (NDC 0378-7280-53) and a carton of five pouches (NDC 0378-7280-98); each pouch contains a blister pack of 21 tablets.
Each blister pack contains 21 white to off-white, round, flat-faced, unscored tablet debossed with 239 on one side and plain on the other side.

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
