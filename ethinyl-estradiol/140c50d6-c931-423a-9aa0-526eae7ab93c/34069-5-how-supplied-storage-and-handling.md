# HOW SUPPLIED:

Nortrel® 1/35 (norethindrone and ethinyl estradiol tablets, USP) (21 Day Regimen) are yellow tablets, round in shape, with a debossed stylized b on one side and 949 on the other side.
Each tablet contains 1mg of norethindrone and 0.035 mg ethinyl estradiol, and are packaged in cartons of 21 tablets each.

Carton of 3 Tablet Dispensers (NDC 0555-9009-42)

Carton of 6 Tablet Dispensers (NDC 0555-9009-57)

Nortrel® 1/35 (norethindrone and ethinyl estradiol tablets, USP) (28 Day Regimen) are packaged in cartons of three and six tablet dispensers.
Each dispenser contains 21 yellow tablets as described under Nortrel® 1/35 (21) Day Regimen and 7 white placebo tablets containing inert ingredients.
(Placebo tablets have a debossed stylized b on one side and 944 on the other side).
(NDC 0555-9010-67) and (NDC 0555-9010-58)

Nortrel® 0.5/35 (norethindrone and ethinyl estradiol tablets, USP) (21 Day Regimen) are light yellow tablets, round in shape, with a debossed stylized b on one side and 941 on the other side.
Each tablet contains 0.5 mg of norethindrone and 0.035 mg ethinyl estradiol, and are packaged in cartons of 21 tablets each.

Carton of 3 Tablet Dispensers (NDC 0555-9007-42)

Carton of 6 Tablet Dispensers (NDC 0555-9007-57)
