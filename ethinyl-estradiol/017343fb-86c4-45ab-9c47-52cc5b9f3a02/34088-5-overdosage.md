# OVERDOSAGE

Overdosage of combination hormonal contraceptives may cause nausea, vomiting, vaginal bleeding, or other menstrual irregularities.
Given the nature and design of NuvaRing® it is unlikely that overdosage will occur.
If NuvaRing® is broken, it does not release a higher dose of hormones.
Serious ill effects have not been reported following acute ingestion of large doses of oral contraceptives by young children.
There are no antidotes and further treatment should be symptomatic.
