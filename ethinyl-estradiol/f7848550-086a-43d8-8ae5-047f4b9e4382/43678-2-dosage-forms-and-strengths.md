# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Xulane® (norelgestromin and ethinyl estradiol transdermal system) is available in one strength of 150 mcg/day norelgestromin (NGMN) and 35 mcg/day ethinyl estradiol (EE).

Xulane® is a 14 cm² peach, transdermal system printed with “Xulane® (norelgestromin and ethinyl estradiol) 150/35 mcg per day” in brown ink.
Each system contains 4.86 mg norelgestromin, USP and 0.53 mg ethinyl estradiol, USP.
