# HOW SUPPLIED

Pilocarpine Hydrochloride Tablets, 5 mg, are white, film coated, debossed round tablets, coded SAL 5.
Each tablet contains 5 mg pilocarpine hydrochloride.
They are supplied as follows:

NDC 0228-2801-11 bottles of 100 with a child-resistant closure.

Store up to 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° - 30°C (59° - 86°F).

Pilocarpine Hydrochloride Tablets, 7.5 mg, are blue, film coated, debossed round tablets, coded SAL 7.5.
Each tablet contains 7.5 mg pilocarpine hydrochloride.
They are supplied as follows:

NDC 0228-2837-11 bottles of 100 with a child-resistant closure.

Store up to 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° - 30°C (59° - 86°F).
