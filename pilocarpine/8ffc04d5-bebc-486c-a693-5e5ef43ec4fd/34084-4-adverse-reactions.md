# ADVERSE REACTIONS

The following adverse experiences associated with pilocarpine therapy have been reported: lacrimation, burning or discomfort, temporal or periorbital headache, ciliary spasm, conjunctival vascular congestion, superficial keratitis and induced myopia.
Systemic reactions following topical administration are extremely rare, but occasional patients are peculiarly sensitive to develop sweating and gastrointestinal overactivity following suggested dosage and administration.
Ocular reactions usually occur during initiation of therapy and often will not persist with continued therapy.
Reduced visual acuity in poor illumination is frequently experienced in older individuals and in those with lens opacity.
A subtle corneal granularity was observed in about 10% of patients treated with PILOPINE HS®.
Cases of retinal detachment have been reported during treatment with miotic agents; especially in young myopic patients.
Lens opacity may occur with prolonged use of pilocarpine.
