# HOW SUPPLIED

Pilocarpine hydrochloride tablets, 5 mg are white, film coated, round tablets, debossed LAN on one side and 1313 on the other side.
Each tablet contains 5 mg pilocarpine hydrochloride.
They are supplied as follows:

Bottles of 100; NDC 0527-1313-01

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
