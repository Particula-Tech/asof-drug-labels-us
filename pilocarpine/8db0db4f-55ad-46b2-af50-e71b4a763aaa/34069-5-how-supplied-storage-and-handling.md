# HOW SUPPLIED

Pilocarpine hydrochloride tablets, 5 mg are white, film coated, round tablets, debossed LAN on one side and 1313 on the other side.
Each tablet contains 5 mg pilocarpine hydrochloride.
They are supplied as follows:

Bottles of 100; NDC 0527-1313-01

Store at 20°- 25°C(68°-77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Pilocarpine hydrochloride tablets, 7.5 mg are blue, film coated, round tablets, debossed LCI on one side and 1407 on the other side.
Each tablet contains 7.5 mg pilocarpine hydrochloride.
They are supplied as follows:

Bottles of 100; NDC 0527-1407-01

Store at 20°- 25°C(68°-77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
