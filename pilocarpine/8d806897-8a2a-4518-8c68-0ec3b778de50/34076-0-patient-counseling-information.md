# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

Night Driving

Caution is advised with night driving and when hazardous activities are undertaken in poor illumination.
[see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)]

Accommodative Spasm

Temporary problems when changing focus between near objects and distant objects may occur.
Advise patients not to drive or use machinery if vision is not clear.
[see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)]

When to Seek Physician Advice

Advise patients to seek immediate medical care with sudden onset of vision loss.
[see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)]

Contact Lens Wear

Contact lens should be removed prior to the instillation of VUITY.
Wait 10 minutes after dosing before reinserting contact lenses.
[see Warnings and Precautions (5.4)]

Avoiding Contamination of the Product

Do not touch dropper tip to any surface, as this may contaminate the contents.
[see Warnings and Precautions (5.5)]

Concomitant Topical Ocular Therapy

If more than one topical ophthalmic medication is being used, the medicines must be administered at least 5 minutes apart.
