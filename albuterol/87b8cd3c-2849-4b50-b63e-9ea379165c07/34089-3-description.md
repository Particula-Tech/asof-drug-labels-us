# DESCRIPTION

Albuterol sulfate tablets contain albuterol sulfate, USP, the racemic form of albuterol and a relatively selective beta2-adrenergic bronchodilator.
Albuterol sulfate has the chemical name ±α1-[(tert-butylamino)methyl]-4-hydroxy-m-xylene-α,α'-diol sulfate (2:1)(salt) and the following structural formula:

Albuterol sulfate has a molecular weight of 576.71, and the molecular formula is (C13H21NO3)2←H2SO4.
Albuterol sulfate is a white crystalline powder, soluble in water and slightly soluble in ethanol.

The World Health Organization recommended name for albuterol base is salbutamol.

Each albuterol sulfate tablet, for oral administration contains 2 or 4 mg of albuterol as 2.4 or 4.8 mg, respectively, of albuterol sulfate.
Each tablet also contains the inactive ingredients anhydrous lactose, magnesium stearate, pregelatinized starch (corn), and sodium starch glycolate.
