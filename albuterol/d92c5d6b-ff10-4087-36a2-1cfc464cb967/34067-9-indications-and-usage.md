# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

## 1.1 Bronchospasm

VENTOLIN HFA is indicated for the treatment or prevention of bronchospasm in patients aged 4 years and older with reversible obstructive airway disease.

## 1.2 Exercise-Induced Bronchospasm

VENTOLIN HFA is indicated for the prevention of exercise-induced bronchospasm in patients aged 4 years and older.
