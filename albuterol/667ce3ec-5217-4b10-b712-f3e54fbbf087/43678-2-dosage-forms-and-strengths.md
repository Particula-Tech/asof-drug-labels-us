# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Inhalation Powder: ProAir Digihaler is a multi-dose breath-actuated dry powder inhaler that meters 117 mcg of albuterol sulfate (equivalent to 97 mcg of albuterol base) from the device reservoir and delivers 108 mcg of albuterol sulfate (equivalent to 90 mcg of albuterol base) from the mouth piece per actuation.
Each inhaler is supplied for 200 inhalation doses.
ProAir Digihaler inhalation powder is supplied as a white dry powder inhaler with a red cap in a sealed foil pouch.
ProAir Digihaler includes a built-in electronic module [see How Supplied/Storage and Handling (16)].
