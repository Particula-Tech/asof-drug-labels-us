# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Inhalation Powder: multi-dose breath-actuated dry powder inhaler that delivers 108 mcg of albuterol sulfate (equivalent to 90 mcg of albuterol base) from the mouth piece per actuation.
Each inhaler is supplied for 200 or 30 inhalations.
