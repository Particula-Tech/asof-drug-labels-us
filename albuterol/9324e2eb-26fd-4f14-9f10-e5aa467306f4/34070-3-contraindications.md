# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

PROAIR RESPICLICK is contraindicated in patients with a history of hypersensitivity to albuterol and/or severe hypersensitivity to milk proteins.
Rare cases of hypersensitivity reactions, including urticaria, angioedema, and rash have been reported after the use of albuterol sulfate.
There have been reports of anaphylactic reactions in patients using inhalation therapies containing lactose [see Warnings and Precautions (5.6)].
