# HOW SUPPLIED

Albuterol Sulfate Inhalation Solution, 0.042% is supplied as a prediluted 3 mL, clear, colorless, sterile, preservative-free, aqueous solution, containing 1.25 mg of albuterol (equivalent to 1.5 mg albuterol sulfate per 3 mL), in unit-dose low-density polyethylene (LDPE) vials.
Each unit-dose LDPE vial is protected in a foil pouch.

NDC 0487-9904-25 25 vials, each in an individual foil pouch.

NDC 0487-9904-01 30 vials, each in an individual foil pouch.

NDC 0487-9904-02 30 vials, each in an individual foil pouch, robot ready.

## Storage and Handling

Store between 2°C and 25°C (36°F - 77°F).

Protect from light and excessive heat.

Store unit-dose vial in protective foil pouch at all times.
Once removed from the foil pouch, use the vial within one week.
Discard the vial if the solution is not colorless.

Keep out of reach of children.
