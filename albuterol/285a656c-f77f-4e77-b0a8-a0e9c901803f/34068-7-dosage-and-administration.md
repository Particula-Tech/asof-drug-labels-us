# DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

The usual starting dosage for patients 2 to 12 years of age is 1.25 mg of albuterol administered 3 or 4 times daily, as needed, by nebulization.
More frequent administration is not recommended.

To administer 1.25 mg of albuterol, use the entire contents of one unit-dose vial (3 mL of Albuterol Sulfate Inhalation Solution, 0.042%) by nebulization.
Adjust nebulizer flow rate to deliver Albuterol Sulfate Inhalation Solution, 0.042% over 5 to 15 minutes.

The use of Albuterol Sulfate Inhalation Solution, 0.042% can be continued as medically indicated to control recurring bouts of bronchospasm.
During this time most patients gain optimum benefit from regular use of the inhalation solution.

Patients 6 to 12 years of age with more severe asthma (baseline FEV1 less than 60% predicted), weight >40 kg, or patients 11 to 12 years of age may achieve a better initial response with the 1.25 mg dose.

Albuterol Sulfate Inhalation Solution, 0.042% has not been studied in the setting of acute attacks of bronchospasm.
A 2.5 mg dose of albuterol provided by a higher concentration product (2.5 mg per 3 mL) may be more appropriate for treating acute exacerbations, particularly in children 6 years old and above.

If a previously effective dosage regimen fails to provide the usual relief, medical advice should be sought immediately, as this is often a sign of seriously worsening asthma which would require reassessment of therapy.

The drug compatibility (physical and chemical), clinical efficacy and safety of Albuterol Sulfate Inhalation Solution, 0.042%, when mixed with other drugs in a nebulizer have not been established.

The safety and efficacy of Albuterol Sulfate Inhalation Solution, 0.042% have been established in clinical trials when administered using the Pari LC Plus™ nebulizer and Pari PRONEB™ compressor.
The safety and efficacy of Albuterol Sulfate Inhalation Solution, 0.042% when administered with other nebulizer systems have not been established.

Albuterol Sulfate Inhalation Solution, 0.042% should be administered via jet nebulizer connected to an air compressor with adequate air flow, equipped with a mouthpiece or suitable face mask.
