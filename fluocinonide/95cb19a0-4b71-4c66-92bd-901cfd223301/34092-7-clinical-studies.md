# CLINICAL STUDIES

Two adequate and well-controlled efficacy and safety studies of VANOS Cream have been completed, one in adult patients with plaque-type psoriasis (Table 1), and one in adult patients with atopic dermatitis (Table 2).
In each of these studies, patients with between 2% and 10% body surface area involvement at Baseline treated all affected areas either once daily or twice daily with VANOS Cream for 14 consecutive days.
The primary measure of efficacy was the proportion of patients whose condition was cleared or almost cleared at the end of treatment.
The results of these studies are presented in the tables below as percent and number of patients achieving treatment success at Week 2.

Table 1: Plaque-Type Psoriasis in Adults

|  | VANOS Cream, once daily (n=107) | Vehicle, once daily (n=54) | VANOS Cream, twice daily (n=107) | Vehicle, twice daily (n=55) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Patients cleared | 0 (0) | 0 (0) | 6 (6%) | 0 (0) |
| Patients achieving treatment successCleared or almost cleared | 19 (18%) | 4 (7%) | 33 (31%) | 3 (5%) |
