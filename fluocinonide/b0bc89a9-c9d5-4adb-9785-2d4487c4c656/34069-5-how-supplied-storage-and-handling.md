# HOW SUPPLIED

FLUOCINONIDE CREAM USP, 0.05% is supplied in 15 g, 30 g, 60 g, and 120 g tubes.
Store at 20°-25°C (68°-77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

FLUOCINONIDE CREAM USP, 0.05% (Emulsified Base) is supplied in 15 g, 30 g, and 60 g tubes.
Store at controlled room temperature.
Avoid excessive heat, above 40°C (104°F).

FLUOCINONIDE GEL USP, 0.05% is supplied in15 g, 30 g and 60 g tubes.
Store at 20°- 25°C (68°- 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

FLUOCINONIDE OINTMENT USP, 0.05% is supplied in 15 g, 30 g and 60 g tubes.
Store at 15°-30°C (59°-86°F) Avoid temperature above 30°C (86°F).
