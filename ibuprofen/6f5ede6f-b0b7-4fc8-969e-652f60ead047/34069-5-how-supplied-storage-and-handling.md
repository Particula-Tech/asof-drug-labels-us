# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

How Supplied

NeoProfen (ibuprofen lysine) Injection is dispensed in single-use vials inert to the complexing of aluminum with ibuprofen, each containing 2 mL of sterile solution (NDC 55292-122-52).
The solution is not buffered and contains no preservatives.
Each milliliter contains 17.1 mg/mL (±)-ibuprofen L-lysine [equivalent to 10 mg/mL (±)-ibuprofen] dissolved in Water for Injection, USP.
NeoProfen is supplied in a carton containing 3 single-use inert vials.

Storage and Handling

Store at 20 – 25°C (68 – 77°F); excursions permitted 15 – 30°C (59 – 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light.
Store the inert vials in carton until contents have been used.
