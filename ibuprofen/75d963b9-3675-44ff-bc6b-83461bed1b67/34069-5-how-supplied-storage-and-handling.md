# HOW SUPPLIED

Ibuprofen Tablets, USP are available in the following strength, color and sizes:

800 mg (capsule-shaped, white, film-coated tablets, debossed with "IP 137" on one side, on other side debossed with "800").

They are available as follows: Bottles of 30: NDC 53746-137-30 Bottles of 50: NDC 53746-137-50 Bottles of 60: NDC 53746-137-60 Bottles of 90: NDC 53746-137-90 Bottles of 100: NDC 53746-137-01 Bottles of 500: NDC 53746-137-05

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Avoid excessive heat 40°C (104°F).
Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP.
