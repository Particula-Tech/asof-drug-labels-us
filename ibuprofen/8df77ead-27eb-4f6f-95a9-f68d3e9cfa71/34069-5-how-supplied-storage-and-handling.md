Ibuprofen Tablets, USP are available in the following strength, color and sizes:

600 mg (oval-shaped, white, film-coated tablets, debossed with "IP 132" on one side, on other side debossed with "600").

They are available as follows:

Bottles of 30: NDC 53746-132-30 Bottles of 50: NDC 53746-132-50 Bottles of 60: NDC 53746-132-60 Bottles of 90: NDC 53746-132-90 Bottles of 100: NDC 53746-132-01 Bottles of 500: NDC 53746-132-05

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Avoid excessive heat 40°C (104°F).
Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP.
