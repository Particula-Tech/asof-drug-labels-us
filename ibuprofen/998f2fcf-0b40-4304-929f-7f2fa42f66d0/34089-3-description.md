# DESCRIPTION

The active ingredient in ibuprofen suspension is ibuprofen, which is a member of the propionic acid group of nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs).
Ibuprofen is a racemic mixture of [+]S- and [-]R-enantiomers.
It is a white to off-white crystalline powder, with a melting point of 74° to 77°C.
It is practically insoluble in water (<0.1 mg/mL), but readily soluble in organic solvents such as ethanol and acetone.
Ibuprofen has a pKa of 4.43±0.03 and an n-octanol/water partition coefficient of 11.7 at pH 7.4.
The chemical name for ibuprofen is (±)-2-(p-isobutylphenyl) propionic acid.
