# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Ranolazine extended-release tablets are supplied as film-coated, oblong-shaped, extended-release tablets in the following strengths:

- 500 mg tablets are peach, with “423” on one side and “SG” on the other side
- 1000 mg tablets are pale yellow, with “424” on one side and “SG” on the other side

Ranolazine extended-release tablets 500 mg are available in: Bottle of 30 Tablets: NDC 50228-423-30 Bottle of 60 Tablets: NDC 50228-423-60 Bottle of 500 Tablets: NDC 50228-423-05

Ranolazine extended-release tablets 1000 mg are available in: Bottle of 30 Tablets: NDC 50228-424-30 Bottle of 60 Tablets: NDC 50228-424-60 Bottle of 500 Tablets: NDC 50228-424-05

Store at 25°C (77°F); with excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]
