# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Ranolazine extended-release tablets are supplied as film-coated, oval-shaped, extended-release tablets in the following strengths:

- 500 mg tablets are orange, debossed with "L31" on one side and "LU" on other side
- 1000 mg tablets are yellow, debossed with "L32" on one side and "LU" on other side
