# OVERDOSAGE:

Excessive topical use of AVAGE® Cream 0.1% may lead to marked redness, peeling, or discomfort (see PRECAUTIONS: General).

AVAGE® Cream 0.1% is not for oral use.
Oral ingestion of the drug may lead to the same adverse effects as those associated with excessive oral intake of Vitamin A (hypervitaminosis A) or other retinoids.
If oral ingestion occurs, the patient should be monitored and appropriate supportive measures should be administered as necessary.
