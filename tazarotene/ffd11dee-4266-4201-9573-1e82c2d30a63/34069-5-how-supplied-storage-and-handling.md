# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Tazarotene Gel is a translucent, aqueous gel, available in concentrations of 0.05% and 0.1%.
It is available in a collapsible aluminum tube with a tamper-evident aluminum membrane over the opening and a white polyethylene screw cap, in 30 g and 100 g sizes.

- Tazarotene Gel 0.05% 30 g NDC 45802-436-94
- Tazarotene Gel 0.05% 100 g NDC 45802-436-01
- Tazarotene Gel 0.1% 30 g NDC 45802-442-94
- Tazarotene Gel 0.1% 100 g NDC 45802-442-01

Storage: Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
Excursions permitted from 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F).
