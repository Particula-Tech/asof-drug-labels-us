# 11 DESCRIPTION

Tazarotene Gel, 0.05% and 0.1% is for topical use and contains the active ingredient, tazarotene.
Each gram of Tazarotene Gel, 0.05% and 0.1% contains 0.5 and 1 mg of tazarotene, respectively in a translucent, aqueous gel.

Tazarotene is a member of the acetylenic class of retinoids.
Chemically, tazarotene is ethyl 6-[(4,4-dimethylthiochroman-6-yl)ethynyl]nicotinate.
The compound has an empirical formula of C21H21NO2S and molecular weight of 351.46.
