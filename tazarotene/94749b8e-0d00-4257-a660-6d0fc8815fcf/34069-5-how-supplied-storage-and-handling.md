# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Tazarotene gel is a clear to yellow translucent, aqueous gel, available in a concentration of 0.1%.
It is available in a collapsible aluminum tube with a tamper-evident aluminum membrane over the opening and a white propylene screw cap, in 30 g and 100 g sizes.

Tazarotene gel 0.1% 30 g NDC 0713-0804-31

Tazarotene gel 0.1% 100 g NDC 0713-0804-99

Storage: Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
Excursions permitted from 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F).
