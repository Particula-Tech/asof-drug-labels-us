# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

## 1.1 Plaque Psoriasis

Tazarotene gel, 0.05% is indicated for the topical treatment of patients with plaque psoriasis of up to 20% body surface area involvement.

## 1.3 Limitations of Use

The safety of tazarotene gel use on more than 20% body surface area has not been established in psoriasis [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1) and Use in Specific Populations (8.1)].
