# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

TAZORAC (tazarotene) Gel is a translucent, aqueous gel, available in concentrations of 0.05% and 0.1%.
It is available in a collapsible aluminum tube with a tamper-evident aluminum membrane over the opening and a white propylene screw cap, in 30 g and 100 g sizes.

TAZORAC Gel 0.05% 30 g NDC 0023-8335-03

TAZORAC Gel 0.05% 100 g NDC 0023-8335-10

TAZORAC Gel 0.1% 30 g NDC 0023-0042-03

TAZORAC Gel 0.1% 100 g NDC 0023-0042-10

Storage: Store at 20ºC to 25ºC (68ºF to 77ºF).
Excursions permitted from 15ºC to 30ºC (59ºF to 86ºF).
