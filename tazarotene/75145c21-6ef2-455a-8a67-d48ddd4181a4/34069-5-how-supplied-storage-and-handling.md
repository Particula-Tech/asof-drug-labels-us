# HOW SUPPLIED

TAZORAC® (tazarotene) Gel is available in concentrations of 0.05% and 0.1%.
It is available in a collapsible aluminum tube with a tamper-evident aluminum membrane over the opening and a white propylene screw cap, in 30 g and 100 g sizes.

|  | TAZORAC® Gel 0.05% | TAZORAC® Gel 0.1% |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 30 g | NDC 0023-8335-03 | NDC 0023-0042-03 |
| 100 g | NDC 0023-8335-10 | NDC 0023-0042-10 |

Storage: Store at 25°C (77°F): excursions permitted to 15-30°C (59-86°F).
