# STORAGE

Nyamyc™ (Nystatin Topical Powder, USP): Store at 20-25°C (68-77°F) and excursions permitted to 15-30°C (59-86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Avoid excessive heat (40°C/104°F).
Keep tightly closed.
Keep out of reach of children.
