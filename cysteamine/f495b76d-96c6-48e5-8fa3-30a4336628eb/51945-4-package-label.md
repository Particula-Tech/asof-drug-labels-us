PRINCIPAL DISPLAY PANEL - 50 mg

NDC 0378-9040-05

CYSTAGON® (cysteamine bitartrate) Capsules 50 mg*

500 CAPSULES (Rx only)

*Each capsule contains: Cysteamine Bitartrate equivalent to 50 mg of cysteamine.

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP using a child-resistant closure.

Keep container tightly closed.

Keep this and all medication out of the reach of children.

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[See USP for Controlled Room Temperature.]

Protect from light and moisture.

Usual Dosage: See prescribing information.

Patient and Parent Information Leaflet provided at end of prescribing information.

This container is not intended for dispensing for household use.
