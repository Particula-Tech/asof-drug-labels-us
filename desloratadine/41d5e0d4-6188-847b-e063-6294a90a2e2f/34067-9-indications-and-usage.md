# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

## 1.1 Seasonal Allergic Rhinitis

Desloratadine oral solution is indicated for the relief of the nasal and non-nasal symptoms of seasonal allergic rhinitis in patients 2 years of age and older.

## 1.2 Perennial Allergic Rhinitis

Desloratadine oral solution is indicated for the relief of the nasal and non-nasal symptoms of perennial allergic rhinitis in patients 6 months of age and older.

## 1.3 Chronic Idiopathic Urticaria

Desloratadine oral solution is indicated for the symptomatic relief of pruritus, reduction in the number of hives, and size of hives, in patients with chronic idiopathic urticaria 6 months of age and older.
