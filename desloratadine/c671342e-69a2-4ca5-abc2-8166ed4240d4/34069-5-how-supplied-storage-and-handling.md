# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

CLARINEX Tablets: Embossed "C5", light blue, film-coated tablets that are packaged in high-density polyethylene plastic bottles of 100 (NDC 78206-119-01).

Storage

- CLARINEX Tablets: Protect Unit-of-Use packaging and Unit-Dose Hospital Pack from excessive moisture.
  Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15–30°C (59–86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
  Heat Sensitive.
  Avoid exposure at or above 30°C (86°F).
