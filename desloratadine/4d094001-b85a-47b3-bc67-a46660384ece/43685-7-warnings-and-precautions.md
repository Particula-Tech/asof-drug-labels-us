# 5 WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS

## 5.1 Hypersensitivity Reactions

Hypersensitivity reactions including rash, pruritus, urticaria, edema, dyspnea, and anaphylaxis have been reported after administration of desloratadine.
If such a reaction occurs, therapy with desloratadine should be stopped and alternative treatment should be considered.
