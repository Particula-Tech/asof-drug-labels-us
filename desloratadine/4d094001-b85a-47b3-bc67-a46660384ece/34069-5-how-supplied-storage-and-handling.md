# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Desloratadine Tablets, USP: Light blue colored, film coated, circular, biconvex tablets debossed with “D” on one side and plain on the other side are supplied as follows:

Bottles of 100 NDC 69452-363-20, with a child-resistant closure.
Bottles of 500 NDC 69452-363-30, with a child-resistant closure.

Storage: Desloratadine Tablets, USP: Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Heat sensitive.
Avoid exposure at or above 30°C (86°F).
Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP using a child-resistant closure.
