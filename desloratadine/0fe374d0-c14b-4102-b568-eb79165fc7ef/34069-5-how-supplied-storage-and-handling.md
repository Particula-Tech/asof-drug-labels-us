# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Desloratadine Tablets: Debossed "5", light blue, round tablets that are packaged in high-density polyethylene plastic bottles of 30 (NDC 69543-107-30), 100 (NDC 69543-107-10), and 500 (NDC 69543-107-50).

Storage:

- Desloratadine Tablets: Store at 25° C (77° F); excursions permitted to 15° C - 30° C (59° F - 86° F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature.]
  Heat sensitive.
  Avoid exposure at or above 30° C (86° F). Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP using a child-resistant closure.
