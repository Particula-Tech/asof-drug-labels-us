# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Desloratadine Tablets: Debossed "5", light blue, round tablets that are packaged in high-density polyethylene plastic bottles of 100 (NDC 69543-107-10) and 500 (NDC 69543-107-50).

Storage

- •Desloratadine Tablets: Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15°-30°C (59°-86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
  Heat sensitive.
  Avoid exposure at or above 30°C (86°F).
  Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP using a child-resistant closure.
