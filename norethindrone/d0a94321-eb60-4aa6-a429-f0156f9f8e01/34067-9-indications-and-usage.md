# INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Norethindrone acetate is indicated for the treatment of secondary amenorrhea, endometriosis, and abnormal uterine bleeding due to hormonal imbalance in the absence of organic pathology, such as submucous fibroids or uterine cancer.
Norethindrone acetate is not intended, recommended or approved to be used with concomitant estrogen therapy in postmenopausal women for endometrial protection.
