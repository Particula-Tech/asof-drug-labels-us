# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Activella is available in two strengths:

- Activella 1 mg/ 0.5 mg contains 1 mg of estradiol and 0.5 mg of norethindrone acetate.
  The tablets are white, round, biconvex, film-coated tablets debossed with “ALH” on the one side and plain on the other side.
- Activella 0.5 mg/ 0.1 mg contains 0.5 mg of estradiol and 0.1 mg of norethindrone acetate.
  The tablets are white, round, biconvex, film-coated tablets debossed with “ALL” on the one side and plain on the other side.
