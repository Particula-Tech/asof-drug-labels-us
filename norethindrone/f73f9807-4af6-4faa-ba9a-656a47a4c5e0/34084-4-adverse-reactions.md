# ADVERSE REACTIONS

See WARNINGS and PRECAUTIONS.

The following adverse reactions have been observed in women taking progestins:

1. •Breakthrough bleeding
2. •Spotting
3. •Change in menstrual flow
4. •Amenorrhea
5. •Edema
6. •Changes in weight (decreases, increases)
7. •Changes in the cervical squamo-columnar junction and cervical secretions
8. •Cholestatic jaundice
9. •Rash (allergic) with and without pruritus
10. •Melasma or chloasma
11. •Clinical depression
12. •Acne
13. •Breast enlargement/tenderness
14. •Headache/migraine
15. •Urticaria
16. •Abnormalities of liver tests (i.e., AST, ALT, Bilirubin)
17. •Decreased HDL cholesterol and increased LDL/HDL ratio
18. •Mood swings
19. •Nausea
20. •Insomnia
21. •Anaphylactic/anaphylactoid reactions
22. •Thrombotic and thromboembolic events (e.g., deep vein thrombosis, pulmonary embolism, retinal vascular thrombosis, cerebral thrombosis and embolism)
23. •Optic neuritis (which may lead to partial or complete loss of vision)
