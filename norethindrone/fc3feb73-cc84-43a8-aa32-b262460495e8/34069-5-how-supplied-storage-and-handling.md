# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

MYFEMBREE film-coated tablets contain relugolix 40 mg, estradiol (E2) 1 mg, and norethindrone acetate (NETA) 0.5 mg.
The tablets are light yellow to yellow, round film-coated tablet with “MVT” on one side and “415” on the other side.

MYFEMBREE is supplied in a white, opaque, high-density polyethylene bottle containing 28 tablets with desiccant and closed with an induction-sealed child-resistant cap (NDC Code 72974-415-01).

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at 15ºC to 30ºC (59ºF to 86ºF).

Dispose unused medication via a take-back option if available.
Otherwise, follow FDA instructions for disposing medication in the household trash, www.fda.gov/drugdisposal.
Do NOT flush down the toilet.
