# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

## 1.1 Heavy Menstrual Bleeding Associated with Uterine Leiomyomas

MYFEMBREE is indicated for the management of heavy menstrual bleeding associated with uterine leiomyomas (fibroids) in premenopausal women [see Clinical Studies (14)].

## 1.2 Moderate to Severe Pain Associated with Endometriosis

MYFEMBREE is indicated for the management of moderate to severe pain associated with endometriosis in premenopausal women [see Clinical Studies (14)].

## 1.3 Limitations of Use

Use of MYFEMBREE should be limited to 24 months due to the risk of continued bone loss that may not be reversible [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2) and Adverse Reactions (6)].
