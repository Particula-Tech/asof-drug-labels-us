# 1. INDICATIONS AND USAGE

MYFEMBREE is indicated for the management of heavy menstrual bleeding associated with uterine leiomyomas (fibroids) in premenopausal women.

Limitations of Use

Use of MYFEMBREE should be limited to 24 months due to the risk of continued bone loss which may not be reversible [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)].
