# OVERDOSAGE

Neither accidental nor intentional overdosing with Ursodiol has been reported.
Doses of Ursodiol in the range of 16-20 mg/kg/day have been tolerated for 6-37 months without symptoms by 7 patients.
The LD50 for ursodiol in rats is over 5000 mg/kg given over 7-10 days and over 7500 mg/kg for mice.
The most likely manifestation of severe overdose with Ursodiol would probably be diarrhea, which should be treated symptomatically.
