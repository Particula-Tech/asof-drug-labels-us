# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

Enteroliths in Patients with Risk for Intestinal Stenosis or Stasis

Advise patients or their caretaker(s) to notify their healthcare provider if they experience obstructive gastrointestinal symptoms due to the risk of enteroliths [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)].

Drug Interactions

Patients should be informed that absorption of URSO 250 and URSO Forte may be reduced if they are taking bile acid sequestering agents, such as cholestyramine and colestipol, aluminum-based antacids, or drugs known to alter the metabolism of cholesterol [see Drug Interactions (7)].
