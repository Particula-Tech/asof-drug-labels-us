# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Ertapenem for injection is supplied as a sterile lyophilized powder in single-dose vials containing ertapenem for intravenous infusion or for intramuscular injection as follows:

No. 051---1 g ertapenem equivalent NDC 44647-051-01 in 20mL of 1 vial.
NDC 44647-051-10 in carton of 10 vials.

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Before reconstitution

Do not store lyophilized powder above 25°C (77°F).

Reconstituted and infusion solutions

The reconstituted solution, immediately diluted in 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection [see Dosage and Administration (2.7)], may be stored at room temperature (25°C) and used within 6 hours or stored for 24 hours under refrigeration (5°C) and used within 4 hours after removal from refrigeration.
Solutions of Ertapenem for injection should not be frozen.
