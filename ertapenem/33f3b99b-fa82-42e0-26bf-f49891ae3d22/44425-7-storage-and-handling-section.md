# STORAGE AND STABILITY

## Before reconstitution

Do not store lyophilized powder above 25°C (77°F).

## Reconstituted and infusion solutions

The reconstituted solution, immediately diluted in 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection (see DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION, PREPARATION OF SOLUTION), may be stored at room temperature (25°C) and used within 6 hours or stored for 24 hours under refrigeration (5°C) and used within 4 hours after removal from refrigeration.
Solutions of INVANZ should not be frozen.
