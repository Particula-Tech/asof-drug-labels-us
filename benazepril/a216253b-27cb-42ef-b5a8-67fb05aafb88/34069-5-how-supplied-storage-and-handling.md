# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Storage: Store at 20º to 25°C (68° to 77ºF); excursions permitted between 15º to 30ºC (59º to 86ºF) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from moisture.

Dispense in a tight container as defined in the USP.
