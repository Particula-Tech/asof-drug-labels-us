# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Benazepril HCl tablets, USP are contraindicated in patients:

- who are hypersensitive to benazapril or to any other ACE inhibitor
- with a history of angioedema with or without previous ACE inhibitor treatment

Benazepril HCl is contraindicated in combination with a neprilysin inhibitor (e.g., sacubitril).
Do not administer benazepril HCl within 36 hours of switching to or from sacubitril/valsartan, a neprilysin inhibitor [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)].

Do not co-administer aliskiren with angiotensin receptor blockers, ACE inhibitors, including benazepril HCl tablets, USP in patients with diabetes [see Drug Interactions (7.4)].
