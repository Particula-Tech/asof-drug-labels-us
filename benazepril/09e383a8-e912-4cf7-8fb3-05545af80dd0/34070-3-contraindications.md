# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Benazepril hydrochloride tablets are contraindicated in patients:

- who are hypersensitive to benazepril or to any other ACE inhibitor
- with a history of angioedema with or without previous ACE inhibitor treatment

Benazepril hydrochloride tablets are contraindicated in combination with a neprilysin inhibitor (e.g., sacubitril).
Do not administer benazepril hydrochloride tablets within 36 hours of switching to or from sacubitril/valsartan, a neprilysin inhibitor [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)].

Do not coadminister aliskiren with angiotensin receptor blockers, ACE inhibitors, including benazepril hydrochloride tablets in patients with diabetes [see Drug Interactions (7.4)].
