# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Benazepril Hydrochloride Tablets USP, 5 mg are light yellow, capsule-shaped, film-coated tablets, debossed with “ANI” on one side and “524” on the other.
The are available in: Bottles of 100 tablets NDC 62559-524-01 Bottles of 500 tablets NDC 62559-524-05

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Protect from moisture.

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP.
