# HOW SUPPLIED

Ceftriaxone for Injection, USP, as sterile powder in Pharmacy Bulk Package bottle containing 10 grams equivalent of ceftriaxone, is available as:

Box of 1 (NDC 0143-9678-01)

## Storage Prior to Reconstitution

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light.
