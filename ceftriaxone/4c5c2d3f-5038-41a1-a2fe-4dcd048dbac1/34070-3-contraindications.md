# CONTRAINDICATIONS

Ceftriaxone Injection, USP is contraindicated in patients with known allergy to the cephalosporin class of antibiotics.
Solutions containing dextrose may be contraindicated in patients with known allergy to corn or corn products.

## Neonates (≤28 days)

Hyperbilirubinemic neonates, especially prematures, should not be treated with Ceftriaxone Injection, USP.
In vitro studies have shown that ceftriaxone can displace bilirubin from its binding to serum albumin and bilirubin encephalopathy can possibly develop in these patients.

Ceftriaxone Injection, USP must not be co-administered with calcium-containing IV solutions, including continuous calcium-containing infusions such as parenteral nutrition, in neonates because of the risk of precipitation of ceftriaxone-calcium salt.
Cases of fatal reactions with ceftriaxone-calcium precipitates in lung and kidneys in neonates have been described.
In some cases the infusion lines and the times of administration of ceftriaxone and calcium-containing solutions differed.

For information regarding all other patients, see WARNINGS.
