# HOW SUPPLIED

Ceftriaxone for injection is supplied as a sterile, white to yellowish crystalline powder in ADD-Vantage® Vials as follows:

ADD-Vantage® Vials containing 1 gm equivalent of ceftriaxone.
Box of 10 (NDC 0409-7333-49).

ADD-Vantage® Vials containing 2 gm equivalent of ceftriaxone.
Box of 10 (NDC 0409-7336-49).

## Storage Prior to Reconstitution

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light.
