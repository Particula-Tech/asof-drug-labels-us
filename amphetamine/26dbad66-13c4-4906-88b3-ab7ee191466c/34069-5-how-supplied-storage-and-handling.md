Bottles of 100 NDC 70010-040-01 10 mg: Light blue, round, biconvex, double scored tablets debossed with "1", "0", "m" and "g" on one side and debossed with "041" on other side.
Bottles of 100 NDC 70010-041-01 Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
