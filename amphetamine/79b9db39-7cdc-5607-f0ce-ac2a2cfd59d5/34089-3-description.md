# DESCRIPTION

Amphetamine Sulfate is a sympathomimetic amino of the amphetamine group.
It is a white, odorless crystalline powder.
It has a slightly bitter taste.
Its solutions are acid to litmus, having a pH of 5 to 8.
It is freely soluble in water, slightly soluble in alcohol and practically insoluble in ether.

Each tablet, for oral administration contains 5 mg or 10 mg of amphetamine sulfate.
Each tablet also contains the following inactive ingredients: colloidal silicon dioxide, crospovidone, silicified microcrystalline cellulose and stearic acid.
The 10 mg tablet also contains FD&C Blue #1 Aluminum Lake.
