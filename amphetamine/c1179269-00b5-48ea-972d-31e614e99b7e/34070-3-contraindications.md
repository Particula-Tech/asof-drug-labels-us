# 4. CONTRAINDICATIONS

ADZENYS XR-ODT is contraindicated:

- In patients known to be hypersensitive to amphetamine, or other components of ADZENYS XR-ODT.
  Hypersensitivity reactions such as angioedema and anaphylactic reactions have been reported in patients treated with other amphetamine products [see Adverse Reactions (6.2)].
- During treatment with MAOIs, and also within 14 days following discontinuation of treatment with a MAOI, because of the risk of hypertensive crisis [see Drug Interactions (7.1)].
