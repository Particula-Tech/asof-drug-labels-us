# CONTRAINDICATIONS

· Known hypersensitivity to amphetamine products. · During or within 14 days following the administration of monoamine oxidase inhibitors (hypertensive crises may result) (see WARNINGS).
Patients with pheochromocytoma or a history of pheochromocytoma.
Serious reactions, including elevated blood pressure and tachyarrhythmia, have been reported in patients with pheochromocytoma or a history of pheochromocytoma who received an amphetamine product.
