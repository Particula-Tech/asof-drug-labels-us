# 4.CONTRAINDICATIONS

DYANAVEL XR is contraindicated:

- In patients known to be hypersensitive to amphetamine, or other components of DYANAVEL XR.
  Hypersensitivity reactions such as angioedema and anaphylactic reactions have been reported in patients treated with other amphetamine products [see Adverse Reactions (6)].
- During treatment with MAOIs, and also within 14 days of following discontinuation of treatment with a MAOI, because of the risk of hypertensive crisis [see Drug Interactions (7)].
