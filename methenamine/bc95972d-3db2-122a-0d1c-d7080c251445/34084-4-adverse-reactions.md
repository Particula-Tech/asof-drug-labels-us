# ADVERSE REACTIONS

Adverse effects of Methenamine Hippurate Tablets have been reported in fewer than 3.5% of patients treated.

These reactions have included the following, in decreasing order of frequency: nausea, vomiting and rarely pruritus, rash, dysuria.

Children have received Methenamine Hippurate Tablets at the recommended dosages as a prophylactic/suppressive regimen after initial treatment of acute episodes of pyuria.
Side effects were encountered in only 1.1% of these children.
