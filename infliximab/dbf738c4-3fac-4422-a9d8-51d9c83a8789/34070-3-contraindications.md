# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

The use of RENFLEXIS at doses >5 mg/kg is contraindicated in patients with moderate or severe heart failure [see Warnings and Precautions (5.5) and Adverse Reactions (6.1)].

RENFLEXIS is contraindicated in patients with a previous severe hypersensitivity reaction to infliximab products or any of the inactive ingredients of RENFLEXIS or any murine proteins [severe hypersensitivity reactions have included anaphylaxis, hypotension, and serum sickness] [see Warnings and Precautions (5.7) and Adverse Reactions (6.1)].
