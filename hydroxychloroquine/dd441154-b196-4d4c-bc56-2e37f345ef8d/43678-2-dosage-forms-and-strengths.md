# 3 DOSAGE FORMS & STRENGTHS

SOVUNA tablets are available in the following strengths:

- 200 mg of hydroxychloroquine sulfate: white to off-white, capsule-shaped, film-coated tablets, debossed with "172" on one side and plain on the other side.

- 300 mg of hydroxychloroquine sulfate: white to off-white, functionally scored, capsule-shaped, film-coated tablets, debossed with "1" on left side of the score and "71" on right side of the score and plain on the other side.
  The scoring allows the tablet to be divided into two equal halves containing 150 mg each.
