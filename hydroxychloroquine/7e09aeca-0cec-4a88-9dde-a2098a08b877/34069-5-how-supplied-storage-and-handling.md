# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Hydroxychloroquine sulfate tablets, USP are white to off white, film coated oval shaped tablets, debossed with “CE”on one side and “173”on other side.

The tablets are available in:

Bottles of 90 tablets (NDC 62135-752-90)

## 16.2 Storage

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP.

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
