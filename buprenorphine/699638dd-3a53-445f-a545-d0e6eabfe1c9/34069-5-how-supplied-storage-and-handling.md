# 16 HOW SUPPLIED / STORAGE AND HANDLING

Buprenorphine sublingual tablets containing 2 mg buprenorphine (as the free base, equivalent to 2.16 mg buprenorphine hydrochloride, USP) are white to off white round convex, tablet debossed with ‘˄’ on one side and plain on other side and are supplied as: B9

Bottles of 30 with child resistant cap……………..NDC 72888-182-30 Bottles of 90 with child resistant cap……………..NDC 72888-182-90

Buprenorphine sublingual tablets containing 8 mg buprenorphine (as the free base, equivalent to 8.64 mg buprenorphine hydrochloride, USP) are white to off white round convex, tablet debossed with ‘˄’ on one side and plain on other side and are supplied as: B10

Bottles of 30 with child resistant cap……………..NDC 72888-183-30 Bottles of 90 with child resistant cap……………..NDC 72888-183-90

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursion permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature.].
Protect from moisture.
Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in USP.

Store Buprenorphine Sublingual Tablets securely and dispose of properly [see Patient Counseling Information (17)].
