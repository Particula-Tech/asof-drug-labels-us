# 11 DESCRIPTION

Buprenorphine Sublingual Tablets are supplied as white, sublingual tablets available in two dosage strengths, 2 mg buprenorphine free base and 8 mg buprenorphine free base.
Each tablet also contains anhydrous citric acid, corn starch, crospovidone, lactose monohydrate, magnesium stearate, mannitol, povidone and sodium citrate.

Chemically, buprenorphine HCl USP is (2S)-2-[17-Cyclopropylmethyl-4,5α-epoxy-3-hydroxy-6-methoxy-6α, 14-etheno-14α-morphinan-7α-yl]-3,3-dimethylbutan-2-ol hydrochloride.

Buprenorphine HCl has the molecular formula C29H41NO4 • HCl and the molecular weight is 504.10.
It is a white or almost white crystalline powder, sparingly soluble in water, freely soluble in methanol, soluble in alcohol and practically insoluble in cyclohexane.
