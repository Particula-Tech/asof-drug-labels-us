# 11 DESCRIPTION

Buprenorphine Sublingual Tablets are supplied as white, sublingual tablets available in two dosage strengths, 2 mg buprenorphine free base and 8 mg buprenorphine free base.
Each tablet also contains citric acid anhydrous, corn starch, crospovidone, lactose monohydrate, magnesium stearate, mannitol, povidone, purified water and sodium citrate.

Chemically, buprenorphine HCl USP is (6R, 7R, 14S)-17-Cyclopropylmethyl-7,8-dihydro-7-[(1S)-1-hydroxy-1,2,2-trimethylpropyl]-6-O-methyl-6,14-ethano-17-normorphine hydrochloride.

Buprenorphine HCl has the molecular formula C29H41NO4 • HCl and the molecular weight is 504.12.
It is a white or almost white crystalline powder, sparingly soluble in water, freely soluble in methanol, soluble in alcohol and practically insoluble in cyclohexane.
