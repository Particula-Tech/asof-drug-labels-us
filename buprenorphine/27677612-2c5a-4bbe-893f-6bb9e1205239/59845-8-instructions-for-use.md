INSTRUCTIONS FOR USE

SUBUTEX (Sub-u-tex) (buprenorphine) Sublingual Tablets, CIII

This “Instructions for Use” contains information on how to correctly take SUBUTEX sublingual tablets.

Important Information You Need to Know Before Taking SUBUTEX sublingual tablets:

- Your healthcare provider should show you how to take SUBUTEX sublingual tablets the right way.

Preparing to take SUBUTEX sublingual tablets:

- Put the tablets under your tongue.
  Let them dissolve completely.
- While SUBUTX sublingual tablet is dissolving, do not chew or swallow the tablet because the medicine will not work as well.
- Talking while the tablet is dissolving can affect how well the medicine in SUBUTEX sublingual tablet is absorbed.
- If you miss a dose of SUBUTEX sublingual tablet, take your medicine when you remember.
  If it is almost time for your next dose, skip the missed dose and take the next dose at your regular time.
  Do not take 2 doses at the same time unless your healthcare provider tells you to.
  If you are not sure about your dosing, call your healthcare provider.
- Do not stop taking SUBUTEX sublingual tablet suddenly.
  You could become sick and have withdrawal symptoms because your body has become used to the medicine.
  Physical dependence is not the same as drug addiction.
  Your healthcare provider can tell you more about the differences between physical dependence and drug addiction.
  To have fewer withdrawal symptoms, ask your healthcare provider how to stop using SUBUTEX sublingual tablet the right way.

If you take too much SUBUTEX sublingual tablet or overdose, call Poison Control or get emergency medical help right away.

Storing SUBUTEX Sublingual tablets:

- Store SUBUTEX sublingual tablets at room temperature between 68°F to 77°F (20°C to 25°C).
- Keep SUBUTEX sublingual tablets in a safe place, out of the sight and reach of children.

Disposing of SUBUTEX Sublingual tablets:

- Dispose of unused SUBUTEX sublingual tablets as soon as you no longer need them.
- Dispose of expired, unwanted or unused SUBUTEX sublingual tablets by promptly flushing down the toilet (if a drug take-back option is not readily available).
  Visit www.fda.gov/drugdisposal for additional information on disposal of unused medicines.

If you need help with disposal of SUBUTEX sublingual tablet, call 1‐877‐782‐6966.
