| IMPORTANT: Keep SUBUTEX sublingual tablet in a secure place away from children. Accidental use by a child is a medical emergency and can result in death. If a child accidentally takes SUBUTEX sublingual tablet, get emergency help or call 911 right away. Tell your healthcare provider if you are living in a household where there are small children. |  |  |  |  |
| What is the most important information I should know about SUBUTEX sublingual tablet? SUBUTEX contains a medicine called buprenorphine. Buprenorphine is an opioid that can cause serious and life-threatening breathing problems, especially if you take or use certain other medicines or drugs. Talk to your healthcare provider about naloxone. Naloxone is a medicine that is available to patients for the emergency treatment of an opioid overdose, including accidental use of SUBUTEX sublingual tablet by a child. If naloxone is given, you must call 911 or get emergency medical help right away to treat an overdose or accidental use of an opioid. SUBUTEX may cause serious and life-threatening breathing problems. Get emergency help right away if you: |  |  |  |  |
| feel faint feel dizzy are confused feel sleepy or uncoordinated |  | have blurred vision have slurred speech are breathing slower than normal cannot think well or clearly |  |  |
| Do not take SUBUTEX with certain medicines. Taking SUBUTEX with other opioid medicines, benzodiazepines, alcohol, or other central nervous system depressants (including street drugs) can cause severe drowsiness, decreased awareness, breathing problems, coma, and death.Do not inject (“shoot-up”) SUBUTEX. Injecting SUBOXONE may cause life-threatening infections and other serious health problems Do not switch from SUBUTEX sublingual tablet to other medicines that contain buprenorphine without talking with your healthcare provider. The amount of buprenorphine in a dose of SUBUTEX sublingual tablet is not the same as in other medicines that contain buprenorphine. Your healthcare provider will prescribe a starting dose of SUBUTEX sublingual tablet that may be different than other buprenorphine containing medicines you may have been taking. Do not stop taking SUBUTEX suddenly. You could become sick and have withdrawal symptoms because your body has become used to the medicine (physical dependence). Physical dependence is not the same as drug addiction. In an emergency, have family members tell emergency department staff that you are physically dependent on an opioid and are being treated with SUBUTEX sublingual tablet. Never give anyone else your SUBUTEX sublingual tablet. They could die from taking it. Selling or giving away SUBUTEX is against the law. Store SUBUTEX sublingual tablet securely, out of sight and reach of children, and in a location not accessible by others, including visitors to the home. |  |  |  |  |
| What is SUBUTEX sublingual tablet? SUBUTEX sublingual tablet is a prescription medicine used to treat opioid addiction in adults and is part of a complete treatment program that also includes counseling and behavioral therapy. |  |  |  |  |
| Who should not take SUBUTEX sublingual tablet? Do not take SUBUTEX sublingual tablet if you are allergic to buprenorphine. |  |  |  |  |
| Before taking SUBUTEX, tell your healthcare provider about all of your medical conditions, including if you have: |  |  |  |  |
| trouble breathing or lung problems a curve in your spine that affects your breathing Addison's disease | an enlarged prostate (men) problems urinating liver, kidney, or gallbladder problems alcoholism |  |  | a head injury or brain problem mental health problems adrenal gland or thyroid gland problems |
| Tell your healthcare provider if you are: pregnant or plan to become pregnant. If you take SUBUTEX while pregnant, your baby may have symptoms of opioid withdrawal at birth that could be life-threatening if not recognized and treated. Talk to your healthcare provider if you are pregnant or plan to become pregnant. Breastfeeding or plan to breastfeed. SUBUTEX can pass into your breast milk and harm your baby. Talk to your healthcare provider about the best way to feed your baby if you take SUBUTEX. Monitor your baby for increased drowsiness and breathing problems if you breastfeed during treatment with SUBUTEX. |  |  |  |  |
| Tell your healthcare provider about all the medicines you take, including prescription and over-the-counter medicines, vitamins, or herbal supplements. |  |  |  |  |
| How should I take SUBUTEX sublingual tablet? Read the Instructions for Use at the end of this Medication Guide for detailed instructions on how to take SUBUTEX Take SUBUTEX sublingual tablet exactly as prescribed by your healthcare provider. Your healthcare provider may change your dose after seeing how it affects you. Do not change your dose unless your healthcare provider tells you to change it. Do not take SUBUTEX sublingual tablet more often than prescribed by your healthcare provider. SUBUTEX is not for occasional or “as needed” use.If you are prescribed a dose of 2 or more SUBUTEX sublingual tablets at the same time: Ask your healthcare provider for instructions on the right way to take SUBUTEX sublingual tablet. Follow the same instructions every time you take a dose of SUBUTEX sublingual tablet. Take the entire SUBUTEX sublingual tablet. Do not cut, chew, or swallow SUBUTEX sublingual tablet, because the medicine will not work as well. If you miss a dose of SUBUTEX, take it as soon as you remember. If it is almost time for your next dose, skip the missed dose and take the next dose at your regular time. Do not take 2 doses at the same time unless your healthcare provider tells you to. If you are not sure about your dosing, call your healthcare provider. Dispose of expired, unwanted, or unused SUBUTEX by promptly flushing down the toilet (if a drug take-back option is not readily available). Visit www.fda.gov/drugdisposal for additional information on disposal of unused medicines. If you take too much SUBUTEX or overdose, call Poison Control or get emergency medical help right away. |  |  |  |  |
| What should I avoid while taking SUBUTEX sublingual tablet? Do not drive, operate heavy machinery, or perform any other dangerous activities until you know how SUBUTEX affects you. Buprenorphine can cause drowsiness and slow reaction times. SUBUTEX can make you sleepy, dizzy, or lightheaded. You should not drink alcohol or take prescription or over-the-counter medicines that contain alcohol while taking SUBUTEX sublingual tablet, because this can lead to loss of consciousness or even death. |  |  |  |  |
| What are the possible side effects of SUBUTEX sublingual tablets? SUBUTEX sublingual tablets can cause serious side effects, including: Trouble breathing. Taking SUBUTEX with other opioid medicines, benzodiazepines, alcohol, or other central nervous system depressants can cause breathing problems that can lead to coma and death. Sleepiness, dizziness, and problems with coordination.Physical dependence or abuse.Liver problems. Call your healthcare provider right away if you notice any of these symptoms: |  |  |  |  |
| your skin or the white part of your eyes turns yellow (jaundice) dark or “tea-colored” urine light colored stools (bowel movements) |  |  | loss of appetite pain, aching, or tenderness on the right side of your stomach area nausea |  |

You may have a rash, hives, swelling of your face, wheezing, low blood pressure, or loss of consciousness. Call your healthcare provider or get emergency help right away. Opioid withdrawal. Call your healthcare provider right away if you get any of these symptoms: |  |  |  |  |
