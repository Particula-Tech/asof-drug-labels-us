# PRINCIPAL DISPLAY PANEL - 75 mcg Film Pouch Box

NDC 59385-021-60

CIII

Belbuca® (buprenorphine hydrochloride) buccal film

Push To Open

75 mcg

60 pouches containing 1 buccal film each

Use entire film.
Do not cut, tear, chew or swallow film.

Keep Belbuca out of sight and reach of children.
Children who accidentally take Belbuca will need emergency medical care.

Dispense the enclosed Medication Guide to each patient.

Rx only
