# HOW SUPPLIED

Guanfacine hydrochloride is available in 2 tablet strengths of guanfacine (as the hydrochloride) as follows:

Guanfacine Hydrochloride Tablets 1 mg, are pink, round tablets, debossed with WATSON 444 and are available in bottles of 100.

Guanfacine Hydrochloride Tablets 2 mg, are peach, round tablets, debossed with WATSON 453 and are available in bottles of 100.

Bottles of 100 tablets are supplied with child-resistant closures.

Store at controlled room temperature 15°-30°C (59°-86°F).

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container.
