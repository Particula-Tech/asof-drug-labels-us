# HOW SUPPLIED

Guanfacine hydrochloride is available in 2 tablet strengths of guanfacine (as the hydrochloride) as follows:

Guanfacine Tablets USP 1 mg, are pink, round tablets, debossed with WATSON 444 and are available in bottles of 100.

Guanfacine Tablets USP 2 mg, are peach, round tablets, debossed with WATSON 453 and are available in bottles of 100.

Bottles of 100 tablets are supplied with child-resistant closures.

Store at 20°-25°C (68°-77°F).
[See USP controlled room temperature.]
Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container.
