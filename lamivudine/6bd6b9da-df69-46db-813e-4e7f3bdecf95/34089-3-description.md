# 11 DESCRIPTION

Lamivudine tablet (HBV) is a synthetic nucleoside analogue with activity against HBV.
The chemical name of lamivudine, USP is 2(1H) - Pyrimidinone, 4-amino-1- [2- (hydroxymethyl)-1,3-oxathio-lan-5-yl], (2R-cis)-.It has a molecular formula of C8H11N3O3S and a molecular weight of 229.26.

Lamivudine USP is a white to an off white solid and soluble in water.
Lamivudine Tablets (HBV) are for oral administration.
Each tablet contains 100 mg of lamivudine, USP and the inactive ingredients crospovidone, isomalt, isopropyl alcohol, magnesium stearate and methylene chloride.
The tablets are coated with Opadry Pink containing hypromellose, iron oxide red, polyethylene glycol, polysorbate 80, titanium dioxide and yellow iron oxide.
