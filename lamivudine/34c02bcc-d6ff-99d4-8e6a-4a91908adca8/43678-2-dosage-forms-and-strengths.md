# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Lamivudine Tablets 150 mg are white to off-white, diamond shaped, biconvex film-coated tablets, engraved “APO” on one side, “LMV” score “150” on the other side.

Lamivudine Tablets 300 mg are gray, diamond shaped, biconvex film-coated tablets, engraved “APO” on one side, “LMV 300” on the other side.
