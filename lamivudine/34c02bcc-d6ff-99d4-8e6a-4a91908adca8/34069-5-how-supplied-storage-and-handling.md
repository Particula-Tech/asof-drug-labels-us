# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Lamivudine tablets, 150 mg are white to off-white, diamond shaped, biconvex film-coated tablets, engraved “APO” on one side, “LMV” score “150” on the other side.

They are supplied as follows:

Bottle of 60 tablets (NDC 60505-3251-6) with child-resistant closure.

Bottle of 1000 tablets (NDC 60505-3251-8)

Unit dose blister of 100 tablets (10x10) (NDC 60505-3251-0)

Lamivudine tablets, 300 mg are grey, diamond shaped, biconvex film-coated tablets, engraved “APO” on one side, “LMV 300” on the other side.
They are supplied as follows:

Bottle of 30 tablets (NDC 60505-3252-3) with child-resistant closure.

Bottle of 1000 tablets (NDC 60505-3252-8)

Unit dose blister of 100 tablets (10x10) (NDC 60505-3252-0)

Recommended Storage

Store lamivudine tablets at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from moisture.
