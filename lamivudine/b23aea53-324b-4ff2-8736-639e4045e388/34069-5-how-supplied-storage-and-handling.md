# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Lamivudine Oral Solution, USP is a clear, colorless to pale yellow, strawberry flavored liquid.
Each mL of the solution contains 10 mg of lamivudine.
Packaged as follows:

Unit-Dose cup 30 mL NDC 68999-706-30

20 Unit-Dose Cups of 30 mL each NDC 68999-706-24.

This product does not require reconstitution.

Recommended Storage:

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
