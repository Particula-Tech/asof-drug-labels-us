# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Lamivudine Tablets USP, 150 mg (Scored) are white to off-white, film-coated, oval shaped tablets, debossed with ‘52’ and ‘Y’ on either side of the score line on one side and plain with a score line on the other side.
Bottle of 60 Tablets NDC 65862-025-60 Carton of 60 Tablets (6 x 10 Unit-dose) NDC 65862-025-10

Lamivudine Tablets USP, 300 mg are white to off-white, film-coated, oval shaped tablets, debossed with ‘C’ on one side and ‘64’ on the other side.

Bottle of 30 Tablets NDC 65862-026-30 Carton of 30 Tablets (3 x 10 Unit-dose) NDC 65862-026-10

Recommended Storage: Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
