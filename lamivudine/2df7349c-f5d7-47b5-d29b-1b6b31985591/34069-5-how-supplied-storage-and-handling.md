# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

EPIVIR-HBV tablets contain 100 mg of lamivudine, are butterscotch-colored, film-coated, biconvex, capsule-shaped tablets imprinted with “GX CG5” on one side.
Packaged as follows:

Bottles of 60 tablets (NDC 0173-0662-00) with child-resistant closure.

Store EPIVIR-HBV tablets at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F and 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

EPIVIR‑HBV oral solution is a clear, colorless to pale yellow, strawberry‑banana-flavored liquid.
Each mL of the solution contains 5 mg of lamivudine.
Packaged as follows:

Plastic bottles of 240 mL (NDC 0173-0663-00) with child-resistant closure.
This product does not require reconstitution.

Store EPIVIR-HBV oral solution at controlled room temperature of 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) (see USP) in tightly closed bottles.
