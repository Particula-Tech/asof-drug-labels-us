# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

- •EPIVIR Scored Tablets

EPIVIR scored tablets contain 150 mg of lamivudine.
The tablets are white, diamond-shaped, scored, film-coated tablets debossed with “GX CJ7” on both sides.

- •EPIVIR Tablets

EPIVIR tablets contain 300 mg of lamivudine.
The tablets are gray, modified diamond-shaped, film-coated, and engraved with “GX EJ7” on one side and plain on the reverse side.

- •EPIVIR Oral Solution

EPIVIR oral solution contains 10 mg of lamivudine per 1 mL.
The solution is a clear, colorless to pale yellow, strawberry-banana flavored liquid.
