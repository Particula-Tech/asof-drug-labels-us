# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

The recommended dosage of EDURANT in patients 12 years of age and older and weighing at least 35 kg is one 25 mg tablet once daily taken orally with a meal [see Use in Specific Populations (8.4) and Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].
EDURANT is not recommended for patients less than 12 years of age.

Rifabutin Co-administration: For patients concomitantly receiving rifabutin, the EDURANT dose should be increased to 50 mg (two tablets of 25 mg each) once daily, taken with a meal.
When rifabutin co-administration is stopped, the EDURANT dose should be decreased to 25 mg once daily, taken with a meal [see Drug Interactions (7), Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].
