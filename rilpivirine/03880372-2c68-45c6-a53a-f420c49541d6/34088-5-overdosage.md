# 10 OVERDOSAGE

There is no specific antidote for overdose with EDURANT or EDURANT PED.
Human experience of overdose with EDURANT or EDURANT PED is limited.
Treatment of overdose with EDURANT or EDURANT PED consists of general supportive measures including monitoring of vital signs and ECG (QT interval) as well as observation of the clinical status of the patient.
It is advisable to contact a poison control center to obtain the latest recommendations for the management of an overdose.
Since rilpivirine is highly bound to plasma protein, dialysis is unlikely to result in significant removal of the active substance.
