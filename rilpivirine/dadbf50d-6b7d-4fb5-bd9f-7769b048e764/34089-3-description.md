# 11 DESCRIPTION

Rilpivirine tablets are a non-nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitor (NNRTI) of human immunodeficiency virus type 1 (HIV-1).

The chemical name for rilpivirine hydrochloride is 4-[[4-[[4-[(E)-2-cyanoethenyl]-2,6-dimethylphenyl]amino]-2-pyrimidinyl]amino]benzonitrile monohydrochloride.
Its molecular formula is C22H18N6 • HCl and its molecular weight is 402.88.

Rilpivirine hydrochloride is a white to off white solid.
It is sparingly soluble in dimethylformamide and practically insoluble in water.

Rilpivirine 25 mg tablets are available as a white to off-white, round, biconvex film coated tablets debossed with ‘5L’ on one side and plain on other side.
Each tablet contains 27.5 mg of rilpivirine hydrochloride, which is equivalent to 25 mg of rilpivirine.
Each rilpivirine 25 mg tablet also contains the inactive ingredients croscarmellose sodium, lactose monohydrate, magnesium stearate, polysorbate 20, povidone K30 and silicified microcrystalline cellulose.
The tablet coating contains hypromellose 2910, polyethylene glycol 8000 and titanium dioxide.
