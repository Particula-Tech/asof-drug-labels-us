# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Rilpivirine Tablets

Rilpivirine 25 mg tablets are supplied as white to off-white, round, biconvex film coated tablets debossed with ‘5L’ on one side and plain on the other side.
Each tablet contains 27.5 mg of rilpivirine hydrochloride, which is equivalent to 25 mg of rilpivirine.

HDPE bottles without Silica gel canister desiccant

Bottles of 30 NDC 42385-999-30

HDPE bottles with Silica gel canister desiccant

Bottles of 30 NDC 42385-999-31

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F).
