# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Each emtricitabine, rilpivirine and tenofovir disoproxil fumarate tablet contains 200 mg of emtricitabine, USP (FTC), 27.5 mg of rilpivirine hydrochloride (equivalent to 25 mg of rilpivirine [RPV]), and 300 mg of tenofovir disoproxil fumarate (TDF, equivalent to 245 mg of tenofovir disoproxil).

The tablets are white to off white, capsule shaped biconvex, film coated tablets, debossed with “LCT” on one side and plain on the other side.
