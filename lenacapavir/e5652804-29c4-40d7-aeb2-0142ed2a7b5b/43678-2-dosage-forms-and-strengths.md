# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

SUNLENCA tablets: Each tablet contains 300 mg of lenacapavir.
The tablets are beige, capsule-shaped, film-coated, and debossed with 'GSI' on one side of the tablet and '62L' on the other side of the tablet.

SUNLENCA injection: Each single-dose vial contains 463.5 mg/1.5 mL (309 mg/mL) of lenacapavir.
The lenacapavir injectable solution is sterile, preservative-free, clear, and yellow with no visible particles.
