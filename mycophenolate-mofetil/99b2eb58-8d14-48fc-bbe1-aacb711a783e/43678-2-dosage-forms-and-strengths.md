# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Mycophenolate mofetil is available in the following dosage forms and strengths:

| Capsules | 250 mg, hard gelatin capsules with opaque blue cap and opaque white body. The cap and body are imprinted with “655” with black ink. |
| --- | --- |
| Tablets | 500 mg, lavender colored, film-coated biconvex tablets with 'SZ' on one side and '327' on the other side |
