# WARNING

Immunosuppression may lead to increased susceptibility to infection and possible development of lymphoma.
Only physicians experienced in immunosuppressive therapy and management of renal, cardiac or hepatic transplant patients should use mycophenolate mofetil.
Patients receiving the drug should be managed in facilities equipped and staffed with adequate laboratory and supportive medical resources.
The physician responsible for maintenance therapy should have complete information requisite for the follow-up of the patient.

Female users of childbearing potential must use contraception.
Use of mycophenolate mofetil during pregnancy is associated with increased risk of pregnancy loss and congenital malformations.
