# INDICATIONS AND USAGE

## Renal, Cardiac, and Hepatic Transplant

Mycophenolate mofetil capsules and mycophenolate mofetil tablets are indicated for the prophylaxis of organ rejection in patients receiving allogeneic renal, cardiac or hepatic transplants.
Mycophenolate mofetil should be used concomitantly with cyclosporine and corticosteroids.
