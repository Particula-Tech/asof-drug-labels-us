# 3 DOSAGE FORMS & STRENGTHS

Mycophenolate mofetil is available in the following dosage forms and strengths:

| Capsules | Blue-brown, two-piece hard gelatin capsules, printed in black with “266” on the brown body. |
| --- | --- |
| Tablets | Lavender-colored caplet shaped biconvex film coated tablets debossed with “265” on one side and plain on the other. |
