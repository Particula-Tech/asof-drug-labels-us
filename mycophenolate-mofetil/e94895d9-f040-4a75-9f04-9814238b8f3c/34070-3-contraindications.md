# CONTRAINDICATIONS

Allergic reactions to Mycophenolate Mofetil Capsules USP and Mycophenolate Mofetil Tablets USP have been observed; therefore, Mycophenolate Mofetil Capsules USP and Mycophenolate Mofetil Tablets USP are contraindicated in patients with a hypersensitivity to mycophenolate mofetil USP, mycophenolic acid or any component of the drug product.
