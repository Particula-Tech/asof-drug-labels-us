# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Mycophenolate Mofetil Capsules USP and Mycophenolate Mofetil Tablets USP are available in the following dosage forms and strengths:

| Capsules | 250 mg capsules are supplied as caramel opaque/blue opaque capsules with product identification “54 848” printed in black ink on the cap and body, containing a white to off-white powder. |
| --- | --- |
| Tablets | 500 mg tablets are supplied as white film coated modified oval biconvex tablets debossed with product identification “54 135” on one side and plain on the other side. |
