# HOW SUPPLIED

Dexamethasone Sodium Phosphate Injection, USP is available in the following package:

10 mg/mL

1 mL DOSETTE vials packaged in 25s (NDC 0641-0367-25)

## Storage

Protect from light: Keep covered in carton until time of use.
Store at 20°-25°C (68°-77°F), excursions permitted to 15°-30°C (59°-86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Avoid freezing.
Do not use if solution is hazy or has a precipitate.
Do not autoclave.
