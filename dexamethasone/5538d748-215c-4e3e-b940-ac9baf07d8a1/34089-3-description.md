# DESCRIPTION

TOBRADEX® (tobramycin and dexamethasone ophthalmic ointment) is a sterile, multiple dose antibiotic and steroid combination for topical ophthalmic use.
The chemical structures for tobramycin and dexamethasone are presented below:

Each gram of TOBRADEX® (tobramycin and dexamethasone ophthalmic ointment) contains: Actives: tobramycin 0.3% (3mg) and dexamethasone 0.1% (1mg).
Preservative: chlorobutanol 0.5%.
Inactives: mineral oil and white petrolatum.
