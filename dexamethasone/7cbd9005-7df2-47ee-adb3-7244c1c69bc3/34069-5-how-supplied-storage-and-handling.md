## Storage

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Sensitive to heat.
Do not autoclave.

Protect from freezing.

Protect from light.

Single dose vials–Store in container until time of use.
Discard unused portion.
