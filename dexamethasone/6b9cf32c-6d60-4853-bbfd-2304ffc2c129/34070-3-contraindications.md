# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

HEMADY is contraindicated in patients with:

- Hypersensitivity to dexamethasone, or any of the excipients.
  Rare instances of anaphylactic reactions have been reported [see Adverse Reactions (‎6), Description (‎11)].
- Systemic fungal infections.
  Corticosteroids may exacerbate systemic fungal infections [see Warnings and Precautions (‎5.2)].
