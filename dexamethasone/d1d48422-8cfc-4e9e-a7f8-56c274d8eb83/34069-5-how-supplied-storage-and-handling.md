# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

TOBRADEX® ST is supplied as a 2.5 mL, 5 mL, or 10 mL suspension in a 4 mL, 8 mL or 10 mL natural polyethylene DROP-TAINER® bottle with a natural polyethylene dispenser tip and a pink polypropylene overcap.
Tamper evidence is provided with a shrink band around the closure and neck area of the bottle.

NDC 0065-0652-25: 2.5 mL

NDC 0065-0652-05: 5 mL

NDC 0065-0652-10: 10 mL

Storage

Store at 2° to 25°C (36° to 77°F).

Protect from light.
