# HOW SUPPLIED

MAXITROL ® (neomycin and polymyxin B sulfates and dexamethasone ophthalmic suspension) is supplied as a sterile ophthalmic suspension in a white, low density polyethylene dispenser with a pink, polypropylene cap as follows:

Storage: Store upright at 8°C to 27°C (46°F to 80°F).
