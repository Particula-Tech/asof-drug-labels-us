# 14 CLINICAL STUDIES

In two randomized, multicenter, double-masked, parallel group, vehicle-controlled trials, patients received DEXTENZA or its vehicle immediately upon completion of cataract surgery.
In both trials, DEXTENZA had a higher incidence of subjects who were pain free at all post-operative days.
Results are shown in Table 1.
