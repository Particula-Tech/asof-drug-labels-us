# DESCRIPTION

DECADRON Registered trademark of MERCK & CO., Inc. (dexamethasone tablets, USP) tablets, for oral administration, are supplied in two potencies, 0.5 mg and 0.75 mg.
Inactive ingredients are calcium phosphate, lactose, magnesium stearate, and starch.
Tablets DECADRON 0.5 mg also contain D&C Yellow 10 and FD&C Yellow 6.
Tablets DECADRON 0.75 mg also contain FD&C Blue 1.
