# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

TOBRADEX ST is supplied as a 2.5 mL or 5 mL suspension in a 4 mL or 8 mL natural polyethylene DROP-TAINER ®bottle with a natural polyethylene dispenser tip and a pink polypropylene overcap.
Tamper evidence is provided with a shrink band around the closure and neck area of the bottle.

| 2.5 mL | NDC 82667-011-00 |
| --- | --- |
| 5 mL | NDC 82667-011-05 |

Storage

Store at 2°C to 25°C (36°F to 77°F).
Protect from light.
After opening, TOBRADEX ST can be used until the expiration date on the bottle.
