# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

16.1 How Supplied

E-Z-CAT DRY (barium sulfate) for suspension is supplied as a white to lightly colored powder (40 % w/w) in a single-dose foil pouch containing 9 grams of barium sulfate.

Provided as: Dispenser Box of 50 x 23 gram foil pouches (NDC 32909-717-01)

16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at USP controlled room temperature 20 to 25°C (68 to 77° F).
