# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

E-Z-CAT DRY is contraindicated in patients with:

- Known or suspected perforation of the gastrointestinal (GI) tract; - Known obstruction of the GI tract; - High risk of GI perforation such as those with a recent prior GI perforation, acute GI hemorrhage or ischemia, toxic megacolon, severe ileus, post GI surgery or biopsy, acute GI injury or burn, or recent radiotherapy to pelvis; - High risk of aspiration such as those with prior aspiration, tracheo-esophageal fistula, or obtundation; - Known severe hypersensitivity to barium sulfate or any of the excipients of E-Z-CAT DRY.
