# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

For oral suspension: 169 g of barium sulfate supplied as a fine, white to lightly colored powder (96 % w/w) in a single-dose HDPE plastic bottle for reconstitution.
The suspension can be reconstituted to a desired strength of 60, 70, 85, 100, or 115% w/v when prepared according to the corresponding fill line on the bottle.
The reconstituted solution should be opaque, white to lightly-colored and free from particles.
