# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

16.1 How Supplied E-Z-HD (barium sulfate) for suspension, is supplied as a fine, white to lightly colored powder (98 % w/w) in a single-dose HDPE plastic bottle containing 334 grams of barium sulfate.

Provided as: 24 bottles per pack (NDC 32909-764-01)

16.2 Storage and Handling Store at USP controlled room temperature, 20 to 25°C (68 to 77° F).
