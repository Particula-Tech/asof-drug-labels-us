# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

VARIBAR HONEY is supplied as a suspension in a multiple-dose polyethylene bottle containing 250 mL of barium sulfate (40 % w/v).

Provided as: 12 x 250 mL bottles (NDC 32909-122-07)

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at USP controlled room temperature 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
Protect from freezing.

Once opened, VARIBAR HONEY may be used for up to 21 days when stored at USP controlled room temperature, 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
