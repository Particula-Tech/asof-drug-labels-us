# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

VARIBAR HONEY is an off-white to lightly colored suspension with an apple aroma for oral administration supplied as 250 mL barium sulfate (40% w/v) in a multiple-dose polyethylene or as 30 mL of barium sulfate (40%w/v) in a multiple-dose polyethylene tube.

Provided as: 12 x 250 mL bottles (NDC 32909-122-07); 24 x 30 mL tubes (NDC 32909-122-54)

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at USP controlled room temperature 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77° F). Protect from freezing.

Once opened, VARIBAR HONEY may be used for up to 21 days when stored at USP controlled room temperature, 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77° F).
