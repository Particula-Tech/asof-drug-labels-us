# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

The recommended dose of E-Z-DISK in adults and pediatric patients aged 12 years and older is one 700 mg tablet orally during imaging.

Swallow one tablet whole with the aid of one or two swallows of water.
Do not cut, crush, or chew the tablet.

Advise patients to hydrate following the E-Z-DISK imaging procedure [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3)].

E-Z-DISK is formulated to disintegrate within the gastrointestinal (GI) tract.
In the event of prolonged retention, consider implementing appropriate interventions.
