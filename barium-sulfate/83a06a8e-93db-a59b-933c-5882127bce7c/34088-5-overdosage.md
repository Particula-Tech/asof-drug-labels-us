# OVERDOSAGE

On rare occasions following repeated administration, severe stomach cramps, nausea, vomiting, diarrhea or constipation may occur.
These are transitory in nature and are not considered serious.
Symptoms may be treated according to currently accepted standards of medical care.

## Dosage and Adinistration

Shake well prior to use.

Pour in a glass and have the patient drink 600 mL within 15 minutes.
The first radio-graph (in pronation) should be taken 15 to 20 minutes after the patient started drinking followed by a radiograph every 20 minutes until terminal ileum is visualized radio-graphically.

Discard any unused portion.
