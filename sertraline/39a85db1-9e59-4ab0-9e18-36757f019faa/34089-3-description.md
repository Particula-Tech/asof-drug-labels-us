# DESCRIPTION

Sertraline hydrochloride tablets are a selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor (SSRI) for oral administration.
It has a molecular weight of 342.7.
Sertraline hydrochloride has the following chemical name: (1S-cis)-4-(3,4-dichlorophenyl)-1,2,3,4-tetrahydro-N-methyl-1-naphthalenamine hydrochloride.
The empirical formula C 17 H 17 NCl 2 •HCl is represented by the following structural formula: Sertraline hydrochloride is a white crystalline powder that is slightly soluble in water and isopropyl alcohol, and sparingly soluble in ethanol.
Sertraline hydrochloride tablets are supplied for oral administration as scored tablets containing sertraline hydrochloride equivalent to 25, 50 and 100 mg of sertraline and the following inactive ingredients: microcrystalline cellulose, sodium starch glycolate, hydroxypropyl cellulose, dibasic calcium phosphate dihydrate, magnesium stearate, hypromellose, titanium dioxide, polyethylene glycol, and polysorbate 80.
Besides, 25 mg contains D&C yellow #10 aluminium lake, FD&C blue #1 aluminium lake, FD&C red # 40 aluminium lake; 50 mg contains FD&C blue # 2 aluminium lake; and 100 mg contains iron oxide yellow.
