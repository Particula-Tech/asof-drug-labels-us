# 9 DRUG ABUSE AND DEPENDENCE

## 9.1 Controlled Substance

Sertraline is not a controlled substance.

## 9.2 Abuse

In a placebo-controlled, double-blind, randomized study of the comparative abuse liability of sertraline, alprazolam, and d-amphetamine in humans, sertraline did not produce the positive subjective effects indicative of abuse potential, such as euphoria or drug liking, that were observed with the other two drugs.
