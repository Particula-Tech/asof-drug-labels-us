# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Finasteride Tablets USP, 5 mg are blue colored, circular, biconvex, beveled edged film-coated tablets debossed with ‘E’ on one side and ‘61’ on the other side.
Bottles of 30 NDC 65862-149-30 Bottles of 90 NDC 65862-149-90 Bottles of 100 NDC 65862-149-01 Bottles of 500 NDC 65862-149-05 Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light and keep container tightly closed.
Women should not handle crushed or broken finasteride tablets when they are pregnant or may potentially be pregnant because of the possibility of absorption of finasteride and the subsequent potential risk to a male fetus [see Warnings and Precautions (5.4), Use in Specific Populations (8.1) and Patient Counseling Information (17.2)].
