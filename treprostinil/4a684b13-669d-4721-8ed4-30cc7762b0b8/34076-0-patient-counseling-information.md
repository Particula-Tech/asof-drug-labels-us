# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

Interruption of Therapy Advise patients and caregivers to seek medical attention if they experience signs or symptoms of abrupt withdrawal of therapy or suspect a pump malfunction [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)].

Overdose Inform patients and their caregivers to seek medical attention if they experience signs or symptoms of Treprostinil injection overdose [see Overdosage (10)].
