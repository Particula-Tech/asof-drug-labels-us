# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

YUTREPIA is supplied in a carton consisting of 1 capsule based, dry powder inhaler (referred to as “inhaler”), 28 capsules (7 foil blister cards of 4 capsules each), and 7 single-use cleaning brushes.
The individual capsule well is connected by an air channel to a separate blister well containing a desiccant strip.

YUTREPIA inhalation powder capsules should only be delivered using the capsule-based inhaler.
The off-white plastic inhaler consists of a blue protective cap marked with YUTREPIA and a base with a mouthpiece, capsule chamber, and two blue push buttons.
Discard the inhaler device after 7 days of use or 56 actuations, whichever comes first.

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Capsules should remain in the blister to protect them from moisture and light, and each capsule should be removed only when ready to administer a dose.

Keep out of the reach of children.
