# 16 HOW SUPPLIED / STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Orenitram is an 8 mm round biconvex tablet with strength identifying color and printing and supplied as follows:

| Strength | Color | Printing on Tablets | NDC # Bottle/100 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.125 mg | White | UT 0.125 | 66302-300-01 |
| 0.25 mg | Green | UT 0.25 | 66302-302-01 |
| 1 mg | Yellow | UT 1 | 66302-310-01 |
| 2.5 mg | Pink | UT 2.5 | 66302-325-01 |

## 16.2 Storage

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [See USP controlled room temperature].
Keep out of reach of children.
