# 7 DRUG INTERACTIONS

## 7.1 Effect of CYP2C8 Inhibitors on Treprostinil

Co-administration of Orenitram and the CYP2C8 enzyme inhibitor gemfibrozil in healthy adult volunteers increases exposure to treprostinil.
Reduce the starting dose of Orenitram to 0.125 mg BID and use 0.125 mg BID increments not more frequently than every 3 to 4 days [see Dosage and Administration (2.4) and Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].
