# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Orenitram (treprostinil extended-release) is available in the following four strengths:

1. - 0.125 mg [White tablet imprinted with UT 0.125]
2. - 0.25 mg [Green tablet imprinted with UT 0.25]
3. - 1 mg [Yellow tablet imprinted with UT 1]
4. - 2.5 mg [Pink tablet imprinted with UT 2.5]
