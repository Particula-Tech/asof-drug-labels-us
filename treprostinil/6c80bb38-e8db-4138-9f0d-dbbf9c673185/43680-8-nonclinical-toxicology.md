# 13 NONCLINICAL TOXICOLOGY

## 13.1 Carcinogenesis, Mutagenesis, Impairment Of Fertility

Long-term studies have not been performed to evaluate the carcinogenic potential of treprostinil.
In vitro and in vivo genetic toxicology studies did not demonstrate any mutagenic or clastogenic effects of treprostinil.
Treprostinil sodium did not affect fertility or mating performance of male or female rats given continuous subcutaneous infusions at rates of up to 450 ng treprostinil/kg/min [about 59 times the recommended starting human rate of infusion (1.25 ng/kg/min) and about 8 times the average rate (9.3 ng/kg/min) achieved in clinical trials, on a ng/m2 basis].
In this study, males were dosed from 10 weeks prior to mating and through the 2-week mating period.
Females were dosed from 2 weeks prior to mating until gestational day 6.
