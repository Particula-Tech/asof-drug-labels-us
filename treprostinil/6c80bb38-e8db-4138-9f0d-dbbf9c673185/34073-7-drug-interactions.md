# 7 DRUG INTERACTIONS

## 7.1 Effect of CYP2C8 Inhibitors and Inducers on Treprostinil

Dose adjustment of treprostinil may be necessary when co-administered with CYP2C8 inducers or inhibitors.
Human pharmacokinetic studies with an oral formulation of treprostinil (treprostinil diolamine) indicated that co-administration of the cytochrome P450 (CYP) 2C8 enzyme inhibitor gemfibrozil increases exposure (both Cmax and AUC) to treprostinil.
Co-administration of the CYP2C8 enzyme inducer rifampin decreases exposure to treprostinil.
It has not been determined if the changes in exposure of treprostinil with inhibitors or inducers of CYP2C8 observed for the oral administration of treprostinil would be similar for treprostinil administered via the parenteral route.
