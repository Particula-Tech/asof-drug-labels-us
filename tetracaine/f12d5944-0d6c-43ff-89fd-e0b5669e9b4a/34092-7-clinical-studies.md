# 14 CLINICAL STUDIES

Topical administration of Tetracaine Hydrochloride Ophthalmic Solution, USP 0.5% results in localized temporary anesthesia.
The maximum effect is achieved within 10–20 seconds after instillation, with efficacy lasting 10–20 minutes.
Duration of effect can be extended with repeated dosing [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)and Overdosage (10)].
