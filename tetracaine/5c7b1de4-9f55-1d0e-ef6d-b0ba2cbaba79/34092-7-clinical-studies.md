# 14 CLINICAL STUDIES

Topical administration of tetracainehydrochloride ophthalmic solution results in localized temporary anesthesia.
The maximum effect is achieved within 10–20 seconds after instillation, with efficacy lasting 10–20 minutes.
Duration of effect can be extended with repeated dosing.
[see Corneal toxicity (5.2) and Overdosage (10)].
