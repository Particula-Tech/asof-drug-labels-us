# 11 DESCRIPTION

Mirabegron extended-release tablets for oral use and are beta-3 adrenergic agonists.

The chemical name of mirabegron is 2-(2-aminothiazol-4-yl)-N-[4-(2-{[(2R)-2-hydroxy-2-phenylethyl]amino}ethyl)phenyl]acetamide having a molecular formula of C21H24N4O2S and a molecular weight of 396.51.
The structural formula of mirabegron is:

Mirabegron is a white to yellowish color powder.
It is practically insoluble in water (0.082 mg/mL).
It is soluble in methanol and dimethyl sulfoxide.

Each mirabegron extended-release tablet for oral use contains either 25 mg or 50 mg of mirabegron and the following inactive ingredients: hydroxypropyl cellulose, hypromellose, iron oxide red, iron oxide yellow, magnesium stearate, polyethylene glycol, polyethylene oxide, and titanium dioxide.
