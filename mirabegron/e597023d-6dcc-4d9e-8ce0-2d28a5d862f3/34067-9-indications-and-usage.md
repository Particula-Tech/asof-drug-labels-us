# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

## 1.1 Adult Overactive Bladder (OAB)

Mirabegron Monotherapy

Mirabegron is indicated for the treatment of OAB in adult patients with symptoms of urge urinary incontinence, urgency, and urinary frequency.

## 1.2 Pediatric Neurogenic Detrusor Overactivity (NDO)

Mirabegron

Mirabegron is indicated for the treatment of NDO in pediatric patients aged 3 years and older and weighing 35 kg or more.
