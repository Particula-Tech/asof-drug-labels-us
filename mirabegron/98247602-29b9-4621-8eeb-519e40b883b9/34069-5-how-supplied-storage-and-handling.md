# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 Mirabegron Extended-Release Tablets

Mirabegron extended-release tablets are supplied as oval, film-coated, extended-release tablets, available in bottles as follows:

| Strength | 25 mg | 50 mg |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shape/Color | Oval/Orange | Oval/Yellow |
| Branding on Tablet | 133 | 137 |
| Bottles of 30 | NDC 67184-0571-1 | NDC 67184-0572-1 |
| Bottles of 90 | NDC 67184-0571-2 | NDC 67184-0572-2 |

Store and Dispense

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) with excursions permitted from 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
