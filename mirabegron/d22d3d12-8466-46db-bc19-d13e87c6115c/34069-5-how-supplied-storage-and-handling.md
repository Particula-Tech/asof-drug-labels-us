# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 Mirabegron Extended-Release Tablets

Mirabegron extended-release tablets are supplied as oval, film-coated, extended-release tablets, available in bottles as follows:

| Strength | 25 mg | 50 mg |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Color | White | Pale-yellow |
| Debossed | M25, LU | M50, LU |
| Bottle of 30 | NDC 68180-151-06 | NDC 68180-152-06 |
| Bottle of 90 | NDC 68180-151-09 | NDC 68180-152-09 |

Store and Dispense

Store at 25°C (77°F) with excursions permitted from 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F).
[see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
