# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Mirabegron extended-release tablets are supplied in two different strengths as described below:

- 25 mg White coloured, oval shaped, biconvex, film coated tablets debossed with "M25" on one side and "LU" on the other side.
- 50 mg Pale-yellow coloured, oval shaped, biconvex, film coated tablets debossed with "M50" on one side and "LU" on the other side.
