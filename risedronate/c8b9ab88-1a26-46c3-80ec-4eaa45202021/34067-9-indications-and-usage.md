# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

## 1.1 Postmenopausal Osteoporosis

Atelvia is indicated for the treatment of osteoporosis in postmenopausal women.
Bone mineral density increases achieved at one year with Atelvia are non-inferior to increases seen with risedronate sodium 5 mg (immediate-release) daily.
Daily risedronate sodium 5 mg (immediate-release) has been shown to reduce the incidence of vertebral fractures and a composite endpoint of nonvertebral osteoporosis-related fractures [see Clinical Studies (14.1)].
