# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Risedronate Sodium Tablets USP are available as:

5 mg: yellow, round, standard convex film-coated, unscored tablets, debossed with “TEVA” on one side of the tablet and “7390” on the other side, in bottles of 30 (NDC 0093-3099-56).

30 mg: white, round, standard convex film-coated, unscored tablets, debossed with “TEVA” on one side of the tablet and “7391” on the other side, in bottles of 30 (NDC 0093-3100-56).

35 mg: orange, round, standard convex film-coated, unscored tablets, debossed with “TEVA” on one side of the tablet and “7389” on the other side, in blister packages of 4 x 1 card in a carton (NDC 0093-3098-44) and 4 x 3 (3 cards each with 4 tablets) in a carton (NDC 0093-3098-29).

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP, with a child-resistant closure (as required).
