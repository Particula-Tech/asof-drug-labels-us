# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Piperacillin and tazobactam for injection, USP pharmacy bulk package is supplied as a white to yellowish powder in a bottle of the following size:

Piperacillin and tazobactam for injection 13.5 g pharmacy bulk bottle contains piperacillin sodium equivalent to 12 grams of piperacillin and tazobactam sodium equivalent to 1.5 grams tazobactam.
