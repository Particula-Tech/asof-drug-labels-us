# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Piperacillin and Tazobactam for Injection, USP is supplied as a white to off-white lyophilized powder in bottles of the following size:

- •40.5 g pharmacy bulk bottle (piperacillin sodium equivalent to 36 grams of piperacillin and tazobactam sodium equivalent to 4.5 grams tazobactam).
