# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Piperacillin and Tazobactam for Injection, USP is supplied as white or almost-white sterile powder in single-dose vials in the following sizes:

- 2.25 g (piperacillin sodium equivalent to 2 grams piperacillin and tazobactam sodium equivalent to 0.25 g of tazobactam).
  Each vial contains 4.69 mEq (108 mg) of sodium.
  Supplied 10 per box - NDC 83270-301-10
- 3.375 g (piperacillin sodium equivalent to 3 grams piperacillin and tazobactam sodium equivalent to 0.375 g of tazobactam).
  Each vial contains 7.04 mEq (162 mg) of sodium.
  Supplied 10 per box - NDC 83270-302-10
- 4.5 g (piperacillin sodium equivalent to 4 grams piperacillin and tazobactam sodium equivalent to 0.5 g of tazobactam).
  Each vial contains 9.39 mEq (216 mg) of sodium.
  Supplied 10 per box - NDC 83270-303-10

Piperacillin and Tazobactam for Injection, USP vials should be stored at controlled room temperature (20°C to 25°C [68°F to 77°F]) prior to reconstitution.
