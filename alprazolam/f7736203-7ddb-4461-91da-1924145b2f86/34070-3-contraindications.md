# Contraindications

NIRAVAM™ is contraindicated in patients with known sensitivity to this drug or other benzodiazepines.
NIRAVAM™ may be used in patients with open angle glaucoma who are receiving appropriate therapy, but is contraindicated in patients with acute narrow angle glaucoma.

NIRAVAM™ is contraindicated with ketoconazole and itraconazole, since these medications significantly impair the oxidative metabolism mediated by cytochrome P450 3A (CYP3A) (see Clinical Pharmacology, Warnings and Drug interactions).
