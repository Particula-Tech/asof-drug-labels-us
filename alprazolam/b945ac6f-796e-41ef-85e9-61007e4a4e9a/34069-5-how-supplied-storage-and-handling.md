# HOW SUPPLIED

Alprazolam Intensol™ Oral Solution (Concentrate) for oral administration is available as:

1 mg per mL (flavorless, colorless solution)

NDC 0054-3068-44: Bottles of 30 mL with calibrated dropper (graduations of 0.25 mL [0.25 mg], 0.5 mL [0.5 mg], 0.75 mL [0.75 mg], and 1 mL [1 mg] on the dropper].

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

PROTECT FROM LIGHT.

Discard opened bottle after 90 days.
