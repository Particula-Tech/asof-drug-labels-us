# CONTRAINDICATIONS

Alprazolam orally disintegrating tablets are contraindicated in patients with known sensitivity to this drug or other benzodiazepines.
Alprazolam orally disintegrating tablets may be used in patients with open angle glaucoma who are receiving appropriate therapy, but is contraindicated in patients with acute narrow angle glaucoma.

Alprazolam orally disintegrating tablets are contraindicated with ketoconazole and itraconazole, since these medications significantly impair the oxidative metabolism mediated by cytochrome P450 3A (CYP3A) (see CLINICAL PHARMACOLOGY, WARNINGS and PRECAUTIONS–Drug Interactions).
