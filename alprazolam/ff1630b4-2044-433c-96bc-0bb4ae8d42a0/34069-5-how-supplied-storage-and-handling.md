# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Blisters of 10 x 10

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15° to 30°C (59° to 86ºF) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
