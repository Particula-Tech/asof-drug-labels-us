# HOW SUPPLIED

Alprazolam tablets, USP for oral administration are available as:

2 mg: Rectangular white multi-scored tablets debossed GG 249 on one side and plain on the reverse side supplied as: NDC 0781-1089-01 bottles of 100 NDC 0781-1089-05 bottles of 500

Store at 20°-25°C (68°-77°F) (see USP Controlled Room Temperature).

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container.
