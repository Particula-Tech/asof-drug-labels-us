# HOW SUPPLIED

Theophylline Oral Solution USP is a clear red solution with a berry flavor.
Each tablespoonful (15 mL) contains 80 mg theophylline anhydrous.

Theophylline Oral Solution USP is available in the following oral dosage forms:

NDC 0121-0820-04: 4 fl oz (118 mL) bottle

NDC 0121-0820-16: 16 fl oz (473 mL) bottle

NDC 0121-4820-15: 15 mL unit dose cup.
Case contains 40 unit-dose cups of 15 mL (NDC 0121-4820-40), packaged in 4 trays of 10 unit-dose cups each.

## RECOMMENDED STORAGE

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Dispense in tight, light-resistant container.
