# HOW SUPPLIED

Theophylline Oral Solution, USP is a clear red solution with a fruit flavor.
Each tablespoonful (15 mL) contains 80 mg theophylline anhydrous.

Theophylline Oral Solution, USP is available in bottles of 473 mL (16 fl oz) NDC 62135-556-47, 15 mL Unit Dose Cup NDC 62135-556-51 and 20 Unit Dose Cups of 15 mL each NDC 62135-556-24.

RECOMMENDED STORAGE Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°); [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Dispense in tight container.
