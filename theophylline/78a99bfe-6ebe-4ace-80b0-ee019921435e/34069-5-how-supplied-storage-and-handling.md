# HOW SUPPLIED

Theophylline Oral Solution, USP 80 mg/15 mL is an orange-flavored, clear, colorless to slightly yellow solution.
Each 15 mL (one tablespoonful) contains 80 mg theophylline anhydrous.

Theophylline Oral Solution, USP is available in bottles of 473 mL NDC 27808-033-01

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted from 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room temperature.].

Dispense in tight container.
