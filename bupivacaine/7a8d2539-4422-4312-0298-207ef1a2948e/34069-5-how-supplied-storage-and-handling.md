# HOW SUPPLIED

Bupivacaine Spinal (Bupivacaine in Dextrose Injection, USP) is supplied in 2 mL ampuls (15 mg bupivacaine hydrochloride with 165 mg dextrose anhydrous) packaged in cartons of 10 (List No. 3613).

Store at 20 to 25°C (68 to 77°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

Caution: Federal (USA) law prohibits dispensing without prescription.
