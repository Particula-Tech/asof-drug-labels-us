# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

Bupivacaine Hydrochloride in Dextrose Injection, USP solution may be autoclaved once at 15 pound pressure, 121°C (250°F) for 15 minutes.
This product is clear and colorless.
Do not use the solution if it is discolored or contains particulate matter.

Single-dose ampules of 2 mL Bupivacaine Hydrochloride in Dextrose Injection, USP (15 mg bupivacaine hydrochloride with 165 mg dextrose) are supplied as follows:
