# HOW SUPPLIED

Single-dose ampules of 2 mL (15 mg bupivacaine hydrochloride with 165 mg dextrose), is supplied as follows:

| NDC | Container | Concentration | Size | Total (mg) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 73293-0002-2 | Single Dose Ampule (Package of 50) | 0.75% (7.5 mg/mL) | 2 mL | 15 |

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F).
[see USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

Bupivacaine Hydrochloride in Dextrose Injection, USP solution may be autoclaved once at 15 pound pressure, 121°C (250°F) for 15 minutes.
Do not administer any solution which is discolored or contains particulate matter.

For single-dose ampules: Discard the unused portion.
