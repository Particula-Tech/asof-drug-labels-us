# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Bupivacaine hydrochloride injection, USP is a clear, colorless, sterile isotonic solution and is available as follows:

Solutions of bupivacaine hydrochloride injection, USP may be autoclaved.
Autoclave at 15-pound pressure, 121°C (250°F) for 15 minutes.
Protect from light.
This product is clear and colorless.
Do not use the solution if it is discolored or if it contains a precipitate.
