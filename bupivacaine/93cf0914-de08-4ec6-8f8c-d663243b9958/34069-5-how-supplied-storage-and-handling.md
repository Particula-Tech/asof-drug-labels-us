# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Store at 20 °C to 25 °C (68 °F to 77 °F); excursions permitted between 15 °C to 30 °C (59 °F to 86 °F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

MARCAINE with epinephrine 1:200,000 (as bitartrate)―Do not autoclave solutions of MARCAINE that contain epinephrine and protect from light.
This product is clear and colorless.
Do not use the solution if it is discolored or if it contains a precipitate.
