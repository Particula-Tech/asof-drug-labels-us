# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

The recommended dosage of INCRUSE ELLIPTA for maintenance treatment of COPD is 1 actuation (umeclidinium 62.5 mcg) once daily by oral inhalation.

- •INCRUSE ELLIPTA should be used at the same time every day.
  Do not use INCRUSE ELLIPTA more than 1 time every 24 hours.
- •No dosage adjustment is required for geriatric patients, patients with renal impairment, or patients with moderate hepatic impairment [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].
