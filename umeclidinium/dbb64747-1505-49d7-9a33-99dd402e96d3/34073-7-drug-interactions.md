# 7 DRUG INTERACTIONS

## 7.1 Anticholinergics

There is potential for an additive interaction with concomitantly used anticholinergic medicines.
Therefore, avoid coadministration of INCRUSE ELLIPTA with other anticholinergic-containing drugs as this may lead to an increase in anticholinergic adverse effects [see Warnings and Precautions (5.4, 5.5)].
