# 10.0 OVERDOSAGE

In a clinical study, 5 patients received protocol-directed single infusions of 10 mg/kg of intravenous SIMPONI without serious adverse reactions or other significant reactions.
The highest weight patient was 100 kg, and therefore received a single intravenous infusion of 1000 mg of SIMPONI.
There were no SIMPONI overdoses in the clinical studies.
