# 13.0 NONCLINICAL TOXICOLOGY

## 13.1 Carcinogenesis, Mutagenesis, Impairment of Fertility

Long-term animal studies of golimumab have not been conducted to evaluate its carcinogenic potential.
Mutagenicity studies have not been conducted with golimumab.
A fertility study conducted in mice using an analogous anti-mouse TNFα antibody showed no impairment of fertility.
