# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

REZVOGLAR (insulin glargine-aglr) injection is supplied as a clear, colorless, sterile solution containing 100 units per mL (U-100) available in:

| REZVOGLAR | Total Volume | NDC Number | Package Size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| REZVOGLAR single-patient-use KwikPen | 3 mL | 0002-8980-05 (HP-8980) | 5 pens |

The prefilled pen REZVOGLAR KwikPen dials in 1 unit increments.

Needles are not included in the packs.

This device is recommended for use with Becton, Dickinson & Company's insulin pen needles which are sold separately.

## 16.2 Storage

Dispense in the original sealed carton with the enclosed Instructions for Use.

REZVOGLAR should not be stored in the freezer and should not be allowed to freeze.
Discard REZVOGLAR if it has been frozen.
Protect REZVOGLAR from direct heat and light.

Storage conditions are summarized in the following table:

|  | Not In-Use (unopened) Refrigerated (36°F to 46°F [2°C to 8°C]) | Not In-Use (unopened) Room Temperature (up to 86°F [30°C]) | In-Use (opened) (see temperature below) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 mL single-patient-use prefilled pen REZVOGLAR KwikPen | Until expiration date | 28 days | 28 days Room temperature only (up to 86°F [30°C]) (Do not refrigerate) |
