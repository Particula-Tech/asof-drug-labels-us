# 10 OVERDOSAGE

Excess insulin administration may cause hypoglycemia and hypokalemia [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3, 5.6)].
Mild episodes of hypoglycemia can be treated with oral glucose.
Lowering the insulin dosage, and adjustments in meal patterns, or physical activity level may be needed.
More severe episodes of hypoglycemia with coma, seizure, or neurologic impairment may be treated with glucagon for emergency use or concentrated intravenous glucose.
Sustained carbohydrate intake and observation may be necessary because hypoglycemia may recur after apparent clinical recovery.
Hypokalemia must be corrected appropriately.
