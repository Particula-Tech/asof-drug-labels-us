# DESCRIPTION

`SOMA' (carisoprodol) Tablets, USP is available as 350 mg round, white tablets.
Chemically, carisoprodol is N-isopropyl-2-methyl-2-propyl-1,3-propanediol dicarbamate.
Carisoprodol is a white, crystalline powder, having a mild, characteristic odor and a bitter taste.
It is very slightly soluble in water; freely soluble in alcohol, in chloroform, and in acetone; its solubility is practically independent of pH.
Carisoprodol is present as a racemic mixture.
The molecular formula is C 12 H 24 N 2 O 4, with a molecular weight of 260.33.
