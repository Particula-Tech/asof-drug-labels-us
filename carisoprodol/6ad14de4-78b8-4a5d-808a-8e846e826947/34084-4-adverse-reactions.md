# ADVERSE REACTIONS

Cardiovascular -- Tachycardia, postural hypotension, and facial flushing.

Central Nervous System -- Drowsiness and other CNS effects may require dosage reduction.
Also observed: dizziness, vertigo, ataxia, tremor, agitation, irritability, headache, depressive reactions, syncope, seizures, and insomnia.
(See also Idiosyncratic Reactions under "Warnings.")

Gastrointestinal -- Nausea, vomiting, hiccup, and epigastric distress.

Hematologic -- Leukopenia, in which other drugs or viral infection may have been responsible, and pancytopenia, attributed to phenylbutazone, have been reported.
No serious blood dyscrasias have been attributed to carisoprodol.
