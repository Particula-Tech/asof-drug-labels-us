# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

250mg Tablets: round, convex, white tablets, inscribed with SOMA 250; available in bottles of 100 (NDC 0037-2250-10) and bottles of 30 (NDC 0037-2250-30).

350mg Tablets: round, convex, white tablets, inscribed with SOMA 350; available in bottles of 100 (NDC 0037-2001-01).

Storage: Store at controlled room temperature 20° - 25°C (68° - 77°F).
