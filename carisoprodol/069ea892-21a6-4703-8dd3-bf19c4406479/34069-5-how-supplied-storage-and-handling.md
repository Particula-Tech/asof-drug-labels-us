# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Carisoprodol Tablets, USP 350 mg: round, convex, white tablets, debossed with SG 109 on one side; available in bottles of 30 (NDC 50228-109-30) and the bottles of 1,000 (NDC 50228-109-10).

Storage Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
