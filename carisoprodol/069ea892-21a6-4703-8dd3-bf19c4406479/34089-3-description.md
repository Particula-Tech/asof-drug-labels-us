# 11 DESCRIPTION

Carisoprodol tablets, USP are available as 350 mg round, white tablets.
Carisoprodol, USP is a white, crystalline powder, having a mild, characteristic odor.
It is very slightly soluble in water; freely soluble in alcohol, in chloroform, and in acetone; and its solubility is practically independent of pH.
Carisoprodol is present as a racemic mixture.
Chemically, carisoprodol is (±)-2-Methyl-2-propyl-1,3-propanediol carbamate isopropylcarbamate and the molecular formula is C12H24N2O4, with a molecular weight of 260.33.
