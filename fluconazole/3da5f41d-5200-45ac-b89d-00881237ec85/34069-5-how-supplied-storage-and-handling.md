# HOW SUPPLIED

Fluconazole Tablets, USP: Pink trapezoidal tablets containing 50, 100, or 200 mg of fluconazole, USP are packaged in bottles.
Fluconazole Tablets, USP 150 mg are pink, oval shaped, packaged in a blister pack of 1 tablet.

Fluconazole Tablets, USP are supplied as follows:

Fluconazole Tablets, USP 50 mg: Engraved with “50” on one side and plain on the other side.

1. NDC 68462-101-30 Bottles of 30
2. NDC 68462-101-10 Bottles of 1000

Fluconazole Tablets, USP 100 mg: Engraved with “100” on one side and plain on the other side.

1. NDC 68462-102-30 Bottles of 30
2. NDC 68462-102-10 Bottles of 1000

Fluconazole Tablets, USP 150 mg: Engraved with “150” on one side and plain on the other side.

1. NDC 68462-119-40 Carton of 1 blister pack of 1 tablet (1 x 1 unit-dose)
2. NDC 68462-119-44 Carton of 12 blister packs of 1 tablet (12 x 1 unit-dose)
3. NDC 68462-103-40 Carton of 12 blister cards of 1 tablet

Fluconazole Tablets, USP 200 mg: Engraved with “200” on one side and plain on the other side.

1. NDC 68462-104-30 Bottles of 30
2. NDC 68462-104-10 Bottles of 1000

Storage: Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
