# CONTRAINDICATIONS

Fluconazole Injection, USP is contraindicated in patients who have shown hypersensitivity to fluconazole or to any of its excipients.
There is no information regarding cross-hypersensitivity between fluconazole and other azole antifungal agents.
Caution should be used in prescribing Fluconazole Injection, USP to patients with hypersensitivity to other azoles.
Coadministration of terfenadine is contraindicated in patients receiving Fluconazole Injection, USP at multiple doses of 400 mg or higher based upon results of a multiple dose interaction study.
Coadministration of cisapride is contraindicated in patients receiving Fluconazole Injection, USP.
(See CLINICAL PHARMACOLOGY: Drug Interaction Studies and PRECAUTIONS.)
