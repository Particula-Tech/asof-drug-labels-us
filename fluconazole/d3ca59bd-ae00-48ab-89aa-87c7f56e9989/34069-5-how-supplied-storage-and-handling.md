# HOW SUPPLIED

## Fluconazole Injection

Fluconazole injection for intravenous infusion administration is formulated as a sterile iso-osmotic solution containing 2 mg/mL of fluconazole.
It is supplied in glass bottles containing 100 mL affording a dose of 200 mg of fluconazole.

Fluconazole Injection in Glass Bottles:

NDC 0143-9899-91 Fluconazole in iso-osmotic Sodium Chloride Diluent 200 mg/100 mL × 6

### Storage

Store between 86°F (30°C) and 41°F (5°C).
Protect from freezing.
