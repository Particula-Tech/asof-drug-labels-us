# HOW SUPPLIED

Fluconazole Injection, USP for intravenous infusion administration is formulated as a sterile iso-osmotic solution containing 2 mg/mL of Fluconazole, USP.
It is supplied in glass bottles containing volume of100 mL, affording a dose of 200 mg of Fluconazole, USP.

NDC 0143-9899-91 Fluconazole in iso-osmotic Sodium Chloride Diluent 200 mg/100 mL packaged in cartons of 6 vials.

Storage: Store at 5º to 30ºC (41º to 86ºF).
Protect from freezing.
