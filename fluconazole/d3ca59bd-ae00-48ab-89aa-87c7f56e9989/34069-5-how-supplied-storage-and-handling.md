# HOW SUPPLIED

Fluconazole Injection, USP: Fluconazole injections for intravenous infusion administration are formulated as a sterile iso-osmotic solution containing 2 mg/mL of fluconazole.
They are supplied in glass bottles containing 100 mL affording a dose of 200 mg of fluconazole.

Fluconazole injections in Glass Bottles:

NDC 0143-9899-91 Fluconazole in iso-osmotic Sodium Chloride Diluent 200 mg/100 mL × 6

Storage: Store at 5º to 30ºC (41º to 86ºF).
Protect from freezing.
