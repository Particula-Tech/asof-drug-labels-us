# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Sugammadex injection is a sterile, clear, colorless to slightly yellow-brown, non-pyrogenic aqueous solution intended for intravenous infusion.
Sugammadex injection is available as follows:

- 200 mg per 2 mL (100 mg per mL) in a single-dose vial for bolus injection
- 500 mg per 5 mL (100 mg per mL), in a single-dose vial for bolus injection
