# 10 OVERDOSAGE

In premarketing clinical trials, one case of accidental overdose with 40 mg/kg sugammadex injection was reported without significant effects.

Sugammadex injection can be removed using hemodialysis with a high-flux filter, but not with a low-flux filter.
Based upon clinical studies, sugammadex injection concentrations in plasma are reduced with a high-flux filter by about 70% after a 3- to 6-hour dialysis session.
