# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

- Advise females of reproductive potential using hormonal contraceptives that sugammadex may reduce the contraceptive effect.
  Instruct females to use an additional, non-hormonal method of contraception for the next 7 days following sugammadex administration [see Drug Interactions (7.3)].
