# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

• Advise females of reproductive potential using hormonal contraceptives that sugammadex injection may reduce the contraceptive effect.
Instruct females to use an additional, non-hormonal method of contraception for the next 7 days following sugammadex injection administration [see Drug Interactions (7.3)].
