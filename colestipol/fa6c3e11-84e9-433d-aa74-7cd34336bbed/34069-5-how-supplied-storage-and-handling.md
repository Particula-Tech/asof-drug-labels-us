# HOW SUPPLIED

Colestipol Hydrochloride Tablets USP, 1 gram are off-white to pale-yellow, film-coated, oval tablets, debossed with "G" on one side and plain on the other.
They are available as follows:

Bottles of 120: NDC 0115-5211-16

Bottles of 500: NDC 0115-5211-02

Each tablet contains 1 gram of colestipol hydrochloride, USP.

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from moisture.
