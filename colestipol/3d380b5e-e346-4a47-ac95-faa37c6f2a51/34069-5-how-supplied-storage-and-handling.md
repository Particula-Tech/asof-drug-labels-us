# HOW SUPPLIED

Colestipol hydrochloride tablets USP, 1 gram are yellow, film-coated, elliptical tablets, debossed with “S” on one side and plain on the other.

They are supplied as follows:

Bottles of 120 NDC 62559-395-12

Each tablet contains 1 gram of micronized colestipol hydrochloride USP.

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from moisture.
