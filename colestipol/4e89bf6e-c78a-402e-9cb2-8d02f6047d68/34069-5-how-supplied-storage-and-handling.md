# HOW SUPPLIED

Colestipol hydrochloride granules are available as follows:

Cartons of 30 foil packets — NDC 59762-0260-1 Cartons of 90 foil packets — NDC 59762-0260-2 Bottles of 500 grams with scoop — NDC 59762-0260-3 Each packet or level scoop supplies 5 grams of colestipol hydrochloride granules.

Store at controlled room temperature 20° to 25° C (68° to 77° F) [see USP].
