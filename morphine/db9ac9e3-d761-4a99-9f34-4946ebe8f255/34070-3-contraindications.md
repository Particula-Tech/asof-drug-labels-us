# CONTRAINDICATIONS

The only absolute contraindication to the use of INFUMORPH is known allergy to morphine.
Contraindications to the use of neuraxial analgesia include: the presence of infection at the injection microinfusion site, concomitant anticoagulant therapy, uncontrolled bleeding diathesis and the presence of any other concomitant therapy or medical condition which would render epidural or intrathecal administration of medication especially hazardous.
