# INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Preservative-free morphine sulfate injection is a systemic narcotic analgesic for administration by the intravenous, epidural or intrathecal routes.
It is used for the management of pain not responsive to non-narcotic analgesics.
Morphine sulfate, administered epidurally or intrathecally, provides pain relief for extended periods without attendant loss of motor, sensory or sympathetic function.
