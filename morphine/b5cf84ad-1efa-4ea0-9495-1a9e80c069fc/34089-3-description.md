# DESCRIPTION

Each tablet for oral administration contains:

Morphine sulfate..........
15 mg, 30 mg, 60 mg, or 100 mg in a tablet that provides for sustained release of the medication.

Morphine sulfate occurs as white, feathery, silky crystals, cubical masses of crystals, or white crystalline powder; it is soluble in water and slightly soluble in alcohol.
Morphine has a pKa of 7.9, with an octanol/water partition coefficient of 1.42 at pH 7.4.
At this pH, the tertiary amino group is mostly ionized, making the molecule water-soluble.
Morphine is significantly more water-soluble than any other opioid in clinical use.
