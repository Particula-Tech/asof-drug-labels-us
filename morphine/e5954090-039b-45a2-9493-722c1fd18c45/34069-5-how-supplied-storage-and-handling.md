## Storage

Protect from light.
Store at 20° - 25°C (68° - 77°F), excursions permitted to 15°30°C (59°86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature] until ready to use.
DO NOT FREEZE.
INFUMORPH contains no preservative or antioxidant.
DISCARD ANY UNUSED PORTION.
DO NOT HEAT-STERILIZE.
