# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Morphine Sulfate Extended-release Capsules, USP contain white to tan polymer coated extended-release pellets of morphine sulfate and are available in seven dose strengths:

10 mg size 4 capsule, light blue opaque cap and body printed with "UPSHER-SMITH" over "0225" on the cap and "10 mg" on the body in black ink.
Capsules are supplied in bottles of 100 (NDC 0832-0225-00) and 500 (NDC 0832-0225-50).

30 mg size 2 capsule, blue violet opaque cap and body printed with "UPSHER-SMITH" over "0227" on the cap and "30 mg" on the body in black ink.

50 mg size 2 capsule, blue opaque cap and body printed with "UPSHER-SMITH" over "0228" on the cap and "50 mg" on the body in black ink.

Store at 20-25°C (68-77°F).
Excursions permitted to 15-30°C (59-86°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]
Protect from light and moisture.

Dispense in a sealed tamper-evident, childproof, light-resistant container.
