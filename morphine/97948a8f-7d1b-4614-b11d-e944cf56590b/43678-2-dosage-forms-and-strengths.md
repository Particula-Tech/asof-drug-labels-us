# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Morphine Sulfate Extended-Release Capsules, USP contain white to off-white or tan colored polymer coated pellets, have an outer opaque capsule with colors as identified below and are available in twelve dose strengths:

10 mg size 4 capsule, light blue opaque cap and body printed with "Upsher-Smith" over "225" on the cap and "10 mg" on the body in black ink.

20 mg size 4 capsule, yellow opaque cap and body printed with "Upsher-Smith" over "226" on the cap and "20 mg" on the body in black ink.

30 mg size 2 capsule, blue violet opaque cap and body printed with "Upsher-Smith" over "227" on the cap and "30 mg" on the body in black ink.

50 mg size 2 capsule, blue opaque cap and body printed with "Upsher-Smith" over "228" on the cap and "50 mg" on the body in black ink.

60 mg size 1 capsule, pink opaque cap and body printed with "Upsher-Smith" over "229" on the cap and "60 mg" on the body in black ink.

80 mg size 1 capsule, light orange opaque cap and body printed with "Upsher-Smith" over "230" on the cap and "80 mg" on the body in black ink.

100 mg size 0 capsule, green opaque cap and body printed with "Upsher-Smith" over "233" on the cap and "100 mg" on the body in black ink.
