# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Extended-release tablets: 15 mg, 30 mg, 60 mg, 100 mg.

- 15 mg extended-release tablets (round, blue-colored, coated tablets ink-printed with “IDS/M15” on one side; and plain on the other)
- 30 mg extended-release tablets (round, purple-colored, coated tablets ink-printed with “IDS/M30” on one side; and plain on the other)
- 60 mg extended-release tablets (round, orange-colored, coated tablets ink-printed with “IDS/M60” on one side; and plain on the other)
- 100 mg extended-release tablets* (round, gray-colored, coated tablets ink-printed with “IDS/M100” on one side; and plain on the other)

*100 mg tablets are for use in opioid-tolerant patients only
