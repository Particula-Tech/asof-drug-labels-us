# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Morphine Sulfate Extended-Release Tablets are supplied as follows:

15 mg – round, blue-colored, film-coated tablets bearing the symbol ABG on one side and 15 on the other.

| Bottles (opaque plastic) of 100 tablets | NDC 42858-801-01 |
| --- | --- |

30 mg – round, lavender-colored, film-coated tablets bearing the symbol ABG on one side and 30 on the other.

| Bottles (opaque plastic) of 100 tablets | NDC 42858-802-01 |
| --- | --- |

60 mg – round, orange-colored, film-coated tablets bearing the symbol ABG on one side and 60 on the other.

| Bottles (opaque plastic) of 100 tablets | NDC 42858-803-01 |
| --- | --- |

100 mg – round, gray-colored, film-coated tablets bearing the symbol ABG on one side and 100 on the other.

| Bottles (opaque plastic) of 100 tablets | NDC 42858-804-01 |
| --- | --- |

200 mg – capsule-shaped, green-colored, film-coated tablets bearing the symbol ABG on one side and 200 on the other.

| Bottles (opaque plastic) of 100 tablets | NDC 42858-805-01 |
| --- | --- |

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted between 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Store morphine sulfate extended-release tablets securely and dispose of properly [see Patient Counseling Information (17)].

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container.
