# INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Morphine Sulfate Extended-Release Tablets are an extended-release oral morphine formulation indicated for the relief of moderate to severe pain.
It is intended for use in patients who require repeated dosing with potent opioid analgesics over periods of more than a few days.

The morphine sulfate extended-release tablets 200 mg strength is a high dose, extended-release, oral morphine formulation indicated for the relief of pain in opioid-tolerant patients only.
