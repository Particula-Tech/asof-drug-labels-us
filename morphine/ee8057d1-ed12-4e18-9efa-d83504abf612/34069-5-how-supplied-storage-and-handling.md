# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Morphine Sulfate Injection, USP is a clear and colorless to pale yellow solution supplied as a sterile solution in glass, single-dose flip top vials for the preparation of large volume parenteral solutions.

| Unit of Sale | Concentration |
| --- | --- |
| NDC 0409-1896-20 Carton containing 1 Single-Dose Flip Top Vial | 1,000 mg/20 mL (50 mg/mL) |
| NDC 0409-2022-01 Carton containing 1 Single-Dose Flip Top Vial | 2,500 mg/50 mL (50 mg/mL) |

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature] until ready to use.
PROTECT FROM LIGHT.
DO NOT FREEZE.
Contains no preservative or antioxidant.
DISCARD ANY UNUSED PORTION.
DO NOT HEAT-STERILIZE.
