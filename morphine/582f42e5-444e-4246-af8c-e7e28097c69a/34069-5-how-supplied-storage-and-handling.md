# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Morphine Sulfate Injection, USP is supplied as a sterile solution in single dose vials for intravenous (IV) or intramuscular (IM) use as follows:

| Product No. | NDC No. | Strength |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

STORE AT: 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Protect from light (keep in outer carton).
Discard unused portion.
Keep from freezing.
Do not autoclave.

Contains no preservative or antioxidant.

The container closure is not made with natural rubber latex.
