# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Morphine Sulfate Injection, USP is a sterile, nonpyrogenic solution of morphine sulfate, free of antioxidants and preservatives to be administered by the intravenous route, available in the following dosage forms and strengths:

|  | Single-dose ‎Carpuject™ Cartridges to be used ONLY with Carpuject™ Holders | 2 mg/mL, 4 mg/mL, 8 mg/mL, 10 mg/mL, and 15 mg/mL |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  | Single-dose ‎NexJect™ Syringes | 2 mg/mL, 4 mg/mL, 8 mg/mL, and 10 mg/mL |
