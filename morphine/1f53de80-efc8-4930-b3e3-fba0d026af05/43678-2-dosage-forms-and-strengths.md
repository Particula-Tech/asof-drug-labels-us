# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Morphine Sulfate Injection, USP, is a sterile, preservative-free, clear, colorless to pale yellow solution.
Morphine Sulfate Injection, USP, is available in the following strengths for intravenous (IV) and intramuscular (IM) administration in a 1 mL single-dose prefilled syringe: 1 mg/0.5 mL, 2 mg/mL, and 4 mg/mL.
