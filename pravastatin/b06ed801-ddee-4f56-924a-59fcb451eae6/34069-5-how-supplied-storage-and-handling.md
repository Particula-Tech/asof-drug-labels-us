# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Pravastatin sodium tablets, USP are supplied as:

Pravastatin Sodium Tablets USP, 10 mg are yellow, rectangular shaped with rounded edges, biconvex, uncoated tablets debossed with ‘Y’ on one side and ‘37’ on the other side.

Bottles of 90 NDC 65862-632-90 Bottles of 500 NDC 65862-632-05 Bottles of 1,000 NDC 65862-632-99 Pravastatin Sodium Tablets USP, 20 mg are white to off-white, rectangular shaped with rounded edges, biconvex, uncoated tablets debossed with ‘T’ on one side and ‘36’ on the other side.

Bottles of 90 NDC 65862-633-90 Bottles of 500 NDC 65862-633-05 Bottles of 1,000 NDC 65862-633-99 Pravastatin Sodium Tablets USP, 40 mg are yellow, rectangular shaped with rounded edges, biconvex, uncoated tablets debossed with ‘Y’ on one side and ‘39’ on the other side.

Bottles of 90 NDC 65862-634-90 Bottles of 500 NDC 65862-634-05 Bottles of 1,000 NDC 65862-634-99

## 16.2 Storage

Store at 20o to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Keep tightly closed (protect from moisture).
Protect from light.
