# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Pravastatin sodium tablets, USP are supplied as:

10 mg of pravastatin sodium: yellow, rectangular shaped with rounded edges, biconvex, uncoated tablets debossed with ‘Y’ on one side and ‘37’ on the other side.

20 mg of pravastatin sodium: white to off-white, rectangular shaped with rounded edges, biconvex, uncoated tablets debossed with ‘T’ on one side and ‘36’ on the other side.

40 mg of pravastatin sodium: yellow, rectangular shaped with rounded edges, biconvex, uncoated tablets debossed with ‘Y’ on one side and ‘39’ on the other side.

80 mg of pravastatin sodium: yellow, oval shaped biconvex, uncoated tablets, debossed with ‘Y’ on one side and ‘38’ on the other side.
