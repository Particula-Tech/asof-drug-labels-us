# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Pravastatin sodium tablets USP are supplied as: 10 mg tablets: Yellow, rectangular shaped with rounded edges, biconvex, uncoated tablets debossed with ‘Y’ on one side and ‘37’ on the other side.
20 mg tablets: White to off-white, rectangular shaped with rounded edges, biconvex, uncoated tablets debossed with ‘T’ on one side and ‘36’ on the other side.
40 mg tablets: Yellow, rectangular shaped with rounded edges, biconvex, uncoated tablets debossed with ‘Y’ on one side and ‘39’ on the other side.
80 mg tablets: Yellow, oval shaped biconvex, uncoated tablets, debossed with ‘Y’ on one side and ‘38’ on the other side.
