# 3. DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Pravastatin Sodium Tablets USP are supplied as:

10 mg tablets: Yellow coloured, capsule shaped, biconvex, film-coated tablets, debossed with 'LU' on one side and 'N01' on the other side.

20 mg tablets: Yellow coloured, capsule shaped, biconvex, film-coated tablets, debossed with 'LU' on one side and 'N02' on the other side.

40 mg tablets: Yellow coloured, capsule shaped, biconvex, film-coated tablets, debossed with 'LU' on one side and 'N03' on the other side.

80 mg tablets: Yellow coloured, oval shaped, biconvex, film-coated tablets, debossed with 'LU' on one side and 'N04' on the other side.
