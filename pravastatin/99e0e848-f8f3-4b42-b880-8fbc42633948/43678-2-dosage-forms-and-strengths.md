# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Pravastatin sodium tablets, USP are supplied as:

- 10 mg of pravastatin sodium: Pink, unscored, round tablet, debossed “TEVA” on one side and “771” on the other side.
- 20 mg of pravastatin sodium: Light-yellow, unscored, round tablet, debossed “TEVA” on one side and “7201” on the other side.
- 40 mg of pravastatin sodium: Light-green, unscored, round tablet, debossed “TEVA” on one side and “7202” on the other side.
