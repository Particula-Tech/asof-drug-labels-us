# 11 DESCRIPTION

GVOKE contains glucagon, an antihypoglycemic agent used to treat severe hypoglycemia.
Glucagon is a single chain containing 29 amino acid residues and has a molecular weight of 3483 and is identical to human glucagon.
Glucagon is produced by solid phase synthesis with subsequent purification.

GVOKE is a clear, colorless to pale yellow, sterile solution for subcutaneous injection available in 0.5 mg per 0.1 mL (auto-injector) or 1 mg per 0.2 mL (auto-injector, pre-filled syringe, and vial and syringe kit).

GVOKE Auto-Injector (HypoPen) and GVOKE Pre-Filled Syringe

Each 0.2 mL of GVOKE contains 1 mg of glucagon, 11.1 mg of trehalose dihydrate NF, and 1.2 mg of 1N sulfuric acid in 209 mg dimethyl sulfoxide diluent.

Each 0.1 mL of GVOKE contains 0.5 mg of glucagon, 5.6 mg of trehalose dihydrate NF, and 0.6 mg of 1N sulfuric acid in 104 mg dimethyl sulfoxide diluent.

GVOKE Vial and Syringe Kit

Each 0.2 mL of GVOKE contains 1 mg of glucagon, 11.1 mg of trehalose dihydrate NF, 5.8 mg of mannitol USP, and 1.32 mg of 1N sulfuric acid in 205 mg dimethyl sulfoxide diluent.
