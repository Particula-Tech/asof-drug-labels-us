# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

BAQSIMI is contraindicated in patients with:

- Pheochromocytoma [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)]
- Insulinoma [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)]
- Known hypersensitivity to glucagon or to any of the excipients in BAQSIMI.
  Allergic reactions have been reported with glucagon and include anaphylactic shock with breathing difficulties and hypotension [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3)]
