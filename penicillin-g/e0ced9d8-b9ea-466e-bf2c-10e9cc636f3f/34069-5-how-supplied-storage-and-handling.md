# HOW SUPPLIED

Penicillin G Potassium for Injection, USP is supplied in dry powder form in vials containing 1,000,000 units (1 million units), 5,000,000 units (5 million units) or 20,000,000 units (20 million units) of crystalline penicillin G as the potassium salt, buffered with sodium citrate to an optimum pH.
Each million units contains approximately 6.9 milligrams of sodium (0.3 mEq) and 65.8 milligrams of potassium (1.68 mEq).

Store the dry powder at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled RoomTemperature].

Sterile constituted solution may be kept in refrigerator 2° to 8°C (36° to 46°F) for 7 days without significant loss of potency.
