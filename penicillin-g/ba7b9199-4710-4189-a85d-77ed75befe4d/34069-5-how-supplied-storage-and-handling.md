# HOW SUPPLIED

Bicillin® C-R 900/300 (penicillin G benzathine and penicillin G procaine injectable suspension) is supplied in packages of 10 disposable syringes as follows:

2 mL size, containing 1,200,000 units per syringe (21 gauge, thin-wall 1-inch needle for pediatric use), NDC 60793-602-10.

Store in a refrigerator, 2° to 8°C (36° to 46°F).

Keep from freezing.
