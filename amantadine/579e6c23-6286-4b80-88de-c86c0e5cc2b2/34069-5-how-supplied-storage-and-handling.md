# HOW SUPPLIED

Amantadine Hydrochloride Capsules, USP, 100 mg are white to off-white powder filled in size "2" empty hard gelatin capsule having red opaque colored cap imprinted with "652" in white ink and red opaque colored body and are supplied as follows:

NDC 65841-835-06 in bottles of 30 capsules with child-resistant closure

NDC 65841-835-01 in bottles of 100 capsules

NDC 65841-835-05 in bottles of 500 capsules

NDC 65841-835-77 in unit-dose blister cartons of 100 Capsules (10 X 10 Unit-dose)

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Dispense in a tight container as defined in the USP.
