# HOW SUPPLIED

Amantadine hydrochloride tablets, USP are available as peach, compressed tablets, round, debossed "832" on one side and "AMT" on the other side.
They are supplied as follows:

| Bottles of 100 with a child-resistant closure, | NDC 0832-0111-00 |
| --- | --- |
| Bottles of 500, | NDC 0832-0111-50 |
| Unit-Dose Cartons of 100, | NDC 0832-0111-03 |
| (10 cards containing 10 tablets each) |  |

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light and moisture.

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP, with child-resistant closure (as required).

Keep out of reach of children.
