# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Lacosamide Injection, USP is supplied as follows:

| NDC | Lacosamide Injection, USP | Package Factor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 25021-791-20 | 200 mg per 20 mL Single-Dose Vial | 10 vials per carton |

Lacosamide Injection, USP is a clear, colorless sterile solution supplied in 20 mL colorless single-dose glass vials.

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

Sterile.

Do not freeze lacosamide injection.
