# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

## 1.1 Refractory Complex Partial Seizures (CPS)

Vigabatrin tablets are indicated as adjunctive therapy for adults and pediatric patients 2 years of age and older with refractory complex partial seizures who have inadequately responded to several alternative treatments and for whom the potential benefits outweigh the risk of vision loss [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
Vigabatrin tablets are not indicated as a first line agent for complex partial seizures.
