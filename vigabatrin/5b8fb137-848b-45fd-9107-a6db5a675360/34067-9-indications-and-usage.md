# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

## 1.1 Refractory Complex Partial Seizures (CPS)

Vigabatrin for oral solution is indicated as adjunctive therapy for adults and pediatric patients 2 years of age and older with refractory complex partial seizures who have inadequately responded to several alternative treatments and for whom the potential benefits outweigh the risk of vision loss [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
Vigabatrin for oral solution is not indicated as a first line agent for complex partial seizures.

## 1.2 Infantile Spasms (IS)

Vigabatrin for oral solution is indicated as monotherapy for pediatric patients with infantile spasms 1 month to 2 years of age for whom the potential benefits outweigh the potential risk of vision loss [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
