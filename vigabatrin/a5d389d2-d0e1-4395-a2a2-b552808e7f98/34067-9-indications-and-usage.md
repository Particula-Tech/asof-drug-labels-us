# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

## 1.1 Refractory Complex Partial Seizures in Adults

SABRIL® is indicated as adjunctive therapy for adult patients with refractory complex partial seizures (CPS) who have inadequately responded to several alternative treatments and for whom the potential benefits outweigh the risk of vision loss [see WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS, Vision Loss (5.1)].
SABRIL is not indicated as a first line agent for complex partial seizures.
