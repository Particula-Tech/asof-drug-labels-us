# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

For injection: 250 mg of bivalirudin, USP as a lyophilized powder in a single-dose vial for reconstitution.
Each vial contains 250 mg of bivalirudin, USP equivalent to an average of 275 mg bivalirudin trifluoroacetate*.

*The range of bivalirudin trifluoroacetate is 270 mg to 280 mg based on a range of trifluoroacetic acid composition of 1.7 to 2.6 equivalents.
