# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Bivalirudin for Injection, USP is supplied as a sterile, white lyophilized powder or cake in single-dose, glass vials.
Each vial contains 250 mg of bivalirudin, USP equivalent to an average of 275 mg of bivalirudin trifluoroacetate*.

*The range of bivalirudin trifluoroacetate is 270 mg to 280 mg based on a range of trifluoroacetic acid composition of 1.7 to 2.6 equivalents.

NDC 67457-256-10 carton containing 10 single-dose vials

## 16.2 Storage

Store bivalirudin for injection dosage units at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
