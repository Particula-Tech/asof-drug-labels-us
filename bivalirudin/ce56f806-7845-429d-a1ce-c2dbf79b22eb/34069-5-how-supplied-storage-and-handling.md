# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Bivalirudin for Injection is supplied as a sterile, lyophilized powder in single-use, glass vials (NDC 0781-3158-94).
Each vial is supplied in cartons of 10 vials (NDC 0781-3158-95).
After reconstitution, each vial delivers 250 mg of Bivalirudin.

Store Bivalirudin dosage units at 20-25°C (68-77°F).
Excursions to 15-30°C permitted.
[see USP Controlled Room Temperature.]
