# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Angiomax is supplied as a sterile, lyophilized powder in single-use, glass vials.
After reconstitution, each vial delivers 250 mg of Angiomax.

NDC 65293-001-01

Store Angiomax dosage units at 20-25°C (68-77°F).
Excursions to 15-30°C permitted.
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]
