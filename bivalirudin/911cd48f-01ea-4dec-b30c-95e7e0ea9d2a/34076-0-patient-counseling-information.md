# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

Advise patients to watch carefully for any signs of bleeding or bruising and to report these to their health care provider when they occur.

Advise patients to discuss with their health care provider their use of any other medications, including over-the-counter medications or herbal products, prior to Angiomax use.
Examples of other medications that should not be taken with Angiomax are warfarin and heparin.
