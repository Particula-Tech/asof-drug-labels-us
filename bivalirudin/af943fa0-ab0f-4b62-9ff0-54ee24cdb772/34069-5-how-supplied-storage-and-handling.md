# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Bivalirudin for Injection is supplied as a sterile, lyophilized powder in single-dose, glass vials.
Each vial contains 250 mg of bivalirudin equivalent to an average of 270 mg of bivalirudin trifluoroacetate*.

*The range of bivalirudin trifluoroacetate is 260 to 280 mg based on a range of trifluoroacetic acid composition of 1.0 to 2.2 equivalents.

| Product No. | NDC No. | Strength |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 506210 | 63323-562-10 | 250 mg per vial 250 mg single-dose vial, packaged in tens. |

Store Bivalirudin for Injection dosage units at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

The container closure is not made with natural rubber latex.
