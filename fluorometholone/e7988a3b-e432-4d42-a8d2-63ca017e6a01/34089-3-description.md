# DESCRIPTION

Fluorometholone Ophthalmic Suspension USP, 0.1% is a sterile, topical anti-inflammatory agent for ophthalmic use.

Fluorometholone, USP is practically white crystalline powder.
It is practically insoluble in water, slightly soluble in alcohol, very slightly soluble in chloroform and practically insoluble in ether.

Chemical name: 9-Fluoro-11ß,17-dihydroxy-6α-methylpregna-1,4-diene-3,20-dione.

Structural formula:

Molecular weight: 376.5 g/mol

Molecular formula: C22H29FO4

Each mL of fluorometholone ophthalmic suspension USP, 0.1% contains:

Active: Fluorometholone USP, 0.1% (1 mg).

Preservative: Benzalkonium chloride, 0.004% (0.04 mg).

Inactives: Edetate disodium; polysorbate 80; polyvinyl alcohol 1.4%; water for injection; sodium chloride; sodium phosphate, dibasic, anhydrous; sodium phosphate, monobasic, monohydrate; and sodium hydroxide to adjust pH.

Fluoromethonolone ophthamic suspension, USP is formulated with a pH from 6.2 to 7.5.
It has an osmolality range of 290 mOsm/kg to 350 mOsm/kg.
