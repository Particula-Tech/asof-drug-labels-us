# HOW SUPPLIED

Fluorometholone Ophthalmic Suspension USP, 0.1% is a sterile, white to off-white homogenous suspension essentially free from foreign particles, filled in 10 mL or 15 mL low density polyethylene white opaque bottles and low density polyethylene white opaque nozzles with high density polyethylene pink caps.

It is available as follows:

5 mL in 10 mL Containers (Filled to 1/2 Capacity): NDC 60219-1585-3

10 mL in 15 mL Containers (Filled to 2/3 Capacity): NDC 60219-1586-6

Storage: Store at 2° to 25°C (36° to 77°F).
Protect from freezing.
Store in an upright position.
