# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Mirtazapine orally disintegrating tablets, USP are supplied as:

- 15 mg orally disintegrating tablets: White to off-white colored, round shaped, beveled edged, uncoated tablets, debossed with '677' on upper face and plain on other side.
- 30 mg orally disintegrating tablets: White to off-white colored, round shaped, beveled edged, uncoated tablets, debossed with '676' on upper face and plain on other side.
- 45 mg orally disintegrating tablets: White to off-white colored, round shaped, beveled edged, uncoated tablets, debossed with '679' on upper face and plain on other side.
