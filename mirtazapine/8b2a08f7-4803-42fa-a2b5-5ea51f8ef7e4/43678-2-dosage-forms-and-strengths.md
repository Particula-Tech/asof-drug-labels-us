# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Mirtazapine tablets are supplied as:

- 7.5 mg tablets: Orange, oval, film-coated tablet debossed with “M1” on one side and plain on the other side.
- 15 mg tablets: Yellow, oblong, biconvex, film-coated tablet, debossed with "M 2" and scored on one side and plain on the other side.
- 30 mg tablets: Beige, oblong, biconvex, film-coated tablet, debossed with "M 3" and scored on one side and plain on the other side.
- 45 mg tablets: White, circular, biconvex, film-coated tablet, debossed with "M4" and plain on the other side.
