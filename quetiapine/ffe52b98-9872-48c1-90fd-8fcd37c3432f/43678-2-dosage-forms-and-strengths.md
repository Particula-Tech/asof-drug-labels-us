# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

- 25 mg tablets are peach, round, biconvex, film-coated tablets, debossed with ‘25’ on one side and plain on the other side
- 50 mg tablets are white, round, biconvex, film-coated tablets, debossed with ‘50’ on one side and plain on the other side
- 100 mg tablets are yellow, round, biconvex, film-coated tablets, debossed with ‘904’ on one side and plain on the other side
- 200 mg tablets are white, round, biconvex, film-coated tablets, debossed with ‘905’ on one side and plain on the other side
- 300 mg tablets are white, capsule-shaped, biconvex, film-coated tablets, debossed with ‘906’ on one side and plain on the other side
- 400 mg tablets are yellow, capsule-shaped, biconvex, film-coated tablets, debossed with ‘907’ on one side and plain on the other side
