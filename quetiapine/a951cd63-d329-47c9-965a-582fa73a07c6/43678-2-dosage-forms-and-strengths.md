# 3. DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

- Quetiapine fumarate tablets, 25 mg (as quetiapine) are pink colored, round, biconvex, film-coated tablets, debossed "LU" on one side and "Y15" on the other side.
- Quetiapine fumarate tablets, 50 mg (as quetiapine) are white, round, biconvex, film-coated tablets, debossed "LU" on one side and "Y16" on the other side.
- Quetiapine fumarate tablets, 100 mg (as quetiapine) are yellow colored, round, biconvex, film-coated tablets, debossed "LU" on one side and "Y17" on the other side.
- Quetiapine fumarate tablets, 200 mg (as quetiapine) are white, round, biconvex, film-coated tablets, debossed "LU" on one side and "Y18" on the other side.
- Quetiapine fumarate tablets, 300 mg (as quetiapine) are white, capsule shape, biconvex, film-coated tablets, debossed "LU" on one side and "Y19" on the other side.
- Quetiapine fumarate tablets, 400 mg (as quetiapine) are yellow colored, capsule shape, biconvex, film-coated tablets, debossed "LU" on one side and "Y20" on the other side.
