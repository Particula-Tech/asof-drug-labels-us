# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

SEROQUEL XR is indicated for the acute and maintenance treatment of schizophrenia [see Clinical Studies (14.1)].

The efficacy of SEROQUEL XR in schizophrenia was established in part, on the basis of extrapolation from the established effectiveness of SEROQUEL.
In addition, the efficacy of SEROQUEL XR was demonstrated in 1 short-term (6-week) controlled trial of schizophrenic inpatients and outpatients [see Clinical Studies (14.1)].
The longer-term benefit of maintaining patients on monotherapy with SEROQUEL XR after achieving a responder status for 16 weeks was demonstrated in a controlled trial [see Clinical Studies (14.1)].
