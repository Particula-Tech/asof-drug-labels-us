- •25 mg tablets are round, white to off-white, standard biconvex tablets, debossed with product identification “54” over “137” on one side and plain on the other side.
- •50 mg tablets are white to off-white, oval, standard biconvex tablets, debossed with product identification “54” on one side and “377” on the other side.
- •100 mg tablets are round, white to off-white, standard biconvex tablets, debossed with product identification “54” over “373” on one side and plain on the other side.
- •200 mg tablets are round, white to off-white, standard biconvex tablets, debossed with product identification “54” over “527” on one side and plain on the other side.
- •300 mg tablets are white to off-white, oval, standard biconvex tablets, debossed with product identification “54 531” on one side and plain on the other side.
- •400 mg tablets are white to off-white, oval, standard biconvex tablets, debossed with product identification “54 735” on one side and plain on the other side.
