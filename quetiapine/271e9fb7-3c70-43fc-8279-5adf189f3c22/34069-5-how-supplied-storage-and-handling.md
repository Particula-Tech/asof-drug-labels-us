# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Quetiapine Fumarate Tablets are supplied as white tablets.
The 25 mg tablets are round and debossed with product identification "54 137" on one side and plain on the other side.

The 50 mg tablets are oval and debossed with product identification "54" on one side and "377" on the other side.

The 100 mg tablets are round and debossed with product identification "54 373" on one side and plain on the other side.

The 200 mg tablets are round and debossed with product identification "54 527" on one side and plain on the other side.

The 300 mg tablets are oval and debossed with product identification "54 531" on one side and plain on the other side.

The 400 mg tablets are oval and debossed with product identification "54 735" on one side and plain on the other side.
