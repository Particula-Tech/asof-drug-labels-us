250 mL (1600 mcg/mL)

List No. 7809 ─ 800 mg Dopamine HCl in 5% Dextrose Injection, USP

500 mL (1600 mcg/mL)

List No. 7810 ─ 800 mg Dopamine HCl in 5% Dextrose Injection, USP

250 mL (3200 mcg/mL)

Exposure of pharmaceutical products to heat should be minimized.
Avoid excessive heat.
Protect from freezing.
It is recommended that the product be stored at room temperature (25°C); however, brief exposure up to 40°C does not adversely affect the product.

Avoid contact with alkalies (including sodium bicarbonate), oxidizing agents or iron salts.

NOTE─ Do not use the injection if it is darker than slightly yellow or discolored in any other way.
