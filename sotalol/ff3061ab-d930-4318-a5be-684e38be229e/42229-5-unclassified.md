# 17 RISK OF HYPOGLYCEMIA

Inform parents or caregivers that there is a risk of hypoglycemia when sotalolis given to patients who are fasting or who are vomiting.
Inform patients to notify their healthcare provider if they experience they experience symptoms of hypoglycemia.
[See Warnings and Precautions (5.7)].
