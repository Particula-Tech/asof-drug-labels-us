# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

## 1.1 Life-Threatening Ventricular Arrhythmia

Sotalol is indicated for the treatment of documented, life-threatening ventricular arrhythmias, such as sustained ventricular tachycardia in adult and pediatric patients.

Limitations of Use:

﻿Sotalol has not been shown to enhance survival in patients with life-threatening ventricular arrhythmias.

## 1.2 Delay in Recurrence of Atrial Fibrillation/Atrial Flutter

Sotalol is indicated for the maintenance of normal sinus rhythm (delay in time to recurrence of atrial fibrillation/atrial flutter (AFIB/AFL)) in adults and pediatric patients with highly symptomatic AFIB/AFL who are currently in sinus rhythm.

Limitation of Use:

Because sotalol can cause life-threatening ventricular arrhythmias, reserve its use for patients in whom AFIB/AFL is highly symptomatic.
