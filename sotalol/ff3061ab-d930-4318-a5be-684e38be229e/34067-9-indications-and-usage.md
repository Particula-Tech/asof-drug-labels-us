# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

## 1.1 Delay in Recurrence of Atrial Fibrillation/Atrial Flutter

Sotalol is indicated for the maintenance of normal sinus rhythm [delay in time to recurrence of atrial fibrillation/atrial flutter (AFIB/AFL)] in patients with symptomatic AFIB/AFL who are currently in sinus rhythm.

## 1.2 Life-Threatening Ventricular Arrhythmia

Sotalol is indicated for the treatment of life-threatening ventricular tachycardia.
Antiarrhythmic drugs may not enhance survival in patients with ventricular arrhythmias.
