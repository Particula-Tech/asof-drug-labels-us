# 6 ADVERSE REACTIONS

The following adverse reactions are described elsewhere:

- Proarrhythmia (5.1, 5.2)
- Negative inotropy (5.3, 5.4)

Adverse reactions related to sotalol use are those which are typical of its Class II (beta- blocking) and Class III (cardiac action potential duration prolongation) effects.
The common documented beta-blocking adverse reactions (bradycardia, dyspnea, and fatigue) and Class III effects (QT interval prolongation) are dose related.
