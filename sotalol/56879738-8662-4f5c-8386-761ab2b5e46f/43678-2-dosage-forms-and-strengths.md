# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Sotalol Hydrochloride Tablets are supplied as capsule-shaped, light-blue, scored tablets:

- 80 mg imprinted with “B104” on one side and 80 mg on the other
- 120 mg imprinted with “B105” on one side and 120 mg on the other
- 60 mg imprinted with “B105” on one side and 160 mg on the other
