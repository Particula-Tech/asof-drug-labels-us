# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Sotalol Hydrochloride Tablets USP are capsule-shaped light-blue scored tablets, debossed ‘B104’, ‘B105’, and ‘B106’ on one side and scored on the other side are available as follows:

80 mg strength, bottles of 100 (NDC 76385-114-01) 80 mg strength, bottles of 500 (NDC 76385-114-50) 120 mg strength, bottles of 100 (NDC 76385-115-01) 120 mg strength, bottles of 500 (NDC 76385-115-50) 160 mg strength, bottles of 100 (NDC 76385-116-01) 160 mg strength, bottles of 500 (NDC 76385-116-50)

Sotalol Hydrochloride Tablets USP (AF) are capsule-shaped white to off-white scored tablets, debossed ‘B107’, ‘B108’, and ‘B109’ on one side and scored on the other side are available as follows:

80 mg strength, bottles of 100 (NDC 76385-125-01) 80 mg strength, bottles of 500 (NDC 76385-125-50) 120 mg strength, bottles of 100 (NDC 76385-126-01) 120 mg strength, bottles of 500 (NDC 76385-126-50) 160 mg strength, bottles of 100 (NDC 76385-127-01) 160 mg strength, bottles of 500 (NDC 76385-127-50)

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15-30°C (59-86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
