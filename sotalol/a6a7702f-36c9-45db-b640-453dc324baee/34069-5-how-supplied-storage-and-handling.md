# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Betapace (sotalol hydrochloride); capsule-shaped light-blue scored tablets, imprinted with the strength and “BETAPACE,” are available as follows:

1. NDC 83107-005-10 80 mg strength, bottle of 100
2. NDC 83107-006-10 120 mg strength, bottle of 100
3. NDC 83107-007-10 160 mg strength, bottle of 100

Betapace AF (sotalol hydrochloride); capsule-shaped white scored tablets, imprinted with the strength and “BHCP” are available as follows:

1. NDC 83107-008-60 80 mg strength, bottle of 60
2. NDC 83107-009-60 120 mg strength, bottle of 60
3. NDC 83107-010-60 160 mg strength, bottle of 60

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15 to 30°C (59 to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
