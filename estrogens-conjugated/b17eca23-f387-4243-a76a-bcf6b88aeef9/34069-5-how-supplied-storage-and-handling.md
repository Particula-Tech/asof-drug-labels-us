# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Conjugated estrogens tablets, USP

• Each green oval biconvex coated tablet contains 0.3 mg, in bottles of 100 (NDC 50742-387-01) and 1,000 (NDC 50742-387-10).

• Each blue oval biconvex coated tablet contains 0.45 mg, in bottles of 100 (NDC 50742-388-01) and 1,000 (NDC 50742-388-10).

• Each peach oval biconvex coated tablet contains 0.625 mg, in bottles of 100 (NDC 50742-389-01) and 1,000 (NDC 50742-389-10).

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Dispense in a well-closed container, as defined in the USP.
