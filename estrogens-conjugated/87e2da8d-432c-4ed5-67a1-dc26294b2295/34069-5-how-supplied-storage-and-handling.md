# HOW SUPPLIED

NDC 0046-0749-05–Each package provides one SECULE® vial containing 25 mg of conjugated estrogens, USP, for injection (also lactose 200 mg, sodium citrate 12.2 mg, and simethicone 0.2 mg).
The pH is adjusted with sodium hydroxide or hydrochloric acid.

Premarin Intravenous (conjugated estrogens, USP) for injection is prepared by cryodesiccation.

SECULE®-Registered trademark to designate a vial containing an injectable preparation in dry form.
