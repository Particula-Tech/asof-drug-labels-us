# INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Azelastine Hydrochloride Nasal Solution (Nasal Spray), 0.1% (137 mcg per spray) is indicated for the treatment of the symptoms of seasonal allergic rhinitis such as rhinorrhea, sneezing, and nasal pruritus in adults and children 5 years and older, and for the treatment of the symptoms of vasomotor rhinitis, such as rhinorrhea, nasal congestion and postnasal drip in adults and children 12 years and older.
