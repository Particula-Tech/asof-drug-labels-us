# 5 WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS

## 5.1 Somnolence in Activities Requiring Mental Alertness

In clinical trials, the occurrence of somnolence has been reported in some patients taking Azelastine HCl Nasal Spray [see Adverse Reactions (6.1)].
Patients should be cautioned against engaging in hazardous occupations requiring complete mental alertness and motor coordination such as operating machinery or driving a motor vehicle after administration of Azelastine HCl Nasal Spray.
Concurrent use of Azelastine HCl Nasal Spray with alcohol or other central nervous system depressants should be avoided because additional reductions in alertness and additional impairment of central nervous system performance may occur [see Drug Interactions (7.1)].
