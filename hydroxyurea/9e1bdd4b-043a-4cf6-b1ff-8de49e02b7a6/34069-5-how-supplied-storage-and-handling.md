# HOW SUPPLIED

Hydroxyurea Capsules USP 500 mg are dark green opaque (cap) printed “724” in white ink/ pink opaque (body) printed “par” in black ink.
They are supplied in bottles of 100 (NDC# 49884-724-01).

Dispense in a tight container as defined in the USP.

Storage -Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15°-30°C (59°-86°F); [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Keep tightly closed.
