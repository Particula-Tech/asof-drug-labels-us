# DESCRIPTION

Hydroxyurea, an antineoplastic agent, available for oral use as capsules.
Each capsule, for oral administration contains 500 mg hydroxyurea.
In addition, each capsule contains the following inactive ingredients: colloidal silicon dioxide, colorants (D&C Yellow No. 10, FD&C Blue No. 1 and FD&C Red No. 40), gelatin, magnesium stearate and titanium dioxide.
Imprinting ink constituents: D&C Yellow No. 10 Aluminum Lake, FD&C Blue No. 1 Aluminum Lake, FD&C Blue No. 2 Aluminum Lake, FD&C Red No. 40 Aluminum Lake, pharmaceutical glaze, pharmaceutical shellac, propylene glycol, polyvinylpyrrolidone, sodium hydroxide, synthetic black iron oxide and titanium dioxide.

Hydroxyurea occurs as an essentially tasteless, white crystalline powder.
