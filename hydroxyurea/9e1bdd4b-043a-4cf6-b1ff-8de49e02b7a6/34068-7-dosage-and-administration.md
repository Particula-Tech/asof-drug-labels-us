Patients must be able to follow directions regarding drug administration and their monitoring and care.

Fetal hemoglobin (HbF) levels may be used to evaluate the efficacy of hydroxyurea capsules in clinical use.
Obtain HbF levels every three to four months.
Monitor for an increase in HbF of at least two-fold over the baseline value.

Hydroxyurea capsules causes macrocytosis, which may mask the incidental development of folic acid deficiency.
Prophylactic administration of folic acid is recommended.

Hydroxyurea capsules is a cytotoxic drug.
Follow applicable special handling and disposal procedures [see REFERENCES (15)].

## 2.2 Dose Modification for Renal Impairment

Reduce the dose of hydroxyurea capsules by 50% in patients with creatinine clearance of less than 60 mL/min or with end-stage renal disease (ESRD) [see Use in Specific Populations (8.6) and Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].
Creatinine clearance values were obtained using 24-hour urine collections.

| Creatinine Clearance Recommended Hydroxyurea Capsules Initial Dose(ml/min) (mg/kg daily) |
| --- |
| ≥60 15 |
| <60 or ESRD* 7.5 |
| *On dialysis days, administer hydroxyurea capsules to patients with ESRD following hemodialysis. |

Monitor the hematologic parameters closely in these patients.
