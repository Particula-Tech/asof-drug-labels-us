# HOW SUPPLIED

DROXIA® (hydroxyurea capsules, USP).

200 mg capsules packaged in HDPE bottles of 60 with a plastic safety screw cap.
(NDC 0003-6335-17).
The cap and body are opaque blue-green.

300 mg capsules packaged in HDPE bottles of 60 with a plastic safety screw cap.
(NDC 0003-6336-17).
The cap and body are opaque purple.
The capsule is marked in black ink on both the cap and body with “DROXIA” and “6336”.

400 mg capsules packaged in HDPE bottles of 60 with a plastic safety screw cap.
(NDC 0003-6337-17).

## Storage

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15-30°C (59-86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Keep tightly closed.
