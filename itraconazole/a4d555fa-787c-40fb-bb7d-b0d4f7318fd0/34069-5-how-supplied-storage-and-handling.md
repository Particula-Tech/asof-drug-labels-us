# HOW SUPPLIED

SPORANOX ®(itraconazole) Capsules are available containing 100 mg of itraconazole, with a blue opaque cap and pink transparent body, imprinted with "JANSSEN" and "SPORANOX 100."
The capsules are supplied in unit-dose blister packs of 3 × 10 capsules (NDC 50458-290-01) and bottles of 30 capsules (NDC 50458-290-04).

Store at controlled room temperature 15°–25°C (59°–77°F).
Protect from light and moisture.
