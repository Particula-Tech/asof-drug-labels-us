# HOW SUPPLIED

SPORANOX® (itraconazole) Capsules are available containing 100 mg of itraconazole, with a blue opaque cap and pink transparent body, imprinted with “JANSSEN” and “SPORANOX 100.”
The capsules are supplied in unit-dose blister packs of 3 x 10 capsules (NDC 50458-290-01), bottles of 30 capsules (NDC 50458-290-04) and in the PulsePak® containing 7 blister packs x 4 capsules each (NDC 50458-290-28).

Store at controlled room temperature 15°-25°C (59°-77°F).
Protect from light and moisture.

Keep out of reach of children.
