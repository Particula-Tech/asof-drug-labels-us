# OVERDOSAGE

Itraconazole is not removed by dialysis.

There are limited data on the outcomes of patients ingesting high doses of itraconazole.
In patients taking either 1000 mg of SPORANOX® (itraconazole) Oral Solution or up to 3000 mg of SPORANOX® Capsules, or b.i.d. dosing for four days with SPORANOX® Injection, the adverse event profile was similar to that observed at recommended doses.
