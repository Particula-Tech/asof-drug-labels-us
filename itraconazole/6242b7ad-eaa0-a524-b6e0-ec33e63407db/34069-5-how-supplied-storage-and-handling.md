# HOW SUPPLIED

Itraconazole Capsules are available containing 100 mg of itraconazole, with a blue opaque cap and pink transparent body, imprinted with “PP 100.”
The capsules are supplied in bottles of 30 capsules (NDC 10147-1700-3) and in the DosingPak containing 7 blister packs x 4 capsules each (NDC 10147-1700-7).

Store at controlled room temperature 15°-25°C (59°-77°F).
Protect from light and moisture.

Keep out of reach of children.
