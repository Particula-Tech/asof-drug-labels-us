# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

How Supplied

ONMEL is available containing 200 mg of itraconazole, as a white to slightly grey, oblong, biconvex tablet engraved with “BARRIER” on one side and “It 200” on the other side.
Each carton (NDC 0145-2500-04) contains two blister cards of 14 tablets each (NDC 0145-2500-02).

Storage

Store at controlled room temperature 15° to 25°C (59° to 77°F).
Excursions permitted to 15˚ to 30˚C (59˚ to 86˚F).
Protect from light and moisture.
Keep out of reach of children.
