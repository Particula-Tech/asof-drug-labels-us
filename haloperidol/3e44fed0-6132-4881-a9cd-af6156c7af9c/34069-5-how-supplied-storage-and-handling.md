# HOW SUPPLIED

Haloperidol Oral Solution USP (Concentrate) 2 mg per mL (as the lactate) is colorless, odorless, and tasteless solution.
This product is available in 120 mL bottles, with dropper graduated at 0.5 mg, and 1 mg, 1.5 mg, 2 mg, 3 mg, 4 mg, and 15 mL bottles, with dropper graduated at 0.5 mg, and 1 mg.

Store at 20°-25°C (68°-77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature] Protect from freezing.
Protect from light.

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as described in the USP.
