# HOW SUPPLIED

Haloperidol Tablets, USP are available containing 0.5 mg, 1 mg, 2 mg, 5 mg, 10 mg or 20 mg of haloperidol.

The 0.5 mg tablets are orange, round tablets debossed with MYLAN over 351 on one side of the tablet and scored on the other side.
They are available as follows:

bottles of 100 tablets

NDC 0378-0351-10

bottles of 1000 tablets
