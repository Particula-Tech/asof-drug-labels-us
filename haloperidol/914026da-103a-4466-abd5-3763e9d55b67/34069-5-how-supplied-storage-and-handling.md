# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Haloperidol Decanoate Injection, equivalent to 100 mg/mL haloperidol, is available as follows:

NDC 0143-9295-01 - 1 mL vial individually boxed.

NDC 0143-9296-01 - 5 mL multiple dose vial individually boxed.

Storage and Handling

Store at room temperature 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) with excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F and 86°F).
Do not refrigerate or freeze.
Protect from light.
Discard unused portion.
