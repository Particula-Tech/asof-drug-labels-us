# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

OSENI is contraindicated in patients with:

- Established NYHA Class III or IV heart failure at the time of OSENI initiation [see Boxed Warning].
- A history of serious hypersensitivity reaction to alogliptin, pioglitazone, or any of the excipients in OSENI.
  Reactions such as anaphylaxis, angioedema and severe cutaneous adverse reactions have been reported [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3), Adverse reactions (6.2)].
