# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Serious hypersensitivity reaction to alogliptin or pioglitazone or any of the excipients in OSENI, such as anaphylaxis, angioedema and severe cutaneous adverse reactions have been reported [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3), Adverse reactions (6.2)].

Do not initiate in patients with NYHA Class III or IV heart failure [see Boxed Warning].
