# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Alogliptin tablets is contraindicated in patients with a history of serious hypersensitivity to alogliptin or any of the excipients in Alogliptin tablets.
Reactions such as anaphylaxis, angioedema and severe cutaneous adverse reactions have been reported [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3), Adverse Reactions (6.2)].
