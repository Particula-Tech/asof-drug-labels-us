# 3. DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

- Simvastatin Tablets 10 mg are brick red colored,oval shaped, biconvex,film-coated tablets, debossed “S 4” on one side and plain on the other side
- Simvastatin Tablets 20 mg are brick red colored,oval shaped, biconvex,film-coated tablets, debossed “S 5” on one side and plain on the other side.
- Simvastatin Tablets 40 mg are brick red colored,oval shaped, biconvex,film-coated tablets,debossed “S 6” on one side and plain on the other side
- Simvastatin Tablets 80 mg are brick red colored, capsule-shaped, biconvex, film-coated tablets, debossed with “SMV” on one side and “80” on the other side
