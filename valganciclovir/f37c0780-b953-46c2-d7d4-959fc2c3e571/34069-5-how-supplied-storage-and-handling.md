# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Valganciclovir Tablets USP, 450 mg are pink colored, oval shaped, film coated tablets debossed with ‘RDY’ on one side and ‘762’ on other side, and are supplied in bottles of 60’s and 500’s and unit dose package of 100’s (10 x 10).

Bottles of 60 NDC 55111-762-60

Bottles of 500 NDC 55111-762-05

Unit-dose packages of 100 (10 x 10) NDC 55111-762-78

Store at 20°C-25°C (68°F-77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
