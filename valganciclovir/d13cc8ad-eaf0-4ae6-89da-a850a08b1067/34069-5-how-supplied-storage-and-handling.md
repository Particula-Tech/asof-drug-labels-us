# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Valganciclovir tablets, USP: Supplied as 450 mg, pink, film-coated biconvex oval tablets debossed with “VL” on one side and “450” on the other side.
Each film-coated tablet contains 496.3 mg of Valganciclovir Hydrochloride, USP equivalent to 450 mg of Valganciclovir.
Valganciclovir tablets, USP are supplied in bottles of 60 tablets with child-resistant closure (NDC 27241-158-60).

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
