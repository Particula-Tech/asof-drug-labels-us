# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Valganciclovir tablets USP, 450 mg are pink, oval, biconvex, film-coated tablets, debossed with "J" on one side and "156" on the other side.
Each film-coated tablet contains 496.3 mg of valganciclovir hydrochloride, USP equivalent to 450 mg of valganciclovir.
Valganciclovir tablets are supplied in: Bottles of 60 tablets NDC 31722-832-60 Blister card of 10 Unit dose tablets NDC 31722-832-31 Blister pack of 100 (10 x 10) Unit dose tablets NDC 31722-832-32 Store at 25oC; excursions permitted between 15o and 30oC (59o and 86oF).
