# HOW SUPPLIED

It is supplied in:

Child-resistant bottles of 30 Capsules (NDC 43386-999-03)

Child-resistant bottles of 100 Capsules (NDC 43386-999-01)

Bottles of 500 Capsules (NDC 43386-999-05)

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
