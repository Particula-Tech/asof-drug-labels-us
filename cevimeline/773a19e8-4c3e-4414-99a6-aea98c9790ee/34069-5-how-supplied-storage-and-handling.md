# HOW SUPPLIED

Cevimeline is available as white, hard gelatin capsules containing 30 mg of cevimeline hydrochloride.
Cevimeline hydrochloride capsules have a white opaque cap and a white opaque body.
The capsules are imprinted with “EVOXAC” on the cap and “30 mg” on the body with a black bar above “30 mg”.
It is supplied in child resistant bottles of:
