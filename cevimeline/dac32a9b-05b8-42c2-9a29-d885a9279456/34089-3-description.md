# 11 DESCRIPTION

EVOXAC capsules, for oral use, and oral solution contain cevimeline hydrochloride, a salt of cevimeline.
Cevimeline is a cholinergic agonist and has a molecular weight of 244.79.
It is a white to off white crystalline powder with a melting point range of 201 to 203°C.
It is freely soluble in alcohol and chloroform, very soluble in water, and virtually insoluble in ether.
The pH of a 1% solution ranges from 4.6 to 5.6.
Cevimeline is cis -2’-methylspiro{1-azabicyclo [2.2.2] octane-3, 5’-[1,3] oxathiolane} hydrochloride, hydrate (2:1).

EVOXAC (cevimeline hydrochloride) capsules

Each capsule contains 30 mg of cevimeline hydrochloride (equivalent to 25.4 mg of cevimeline) and the following inactive ingredients: hydroxypropyl cellulose, lactose monohydrate, and magnesium stearate.

EVOXAC (cevimeline hydrochloride) oral solution

Each 5 mL contains 30 mg of cevimeline hydrochloride (equivalent to 25.4 mg of cevimeline) and the following inactive ingredients: anhydrous citric acid, carboxymethylcellulose sodium, edetate disodium, glycerin, masking agent flavor, methylparaben, propylparaben, propylene glycol, purified water, raspberry flavor, sodium citrate dihydrate, sodium thiosulfate, sorbitol solution 70%, sucralose.
Each 5 mL of EVOXAC oral solution contains 10 mg of sodium.
