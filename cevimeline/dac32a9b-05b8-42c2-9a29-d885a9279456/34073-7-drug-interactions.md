# 7 DRUG INTERACTIONS

| Beta-adrenergic Antagonists |  |
| --- | --- |
| Prevention or Management | Closely monitor patients who concomitantly use EVOXAC with beta-adrenergic antagonists. |
| Mechanism and Clinical Effect(s) | Concomitant use of EVOXAC with beta-adrenergic antagonists may result in cardiac conduction disturbances. |
| Muscarinic Antagonists |  |
| Prevention or Management | Closely monitor patients who concomitantly use EVOXAC with muscarinic antagonists. |
| Mechanism and Clinical Effect(s) | Concomitant use of EVOXAC with muscarinic antagonists may interfere with antimuscarinic drugs effects. |
| Cholinergic Agonists |  |
| Prevention or Management | Closely monitor patients who concomitantly use EVOXAC with other cholinergic agonists. |
| Mechanism and Clinical Effect(s) | Concomitant use of EVOXAC with other cholinergic agonists can potentiate the cholinergic effects of EVOXAC and increase the risk of adverse reactions [see Warnings and Precautions (5.4)]. |
| CYP2D6 and CYP3A3/4 Inhibitors |  |
| Prevention or Management | Closely monitor patients who concomitantly use EVOXAC with CYP2D6 and/or CYP3A3/4 inhibitors for EVOXAC-associated adverse reactions. |
| Mechanism and Clinical Effect(s) | CYP2D6 and CYP3A3/4 inhibitors can inhibit the metabolism of cevimeline and may increase the risk of EVOXAC-associated adverse reactions. |
