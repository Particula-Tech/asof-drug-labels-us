# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

Advise patients to read the FDA-approved patient labeling (Patient Information).

Cardiovascular Adverse Reactions

Advise patients that EVOXAC can alter cardiac conduction and/or heart rate [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].

Pulmonary Adverse Reactions

Advise patients that EVOXAC can increase airway resistance, bronchial smooth muscle tone, and bronchial secretions [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)].

Ophthalmic Adverse Reactions

Advise patients that EVOXAC may cause visual disturbances, especially at night, that could impair their ability to drive safely or perform hazardous activities [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3)].

Cholinergic Adverse Reactions

If hyperhidrosis occurs, advise patients to keep hydrated and inform their healthcare provider.
Inform patients that EVOXAC may cause contractions of the gallbladder, biliary smooth muscle and ureteral smooth muscle, resulting in complications such as cholecystitis, cholangitis, biliary obstruction, renal colic and nephrolithiasis [see Warnings and Precautions (5.4)].

Lactation

Advise patients that breastfeed is not recommended during treatment with EVOXAC and for one day after the last dose [see Use in Specific Populations (8.2)].
