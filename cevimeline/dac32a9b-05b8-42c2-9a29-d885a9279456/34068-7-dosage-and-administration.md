# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

EVOXAC Capsules

The recommended dosage of EVOXAC capsules is 30 mg (1 capsule) orally three times a day with or without food.

EVOXAC Oral Solution

The recommended dosage of EVOXAC oral solution is 30 mg (5 mL) three times a day taken with a glass of water.
Administer EVOXAC with or without food.

Missed Doses

If a dose is missed, administer it as soon as possible.
If less than 4 hours remain before the next scheduled dose, wait until the next scheduled dose.
Do not double the dose to make up for the missed dose.
