# 14 CLINICAL STUDIES

The efficacy of EVOXAC capsules for the treatment of symptoms of dry mouth in adults with Sjögren’s disease was studied in three trials (Trial 1, 2, and 3).
Trial 1 was a 6-week, randomized, double blind, placebo-controlled trial conducted in 75 adult subjects (10 men, 65 women) with a mean age of 53.6 years (range 33-75).
The racial distribution of enrolled subjects was 92% White, 1% Black, and 7% other.
EVOXAC capsules 30 mg three times daily and EVOXAC capsules 60 mg three times daily (unapproved dosage), administered orally, were compared to placebo.
Subjects were evaluated by a measure called global improvement, which is defined as a response of "better" to the question, "Please rate the overall condition of your dry mouth now compared with how you felt before starting treatment in this study."
Subjects also had the option of selecting “worse” or “no change” as answers.
Seventy-six percent of subjects in the EVOXAC capsules 30 mg three times daily group reported global improvement in their dry mouth symptoms compared to 35% of the subjects in the placebo group.
This difference was statistically significant at p=0.0043.
There was no evidence that subjects in the EVOXAC capsules 60 mg three times daily group had better global evaluation scores than subjects in the EVOXAC capsules 30 mg three times daily group.

Trial 2 was a 12-week, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial conducted in 197 adult subjects (10 men, 187 women) with a mean age of 54.5 years (range 23-74).
The racial distribution of enrolled subjects was 92% White, 3% Black, and 6% other.
EVOXAC capsules 15 mg three times daily (unapproved dosage) and EVOXAC capsules 30 mg three times daily, administered orally, were compared placebo.
Statistically significant global improvement in the symptoms of dry mouth (p=0.0004) was seen for the EVOXAC capsules 30 mg three times daily group compared to placebo, but not for the EVOXAC capsules 15 mg three times daily group when compared to placebo.
EVOXAC capsules 30 mg three times daily demonstrated statistically significant increases in salivary flow compared to placebo.

Trial 3 was a 12-week, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial conducted in 212 adult subjects (11 men, 201 women) with a mean age of 55.3 years (range 24-75).
The racial distribution of enrolled subjects was 89% White, 2% Black, and 9% other.
EVOXAC capsules 15 mg three times daily (unapproved dosage) and EVOXAC capsules 30 mg three times daily were compared to placebo.
No statistically significant differences were noted in the patient global evaluations.
However, there was a higher placebo response rate in this trial compared to Trial 1 and 2.
The EVOXAC capsules 30 mg three times daily group showed a statistically significant increase in salivary flow from pre-dose to post-dose compared to placebo (p=0.0017).

The efficacy of EVOXAC oral solution was established based on trials of EVOXAC capsules [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].
