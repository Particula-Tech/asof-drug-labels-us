# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

- Capsules: 30 mg of cevimeline hydrochloride as a white, hard gelatin capsule with “EVOXAC” on the cap and “30 mg” with a black bar above “30 mg” on the body.
- Oral Solution: 30 mg of cevimeline hydrochloride per 5 mL as a clear, colorless to slightly yellow solution.
