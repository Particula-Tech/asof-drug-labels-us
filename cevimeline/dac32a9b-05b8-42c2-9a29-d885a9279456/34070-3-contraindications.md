# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

EVOXAC is contraindicated in patients:

- when miosis is undesirable, e.g., acute iritis and narrow-angle (angle-closure) glaucoma [see Warnings and Precautions (5.4)].
- with known hypersensitivity to cevimeline.
- with uncontrolled asthma [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)].
