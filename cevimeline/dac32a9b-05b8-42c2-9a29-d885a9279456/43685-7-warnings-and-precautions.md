# 5 WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS

## 5.1 Cardiovascular Adverse Reactions

EVOXAC can alter cardiac conduction and/or heart rate.
Monitor patients with a history of atrial fibrillation or cardiovascular disease during treatment with EVOXAC.

## 5.2 Pulmonary Adverse Reactions

EVOXAC can increase airway resistance, bronchial smooth muscle tone, and bronchial secretions.
Monitor patients with controlled asthma, chronic bronchitis, or chronic obstructive pulmonary disease during treatment with EVOXAC.

## 5.3 Ophthalmic Adverse Reactions

EVOXAC may cause ophthalmic adverse reactions.
Topical ophthalmic dosage forms of muscarinic agonists have been reported to cause visual blurring which may result in decreased visual acuity (especially at night and in patients with central lens changes) and impairment of depth perception.
EVOXAC is not approved for topical ophthalmic use.
Advise caution while driving or performing hazardous activities.

## 5.4 Cholinergic Adverse Reactions

EVOXAC is a cholinergic agonist and can cause adverse reactions related to its cholinergic effects.
Monitor patients for adverse reactions related EVOXAC’s cholinergic effects which may include headache, visual disturbance, lacrimation, sweating, respiratory distress, gastrointestinal spasm, nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, atrioventricular block, tachycardia, bradycardia, hypotension, hypertension, shock, mental confusion, cardiac arrhythmia, and tremors.
Hyperhidrosis leading to dehydration may occur.
If hyperhidrosis occurs, advise patients to keep hydrated and inform their healthcare provider.

Contractions of the gallbladder or biliary smooth muscle may occur which could precipitate complications such as cholecystitis, cholangitis and biliary obstruction.
An increase in the ureteral smooth muscle tone may occur which could precipitate renal colic or ureteral reflux in patients with nephrolithiasis.
Closely monitor patients with a history of nephrolithiasis or cholelithiasis during treatment with EVOXAC.
