# 10 OVERDOSAGE

Overdoses have been reported in clinical trials and post-marketing use of liraglutide.
Effects have included severe nausea, severe vomiting and severe hypoglycemia.
In the event of overdosage, consider contacting the Poison Helpline (1-800-222-1222) or a medical toxicologist for additional overdosage management recommendations.
Initiate appropriate supportive treatment according to the patient’s clinical signs and symptoms.
