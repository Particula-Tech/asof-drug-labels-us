# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

VICTOZA is contraindicated in patients with a:

- •personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC) or in patients with Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia syndrome type 2 (MEN 2) [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
- •serious hypersensitivity reaction to liraglutide or to any of the excipients in VICTOZA.
  Serious hypersensitivity reactions including anaphylactic reactions and angioedema have been reported with VICTOZA [see Warnings and Precautions (5.6)].
