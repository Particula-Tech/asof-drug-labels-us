# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Liraglutide Injection is indicated:

- as an adjunct to diet and exercise to improve glycemic control in adults and pediatric patients aged 10 years and older with type 2 diabetes mellitus.

Limitations of Use:

Liraglutide Injection should not be used in patients with type 1 diabetes mellitus.

Liraglutide Injection contains liraglutide and should not be coadministered with other liraglutide-containing products.
