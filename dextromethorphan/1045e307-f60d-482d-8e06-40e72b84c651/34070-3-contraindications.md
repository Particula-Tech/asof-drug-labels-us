# CONTRAINDICATIONS

Hypersensitivity to any of the ingredients.
Do not use in the newborn, in premature infants, in nursing mothers, or in patients with severe hypertension or severe coronary artery disease.
Do not use dextromethorphan in patients receiving monoamine oxidase inhibitors (MAOIs) (see Drug Interactions).

Antihistamines should not be used to treat lower respiratory tract conditions including asthma.
