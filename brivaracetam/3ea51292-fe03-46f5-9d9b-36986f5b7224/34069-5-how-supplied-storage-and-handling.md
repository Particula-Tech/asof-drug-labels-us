# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Brivaracetam Injection

- 50 mg/5 mL is a clear, colorless, sterile solution supplied in colorless single-dose glass vials.

|  | Carton of 10 vials | NDC 14335-420-10 |
| --- | --- | --- |

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F).
See USP Controlled Room Temperature.
Do not freeze brivaracetam injection.

Brivaracetam injection vials are single-dose only [see Dosage and Administration (2.3)].
