# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

|  | 300 mL bottles | NDC 72673-031-10 |

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at 20°C to 25ºC (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F).
See USP Controlled Room Temperature.
Do not freeze brivaracatam oral solution.
Discard any unused brivaracatam oral solution remaining after 5 months of first opening the bottle.
