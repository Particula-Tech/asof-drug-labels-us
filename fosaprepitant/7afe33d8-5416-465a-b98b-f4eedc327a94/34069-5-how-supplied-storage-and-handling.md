# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

FOCINVEZ (fosaprepitant injection) contains 150 mg/50 mL (3 mg/mL) of fosaprepitant as a clear and colorless ready-to-use injection solution in a single-dose vial.
Supplied as follows:

NDC 70121-2631-1 1 vial per carton.

Storage

- Refrigerate FOCINVEZ at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F).

- FOCINVEZ vials, when kept in original carton, can remain at room temperature 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) for up to 90 days.
