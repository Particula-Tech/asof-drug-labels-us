# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Fosaprepitant for Injection is available in a single dose glass vial containing 150 mg of fosaprepitant as a white to off-white fine fluffy powder or dry cake for reconstitution available as follows:

NDC 67457-889-10 carton containing 1 vial

Storage Fosaprepitant for injection vials must be refrigerated, store at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F).

The reconstituted final drug solution is stable for 24 hours at ambient room temperature [at or below 25°C (77°F)].
Discard unused portion.
