# 10 OVERDOSAGE

There is no specific information on the treatment of overdosage with fosaprepitant or aprepitant.

In the event of overdose, fosaprepitant should be discontinued and general supportive treatment and monitoring should be provided.
Because of the antiemetic activity of fosaprepitant, drug-induced emesis may not be effective in cases of fosaprepitant overdosage.

Aprepitant is not removed by hemodialysis.
