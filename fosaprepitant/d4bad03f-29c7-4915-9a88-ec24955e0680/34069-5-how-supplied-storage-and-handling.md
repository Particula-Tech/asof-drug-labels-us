# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

No. 3061 — Single-dose glass vial containing 150 mg of fosaprepitant as a white to off-white lyophilized powder for reconstitution.
Supplied as follows:

| NDC 0006-3061-02 | 1 vial per carton. |
| --- | --- |

Storage

Emend for injection vials must be refrigerated, store at 2°C-8°C (36°F-46°F).

The reconstituted final drug solution is stable for 24 hours at ambient room temperature [at or below 25°C (77°F)].
Discard unused portion.
