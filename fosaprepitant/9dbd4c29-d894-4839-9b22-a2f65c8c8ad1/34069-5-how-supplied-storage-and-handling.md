# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

One single-dose glass vial containing 150 mg fosaprepitant as a sterile, white to off-white lyophilized powder for reconstitution.
Supplied as follows:

NDC 0591-4385-79 1 vial per carton.

Storage

Store at 2° to 8°C (36° to 46°F).

For storage of reconstituted drug solution see Dosage and Administration 2.2.
