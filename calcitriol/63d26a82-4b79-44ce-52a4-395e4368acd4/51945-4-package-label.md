1 mL Single-dose 5 Ampuls

CALCIJEX® Calcitriol Injection (1 mcg/mL)

For I.V. use.
Protect from light.
Keep ampuls in package until time of use.

Each mL contains calcitriol, 1 mcg; polysorbate 20, 4 mg; sodium ascorbate 2.5 mg added.
May contain hydrochloric acid and/or sodium hydroxide for pH adjustment. pH is 6.5 (5.9 to 7.0).
Contains no more than 1 mcg/mL of aluminum.
Usual dosage: See insert.
Store at controlled room temperature 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F).
