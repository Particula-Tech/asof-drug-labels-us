# HOW SUPPLIED

Calcitriol Oral Solution is a clear, colorless to pale yellow liquid containing 1 mcg/mL of calcitriol.
Each 15 mL amber glass bottle is supplied with 20 single-use, graduated oral dispensers (NDC 70954-831-10).

Calcitriol Oral Solution should be protected from light.

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
