# HOW SUPPLIED

Calcitriol Capsules

0.25 mcg capsules are supplied as yellow, oval soft gelatin capsules, imprinted with “547” in black ink.

NDC 0054-0007-13: Bottle of 30 Capsules

NDC 0054-0007-25: Bottle of 100 Capsules

Calcitriol Capsules should be protected from light.

Storage

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]
Protect from light.
