# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

VECTICAL Ointment 3 mcg/g is available in collapsible aluminum tubes of the following package sizes:

- 100 g tube (NDC 0299-2012-10)

## 16.2 Storage

Store at Controlled Room temperature 68° - 77°F (20° - 25°C) with excursions permitted between 59° - 86°F (15° - 30°C).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]
Do not freeze or refrigerate.
