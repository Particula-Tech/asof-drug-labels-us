# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

Apply VECTICAL Ointment to affected areas twice daily, morning and evening Adults: • The maximum weekly dose should not exceed 200 grams.
Pediatrics: • 2 to 6 years of age: the maximum weekly dose should not exceed 100 grams • 7 years of age and older: the maximum weekly dose should not exceed 200 grams VECTICAL Ointment should not be applied to the eyes, lips, or facial skin.
VECTICAL Ointment is for topical use only.
VECTICAL Ointment is not for oral, ophthalmic or intravaginal use.
