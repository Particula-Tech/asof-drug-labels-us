# 14 CLINICAL STUDIES

In two, multicenter, double-blind, vehicle-controlled studies, a total of 839 subjects with psoriasis rated "mild" or "moderate" using an investigator global assessment scale were treated twice daily for 8 weeks.
Subjects were randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive either VECTICAL Ointment or vehicle ointment.
The mean age of the subjects was 48 years and 66% were male; most subjects were rated "moderate" at baseline.

Success was defined as "Clear or Minimal" (up to light red or pink in coloration, surface dryness with some white coloration, and slight elevation above normal skin) with at least a 2-grade change from baseline.
