# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

BENICAR HCT (olmesartan / hydrochlorothiazide) is supplied as film-coated, non-scored tablets:

- 20 mg/12.5 mg reddish-yellow, circular, debossed with C22 on one side and plain on the other side
- 40 mg/12.5 mg reddish-yellow, oval, debossed with C23 on one side and plain on the other side
- 40 mg/25 mg pink, oval, debossed with C25 on one side and plain on the other side
