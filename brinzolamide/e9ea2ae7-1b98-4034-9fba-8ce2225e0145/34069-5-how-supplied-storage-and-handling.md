# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

AZOPT (brinzolamide ophthalmic suspension) 1% is supplied in plastic dispensers with a controlled dispensing-tip as follows:

10 mL NDC 0065-0275-10

15 mL NDC 0065-0275-15

Storage and Handling

Store AZOPT at 4°C to 30°C (39°F to 86°F).
Shake well before use.
After opening, AZOPT can be used until the expiration date on the bottle.
