# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Brinzolamide ophthalmic suspension USP, 1% is supplied in natural, round boston, LDPE bottles with an extended controlled drop tip LLDPE tip as follows:

10 mL NDC 68682-464-10

15 mL NDC 68682-464-15

Storage and Handling

Store brinzolamide ophthalmic suspension USP, 1% at 4° to 30°C (39° to 86°F).
Shake well before use.
After opening, brinzolamide ophthalmic suspension USP, 1% can be used until the expiration date on the bottle.
