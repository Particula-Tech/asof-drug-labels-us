# 14 CLINICAL STUDIES

In two, three-month clinical studies, brinzolamide ophthalmic suspension 1% dosed three times per day in patients with elevated intraocular pressure (IOP), produced significant reductions in IOPs (4 to 5 mmHg).
These IOP reductions are equivalent to the reductions observed with TRUSOPT* (dorzolamide hydrochloride ophthalmic solution) 2% dosed three times per day in the same studies.

In two clinical studies in patients with elevated intraocular pressure, brinzolamide ophthalmic suspension 1% was associated with less stinging and burning upon instillation than TRUSOPT* 2%.
