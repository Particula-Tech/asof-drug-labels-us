# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Brinzolamide ophthalmic suspension 1% is supplied in plastic DROP-TAINER® dispensers with a controlled dispensing-tip as follows:

10 mL NDC 0781-6014-70

15 mL NDC 0781-6014-85

Storage and Handling

Store brinzolamide ophthalmic suspension 1% at 4°C to 30°C (39°F to 86°F).
Shake well before use.
