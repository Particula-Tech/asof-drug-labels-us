# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

The recommended dose is one drop of brinzolamide ophthalmic suspension 1% in the affected eye(s) 3 times daily.
Shake well before use.
Brinzolamide ophthalmic suspension 1% may be used concomitantly with other topical ophthalmic drug products to lower IOP.
If more than one topical ophthalmic drug is being used, the drugs should be administered at least 10 minutes apart.
