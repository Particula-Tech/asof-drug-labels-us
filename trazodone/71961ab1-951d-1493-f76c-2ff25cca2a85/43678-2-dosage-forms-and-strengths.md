# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Trazodone hydrochloride tablets are available in the following strengths:

50 mg: white, round, biconvex, tablets.
Engraved “APO” bisect “T50” on one side, other side plain.

100 mg: white, round, biconvex, tablets.
Engraved “APO” bisect “T100” on one side, other side plain.

150 mg: white, oval, fl at faced beveled-edge tablets with a full bisect and 2 partial trisects.
Engraved “APO” bisect “T150” on one side, and “50 50 50” on the other side.

300 mg: white, oval, fl at faced beveled-edge tablets with a full bisect and 2 partial trisects.
Engraved “APO” bisect “T300” on one side, and “100 100 100” on the other side with the middle “100” perpendicular to the others.
