# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

## 4.1 Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors (MAOIs)

The use of MAOI’s intended to treat psychiatric disorders with Oleptro or within 14 days of stopping treatment with Oleptro is contraindicated because of an increased risk of serotonin syndrome.
The use of Oleptro within 14 days of stopping an MAOI intended to treat psychiatric disorders is also contraindicated.
[see DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION (2.3) and WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS (5.8)].

Starting Oleptro in a patient who is being treated with MAOIs such as linezolid or intravenous methylene blue is also contraindicated because of an increased risk of serotonin syndrome [see DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION (2.4) and WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS (5.2)].
