# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 Safe Handling and Disposal

Care should be exercised in the handling and preparation of temozolomide capsules.
Capsules should not be opened.
If capsules are accidentally opened or damaged, rigorous precautions should be taken with the contents to avoid inhalation or contact with the skin or mucous membranes.
The use of gloves and safety glasses is recommended to avoid exposure in case of breakage of the capsules.
Procedures for proper handling and disposal of anticancer drugs should be considered1 to 4.
Several guidelines on this subject have been published.

## 16.3 Storage

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15° and 30°C (59° and 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Dispense in tight, light-resistant containers as defined in USP/NF or retain in original bottle.
