# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Clobazam tablets are intended for oral administration.
Clobazam tablets 10 mg are white to off-white, oval tablets with functional scoring on one side and engraved with "U 1" on the other side.
Clobazam tablets 20 mg are white to off-white, oval tablets with functional scoring on one side and engraved with "U 2" on the other side.
