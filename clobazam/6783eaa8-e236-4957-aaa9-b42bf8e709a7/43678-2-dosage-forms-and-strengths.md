# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Tablets: 10 mg and 20 mg with a functional score for oral administration.

Clobazam tablets, 10 mg are white to off-white, oval tablet with a functional score on one side, debossed with a “C” on the left side of the score and “2” on the right side of the score and plain on other side.

Clobazam tablets, 20 mg are white to off-white, oval tablet with a functional score on one side, debossed with a “C” on the left side of the score and “3” on the right side of the score and plain on other side.
