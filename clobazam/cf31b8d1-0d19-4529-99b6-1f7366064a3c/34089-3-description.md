Clobazam is a white or almost white, crystalline powder; is freely soluble in dichloromethane.
The melting range of clobazam is from 180ºC to 182ºC.
The molecular formula is C 16H 13ClN 2O 2and the molecular weight is 300.74.
Each clobazam tablet contains 10 mg or 20 mg of clobazam.
Tablets also contain as inactive ingredients: colloidal silicon dioxide, granulated corn starch, lactose monohydrate, magnesium stearate and talc.
