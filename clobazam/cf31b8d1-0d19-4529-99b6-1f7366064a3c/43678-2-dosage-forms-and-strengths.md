# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Tablets: 10 mg and 20 mg with a functional score for oral administration.
Clobazam tablets, 10 mg are white to off white, oval tablets debossed ‘C’ and ‘9’ along with functional score on one side and ‘H’ on the other side.
Clobazam tablets, 20 mg are white to off white, oval tablets debossed ‘C’ and ‘13’ along with functional score on one side and ‘H’ on the other side.
