# 16. HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Each ONFI tablet contains 5 mg, 10 mg, or 20 mg of clobazam and is white, round, and debossed with "LU" on one side and "5," "10," and "20" on the other side, respectively.

NDC 67386-310-01: 5 mg tablet, Bottles of 100 NDC 67386-311-01:10 mg tablet, Bottles of 100 NDC 67386-312-01: 20 mg tablet, Bottles of 100

Store at 20-25°C (68-77°F).
See USP controlled room temperature.
