# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Tablets: 5 mg, 10 mg, and 20 mg for oral administration.

Each ONFI tablet is white, round, and debossed with "LU" on one side and "5," "10," or "20" on the other side.

Oral Suspension: 2.5 mg/mL for oral administration.
Each bottle contains 120 mL of an off-white suspension.
