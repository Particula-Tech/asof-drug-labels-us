# INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Naloxone Hydrochloride Injection is indicated for the complete or partial reversal of narcotic depression, including respiratory depression, induced by opioids including natural and synthetic narcotics, propoxyphene, methadone, and the narcotic-antagonist analgesics: nalbuphine, pentazocine and butorphanol.
Naloxone hydrochloride is also indicated for the diagnosis of suspected acute opioid overdosage.
