# HOW SUPPLIED

Naloxone Hydrochloride Injection, USP is supplied in the following:

| List | Container | Concentration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1212 | Single-dose Ampul | 0.4 mg/1 mL (0.4 mg/mL) |
| 1215 | Single-dose Fliptop Vial | 0.4 mg/1 mL (0.4 mg/mL) |
| 1219 | Multiple-dose Fliptop Vial | 4 mg/10 mL (0.4 mg/mL) |

Store at 20 to 25°C (68 to 77°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

Protect from light.
