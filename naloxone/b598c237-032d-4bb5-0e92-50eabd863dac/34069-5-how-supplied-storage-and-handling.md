# HOW SUPPLIED

Naloxone hydrochloride injection, USP for intravenous, intramuscular, and subcutaneous administration is available as:

0.4 mg/mL (1 mL fill in 2 mL cartridge) CARPUJECT® sterile cartridge unit, with Luer Lock box of 10 (NDC 0409-1782-69).

Protect from light.

Store at 20 to 25°C (68 to 77°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]
