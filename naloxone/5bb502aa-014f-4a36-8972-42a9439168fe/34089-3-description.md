# 11 DESCRIPTION

Rezenopy (naloxone hydrochloride) nasal spray is a pre-filled, single dose intranasal spray.
Chemically, naloxone hydrochloride is the hydrochloride salt of 17-Allyl-4,5α-epoxy- 3,14-dihydroxymorphinan-6-one hydrochloride with the following structure:

Naloxone hydrochloride, an opioid antagonist, occurs as a white, to slightly off-white powder or almost white crystalline powder and contains two molecules of water of hydration.
It is freely soluble in water, in dilute acids, and in strong alkali; slightly soluble in ethanol. pKa values (proton on Nitrogen) are respectively 7.94 at 20°C and 7.82 at 37°C.

Each REZENOPY nasal spray delivers 10 mg naloxone hydrochloride (equivalent to 9 mg of naloxone) in a 0.11 mL aqueous solution.

Inactive ingredients include glycerin, trisodium citrate dihydrate, hydrochloric acid and sodium hydroxide to adjust pH, and purified water.
The pH range is 3.9 to 4.5.
