# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Carton containing two blister packages each with a single NARCAN Nasal Spray (single 4 mg dose of naloxone hydrochloride intranasal spray).

NARCAN Nasal Spray is not made with natural rubber latex.

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store NARCAN Nasal Spray in the blister and cartons provided.

Store at controlled room temperature 59°F to 77°F (15°C to 25°C).
Excursions permitted between 4°C to 40°C (39°F to 104°F).
Do not freeze.
Protect from light.
