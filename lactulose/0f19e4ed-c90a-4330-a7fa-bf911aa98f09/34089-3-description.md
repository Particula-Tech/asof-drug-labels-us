# DESCRIPTION

Lactulose is a synthetic disaccharide in solution form for oral administration.
Each 15 mL of Lactulose Solution contains: 10 g lactulose (and less than 1.6 g galactose, less than 1.2 g lactose, and 1.2 g or less of other sugars).
Also contains FD&C Yellow No. 6, purified water, and flavoring.
Sodium hydroxide used to adjust pH.
The pH range is 2.5 to 6.5.

Lactulose is a colonic acidifier which promotes laxation.
