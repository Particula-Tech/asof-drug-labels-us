# HOW SUPPLIED:

Midazolam HCl syrup is supplied as a clear, red to purplish-red, mixed fruit flavored syrup containing midazolam hydrochloride equivalent to 2 mg of midazolam per mL; each amber glass bottle of 118 mL is supplied with 1 press-in bottle adapter, 4 single-use, graduated, oral dispensers and 4 tip caps; 10 × bottle of 2.5 mL is supplied with 10 single-use, graduated, oral dispensers and 10 tip caps.

NDC 0574-0150-04 Bottle of 118 mL.

NDC 0574-0150-25 10 × Bottle of 2.5 mL.

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° - 30°C (59° - 86°F).
