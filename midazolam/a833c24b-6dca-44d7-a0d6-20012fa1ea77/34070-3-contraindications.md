# CONTRAINDICATIONS

Midazolam is contraindicated in patients with a known hypersensitivity to the drug or allergies to cherries or formulation excipients.
Benzodiazepines are contraindicated in patients with acute narrow-angle glaucoma.
Benzodiazepines may be used in patients with open-angle glaucoma only if they are receiving appropriate therapy.
Measurements of intraocular pressure in patients without eye disease show a moderate lowering following induction of general anesthesia with injectable midazolam; patients with glaucoma have not been studied.
