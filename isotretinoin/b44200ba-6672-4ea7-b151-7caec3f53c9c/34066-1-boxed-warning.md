# WARNING: EMBRYO-FETAL TOXICITY – CONTRAINDICATED IN PREGNANCY

Isotretinoin capsules can cause severe life-threatening birth defects and is contraindicated in pregnancy.

There is an extremely high risk that severe birth defects will result if pregnancy occurs while taking any amount of isotretinoin capsules even for short periods of time.
Potentially any fetus exposed during pregnancy can be affected.
There are no accurate means of determining prenatally whether an exposed fetus has been affected.
If pregnancy occurs, discontinue isotretinoin capsules immediately and refer the patient to an Obstetrician- Gynecologist experienced in reproductive toxicity for further evaluation and counseling [see Contraindications (4), Warnings and Precautions (5.1), and Use in Specific Populations (8.1)].

Because of the risk of embryo-fetal toxicity, isotretinoin capsules are available only through a restricted program under a Risk Evaluation and Mitigation Strategy (REMS) called the iPLEDGE REMS [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)].
