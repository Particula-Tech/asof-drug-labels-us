# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Zenatane is indicated for the treatment of severe recalcitrant nodular acne in non-pregnant patients 12 years of age and older with multiple inflammatory nodules with a diameter of 5 mm or greater.
Because of significant adverse reactions associated with its use, Zenatane is reserved for patients with severe nodular acne who are unresponsive to conventional therapy, including systemic antibiotics.

Limitations of Use:

If a second course of Zenatane treatment is needed, it is not recommended before a two-month waiting period because the patient's acne may continue to improve following a 15 to 20-week course of treatment [see Dosage and Administration (2.2)].
