# 7 DRUG INTERACTIONS

## 7.1 Vitamin A

Avoid concomitant use of Zenatane with supplements containing vitamin A.

Zenatane is closely related to vitamin A. Therefore, concomitant use of Zenatane with vitamin A may lead to Zenatane-related adverse reactions.

## 7.2 Tetracyclines

Avoid concomitant use of Zenatane with tetracyclines.

Zenatane use has been associated with a number of cases of intracranial hypertension (pseudotumor cerebri), some of which involved concomitant use with tetracyclines [see Warnings and Precautions (5.4)].

## 7.3 Oral Contraceptives

It is not known if there is an interaction between Zenatane with oral contraceptives that do not contain norethindrone and ethinyl estradiol.

Zenatane did not result in clinically significant changes in the pharmacokinetics of norethindrone and ethinyl estradiol when used concomitantly with norethindrone and ethinyl estradiol oral contraceptive [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)]
