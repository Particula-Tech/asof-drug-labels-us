# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Amnesteem (Isotretinoin Capsules, USP) contains 10 mg, 20 mg, 30 mg or 40 mg of isotretinoin, USP.

- •The 10 mg capsules are reddish brown and imprinted with I10.
- •The 20 mg capsules are reddish brown and cream and imprinted with I20.
- •The 30 mg capsules are cream opaque and imprinted with I30.
- •The 40 mg capsules are orange-brown and imprinted with I40.
