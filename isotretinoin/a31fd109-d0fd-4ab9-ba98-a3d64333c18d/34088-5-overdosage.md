# 10 OVERDOSAGE

Isotretinoin overdosage has been associated with vomiting, facial flushing, cheilosis, abdominal pain, headache, dizziness, and ataxia.

Evaluate patients who can become pregnant who present with an isotretinoin overdosage for pregnancy.
Because an overdosage would be expected to result in higher levels of isotretinoin in semen than found during a normal treatment course, instruct male patients treated with Claravis to use a condom, or avoid reproductive sexual activity with a patient who is or might become pregnant, for 1 month after the overdose.

Instruct all patients with Claravis overdose not donate blood for at least 1 month.
If an overdose occurs, consider contacting the Poison Help line (1-800-222-1222) or a medical toxicologist for additional overdose management recommendations.
