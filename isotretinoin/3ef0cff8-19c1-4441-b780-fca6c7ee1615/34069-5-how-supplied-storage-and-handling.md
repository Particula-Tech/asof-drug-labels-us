# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

ABSORICA and ABSORICA LD have different dosage regimens.
Although ABSORICA and ABSORICA LD have a 20 mg strength, these strengths have different bioavailability and are not substitutable [see Dosage and Administration (2.1)].

Storage and Handling of ABSORICA and ABSORICA LD

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F), excursions permitted between 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP controlled room temperature].
Protect from light.
