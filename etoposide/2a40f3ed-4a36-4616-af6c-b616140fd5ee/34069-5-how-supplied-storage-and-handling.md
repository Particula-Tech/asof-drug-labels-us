# HOW SUPPLIED

VePesid (Etoposide Capsules, USP), 50 mg, are supplied as pink capsules with “V3545” printed in black and are available in blister packs of 20 individually labeled blisters, each containing one capsule (NDC 69238-2811-2).

Capsules are to be stored under refrigeration, between 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F).

DO NOT FREEZE.
