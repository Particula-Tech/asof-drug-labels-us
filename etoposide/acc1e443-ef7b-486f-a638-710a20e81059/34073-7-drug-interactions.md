# 7 DRUG INTERACTIONS

## 7.1 Effect of Other Drugs on AVOPEF

CYP3A Inhibitors

Avoid concomitant use of strong CYP3A inhibitors.

Etoposide is a CYP3A4 substrate.
Strong CYP3A inhibitors may increase etoposide exposure, which may increase the risk of AVOPEF-associated adverse reactions [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].

CYP3A Inducers

Avoid concomitant use of strong CYP3A inducers.

Etoposide is a CYP3A4 substrate.
Strong CYP3A inducers may reduce etoposide exposure, which may decrease the effectiveness of AVOPEF [see Clinical Pharmacology (12.3)].

## 7.2 Effect of AVOPEF on Other Drugs

Vitamin K Antagonists

Monitor INR more frequently and modify the dosage of the vitamin K antagonists as appropriate.

Co-administration of AVOEPF with warfarin can result in elevated international normalized ratio (INR).
