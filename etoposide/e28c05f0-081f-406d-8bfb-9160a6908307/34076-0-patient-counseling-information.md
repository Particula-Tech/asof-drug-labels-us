# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

Myelosuppression

- Advise patients that periodic monitoring of their blood counts is required.
  Advise patients to contact their healthcare provider for new onset of bleeding, fever, or symptoms of infection [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].

Embryo-Fetal Toxicity

- Advise females of reproductive potential of the potential risk to a fetus and to inform their healthcare provider of a known or suspected pregnancy [see Warnings and Precautions (5.4) and Use in Specific Populations (8.1, 8.3)].
- Advise females of reproductive potential to use effective contraception during and 6 months after treatment with ETOPOPHOS [see Warnings and Precautions (5.4) and Use in Specific Populations (8.1, 8.3)].
- Advise males with female sexual partners of reproductive potential to use condoms during treatment with ETOPOPHOS and for at least 4 months after the final dose [see Warnings and Precautions (5.4) and Use in Specific Populations (8.3)].
