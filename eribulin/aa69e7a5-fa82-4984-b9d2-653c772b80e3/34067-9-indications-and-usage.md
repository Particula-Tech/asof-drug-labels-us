# 1INDICATIONS AND USAGE

## 1.1 Metastatic Breast Cancer

Eribulin mesylate injection is indicated for the treatment of patients with metastatic breast cancer who have previously received at least two chemotherapeutic regimens for the treatment of metastatic disease.
Prior therapy should have included an anthracycline and a taxane in either the adjuvant or metastatic setting [seeClinical Studies (14.1)].

## 1.2 Liposarcoma

Eribulin mesylate injection is indicated for the treatment of patients with unresectable or metastatic liposarcoma who have received a prior anthracycline-containing regimen [seeClinical Studies (14.2)].
