# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

NDC 68083-592-01

Eribulin Mesylate Injection: 1 mg/2 mL (0.5 mg/mL) single-dose vial.
Discard unused portion.
One vial per carton.
Eribulin mesylate injection is a clear, colorless solution.

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30° C (59° to 86° F). Do not freeze or refrigerate.
Store the vials in their original cartons.

Eribulin mesylate injection is a cytotoxic drug.
Follow applicable special handling and disposal procedures.¹
