# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Argatroban Injection is supplied as a single-use vial, containing 250 mg/2.5 mL (100 mg/mL).

NDC 0143-9674-01 (Package of 1)

Storage and Handling

Store the vial in original carton at 20° - 25° C (68° - 77° F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Do not freeze.
Retain in the original carton to protect from light.
