# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Argatroban injection is a sterile, clear, colorless to pale yellow, slightly viscous solution supplied in 2.5 mL solution in single-dose amber vials at the concentration of 100 mg/mL.
Each vial contains 250 mg of argatroban.

2.5 mL, Single Dose Vial in a Carton: NDC 70121-1037-1 Storage

Store the vials in original cartons at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Do not freeze.
Retain in the original carton to protect from light.
If the solution is cloudy, or if an insoluble precipitate is noted, the vial should be discarded.
