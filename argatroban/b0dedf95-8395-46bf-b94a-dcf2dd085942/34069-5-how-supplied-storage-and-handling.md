# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Argatroban Injection is supplied in 2.5 mL solution in single-dose vials at the concentration of 100 mg/mL.
Each vial contains 250 mg of argatroban, USP.

NDC 67457-212-02 carton containing one single-dose vial

Storage: Store the vials in original cartons at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

Do not freeze.

Retain in the original carton to protect from light.

If the solution is cloudy, or if an insoluble precipitate is noted, the vial should be discarded.

Preservative Free.

Sterile.
Non-pyrogenic.
