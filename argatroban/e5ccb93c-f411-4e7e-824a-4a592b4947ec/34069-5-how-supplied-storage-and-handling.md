# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Argatroban Injection is supplied as a single use vial containing 125 mg argatroban in 125 mL of 0.9% aqueous sodium chloride solution (1 mg/mL).
The vial is sealed with a gray rubber stopper and a red aluminum flip-off seal.
NDC 68083-141-02 one package containing two vials of Argatroban Injection (each vial contains 125 mg of argatroban).
Vial may be inverted for use with a medical infusion set.
Storage Store the vials in original cartons at 20º to 25º C (68º to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Do not freeze.
Retain in the original carton to protect from light.
