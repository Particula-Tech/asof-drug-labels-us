# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Argatroban in 0.9% sodium chloride injection is a clear, colorless to pale yellow solution.
Argatroban in 0.9% sodium chloride injection is supplied as a single-dose vial containing 125 mg argatroban in 125 mL of 0.9% aqueous sodium chloride solution (1 mg/mL).
The vial is sealed with a gray rubber stopper and a red aluminum flip-off seal.

NDC 68083-141-02 one package containing two vials of argatroban in 0.9% sodium chloride injection (each vial contains 125 mg of argatroban).
Vial may be inverted for use with a medical infusion set.

Storage Store the vials in original cartons at 20º to 25ºC (68º to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Do not freeze.
Retain in the original carton to protect from light.
If the solution is cloudy, or if an insoluble precipitate is noted, the vial should be discarded.
