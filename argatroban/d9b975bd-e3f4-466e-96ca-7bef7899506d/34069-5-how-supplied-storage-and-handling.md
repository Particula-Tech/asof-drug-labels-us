# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Argatroban in sodium chloride injection is supplied as a single-dose vial containing 50 mg argatroban in 50 mL of aqueous solution (1 mg/mL).
50 mL single-dose vials packaged in carton of 10 vials NDC 55150-241-10 50 mL single-dose vial packaged individually NDC 55150-241-01 Storage and Handling: Store the vials in original cartons at 20º to 25ºC (68º to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Do not refrigerate or freeze.
Retain in the original carton to protect from light.
If the solution is cloudy, or if an insoluble precipitate is noted, the vial should be discarded.
The vial stopper is not made with natural rubber latex.
