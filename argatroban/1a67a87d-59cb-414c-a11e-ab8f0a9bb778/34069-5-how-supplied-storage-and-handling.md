# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Argatroban Injection, USP is supplied in 2.5 mL solution in single-use vials at the concentration of 100 mg/mL.
Each vial contains 250 mg of argatroban.

NDC 42023-182-89 (Package of 1)

Storage

Store the vials in original cartons between 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
(See USP Controlled Room Temperature.)
Do not freeze.
Retain in the original carton to protect from light.
If the solution is cloudy, or if an insoluble precipitate is noted, the vial should be discarded.
