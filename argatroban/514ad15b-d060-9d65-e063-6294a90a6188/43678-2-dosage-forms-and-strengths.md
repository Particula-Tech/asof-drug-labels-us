# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Argatroban in 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection is a clear, colorless to pale yellow solution.

Argatroban in 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection is supplied in a single-dose vial containing 125 mg argatroban in 125 mL aqueous sodium chloride solution (1 mg/mL).
The solution is ready for intravenous infusion.
