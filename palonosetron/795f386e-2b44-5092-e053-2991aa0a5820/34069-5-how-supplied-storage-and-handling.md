# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

ALOXI (palonosetron HCl) injection is supplied as a sterile, clear and colorless solution:

- 0.25 mg palonosetron in 5 mL (0.05 mg/mL) in a single-dose vial individually packaged in a carton (NDC # 69639-103-01).
- 0.075 mg palonosetron in 1.5 mL (0.05 mg/mL) in a s single-dose vial packaged in a carton containing 5 vials (NDC # 69639-103-05).

Storage

- Store at controlled temperature of 20–25°C (68–77°F).Excursions permitted to 15–30°C (59-86°F).
- Protect from freezing.
- Protect from light.
