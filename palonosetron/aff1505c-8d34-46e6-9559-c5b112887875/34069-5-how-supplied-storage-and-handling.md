# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Palonosetron HCl injection is supplied as:

NDC 0781-3312-75, 0.25 mg (free base)/5 mL single-dose vial individually packaged in a carton.

Storage

- •Store at controlled room temperature of 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
- •Protect from freezing.
- •Protect from light.
- •Discard unused portion.
