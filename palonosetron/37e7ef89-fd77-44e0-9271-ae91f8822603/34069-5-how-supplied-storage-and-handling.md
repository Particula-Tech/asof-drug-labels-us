# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

NDC 69097-927-35, Palonosetron Hydrochloride Injection 0.25 mg/5 mL (free base) single-use vial individually packaged in a carton.

Storage

- Store at controlled temperature of 20–25°C (68°F–77°F).
  Excursions permitted to 15–30°C (59-86°F).
- Protect from freezing.
- Protect from light.
