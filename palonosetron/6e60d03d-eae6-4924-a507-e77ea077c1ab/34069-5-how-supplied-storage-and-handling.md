# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Palonosetron Hydrochloride Injection is supplied as follows:

|  | Palonosetron Hydrochloride Injection |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NDC | (0.05 mg per mL) | Package Factor |
| 25021-783-05 | 0.25 mg per 5 mL Single-Dose Vial | 1 vial per carton |

Palonosetron Hydrochloride Injection is a clear and colorless solution.

Storage Conditions Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15° and 30°C (59° and 86°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

Do not freeze.
Protect from light.
Retain in carton until time of use.

Discard unused portion.

Sterile, Nonpyrogenic, Preservative-free.
The container closure is not made with natural rubber latex.
