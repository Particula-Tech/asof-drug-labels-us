# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Palonosetron hydrochloride injection is supplied as a sterile, clear and colorless solution:

- 0.25 mg palonosetron in 5 mL (0.05 mg/mL) in a single-dose vial individually packaged in a carton (NDC # 55111-694-07).
- 0.075 mg palonosetron in 1.5 mL (0.05 mg/mL) in a s single-dose vial packaged in a carton containing 5 vials (NDC # 55111-694-19).

Storage

- Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
- Protect from freezing.
- Protect from light.
