# 5 WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS

## 5.1 Hypersensitivity

Hypersensitivity reactions may occur in patients who have exhibited hypersensitivity to other 5-HT3 receptor antagonists.
Hypersensitivity reactions have been very rarely reported postmarketing for intravenous palonosetron: dyspnea, bronchospasm, swelling/edema, erythema, pruritus, rash, urticaria.
No hypersensitivity reactions have been reported for oral palonosetron.
