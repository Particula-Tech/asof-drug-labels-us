# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

See FDA-Approved Patient Labeling (17.2)

## 17.1 Instructions for Patients

Patients should be advised to report to their physician all of their medical conditions, any pain, redness, or swelling in and around the infusion site [see Adverse Reactions (6.2)].

Patients should be instructed to read the patient insert.
