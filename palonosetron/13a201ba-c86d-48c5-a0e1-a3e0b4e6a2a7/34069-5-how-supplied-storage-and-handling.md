# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

How Supplied

POSFREATM injection is clear and colorless solution and is supplied in single-dose vials as follows:

| NDC Number | Strength | Package |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 83831-105-01 | 0.25 mg palonosetron in 5 mL (0.05 mg/mL) | 1 vial/carton |
| 83831-104-05 | 0.075 mg palonosetron in 1.5 mL (0.05 mg/mL) | 5 vials/carton |

Storage

- Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); [Excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F)].
- Protect from freezing.
- Protect from light.
