# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

PRADAXA Oral Pellets are yellowish in a silver-colored, child-resistant packet.
The packets are placed in an aluminum bag with a desiccant.
PRADAXA Oral Pellets are supplied as follows:

| Strength | Package | NDC |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 20 mg | unit of use carton with 1 aluminum bag containing 60 packets | 0597-0425-78 |
| 30 mg | unit of use carton with 1 aluminum bag containing 60 packets | 0597-0430-18 |
| 40 mg | unit of use carton with 1 aluminum bag containing 60 packets | 0597-0435-96 |
| 50 mg | unit of use carton with 1 aluminum bag containing 60 packets | 0597-0440-53 |
| 110 mg | unit of use carton with 1 aluminum bag containing 60 packets | 0597-0445-87 |
| 150 mg | unit of use carton with 1 aluminum bag containing 60 packets | 0597-0450-16 |

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Store in the original package to protect from moisture.

Do not open the packets until ready for use.
Use the PRADAXA Oral Pellets within 6 months of opening the aluminum bag containing the packets.
