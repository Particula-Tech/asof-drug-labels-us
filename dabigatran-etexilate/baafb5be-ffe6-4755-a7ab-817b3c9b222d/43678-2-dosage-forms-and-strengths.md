# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Dabigatran Etexilate Capsules are available containing 86.48 mg or 172.95 mg of dabigatran etexilate mesylate equivalent to 75 mg or 150 mg of dabigatran etexilate, respectively.

- •The 75 mg capsules are hard-shell hypromellose capsules with a white opaque cap and a white opaque body filled with white to off-white powder.
  The capsules are axially printed with MYLAN over DE 75 in black ink on both the cap and body.
- •The 150 mg capsules are hard-shell hypromellose capsules with a light purple opaque cap and a white opaque body filled with white to off-white powder.
  The capsules are axially printed with MYLAN over DE 150 in black ink on both the cap and body.
