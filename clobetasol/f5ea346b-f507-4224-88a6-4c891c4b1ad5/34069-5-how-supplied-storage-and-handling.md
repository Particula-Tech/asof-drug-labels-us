# HOW SUPPLIED

Clobetasol Propionate Topical Solution USP, 0.05% is supplied in plastic squeeze bottles, 25 mL (NDC 51672-1293-2) and 50 mL (NDC 51672-1293-3).

Store in tight containers.
Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Do not refrigerate.
Do not use near an open flame.
