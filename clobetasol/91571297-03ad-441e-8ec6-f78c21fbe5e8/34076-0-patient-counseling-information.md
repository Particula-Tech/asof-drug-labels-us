# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

Inform patients using topical corticosteroids of the following information and instructions:

- Clobetasol propionate cream (emollient) USP, 0.05% is for external use only.
  Avoid contact with the eyes.
- Use as directed.
  Do not use clobetasol propionate cream (emollient) USP, 0.05% for any disorder other than that for which it was prescribed.
  Do not use longer than the prescribed time period.
- Do not use other corticosteroid-containing products while using clobetasol propionate cream (emollient) USP, 0.05% unless directed by the physician.
- The treated skin area should not be bandaged, otherwise covered, or wrapped so as to be occlusive unless directed by the physician.
- Wash hands after applying the medication.
- Report any signs of local or systemic adverse reactions to the physician.
- Inform their physicians that they are using clobetasol propionate cream (emollient) USP, 0.05% if surgery is contemplated.
  If you go to another doctor for illness, injury or surgery, tell the doctor you are using clobetasol propionate cream (emollient) USP, 0.05%.
- Do not use clobetasol propionate cream (emollient) USP, 0.05% on the face, underarms or groin areas.
- As with other corticosteroids, therapy should be discontinued when control is achieved.
  If no improvement is seen within 2 weeks, contact the physician.
- Use no more than 50 grams per week of clobetasol propionate cream (emollient) USP, 0.05%.
- Store between 68°F and 77°F (20°C and 25°C).
  Do not refrigerate.
