# 14 CLINICAL STUDIES

The safety and efficacy of Clobetasol Propionate Shampoo, 0.05%, have been evaluated in two clinical trials involving 290 subjects with moderate to severe scalp psoriasis.
In both trials, subjects were treated with either Clobetasol Propionate Shampoo or the corresponding vehicle applied once daily for 15 minutes before lathering and rinsing for a period of 4 weeks.
Efficacy results are presented in Table 2 below.
