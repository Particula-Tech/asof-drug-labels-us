# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Clobetasol Propionate Shampoo, 0.05%, is a translucent, colorless to pale yellow viscous liquid, supplied in 4 fl. oz.
(118 mL) bottles.

NDC 68308-664-04 Storage: Keep tightly closed.
Store at USP controlled room temperature 68˚ to 77˚F (20˚ - 25˚C), with excursions permitted between 59˚ and 86˚F (15˚ - 30˚C).
