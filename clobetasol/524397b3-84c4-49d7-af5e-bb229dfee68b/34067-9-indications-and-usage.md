# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

## 1.1 Indication

Clobetasol Propionate Shampoo, 0.05%, is a super-high potent topical corticosteroid formulation indicated for the treatment of moderate to severe forms of scalp psoriasis in subjects 18 years of age and older.
Treatment should be limited to 4 consecutive weeks.
The total dosage should not exceed 50 g (50 mL or 1.75 fl. oz.) per week.
Patients should be instructed to use Clobetasol Propionate Shampoo, 0.05%, for the minimum time period necessary to achieve the desired results [see Dosage and Administration(2)].
Use in patients younger than 18 years of age is not recommended due to numerically high rates of hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal (HPA) axis suppression [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1) and Use in Specific Populations (8.4)].

## 1.2 Limitations of Use

Clobetasol Propionate Shampoo, 0.05%, should not be used on the face, groin or axillae.
Avoid any contact of the drug product with the eyes and lips.
In case of contact, rinse thoroughly with water all parts of the body that came in contact with the shampoo.
