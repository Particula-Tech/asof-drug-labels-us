# CLINICAL STUDIES

The efficacy of CLOBEX® (clobetasol propionate) Lotion, 0.05% in psoriasis and atopic dermatitis has been demonstrated in two adequate and well-controlled clinical trials.
The first study was conducted in patients with moderate to severe plaque psoriasis.
Patients were treated twice daily for 4 weeks with either CLOBEX® (clobetasol propionate) Lotion, 0.05% or vehicle lotion.
Study results demonstrated that the efficacy of CLOBEX® Lotion, 0.05% in treating moderate to severe plaque psoriasis was superior to that of vehicle.

At the end of treatment (4 weeks), 30 of 82 patients (36.6%) treated with CLOBEX® Lotion, 0.05% compared with 0 of 29 (0%) treated with vehicle achieved success.
Success was defined as a score of none or very mild (no or very slight clinical signs or symptoms of erythema, plaque elevation, or scaling) on the Global Severity scale of psoriasis.

The second study was conducted in patients with moderate to severe atopic dermatitis.
Patients were treated twice daily for 2 weeks with either CLOBEX® (clobetasol propionate) Lotion, 0.05% or vehicle lotion.
Study results demonstrated that the efficacy of CLOBEX® Lotion, 0.05% in treating moderate to severe atopic dermatitis was superior to that of vehicle.

At the end of treatment (2 weeks), 41 of 96 patients (42.7%) treated with CLOBEX® Lotion, 0.05% compared with 4 of 33 (12.1%) treated with vehicle achieved success.
Success was defined as a score of none or very mild (no or very slight clinical signs or symptoms of erythema, induration/papulation, oozing/crusting, or pruritus) on the Global Severity scale of atopic dermatitis.
