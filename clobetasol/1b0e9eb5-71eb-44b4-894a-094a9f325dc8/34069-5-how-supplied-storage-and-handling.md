# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Clobetasol Propionate Cream, 0.025% is a white to off-white cream, supplied as follows: 100g aluminum tube NDC 74157-710-10

## 16.2 Storage

Store at 20°C-25°C (68°F-77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C-30°C (59°F-86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature] Do not freeze.
