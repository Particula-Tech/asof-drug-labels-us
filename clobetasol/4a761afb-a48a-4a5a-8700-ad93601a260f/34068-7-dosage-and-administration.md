# DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

Apply a thin layer of Clobetasol Propionate Cream USP to the affected skin areas twice daily and rub in gently and completely.

Clobetasol Propionate Cream USP is a super-high potency topical corticosteroid; therefore, treatment should be limited to 2 consecutive weeks, and amounts greater than 50 grams per week should not be used.

As with other highly active corticosteroids, therapy should be discontinued when control has been achieved.
If no improvement is seen within 2 weeks, reassessment of diagnosis may be necessary.

Clobetasol Propionate Cream USP should not be used with occlusive dressings.
