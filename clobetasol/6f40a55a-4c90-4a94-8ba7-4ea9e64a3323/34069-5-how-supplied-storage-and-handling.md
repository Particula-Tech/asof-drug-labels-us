# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

IMPEKLO™ lotion, 0.05% is a white to off white, opaque to translucent, homogenous and lump free lotion without any phase separation provided in a white bottle with a metered-dose pump having an integral pump locking feature.
Each pump actuation delivers 0.15 mg of clobetasol propionate, USP in 0.30 g of lotion.
The metered-dose pump is capable of dispensing not less than 138 actuations to deliver not less than 41.4 g of lotion.
It is available as follows:

NDC 49502-537-35 one metered dose pump contains 68 g lotion

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from freezing.
