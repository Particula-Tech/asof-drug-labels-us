# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

IMPEKLO lotion is a white to off white, opaque to translucent, homogenous and lump free lotion without any phase separation packed in a white bottle with a metered-dose pump having an integral pump locking feature.
Each pump actuation delivers on average 0.15 mg of clobetasol propionate, USP in 0.30 g of lotion.
