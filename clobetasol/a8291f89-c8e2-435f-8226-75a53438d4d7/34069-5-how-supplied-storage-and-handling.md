# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

OLUX-E (clobetasol propionate) Foam, 0.05% is a white to off-white aerosol foam supplied as follows:

- •50-g aluminum can NDC 63032-101-50
- •100-g aluminum can NDC 63032-101-00

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at controlled room temperature 68°F to 77°F (20°C to 25°C) with excursions permitted between 59°F to 86°F (15°C to 30°C).

FLAMMABLE.
AVOID FIRE, FLAME OR SMOKING DURING AND IMMEDIATELY FOLLOWING APPLICATION.

Contents under pressure.
Do not puncture or incinerate.
Do not expose to heat or store at temperatures above 120°F (49°C).

Keep out of reach of children.
