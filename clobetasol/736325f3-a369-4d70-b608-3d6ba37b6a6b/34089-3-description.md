# DESCRIPTION:

Clobetasol propionate topical solution, 0.05% contains the active compound clobetasol propionate, a synthetic corticosteroid for topical dermatological use.
Clobetasol, an analog of prednisolone, has a high degree of glucocorticoid activity and a slight degree of mineralocorticoid activity.

Clobetasol propionate has the molecular formula C25H32ClFO5 and a molecular weight of 466.98.
It is a white to cream-colored crystalline powder insoluble in water.

Clobetasol propionate topical solution 0.05% contains: 0.5 mg/g clobetasol propionate in a base composed of purified water, isopropyl alcohol (39.3%), carbomer 934P and sodium hydroxide.
