# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

IMPOYZ Cream, 0.025% is a white to off-white cream, supplied as follows:

60 g aluminum tube NDC 67857-811-57

112 g aluminum tube NDC 67857-811-58

## 16.2 Storage

Store at 20ºC - 25ºC (68 ºF - 77ºF); excursions permitted to 15ºC-30ºC (59º-86ºF) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Do not freeze.
