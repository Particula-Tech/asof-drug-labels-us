# INDICATIONS AND USAGE

TEMOVATE® Gel is a super-high potency cor-ticosteroid formulation indicated for the relief of the inflammatory and pruritic manifestations of corticosteroid-responsive dermatoses.
Treatment beyond 2 consecutive weeks is not recommended, and the total dosage should not exceed 50 g/week because of the potential for the drug to suppress the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal (HPA) axis.
Use in pediatric patients under 12 years of age is not recommended.
