# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

IMPOYZ Cream, 0.025% is a white to off-white cream, supplied as follows:

60 g aluminum tube NDC 69482-700-60

## 16.2 Storage

Store at 20°C - 25°C (68 °F - 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C-30°C (59°-86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Do not freeze.
