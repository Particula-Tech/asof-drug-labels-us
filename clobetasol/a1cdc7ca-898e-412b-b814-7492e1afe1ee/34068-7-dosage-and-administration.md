# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

Apply a thin layer of IMPOYZ Cream to the affected skin areas twice daily and rub in gently and completely.
Use IMPOYZ Cream for up to 2 consecutive weeks of treatment.
Treatment beyond 2 consecutive weeks is not recommended, and the total dosage should not exceed 50 g per week because of the potential for the drug to suppress the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal (HPA) axis [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].

Discontinue IMPOYZ Cream when control is achieved.

Do not use if atrophy is present at the treatment site.

Do not bandage, cover, or wrap the treated skin area unless directed by a physician.

Avoid use on the face, scalp, axilla, groin, or other intertriginous areas.

IMPOYZ Cream is for topical use only.
It is not for oral, ophthalmic, or intravaginal use.

Wash hands after each application.
