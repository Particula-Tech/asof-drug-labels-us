# HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## D.H.E. 45® (dihydroergotamine mesylate) Injection, USP

Available as a clear, colorless, sterile solution in single 1 mL sterile ampuls containing 1 mg of dihydroergotamine mesylate per mL, in packages of 10 (NDC 66490-041-01).

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F).
Use carton to protect contents from light until used.

Do not refrigerate or freeze.

To assure constant potency, protect the ampuls from light and heat.
Administer only if clear and colorless.
