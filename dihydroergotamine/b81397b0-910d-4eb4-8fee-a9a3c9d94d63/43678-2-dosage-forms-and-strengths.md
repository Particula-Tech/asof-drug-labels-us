# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

TRUDHESA (dihydroergotamine mesylate) nasal spray is a single-dose, drug-device combination product containing a vial of dihydroergotamine mesylate with a clear and colorless to faintly yellow solution and an intranasal delivery device.
Each spray delivers 0.725 mg of dihydroergotamine mesylate.
