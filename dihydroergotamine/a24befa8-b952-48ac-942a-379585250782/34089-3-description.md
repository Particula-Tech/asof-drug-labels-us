# DESCRIPTION

MIGRANAL is ergotamine hydrogenated in the 9,10 position as the mesylate salt.
MIGRANAL is known chemically as ergotaman-3’, 6’, 18-trione, 9,10-dihydro-12’-hydroxy-2’-methyl-5’- (phenylmethyl)-, (5’α)-, monomethane-sulfonate.
Its molecular weight is 679.78 and its empirical formula is C33H37N5O5•CH4O3S.
