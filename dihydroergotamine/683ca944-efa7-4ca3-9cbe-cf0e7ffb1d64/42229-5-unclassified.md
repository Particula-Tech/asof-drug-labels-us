# DRUG ABUSE AND DEPENDENCE

Currently available data have not demonstrated drug abuse or psychological dependence with dihydroergotamine.
However, cases of drug abuse and psychological dependence in patients on other forms of ergot therapy have been reported.
Thus, due to the chronicity of vascular headaches, it is imperative that patients be advised not to exceed recommended dosages.
