# HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Dihydroergotamine Mesylate Injection, USP

Available as a clear, colorless, sterile solution in single 1 mL sterile vials containing 1 mg of dihydroergotamine mesylate per mL, in packages of 10 (NDC 0143-9151-10).

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15° to 30ºC (59º to 86ºF) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Use carton to protect contents from light until used.

Do not refrigerate or freeze.

To assure constant potency, protect the vials from light and heat.
Administer only if clear and colorless.
