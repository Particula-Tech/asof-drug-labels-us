# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Tadalafil tablets, USP are supplied as follows: 20 mg, oval shaped, biconvex, yellow colored, film-coated tablet, debossed with on one side and plain on other side.

Bottles of 30 NDC 43598-578-30

Bottles of 60 NDC 43598-578-60

## 16.2 Storage

Store at 20ºC to 25ºC (68ºF to 77ºF); [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Keep out of reach of children.
