# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

## 1.1 Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension

ADCIRCA® is indicated for the treatment of pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH) (WHO Group 1) to improve exercise ability.
Studies establishing effectiveness included predominately patients with NYHA Functional Class II - III symptoms and etiologies of idiopathic or heritable PAH (61%) or PAH associated with connective tissue diseases (23%).
