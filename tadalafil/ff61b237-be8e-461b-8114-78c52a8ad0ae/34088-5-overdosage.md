# 10 OVERDOSAGE

Single doses up to 500 mg have been given to healthy male subjects, and multiple daily doses up to 100 mg have been given to male patients with erectile dysfunction.
Adverse reactions were similar to those seen at lower doses.
Doses greater than 40 mg have not been studied in patients with pulmonary arterial hypertension.
In cases of overdose, standard supportive measures should be adopted as needed.
Hemodialysis contributes negligibly to tadalafil elimination.
