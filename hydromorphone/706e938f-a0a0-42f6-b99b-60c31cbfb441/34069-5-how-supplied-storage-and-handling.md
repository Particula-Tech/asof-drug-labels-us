# HOW SUPPLIED

Palladone™ (hydromorphone hydrochloride extended-release) Capsules 12 mg are cinnamon-colored capsules imprinted with P-XL on the cap and 12 mg on the body.
They are available in multiple-use containers, not intended for dispensing directly to the patient.
They are supplied as follows:

NDC 59011-312-60: opaque plastic bottles of 60 capsules NDC 59011-312-20: Unit dose packaging with 20 capsules; 10 individually numbered blister units per card: two cards per carton
