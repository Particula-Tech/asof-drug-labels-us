# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Hydromorphone Hydrochloride Injection, USP (HIGH POTENCY) is contraindicated in patients with:

- Significant respiratory depression [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3)]
- Acute or severe bronchial asthma in an unmonitored setting or in the absence of resuscitative equipment [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3)]
- Known or suspected gastrointestinal obstruction, including paralytic ileus [see Warnings and Precautions (5.10)]
- Hypersensitivity to hydromorphone, hydromorphone salts or any other components of the product (e.g., anaphylaxis).

Hydromorphone Hydrochloride Injection, USP (HIGH POTENCY) is contraindicated in patients who are not opioid tolerant [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1)].
