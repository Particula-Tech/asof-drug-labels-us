# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Hydromorphone Hydrochloride Injection, USP [high potency formulation (HPF)] is a clear, colorless to nearly colorless aqueous sterile solution.

Each amber vial contains 10 mg/mL of hydromorphone hydrochloride and is available in 1 mL, 5 mL or 50 mL single-dose vials.
