# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Hydromorphone Hydrochloride Injection, USP is available as:

| •Single-dose NexJect™ Syringes | 0.25 mg/0.5 mL, 0.5 mg/0.5 mL, 1 mg/mL, 2 mg/mL, and 4 mg/mL |
| --- | --- |

The drug product is a clear, colorless to nearly colorless aqueous sterile solution.
Each 1 mL of sterile solution contains 0.5 mg, 1 mg, 2 mg, or 4 mg of hydromorphone hydrochloride.
