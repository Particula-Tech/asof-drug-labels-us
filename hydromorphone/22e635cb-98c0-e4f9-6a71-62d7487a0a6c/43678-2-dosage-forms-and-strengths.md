# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

EXALGO is available in 8 mg, 12 mg or 16 mg dosage strengths.
The 8 mg tablets are round, biconvex, red tablets imprinted with "EXH 8" on one side.
The 12 mg tablets are round, biconvex, dark yellow tablets imprinted with "EXH 12" on one side.
The 16 mg tablets are round, biconvex, yellow tablets imprinted with "EXH 16" on one side.
