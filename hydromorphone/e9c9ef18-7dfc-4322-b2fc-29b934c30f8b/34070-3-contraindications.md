# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

1. Hydromorphone Hydrochloride Injection is contraindicated in patients with:
2. •Significant respiratory depression [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)]
3. •Acute or severe bronchial asthma in an unmonitored setting or in the absence of resuscitative equipment [see Warnings and Precautions (5.5)]
4. •Known or suspected gastrointestinal obstruction, including paralytic ileus [see Warnings and Precautions (5.9)]
5. •Hypersensitivity to hydromorphone (e.g., anaphylaxis) [see Adverse Reactions (6)]
