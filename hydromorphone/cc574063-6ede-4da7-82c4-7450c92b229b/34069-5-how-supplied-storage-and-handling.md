# HOW SUPPLIED

Hydromorphone Hydrochloride Tablets, USP, are supplied as white to off-white, round tablets.
The 4 mg tablet is debossed with "4" on one side and "54 196" on the other side.
The 8 mg tablet is scored on one side and debossed with product identification "54 425" on the other side.

NDC 0054-0264-24 4 mg, 4 x 25 Reverse Numbered Unit Dose

NDC 0054-0264-25 4 mg, bottle of 100

NDC 0054-0265-25 8 mg, bottle of 100

## Storage

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Dispense in a tight container as defined in the USP/NF.

Protect from light.

A schedule CS-II Narcotic.
DEA Order Form is Required.
