# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Hydromorphone Hydrochloride Oral Solution, USP

1 mg/mL oral solution is supplied as a clear, red solution.

NDC 0054-0386-63: Bottle of 473 mL

Hydromorphone Hydrochloride Tablets, USP

4 mg tablet is supplied as a white to off-white, round, flat-faced tablet with beveled edges; debossed with “4” on one side and “54 196” debossed on the other side.

NDC 0054-0264-24: 10 x 10 Unit-Dose

NDC 0054-0264-25: Bottle of 100 Tablets

8 mg tablet is supplied as a white to off-white, round, flat-faced tablet with beveled edges; scored on one side and “54 425” debossed on the other side.

NDC 0054-0265-25: Bottle of 100 Tablets

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]
Dispense in a tight, light-resistant, child-resistant container as defined in the USP/NF.

Store Hydromorphone Hydrochloride Oral Solution and Hydromorphone Hydrochloride Tablets securely and dispose of properly.
