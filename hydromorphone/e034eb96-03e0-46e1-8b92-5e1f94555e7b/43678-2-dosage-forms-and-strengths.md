# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Hydromorphone Hydrochloride Injection:

Each 1 mL colorless single dose vial contains 1 mg/mL, 2 mg/mL, or 4 mg/mL of hydromorphone hydrochloride in a sterile, aqueous solution.

Hydromorphone Hydrochloride Injection [high potency formulation (HPF)] (for use in opioid-tolerant patients only): Each amber single dose vial contains 10 mg/mL of hydromorphone hydrochloride in a sterile, aqueous solution and is available in 1 mL, 5 mL and 50 mL single dose vials.
