# HOW SUPPLIED

It is available in: Bottles of 1 pint (473 mL) - NDC# 0074-2452-02

DILAUDID 8 mg TABLETS are white and triangular shaped, embossed with the number 8 on one side and bisected and embossed with a double Abbott “A” logo on the other side.
They are available in: Bottles of 100 - NDC# 0074-2426-14
