# DESCRIPTION

Hydromorphone Hydrochloride Tablets, USP, a hydrogenated ketone of morphine, are an opioid analgesic.

The chemical name of hydromorphone hydrochloride is 4,5α-epoxy-3-hydroxy-17-methylmorphinan-6-one hydrochloride.

Color Coded Tablets (for oral administration) contain:

2 mg hydromorphone hydrochloride (orange tablet) and D&C red #30 Lake dye, D&C yellow #10 Lake dye, lactose, and magnesium stearate.
Hydromorphone Hydrochloride 2 mg Tablets may contain traces of sodium metabisulfite.

4 mg hydromorphone hydrochloride (yellow tablet) and D&C yellow #10 Lake dye, lactose, and magnesium stearate.
Hydromorphone Hydrochloride 4 mg Tablets may contain traces of sodium metabisulfite.

8 mg hydromorphone hydrochloride (white tablet) and lactose anhydrous, and magnesium stearate.
Hydromorphone Hydrochloride 8 mg Tablets may contain traces of sodium metabisulfite.
