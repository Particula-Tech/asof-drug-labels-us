# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Hydromorphone hydrochloride extended release tablets are available in 8 mg, 12 mg or 16 mg dosage strengths.
The 8 mg tablets are round, biconvex, dark beige tablets imprinted with “P293” over “8” on one side.
The 12 mg tablets are round, biconvex, white tablets imprinted with “P294” over “12” on one side.
The 16 mg tablets are round, biconvex, light beige tablets imprinted with “P295” over “16” on one side.
