# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

DILAUDID INJECTION is contraindicated in patients with:

- Significant respiratory depression [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)]
- Acute or severe bronchial asthma in an unmonitored setting or in the absence of resuscitative equipment [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)]
- Known or suspected gastrointestinal obstruction, including paralytic ileus [see Warnings and Precautions (5.9)]
- Hypersensitivity to hydromorphone, hydromorphone salts, any other components of the product, or sulfite containing medications (e.g., anaphylaxis) [see Warnings and Precautions (5.13)]
