# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

OPDIVO® (nivolumab) is available as follows:

| Carton Contents | NDC |
| --- | --- |
| 40 mg/4 mL single-use vial | 0003-3772-11 |
| 100 mg/10 mL single-use vial | 0003-3774-12 |

Store OPDIVO under refrigeration at 2°C to 8°C (36°F-46°F).
Protect OPDIVO from light by storing in the original package until time of use.
Do not freeze or shake.
