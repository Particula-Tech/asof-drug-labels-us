# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

JAVADIN (clonidine hydrochloride) is a clear, colorless solution supplied in a high-density polyethylene (HDPE) bottle with child-resistant closure.
The 0.02 mg/mL oral solution is available in bottles of 250 mL (NDC 24338-115-01).

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C and 30°C (59°F and 86° F) (see USP Controlled Room Temperature).
Discard unused portion 60 days after first opening.
