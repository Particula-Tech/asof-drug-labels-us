# HOW SUPPLIED

Clonidine Hydrochloride tablets USP are white, rounded off oval shaped, biconvex with scoreline on one side containing 0.1 mg, 0.2 mg or 0.3 mg of clonidine hydrochloride USP.

0.1 mg tablets have "U" and "135" debossed across the scoreline.

Bottles of 100: NDC 29300-135-01

Bottles of 500: NDC 29300-135-05

Bottles of 1000: NDC 29300-135-10

Bottles of 5000: NDC 29300-135-50

Bottles of 9000: NDC 29300-135-90

0.2 mg tablets have "U" and "136" debossed across the scoreline.

Bottles of 100: NDC 29300-136-01

Bottles of 500: NDC 29300-136-05

Bottles of 1000: NDC 29300-136-10

Bottles of 5000: NDC 29300-136-50

0.3 mg tablets have "U" and "137" debossed across the scoreline.

Bottles of 100: NDC 29300-137-01

Bottles of 500: NDC 29300-137-05

Bottles of 1000: NDC 29300-137-10

Bottles of 2500: NDC 29300-137-25

Store at 200- 250C (680- 770F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container.
