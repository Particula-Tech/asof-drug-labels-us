# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Clonidine Extended-Release Tablets are scored, splitable, capsule-shaped coated tablets that are supplied in bottles of 30.

| Strength | Color | Markings | NDC |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.17 mg | White | NP 2 | 68134-603-30 |

Store at 20º to 25ºC (68º to 77ºF); excursions permitted to 15º to 30ºC (59º to 86ºF).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container with a child-resistant closure.
