The 500μg/mL strength product should be diluted prior to use in an appropriate solution.

NOTE: Duraclon®(epidural clonidine) is not recommended for obstetrical, postpartum, or peri-operative pain management.
The risk of hemodynamic instability, especially hypotension and bradycardia, from epidural clonidine may be unacceptable in these patients.
However, in a rare obstetrical, post-partum or peri-operative patient, potential benefits may outweigh the possible risks.
