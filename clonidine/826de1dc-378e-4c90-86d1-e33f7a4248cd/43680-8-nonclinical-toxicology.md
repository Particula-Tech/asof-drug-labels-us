# 13 NONCLINICAL TOXICOLOGY

## 13.1 Carcinogenesis, Mutagenesis, Impairment of Fertility

Chronic dietary administration of clonidine was not carcinogenic to rats (132 weeks) or mice (78 weeks) dosed, respectively, at up to 2 or 1 times the MRHD on a mg/m2 basis.

There was no evidence of genotoxicity in the Ames test for mutagenicity or mouse micronucleus test for clastogenicity.

Fertility of female rats appeared to be affected at dose levels of 0.5 and 2.0 mg/kg/day (2 to 8 times the MRHD on a mg/m2 basis).
Lower doses have not been adequately evaluated and a no adverse effect level could not be established.
