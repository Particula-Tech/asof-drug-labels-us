PRINCIPAL DISPLAY PANEL - 0.1 mg/day

NDC 0378-0871-99

CLONIDINE TRANSDERMAL SYSTEM, USP 0.1 mg/day

Rx only

Contents: 4 Systems and 4 Adhesive Covers

Programmed delivery in vivo of 0.1 mg clonidine per day for one week.

Each system contains 2.52 mg of clonidine.
The inactive components are mineral oil, polyisobutylene, colloidal silicon dioxide, pigmented polyethylene/polyester film, and silicone/polyester film.

FOR TRANSDERMAL USE ONLY.
Keep this and all drugs out of the reach of children.

See package insert for dosage information.

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
