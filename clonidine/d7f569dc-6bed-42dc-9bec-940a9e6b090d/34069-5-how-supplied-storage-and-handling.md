# HOW SUPPLIED

Catapres® (clonidine hydrochloride USP) is supplied in scored oval tablets containing 0.1 mg, 0.2 mg or 0.3 mg of clonidine hydrochloride.

| Dose (mg) | Color | Marking | Bottle of 100 | Bottle of 1000 | Unit Dose of 100 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.1 | Tan | BI 6 | NDC0597-0006-01 | NDC0597-0006-10 | NDC0597-0006-61 |
| 0.2 | Orange | BI 7 | NDC0597-0007-01 | NDC0597-0007-10 | NDC0597-0007-61 |
| 0.3 | Peach | BI 11 | NDC0597-0011-01 |  |  |

Store at 25°C (77°F).
Excursions permitted to 15-30°C (59-86°F).
(See USP controlled room temperature).
Dispense in tight, light-resistant container.
