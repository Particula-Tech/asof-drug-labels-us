# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Doxorubicin HCl liposome injection is a sterile, translucent, red liposomal dispersion in 10-mL or 30-mL glass, single-dose vials.

Each 10-mL vial contains 20 mg doxorubicin HCl at a concentration of 2 mg/mL.

Each 30-mL vial contains 50 mg doxorubicin HCl at a concentration of 2 mg/mL.

The following individually cartoned vials are available:

| 50 mg vial | 25-mL | 30-mL | 0338-0086-01 |

Refrigerate unopened vials of doxorubicin HCl liposome injection at 2°- 8°C (36°- 46°F).
Do not freeze.

Doxorubicin HCl liposome injection is a cytotoxic drug.
Follow applicable special handling and disposal procedures.1
