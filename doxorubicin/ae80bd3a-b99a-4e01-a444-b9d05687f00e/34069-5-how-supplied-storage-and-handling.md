Doxorubicin Hydrochloride Injection, USP is available as a parenteral, isotonic, red colored solution.

Storage Conditions

Store refrigerated between 2° and 8°C (36° and 46°F).

Protect from light.
Retain in carton until time of use.

Discard unused portion.

Storage of Doxorubicin Hydrochloride Injection, USP under refrigerated conditions can result in the formation of a gelled product.
Place gelled product at room temperature [15º to 30ºC (59º to 86ºF)] for 2 to 4 hours to return the product to a slightly viscous, mobile solution.

Sterile, Nonpyrogenic, Preservative-free.

The container closure is not made with natural rubber latex.

Handling and Disposal

Handle and dispose of Doxorubicin Hydrochloride Injection, USP consistent with recommendations for the handling and disposal of hazardous drugs.1
