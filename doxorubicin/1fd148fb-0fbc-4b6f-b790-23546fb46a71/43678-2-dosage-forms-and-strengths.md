# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Doxorubicin Hydrochloride Injection: Vials contain 10 mg/5 mL, 20 mg/10 mL, 50 mg/25 mL, 150 mg/75 mL, and 200 mg/100 mL doxorubicin hydrochloride as a clear red solution.

Doxorubicin Hydrochloride for Injection: Vials contain 10 mg, 20 mg, 50 mg, and 150 mg doxorubicin hydrochloride as a red-orange lyophilized powder.
