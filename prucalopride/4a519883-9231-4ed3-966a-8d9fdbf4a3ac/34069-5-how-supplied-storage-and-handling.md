# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Prucalopride tablets containing 1 mg prucalopride are white to off-white colored, round biconvex, film-coated tablets debossed with "76" on one side and "V1" on other side.
They are supplied as: HDPE bottle of 30 tablets, with child-resistant closure NDC 31722-391-30 Prucalopride tablets containing 2 mg prucalopride are yellow colored, round biconvex film-coated tablets debossed with "77" on one side and "V1" on other side.
They are supplied as: HDPE bottle of 30 tablets, with child-resistant closure NDC 31722-392-30 Store prucalopride tablets at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C to 30°C (between 59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Store prucalopride tablets in the original container to protect from moisture.
