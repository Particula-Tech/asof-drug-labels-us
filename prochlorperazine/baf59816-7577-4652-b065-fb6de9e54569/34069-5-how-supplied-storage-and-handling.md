# HOW SUPPLIED

Prochlorperazine Edisylate Injection, USP 5 mg/mL

2 mL (10 mg) vials packaged in 25s (NDC 0641-6135-25)

## Storage

PROTECT FROM LIGHT.
Store in the box until ready to use.
Discard if markedly discolored.

Store at 20º-25ºC (68º-77ºF), excursions permitted to 15º-30ºC (59º-86ºF) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
