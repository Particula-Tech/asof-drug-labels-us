# 14. CLINICAL STUDIES

In a randomized, parallel-group, multi-center, double-blind, double-dummy study in adults and pediatrics (age ≥ 12 years) with tonsillitis and/or pharyngitis secondary to S. pyogenes, MOXATAG 775 mg QD for 10 days was non-inferior to penicillin VK 250 mg QID for 10 days.

Using strict evaluability and microbiologic response criteria 4-8 days post-therapy, the following bacteriological eradication rates and statistical outcomes in the per-protocol (PPb) and modified intent-to-treat (mITT) populations were obtained (Table 4).
The mITT population included all randomized patients with a positive throat culture for S. pyogenes at baseline.
The PPb population included mITT patients who had post-therapy cultures, were compliant with treatment, and didn't have major protocol violations.
