AUGMENTIN ES-600®

amoxicillin/clavulanate potassium

powder for oral suspension

75 mL (when reconstituted)

600 mg / 5 mL

Rx only

When reconstituted, each 5 mL contains: AMOXICILLIN, 600 MG, as the trihydrate CLAVULANIC ACID, 42.9 MG, as clavulanate potassium

Directions for mixing:

1. Tap bottle until all powder flows freely.
   ADD 70 mL OF WATER IN TWO PARTS.
2. Add approximately 2/3 of the water for reconstitution.
   Shake vigorously.
3. Add remaining water.
   Shake vigorously.

Dosage: Administer every 12 hours.
See accompanying prescribing information.

Phenylketonurics: Contains phenylalanine 7 mg per 5 mL.

Keep tightly closed.
Shake well before using.
Must be refrigerated.
Discard after 10 days.

Use only if inner seal is intact.

Net contents: Equivalent to 9 g amoxicillin and 0.643 g clavulanic acid.

Store dry powder at or below 25oC (77oF).
