# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Capsules: 250 mg, 500 mg.

Each amoxicillin capsule, with yellow opaque cap and body, contains 250 mg or 500 mg amoxicillin as the trihydrate.
The 250 mg capsule is imprinted AMOX 250 on one side and GG 848 on the other side; the 500 mg capsule is imprinted AMOX 500 on one side and GG 849 on the other side.

Tablets: 500 mg, 875 mg.

Each tablet contains 500 mg or 875 mg amoxicillin as the trihydrate.
Each film coated tablets are oval-shaped and white to yellowish.
The 500 mg tablet is unscored, embossed GG-961 on one side and 500 on the other side.
The 875 mg tablet is scored and embossed GG-962 on one side and 875 on the other side.

For Oral Suspension: 125 mg/5 mL, 200 mg/5 mL, 250 mg/5 mL, 400 mg/5 mL.

Each 5 mL of reconstituted suspension contains 125 mg, 200 mg, 250 mg or 400 mg amoxicillin as the trihydrate.
