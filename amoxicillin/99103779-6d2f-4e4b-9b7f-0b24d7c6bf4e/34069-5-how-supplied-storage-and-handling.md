# HOW SUPPLIED

Amoxicillin Tablets, USP contains 500 mg or 875 mg amoxicillin as the trihydrate.
500 mg Tablet Pink colored, capsule shaped, film coated tablets debossed with “A” on one side and “66” on the other side.
Bottles of 20 NDC 65862-014-20 Bottles of 100 NDC 65862-014-01 Bottles of 500 NDC 65862-014-05 875 mg Tablet Pink colored, capsule shaped, film coated tablets debossed with “A” on one side and with a score line in between “6” and “7” on the other side.
Bottles of 20 NDC 65862-015-20 Bottles of 100 NDC 65862-015-01 Bottles of 500 NDC 65862-015-05 Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Dispense in a tight container.
