# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Each TALICIA delayed-release capsule contains omeprazole 10 mg (equivalent to 10.3 mg of omeprazole magnesium), amoxicillin 250 mg and rifabutin 12.5 mg.
The capsules are orange, opaque, with “RHB” imprinted in black on the capsule cap and “105” imprinted in black on the capsule base.
