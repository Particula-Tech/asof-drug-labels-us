# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

## 2.1 Recommended Dosage

Administer four (4) TALICIA capsules three times daily (at least 4 hours apart, e.g., morning, mid-day, and evening) with food for 14 days.
Instruct patients to swallow the TALICIA capsules whole, with a full glass of water (8 ounces).
Each dose (4 capsules) of TALICIA includes rifabutin 50 mg, amoxicillin 1,000 mg and omeprazole 40 mg.
Do not crush or chew TALICIA capsules.
Do not take TALICIA with alcohol.

## 2.2 Missed Doses

- If a dose is missed and the next dose is not within 4 hours, administer the missed dose as soon as possible.
- If a dose is missed and the next dose is within 4 hours, administer the missed dose as soon as possible and delay the next dose to ensure there are at least 4 hours between two doses.
