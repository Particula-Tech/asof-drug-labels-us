# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

TALICIA is supplied as an orange, opaque capsule containing omeprazole 10 mg (equivalent to omeprazole magnesium 10.3 mg), amoxicillin 250 mg and rifabutin 12.5 mg with “RHB” imprinted in black on the capsule cap and “105” imprinted in black on the capsule body.
TALICIA capsules are supplied in a carton containing two bottles of 84 capsules each.

NDC 57841-1150-1 Bottle containing 84 capsules

NDC 57841-1150-2 Carton containing 2 Bottles of 84 capsules

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Store and Dispense in original container with a child-resistant closure.
Keep bottle tightly closed.
