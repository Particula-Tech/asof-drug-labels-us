# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Amoxicillin Tablets, USP 875 mg: Each film-coated tablet contains 875 mg amoxicillin as the trihydrate.
Film-coated, capsule-shaped, white tablet is scored on one side and imprinted WW951 on the other side.

Bottles of 20 NDC 0143-9285-20

Bottles of 100 NDC 0143-9285-01

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP using a child-resistant closure.
