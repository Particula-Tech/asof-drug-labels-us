# HOW SUPPLIED

Amoxicillin Capsules, USP contains 250 mg or 500 mg amoxicillin as the trihydrate.
250 mg Capsule Blue/Pink size “1” hard gelatin capsule filled with white to off white granular powder and imprinted with “A44” on pink body with black ink.
Bottles of 20 NDC 65862-016-20 Bottles of 100 NDC 65862-016-01 Bottles of 500 NDC 65862-016-05 500 mg Capsule Blue/Pink size “0EL” hard gelatin capsule filled with white to off white granular powder and imprinted with “A45” on pink body with black ink.
Bottles of 20 NDC 65862-017-20 Bottles of 100 NDC 65862-017-01 Bottles of 500 NDC 65862-017-05 Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Dispense in a tight container.
