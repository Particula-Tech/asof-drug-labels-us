# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Amoxicillin Capsules, USP are supplied as follows:

250 mg: Size “2” hard gelatin capsules, caramel opaque cap with buff opaque body imprinted “ CE” in black ink on cap and imprinted “ 118” in black ink on body, filled with white to off white crystalline powder.

NDC 62135-824-01 bottles of 100

NDC 62135-824-05 bottles of 500

500 mg: Size “0” hard gelatin capsules, buff opaque cap with buff opaque body imprinted “ CE” in black ink on cap and imprinted “ 114” in black ink on body, filled with white to off white crystalline powder.

NDC 62135-825-60 bottles of 60

NDC 62135-825-05 bottles of 500

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP, with a child-resistant closure (as required).

Keep this and all medications out of the reach of children.
