# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Amoxicillin Capsules, USP:

250 mg: Size “2” hard gelatin capsules, caramel opaque cap with buff opaque body imprinted “ CE” in black ink on cap and imprinted “ 118” in black ink on body, filled with white to off white crystalline powder, and contain 250 mg amoxicillin as the trihydrate.

500 mg: Size “0” hard gelatin capsules, Buff opaque cap with buff opaque body imprinted “ CE” in black ink on cap and imprinted “ 114” in black ink on body, filled with white to off white crystalline powder, and contain 500 mg amoxicillin as the trihydrate.
