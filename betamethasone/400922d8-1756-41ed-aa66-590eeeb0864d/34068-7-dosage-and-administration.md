# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

## 2.1 Usual Dosage and Administration

Apply Taclonex Scalp® Topical Suspension to affected areas on the scalp once daily for 2 weeks or until cleared.
Treatment may be continued for up to 8 weeks.
The maximum weekly dose should not exceed 100 g. Patients should shake the bottle prior to using the product.
Patients should wash their hands after applying Taclonex Scalp® Topical Suspension.

Taclonex Scalp® Topical Suspension is not for oral, ophthalmic, or intravaginal use.
