# 14 CLINICAL STUDIES

The safety and efficacy of DIPROLENE AF Cream for the treatment of corticosteroid-responsive dermatoses have been established in two randomized and active controlled trials in subjects with chronic plaque psoriasis.
A total of 81 subjects who received DIPROLENE AF Cream were included in these trials.
These trials evaluated DIPROLENE AF Cream applied once or twice daily for 14 and 21 days, respectively, on bilateral paired psoriatic lesions.
DIPROLENE AF Cream was shown to be effective in relieving the signs and symptoms of chronic plaque psoriasis.
