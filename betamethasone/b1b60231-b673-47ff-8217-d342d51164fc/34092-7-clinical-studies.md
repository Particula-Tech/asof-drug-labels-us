# 14 CLINICAL STUDIES

The safety and efficacy of DIPROLENE Ointment for the treatment of corticosteroid-responsive dermatoses, psoriasis and atopic dermatitis, have been evaluated in three randomized active-controlled trials, two in psoriasis and one in atopic dermatitis.
A total of 378 subjects, of whom 152 received DIPROLENE Ointment, were included in these trials.
These trials evaluated DIPROLENE Ointment applied twice daily, for 14 days.
DIPROLENE Ointment was shown to be effective in relieving signs and symptoms of psoriasis and atopic dermatitis.
