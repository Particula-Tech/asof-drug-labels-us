# DESCRIPTION

CELESTONE® Oral Solution, for oral administration, contains 0.6 mg betamethasone in each 5 mL.
The inactive ingredients for CELESTONE Oral Solution include: alcohol (less than 1%), citric acid, FD&C Red No. 40, FD&C Yellow No. 6, flavors, propylene glycol, sodium benzoate, sodium chloride, sorbitol, sugar, and water.

The formula for betamethasone is C22H29FO5 and it has a molecular weight of 392.47.

Betamethasone is a white to practically white, odorless crystalline powder.
It melts at about 240°C with some decomposition.
Betamethasone is sparingly soluble in acetone, alcohol, dioxane, and methanol; very slightly soluble in chloroform and ether; and is insoluble in water.
