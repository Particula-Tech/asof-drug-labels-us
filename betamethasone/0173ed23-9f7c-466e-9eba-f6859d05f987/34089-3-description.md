# DESCRIPTION

Betamethasone dipropionate ointment, USP contain betamethasone dipropionate USP, a synthetic adrenocorticosteroid, for dermatologic use.
Betamethasone, an analog of prednisolone, has a high degree of glucocorticoid activity and a slight degree of mineralocorticoid activity.

Betamethasone dipropionate is a white to cream white odorless crystalline powder insoluble in water.
Chemically, it is 9-fluoro-11β,17,21-trihydroxy-16β-methylpregna-1,4-diene-3,20-dione 17,21-dipropionate.
The structural formula is:

Molecular Formula: C28H37FO7

Molecular Weight: 504.60

Each gram of the 0.05% Ointment contains 0.64 mg betamethasone dipropionate (equivalent to 0.5 mg betamethasone) in an ointment base of mineral oil and white petrolatum.
