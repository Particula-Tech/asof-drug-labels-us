# CLINICAL STUDIES

The safety and efficacy of Luxíq has been demonstrated in a four-week trial.
An adequate and well-controlled clinical trial was conducted in 190 patients with moderate to severe scalp psoriasis.
Patients were treated twice daily for four weeks with Luxíq Foam, Placebo foam, a commercially available betamethasone valerate lotion 0.12% (formerly expressed as 0.1% betamethasone), or Placebo lotion.
At four weeks of treatment, study results of 159 patients demonstrated that the efficacy of Luxíq Foam in treating scalp psoriasis is superior to that of Placebo foam, and is comparable to that of a currently marketed BMV lotion (see Table below).
