# DESCRIPTION

Betamethasone dipropionate ointment (augmented) contains betamethasone dipropionate, USP, a synthetic adrenocorticosteroid, for dermatologic use.
Betamethasone, an analog of prednisolone, has a high degree of corticosteroid activity and a slight degree of mineralocorticoid activity.
Betamethasone dipropionate is the 17,21-dipropionate ester of betamethasone.

Chemically, betamethasone dipropionate is 9-fluoro-11β,17,21-trihydroxy-16β-methyl-pregna-1,4-diene-3,20-dione 17,21-dipropionate, with the molecular formula C28H37FO7, a molecular weight of 504.6, and the following structural formula:

Betamethasone dipropionate is a white to creamy white, odorless crystalline powder, insoluble in water.

Each gram of betamethasone dipropionate ointment (augmented) contains: 0.64 mg betamethasone dipropionate, USP (equivalent to 0.5 mg betamethasone), in an augmented vehicle of propylene glycol, propylene glycol stearate, white wax and white petrolatum.
