# DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

Apply a thin film of Augmented Betamethasone Dipropionate Ointment to the affected skin once or twice daily.
Augmented Betamethasone Dipropionate Ointment is a super-high potency topical corticosteroid.
Treatment with Augmented Betamethasone Dipropionate Ointment should be limited to 50 g per week.

As with other corticosteroids, therapy should be discontinued when control is achieved.
If no improvement is seen within 2 weeks, reassessment of diagnosis may be necessary.

Augmented Betamethasone Dipropionate Ointment should not be used with occlusive dressings.
Augmented Betamethasone Dipropionate Ointment should not be applied to the diaper area if the patient requires diapers or plastic pants as these garments may constitute occlusive dressing.
