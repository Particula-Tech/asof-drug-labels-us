# HOW SUPPLIED

PerioGard® is supplied as a blue liquid in a 16-fluid ounce (473 mL) (NDC 0126-0272-16) amber plastic bottle with child-resistant dosage cap.

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) excursions permitted between 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Rx Only.
Keep out of reach of children.
