# OVERDOSAGE

Ingestion of 1 or 2 ounces of PerioGard® (Chlorhexidine Gluconate Oral Rinse USP, 0.12%) by a small child (~10 kg body weight) might result in gastric distress, including nausea.
Medical attention should be sought if more than 4 ounces of PerioGard® Oral Rinse is ingested by a small child.
