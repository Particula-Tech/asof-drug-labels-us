# HOW SUPPLIED

PerioChip (chlorhexidine gluconate) 2.5 mg is supplied as a small, orange-brown, rectangular chip (rounded at one end), in cartons of 20 chips (NDC 64239-101-20).
Each chip is individually packed in a separate compartment of an aluminum blister pack.

Store at 20° - 25°C with excursions permitted to 15° - 30° C (59° - 86°F).

Rx only.
