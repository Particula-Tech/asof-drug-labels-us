# INDICATION:

Peridex is indicated for use between dental visits as part of a professional program for the treatment of gingivitis as characterized by redness and swelling of the gingivae, including gingival bleeding upon probing.
Peridex has not been tested among patients with acute necrotizing ulcerative gingivitis (ANUG).
For patients having coexisting gingivitis and periodontitis, see PRECAUTIONS.
