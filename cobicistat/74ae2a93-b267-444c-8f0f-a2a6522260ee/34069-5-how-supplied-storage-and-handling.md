# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

STRIBILD tablets are green, capsule-shaped, film-coated, debossed with "GSI" on one side and the number "1" surrounded by a square box (1) on the other side.
Each bottle contains 30 tablets (NDC 61958-1201-1), a silica gel desiccant and closed with a child-resistant closure.

Store at 25 °C (77 °F), excursions permitted to 15–30 °C (59–86 °F) (see USP Controlled Room Temperature).

- Keep container tightly closed.
- Dispense only in original container.
- Do not use if seal over bottle opening is broken or missing.
