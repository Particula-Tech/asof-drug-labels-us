# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

STRIBILD tablets are green, capsule shaped, film coated, and debossed with "GSI" on one side and the number "1" surrounded by a square box () on the other side.
Each bottle contains 30 tablets (NDC 61958-1201-1) and a silica gel desiccant, and is closed with a child-resistant closure.

Store at 25 °C (77 °F), excursions permitted to 15–30 °C (59–86 °F) (see USP Controlled Room Temperature).

- Keep container tightly closed.
- Dispense only in original container.
