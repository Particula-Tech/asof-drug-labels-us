# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

TYBOST tablets are available in bottles containing 30 tablets and a silica gel desiccant, with a child-resistant closure.

- TYBOST tablets, 150 mg, are orange, round, biconvex, film-coated, and debossed with “GSI” on one side and plain faced on the other side.
  (NDC 61958-1401-1).
- TYBOST tablets, 90 mg, are white, round, biconvex, film-coated, and debossed with “TYB” on one side and “90” on the other side.
  (NDC 61958-1402-1).

Store at 25 °C (77 °F); excursions permitted to 15°C –30 °C (59–86 °F) (see USP Controlled Room Temperature).

- Keep container tightly closed.
- Dispense only in original container.
- Do not use if seal over bottle opening is broken or missing.
