# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

ATMEKSI (methocarbamol) Oral Suspension contains 750 mg/5 mL methocarbamol.
It is a white to off-white suspension with a fruit flavor and is supplied in bottles of 150 mL with a child-resistant closure (NDC 69528-701-05).

## 16.2 Storage and Handling

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Keep out of reach and sight of children.
