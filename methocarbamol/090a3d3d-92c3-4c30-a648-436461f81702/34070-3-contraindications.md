# CONTRAINDICATIONS

ROBAXIN Injectable should not be administered to patients with known or suspected renal pathology.
This caution is necessary because of the presence of polyethylene glycol 300 in the vehicle.

A much larger amount of polyethylene glycol 300 than is present in recommended doses of ROBAXIN Injectable is known to have increased pre-existing acidosis and urea retention in patients with renal impairment.
Although the amount present in this preparation is well within the limits of safety, caution dictates this contraindication.

ROBAXIN Injectable is contraindicated in patients hypersensitive to methocarbamol or to any of the injection components.
