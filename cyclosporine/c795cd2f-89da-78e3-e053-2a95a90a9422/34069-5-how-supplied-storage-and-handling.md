# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Verkazia (cyclosporine ophthalmic emulsion) 0.1% is packaged in low-density polyethylene single-dose vials.
Each vial contains 0.3 mL fill; 5 vials are packaged in an aluminum pouch; 6, 12, or 24 pouches are packaged in a box.

The entire contents of each box of 30, 60, or 120 vials must be dispensed intact.

30 Single-Dose Vials 0.3 mL each - NDC 65086-001-30

60 Single-Dose Vials 0.3 mL each – NDC 65086-001-60

120 Single-Dose Vials 0.3 mL each – NDC 65086-001-12

Storage: Do not freeze Verkazia.
Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F).
After opening the aluminum pouch, the single-dose vial should be kept in the pouch to protect from light and avoid evaporation.
Any opened individual single-dose vial with any remaining emulsion should be discarded immediately after use.
