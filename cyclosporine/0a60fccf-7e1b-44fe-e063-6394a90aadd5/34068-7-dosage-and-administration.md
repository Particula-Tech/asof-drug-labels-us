# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

## 2.1 Recommended Dosage

Instill one drop of VEVYE twice a day in each eye approximately 12 hours apart.

## 2.2 Administration Instructions

Wash hands before using.

Gently pull the lower eyelid downward.
Tip the bottle upside down over the eye to allow one drop to dispense on its own into the eye.
If a drop does not dispense after a few seconds, gently apply slight pressure to the sides of the bottle while holding over the eye.

Note:You may not feel the drop falling into your eye.

If VEVYE is used with other eye drops, a 15-minute interval between products should occur.
