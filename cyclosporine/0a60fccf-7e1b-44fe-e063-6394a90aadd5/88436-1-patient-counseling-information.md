# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

Risk of Contamination

Advise patients to wash their hands well before each use.
Advise patients not to allow the dropper tip to touch the eye or any other surface, as this may contaminate the solution [see Warning and Precautions (5.1)].

Contact Lens Wear Contact lenses should be removed prior to instillation of VEVYE and may be reinserted 15 minutes following administration [see Warning and Precautions (5.2)].
