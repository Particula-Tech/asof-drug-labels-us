# HOW SUPPLIED

RESTASIS® ophthalmic emulsion is packaged in single use vials.
Each vial contains 0.4 mL fill in a 0.9 mL LDPE vial; 30 vials are packaged in a polypropylene tray with an aluminum peelable lid.
The entire contents of this tray (30 vials) must be dispensed as one unit.

RESTASIS® 30 Vials 0.4 mL each - NDC 0023-9163-30

Storage: Store RESTASIS® ophthalmic emulsion at 15° to 25° C (59°-77° F).

KEEP OUT OF THE REACH OF CHILDREN.
