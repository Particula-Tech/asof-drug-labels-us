# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

RESTASIS MULTIDOSETM ophthalmic emulsion is packaged in a sterile, multi-dose preservative-free bottle.
Each bottle consists of a white opaque LDPE bottle, a white opaque polypropylene top with unidirectional valve and air filter, a protective olive green polypropylene cap, and a clear disposable shipping cover over the colored cap.

5.5 mL in 10-mL bottle - NDC 0023-5301-05

Storage: Store at 15-25 °C (59-77 °F).
