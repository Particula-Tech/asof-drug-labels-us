# 16HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

RESTASIS MULTIDOSE ophthalmic emulsion is packaged in a sterile, multi-dose bottle.
Each bottle consists of a white opaque LDPE bottle, a white opaque polypropylene top with unidirectional valve and air filter, a protective olive green polypropylene cap, and a clear disposable shipping cover over the colored cap.
RESTASIS MULTIDOSE contains no preservatives.

5.5 mL in 10-mL bottle - NDC 0023-5301-05

Storage: Store at 15°C to 25°C (59°F to 77°F).
After opening, RESTASIS MULTIDOSE can be used until the expiration date on the bottle.
