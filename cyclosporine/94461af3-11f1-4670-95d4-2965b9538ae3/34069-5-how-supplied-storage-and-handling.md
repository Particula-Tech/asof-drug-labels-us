### Store and Dispense

In the original unit-dose container at controlled room temperature 68°-77°F (20°-25°C).

## Neoral® Oral Solution (cyclosporine oral solution, USP) MODIFIED

A clear, yellow liquid supplied in 50 mL bottles containing 100 mg/mL (NDC 0078-0274-22).

### Store and Dispense

In the original container at controlled room temperature 68°-77°F (20°-25°C).
Do not store in the refrigerator.
Once opened, the contents must be used within two months.
At temperatures below 68°F (20°C) the solution may gel; light flocculation or the formation of a light sediment may also occur.
There is no impact on product performance or dosing using the syringe provided.
Allow to warm to room temperature 77°F (25°C) to reverse these changes.
