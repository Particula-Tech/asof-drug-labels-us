# 14 CLINICAL STUDIES

Two multicenter, randomized, adequate and well-controlled clinical studies treated 1,048 patients with keratoconjunctivitis sicca (NCT # 02254265 and NCT # 02688556).
In both studies, compared to vehicle at Day 84, there was a statistically significant (p<0.01) higher percentage of eyes with increases of ≥ 10 mm from baseline in Schirmer wetting.
This effect was seen in approximately 17% of CEQUA-treated patients versus approximately 9% of vehicle-treated patients.
