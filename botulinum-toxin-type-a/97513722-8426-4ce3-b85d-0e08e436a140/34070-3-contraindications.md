# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

DYSPORT is contraindicated in patients with:

- Known hypersensitivity to any botulinum toxin products, cow's milk protein, or to any of the components in the formulation [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3)].
  This product may contain trace amounts of cow's milk protein [see Description (11)].
- Infection at the proposed injection site(s).
