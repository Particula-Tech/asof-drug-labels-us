# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

DYSPORT® is contraindicated in patients with:

- Known hypersensitivity to any botulinum toxin preparation or to any of the components in the formulation [see Adverse Reactions (6.1), Description (11)].This product may contain trace amounts of cow's milk protein.
  Patients known to be allergic to cow's milk protein should not be treated with DYSPORT®.
- Infection at the proposed injection site(s).
