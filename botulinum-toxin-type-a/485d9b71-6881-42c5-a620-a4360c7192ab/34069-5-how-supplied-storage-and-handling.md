# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

BOTOX Cosmetic is supplied in a single-use vial in the following sizes:

50 Units: NDC 0023-3919-50 100 Units: NDC 0023-9232-01

Vials of BOTOX Cosmetic have a holographic film on the vial label that contains the name “Allergan” within horizontal lines of rainbow color.
In order to see the hologram, rotate the vial back and forth between your fingers under a desk lamp or fluorescent light source.
(Note: the holographic film on the label is absent in the date/lot area.)
If you do not see the lines of rainbow color or the name “Allergan,” do not use the product and contact Allergan for additional information at 1-800-890-4345 from 7:00 AM to 3:00 PM Pacific Time.

Storage

Unopened vials of BOTOX Cosmetic should be stored in a refrigerator (2° to 8°C).
Do not use after the expiration date on the vial.
