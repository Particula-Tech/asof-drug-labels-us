## 16.2 Storage

Unopened vials of XEOMIN can be stored at room temperature 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F), in a refrigerator at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F), or a freezer at -20°C to -10°C (-4°F to 14°F).
Do not use after the expiration date on the vial.

## 16.3 Handling

XEOMIN is reconstituted prior to use with sterile preservative-free 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection, USP and may be stored in a refrigerator at 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) for up to 24 hours until time of use [see Dosage and Administration (2.8)].
