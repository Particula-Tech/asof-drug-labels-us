# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

BOTOX is supplied in a single-use vial in the following sizes: 100 Units NDC 0023-1145-01 200 Units NDC 0023-3921-02

Vials of BOTOX have a holographic film on the vial label that contains the name “Allergan” within horizontal lines of rainbow color.
In order to see the hologram, rotate the vial back and forth between your fingers under a desk lamp or fluorescent light source.
(Note: the holographic film on the label is absent in the date/lot area.)
If you do not see the lines of rainbow color or the name “Allergan”, do not use the product and contact Allergan for additional information at 1-800-890-4345 from 7:00 AM to 3:00 PM Pacific Time.

Storage

Unopened vials of BOTOX should be stored in a refrigerator (2° to 8°C) for up to 36 months for the 100 Unit vial or up to 24 months for the 200 Unit vial.
Do not use after the expiration date on the vial.
Administer the 200 Unit vial of BOTOX within 4 hours of reconstitution, or the 100 Unit vial within 24 hours of reconstitution; during this period reconstituted BOTOX should be stored in a refrigerator (2° to 8°C).
Reconstituted BOTOX should be clear, colorless, and free of particulate matter.

All vials, including expired vials, or equipment used with the drug should be disposed of carefully, as is done with all medical waste.
