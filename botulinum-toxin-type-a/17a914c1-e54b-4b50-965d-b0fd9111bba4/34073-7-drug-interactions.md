# 7 DRUG INTERACTIONS

No formal drug interaction studies have been conducted with JEUVEAU (prabotulinumtoxinA-xvfs) for injection.
However, the potential for certain drugs to potentiate the effects of JEUVEAU warrant consideration given the potential risks involved and should be used with caution.

- •Aminoglycosides or other agents interfering with neuromuscular transmission
- •Anticholinergic drugs
- •Botulinum neurotoxin products
- •Muscle relaxant
