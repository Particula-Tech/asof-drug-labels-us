# WARNING: DISTANT SPREAD OF TOXIN EFFECT

The effects of all botulinum toxin products, including DAXXIFY, may spread from the area of injection to produce symptoms consistent with botulinum toxin effects.
These symptoms have been reported hours to weeks after injection.
Swallowing and breathing difficulties can be life threatening and there have been reports of death.
DAXXIFY is not approved for any condition other than glabellar lines.
