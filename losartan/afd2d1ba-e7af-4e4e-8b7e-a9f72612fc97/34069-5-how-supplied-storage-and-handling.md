# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

TRADENAME (losartan potassium) is a white, translucent oral suspension that contains 10 mg of losartan potassium per mL.
It is supplied as 165 mL in a 6-ounce high-density polyethylene (HDPE) bottle with a child-resistant cap and tamper-evident seal.
SHAKE BEFORE USING.

NDC 83245-053-06

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Keep container tightly closed.
Protect from light.
