# 16 HOW SUPPLIED

Arbli (losartan potassium)is a white, translucent oral suspension.
Each mL contains 10 mg losartan potassium (equivalent to 9.2 mg losartan).
It is supplied as 165 mL in a high-density polyethylene (HDPE) bottle with a child-resistant cap and tamper-evident seal.
Shake for 20 seconds prior to each use.

NDC 83245-053-06

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted between 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Keep container tightly closed.
Protect from light.

Store and dispense in original container.
Once the bottle is opened, use it within 60 days.
