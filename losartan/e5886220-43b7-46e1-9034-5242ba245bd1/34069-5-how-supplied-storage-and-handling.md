# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Losartan potassium tablet USP is a white film-coated tablet supplied as follows:

| Losartan | Shape | Engraving |  | NDC 68180-xxx-xx |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  |  | One side | Other side | Bottle/90 | Bottle/1000 |
| 25 mg | Capsule shaped | LU | P21 | 376-09 | 376-03 |
| 50 mg | Capsule shaped | L and U on eitherside of breakline (Scored) | P22 | 377-09 | 377-03 |
| 100 mg | Capsule shaped | LU | P23 | 378-09 | 378-03 |

Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Keep container tightly closed.
Protect from light.
