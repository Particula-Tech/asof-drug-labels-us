PRINCIPAL DISPLAY PANEL - 25 mg Bottle Label

NDC 33342-044-07

Losartan Potassium Tablets USP 25 mg

Each tablet contains 25 mg of losartan potassium.

Store at 20 to 25°C (68 to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15-30°C

(59-86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Keep container tightly closed.

Protect from light.

Rx only

30 Tablets
