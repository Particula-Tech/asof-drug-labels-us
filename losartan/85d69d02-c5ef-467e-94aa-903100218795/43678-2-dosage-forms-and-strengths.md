# 3 DOSAGE FORMS & STRENGTHS

• Losartan potassium tablets, USP 25 mg are white to off white oval, biconvex film-coated tablets with CL11 on one side and plain on the other. • Losartan potassium tablets, USP 50 mg are white, off white oval, biconvex film-coated tablets with CL14 on one side and scored on the other. • Losartan potassium tablets, USP 100 mg are white, off white oval, biconvex film-coated tablets with CL17 on one side and plain on the other.
