# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Losartan potassium tablets USP, 25 mg are white to off-white, film coated, oval shaped tablets debossed with 'I' on one side and '5' on the other side.

Bottles of 90 tablets (NDC72603-340-01) Bottles of 1000 tablets (NDC72603-340-02)

Losartan potassium tablets USP, 50 mg are white to off-white, film coated, oval shaped tablets debossed with 'I' on one side and '6' on the other side with score line.

Bottles of 90 tablets (NDC72603-341-01)

Bottles of 1000 tablets (NDC72603-341-02)

Losartan potassium tablets USP, 100 mg are white to off-white, film coated, tear drop shaped tablets debossed with 'H' on one side and '145' on the other side.

Bottles of 90 tablets (NDC72603-342-01) Bottles of 1000 tablets (NDC72603-342-02)

Storage Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Keep container tightly closed.
Protect from light.
