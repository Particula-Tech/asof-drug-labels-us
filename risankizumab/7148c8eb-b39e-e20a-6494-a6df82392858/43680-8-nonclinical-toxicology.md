# 13 NONCLINICAL TOXICOLOGY

## 13.1 Carcinogenesis, Mutagenesis, Impairment of Fertility

Carcinogenicity and mutagenicity studies have not been conducted with SKYRIZI.

No effects on male fertility parameters were observed in sexually mature male cynomolgus monkeys dosed weekly for 26 weeks with 50 mg/kg risankizumab-rzaa at 10 times the exposure (AUC) in humans administered the 600 mg induction regimen for Crohn’s disease, and 39 times the exposure in humans administered the 360 mg maintenance regimen for Crohn’s disease, respectively.
