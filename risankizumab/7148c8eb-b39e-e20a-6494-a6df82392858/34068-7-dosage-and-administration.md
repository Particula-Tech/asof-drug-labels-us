# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

## 2.1 Procedures Prior to Treatment Initiation

• For the treatment of moderately to severely active Crohn’s disease and ulcerative colitis, obtain liver enzymes and bilirubin levels prior to initiating treatment with SKYRIZI [see Warnings and Precautions (5.4)].

• Consider evaluating patients for tuberculosis (TB) infection prior to initiating treatment with SKYRIZI based on clinical judgement [see Warnings and Precautions (5.3)].

• Complete all age-appropriate vaccinations as recommended by current immunization guidelines [see Warnings and Precautions (5.5)].

## 2.2 Recommended Dosage for Moderate-to-Severe Plaque Psoriasis

Adults

The recommended dosage for adults is 150 mg administered by subcutaneous injection at Week 0, Week 4, and every 12 weeks thereafter [see Dosage and Administration (2.7)].

Pediatric Patients 6 Years of Age and Older

The recommended dosage for pediatric patients 6 years of age and older is based on body weight (see Table 1) and administered by subcutaneous injection at Week 0, Week 4, and every 12 weeks thereafter [see Dosage and Administration (2.7)].

Table 1.
Recommended Dose of SKYRIZI for Pediatric Patients 6 Years of Age and Older with Moderate-to-Severe Plaque Psoriasis

| Body Weight | Recommended Dose |
| --- | --- |
| less than 40 kg | 55 mg |
| 40 kg or greater | 150 mg |

## 2.3 Recommended Dosage for Active Psoriatic Arthritis

Adults

The recommended dosage for adult patients is 150 mg administered by subcutaneous injection at Week 0, Week 4, and every 12 weeks thereafter [see Dosage and Administration (2.7)].

SKYRIZI may be administered alone or in combination with non-biologic disease-modifying antirheumatic drugs (DMARDs).

Pediatric Patients 6 Years of Age and Older

The recommended dosage for pediatric patients 6 years of age and older is based on body weight (see Table 2) and administered by subcutaneous injection at Week 0, Week 4, and every 12 weeks thereafter [see Dosage and Administration (2.7)].

Table 2.
Recommended Dose of SKYRIZI for Pediatric Patients 6 Years of Age and Older with Active Psoriatic Arthritis

| Body Weight | Recommended Dose |
| --- | --- |
| less than 40 kg | 55 mg |
| 40 kg or greater | 150 mg |

SKYRIZI may be administered alone or in combination with non-biologic disease-modifying antirheumatic drugs (DMARDs).

## 2.4 Recommended Dosage for Moderately to Severely Active Crohn’s Disease

Adults

Induction

The recommended induction dosage of SKYRIZI is:

• 600 mg administered by intravenous infusion over a period of at least one hour at Week 0, Week 4, and Week 8 [see Dosage and Administration (2.6)]

or

• 720 mg administered by subcutaneous injection at Week 0, Week 2, Week 4, and Week 8 [see Dosage and Administration (2.7)]

Maintenance

The recommended maintenance dosage of SKYRIZI is 180 mg or 360 mg administered by subcutaneous injection at Week 12, and every 8 weeks thereafter.
Use the lowest effective dosage needed to maintain therapeutic response [see Dosage and Administration (2.7)].

## 2.5 Recommended Dosage for Moderately to Severely Active Ulcerative Colitis

Adults

Induction

The recommended induction dosage of SKYRIZI is 1,200 mg administered by intravenous infusion over a period of at least two hours at Week 0, Week 4, and Week 8 [see Dosage and Administration (2.6)].

Maintenance

The recommended maintenance dosage of SKYRIZI is 180 mg or 360 mg administered by subcutaneous injection at Week 12, and every 8 weeks thereafter.
Use the lowest effective dosage needed to maintain therapeutic response [see Dosage and Administration (2.7)].

## 2.6 Preparation and Administration Instructions for Intravenous Infusion (Moderately to Severely Active Crohn’s Disease and Ulcerative Colitis)

SKYRIZI vial for intravenous administration is intended for administration by a healthcare provider using aseptic technique.

1.
Withdraw the appropriate dosage volume of SKYRIZI from the vial(s) and inject it into an intravenous infusion bag or glass bottle containing 5% Dextrose Injection or 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection (see Table 3 below) for a final concentration of approximately 1.2 mg/mL to 6 mg/mL.
Discard any remaining solution in the vial(s).

Table 3.
Total Volume of Diluent Required for Intravenous Induction Dose

| Indication | Intravenous Induction Dose | Total Volume of 5% Dextrose or 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crohn’s disease | 600 mg | 100 mL, 250 mL, or 500 mL |
| Ulcerative colitis | 1,200 mg | 250 mL or 500 mL |

2.
Visually inspect diluted solution for particulate matter and discoloration prior to infusion.
The diluted solution should be colorless to slightly yellow, and clear to slightly opalescent solution and may contain a few translucent to white particles.
Do not use if the solution contains large particles or is cloudy or discolored.

3.
Infuse the diluted solution intravenously over a period of at least one hour for the SKYRIZI 600 mg dose and at least two hours for the SKYRIZI 1,200 mg dose.
If stored refrigerated, allow the diluted solution to warm to room temperature prior to the start of the intravenous infusion.

4.
Do not administer diluted solution concomitantly in the same intravenous line with other medicinal products.

Handling and Storage of the Diluted Solution:

- Do not shake the diluted solution [see How Supplied/Storage and Handling (16)].
- Use the prepared infusion immediately.
  If not used immediately, refrigerate the diluted solution for up to 20 hours between 36°F to 46°F (2°C to 8°C) and protect from light.
- The diluted solution can be stored at room temperature at up to 77°F (25°C) (protected from sunlight) for up to 8 hours.
  Storage time at room temperature begins once the diluted solution has been prepared.
  Discard the diluted solution if it cannot be completely infused within the 8-hour room temperature storage limit.
- Exposure to indoor light is acceptable during room temperature storage and administration.
- Do not freeze.

## 2.7 Preparation and Administration Instructions for Subcutaneous Injection

• SKYRIZI is available for subcutaneous use in the following presentations: prefilled pen, prefilled syringe, and prefilled cartridge for use with the on-body injector [see Dosage Forms and Strengths (3) and How Supplied/Storage and Handling (16)].

• Provide proper training to patients and/or caregivers on the subcutaneous injection technique of SKYRIZI.
After proper training:

Adults

○ Adults may self-inject SKYRIZI.

Pediatric Patients 6 Years of Age and Older

○ For pediatric patients 10 years of age and older, it is recommended that SKYRIZI be administered by or under the supervision of an adult.

○ For pediatric patients 6 to less than 10 years of age, SKYRIZI should be administered by an adult.

- Do not inject into areas where the skin is tender, bruised, erythematous, indurated or affected by any lesions.
  Appropriate sites for injection by the patient are: front of the thighs or the abdomen at least 2 inches from the navel.
- Visually inspect SKYRIZI for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration.
  The solution should be colorless to yellow, and clear to slightly opalescent and may contain a few translucent to white particles.
  Do not use if the solution contains large particles or is cloudy or discolored.
- If a dose is missed, administer the dose as soon as possible.
  Thereafter, resume dosing at the regular scheduled time.
- The SKYRIZI “Instructions for Use” contains more detailed instructions on the preparation and administration of SKYRIZI [see Instructions for Use].
  Instruct the patient to read the Instructions for Use before administration.

Prefilled pens and prefilled syringes:

- Remove from the refrigerator and allow to reach room temperature out of direct sunlight (30 minutes to 90 minutes for the prefilled pen and 15 minutes to 30 minutes for the prefilled syringe) without removing the prefilled pens or prefilled syringes from the carton.
- Administration in the upper, outer arm may only be performed by a healthcare professional or caregiver.
- If the dose requires more than one injection: Inject in different locations at least 1 inch apart (such as thighs or abdomen).

On-body injectors with prefilled cartridges:

- Remove from the refrigerator and allow to reach room temperature out of direct sunlight (45 minutes to 90 minutes) without removing the prefilled cartridges or on-body injectors from the carton.
- Start the injection within 5 minutes after inserting the prefilled cartridge into the on-body injector.
- If the dose requires more than one injection: Inject in different locations at least 2 inches apart (such as thighs or abdomen).
