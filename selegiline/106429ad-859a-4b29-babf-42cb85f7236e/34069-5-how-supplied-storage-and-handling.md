# HOW SUPPLIED

ELDEPRYL Capsules are available containing 5 mg of selegiline hydrochloride, USP.

The 5 mg capsule is a hard-shell gelatin capsule with an aqua blue opaque cap and an aqua blue opaque body band imprinted with the Somerset logo on the cap and Eldepryl 5 mg on the body in black ink.
The capsule is filled with white powder.
They are available as follows:

NDC 49502-420-60 bottles of 60 capsules

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
[See USP Controlled Room Temperature.]

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container as defined in the USP using a child-resistant closure.
