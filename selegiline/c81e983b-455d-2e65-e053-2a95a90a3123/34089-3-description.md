# DESCRIPTION

Selegiline hydrochloride is a levorotatory acetylenic derivative of phenethylamine.
It is commonly referred to in the clinical and pharmacological literature as 1-deprenyl.

The chemical name is: (R)-(-)- N,2-dimethyl- N-2-propynylphenethylamine hydrochloride.
It is a white to near white crystalline powder, freely soluble in water, chloroform, and methanol, and has a molecular weight of 223.74.
The molecular formula is C 13H 17N•HCl.
