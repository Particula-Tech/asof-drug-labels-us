# HOW SUPPLIED

EMSAM (selegiline transdermal system) is supplied as 6 mg/24 hours (20 mg/20 cm2), 9 mg/24 hours (30 mg/30 cm2) and 12 mg/24 hours (40 mg/40 cm2) transdermal systems.

They are available as: NDC 49502-900-30: 6 mg/24 hours (20 mg/20 cm2) box of 30 transdermal systems.
NDC 49502-901-30: 9 mg/24 hours (30 mg/30 cm2) box of 30 transdermal systems.
NDC 49502-902-30: 12 mg/24 hours (40 mg/40 cm2) box of 30 transdermal systems.
