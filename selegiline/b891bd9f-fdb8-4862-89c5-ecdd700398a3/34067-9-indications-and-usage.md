# 1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

EMSAM (selegiline transdermal system) is a monoamine oxidase inhibitor (MAOI) indicated for the treatment of major depressive disorder (MDD).
The efficacy of EMSAM in the treatment of major depressive disorder was established in two short term (6-week and 8-week) trials of outpatients with major depressive disorder.
Efficacy was studied in two short term studies and one maintenance study [see Clinical Studies (14.1)].
