# DESCRIPTION

Selegiline Hydrochloride, USP is a levorotatory acetylenic derivative of phenethylamine.
It is commonly referred to in the clinical and pharmacological literature as l-deprenyl.

The chemical name is: (R)-(-)-N,2-dimethyl-N-2-propynylphenethylamine hydrochloride.
It is a white to near white crystalline powder, freely soluble in water, chloroform, and methanol.
The molecular formula is C 13H 17N•HCl; and the molecular weight is 223.75.
