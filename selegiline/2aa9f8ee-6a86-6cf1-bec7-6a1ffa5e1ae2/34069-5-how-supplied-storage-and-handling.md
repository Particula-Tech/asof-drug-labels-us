# HOW SUPPLIED

Selegiline Hydrochloride Tablets USP 5 mg are available for oral administration as white or slightly mottled, round, unscored tablets imprinted with on one side and “3438” on the other side.
They are supplied as bottles of 60 (NDC 60505-3438-3) and bottles of 500 (NDC 60505-3438-8).

Storage Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container [see USP] with a child-resistant closure as required.
