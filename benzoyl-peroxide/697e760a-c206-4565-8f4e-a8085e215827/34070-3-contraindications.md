# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

## 4.1 Hypersensitivity

ONEXTON Gel is contraindicated in those individuals who have shown hypersensitivity to clindamycin, benzoyl peroxide, any components of the formulation, or lincomycin.
Anaphylaxis, as well as allergic reactions leading to hospitalization, has been reported in postmarketing use with ONEXTON Gel [see Postmarketing Experience (6.2)].

## 4.2 Colitis/Enteritis

ONEXTON Gel is contraindicated in patients with a history of regional enteritis, ulcerative colitis, or antibiotic-associated colitis [seeWarnings and Precautions (5.1)].
