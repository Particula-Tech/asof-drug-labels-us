# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

## 16.1 How Supplied

Adapalene and Benzoyl Peroxide gel 0.3%/2.5% is white to very pale yellow in color and opaque in appearance, and is supplied as follows: 45 gram pump NDC 68308-662-45

## 16.2 Storage and handling

- Store at controlled room temperature 20 – 25˚C (68 – 77˚F) with excursions permitted to 15˚ – 30˚C (59˚ – 86˚F) [see USP controlled room temperature].
- Keep away from heat.
- Protect from light.
- Keep out of reach of children.
