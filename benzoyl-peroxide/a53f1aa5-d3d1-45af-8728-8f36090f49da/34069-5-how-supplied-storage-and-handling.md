# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Adapalene and Benzoyl Peroxide Gel 0.1%/2.5% is white to pale yellow opaque gel and is supplied as follows:

| 45 gram tube | NDC 51672-1364-6 |
| --- | --- |
| 45 gram pump | NDC 51672-1364-3 |

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Protect from light.
Keep out of reach of children.
Keep away from heat.
Keep tube tightly closed.
