# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

How Supplied

TWYNEO (tretinoin and benzoyl peroxide) cream, 0.1%/3%, is a yellow cream and is supplied as:

- 30-gram bottle with a pump, NDC 79167-301-30
- 50-gram bottle with a pump, NDC 79167-301-50

Storage and Handling

- Prior to Dispensing: Store TWYNEO between 2°C to 8°C (36°F to 46°F) until dispensed to the patient.
- After Dispensing 30-gram bottle with a pump: Store TWYNEO at room temperature 20ºC to 25ºC (68ºF to 77ºF).
  Discard 12 weeks after date of dispensing or 30 days after first opening, whichever is sooner.
- After Dispensing 50-gram bottle with a pump: Store TWYNEO at room temperature 20°C to 25°C (68ºF to 77°F).
  Discard 12 weeks after date of dispensing or 60 days after first opening, whichever is sooner.
- Do not freeze.
