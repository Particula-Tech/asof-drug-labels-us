# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

How Supplied

EPSOLAY is a white to off-white cream supplied in an airless pump as follows: 30 gram pump: NDC 51862-772-30

Storage and Handling

- Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) with excursions permitted to 15°C to 30°C (59°F to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
- Keep away from heat.
- Do not freeze.
- Discard unused cream 60 days after first use.
