# 2 DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION

- Apply a thin layer of TWYNEO Cream to the affected areas once daily on clean and dry skin.
  Avoid contact with the eyes, lips, paranasal creases, and mucous membranes.
- Wash hands after application.
- TWYNEO Cream is for topical use only.
  TWYNEO Cream is not for oral, ophthalmic, or intravaginal use.
