# 17 PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION

See FDA-approved patient labeling (Patient Information).

- Patients who develop allergic reactions such as severe swelling or shortness of breath should discontinue use and contact their physician immediately.
- Clindamycin phosphate and benzoyl peroxide gel may cause irritation such as erythema, scaling, itching, or burning, especially when used in combination with other topical acne therapies.
- Excessive or prolonged exposure to sunlight should be limited.
  To minimize exposure to sunlight, a hat or other clothing should be worn.
  Sunscreen may also be used.
- Clindamycin phosphate and benzoyl peroxide gel may bleach hair or colored fabric.
- Clindamycin phosphate and benzoyl peroxide gel may cause skin and facial hair to temporarily change color (yellow/orange) when used with topical sulfone products.
