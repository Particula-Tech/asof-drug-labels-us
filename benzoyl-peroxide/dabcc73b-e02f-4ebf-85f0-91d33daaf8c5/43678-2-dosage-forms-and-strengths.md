# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Once admixed, ACANYA Gel contains clindamycin phosphate 1.2% and benzoyl peroxide 2.5%, formulated as a topical gel.
Each gram of ACANYA Gel contains, as dispensed, 10 mg (1%) clindamycin as phosphate, and 25 mg (2.5%) benzoyl peroxide.
ACANYA Gel is available in 50 gram jars.
