# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Abacavir tablets USP 300 mg, containing abacavir sulfate equivalent to 300 mg abacavir are yellow colored, capsule shaped, film coated biconvex tablets with scoreline on both sides.
They are packaged as follows

Bottles of 60 tablets (NDC 69097-514-03).

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
