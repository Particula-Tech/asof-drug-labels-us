# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

ZIAGEN Tablets contain 300 mg of abacavir as abacavir sulfate.
The tablets are yellow, biconvex, scored, capsule-shaped, film-coated, and imprinted with “GX 623” on both sides.

ZIAGEN Oral Solution contains 20 mg/mL of abacavir as abacavir sulfate.
The solution is a clear to opalescent, yellowish, strawberry-banana-flavored liquid.
