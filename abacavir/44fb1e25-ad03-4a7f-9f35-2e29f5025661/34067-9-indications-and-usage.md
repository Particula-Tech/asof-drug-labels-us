# 1 INDICATIONS & USAGE

Abacavir tablets USP, in combination with other antiretroviral agents, are indicated for the treatment of human immunodeficiency virus (HIV-1) infection.

Additional important information on the use of abacavir tablet USP for treatment of HIV-1 infection:

• Abacavir tablet USP is one of multiple products containing abacavir.
Before starting abacavir tablet USP, review medical history for prior exposure to any abacavir-containing product in order to avoid reintroduction in a patient with a history of hypersensitivity to abacavir [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1),Adverse Reactions (6)].
