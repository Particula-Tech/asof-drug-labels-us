# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Abacavir oral solution USP is a clear yellowish, strawberry-banana flavored liquid filled in 250 cc HDPE opaque bottles.
Each mL of the solution contains abacavir sulfate USP equivalent to 20 mg of abacavir.
They are supplied in: Bottles of 240 mL with Induction Sealing FSE Wad (NDC 31722-562-24).
This product does not require reconstitution.
Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
DO NOT FREEZE.
May be refrigerated.
