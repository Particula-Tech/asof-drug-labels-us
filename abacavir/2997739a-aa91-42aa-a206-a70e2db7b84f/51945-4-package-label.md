Triumeq

TABLETS

Notice ot Authorized Dispenser:

Each time TRIUMEQ is dispensed, give the patient a Medication Guide and Warning Card.

Rx only

30 Tablets

Each film-coated tablet contains abacavir sulfate equivalent to 600 mg of abacavir, dolutegravir sodium equivalent to 50 mg of dolutegravir, and 300 mg of lamivudine.

This package is child-resistant.
Keep out of reach of children.

Store in original package at controlled room temperature of 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) (See USP).
Keep bottle tightly closed; protect from moisture.

Do not remove desiccant.
