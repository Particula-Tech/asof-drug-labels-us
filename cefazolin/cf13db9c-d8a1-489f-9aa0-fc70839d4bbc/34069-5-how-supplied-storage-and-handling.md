# HOW SUPPLIED

Cefazolin for Injection, USP, is available in the following SmartPak® Pharmacy Bulk Packages:

- 100 grams* (1 Pharmacy Bulk Package) Product No. 1100 NDC 66288-1100-1
- 300 grams** (1 Pharmacy Bulk Package) Product No. 1300 NDC 66288-1300-1
- *Each 100 gram Pharmacy Bulk Package contains sterile cefazolin sodium equivalent to100 grams of cefazolin.
- **Each 300 gram Pharmacy Bulk Package contains sterile cefazolin sodium equivalent to 300 grams of cefazolin.

No component of the SmartPak® system contains natural rubber latex.

As with other cephalosporins, cefazolin tends to darken depending on storage conditions within the stated recommendations; however, product potency is not adversely affected.
Prior to reconstitution, store dry powder at 20° to 25° C (68° to77° F). [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect From Light.
