# 16 HOW SUPPLIED

Cefazolin for Injection is available as 2 grams or 3 grams of cefazolin as a white to off-white crystalline powder in single-dose vial for reconstitution.

| Cefazolin for Injection, USP | Packaged | NDC No. |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 grams/vial | Carton of 25 vials | 0143-9139-25 |
| 3 grams/vial | Carton of 25 vials | 0143-9140-25 |

Before reconstitution protect from light and store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Storage conditions for reconstituted and diluted solutions of Cefazolin for Injection are described in another section of labeling [see Dosage and Administration (2.6)].
