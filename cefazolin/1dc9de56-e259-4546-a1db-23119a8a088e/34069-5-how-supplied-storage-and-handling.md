# HOW SUPPLIED

Cefazolin for Injection, USP, is supplied in vials containing cefazolin sodium equivalent to 500 mg or 1 gram cefazolin and in infusion bottles containing cefazolin sodium equivalent to 1 gram cefazolin:

NDC 0143-9923-90 500mg / 10mL vial, Carton of 25 vials NDC 0143-9924-90 1g / 10mL vial, Carton of 25 vials NDC 0143-9925-91 1g / 100mL vial, Carton of 10 bottles

As with other cephalosporins, Cefazolin for Injection, USP, tends to darken depending on storage conditions; within the stated recommendations, however, product potency is not adversely affected.
Before reconstitution, protect from light and store at 20°-25°C (68° - 77°F), [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
