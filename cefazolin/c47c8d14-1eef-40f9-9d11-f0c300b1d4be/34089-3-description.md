# DESCRIPTION

Cefazolin for Injection, USP is a semi-synthetic cephalosporin for parenteral administration.
It is the sodium salt of 3-{[(5-methyl-1,3,4-thiadiazol-2-yl)thio]-methyl}-8-oxo-7-[2-(1H-tetrazol-1-yl)acetamido]-5-thia-1-azabicyclo [4.2.0]oct-2-ene-2-carboxylic acid.

Cefazolin for Injection, USP is a sterile white to cream powder supplied in vials.
Each vial contains, cefazolin sodium equivalent to 500 mg or 1 gram of cefazolin.

The sodium content is approximately 24 mg (1 mEq)/500 mg of cefazolin sodium or approximately 48 mg (2.1 mEq)/1 gram of cefazolin sodium.

The color of Cefazolin for Injection, USP solutions may range from pale yellow to yellow without a change in potency.

Cefazolin for Injection, USP is to be administered by intramuscular or intravenous routes.
