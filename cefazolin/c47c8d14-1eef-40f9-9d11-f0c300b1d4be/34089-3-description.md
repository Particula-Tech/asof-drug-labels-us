# DESCRIPTION

Cefazolin for Injection, USP is a semi-synthetic cephalosporin for parenteral administration.
It is 5-thia-1-azabicyclo[4.2.0]oct-2-ene-2-carboxylic acid, 3-{[(5-methyl-1,3,4-thiadiazol-2-yl)thio]-methyl}-8-oxo-7-[[(1H-tetrazol-1-yl)acetyl] amino]-, monosodium salt (6R-trans).
The sodium content is 48 mg/g of cefazolin sodium.

The structural formula is as follows:

C14H13N8NaO4S3 M.W.
476.5

The pH of the constituted solution is between 4.5 and 6.

Cefazolin for Injection, USP is supplied in 500 mg or 1 gram vials.
Each vial contains cefazolin sodium equivalent to 500 mg or 1 gram of cefazolin.
It is to be administered by intramuscular or intravenous routes.
