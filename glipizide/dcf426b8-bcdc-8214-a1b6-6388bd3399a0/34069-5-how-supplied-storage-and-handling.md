# HOW SUPPLIED

Glipizide Tablets USP are supplied as white to off-white, round, scored tablets, imprinted as follows: 5 mg- “APO” on the side and “GLP” over bisect “5” on the other side; 10 mg- “APO” on one side and “GLP” over bisect “10” on the other side.

5 mg Bottles: 100’s (NDC 60505-0141-0);

500’s (NDC 60505-0141-2);

1000’s (NDC 60505-0141-1);

18,000’s (NDC 60505-0141-8);

10 mg Bottles: 100’s (NDC 60505-0142-0);

500’s (NDC 60505-0142-2);

1000’s (NDC 60505-0142-1);

10,000’s (NDC 60505-0142-4).
