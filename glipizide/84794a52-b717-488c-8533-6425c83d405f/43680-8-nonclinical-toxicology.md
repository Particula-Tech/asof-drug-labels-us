# 13 NONCLINICAL TOXICOLOGY

## 13.1 Carcinogenesis, Mutagenesis, Impairment of Fertility

A twenty month study inrats and an eighteenmonth study in mice at doses upto 75 times the maximum human dose revealednoevidence of drug-relatedcarcinogenicity.Bacterialandinvivomutagenicity tests were uniformly negative.Studiesinrats of both sexes at doses upto 20 times the human dose based on body surface area, showednoeffects on fertility.
