# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Glipizide Extended-Release Tablets 2.5 mg are white to off-white, round, biconvex film- coated tablets imprinted with ‘G’ over ‘2.5’ on one side with black ink.
Glipizide Extended-Release Tablets 5 mg are white to off-white, round, biconvex film- coated tablets imprinted with ‘G 5’ on one side with black ink.
Glipizide Extended-Release Tablets 10 mg are white to off-white, round, biconvex film-coated tablets imprinted with ‘G’ over ‘10’ with black ink.
