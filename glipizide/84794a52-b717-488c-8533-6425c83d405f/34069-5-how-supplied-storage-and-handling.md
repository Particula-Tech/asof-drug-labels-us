# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Bottles of 30 NDC 59651-780-30

Bottles of 30 NDC 59651-781-30

Bottles of 100 NDC 59651-781-01

Bottles of 500 NDC 59651-781-05

Glipizide Extended-Release Tablets, 10 mg are white to off-white, round, biconvex film-coated tablets imprinted with ‘G’ over ‘10’ with black ink.

Bottles of 30 NDC 59651-782-30

Bottles of 100 NDC 59651-782-01

Bottles of 500 NDC 59651-782-05

Bottles of 1,000 NDC 59651-782-99

Recommended Storage: The tablets should be protected from moisture and humidity.
Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
