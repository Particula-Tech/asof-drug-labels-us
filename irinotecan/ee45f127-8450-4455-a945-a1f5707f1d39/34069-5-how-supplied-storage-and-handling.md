# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Each mL of irinotecan hydrochloride injection contains 20 mg irinotecan (on the basis of the trihydrate salt); 45 mg sorbitol; and 0.9 mg lactic acid.

| Product No. | NDC No. | Strength | Fill Volume |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 109352 | 63323-193-52 | 40 mg/2 mL (20 mg/mL) | Irinotecan Hydrochloride Injection, in a 2 mL single dose amber glass vial, packaged individually. |
| 109355 | 63323-193-55 | 100 mg/5 mL (20 mg/mL) | Irinotecan Hydrochloride Injection, in a 5 mL single dose amber glass vial, packaged individually. |

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F).
See USP Controlled Room Temperature; excursions premitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F).
Protect from light.
Retain in carton until time of use.

Inspect the vial for damage and visible signs of leaks before removing from the carton.
If damaged, incinerate the unopened package.

This container closure is not made with natural rubber latex.
