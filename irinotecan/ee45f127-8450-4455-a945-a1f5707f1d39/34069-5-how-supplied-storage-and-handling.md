# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Each mL of irinotecan hydrochloride injection contains 20 mg irinotecan (on the basis of the trihydrate salt); 45 mg sorbitol; and 0.9 mg lactic acid.

| Product No. | NDC No. | Strength | Fill Volume |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 109352 | 63323-193-52 | 40 mg per 2 mL (20 mg per mL) | Irinotecan Hydrochloride Injection, in a 2 mL single dose amber glass vial, packaged individually. |
| 109355 | 63323-193-55 | 100 mg per 5 mL (20 mg per mL) | Irinotecan Hydrochloride Injection, in a 5 mL single dose amber glass vial, packaged individually. |

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Protect from light (keep in outer carton).
Inspect the vial for damage and visible signs of leaks before removing from the carton.
If damaged, incinerate the unopened package.
The container closure is not made with natural rubber latex.
