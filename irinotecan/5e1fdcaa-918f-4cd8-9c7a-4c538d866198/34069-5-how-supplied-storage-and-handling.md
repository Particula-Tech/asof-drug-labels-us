# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Irinotecan Hydrochloride Injection, USP is available as a sterile, pale yellow, clear, aqueous solution in a single-dose, amber glass vial within a carton in the following presentations:

- 40 mg/2 mL (20 mg/mL) NDC 45963-614-51
- 100 mg/5 mL (20 mg/mL) NDC 45963-614-55

Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Protect from light.
It is recommended that the vial should remain in the carton until the time of use.

Inspect the vial for damage and visible signs of leaks before removing from the carton.
If damaged, incinerate the unopened package.

Sterile, Nonpyrogenic, Preservative-free.

The vial stopper is not made with natural rubber latex.

Irinotecan Hydrochloride Injection, USP is a hazardous drug.
Follow special handling and disposal procedures.1
