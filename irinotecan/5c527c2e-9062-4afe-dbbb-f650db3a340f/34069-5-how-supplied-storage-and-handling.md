# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Each mL of Irinotecan Hydrochloride Injection, USP contains 20 mg irinotecan (on the basis of the trihydrate salt); 45 mg sorbitol; 0.9 mg of lactic acid, USP and Water for Injection, USP.
When necessary, pH has been adjusted to 3.5 (range, 3.0 to 3.8) with sodium hydroxide or hydrochloric acid.

Irinotecan Hydrochloride Injection, USP is available in single-dose amber glass vials in the following package size:

NDC 61703-349-16, 40 mg/2 mL (20 mg/mL)

NDC 61703-349-09, 100 mg/5 mL (20 mg/mL)

NDC 61703-349-36, 500 mg/25 mL (20 mg/mL)

The vial should be inspected for damage and visible signs of leaks before removing from the carton.
If damaged, incinerate the unopened package.

Store at controlled room temperature 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F).
Protect from light.
Keep the vial in the carton until the time of use.

Inspect the vial for damage and visible signs of leaks before removing from the carton.
If damaged, incinerate the unopened package.
