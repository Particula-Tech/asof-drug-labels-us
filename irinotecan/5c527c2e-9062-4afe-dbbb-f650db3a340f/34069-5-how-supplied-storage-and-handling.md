# HOW SUPPLIED

Each mL of Irinotecan Hydrochloride Injection contains 20 mg irinotecan (on the basis of the trihydrate salt); 45 mg sorbitol; 0.9 mg lactic acid; and Water for Injection, USP.
When necessary, pH has been adjusted to 3.5 (range, 3.0 to 3.8) with sodium hydroxide or hydrochloric acid.

Irinotecan Hydrochloride Injection is available in single-use amber glass vials in the following package sizes:

NDC 61703-349-16 40 mg/2 mL

NDC 61703-349-09 100 mg/5 mL

Store at 20°-25° C (68°-77° F). See USP Controlled Room Temperature (excursions permitted to 15°-30° C (59°-86° F)). Protect from light.
It is recommended that the vial should remain in the carton until the time of use.
Protect from freezing.
Store Upright.
