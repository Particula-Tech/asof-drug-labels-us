# HOW SUPPLIED

Each mL of CAMPTOSAR Injection contains 20 mg irinotecan (on the basis of the trihydrate salt); 45 mg sorbitol; and 0.9 mg lactic acid.
When necessary, pH has been adjusted to 3.5 (range, 3.0 to 3.8) with sodium hydroxide or hydrochloric acid.

CAMPTOSAR Injection is available in single-dose amber glass vials in the following package sizes:

2 mL NDC 0009-7529-02

5 mL NDC 0009-7529-01

The vial should be inspected for damage and visible signs of leaks before removing from the carton.
If damaged, incinerate the unopened package.

Store at controlled room temperature 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F).
Protect from light.
It is recommended that the vial should remain in the carton until the time of use.

Rx only
