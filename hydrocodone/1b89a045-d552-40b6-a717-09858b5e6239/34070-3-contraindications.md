# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

ZOHYDRO ER is contraindicated in patients with:

- Significant respiratory depression [see Warnings and Precautions (5.2)]
- Acute or severe bronchial asthma in an unmonitored setting or in the absence of resuscitative equipment [see Warnings and Precautions (5.6)]
- Known or suspected gastrointestional obstruction, including paralytic ileus [see Warnings and Precautions (5.10)]
- Hypersensitivity (e.g., anaphylaxis) to hydrocodone or any other ingredients in ZOHYDRO ER
