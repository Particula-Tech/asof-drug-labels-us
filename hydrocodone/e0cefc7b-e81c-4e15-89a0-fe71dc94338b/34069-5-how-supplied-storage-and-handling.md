# HOW SUPPLIED

Hydrocodone Bitartrate and Acetaminophen Tablets, USP 10 mg / 325 mg are available as white to off white, scored, oblong biconvex tablets, debossed “IP 110” on obverse and bisected on the reverse.
Each tablet contains 10 mg hydrocodone bitartrate and 325 mg acetaminophen.

They are available as follows:

Bottles of 30: NDC 53746-110-30

Bottles of 500: NDC 53746-110-05

Storage: Store at 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15° to 30°C (59° to 86°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Dispense in a tight, light-resistant container with a child-resistant closure.

A Schedule CIII Opioid.
