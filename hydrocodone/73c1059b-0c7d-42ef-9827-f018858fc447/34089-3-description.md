# DESCRIPTION

Hydrocodone bitartrate and acetaminophen is available in tablet form for oral administration.

Hydrocodone bitartrate is an opioid analgesic and occurs as fine, white crystals or as a crystalline powder.
It is affected by light.
The chemical name is 4,5α-epoxy-3-methoxy-17-methylmorphinan-6-one tartrate (1:1) hydrate (2:5).

Each Hydrocodone Bitartrate and Acetaminophen Tablet USP contains:

| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 mg/325 mg | 5 mg | 325 mg |
