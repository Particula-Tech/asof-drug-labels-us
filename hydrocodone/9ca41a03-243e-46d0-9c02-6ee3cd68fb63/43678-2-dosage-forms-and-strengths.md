# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

VANTRELA ER is available as 15 mg, 30 mg, 45 mg, 60 mg, and 90 mg extended-release tablets.

- •15 mg: Light red capsule-shaped tablets, debossed with C on one side and 15 on the other.
- •30 mg: Yellow capsule-shaped tablets, debossed with C on one side and 30 on the other.
- •45 mg: White to off-white capsule-shaped tablets, debossed with C on one side and 45 on the other.
- •60 mg: Light blue capsule-shaped tablets, debossed with C on one side and 60 on the other.
- •90 mg: Light green capsule-shaped tablets, debossed with C on one side and 90 on the other.
