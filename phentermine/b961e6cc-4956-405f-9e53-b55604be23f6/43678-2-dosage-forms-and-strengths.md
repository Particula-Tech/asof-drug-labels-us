# 3 DOSAGE FORMS AND STRENGTHS

Orally disintegrating tablets (ODT) containing 15 mg or 30 mg phentermine hydrochloride (equivalent to 12 mg or 24 mg phentermine base, respectively).
The tablets are not scored.
The 15 mg ODT is a yellow with blue spots round tablet embossed with AX4 on one side.
The 30 mg ODT is a yellow round tablet embossed with AX7 on one side.
