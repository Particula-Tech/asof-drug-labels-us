# HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Phentermine Hydrochloride Tablets, USP is supplied as white round scored tablets, debossed with " C" above the score line and " E" below on one side and " 263" on the other side.

Bottle of 90 tablets NDC 62135-959-90

Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].

Dispense in a tight container as defined in the USP, with a child-resistant closure (as required).

Keep out of the reach of children.
