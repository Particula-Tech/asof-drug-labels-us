# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Qsymia is contraindicated in the following conditions:

- Pregnancy [see Warnings and Precautions (5.1) and Use in Specific Populations (8.1)]
- Glaucoma [see Warnings and Precautions (5.4)]
- Hyperthyroidism
- During or within 14 days following the administration of monoamine oxidase inhibitors [see Drug Interactions (7.1)]
- Known hypersensitivity or idiosyncrasy to the sympathomimetic amines [see Adverse Reactions (6.2)].
