# INSTRUCTIONS FOR USE

PRILOSEC® (pry-lo-sec) (omeprazole magnesium) for delayed-release oral suspension

Taking PRILOSEC in water:

1. 1.PRILOSEC comes in packets containing 2.5 mg and 10 mg of PRILOSEC.
2. 2.Use an oral syringe to draw up the amount of water needed to mix your dose.
   Ask your pharmacist for an oral syringe.
3. 3.If your dose of PRILOSEC is 2.5 mg, add 5 mL of water to a clean container.
   Empty the contents of the 2.5 mg packet into the container of water.
4. 4.If your dose of PRILOSEC is 10 mg, add 15 mL of water to a clean container.
   Empty the contents of the 10 mg packet into the container of water.
5. 5.If you or your child are instructed to use more than 1 packet for your dose of PRILOSEC, follow the mixing instructions provided by your pharmacist or doctor.
6. 6.Stir.
7. 7.Leave the mixture for 2 to 3 minutes to thicken.
8. 8.Stir and drink the mixture within 30 minutes.
   If not used within 30 minutes, throw away this dose and mix a new dose.
9. 9.If any medicine remains after drinking, add more water, stir, and drink right away.

Giving PRILOSEC with water through a nasogastric (NG) tube or gastric tube:

For people who have an NG tube or gastric tube that is size 6 or larger, PRILOSEC may be given as follows:

1. 1.PRILOSEC comes in packets containing 2.5 mg and 10 mg of PRILOSEC.
2. 2.Use only a catheter tipped syringe to give PRILOSEC through an NG tube or gastric tube that is size 6 or larger.
3. 3.If your dose of PRILOSEC is 2.5 mg, add 5 mL of water to a catheter tipped syringe.
   Add the contents of the 2.5 mg packet to the syringe.
4. 4.If your dose of PRILOSEC is 10 mg, add 15 mL of water to a catheter tipped syringe.
   Add the contents of the 10 mg packet to the syringe.
5. 5.Shake the syringe right away and then leave the mixture for 2 to 3 minutes to thicken.
6. 6.Shake the syringe and inject through the NG tube or gastric tube into the stomach within 30 minutes.
7. 7.Refill the syringe with the same amount of water you used to prepare your dose of PRILOSEC (5 mL or 15 mL of water depending on your dose).
8. 8.Shake the syringe and flush any remaining medicine from the NG tube or gastric tube into the stomach.
