# INSTRUCTIONS FOR USE

Omeprazole (oh-MEP-ra-zol) Delayed-release Capsules, USP

Omeprazole delayed-release capsules

Taking Omeprazole delayed-release capsules with applesauce:

1. Place 1 tablespoon of applesauce into a clean container.
2. Carefully open the capsule and sprinkle the pellets onto the applesauce.
   Mix the pellets with the applesauce.
3. Swallow the applesauce and pellet mixture right away.
   Do not chew or crush the pellets.
   Do not store the applesauce and pellet mixture for later use.
