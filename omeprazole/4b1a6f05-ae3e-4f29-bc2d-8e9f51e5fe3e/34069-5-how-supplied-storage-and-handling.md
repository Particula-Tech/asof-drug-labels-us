# 16 HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

Omeprazole Delayed-Release Capsules, USP, 10 mg, are brown cap and brown body, each imprinted with "OME 10".
They are supplied as follows:

NDC 62135-559-30, bottle of 30 capsules (with desiccant)

Storage

Store omeprazole delayed-release capsules, USP in a tight container protected from light and moisture.
Store at 20° to 25°C (68° to 77°F) [see USP Controlled Room Temperature].
