## Information for Patients

ZEGERID should be taken on an empty stomach at least one hour prior to a meal.
ZEGERID is available either as 40 mg or 20 mg capsules with 1100 mg sodium bicarbonate.
ZEGERID is also available either as 40 mg or 20 mg single-dose packets of powder for oral suspension with 1680 mg sodium bicarbonate.

Directions for Use:

Capsules: Swallow intact capsule with water.
DO NOT USE OTHER LIQUIDS.
DO NOT OPEN CAPSULE AND SPRINKLE CONTENTS INTO FOOD.

Powder for Oral Suspension: Empty packet contents into a small cup containing 1-2 tablespoons of water.
DO NOT USE OTHER LIQUIDS OR FOODS.
Stir well and drink immediately.
Refill cup with water and drink.
