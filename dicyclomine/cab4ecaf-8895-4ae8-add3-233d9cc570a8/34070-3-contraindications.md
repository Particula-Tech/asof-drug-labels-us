# 4 CONTRAINDICATIONS

Dicyclomine hydrochloride is contraindicated in infants less than 6 months of age [see USE IN SPECIFIC POPULATIONS (8.4)], nursing mothers [see USE IN SPECIFICPOPULATIONS (8.3)], and in patients with:

- unstable cardiovascular status in acute hemorrhage
- myasthenia gravis [see WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS (5.4)]
- glaucoma [see ADVERSE REACTIONS (6.3) and DRUG INTERACTIONS (7.1)]
- obstructive uropathy [see WARNINGS AND PRECATIONS (5.8)]
- obstructive disease of the gastrointestinal tract [see WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS (5.5)]
- severe ulcerative colitis [see WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS (5.7)]
- reflux esophagitis
