# HOW SUPPLIED

Dicyclomine Hydrochloride Tablets USP, 20 mg are blue, round, flat-faced, beveled edge tablets, debossed LAN over 1282, supplied in bottles of 100, 500 and 1000 tablets.

Bottles of 100 Tablets NDC 0527-1282-01 Bottles of 500 Tablets NDC 0527-1282-05 Bottles of 1000 Tablets NDC 0527-1282-10

To prevent fading, avoid exposure to direct sunlight.
Store at 20° - 25°C (68° - 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Dispense in a well-closed container as defined in the USP.

Dicyclomine Hydrochloride Capsules USP, 10 mg are blue capsules with a white powder fill, imprinted logo LANNETT on the cap and 0586 on the body, supplied in bottles of 100, 500, and 1000 capsules.

Bottles of 100 Capsules NDC 0527-0586-01 Bottles of 500 Capsules NDC 0527-0586-05 Bottles of 1000 Capsules NDC 0527-0586-10

Store at 20° - 25°C (68° - 77°F) [See USP Controlled Room Temperature].
Dispense in a well-closed container as defined in the USP.
