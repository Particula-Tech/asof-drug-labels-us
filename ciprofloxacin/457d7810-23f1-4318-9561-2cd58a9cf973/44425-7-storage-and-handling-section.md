# STORAGE

Flexible Container: Store between 5 – 25°C (41 – 77°F).
Protect from light, avoid excessive heat, protect from freezing.
Ciprofloxacin is also available as CIPRO (ciprofloxacin HCl) Tablets 250, 500 and CIPRO (ciprofloxacin*) 5% and 10% Oral Suspension. * Does not comply with USP with regards to “loss on drying” and “residue on ignition”.
