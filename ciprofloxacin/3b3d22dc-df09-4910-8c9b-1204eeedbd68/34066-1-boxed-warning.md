# WARNING

Fluoroquinolones, including Ciprofloxacin Tablets, are associated with an increased risk of tendinitis and tendon rupture in all ages.
This risk is further increased in older patients usually over 60 years of age, in patients taking corticosteroid drugs, and in patients with kidney, heart or lung transplants (See WARNINGS).

Fluoroquinolones, including Ciprofloxacin Tablets, may exacerbate muscle weakness in persons with myasthenia gravis.
Avoid Ciprofloxacin Tablets in patients with known history of myasthenia gravis.
(See WARNINGS).
