# DESCRIPTION

Ciprofloxacin Tablets, USP is a synthetic broad spectrum antimicrobial agent for oral administration.
Ciprofloxacin hydrochloride, USP, a fluoroquinolone, is the monohydrochloride monohydrate salt of 1-cyclopropyl-6-fluoro-1,4-dihydro-4-oxo-7-(1-piperazinyl)-3-quinolinecarboxylic acid.
It is a faintly yellowish to light yellow crystalline substance with a empirical weight of 385.8.

Ciprofloxacin tablets are white to slightly yellowish.
The inactive ingredients are colloidal silicon dioxide, corn starch, hydrogenated vegetable oil, hypromellose, magnesium stearate, microcrystalline cellulose, polyacrylate dispersion (methylacrylate and ethylacrylate copolymer), polyethylene glycol, purified water, simethicone emulsion, sodium starch glycolate, talc, and titanium dioxide.
