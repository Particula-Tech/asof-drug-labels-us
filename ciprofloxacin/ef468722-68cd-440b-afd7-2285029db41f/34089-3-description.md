# 11 DESCRIPTION

Ciprofloxacin (ciprofloxacin hydrochloride) tablets USP are synthetic antimicrobial agents for oral administration.
Ciprofloxacin hydrochloride, USP, a fluoroquinolone, is the monohydrochloride monohydrate salt of 1-cyclopropyl-6-fluoro-1, 4-dihydro-4-oxo-7-(1-piperazinyl)-3-quinolinecarboxylic acid.
It is a faintly yellowish to light yellow crystalline substance with a molecular weight of 385.8.

Ciprofloxacin film-coated tablets USP are available in 250 mg, 500 mg and 750 mg (ciprofloxacin equivalent) strengths.
Ciprofloxacin tablets USP are white film coated tablets.
The inactive ingredients are pregelatinized starch, microcrystalline cellulose, colloidal silicon dioxide, crospovidone, magnesium stearate, hypromellose, titanium dioxide and polyethylene glycol.
