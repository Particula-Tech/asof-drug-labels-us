# OVERDOSAGE

In the event of an acute overdosage, the stomach should be emptied by inducing vomiting or by gastric lavage.
The patient should be carefully observed and given supportive treatment.
Adequate hydration must be maintained.
Only a small amount of ciprofloxacin (<10%) is removed from the body after hemodialysis or peritoneal dialysis.
Serious adverse effects were not observed in rats receiving single oral doses of ciprofloxacin as high as 2,000 mg/kg.
