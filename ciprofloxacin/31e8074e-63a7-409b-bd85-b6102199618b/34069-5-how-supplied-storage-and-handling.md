# 16HOW SUPPLIED/STORAGE AND HANDLING

OTIPRIO is a sterile, preservative-free, otic suspension of 6% (60 mg/mL, w/v) ciprofloxacin in a neutral pH buffered, isotonic solution containing poloxamer 407.

Each OTIPRIO carton contains 1 mL of 6% (60 mg/mL, w/v) ciprofloxacin in a 2 mL single-patient use glass vial fitted with a stopper not made with natural rubber latex.
(NDC 0268-8200-01)

OTIPRIO should be stored at 2 to 8°C (36 to 46°F) until prior to use to prevent thickening during preparation.
Protect from light.
Store in the original carton until dose preparation.
