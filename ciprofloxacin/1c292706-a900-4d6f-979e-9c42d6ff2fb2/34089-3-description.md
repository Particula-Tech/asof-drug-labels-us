# DESCRIPTION

CILOXAN® (ciprofloxacin hydrochloride ophthalmic ointment) is a synthetic, sterile, multiple dose, antimicrobial for topical use.
Ciprofloxacin is a fluoroquinolone antibacterial.
It is available as the monohydrochloride monohydrate salt of 1-cyclopropyl-6-fluoro-1,4-dihydro-4-oxo-7-(1-piperazinyl)-3-quinolinecarboxylic acid.
Ciprofloxacin is a faint to light yellow crystalline powder with a molecular weight of 385.82.

Ciprofloxacin differs from other quinolones in that it has a fluorine atom at the 6-position, a piperazine moiety at the 7-position, and a cyclopropyl ring at the 1-position.

Each gram of CILOXAN®(ciprofloxacin hydrochloride ophthalmic ointment) contains: Active: ciprofloxacin HCl 3.33 mg equivalent to 3 mg base.
Inactives: mineral oil, white petrolatum.
