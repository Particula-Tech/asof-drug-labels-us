# How Supplied:

100-mg Tablets (white, scored, round-shaped), containing 100 mg trimethoprim–bottle of 100 (NDC 61570-057-01).
Imprint on tablets “PROLOPRIM 09A.”Store at 15° to 25°C (59° to 77°F) in a dry place.

200-mg Tablets (yellow, scored, round-shaped), containing 200 mg trimethoprim–bottle of 100 (NDC 61570-058-01).
Imprint on tablets “PROLOPRIM 200.”Store at 15° to 25°C (59° to 77°F) in a dry place and protect from light.

Rx Only.
