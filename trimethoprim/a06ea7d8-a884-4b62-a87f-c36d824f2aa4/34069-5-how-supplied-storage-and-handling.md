# HOW SUPPLIED

PRIMSOL (trimethoprim hydrochloride oral solution), dye-free, alcohol-free, bubble gum flavored, containing trimethoprim hydrochloride equivalent to 50 mg of trimethoprim in each 5 mL: bottle of 473 mL (1 pint).
NDC 13551-501-05.
Store between 15-25°C (59-77°F).
Dispense in tight, light-resistant glass or PET plastic containers as defined in USP.
