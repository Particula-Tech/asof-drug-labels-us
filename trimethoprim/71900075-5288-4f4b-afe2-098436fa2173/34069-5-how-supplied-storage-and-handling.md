# HOW SUPPLIED

Polymyxin B Sulfate and Trimethoprim Ophthalmic Solution, USP is a sterile solution.
Each mL contains trimethoprim sulfate equivalent to 1 mg trimethoprim and polymyxin B sulfate 10,000 units in a plastic dropper bottle of 10 mL (NDC 61314-628-10).

Storage: Store at 15° - 25°C (59° - 77°F) and protect from light.
